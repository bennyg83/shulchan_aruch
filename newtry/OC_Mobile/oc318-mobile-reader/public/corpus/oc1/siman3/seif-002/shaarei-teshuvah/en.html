"He shall not speak" — Baer Hetev. See Rambam, Laws of Character Traits chapter 5: it states that one shall not speak while relieving oneself, even for a great need. Mishnah Berurah wrote that this emerges from the incident of Rav Safra that he brings in Berachot and Tamid — see there. Perhaps Rambam speaks specifically at the time when he is actually relieving himself, that even for a great need it is forbidden; whereas the Rema, who wrote "and he shall not speak there" — that is, in the bathroom — perhaps at a time when he is not yet relieving himself it is permitted for a need.
