(א) And he blesses for them Birkat Hamazon, etc. — for if he blesses for them he can no longer drink at home Birkat Hamazon (Tur in the name of Rav Amram); Beit Yosef and Taz strained in explanation — see their lengthy discussion; I found in Ra'avan 74 who concluded Rav Amram himself gave the reason Mar said between the third and fourth cups one should not drink — until here; meaning: when he blesses for them he must drink from the cup and it becomes his third cup, therefore Rosh enacted to prompt him word by word.
