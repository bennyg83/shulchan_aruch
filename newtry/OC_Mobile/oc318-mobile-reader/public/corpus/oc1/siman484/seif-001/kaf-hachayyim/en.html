1) [Se'if 1] One who wishes to recite the blessing, etc. This is the language of Rif at the end of the chapter Arvei Pesachim, matching the Aramaic language of the Gemara. Tur reproduced it exactly, and the Shulchan Aruch therefore also reproduced it exactly, since its usual practice is to cite the language of Tur or Rambam without alteration.
