(א) And he blesses Birkat Hamazon. But without Birkat Hamazon it is forbidden to uproot, even if he wishes to eat there more — because this is not called a passing mitzvah, for he can go to another place for Birkat Hamazon (Avudraham) siman 478 and siman 479.

(ב) And that’s not eating. Dea has already eaten an epicoman in his house and wrote the column in the name of Rabbi Dafi if he wants not to eat an epicoman until he eats a second in another house, and he will return and blesses a name where he is not allowed to eat at this night in the P, but in a written book that he does not eat an epicoman, but once, and he says:

(ג) And he blesses for them Birkas Hamazon — but he is forbidden to bless Birkas Hamazon on their behalf even though it is obligatory to eat matzah — if so Birkas Hamazon is also obligatory and he can discharge others [as siman 467] — Birkas Hamazon is different, as written And you shall eat and be satisfied and bless — one who ate blesses [Avudraham]; see Tur siman 486.

(ד) And he does not eat, etc. — for it is forbidden to taste before kiddush, as written siman 472; and if he eats there he will need to bless Birkat Hamazon as well.
