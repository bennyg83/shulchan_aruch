And he does not eat and drink with them. For he already ate the afikoman and drank the third cup; and even if he wants not to eat the afikoman until he eats a second time in another house, it is forbidden, for it is forbidden to eat on this night twice and in two places, since it appears as one who eats two Pesach offerings and in two places; and even though there is no practical difference in this only regarding eating the afikoman, as explained above at the end of the siman in the gloss, nevertheless l'chatchilah even for other mitzvos one should not do so (and see there in the Magen Avraham); and some later authorities are lenient in the matter to eat in another house; and all this discusses only one who wants to recite blessings in two or three houses because they do not have in that house anyone who knows how to bless, as explained in the Tur and Beit Yosef on this; but if they have among them someone who knows how to bless, only that they do not have a joining for zimun, and therefore he wants to go from house to house to join for zimun, this is certainly not proper, for what is different about Pesach from the whole year, that we do not say one must specifically seek out a joining for zimun; and specifically regarding saying Hallel and answering Hodu that is in Hallel they needed a joining, and not regarding Birkas HaMazon, as explained in the words of the later authorities above in siman 479, and as the Bach also wrote there in the name of the Maharal of Prague, and he protests this custom; and so it appears to me as the main ruling, and I praise the lazy ones who do not leave the doorway of their houses on the festival.
