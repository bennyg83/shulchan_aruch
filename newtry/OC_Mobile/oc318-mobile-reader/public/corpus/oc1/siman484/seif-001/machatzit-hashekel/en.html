(א) And he blesses, etc. — for this is not a passing mitzvah — meaning we hold as Rama in siman 168 that bread, since blessing follows it — Birkat Hamazon — even if he went elsewhere, if he ate there he may bless Birkat Hamazon there at the second eating place; nevertheless l'chatchila forbidden to uproot from his place to go elsewhere except for a passing mitzvah, as stated there.
