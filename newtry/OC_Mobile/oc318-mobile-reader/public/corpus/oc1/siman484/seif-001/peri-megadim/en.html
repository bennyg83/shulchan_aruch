And afterward — Taz. What he wrote, and Rosh wrote since the enactment of the Sages is, etc. — see Rosh Hashanah 29b Tosafot s.v. berakhat; in Penei Yehoshua it was questioned on the word 'specifically' Tosafot wrote — see there; I do not know why Maharan questioned this; why not say the implication is not only Shabbat bread which is also obligatory to eat, nevertheless one does not discharge as Magen Avraham wrote siman 167 letter mem alef — one in distress is exempt; unlike matzah which is obligatory — like other mitzvot; and Tosafot's reason: specifically wine of kiddush and motzi of matzah — because their benefit is not direct; and for the girsa 'for their benefit' also — plainly only this is questioned; plainly eating matzah need not be questioned as Rashi explained — it is certainly a mitzvah blessing and discharges, unlike motzi which is enjoyment; nevertheless it is simple since it is obligatory he discharges. And already we wrote above in Peri Megadim that all mitzvot one who left discharges because of partnership and enjoyment — not to bless and not to benefit; and Ra'avad Berakhot 20b — possibly partnership does not apply to women (and Rashi Chumash Nitzavim 'with you in covenant' — see there); if so women although obligated in mitzvot d'oraisa, all who left do not discharge if we say no partnership for them; and Haggadah recitation — possibly even if she has not yet fulfilled she does not discharge men — see Tosafot Megillah 4a s.v. she'af hen; one answer — they are d'rabbanan; nevertheless for women discharges when they have not yet fulfilled, as stated; and blessings of eating matzah and motzi — different even for men, as written.
