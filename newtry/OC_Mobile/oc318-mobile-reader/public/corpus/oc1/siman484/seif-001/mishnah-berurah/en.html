(1) One who wants, etc.: because there is no one among them who knows how to make Kiddush and say the Haggadah.
(2) He recites the blessing first, etc.: that is, that he makes Kiddush and says the Haggadah, everything in order, and eats matzah and maror and afikoman until Birkas HaMazon.
(3) And he recites Birkas HaMazon: and drinks the cup; and without Birkas HaMazon it is forbidden to leave his place to go to another place, and even if he intends to eat more there.
(4) And afterward he recites the blessings: that is to say, that he makes Kiddush for them and also recites Borei Pri HaGafen for them, and recites for them the blessing of karpas, and says the Haggadah before them and the blessing of the second cup, and also HaMotzi and the blessing of eating matzah and maror; and even though regarding other blessings of enjoyment we hold that one cannot bless for others unless he also partakes with them, the blessing over the bread of matzah and Kiddush of the day are different, for they are an obligation; therefore he can bless to discharge others even though he does not partake; and likewise the blessing over the vegetables is also considered a mitzvah, and he can discharge others.
(5) Vegetables: that is, karpas and maror.
(6) And he does not eat, etc.: for he already ate the afikoman, and it is forbidden for him to taste again, as above in siman 478; and drinking as well is forbidden according to all opinions, for he stands between the third cup and the fourth, as above in siman 479.
(7) And they recite Birkas HaMazon: but he is forbidden to recite Birkas HaMazon for them, for with Birkas HaMazon one may not discharge another unless he also ate and became obligated, as it is written, "And you shall eat and be satisfied and bless"; he who ate is the one who should bless.
(8) He should recite word by word: and they should answer after him; for whenever he reads word by word and the other answers with him, it is not a blessing in vain even though he already fulfilled his obligation, for he is only teaching him how to bless.
(9) And he does not eat, etc.: and it is forbidden to taste before Kiddush, as written in siman 271. And if he also intends to fulfill his obligation with the Kiddush that he says before them, he will need to eat a kezayis there and also recite Birkas HaMazon.
(10) He should finish everything: even Hallel.
(11) And he should not eat or drink: as above in s.k. 6.
