(א) Siman 61 "one who," etc., and "and afterward," etc. But within the meal it is not as written there — friends who were reclining, etc. — and one must apply Birkat Hamazon per the Rif's view; and even if he left some friends there, it is forbidden to uproot l'chatchila unless for a passing mitzvah, as we wrote.

(ב) He blesses for each one, etc. And so Taz, and so too the Rif there from this [proof] of Rosh Hashana 29; and that is what is written "and they drink," etc., and "and they eat," etc., and over all of them he blesses.

(ג) And that's where he is. As a third-year-old, there is no such thing as Patrin as:

(ד) And he blesses for them — as in Yerushalmi Berachos brought by Ran end of siman 485: all blessings one is exempt from, one discharges the public — except Birkas Hamazon, as written And you shall eat and be satisfied and bless — one who ate blesses; see Hagahot Maimoniyot. And although they said in chapter Three Who Ate — but one scribe and one boor: scribe blesses and boor is discharged — there the scribe is also obligated only that it is a mitzvah to divide. And this requires further study in Ran end of siman 485 there.

(ה) And if not so — Taz and so in Tur; meaning from what is written in chapter 3 of Sukkah: if there was a slave, woman, or minor, etc. — and it deals even if they are not obligated since they already recited, similar to adult at end who discharges even if already recited; for all mitzvah blessings a person discharges his fellow as explained; and Yerushalmi brought only Birkas Hamazon and Shema and prayer.

(ו) And afterward he goes, etc. To include that one need not adjoin Hallel to the meal, as in the Talmud 101b; and all the more so after Hallel or before everything, as we wrote.
