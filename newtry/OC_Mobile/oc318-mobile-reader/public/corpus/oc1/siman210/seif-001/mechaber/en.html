One who eats less than a kezayis — what is his law. It contains two se'ifim.
One who eats less than a kezayis, whether of bread or of other foods, and one who drinks less than a revi'is, whether of wine or of other beverages, recites beforehand the blessing appropriate to that type, but afterward does not recite any blessing at all. And some are in doubt, saying that upon something that is in its natural whole state, such as a single grape or a pomegranate seed, one recites an after-blessing even though it does not contain a kezayis; therefore it is proper to be careful not to eat a whole creation [beriah] that is less than a kezayis. {Rama: It is called a beriah only if he ate it as it is; but if he removed the seed from it, it is not called a beriah. [Rabbeinu Yonah, chapter "Keitzad Mevarchin," and Beis Yosef in the name of the Rashba.]} And some are further in doubt regarding the after-blessing on wine, whether one recites it only upon a kezayis; therefore it is good to be careful not to drink less than a kezayis or a revi'is.
