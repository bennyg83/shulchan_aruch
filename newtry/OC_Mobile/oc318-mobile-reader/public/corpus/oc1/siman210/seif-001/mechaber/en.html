One who eats less than a kezayit — what is his law. It
contains 2 seifim: One who eats less than a kezayit, whether from bread or from other foods, and one who drinks less than a revi'it, whether from wine or from other beverages — blesses initially the blessing fitting for that species, and afterward does not bless at all. And some are uncertain to say that on something that is in its created form, such as a grape berry or pomegranate seed, that one blesses after it even though there is not a kezayit in it — therefore it is proper to be careful not to eat a beriah less than a kezayit.And it is not called a beriah unless he ate it as it is; but if he removed the pit from it, it is not called a beriah [R' Yitzchak, chapter Keitzad Mevarchin, and Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba].} And some are further uncertain regarding the after-blessing on wine whether one blesses it on a kezayit — therefore it is good to be careful not to drink less than a kezayit or a revi'it.] ויש מסתפקין עוד בברכה אחרונה של יין אם מברכין אותו על כזית לכן טוב ליזהר שלא לשתות (אלא) פחות מכזית או רביעית:
