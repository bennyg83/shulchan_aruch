1) [Seif 1] One who eats less than a kezayis, etc. This excludes Rabbeinu Yonah's view that before eating less than the requisite measure of fruit, one recites Shehakol. It also excludes what Kol Bo writes in the name of Rav Achai Gaon, that over less than the requisite measure one need not recite even Shehakol. The halachic authorities do not agree. Rather, before eating, one must recite the blessing appropriate to that species even if it is less than the requisite measure, as stated in Beis Yosef; see there. Bach writes the same, and this is the consensus of the later authorities and the common practice. See above, siman 17, paragraph 2.
2) There: One who eats less than a kezayis, etc. This is because it is forbidden to derive enjoyment from this world without a blessing. Tur; Levush.
