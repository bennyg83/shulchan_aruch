A kezayit, etc. Beit Yosef writes in this siman and in siman 184 that according to all opinions an after-blessing is recited over a kezayit, and this is the practice. I am therefore surprised by Orchot Chaim, who cites Seder Berachot as saying that initially a person should not eat less than a kebeitzah, explaining that this is because there is a doubt whether the measure for an after-blessing on food is a kezayit or a kebeitzah.
That he should not eat a complete creature, etc. For example, one should not eat a single small berry by itself, nor one single legume.
