Until a revi'it — see in Mishna Berurah what he wrote in the name of Magen Avraham, that even to taste from many pots is permitted. And behold it cannot be said his intent is from all of them to reach a revi'it — for then what is the novelty; and further, what is this that he concludes afterward "however, if his intent is to eat only a little," etc., "for this is full eating" — what superiority is there here? Since from many pots together there was only the amount of a revi'it; and if because his intent is to eat — then even from one pot it would also be forbidden. If so, it appears Magen Avraham's intent is that a revi'it is permitted from each and every pot, for on each one separately it is called tasting to know if it needs salt and spices. And ostensibly his words apply to both opinions brought in siman 567 seif 2 — namely, to the poskim there who hold even at once a revi'it is permitted, a revi'it would be permitted from each pot separately in one act; and to the poskim there who hold specifically little by little, little by little from each pot until a revi'it. However, I saw later there in Biur HaGra that he holds both opinions there are only regarding affliction (inui), but regarding blessing — if we hold the Gemara's sugya refers to it — if so, what the Gemara says "the amount of a revi'it" means at once; and in our matter, behold per the first opinion, which is Rambam's view, it is known he holds the sugya refers also to blessing, as the Gra wrote — if so, it necessarily follows he holds even at once is permitted. And know further: what Magen Avraham concluded that if his intent is to eat only a little it is forbidden to do so many times, for this is full eating — ostensibly, since his intent is to eat, even once it is forbidden, as he wrote earlier in sk 6 that the main thing depends on intent of the heart (and he wrote at the end a second reasoning as primary, as written, that this refers to this reasoning, as Yad Efraim and Magen Gibborim wrote). And it appears this is Peri Megadim's intent in letter 4, that he wrote in letter 7 see letter 6 — and there is slight room to study this; and it appears to me Magen Avraham's intent is: even according to what he brought initially as proof from Rokeach to be lenient even when intentional, since here he tastes only a little, when he does it many times it is forbidden even according to him.
