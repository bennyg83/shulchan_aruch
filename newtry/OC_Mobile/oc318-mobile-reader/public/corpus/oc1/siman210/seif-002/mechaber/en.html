One who tastes a cooked dish does not need to recite a blessing unless the amount is a רביעית, even if he swallows it. However, some say that if he swallows it, a blessing is required, and they exempt the one who tastes only when he spits it out — in which case, even a large amount does not require a blessing. {Rama: Rema : And in cases of doubt regarding blessings, we are lenient.}
