One who tastes a cooked dish need not recite a blessing, up to a revi'is, even if he swallows it. And some say that if he swallows it, it requires a blessing, and they exempted the one who tastes only when he tastes and then spits it out — in which case, even upon a large amount, he need not recite a blessing. {Rama: And in a doubt concerning blessings, we are lenient.}
