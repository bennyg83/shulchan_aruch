דין מי שבדק ולא מצא מספר ככרות שהניח. ובו ד סעיפים:
The law of one who checked and did not find the number of loaves he had left. Contains 4 seifim. Nine piles of matza and one of chametz, and a mouse comes and takes and we do not know if it took chametz or matza, and it entered a checked house — he must return and check it, for every established place is like half of half. (However if the loaf is small enough that the mouse could eat it, we rely to be lenient that it ate it and one need not check (Tur).} If the loaf separated from its established place and the mouse took it from there, he need not return and check, for whatever separates — separates from the majority.) שכל הקבוע כמחצה על מחצה ואם פירש הככר ממקום קביעתו ונטלו העכבר משם א"צ לחזור ולבדוק דכל דפריש מרובא פריש:
