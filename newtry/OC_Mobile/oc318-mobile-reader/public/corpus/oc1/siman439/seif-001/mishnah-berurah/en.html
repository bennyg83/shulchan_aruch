(א) And it entered a checked house — for perhaps it brought chametz in its mouth from the pile.
(ב) He must return and check — the entire house.
(ג) However if the loaf is small, etc. — we rely that the mouse ate it in the pile and did not bring into the house; and if it brought, it finished it on the way.
(ד) For every established place, etc. — meaning each pile is considered separate regarding majority, and therefore in the house we are concerned for each pile separately as half of half.
(ה) And if it separated, etc. — for whatever separates is presumed to separate from the majority.
(ו) He need not return and check — the house.
