1) [Se'if 1] And a mouse came and took, etc. The reason is that anything fixed in its place is regarded as equally divided, as stated afterward. This applies specifically when the mouse took the item in our presence from its fixed place. If it took it outside our presence, it is regarded as though the item separated outside our presence and was then taken, in which case we say that anything that separates is presumed to have separated from the majority. Tosafos, Pesachim 9b; Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Peri Chadash; Chok Yaakov, subsection 5; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1; Chok Yosef, subsection 4; Mekor Chaim, subsection 1; Chemed Moshe, subsection 1.
