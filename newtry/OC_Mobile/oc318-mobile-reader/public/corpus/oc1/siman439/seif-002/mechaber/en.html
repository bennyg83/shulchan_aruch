Two piles, one of chametz and one of matza, and two houses, one checked and one not checked, and two mice came — one took chametz and one took matza — and we do not know which mouse entered which house; or two houses checked and one pile of chametz and a mouse came and took and we do not know into which house it entered; or even if we know it entered one of them and another entered after it and checked and found nothing; or he checked part of the house and found a loaf — in all these he need not return and check. ((These are Rambam's words, but the conclusion of the poskim is not so.)):
