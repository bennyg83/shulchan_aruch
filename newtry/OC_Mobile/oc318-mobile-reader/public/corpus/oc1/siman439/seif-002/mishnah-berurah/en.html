(א) Two piles, etc. — all these cases in the Gemara are when the mouse took from the pile outside and we do not know if chametz or matza; therefore when there are two houses we are concerned each is half of half; and when one house is checked and one not, we are concerned only for the unchecked one; and when both checked we are not concerned at all.
(ב) And we do not know into which house — when both houses were checked.
(ג) Or even if we know it entered one of them — and another checked after it.
(ד) So that he not forget, etc. — and although for other eating and benefit prohibitions we do not require a partition, here it is different because they do not avoid it all year — we are concerned lest he come to eat it; therefore a partition is required for recognition; and covering a vessel on top of the chametz does not suffice, lest he remove the vessel for his use and the chametz be exposed and they did not permit covering a vessel [in siman 446 seif 1] except for chametz found in his house on Yom Tov when a partition cannot be made, and also only for a short time, for at night he will burn it.
(ה) Or he checked part and found a loaf — we are not concerned there is another loaf, for presumably this is from the pile and the mouse ate the rest on the way.
