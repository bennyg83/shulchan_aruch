(א) He left nine and found ten — check after all nine, for the nine are presumed the original and the tenth is new.
(ב) And likewise ten and found nine — check after all ten.
(ג) And some say only when tied together — for then they are one body; but when separate, check only the one missing or added.
(ד) He need only check after the one — when not tied.
(ה) After the one — meaning the missing one when ten and found nine; the added one when nine and found ten.
(ו) When not tied — all agree.
