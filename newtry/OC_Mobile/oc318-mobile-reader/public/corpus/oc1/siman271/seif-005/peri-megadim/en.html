Two. The Taz means that when they said 'come and let us recite kiddush,' even the kiddush cup requires Borei pri hagefen, following the Olas Shabbos cited by the Magen Avraham. Without that declaration, even if one may not drink before kiddush, the prohibition alone is not an interruption. The declaration is stronger. He analyzes the Rambam and Raavad: the Rambam may mean that Shabbos at night establishes kiddush even without declaration, like Havdalah after declaration, while the Raavad objects that kiddush itself establishes without declaration. The Beit Yosef's 'some say' may refer to the Raavad, though the Magen Avraham understands the dispute mainly regarding voluntary drinking, not the kiddush cup itself.
