That he ate. That is, he had not yet eaten the slice of ha-motzi. Nevertheless he must bring lechem mishneh before him and it is not an interruption — Magen Avraham. And Taz wrote it appears obvious that even after he ate somewhat it is so that he sanctifies and need not bless ha-motzi again, and even for that view in seif 4 that requires blessing ha-motzi — that is since he already ate from before nightfall; but here where he blessed ha-motzi after Shabbat entered and did not remember here before eating, only because of the end of havdalah — he should not make havdalah before eating since we do not make havdalah on bread; it is as though havdalah is an interruption between ha-motzi and eating — rather he should eat the slice of ha-motzi first and afterward must interrupt and make havdalah, end of his words; and see Magen Avraham.
