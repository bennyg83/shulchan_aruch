Seif katan 5, it is forbidden, etc. See Terumas HaDeshen and siman 567: although tasting on a fast means tasting and spitting out, and is limited to less than a revi'is, rinsing one's mouth with water on an individual fast is permitted even with more than a revi'is.
Only tasting intended for enjoyment is forbidden beyond a revi'is. Rinsing the mouth is different because there is no intent for enjoyment, and water drunk not for thirst is not considered enjoyment, as above in siman 204.
The same applies to rinsing one's mouth with water before kiddush; it is permitted, for it is no more stringent than an individual fast.
And it appears to me, etc. To recite kiddush and eat means within the half hour before the time of Krias Shema, as above in siman 267.
And Shibolei HaLeket wrote, etc. 'And he recites Birkas HaMazon without a cup' means even according to the view that Birkas HaMazon requires a cup, as above in siman 182.
He should not eat beforehand, because although the Rif holds that a weekday meal that extended into Shabbos can count for kiddush in the place of a meal, the Rosh disagrees when that meal was not made for Shabbos honor. Here, even if the meal would count because it is for Shabbos honor, everyone agrees that kiddush must come first, since the Sages enacted kiddush before the meal.
