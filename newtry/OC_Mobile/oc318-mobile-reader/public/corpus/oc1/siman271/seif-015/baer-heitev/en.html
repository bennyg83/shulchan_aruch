(1) In speech. Tosafos wrote in Berachos 40a: if he spoke between kiddush and drinking he must return and bless — if it is not a matter related to the meal, end of his words. It implies that even though it is not related to kiddush, since it is related to the meal it is not an interruption. Magen Avraham.

(2) It spilled. But if he took in his hand a cup of water thinking it was wine and blessed borei peri hagafen and sanctified, he must return and bless borei peri hagafen and sanctify again on a cup of wine — ruled Maharai siman 115. And so agreed Taz, and distinguished: if there was wine at the time of kiddush on the tray or on the table ready to drink, then certainly he need not borei peri hagafen nor kiddush, only he drinks what is ready before him, end of his words; and Magen Avraham wrote if his intent is to drink more wine he need not bless borei peri hagafen again, and the same if it spilled, etc. — see siman 209 seif 2 in the gloss and end of siman 206. And if he sanctified in the morning on beer and the cup spilled, he need not bring another, for some say bread suffices as written siman 272. See Magen Avraham and Taz at length.
