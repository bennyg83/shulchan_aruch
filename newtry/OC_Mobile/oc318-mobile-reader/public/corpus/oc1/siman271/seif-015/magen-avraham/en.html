He interrupted with speech. Tosafot in Berachos write that if one spoke between kiddush and drinking, he must repeat the blessing unless it was a matter related to the meal. This implies that even if it is not related to kiddush, since it relates to the meal it is not an interruption, as in siman 167 regarding HaMotzi; the same applies to kiddush, which is also a need of the meal.
He should bring another cup. The Levush asks why another cup is needed if drinking does not prevent fulfillment. This is mistaken: Tosafot mean that the drinking of the assembled participants does not prevent fulfillment, since it is enough if one of them tastes. But if no one tastes at all, one has not fulfilled the mitzvah properly. Therefore the Shulchan Aruch writes that another cup should be brought and tasted from. If he had intent to drink more wine, he need not bless Borei pri hagefen again; the same applies if the cup spilled. If one recited kiddush in the morning over beer and the cup spilled, he need not bring another cup, since some say that bread suffices.
