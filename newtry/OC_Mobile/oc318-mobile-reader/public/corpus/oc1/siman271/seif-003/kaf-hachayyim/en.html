(3) Rambam, Hilchos Kiddush HaYom 29, says the Torah requires Kiddush without wine and the Sages instituted wine; Tosafos, Sukkah 38a, agrees. Magen Avraham says Torah law is fulfilled by Kiddush in prayer, since 'remember the Shabbos day' requires verbal mention, while Kiddush with a meal is rabbinic, as Beis Yosef, siman 269, writes. Tosafos, Pesachim 106a, first says similarly, though then suggests wine may be Torah law. Shaagas Aryeh 60 concludes wine is not Torah-required; Pri Chadash, siman 678, and Riaz in HaMachria 71 agree. Riaz says Kedushas HaYom in prayer fulfills Torah law, and that is the Kiddush for which women are Torah-obligated; meal Kiddush is rabbinic. Birkei Yosef cites him. Rashba in manuscript responsa says Torah law is fulfilled by Vayechulu, Veshamru, or any verbal mention of Shabbos; everything else is rabbinic [Machazik Berachah]. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, Chayei Adam, Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, and Pischei HaDevir agree. An unlearned person unable to pray who intends to fulfill his obligation through the chazzan's abridged seven-faceted blessing likewise fulfills Torah law. See the end of paragraph 9.
