Seif katan 3, needs, etc. See what I wrote at the beginning of siman 263, proving that bread takes precedence over Shabbos candles, and Shabbos candles take precedence over kiddush; all the more so, bread takes precedence over kiddush.
And 'honor of the day' means the needs of the daytime meals, food and drink.
The Ran in Arvei Pesachim explains why daytime kiddush contains no additional blessing beyond Borei pri hagefen: since daytime kiddush is not required according to the Torah, the Sages enacted only wine because daytime honor is greater than nighttime honor. Thus the daytime meal is primary and more significant than kiddush, and therefore daytime honor may take precedence over daytime kiddush.
Nevertheless, perhaps bread suffices, etc. The Tosefes Shabbos writes that perhaps one loaf suffices even without lechem mishneh, as the Magen Avraham writes at the end of siman 254.
