It is better that he buy, etc. Kiddush is a positive commandment according to the Torah, whereas preparing the needs of the meal is the mitzvah of delight from the words of the Prophets, as it is written, and you shall call Shabbos a delight. Although the positive commandment of Remember can be fulfilled merely by verbal remembrance of the sanctity of Shabbos, and over wine is only from the words of the Sages, as above in seif katan 2, nevertheless, since the essence of kiddush is according to the Torah, his wine precedes everything. The later authorities agree that all this is specifically when he has bread for the night and for the day; but if he has no bread, it is better that he buy bread and recite kiddush over it, for he is obligated to eat bread at night and also the next day of Shabbos. If he has for kiddush at night but not for kiddush by day and for the honor of the day, although it is logical that the honor of the day takes precedence over kiddush of the day, still perhaps bread suffices for him and with the remainder he should buy wine for the daytime kiddush. See Biur Halacha.
Wine for the need of the day. This means for daytime kiddush. The reason is that nighttime kiddush is essentially according to the Torah, whereas daytime kiddush is only D'Rabbanan. Although at night he could say the blessing of kiddush over bread, while by day, if he has nothing over which to recite kiddush, he will remain without kiddush, nevertheless, since where there is wine one does not recite the nighttime kiddush over bread, the night takes precedence.
Other meal needs. For example, if he has a few delicacies, it is better that he leave them for the daytime meal. Yam Shel Shlomo protests that people are not careful about this, and on the contrary, they add more on Friday night.
Precedes the honor of the day. This means both the needs of the daytime meal and the kiddush made by day, as written at the beginning of the seif.
