Levush, at its entrance, etc. Once it is dark, even if he did not accept Shabbos, it is forbidden to taste; if he accepted Shabbos while it is still day, it is also forbidden. It is always permitted to rinse one's mouth, since he does not intend enjoyment of tasting. If one wishes to accept Shabbos while it is still day, recite kiddush and eat, and pray Maariv at night, he may do so, provided this is before the restricted time for Maariv as explained in siman 235.
