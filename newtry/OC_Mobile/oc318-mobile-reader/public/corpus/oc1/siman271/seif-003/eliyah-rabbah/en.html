Levush, they discharge, etc. The Bach asked why women may discharge men in kiddush but not in Megillah. He missed Tosafot in Sukkah, who explain that when there is a public group it is degrading for them to be discharged by a woman. In practice, it appears that she should not discharge men who are not members of her household, but she may discharge her household when there is no householder, though this is not taught as the usual ruling. If the household contains only women and minors, she may certainly discharge them. A minor cannot discharge a woman, even at thirteen, because for a Torah-level matter we do not rely on the presumption of two hairs until his beard has filled out. A woman who prayed Maariv may discharge others, since her Torah-level obligation has already passed. Kiddush of Yom Tov is only D'Rabbanan according to the Maggid Mishneh, yet it has the same laws as Shabbos kiddush, and if he has only one cup, Shabbos takes precedence.
