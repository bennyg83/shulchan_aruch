Needs of the meal. This applies specifically when he has bread. If he has no bread, it is better to buy bread and recite kiddush over it, because kiddush must be in the place of the meal, and there is an obligation to eat bread as stated in siman 188. If he has enough for nighttime kiddush but not for daytime kiddush and daytime honor, perhaps daytime honor is preferable; nevertheless, perhaps bread is sufficient and the remainder should be used for wine.
And that which was taught. See Zohar, Yisro, which says that one must arrange his table on Shabbos night with bread and foods, and Rabbi Yitzchak says that this applies also by day.
