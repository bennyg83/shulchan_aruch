Seif katan 8, cup, etc. It is not a diversion of mind. Even though according to one view HaMotzi must be repeated because kiddush is an interruption, this is not so for wine, since he need not bless again on the kiddush wine. It is as if he blessed Borei pri hagefen before kiddush, and kiddush is the normal interruption between that blessing and drinking.
He therefore need not bless again over wine drunk during the meal.
