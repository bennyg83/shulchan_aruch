Seif 6 in the gloss. To remove himself from dispute. This is difficult for me, for he places himself into a dispute, since according to the first opinion he must recite Birkas HaMazon immediately, for since he washed his hands he is forbidden to eat more before Birkas HaMazon, as the Mechaber ruled above in siman 179. If so, at first glance it would be proper to act according to the first opinion and recite Birkas HaMazon first. The earlier doubts cannot be repaired: he should not taste from the cup of Birkas HaMazon until after he recites kiddush over the second cup, and he should recite kiddush and then eat again, so that it will certainly be kiddush in the place of a meal. Regarding mentioning Retzeh, the Rama already decided that he should not mention it. If so, this appears to be a clear path by which he fulfills according to all views. This requires study.
