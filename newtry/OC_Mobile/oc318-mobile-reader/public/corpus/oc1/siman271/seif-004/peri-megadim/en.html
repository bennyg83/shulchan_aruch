Prayer differs from kiddush: by prayer, tasting is at least permitted and only eating is forbidden; by kiddush even tasting is forbidden. On the wine he need not bless again, because kiddush is not a new act with respect to wine that was already before him, and similarly when he has no wine, kiddush over bread is not an interruption. For halacha, the conclusion follows the view that HaMotzi is not repeated, because doubts in blessings are ruled leniently. Although generally the first anonymous opinion is primary, in doubtful blessings we are lenient even against a majority unless it is a lone opinion against many.
