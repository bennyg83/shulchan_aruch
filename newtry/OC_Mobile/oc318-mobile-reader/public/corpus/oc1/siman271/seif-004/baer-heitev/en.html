(1) To taste. It appears to me it is permitted to rinse his mouth since he does not intend for the pleasure of tasting. And if he wishes to accept Shabbat from before nightfall and to sanctify and eat and pray Maariv at night he is permitted — Magen Avraham, end of his words; and see siman 267.

(2) A cloth. And it is obvious he need not pray Maariv if he spreads a cloth and sanctifies, since he began with permission as is the law of all Maariv prayer. Taz.

(3) At first. That is, that they would also drink wine at first with bread.

(4) The wine. Because the blessing on wine precedes kiddush and kiddush is not an interruption. Not so with the blessing on ha-motzi — kiddush is an interruption, therefore he must bless again. And the same law that he need not bless on wine within the meal, for after drinking the cup of kiddush there is no diversion of attention between that drinking and what follows it.

(5) Ha-motzi. For then he blesses ha-motzi before kiddush and kiddush is not an interruption.

(6) Ha-motzi. For he holds kiddush is not an interruption since doubtful blessings are decided leniently — certainly he should not also bless ha-motzi. Taz and so wrote the later authorities.
