The Taz explains that the Mechaber here follows the Rambam: if they said 'come and let us recite kiddush,' even during bein hashemashos or while it is still day, they may not drink even with a blessing, because kiddush has taken effect and one may not taste before kiddush. This contrasts with siman 179, where one may bless Borei pri hagefen if he wishes to drink.
