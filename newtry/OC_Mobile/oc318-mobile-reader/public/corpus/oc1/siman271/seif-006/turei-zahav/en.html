And he must mention Shabbos. This is like one who did not pray Mincha on Friday afternoon and prays two Shabbos prayers. See what I wrote in siman 188 regarding this matter.
And some say that even when he finished his meal, etc. This implies all the more so in the middle of his meal that the law is so. It is difficult: why does he write here that he blesses HaMotzi, when in seif 4 he brought opinions that he need not, and it implies that in doubt regarding blessings we are lenient? Here he writes that he should bless. In truth, the words of this Some say, that even when he finished, etc., are the words of the Rosh, as the Tur wrote and as the Beit Yosef copied, who agreed with Mahari of Trani. According to him, indeed in seif 4 as well one must bless HaMotzi, which is the first opinion there. But the difficulty is on the Shulchan Aruch. It appears to me that the phrase even when he finished does not refer to everything that follows, but only to this matter, that he spreads a cloth and recites kiddush. What he concludes afterward, that he blesses HaMotzi, applies specifically to where he finished his meal and washed his hands or said, Let us bless, for then he must bless HaMotzi a second time even on a weekday. If so, should he not also need Borei peri hagefen according to all views? Indeed, the word recites kiddush written here means the usual kiddush all year, that he first says Borei peri hagefen.
