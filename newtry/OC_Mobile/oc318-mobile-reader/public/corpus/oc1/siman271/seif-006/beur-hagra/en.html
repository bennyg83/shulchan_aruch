Seif 6, and he must. This is from the Tosefta, as we say in the fourth chapter of Berachos, 26b, that if one erred, etc.; although he is praying for a weekday, the same applies here.
And some say that he does not. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 15, and siman 188, seif katan 17.
And there is a dispute. The dispute is whether we consider that cup as part of the meal, in which case it is permitted. Although according to Rabbi Yosei there he must taste, that follows his own view that one need not interrupt.
Also whether. This depends on whether what he ate is considered the meal in the place of kiddush, since it was a weekday meal. See Tosafot there, s.v. venimrinhu, for Rabbi Yosei may hold like Rav that kiddush need not be in the place of the meal.
And some say. Since this view holds that one must eat after kiddush, why should he recite Birkas HaMazon first, as above.
And he blesses. This follows the first opinion in seif 4. See Tosafot 100a, s.v. Rabbi Yosei; this is the view of the Rif, and Tosafot 102b, s.v. rishon, and so too the text of the Rif cited above.
