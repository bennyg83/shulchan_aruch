Seif katan 6, he must, etc. Kiddush is different from lulav. By lulav, interruption is required only because one began in prohibition; if he began permissibly, he need not interrupt. By kiddush, the original enactment was that kiddush be before the meal and in the place of the meal. Therefore even if he already ate before kiddush, whatever can still be corrected should be corrected, so he interrupts, recites kiddush, and then completes his meal.
