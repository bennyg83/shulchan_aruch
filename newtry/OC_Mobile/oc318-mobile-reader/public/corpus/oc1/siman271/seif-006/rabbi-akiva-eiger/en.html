Magen Avraham, seif katan 6. Although in siman 652. This is difficult for me: what did Tosafot and the Ran ask? One can distinguish that only regarding Krias Shema and lulav, where eating is not inherently prohibited but only because of concern that one may forget to perform the mitzvah, and mere tasting is permitted, as in Magen Avraham, siman 235, seif 4 and siman 651, seif 4, therefore if they began permissibly they need not interrupt. But regarding kiddush, where even tasting is forbidden and the eating itself is the prohibition, as the Magen Avraham wrote here in seif katan 9 and seif katan 10, beginning permissibly should not help. This requires study.
