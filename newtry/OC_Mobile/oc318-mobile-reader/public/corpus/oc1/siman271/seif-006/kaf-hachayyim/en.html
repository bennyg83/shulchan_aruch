(6) Know that Yom Tov Kiddush is not Torah law [Maggid Mishneh 29:18; Magen Avraham; Tosefes Shabbos; Birkei Yosef; Machazik Berachah; Shulchan Aruch HaRav; Chayei Adam; Ben Ish Chai]. Eshel Avraham, siman 296, paragraph 11, comments on Magen Avraham, but we need not follow Rav Assi; see the Gemara. Nevertheless Yom Tov Kiddush follows all Shabbos Kiddush laws. If one has only one cup, Shabbos takes precedence [Magen Avraham and others]. This means Yom Tov falls on Friday and the cup is reserved for Shabbos, whose core Kiddush is Torah law though wine is rabbinic. Although one could argue today's rabbinic mitzvah takes precedence, he can use bread for Yom Tov Kiddush, so it is better to perform the Torah-rooted mitzvah [Levush Serad].
