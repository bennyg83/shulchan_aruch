Magen Avraham, seif katan 13. All still agree. This is according to the Rosh's understanding of the Rif, that he must return and eat. But according to the Ran, that he need not return and eat, then if he did not wash his hands he recites kiddush before Birkas HaMazon. He also need not spread a cloth, for the reason that the meal should come in honor of Shabbos does not apply. Nor does the reason that bread must be given precedence apply, since he does not wish to eat bread and there is no obligation of precedence.
