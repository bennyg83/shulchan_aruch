Seif katan 9, he blesses, etc. Kiddush is an interruption through two factors: Shabbos became sanctified, so eating became forbidden and the prior meal was ended, and afterward he actively interrupted by reciting kiddush. Together these require a new HaMotzi, but not a new blessing over wine, since kiddush always interrupts between Borei pri hagefen and drinking.
And the Olas Shabbos asked how this differs from prayer, etc. Here the Beit Yosef brings two opinions whether kiddush is an interruption, while above in siman 178 he rules without dispute that prayer during a meal is not an interruption.
Eating became forbidden to him, meaning even tasting.
The Magen Avraham adds the word 'tasting' so that one not ask from prayer, where even if there is no time to pray later, tasting is still permitted. By kiddush, even tasting is forbidden, making it a stronger removal from the meal.
According to his question, he should have asked against the Rosh and Tur, for they rule plainly that HaMotzi must be repeated here and also rule that prayer is not an interruption. Therefore one must answer as the Rosh did, that kiddush is different because tasting is forbidden.
'And some say' means the opinion brought by the Beit Yosef here, that even when one recites kiddush over wine, etc., it is not considered an interruption.
And if he recites kiddush over bread, etc. He is exempt according to all opinions. Even the first opinion does not require a new HaMotzi, because when kiddush is recited over bread, the bread has the status of wine for kiddush; when the HaMotzi obligation arose, he had not yet made an act of interruption.
