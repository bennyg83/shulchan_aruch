On top of it. In Tur in the name of the Yerushalmi that he should not show the bread in his cup, for we do not sanctify on it but on wine; and therefore if he sanctifies on bread he need not cover it with a cloth. But for another reason that Tur wrote: I heard in remembrance of the manna that was placed in a box with dew above and dew below — therefore also when sanctifying on bread one must cover with a cloth, and so is the practice — Taz. And see siman 262 s.k. 3, end of his words.
