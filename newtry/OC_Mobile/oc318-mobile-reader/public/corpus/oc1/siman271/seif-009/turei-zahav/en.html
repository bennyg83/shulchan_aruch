A piece of bread. In the Tur in the name of the Yerushalmi: so that the bread should not see its shame, that kiddush is not recited over it but over wine. According to this, if he recites kiddush over bread he need not cover it with a cloth. But according to another reason written by the Tur, namely, I heard a reason to make a remembrance of the manna, which lay in a box, dew above and dew below, automatically even when reciting kiddush over bread one must cover it with a cloth, and this is our practice.
