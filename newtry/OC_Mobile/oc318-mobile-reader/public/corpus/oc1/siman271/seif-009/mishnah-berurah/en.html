Over it. The Tur cites the Yerushalmi, that the bread should not see its shame, that kiddush is not recited over it but over wine, though it has precedence in other places. According to this, if one recites kiddush over bread, he need not cover it with a cloth. But according to another reason written by the Tur, that it is a remembrance of the manna, which lay in a box with dew above and dew below, automatically even when reciting kiddush over bread one must cover it with a cloth, and so is the practice. It is implied from the Peri Megadim that according to all reasons, when reciting kiddush over wine it is enough for the bread to be covered until after he finishes kiddush. So too in the morning the bread should be covered until after kiddush for these reasons. In Chayei Adam it implies that according to the reason of remembering the manna, it is good for it to be covered until after HaMotzi. Eliyah Rabbah and Derech HaChaim wrote that when he recites kiddush over bread, he should place his hands on the cloth and on the lechem mishneh during kiddush. When he reaches HaMotzi, he uncovers the lechem mishneh, places his hand on them, blesses HaMotzi, and when he reaches Hashem's name he raises both until he finishes the name. Then he sets them down, and after finishing HaMotzi he covers them again with the cloth and places his hand on them until he finishes kiddush.
