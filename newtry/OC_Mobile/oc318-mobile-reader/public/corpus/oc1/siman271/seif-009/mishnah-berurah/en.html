(41) On top of it. In Tur in the name of the Yerushalmi that he should not show the bread in his cup, for we sanctify only on wine, and it has a law of precedence in other places; therefore if he sanctifies on bread he need not cover it with a cloth. But for another reason Tur wrote — in remembrance of the manna that was placed in a box with dew above and dew below — therefore also when sanctifying on bread one must cover with a cloth, and so is the practice; and it implies in Peri Megadim that for all reasons it suffices when sanctifying on wine that it be covered until after he finishes kiddush, and similarly in the morning the bread too must be covered until after kiddush for these reasons. In Chayei Adam it implies that for the manna reason it is good that it be covered until after the blessing on ha-motzi. Aruch HaShulchan wrote: when he sanctifies on bread he places his hands on the cloth and on lechem mishneh at the time of kiddush; when he reaches ha-motzi he uncovers lechem mishneh and places his hand on them and blesses ha-motzi; when he reaches the Name he raises both upward until he finishes the Name and then puts them down; after finishing ha-motzi he returns to cover with the cloth and places his hand on them until he finishes kiddush.
