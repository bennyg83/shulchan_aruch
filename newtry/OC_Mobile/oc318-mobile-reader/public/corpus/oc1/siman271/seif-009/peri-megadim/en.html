And some say. The Taz and Magen Avraham explain that this applies specifically when the meal was finished, he washed his hands, and he had not mentioned 'let us bless.' According to Rabbeinu Yonah and the Ran in siman 179, eating is permitted without a new HaMotzi as long as he has not washed. Here, however, because there is both a prohibition and the act of saying 'let us bless,' they may agree that HaMotzi must be repeated. The Rama advises acting so as to avoid dispute: spread a cloth first, recite kiddush with Borei pri hagefen, and also recite HaMotzi.
