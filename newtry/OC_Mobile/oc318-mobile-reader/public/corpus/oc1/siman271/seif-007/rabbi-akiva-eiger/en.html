In my humble opinion, one can say the Rama omitted these words because this is only according to the one who holds havdalah is according to the Torah, for one interrupts when he began in prohibition, as the Magen Avraham wrote at the beginning of siman 239. But for us, since it is doubtful whether the halacha is that havdalah is according to the Torah or D'Rabbanan, see below at the end of siman 296, regarding interruption it is a D'Rabbanan doubt whether he is obligated to interrupt, and we rule leniently that he need not interrupt.
