The reason the Mechaber says 'before he ate' may be that after he ate he should recite kiddush over wine if he has wine, whereas before eating he must recite kiddush over bread even if he has wine, so that Borei pri hagefen not interrupt between HaMotzi and eating. This still requires study; see Tosefes Shabbos, Magen Avraham, and Eliyah Rabbah.
