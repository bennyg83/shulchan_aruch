(7) [Se'if 2] Women are obligated, etc. If a man prayed and now owes only rabbinic Kiddush, having fulfilled Torah law in prayer, while a woman did not pray and remains Torah-obligated, he may nevertheless discharge her. For Kiddush, one who already fulfilled his obligation may discharge another, as above at the end of siman 167 and below, siman 273:4. Rabbi Akiva Eiger, responsum 7, rules so and challenges Dagul MeRevavah's doubt; Mishbetzos Zahav agrees. See above, siman 193, paragraph 2, regarding women hearing a language they do not understand; we recorded that custom follows Rashi that they fulfill the obligation.
