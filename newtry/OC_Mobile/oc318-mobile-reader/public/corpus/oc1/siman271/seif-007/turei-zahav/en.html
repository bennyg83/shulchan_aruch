If he tasted, he recites kiddush. Meaning whenever he remembers, even at night. This is the dispute in the Gemara: according to the opinion that if he tasted he does not recite kiddush, Rashbam explains that he does not recite kiddush at night, but only the next day before eating. Automatically, according to the opinion that if he tasted he recites kiddush, which is the halacha, this means even at night. My teacher, the Bach, erred in haste and wrote that it is implied from Rashbam that he should not recite kiddush at night but only the next day before eating. He thought Rashbam explained this according to the opinion that if he tasted he recites kiddush, but this is not so; Rashbam wrote it only to explain the opinion that he should not recite kiddush. According to halacha, he certainly recites kiddush even at night.
