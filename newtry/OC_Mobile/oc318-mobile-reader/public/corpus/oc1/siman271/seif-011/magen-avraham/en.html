He pours from it into another cup. Tosafot at the end of Pesachim 105 write that for an obligatory cup one must taste from a cup containing a revi'is, and so writes the Agudah. Therefore here he should taste from the first cup and afterward add to it from the second cup, as in siman 182. Had the Beit Yosef seen Tosafot there at that time, he certainly would have ruled like them.
That he leave it for Havdalah. Tosafot in Arvei Pesachim and the Agudah disagree and hold that kiddush is preferable. Even according to the Beit Yosef, if he has beer for Havdalah it is better to recite kiddush over wine, for everyone agrees that Havdalah may be recited over beer where it is the local beverage, while many poskim dispute kiddush over bread and certainly over beer. Therefore even daytime kiddush takes precedence over Havdalah when Havdalah can be made on beer. It is good to recite kiddush over a large cup, leaving from that cup for daytime kiddush and Havdalah.
