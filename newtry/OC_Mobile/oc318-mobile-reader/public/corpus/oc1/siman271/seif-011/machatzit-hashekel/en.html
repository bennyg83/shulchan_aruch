Seif katan 11, before, etc. Meaning that he had not yet eaten the slice of HaMotzi. Since seif 4 says that one in the middle of a meal spreads a cloth and recites kiddush over wine, here must mean that he has not even eaten the HaMotzi slice. If he recites kiddush over wine, the original HaMotzi would become a blessing in vain, for kiddush would interrupt between HaMotzi and eating; therefore he recites kiddush over the bread.
And it is not an interruption. Waiting or instructing someone to bring lechem mishneh is not an interruption because it is a need of the meal, as in siman 167. Since he forgot it was Shabbos, he probably cut the bread as on a weekday, and it can count for lechem mishneh only if the pieces remain attached enough that lifting the small piece raises the large one with it.
