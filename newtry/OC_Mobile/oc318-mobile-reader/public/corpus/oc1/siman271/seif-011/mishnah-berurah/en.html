(50) He pours from it, etc. - The measure of a cheekful.
(51) From the second cup - Magen Avraham agreed with Tosafot that a cup of obligation must specifically be tasted from a cup that contains a revi'it of wine. Therefore the proper practice is as follows: first pour from the first cup into another cup, taking care that a revi'it of wine remains in it; taste a cheekful from the first cup; afterward pour the wine from the other cup back into this cup, thereby repairing its blemish, as above in siman 182 seif 6. He may then make Kiddush over it if it now contains a revi'it of wine. Although Eliyah Rabbah rejected his words and holds that the Shulchan Aruch's advice is preferable, see Tosefet Shabbat who reconciles them.
(52) He makes Kiddush with what remains - This wording implies that he makes Kiddush with this cup itself. Although above in seif 10 it is written that the cup should be full, that is only for the mitzvah and does not invalidate if he has no other. It is obvious that if he has another cup smaller than this, he should pour the wine into it so that it be full.
(53) Exactly a revi'it - Not specifically; the same applies even if he has more than this, but after tasting a cheekful he will not have a revi'it left. It stated this as a novelty, that even in this case he has a remedy by mixing in water. It refers to wine that was strong enough that after dilution he can make Kiddush over it. See below siman 272 seif 5.
(54) And this is specifically, etc. - This refers above, where he wrote that when he has one cup, he makes Kiddush with it at night; it teaches that this is specifically, etc.
(55) It is better, etc. - The Shulchan Aruch speaks where the cup has an exact measure and is already diluted; otherwise he could make Kiddush at night and dilute it to complete it also for Havdalah, as above.
(56) That he leave it for Havdalah - Some say that nighttime Kiddush has priority over Havdalah. Even according to the Shulchan Aruch, if he has beer for Havdalah, it is better to make Kiddush over wine, for according to everyone one may make Havdalah over beer, as written in siman 296 seif 2, whereas many poskim forbid Kiddush over beer, and Rabbeinu Tam is stringent even regarding bread. Therefore even in the morning it is better to make Kiddush over wine if he has beer for Havdalah. However, regarding the practice to make Kiddush in the synagogue and also Havdalah there, certainly Havdalah, which discharges the public, has priority over Kiddush, which is only a custom, as written in siman 269, according to Magen Avraham and other Acharonim.
(57) For nighttime Kiddush has priority - As above in this siman seif 3; see there in Mishna Berurah seif katan 8. Magen Avraham cited Knesset HaGedolah that it is good to make Kiddush over a large cup, leaving from that cup for daytime Kiddush and Havdalah; that is, he should repair it each time so it not be blemished, and in the morning and at Havdalah take a smaller cup than the previous one so it will be full. But Eliyah Rabbah questioned this practice, and Ma'amar Mordekhai also rejected it and wrote that it is better to do as our custom.
