(1) From the second cup. Nevertheless he wrote one tastes from the first cup and afterward adds to it from the second cup — see there. (And in Eliyah Rabbah he rejected Magen Avraham's words regarding placing two hands on what he challenged on Beit Yosef from Tosafos Pesachim, end of his words.)

(2) For havdalah. Tosafos and Agudah disagree on this and hold kiddush is preferable, and even in morning kiddush it precedes havdalah, for havdalah is possible on beer for all in a place where wine is not the law of the land and a person's soul is weary at dawn on beer; therefore where he has wine, kiddush is more preferable than havdalah; however in the synagogue it is more preferable to leave it for havdalah, and it is good to sanctify on a large cup from which he will leave for kiddush of the day and havdalah. Knesset HaGedolah, end of his words.
