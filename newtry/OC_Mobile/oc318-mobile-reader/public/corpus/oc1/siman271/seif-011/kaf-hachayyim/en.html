(11) Maharash's Derashos says two householders should not recite Kiddush simultaneously, because two voices are not heard; their families would not fulfill the obligation. This differs from Megillah, siman 690:2, and Hallel, siman 488, where listeners concentrate because of publicizing the miracle. The matter requires consideration. Accordingly, members of the meal should not recite aloud along with the head of the household [Eliyah Rabbah; Mishbetzos Zahav].
