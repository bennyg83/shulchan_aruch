Except. The Taz notes that at night, even at the end of Shabbos, he says Vayechulu. If one did not recite kiddush all day and remembered during bein hashemashos, then according to the Magen Avraham, if he already fulfilled kiddush in prayer, the remaining cup is only D'Rabbanan and a doubt D'Rabbanan is lenient. Perhaps he should say it without opening and closing blessing. But if he did not pray at all during Shabbos, he certainly recites kiddush even during bein hashemashos, since it is a Torah-level doubt.
