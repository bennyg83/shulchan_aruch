(1) And some say that since among all of them they tasted, etc. — see in Aruch HaShulchan who tends to decide halachah per this opinion, because the first reason is only Rosh's own view, end of his words; and it is not clear, for Tosafos on Yoma 80 wrote in the name of Rabbenu Tam the reason for 'full mouthful' regarding kiddush is settling the mind, and for that reason they agreed there, and similarly Pesachim 107 regarding 'if he tasted' — for a large person the measure changes because each person is settled by his own mouthful, as on Yom Kippur; therefore per this reason certainly they also hold like Rosh [and per the geonim in Tur that only one who sanctifies himself must drink his full mouthful — certainly all the more they do not hold combination of all reclining helps]. What Ritva mentioned in the name of Tosafos is not in our Tosafos — they imply the opposite; and the Mechaber ruled the first view anonymously implying it is primary. Therefore even though one should not protest those who are lenient who have whom to rely on, nevertheless l'chatchila one should be very careful, especially since Magen Avraham wrote that even those who hold combination helps hold it only b'dieved; so too Bach, Shulchan Aruch HaRav, and Chayei Adam did not permit except b'dieved. Know that Bach wrote that b'dieved combination of all reclining helps even if the one who sanctified did not taste at all.
