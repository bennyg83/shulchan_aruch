Seif katan 14, and he must, etc. There is no concern if he mentions Shabbos unnecessarily. Above in siman 188, when one began eating on Shabbos and finished after Shabbos, he mentions Shabbos because perhaps we follow the beginning of the meal. Here too, even if the mention may be unnecessary, it is not an interruption, as at the end of siman 108, where mentioning an event of another day in prayer is not an interruption. The Taz disputes this.
