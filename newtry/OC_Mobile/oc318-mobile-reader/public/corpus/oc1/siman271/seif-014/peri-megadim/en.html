Because the Sages did not distinguish, since when one recites kiddush over bread everyone agrees he must wash first, the same practice applies to wine. Also, nowadays one's hands are usually not clean, and it is close to certain that he touched an unclean place. Nevertheless, because of this reason he should wash without the blessing of netilas yadayim; otherwise it may appear somewhat haughty. See the Taz citing the Bach and the Magen Avraham.
