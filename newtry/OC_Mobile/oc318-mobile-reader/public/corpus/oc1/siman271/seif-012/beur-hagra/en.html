That initially. These are Rema's words. See Responsa Rashba 752, who explains that the statement 'one who washed his hands should not recite Kiddush' is because he appears to belittle Kiddush: had he intended to recite it over wine, he would not have washed both hands, for one who washes his hands for fruit, etc. The statement that sometimes bread is dearer does not appear disrespectful, for perhaps he intended to recite Kiddush over bread; he may therefore recite Kiddush either over bread or wine. Accordingly, nowadays everyone customarily washes before Kiddush, except on Erev Pesach, because Kiddush then cannot be recited over bread, as the first cup is used for Kiddush of the day. The primary ruling, however, follows the Shulchan Aruch, and Taz agrees.
