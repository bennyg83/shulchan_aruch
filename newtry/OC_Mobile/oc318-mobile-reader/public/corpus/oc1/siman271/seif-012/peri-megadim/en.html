A slice. The Taz gives three reasons for covering the bread: so the meal should come in honor of Shabbos, as a remembrance of the mann, and so as not to shame the bread. It may seem that here one covers only the bread, while in seif 4 he covers the entire table, but the wording can imply the table. The Tur also implies that spreading a cloth makes it as though the table is not present. For the remembrance of the mann, a covering beneath the bread also helps, so that the table is set. If one recites kiddush over bread, Eliyah Rabbah writes that he should place his hands on it as one holds a cup, but he should cover it before kiddush and reveal it when he begins kiddush. When saying HaMotzi he certainly reveals and holds it. For the mann reason a brief covering suffices; the Magen Avraham emphasizes the honor of Shabbos.
