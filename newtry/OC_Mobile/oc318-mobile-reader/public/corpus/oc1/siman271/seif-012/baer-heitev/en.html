(1) To change. Taz wrote: it appears to me that even though Rama wrote one should not change, nevertheless one who trembled at the matter and wishes to act per the first view and to wash his hands after kiddush — one should not say to him "all who change place their hands on the lower" — God forbid; rather he may say, I will act per everyone.

(2) On Passover night. Because we interrupt much in the Haggadah.
