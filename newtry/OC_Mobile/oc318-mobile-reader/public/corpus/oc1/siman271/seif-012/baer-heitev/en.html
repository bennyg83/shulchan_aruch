To change. Taz writes: It appears to me that although Rama wrote that one should not change the custom, nevertheless, one who trembles at the matter and wishes to follow the first opinion by washing his hands after Kiddush should not, Heaven forbid, be described by the rule that one who deviates has the lower hand. Rather, he may say, “I will act in accordance with all opinions.” On Pesach night. This is because there is a lengthy interruption during the Haggadah.
