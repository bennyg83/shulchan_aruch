After he recited kiddush, etc. Not beforehand, so that kiddush should not interrupt between netilas yadayim and HaMotzi. However, his household members who do not recite kiddush themselves but fulfill by hearing from the head of the household may wash their hands beforehand.
That bread, etc. Specifically here, where there is also a concern of interruption, since he recites kiddush over wine after washing, as we wrote in seif katan 58. But generally, where he did not wash his hands, he may recite kiddush over wine even if bread is more beloved to him than wine. See below in siman 272, seif 9 in the gloss, and what we wrote there.
It is beloved to him. Meaning that for this reason he washes his hands, because he is hungry and hurries to eat bread.
And some say that initially, etc. They hold that kiddush is not called an interruption, since it is needed for the meal. Therefore he recites kiddush over wine, drinks the cup, and afterward blesses HaMotzi and breaks the bread. Since it is not an interruption, the Rama holds that it is good to conduct oneself this way initially, because when one has no wine and recites kiddush over bread, he necessarily washes before kiddush; therefore it is good always to conduct oneself in one uniform manner. But to mix the cup with hot water after washing before HaMotzi certainly should not be done according to all views, since one must be careful to mix it properly, neither too little nor too much, and this is a diversion of mind.
And one should not change. Several later authorities wrote that it is better initially to recite kiddush over wine before netilas yadayim, like the view of the Mechaber, for in this way one fulfills according to law according to all views; in many places they practiced like them. However, if he already washed his hands before kiddush, then one should act like the Rama, that he nevertheless recites kiddush over wine.
On Pesach night. Because then there is a long interruption in saying the Haggadah before the meal. Derech HaChaim wrote: if one recites kiddush over bread to discharge the listeners as well, the listeners must intend to fulfill also with the blessing of HaMotzi. If they intend to fulfill only kiddush and wish to bless HaMotzi themselves later at the time of eating, they commit a prohibition, for they reverse the order of kiddush. According to this, they must also be careful to wash their hands together with the head of the household when he recites kiddush over bread; otherwise, how can they intend to fulfill with his HaMotzi blessing?
