(12) If two people recite Kiddush together, and their practice is to pause while one says Borei Pri HaGefen and the other answers amen, then the second says Borei Pri HaGefen, the first may not answer amen. That would interrupt between his blessing and tasting, and they may not arrange matters this way [Panim Meiros II 5; Shaarei Teshuvah]. If he mistakenly answered, he does not repeat the blessing, as above in siman 167, paragraph 61.
