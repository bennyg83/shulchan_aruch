Seif katan 12, he should eat, etc. The responsum of Maharam disagrees with the ruling here that he should eat first. Ritzba ruled that if one forgot Havdalah and remembered after HaMotzi, he should recite Havdalah and then repeat HaMotzi, for otherwise Havdalah would be an interruption.
And it is not comparable to feeding, etc. Saying to feed one's animals is not an interruption because it is a true need of the meal, since one may not eat before feeding his animals. Havdalah merely removes a prohibition and is not itself a meal need, unless the prohibition is according to the Torah.
