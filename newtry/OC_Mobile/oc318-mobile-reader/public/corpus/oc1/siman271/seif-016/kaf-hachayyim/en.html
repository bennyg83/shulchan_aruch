(16) There: What was taught, that daytime honor takes precedence, etc. Maharshal, Yam Shel Shlomo, Gittin 4:51, protests that people neglect this and instead add delicacies Friday night. He personally refrained from fish Friday night, since it was important to him, so no number of nighttime delicacies equaled a morning meal containing fish. Machazik Berachah and Shaarei Teshuvah cite him. Shaarei Teshuvah notes that Maharshal similarly avoided beloved fish on Rosh Hashanah; one who favors another food should reserve it for the morning meal. According to the Arizal, fish must be eaten Friday night; one should instead honor the morning meal with another favorite food or increase a dish absent at night.
