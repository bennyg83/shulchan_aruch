A cheekful. See the Taz and siman 190. There it is stated that a cup of blessing requires a revi'is because of the doubt of a final blessing. A kiddush cup is different, because Birkas HaMazon exempts it, as in siman 272 and siman 174. Therefore most of a revi'is suffices. It also implies that even if he recites kiddush before washing, he need not recite a final blessing over the kiddush cup, because kiddush must be in the place of a meal and is considered a need of the meal.
