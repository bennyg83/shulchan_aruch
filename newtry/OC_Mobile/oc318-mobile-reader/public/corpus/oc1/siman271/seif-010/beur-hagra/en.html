Seif 10, one recites kiddush over. This follows the Yerushalmi regarding Rabbi Yonah, who tasted and adjusted, which the Rosh brings in the eighth chapter of Berachos. See also Rashi to Pesachim 106a, Rashbam there, and Rashi to Berachos 53a.
And it requires. The Rambam explains that the statement that a cup of blessing requires ten, etc., refers to all matters that require a cup, for according to him Birkas HaMazon does not require a cup. This is as he writes in Hilchos Berachos from the conclusion of Pesachim 117b, and it also appears in the Rif there. Further support is from Pesachim 105b and Berachos there, and from the Yerushalmi in the seventh chapter of Berachos, where the Sages agree to Rabbi Eliezer regarding a cup of blessing; so the Sages practiced with the cup of kiddush.
And he says Vayechulu. Pesachim 106a, in Tosafot s.v. zachreihu, and Rashbam there, 106b, s.v. dechaviva.

For they begin Yom Hashishi. The reason is that it refers to tosefes Shabbos, as written in Bereishis Rabbah, parshas Bereishis, section 9. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 22. In the Rambam, Hilchos Shabbos 29:2, the three words 'for it is the day' are absent, and this is primary because they are superfluous. But 'for You chose us' should not be omitted, and so it appears in the Rambam there. See also the Rosh, fourth chapter of Rosh Hashanah, siman 3, regarding additions.
