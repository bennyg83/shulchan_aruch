And he says Vayechulu. If he forgot to say it, he says it during the meal over the cup.
For they begin Yom Hashishi. This is also to complete the seventy-two words of kiddush. According to this, one should not say the additions 'for You chose us' and 'You sanctified us from all nations.' Some begin from Vayechulu and count zachor and shamor as two. Tikkunei Shabbos writes that it is seventy words and therefore not to say 'for it is the day.' It seems that one should not change any custom, because each custom has a reason and foundation.
He should place his eyes on the candles. Twice the word ner equals five hundred, and this is a remedy for a large stride. Although one must look at the cup, one can look at both the cup and the candle. In the Tur, siman 269, it implies that one places wine from the cup on the eyes as a remedy. Rashi and the Ran in the fifteenth chapter of Shabbos write that drinking the cup is the remedy.
