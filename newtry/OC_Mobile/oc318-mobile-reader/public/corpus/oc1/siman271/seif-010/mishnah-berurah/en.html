(42) Over a full cup - For the cup of kiddush and havdalah is included among other cups of berachah, which must be full, as above in siman 183 seif 2 in the gloss. If it is not full and he has no other cup into which to pour it, he may make kiddush over it, since there is the measure of a revi'it in the wine inside it, as is proven just below in seif 11.
(43) That it should not be blemished - That is, if someone drank from it, he blemished it and one should not make kiddush over it. See above in siman 182, where the details of the law of a blemished cup are explained; and see there in seif 7, that in pressing circumstances, where he has no other cup and no way to repair the cup, one recites over a blemished cup. See Eshel Avraham seif katan 30, that for the night kiddush perhaps it is better that he make kiddush over bread.
(44) Everything that requires, etc. - That is, the cup should be whole, and it should be well rinsed inside and outside, and all the other matters mentioned above in siman 183 regarding the cup of birkat hamazon.
(45) And he says Vayechulu standing - For it is testimony about the creation of heaven and earth, and testimony requires standing. Although he says it in prayer, he repeats it in order to discharge his sons and household [Tur]. In Rokeach he brought in the name of a Midrash that one must say Vayechulu three times: once in prayer, once after prayer, and once over the cup. The Acharonim wrote that if he forgot to say Vayechulu at the time of kiddush, he says it in the middle of the meal over the cup.
(46) And it is better to sit - For in this way it is more called kiddush in the place of a meal, since he sits in the place of his meal at the time of kiddush. So too the Biur HaGra agreed, and for another reason: since one person exempts another, we need keviut, that they establish themselves together; and sitting is called keviut, as above in siman 167. According to his words, the listeners also must sit. It is good to be careful about this at the outset. One should say that his view too is only at the outset. At any rate, one must be careful that the listeners establish themselves together at the time of kiddush in order to fulfill the obligation, and that they not be scattered and separated, one going here and one going there, for this is not called keviut at all.
(47) Even at the time, etc. - Since they already said it in synagogue while standing, we are no longer particular about this, and they say it seated like the rest of kiddush.
(48) He should place his eyes on the candle - This is a healing remedy for eyes that were weakened by taking large steps. However, we are not so exacting about this [Eliyah Rabbah].
(49) And see above siman 183 - Meaning that there it is explained that he should place his eyes on the cup of berachah so that he not divert his attention.
