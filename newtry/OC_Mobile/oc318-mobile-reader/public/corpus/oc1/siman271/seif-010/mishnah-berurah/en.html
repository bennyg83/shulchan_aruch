Over a full cup. The cup of kiddush and havdalah is included among other cups of blessing, which must be full, as above in siman 183, seif 2 in the gloss. If it is not full and he has no other cup into which to pour, he may recite kiddush over it, since the wine in it contains the measure of a reviis, as is proven below in seif 11.
That it not be defective. Meaning that if someone drank from it, he made it defective and one should not recite kiddush over it. See above in siman 182, where the detailed laws of a defective cup are explained. See there, seif 7, that in pressing circumstances, when he has no other cup and no way to repair the cup, one blesses over a defective cup. See Eshel Avraham, seif katan 30, that for nighttime kiddush it is perhaps better to recite kiddush over bread.
All that requires, etc. Meaning that the cup should be whole, thoroughly rinsed inside and outside, and all the other matters mentioned above in siman 183 regarding the cup of Birkas HaMazon.
And he says Vayechulu standing. For it is testimony about the creation of heaven and earth, and testimony requires standing. Although he says it in prayer, he repeats it in order to discharge his sons and household. The Rokeach brought in the name of a Midrash that one must say Vayechulu three times: once in prayer, once after prayer, and once over the cup. The later authorities wrote that if he forgot to say Vayechulu at kiddush, he should say it during the meal over a cup.
And it is better to sit. In this way it is more considered kiddush in the place of a meal, since he sits in the place of his meal at the time of kiddush. The Gra agreed with this, and for another reason: since one discharges another, a fixed setting is required, that they establish themselves together, and sitting is called a fixed setting, as above in siman 167. According to his words, the listeners must also sit. It is good to be careful about this initially, though one may say that his view too is only initially. In any case, one must be careful that the listeners establish themselves together at kiddush in order to fulfill, and that they not be scattered and separated, going one here and one there, for that is not called a fixed setting at all.
Even at the time, etc. Since they already said it standing in the synagogue, they are no longer particular about this and say it seated like the rest of kiddush.
He should place his eyes on the candle. It is a remedy for eyes weakened by taking large steps. However, people are not so careful about this.
See above, siman 183. Meaning that it is explained there that he should place his eyes on the cup of blessing so that he not divert his mind.
