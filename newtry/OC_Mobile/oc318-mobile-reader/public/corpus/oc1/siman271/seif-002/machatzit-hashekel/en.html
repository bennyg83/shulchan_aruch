Seif katan 2, and they discharge, etc. The question is why this differs from Megillah, where women are also obligated and nevertheless do not discharge men.
For a minor, etc. A minor's obligation is only D'Rabbanan, whereas the woman's obligation in kiddush is according to the Torah.
Perhaps he has not brought two hairs and is still a minor until twenty if he has signs of a saris; if not, he remains a minor until most of his years have passed, as explained in Choshen Mishpat, beginning of siman 35.
For in Torah-level matters, etc. Although regarding prayer the Rama relies on the presumption that a thirteen-year-old brought two hairs, that is because prayer is D'Rabbanan. Here kiddush is according to the Torah. If the woman already prayed, then according to the Torah she has fulfilled kiddush, and there is room to say that a minor can discharge her, though the Tosefes Shabbos objects because intent to fulfill is required.
See what I wrote at the beginning of siman 193, that it is good for the woman to say the words with the one reciting kiddush, word by word, because according to many poskim one does not fulfill by listening unless he understands the holy tongue, and because it is difficult to concentrate on every word. If she says it together with him, she may fulfill even when the one reciting is a minor, as we rule regarding Hallel.
