And they discharge men, etc. Although in siman 689 the Shulchan Aruch ruled like Behag that women do not discharge men in reading the Megillah, even though they are obligated in reading the Megillah, that is not comparable to here. Regarding Megillah there are opinions that women should not bless over reading the Megillah but over hearing the Megillah, as the Beit Yosef wrote there; therefore it is certainly not proper initially for them to discharge men. Here, however, all agree that there is no distinction at all between men and women, and therefore they properly discharge them. Maharshal and my teacher, the Bach, ruled here too that they do not discharge, as with Megillah, but this is not compelling at all.
