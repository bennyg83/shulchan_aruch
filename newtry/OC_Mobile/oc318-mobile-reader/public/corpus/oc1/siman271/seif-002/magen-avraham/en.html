And they discharge the men. The Bach asked why this differs from Megillah, where women do not discharge men. He missed Tosafot in Sukkah, who write that where many people are involved it is degrading for them to be discharged by a woman. See siman 186. It is simple that a minor cannot discharge a woman. Even a thirteen-year-old boy is a concern, lest he has not brought two hairs; in Torah-level matters we do not rely on that presumption until his beard fills out. Therefore the woman should recite kiddush for herself.
