To eat. See the Taz and Chiddushei HaRashba to Berachos 27b. Even if tosefes Shabbos itself is not according to the Torah, one fulfills kiddush from plag haminchah, since the mitzvah is to sanctify Shabbos at its entrance or near it. Before plag haminchah, however, one certainly does not fulfill kiddush over a cup, and even if he prayed Maariv later he must recite kiddush again over a cup. Whether kiddush over wine is according to the Torah is discussed by the Magen Avraham and Tosafot. If one prayed a weekday Amidah and merely mentioned Shabbos without the blessing 'Who sanctifies Shabbos,' perhaps he did not fulfill even the Torah-level kiddush. This affects whether a minor can discharge an adult. The Levush seems to imply that kiddush over wine is according to the Torah, perhaps following Rabbeinu Tam, though from the discussion of beer as the local beverage this requires study; in Nazir it appears that wine is D'Rabbanan.
And one should. Since women generally do not pray Maariv, one might ask how men who already prayed, and are now obligated only D'Rabbanan, can discharge women who remain obligated according to the Torah. This is mistaken, because even one who already fulfilled his obligation can discharge another through arvus, and this applies to women as explained. A minor could discharge an adult who already prayed Maariv but not a woman who has not, and according to the view that kiddush over wine is according to the Torah he cannot discharge the adult either. A strong asmachta may also have the force of a Torah-level matter for this purpose.
