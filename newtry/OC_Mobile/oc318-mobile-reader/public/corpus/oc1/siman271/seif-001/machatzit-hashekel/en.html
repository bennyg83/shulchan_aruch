Seif katan 1, to eat immediately means to recite kiddush, etc. The Tur's reason is derived from remembering Shabbos at its entrance, which teaches primarily that kiddush should be said immediately. Since kiddush must be in the place of a meal, eating must also follow immediately. He also discusses the statement in Tikkunei Shabbos about reciting kiddush before night because of the planetary hour, and notes that this applies only when the day and night are equal. From the Yerushalmi and Rashi it appears that people would eat the Shabbos night meal after nightfall.
This is also implied by Tosafot according to Rabbeinu Tam. If one without wine would not recite kiddush at all, that could not uproot a positive commandment according to the Torah, since wine is not required according to the Torah. Rather, according to the Torah he already fulfilled kiddush in prayer.
The Gemara in Berachos teaches that the Men of the Great Assembly originally enacted Havdalah in prayer, later on the cup, and again in prayer when people became poor. The Gemara compares Havdalah to kiddush, saying that even though one sanctifies in prayer he sanctifies over a cup. The Magen Avraham infers that prayer is called kiddush, meaning that according to the Torah one fulfills kiddush through prayer; otherwise it would not be called kiddush.
