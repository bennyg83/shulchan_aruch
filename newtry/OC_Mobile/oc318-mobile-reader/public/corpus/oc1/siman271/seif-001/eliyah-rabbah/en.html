He should hurry, etc. The Magen Avraham holds that one may wait to recite kiddush, since he already mentioned Shabbos at its entrance in the synagogue. Levush, and he should recite kiddush, etc. The Derashos Maharash writes that two householders should not recite kiddush at the same time, because two voices are not heard distinctly. This means that the members of the household would not fulfill their obligation. This is not like Megillah and Hallel, where they concentrate because of publicizing the miracle. Accordingly, even some of those reclining should not say it aloud together with the householder.
