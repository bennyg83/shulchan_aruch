He should hurry, etc. The Magen Avraham holds that one may wait to recite kiddush, since he already mentioned Shabbos at its entrance in the synagogue. He writes that prayer is only D'Rabbanan, while kiddush over wine and in the place of the meal has its own enactment. See Semag, Sefer HaChinuch, Bach siman 270, Shiltei Gibborim in Arvei Pesachim, and the end of siman 293 in the Tur and Beit Yosef, and siman 267, seif katan 2.
