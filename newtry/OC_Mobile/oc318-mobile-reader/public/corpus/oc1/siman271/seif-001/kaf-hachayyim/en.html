(1) [Se'if 1] Upon arriving home, he should hurry to eat immediately. A baraisa says, 'Remember the Shabbos day to sanctify it'; remember it over wine upon its entrance [Pesachim 106a; Tur]. Beis Yosef explains that immediately upon entering he should sanctify it over wine. Rosh writes in Tefillas HaShachar and the beginning of Arvei Pesachim that on Shabbos and Yom Tov one may add from weekday to sacred time, eat before dark, and count that meal as the Shabbos meal through this Kiddush. See above, siman 267, paragraph 6.
