To eat immediately. That is, to sanctify so he remembers Shabbat upon his entry. He wrote in Tikunei Shabbat that he should sanctify before night because at the beginning of Shabbat night the mazal of Mars and Samael rule over it, and at the end of the sixth day the mazal of Zedek and its angel Tzadkiel — and they are two angels, one evil and one good; therefore he should sanctify in righteousness or wait until Mars's dominion passes and then sanctify. And if he does not wish to eat he may wait, for he already remembered it upon his entry in the synagogue — see Magen Avraham. (And in Sefer HaChinuch parshat Yitro it implies they were accustomed not to sanctify in the home until dark.) Rambam wrote perek 29 of Kiddush HaYom: kiddush is d'oraisa without wine and the rabbis enacted it over wine, and so Tosafos on Sukkah 38a; and it appears to me that d'oraisa the kiddush said in prayer suffices and kiddush in the place of a meal is rabbinic. And know that kiddush on Yom Tov is d'rabbanan, nevertheless it has all the laws of Shabbat kiddush. And if he has only one cup for Shabbat he uses it first. Magen Avraham.
