He should hurry to eat, etc. This means to recite kiddush, so that he remembers Shabbos at the very beginning of its entrance, for the earlier we advance it the better. Once he has recited kiddush he must eat immediately, as explained in siman 273, seif 3 in the gloss. If he has no appetite, he may wait to recite kiddush until he becomes hungry, for he has already remembered Shabbos in his prayer in the synagogue, and according to the Rambam he thereby fulfills the positive commandment according to the Torah, as below, from Magen Avraham in the name of Rema MiPano. Nevertheless, where this would affect shalom bayis, or where he has servants or other guests in his home, especially a poor guest, he should not delay in any case, for since they depend on him, he may not hold them back because he wishes to fulfill the mitzvah in the preferred manner.
Immediately. Nevertheless, it is proper to read Krias Shema first if he is uncertain whether he read it in its time in the synagogue; see above, siman 267, Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 6. The Rambam writes in chapter 29 of Hilchos Shabbos that it is a positive commandment according to the Torah to sanctify the Shabbos day with words, as it says, Remember the Shabbos day to sanctify it, meaning, remember it with remembrance and sanctification of Shabbos. From the words of the Sages, this remembrance is to be over a cup of wine. Tosafot likewise wrote that over wine is only D'Rabbanan. The Magen Avraham wrote that according to this, according to the Torah, the kiddush said in prayer suffices, for the verse says Remember the Shabbos day, and he has remembered it. Kiddush in the place of a meal is D'Rabbanan, as the Beit Yosef wrote in siman 269, and the Magen Avraham brought proof from the Gemara that one fulfills according to the Torah through prayer. Therefore, if he is uncertain whether he recited kiddush, he need not return and recite kiddush, for a D'Rabbanan doubt is ruled leniently. According to this, a minor who reached the age of training could discharge even an adult in kiddush if the adult already prayed. However, there is much room to question this, for we rule above in siman 60, seif 4, that mitzvos require intent to fulfill them, and presumably people do not intend to fulfill the positive commandment of remembering in prayer when they have wine or bread and can later recite kiddush over them with the blessing according to law. It is better to fulfill the positive commandment according to the Torah then, rather than now without a cup and not in the place of a meal. There are also several other reasons to question this, as I explained in Biur Halacha. Therefore one should avoid fulfilling kiddush through a minor, and even if the listener is careful to say it with him word by word, it is also proper to avoid this unless bread or wine is also lying before him at the time of kiddush, as below at the end of seif katan 3. Know that the kiddush of Yom Tov is D'Rabbanan, yet it has all the laws of Shabbos kiddush. If Yom Tov falls on Friday and he has only one cup, he leaves it for Shabbos, which is according to the Torah, and on Yom Tov he recites kiddush over bread.
