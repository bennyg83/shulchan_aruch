Magen Avraham seif katan 1 In order that he remember, etc. Meaning, with respect to eating there was no need to hurry; on the contrary, it is preferable that he eat at night, as explained in siman 268, see there in Magen Avraham seif katan 1. Rather, he should hurry for the sake of kiddush, so that he remembers Shabbat at its entrance, since he has already prayed the Shabbat prayer; and since he made kiddush, he must eat immediately, as explained in 273 seif 3.
