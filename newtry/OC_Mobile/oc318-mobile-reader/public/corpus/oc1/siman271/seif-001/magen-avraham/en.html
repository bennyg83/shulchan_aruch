To eat immediately. Meaning, to recite kiddush, so that he remembers Shabbos at its entrance. See siman 273, seif 3. Tikkunei Shabbos writes to recite kiddush before night, since the beginning of Shabbos night is under Mars and the end of Friday is under Jupiter. Maharil also wrote this. But if one has no appetite, he may wait, since he already mentioned Shabbos in the synagogue. Kiddush of the day according to the Torah does not require wine; the Sages enacted it over wine. It appears that according to the Torah the kiddush said in prayer suffices, for the verse says to remember Shabbos, and he has remembered it. Kiddush in the place of the meal is D'Rabbanan. Kiddush of Yom Tov is D'Rabbanan, yet it has all the laws of Shabbos kiddush; if he has only one cup, Shabbos takes precedence.
