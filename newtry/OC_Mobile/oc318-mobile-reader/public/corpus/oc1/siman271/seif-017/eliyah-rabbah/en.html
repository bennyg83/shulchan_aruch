[17] [Levush] a remembrance of manna, etc. — that it would not descend on Shabbatot and Yamim Tovim (Tosafos Pesachim 100). And a practical difference emerges: some say for the first reason, immediately when he blesses Borei peri hagafen he may uncover it; and for the second reason only after kiddush — so it appears to me. In Perishah I found this is his wording: one should not remove the cloth until after all those reclining have drunk and wish to bless on the bread — for then he takes out the bread from under the cloth and afterward removes it, for it is similar to dew that was on the manna that they took it out from under the dew — until here. And study is required: for in the verse in Parashat Beshalach it is written "and the layer of dew ascended," etc. — Rashi explained: when the sun shines the dew on the manna ascends and they saw, etc. — behold the dew departed first; and so in truth I did not see to be strict in this that they take out the bread before removing the cloth; and it is possible he holds that specifically on the first day before they knew it was manna it remained there that the dew ascends; but afterward once they knew they would take it out before sunrise while dew was still on it — and this is what is written "and they gathered it in the morning, in the morning" twice — meaning at dawn. And behold a practical difference emerges: for Levush wrote that for the second reason even if he makes kiddush on bread he must cover; and Taz and Olas Tamid wrote that such is the custom — study is required for me, for in Sefer Tzidah LaDerech he wrote one need not spread a cloth even though he also wrote the second reason; and further one must place his hands on the bread at the time of kiddush as he took the cup in his hand, as below siman 483; and it appears before kiddush he should cover and when he begins kiddush he should uncover and place his hands on it.
