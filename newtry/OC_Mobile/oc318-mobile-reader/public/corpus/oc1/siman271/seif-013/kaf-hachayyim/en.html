(13) [Se'if 3] If he cannot afford wine, etc. If one lacks wine for Kiddush during twilight on Friday night and a nearby neighbor wishes to give him wine, with a karmelis separating them, two people may transfer it; one lifting and the other placing. This is for the mitzvah of Kiddush over wine, at least rabbinically; according to major authorities the Sages did not prohibit divided transfer in a karmelis. It is also twilight, and Taz, siman 349, says the combination of twilight and karmelis (two rabbinic factors) is treated leniently [Os Hi LeOlam, maareches heh 71; Pischei HaDevir].
