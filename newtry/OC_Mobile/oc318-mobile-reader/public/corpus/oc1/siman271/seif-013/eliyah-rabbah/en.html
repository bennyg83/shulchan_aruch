Levush, before he ate, etc. This means before he ate the slice of HaMotzi. The Taz explains that if he remembered after eating, he should recite kiddush and need not repeat HaMotzi. It appears to me that before eating he recites kiddush over bread so that he not eat before kiddush; after he ate, he should recite kiddush over wine. Yet according to Shiltei Gibborim, once he washed his hands kiddush became fixed over bread, and this may apply all the more after eating bread, though perhaps having eaten makes it worse. This also relates to seif 12, that initially one should wash, since kiddush must be in the place of the meal.
