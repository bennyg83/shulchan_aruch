Seif katan 13, he finished, etc. The Rif rules that if they finished the meal before Shabbos began and while they were washing mayim acharonim Shabbos began, they first recite Birkas HaMazon on one cup and afterward kiddush on a second cup, because two sanctifications are not placed on one cup. The Rosh objects that since kiddush must be in the place of the meal, they should rather spread a cloth, recite kiddush and HaMotzi, and continue. The Ran answers for the Rif that the weekday meal can still count as the meal place for kiddush. The remaining issue depends on the laws of removal from the meal in siman 179. According to the Rif, once they washed, Birkas HaMazon is required first; according to the Rosh as understood by the Magen Avraham, even washing mayim acharonim need not force Birkas HaMazon before continuing.
But if he did not wash, etc. Meaning that he also did not say 'let us bless.' In that case even the Rif agrees that spreading a cloth is enough. If they did say 'let us bless,' there is room to doubt whether the Rif treats this like washing and requires Birkas HaMazon first, or whether he agrees that spreading a cloth suffices.
