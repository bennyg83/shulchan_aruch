And he says. The Taz discusses why Shehecheyanu is not said again within thirty days, as the Kenesses HaGedolah cites from the Rada. For two species one says it, as with sukkah and Shulchan Aruch, and for one type not dependent on days, such as two sons from two wives or new utensils, one says it even within thirty days. In siman 268 we wrote that when ten are present, Vayechulu is said standing; otherwise not.
