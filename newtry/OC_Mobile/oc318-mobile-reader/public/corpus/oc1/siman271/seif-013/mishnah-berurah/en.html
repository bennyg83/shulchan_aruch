A cheekful. For we require a significant amount, through which his mind is settled.
That is, etc. Meaning, we do not require his mouth to be literally full from both sides.
And it is, etc. This measure suffices even if the cup was large and held several reviiyos.
Most of a reviis. For an average person, his measure contains this amount, but for an especially large person we measure according to his own cheekful in proportion to his size. Nevertheless, he need not drink more than a reviis. Know that the measure of a reviis is almost the volume of two shells of an average chicken egg, which is one and a half eggs with the shell, for a log is six eggs, as stated in the Gemara, and a quarter of a log is automatically an egg and a half. Some are very stringent regarding the measurements and proved that eggs in our times have diminished to half of what they were in the days of the Gemara; therefore the measure of a reviis is double. This also affects the measure of a kezayis of matzah, which is about half an egg, as explained in siman 486. See Biur Halacha, where we wrote that it is proper to be concerned for their view regarding nighttime kiddush. In any case, initially one should see that the cup holds about two eggs. As for drinking, even if the cup is large, it suffices that he drink his own cheekful. Know further that the measure of a cheekful must be drunk without a long interruption between, meaning that from the beginning of the first drink until the end of the final drink he should not delay more than the time for drinking a reviis. At least, he should not interrupt for the time of eating a pras. If he interrupted for the time of eating a pras, even after the fact he did not fulfill, and one must be careful about this when making kiddush in the morning over liquor, for then people commonly stumble in this.
