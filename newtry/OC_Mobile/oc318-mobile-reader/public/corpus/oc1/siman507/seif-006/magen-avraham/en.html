It is permitted to bake. Orchos Chaim wrote in the name of the Raah that specifically when he intends to improve the bread, but otherwise it is forbidden; thus far. And it does not appear so from the words of the poskim (Beis Yosef). And it appears to me to be stringent even though in siman 503 he wrote that even if he does for weekday need it is permitted to cook in one pot: that case is different, for it is one trouble; but here each loaf needs trouble of its own; and see what I wrote there.
In our ovens. And Maharshal wrote: from the fact that the Rif, Rambam, Rosh, and Tur stated it simply, we learn that even in our ovens it is permitted; and specifically when he needs to eat, but if he does not need to eat, only that he pretends in order to eat a little of it, it is forbidden; see siman 503; end quote.
