(s.k. 6) And the ash, etc.; but, etc., the dust:
That is to say: the clay plaster.
Also forbidden. That is to say: as long as it is hot, fit for roasting an egg in it, it stands for its first work and it is permitted to move it; which is not so when it cools, for it is nolad. And as he wrote in siman 310, that there is no muktzeh for half a Shabbos; namely something that was fit at twilight and afterward became forbidden on Shabbos, and when the issur goes it returns to its first permission. And likewise here: at twilight, and also while it was hot it was permitted to move; afterward when it cooled it became forbidden; and when it was again heated it returns to its first permission. And see the end of siman 509 regarding moving ash.
