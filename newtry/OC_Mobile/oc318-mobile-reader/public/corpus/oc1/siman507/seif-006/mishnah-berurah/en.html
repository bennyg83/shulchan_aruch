{32} A full oven of bread even though he does not need, etc. - There are among the poskim who hold that specifically when he intends that thereby the bread be improved; but when he intends for weekday need it is forbidden. And even though above in siman 503 regarding filling a pot with meat, even if he does for weekday need it is permitted because thereby the meat needed for Yom Tov is improved: that case is different, for it is one trouble; but here each loaf needs trouble of its own.
{33} And they would stick the bread to their walls - And in places where it is not their way to stick, large and small are equal for prohibition.
{34} But in our ovens, etc. - The reason is that in each and every loaf there is trouble of kneading and baking; and therefore it is not permitted to bake more whenever the bread is not baked nicer by filling the oven. And even through a non-Jew it is forbidden.
{35} One should not bake more, etc. - And there are poskim who permit even in our oven; and specifically when he truly needs at least one loaf for Yom Tov; but if he does not need, only that he pretends to eat a little of it in order to permit himself the baking, it is forbidden. And in a time of pressure, for example one who did not leave eruvei tavshilin and needs bread for Shabbos, one may rely on this view to bake before the morning meal if he needs at least one loaf on that very day itself; but if he does not need, only pretends to eat a little in order to permit himself the baking, it is forbidden even if he does this for the sake of Shabbos. And bakers who intend to do their work through a non-Jew on Yom Tov so that the bread be ready for them immediately at the end of Yom Tov, and pretend to eat one loaf of them on that day, do a prohibition according to everyone.
