There, seif 6. "It is permitted to bake an oven full of bread even though he needs only one loaf, etc." NB: See my Torah novellae to Parashat Emor of this year, 5616, where I wrote to ask: from where do we know this? Perhaps only if he needs most of the bread is it permitted to bake the minority for the sake of the majority. But if he needs only the minority, from where do we know that he may trouble himself with the majority for nothing because of the minority? Especially since there is a tanna who forbids entirely even if he needs the majority, from where does the lenient view know to broaden the dispute? Perhaps even he speaks only where he needs the majority. See there, where I left this requiring study; see there and examine very carefully.
