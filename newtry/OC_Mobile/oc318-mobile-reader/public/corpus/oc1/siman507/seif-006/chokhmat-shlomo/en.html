(There, se'if 6) It is permitted to bake a full oven of bread even though he needs only one loaf, etc. Marginal note: see in my novellae on the Torah, Parashas Emor of this year, year 616, what I wrote to ask: from where do we know this; and perhaps only if he needs most of the bread is he permitted to bake the minority for the sake of the majority, but if he needs only a minority, from where do we know that he may trouble himself with the majority for nothing for the sake of the minority? And especially since there is a Tanna who forbids entirely, even if he needs the majority; if so, even the lenient view, from where do we know to multiply dispute, and perhaps he too says only if he needs the majority? See there, where I left it as requiring further study; see there and examine carefully.
