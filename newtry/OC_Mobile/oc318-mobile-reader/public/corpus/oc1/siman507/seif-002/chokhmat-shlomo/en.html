Se'if 2. Even though wood that fell from the palm tree on Yom Tov or on Shabbos, etc. Marginal note: regarding what the words of Issur v'Heter, klal 24, se'if 10 are somewhat difficult, see what I wrote to explain them properly in my work on Orach Chaim, year 616, in a responsum to the holy community of Nikolsburg, folio 120; see there and examine carefully. And the Magen Avraham concluded that muktzeh is permitted for benefit. And a certain scholar asked me, according to those who hold that muktzeh is forbidden for benefit, from Chullin folio 14; see what I wrote in the omissions to Nedarim, siman 228, to the holy community of Rawa; acquire its place there in passing, see there with Hashem's help what I wrote in a responsum. And I concluded that for the joy of Yom Tov it is permitted for benefit according to everyone, for it is no worse than moving; which is not so regarding eating, which is forbidden even on Yom Tov; see there and examine carefully.
