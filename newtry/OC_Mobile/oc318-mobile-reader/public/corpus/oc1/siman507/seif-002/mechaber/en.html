Even though wood that fell from a palm tree on Yom Tov or on the Shabbat preceding it is forbidden to kindle, if the wood fell on Yom Tov into the oven, one may add prepared wood upon it and nullify it, provided that one does not touch it until it is nullified in the majority. But if the wood fell into the oven on Shabbat, it is forbidden to kindle it on the Yom Tov that follows, even through nullification in the majority. {Rama: [so that the forbidden wood should not be recognizable] (Ran, first chapter of Beitzah)}
