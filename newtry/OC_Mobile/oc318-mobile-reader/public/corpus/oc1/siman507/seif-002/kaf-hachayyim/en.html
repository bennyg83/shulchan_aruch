2) There. One may bake in a large oven, etc. This applies specifically when one has much food and needs to bake in the large oven. Olas Shabbos, subsection 1. This follows the aforementioned words of Rashi. It implies that if one has only a small quantity of food that can be baked in a small oven, he may not bake it in a large oven, because firing and sweeping the entire oven constitutes unnecessary exertion. If, however, he has only a large oven, then even though he has only a small quantity of food, he may bake in it. No concern is given to the exertion because no other course is possible, and the Sages permitted many melachos for the joy of Yom Tov. Shulchan Gavoah, subsection 1.
