[2] Into the oven, etc. It implies that he stated "inside the oven" because wood is commonly there (Magen Avraham); but Yam Shel Shlomo siman 6 permits even if there is no wood there, unlike the Ran. And as for his stating "inside the oven," it appears to me that it is for a stringency: that even inside the oven it is forbidden until he adds more; and so it is implied in Rabbeinu Yeruham, folio 34. But in Responsa of the Rashba, siman 336, he ruled like the Ran and Magen Avraham; and in my book on Yoreh De'ah, siman 59, se'if 6, it will be further explained.
