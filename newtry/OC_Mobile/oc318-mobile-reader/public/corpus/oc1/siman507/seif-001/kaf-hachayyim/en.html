1) [Se'if 1] In a large oven, etc. When one has much food and an ordinary oven is insufficient, no concern is given to the exertion. Rashi, Beitzah 34a. Ran there writes likewise. This teaches that when one has much food and wishes to bake all of it in the large oven called a purni, although firing it and sweeping out the coals with brooms before placing the bread in it entail great exertion, no concern is given to that exertion. It is certainly forbidden, however, to bake more than he needs. Rashba's statement below, se'if 6, that a woman may fill the entire oven with bread although she needs only one loaf, applies only to small ovens, not to a large oven. Bach.
