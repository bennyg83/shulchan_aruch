דין אפייה ביום טוב. ובו ז סעיפים:
Laws of baking on Yom Tov. Containing 7 pargraphs: One may bake in a forni which is our large ovens whose openings are on their side provided it is not new, for we' are concerned that' it might crack and the bread will be ruined and prevent the joy of Yom Tov. And one may heat water in an antiki .[[meaning: a large pot]]:
