The kind. See Taz; see Beis Yosef, the Ran; but the Maggid Mishneh explained that it is a large pot with much water; and Kol Bo explained that we are not concerned about forging with copper. And Darkei Moshe brought only the Maggid Mishneh and Kol Bo; but the Levush understood in his teacher's glosses like the Ran, and Antichi has two bottoms, etc. For as to much water, it was explained in siman 503 that it is permitted for a baker to fill, etc. And Beer HaGolah note 3 cited Maggid Mishneh chapter 2, etc. (it should say chapter 3, in law 11). And in Olas Tamid it is implied that the Maggid Mishneh and the Ran are equal, and this requires further study. And regarding forging, see siman 318 in Magen Avraham 17, and siman 510 letter 7, and siman 502 in Magen Avraham 14; and it is unlike forging metal, which is lighter when unintended and permitted.
