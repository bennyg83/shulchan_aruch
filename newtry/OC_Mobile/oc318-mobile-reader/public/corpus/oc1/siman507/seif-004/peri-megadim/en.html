See Taz: even according to the view that muktzeh is permitted on Yom Tov, this is forbidden, for it is like fixing a vessel, unless it was prepared from before Yom Tov; and with preparation alone it is permitted. See Beis Yosef regarding a wood-enclosure, etc. And regarding a door-bolt one can say that it requires an act of fixing; see siman 313, se'if 1; and dry wood is also forbidden. And that is what he wrote regarding a wood-enclosure: ordinarily it is dry; see Yam Shel Shlomo on this.
