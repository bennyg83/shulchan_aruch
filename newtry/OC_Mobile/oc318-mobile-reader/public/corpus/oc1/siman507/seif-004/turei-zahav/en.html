(א) Oven into which fell, etc. This seif is Tur's language including Beit Yosef explanation; in my humble opinion explanation another way as in s.k. 4. We learned in mishnah: one does not break pottery, cut paper to roast salted fish, scrape oven and stove but may press; gemara: why? because fixing vessel; Rashi: this reason applies also to not scraping; follows Rabbis who hold food-preparation forbidden; further gemara: do not scrape oven — Rabbi Chiya bar Yosef taught: if impossible to bake unless scraped — permitted; Rashi: like Rabbi Yehuda who permits food-preparation when impossible from Yom Tov need; Ra'ash: this scraping when clay fell in oven from plaster and oven clay — not much scraping needed only bread look nice not touch bread and roast and scorch — nevertheless permitted only plain moving, end quote; Tur wrote: oven into which oven clay fell — if possible bake and roast without scraping forbidden to scrape; permitted to lay down fire and ash so separate and bread not touch; but if must scrape even if possible bake and roast without scraping only to beautify bread and roasting so clay not touch and scorch — permitted, end quote; Beit Yosef explained Tur and Ra'ash dispute Rashi who explains scraping prohibition due to fixing vessel — for Ra'ash and Tur it is needless moving; in my humble opinion Rashi also holds Ra'ash reason, did not state fixing vessel reason in mishnah except for Rabbis forbidding food-preparation all cases; Rabbi Yehuda permits preparations when impossible from Yom Tov need — likewise impossible fell on Yom Tov or fell on Yom Tov and did not know — no fixing vessel only moving — braita of Rabbi Chiya bar Yosef; Rashi compelled: mishnah stated oven scraping with others — certainly reason fixing vessel; mishnah implies even where impossible without scraping forbidden to scrape — fixing vessel reason, food-preparation permission not applicable; braita permitting when impossible — therefore not fixing vessel reason, like Rabbi Yehuda; nevertheless forbidden where possible without scraping — therefore moving reason; we hold like Rabbi Yehuda siman 509; therefore Rashi holds this law like Ra'ash and Tur only mishnah explanation differs for Rashi and Ra'ash. Beit Yosef further explains Tur on three divisions: if possible bake without scraping and will not scorch — forbidden scrape as moving; laying fire and ash permitted so not touch at all even though touch would not scorch; if certain fell to scorch bread via touch — permitted as moving for need; implies laying down is fire and ash not fallen plaster clay — unlike Rashi who wrote lay ash and dust, end quote; I wonder how to divide regarding touch lest scorch — certainly will scorch if fire touches — verse full 'fire laid in bosom' etc.; why permitted lay where damage will not reach bread at all; why not state stringency initially even where concern fire or clay touch forbidden scrape since no scorching repair; not inclined — only two divisions: whatever touches certainly scorches; two defects: from fallen clay if touches scorches — only remedy scraping; second from fire touch — remedy without scraping only laying apart; therefore Tur: if possible bake without scraping no clay-touch concern to bread — forbidden scrape clay and included fire and ash forbidden scrape; even if fire-touch concern and would burn, still forbidden scrape since permission in laying fire and ash — not permit more; why not mention laying fallen clay to forbid scraping even with touch concern — laying fails for coarse clay needing scraping; unlike fire and ash fine where laying helps — not for naught Rashi lay ash and dust not clay he began with — certainly fine dust from oven plaster fallen like ash and laying helps like ash and fire — forbidden scrape even if concern touch; coarse like clay chunk — laying not applicable only scraping; distinction: no touch concern forbidden scrape — first clause; touch concern on clay certainly scorches — second clause permitting even scrape since laying not effective; main distinction first vs second clause touch concern on clay or not; inserted laying law even whole oven fearing fire touch — teaches different law since laying effective — therefore forbidden scrape even with touch concern; unlike clay-touch concern coarse without remedy except scrape — teaches second clause permitted even scrape to escape touch-scorch — certainly as I explained Tur's words.

(2) Since one may not bake, etc. — in Tur it is written; in our ovens where coals are extinguished by raking, if one can bake otherwise but wishes to rake to beautify bread — forbidden; if impossible without raking — permitted — end; Beit Yosef wondered since our ovens generally cannot without raking; Rashal answered it deals with thin coals after first extinguishing; practical difference: certainly one places bread not on coals but makes space and extinguishes coals there — Tur deals with raking all coals; in our ovens we rake only for beautification without scorch concern — permitted if impossible otherwise. Maharach challenged: why forbid extinguishing to beautify since permitted for ochel nefesh — answer per Tosafos: only regarding extinguishing we say miut hatzi permitted for ochel nefesh correction; Shulchan Aruch decided well.
