{14} It is forbidden to take a stick, etc. - Whether wet or dry, even though it is fit for heating; and specifically if he did not prepare it from before Yom Tov.
{15} For it is fixing a vessel - And even if he does not fix it at all, nevertheless he makes it into a vessel on Yom Tov. And likewise it is forbidden to take a stick to make of it a door-bolt to bolt the door, for this reason, unless he prepared it from before Yom Tov; and so all such cases; and see siman 518, se'if 7.
