3) There. Provided that it is not new, etc. Regarding our ovens, which are made of clay and bricks, there is no concern at all, even when they are new. When they are made, they are fired with a strong flame to dry and strengthen the clay. There is subsequently no concern that they might collapse, because their firing has already been completed. Shulchan Gavoah, subsection 2. This applies even when the oven is large. Ibid., subsection 4.
