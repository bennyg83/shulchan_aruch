I do not know the comparison; for there the essence of the burning is because of the mitzvah, except that after its mitzvah has been fulfilled and it has become a coal, one may benefit from it, and in this it is comparable to vows and freewill offerings, of which we say that one receives from the table of Heaven; which is not so in an ordinary case, where in every circumstance it is permitted. This is what Akiva meant. And afterward I found that so wrote Mishbetzos Zahav on Yoreh De'ah, siman 99, se'if 12.
