(s.k. 5) Fixing a vessel. And the Rosh wrote that it appears, etc. And according to this reason, that is to say: not as he wrote in the Shulchan Aruch that it is fixing a vessel; and whether we say like the Rosh that it appears like fixing, as against the view of Rashi who wrote the reason that wood was given only for heating: and if so, with dry wood it is permitted, for what difference is it to me to roast with it or with its coal, as above in siman 502, se'if 3; which is not so according to the reason of fixing, for even dry wood is forbidden.
