A new oven and new stoves may not be coated with oil nor wiped with a cloth, but it is permitted to heat them even for the first heating, provided that one does not cool them with cold water in order to seal them. And if they were heated too much and it became necessary to cool them with cold water in order to be able to bake in them, it is permitted. Therefore, it is permitted to soak the broom with which one sweeps the oven, even though this extinguishes [coals].
