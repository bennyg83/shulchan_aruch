{26} One does not smear them with oil, etc. - For their way was to smear new ovens with oil and rub them in order to smooth and polish them; and it is forbidden because of fixing a vessel.
{27} But it is permitted to heat them, etc. - And it is unlike whitening tiles in order to roast upon them, which is forbidden as below in siman 508; and likewise one who cooks in a new pot, which is forbidden according to several poskim, as above at the end of siman 502, because it is hardened [strengthened] by that libun and heating, and it is like completing a vessel on Yom Tov. One must say that an oven is not hardened by heating that one heats inside it. And the view of Magen Avraham is that ovens of our times are well hardened by a first heating and it is forbidden; and there are those who disagree with him, as we wrote in se'if 1.
{28} Provided that he not temper them with cold, etc. - For by the cold that comes to them after heating while they are still hot they are strengthened more, and it is fixing a vessel.
{29} In order to bake in them - So that the bread not burn.
{30} It is permitted - Even though automatically it is hardened, we have no concern. And even though regarding libun of tiles we do not attend to the fact that his intent is to roast upon them and it is forbidden: that case is different, for the fixing of the tiles is before the use with which he will afterward use them for Yom Tov need; but here the bread is ready to place in the oven, only that he needs to cool the oven a little; and if so, in its fixing he is fixing the bread, and it is permitted. And there are those who wrote that cold tempering does only a slight hardening, and therefore the Sages permitted whenever he does not intend explicitly to harden it.
{31} Therefore, etc. - That is to say: if the oven was heated too much and he is concerned that the bread will burn, he soaks the clearing tool in water in order to cool the oven with it, and also in order to scrape with it further the fine coals that remained after the first scraping; and all this is permitted even though he extinguishes, since it is a need of food-preparation needs of the soul. And we already wrote that in this, and likewise regarding tempering the oven, it is specifically if without this the bread will be ruined; but if it will not be ruined, only scorched a little and not be nice, it is forbidden since he extinguishes. And the poskim wrote that after he cleared the oven it is forbidden to dip the clearing tool in water so that it not burn because of sparks of fire in it; for that is extinguishing not for the sake of food-preparation needs of the soul, even if he still needs it for baking on this day.
