And it is permitted to knead the ash. So wrote the Rambam and the Rif; and Rabbeinu Tam asked on this, for ash is subject to kneading, as he wrote in siman 324. The Maggid answered: since it is impossible to do this kneading from before Yom Tov, for it dries at its time, therefore it is permitted to knead it on Yom Tov because of food-preparation needs of the soul. And Maharshal ruled that since according to Rabbeinu Tam and the Rosh it is forbidden, one should not permit kneading ash with water on Yom Tov.
