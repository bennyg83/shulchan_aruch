For we are concerned that it might collapse, etc. Even if the oven was fired before Yom Tov, bread may not be placed into it on Yom Tov, because there remains a concern that it might collapse after the bread is placed in it on Yom Tov. Birkei Yosef, subsection 1. He writes that this follows both Rashi's reason and Rambam's reason, unlike Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah, who distinguishes between their reasons. See there. This applies to the large oven discussed by Shulchan Aruch. Regarding a small oven, we have already written that some permit it even if its first firing took place on Yom Tov.
