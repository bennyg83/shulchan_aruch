(In the Shulchan Aruch, se'if 20) Even though wood that fell, etc. And he wrote in Reishis Yosef that in the book Beis Moed he wrote in the name of the Raavad that bread baked with muktzeh wood: the bread is forbidden as if he baked it with something forbidden for benefit, for there is benefit of the wood in the bread; end quote. And these words require further study; and see above, siman 445, and in Yoreh De'ah siman 142. And he wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Gershom that if muktzeh was cooked in a vessel, it is permitted to cook in that vessel on Yom Tov, because the issur was burned and absorbed in the vessel, and there is no muktzeh present; see there. And this too requires further study, for below in siman 413 it is implied that even its taste forbids, except that it does not have the law of something that has a permitted form. And perhaps one word is missing, and one should read "with muktzeh wood," etc.; and that is what he means by "because the issur was burned," and his intent is to what we say, that the issur is burned away; and even though that was said regarding something that has a permitted form, he holds that the same applies to something cooked by means of it: its taste does not forbid; and this requires further study.
