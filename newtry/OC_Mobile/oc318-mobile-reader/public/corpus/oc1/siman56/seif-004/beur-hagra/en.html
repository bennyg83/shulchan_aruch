When he says, etc.—Kol Bo and Radak and the Tur and Sefer haPardes wrote a different order of four bowings and a fifth at "oseh shalom," and they called it "reshut"—and likewise in Hagahot Maimoniyot; and Taz there considers the extra bowing. And likewise Taz on the Tur—see there—and one follows what Kol Bo and Shulchan Aruch wrote, except that in Shulchan Aruch there are only five bowings, "oseh shalom"—see there. But it appears one should not bow at all in Kaddish, for one may not add at all to the bowings; and he said: since it is not in the Shemoneh Esreh prayer we have no concern—and as Tosafot wrote in Berakhot 31a, "and he found it," etc.: there it speaks of the supplications of an individual; but what they established in prayer—even if it is not in the Shemoneh Esreh, one does not bow, for even one who bows in the thanksgiving of Birkat haMazon and Hallel—behold, this is repugnant.
