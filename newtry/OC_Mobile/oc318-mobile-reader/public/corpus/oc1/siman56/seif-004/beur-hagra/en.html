Seif 4: When he says, etc. Kol Bo and Abudraham follow this arrangement. The Tur and Sefer HaPardes record a different order: four bowings, with a fifth at “Oseh shalom,” which they call optional. Hagahos Maimoniyos states likewise, though there is a scribal error there because it counts an extra bowing. There is likewise a scribal error in the Tur; see there. Its sequence follows the arrangement stated by Kol Bo and the Shulchan Aruch, except that the Shulchan Aruch has five bowings apart from “Oseh shalom”; see there. It appears, however, that one should not bow at all during Kaddish, because one may not add to the ordained bowings. One cannot argue that since this is not within the Amidah there is no concern, as Tosafos write in Berachos 31a, s.v. “and he finds him,” because there they discuss an individual's supplications that were not formally instituted as prayer. In a text formally instituted by the Sages, even outside the Amidah, one should not bow. Indeed, one who bows during the thanksgiving blessing of Bircas HaMazon or during Hallel is deemed blameworthy.
