(4) And if there are two synagogues before him, in one they reach Kedushah and in one the Kaddish after the eighteen blessings — he goes and answers Kedushah, for there he will also hear Kaddish after the prayer. Magen Avraham there; Mishnah Berurah there; he wrote it is straightforward; Chayei Adam letter alef; Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham letter alef; Radbaz there; Chayei Adam there; Siddur Beit Yosef letter vav — he wrote it appears specifically when the two synagogues are before him equally, so whichever synagogue he goes to he does not pass over the other; but if in his walking he encounters one synagogue first, and in order to go to the other synagogue he would need to pass over the first synagogue he encountered — he should go to answer the nearer one he encountered first; and even though in that synagogue he will only answer the Kaddish after the eighteen blessings because one does not pass over, until here. In my view they said one does not pass over only when both are equal, but not when they are unequal — for this is one mitzvah and that is two mitzvot, Kedushah and Kaddish; understand. See Ma'amar Mordechai letter dalet, who wrote if he can go to a place where they say Kaddish and afterward to a place where they say Kedushah and Kaddish — that is preferable; they brought Shulchan Aruch HaRav page 87b.
