[4] And now let power be magnified, etc. Maharil wrote that one should not say "and now let power be magnified" at all. Tosafot conclude that in Kaddish between Yishtabach and Yotzer, and in the Kaddish before the evening prayer, one should not say it; but in other Kaddishes one says it. So too ruled the Shlah, page 254. Magen Avraham (se'if 6) wrote that specifically before the chazzan begins one says "and now let power be magnified," for when the chazzan says "yitgadal" one does not say "and now let power be magnified" aloud but whispers it; so too appears in Kol Bo. He added further that one also says "the Omnipresent desires those who fear Him," etc.
