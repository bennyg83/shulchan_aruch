[4] "Ve'atah yigdal," etc. Maharil writes that "Ve'atah yigdal" should not be said at all. Piskei Tosafos concludes that it should not be said during the Kaddish between Yishtabach and Yotzer or during the Kaddish before Maariv, but it is said during other Kaddish recitations. Shelah rules likewise on folio 254. Magen Avraham, paragraph 6, writes that one should say "Ve'atah yigdal" only before the chazzan begins; while the chazzan says "Yisgadal," one should not say "Ve'atah yigdal," unless he says it quietly. This is also implied by Kol Bo, who adds that one should also say "Hashem chafetz," etc.
