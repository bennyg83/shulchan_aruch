[4] And now, may the strength, etc. Maharil writes that one should not say “And now, may the strength” at all. Piskei Tosafos concludes that it should not be said during the Kaddish between Yishtabach and Yotzer, nor during the Kaddish before Maariv, but that it is said during other Kaddish recitations. The Shelah likewise rules on folio 254. Magen Avraham, seif katan 6, writes that one should say “And now, may the strength” only before the chazzan begins. While the chazzan is saying “Yisgadal,” one should not say “And now, may the strength,” except quietly. This is also implied by Kol Bo, who further adds that one should also say, “Hashem desired,” etc.
