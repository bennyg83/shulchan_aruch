(15) "Almaya" to "yitbarakh"—it appears in Beit Yosef that one must say the entire yitbarakh text through v'imru amen for 28 words. See Magen Avraham, who concludes that the main custom of the ancients was not to say only through yitbarakh and then be silent and intend what the shatz says while listening as if responding. The Gra wrote one should not say only through "almaya," for there ends the praise of Amen Y'hei sh'meih rabbah... and a new section begins below; one should not mingle them in one breath. It seems that if said in two breaths the Gra would permit. Customs vary on this. In any case, if standing where interruption is forbidden, be careful not to say only through almaya [Chayei Adam].
