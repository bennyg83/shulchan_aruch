{There,} note 4—to teach a fortiori. NB: one may explain the intention of the Arizal thus: if we had the reading "Kum ki davar the Omnipresent eilecha," then it would be a positive commandment from words of Kabbalah; then one sitting to speak a mitzvah matter, such as in Tachanun or in the blessing Yotzer Or, would also need to stand—for standing for Kaddish is like a positive commandment of words of Kabbalah. But now that the correct reading is "Vayakom me'al kis'o," there is only a fortiori; then it is sufficient, for there one sits to speak optional matters, but one sitting to speak a mitzvah matter—not so.
