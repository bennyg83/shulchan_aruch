In my view the custom of the ancients was primarily to say only until "yitbarakh"; for Beit Yosef siman 125 in the name of the Rosh wrote one must be silent and concentrate when the chazzan says Kaddish and not say with him, for we hold "one who hears is as one who answers"; and especially when the chazzan says quickly — he would need to hurry more to finish before the chazzan, as written siman 51a; and speed nullifies intention — therefore it is good to hear from the shaliach tzibbur. The Shlah wrote "v'yitbarakh" with vav; but our custom is to say without vav, as in Beit Yosef and writings.
