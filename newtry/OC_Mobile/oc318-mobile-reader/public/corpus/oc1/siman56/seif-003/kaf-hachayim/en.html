(3) There — one should concentrate when answering Kaddish. If it happened that he heard Kaddish and must answer "amen, y'hei sh'meih rabbah m'varakh" and Kedushah — if he answers Kedushah he cannot answer Kaddish, and if he answers Kaddish he cannot answer Kedushah; he answers "amen, y'hei sh'meih rabbah," etc., and does not answer Kedushah.
