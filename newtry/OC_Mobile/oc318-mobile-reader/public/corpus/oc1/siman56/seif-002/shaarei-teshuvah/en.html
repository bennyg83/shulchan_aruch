Yitbarach. See Ba'er Heitev. See there in the Kavanot: If the responder finishes through be'alma before the prayer leader, he must answer Amen after Shemei deKudsha Berich Hu; see there. This demonstrates that Berich Hu must be joined to the words deKudsha, unlike what Darkei Moshe writes in the name of Or Zarua, as cited by Magen Avraham and stated in the gloss. Rather, a slight pause, like an etnachta, follows the words Berich Hu, and one then begins le'eila, etc.; meaning that the name of the Holy One, blessed is He, should be blessed and praised above, etc. See below, seif 6: At a point where one may not interrupt, one should say only through Yitbarach; see there.
