(2) There — one should concentrate when answering Kaddish, etc. It is written in Sefer Chassidim: a story of one pious man who saw another pious man after death, and his face was glowing. He said to him: why is your face glowing? He said: because I was speaking when the shaliach tzibbur was saying Vayechulu, and in the blessing Magen Avot, and in yitgadal, until here. In Matteh Moshe siman 411 he brought in the name of the Midrash: a certain sage appeared to his disciple in a dream, and the disciple saw he had a mark on his forehead; he said: why did this happen to him? He said: because I was not careful to refrain from speaking when the chazzan was saying Kaddish. Shulchan Aruch HaRav letter alef; Avnei Tzedek letter alef; Ma'amar Mordechai letter alef; Kaf HaChaim to Rav Chaim Palagi siman 13 letter tet — he wrote this is not only in prayer Kaddishes but in any Kaddish that occurs, see there.
