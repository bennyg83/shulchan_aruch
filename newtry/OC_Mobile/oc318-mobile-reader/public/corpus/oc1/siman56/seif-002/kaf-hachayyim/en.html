2) There: One should concentrate when answering Kaddish, etc. Sefer Chasidim relates that one pious man saw another pious man after the latter's death, and his face was green. He asked, "Why is your face green?" The deceased answered, "Because I would speak while the shaliach tzibbur recited Vayechulu, the blessing Magen Avos, and Yisgadal." Mateh Moshe, siman 411, cites a Midrash in which a sage appeared to his student in a dream. The student saw a stain on his forehead and asked why this had happened to him. He answered that it was because he had not guarded himself from speaking while the chazzan recited Kaddish. Knesses HaGedolah, paragraph 1; Olas Tamid, paragraph 1; Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 1; Kaf HaChaim by Rabbi Chaim Palagi, siman 13, paragraph 9. The latter writes that this applies not only to the Kaddish recitations within the prayers, but to every Kaddish; see there.
