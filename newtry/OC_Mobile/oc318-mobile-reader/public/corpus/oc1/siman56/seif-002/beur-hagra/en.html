When the shaliach tzibbur, etc.—Rambam and Tur in the name of R' Eliezer.

And they did not practice, etc.—Tur.

And one should not interrupt, etc.—Magen Avraham z"l, Dvar Malchut, etc., and he explains that "Kadosh baruch hu" concludes and "b'rikh hu" begins another blessing—and his words are difficult, for it appears "Kadosh baruch hu" refers to the Holy Name, and "l'eila" begins and refers to above what he said "yitbarakh," etc.; and so appears in Rambam and Shulchan Aruch, that after "baruch hu" they answer "Amen." And the Tur wrote in the name of R' Eliezer: one says "yitbarakh Amen yishtabach v'yitpa'ar," etc., "sh'mei dekudsha," and they say "Amen l'eila mikol," etc.—and this is a mistake.
