Seif 2: When the chazzan, etc. Rambam and the Tur in the name of Rav Amram.
They did not adopt the practice, etc. Tur.
He should not pause, etc. See Magen Avraham, who cites the Darchei Moshe, etc., and explains that “Kudsha” concludes the preceding phrase, while “berich hu” begins another blessing. His words require study. It appears instead that “Kudsha berich hu” refers to His blessed Name, while “le'eila” begins the next phrase and refers back to the preceding words, “may He be blessed,” etc. This is also implied by Rambam and the Shulchan Aruch, who write that after “berich hu” one answers Amen. The Tur writes in the name of Rav Amram: “He says, ‘Yisbarach, Amen; yishtabach v'yispa'ar,’ etc., ‘the Name of the Holy One, blessed is He,’ and they say Amen; ‘le'eila mikol,’ etc.” The alternative reading is an error.
