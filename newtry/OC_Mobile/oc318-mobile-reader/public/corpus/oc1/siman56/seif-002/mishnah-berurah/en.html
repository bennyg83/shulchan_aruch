(12) After Yisbarach. However, after ‘Yisgadal veyiskadash shemei rabbah,’ the custom is to answer amen. It is surprising that the Shulchan Aruch did not record this amen, which is explicit in Rambam and cited by the Beit Yosef.
(13) After Berich Hu. Some err by responding, when the chazzan says ‘Berich Hu,’ with those same words, ‘Berich Hu le’eila.’ This is certainly blasphemous, for it implies that He is blessed only above and not below, Heaven forbid. Rather, they should also say ‘mikol birchasa,’ or say only ‘Berich Hu,’ in accordance with our custom.
(14) He should not interrupt. See Shaarei Teshuvah, who cites Sefer HaKavanos as proving the opposite, namely, that ‘Berich Hu’ is connected to ‘deKudsha.’ One then pauses slightly, like an esnachta, and begins anew ‘le’eila,’ etc., meaning that the Name of the Holy One, blessed is He, should be blessed and praised above, etc. Biur HaGra likewise agrees.
