Be blessed. In the Kabbalistic intentions, five Amens are counted that one must answer during Kaddish. Yad Aharon writes likewise.
Above. The Taz writes: I have heard many people err when the chazzan says “berich hu,” for they too answer these words, “berich hu le'eila.” This is certainly blasphemous in implication, since it sounds as though He is blessed only above and not below, Heaven forbid. Rather, they must also say “mikol birchasa v'shirasa.” The Shelah writes that one should continue through “da'amiran b'alma,” and the Kabbalistic intentions state likewise. The Magen Avraham, however, writes: It appears to me that the practice of the early authorities is primary, for they would answer only through “yisbarach.” See there for the reason.
