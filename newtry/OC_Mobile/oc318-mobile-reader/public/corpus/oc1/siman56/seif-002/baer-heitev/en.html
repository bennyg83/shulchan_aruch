"Yitbarakh." In Kavanot he considers five "Amen"s that one must face during Kaddish—and likewise Yad Aharon.

"L'eila." Taz wrote: I heard many err when the shaliach tzibbur says "baruch hu"—they too answer these words "b'rikh hu l'eila," and this is certainly blasphemy, for it implies he blesses only above and not below, heaven forbid. Rather they should say also "mikol birkhata v'shirata." In Shlah he wrote one should say until "d'amiran ba'alma"—and likewise in Kavanot. But Magen Avraham wrote: it seems to us the custom of the ancients is the main thing—that they said only until "yitbarakh"—see there the reason.
