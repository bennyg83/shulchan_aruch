And one does not interrupt. Thus Darkei Moshe: Or Zarua wrote that one says "k'dushah" and rests in "achanachta," and afterward "barukh hu l'eila" from every blessing, etc.; but one who interrupts at "barukh hu" and afterward begins "l'eila" errs, until here. Therefore some are accustomed to say aloud with the chazzan "barukh hu l'eila" so he knows not to interrupt; nevertheless the chazzanim do ill who chant on Shabbat "l'eila" from each and interrupt between "mikol" and "birkhata."
