He should not pause. Darchei Moshe states as follows: Or Zarua writes that one should say “Kudsha,” and pause there, and afterward say “berich hu le’eila mikol birchasa,” etc. One who pauses after “berich hu” and then begins “le’eila” is mistaken. For this reason some are accustomed to say aloud together with the chazzan, “berich hu le’eila,” so that he will know not to pause. Nevertheless, the chazzanim do not act properly when on Shabbos they sing “le’eila mikol” and pause between “mikol” and “birchasa.”
