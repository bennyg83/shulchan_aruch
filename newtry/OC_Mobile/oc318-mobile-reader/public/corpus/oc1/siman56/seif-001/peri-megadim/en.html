On answering Kaddish—the Mechaber wrote two explanations for "all who answer Amen yehei shmei rabbah with all their might" (Shabbat 119): with full intent or with a loud voice; therefore both apply. And answer note (bet): do not raise one's voice above the one saying m'varakh (siman 124, se'if 12). On Shabbat 119, Amen yehei shmei rabbah has two explanations for "with all their might," but they did not explain Amen with all one's might, for one may say that regarding Amen of praise one does not raise [voice] more than the blesser, as Berakhot 45 says "Gadlu..." together—i.e., in praise—unlike a petition blessing; and answering Amen yehei shmei rabbah, which is faith, one may perhaps raise louder. Amen on a petition is that it be fulfilled speedily in our days; nevertheless they said Amen generally louder than m'varakh.
