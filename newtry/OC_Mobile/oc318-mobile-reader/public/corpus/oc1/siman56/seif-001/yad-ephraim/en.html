(In Magen Avraham se'if kaf-bet) "One may interrupt"—some emend "and one may not interrupt," etc.; in my humble opinion no emendation is needed, and he means that per the Ri's explanation there is no place to distinguish between sh'meih rabbah and between rabbah and m'varakh; and since Rav Safra said "either way it is settling the matter and we have no [firm ruling] in it," if so according to him in both one may interrupt, since they are equivalent—from which do you extract that here one may not interrupt? And on this he answered that Rama holds that since in Even HaEzer, etc. It appears he means that regarding "I did not desire to take her" according to Rashi one need not read in one breath, and per the Rif whether with or without "I did not desire."
