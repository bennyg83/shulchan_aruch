In Magen Avraham, se'if katan 2: He may interrupt. Some emend the text to read, 'He may not interrupt, etc.' In my humble opinion no emendation is necessary. It means that according to Ri's explanation there is no basis to distinguish between 'Shemei Rabbah' and 'Rabbah mevarach.' Since Rav Safra says that both are completions of a phrase and there is no concern, according to him one may interrupt in either place. They are equivalent; which one could be excluded so as to say that interruption is forbidden there? Magen Avraham answers that Rama holds this way because in Even HaEzer, etc. It appears that he means that regarding the phrase 'I did not desire to take her,' Rashi holds it need not be read in one breath, while according to Rif this applies both to 'did not consent' and to 'did not desire.'
