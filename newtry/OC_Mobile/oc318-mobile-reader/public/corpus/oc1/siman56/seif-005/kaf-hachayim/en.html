(5) And if he already heard Kaddish and did not answer Kedushah — he precedes answering Kedushah so he fulfills both Kaddish and Kedushah, for both are good as one. Ma'amar Mordechai letter dalet; Shulchan Aruch HaRav page 87, side 3; Chassid Lavan letter hei; Ben Ish Chai there. In my view this is when he cannot afterward answer Kedushah to fulfill the eighteen; but if it will happen to him afterward also to answer Kedushah — even though he has not yet answered Kedushah, Kaddish comes first. Further it seems to me: when they said Kaddish precedes Kedushah, this is when he is not obligated in that Kedushah to fulfill the eighteen with it — such as he has not yet prayed the eighteen, or he already prayed the Amidah and repetition and answered Kedushah. But if that Kedushah he is obligated to answer now — such as he prayed the eighteen and the shaliach tzibbur repeats the repetition aloud and reached saying Kedushah, and he heard Kaddish from elsewhere and cannot say it and finish it and afterward answer Kedushah — Kedushah comes first, for a mitzvah in its time is preferable; and here is its place that has a reason in secret, as explained in Sha'ar HaKavanot, derush 2 of repetition of the Amidah, see there.
