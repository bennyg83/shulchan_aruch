In the synagogue. Turei Zahav. We are accustomed to be somewhat lenient regarding a candela lamp, as Turei Zahav writes in paragraph 2. Nevertheless, one should not recite the Maaravis piyyut when Yom Tov falls on Shabbos, because the Sages did not distinguish between cases. At times there will be oil lamps, in which case there is a concern of a prohibition lest he tilt the lamp.
