(ד) [Levush] And he cuts, etc. I wonder at Olat Tamid who wrote this wording — the Levush wrote lest he cut the head of the wick with a vessel, and you would say that on Yom Tov too it should be forbidden until here his words; but the word vessel was not mentioned in the Levush at all. And perhaps his intent is from the wording yichatech — it implies with a vessel; but this too is wondrous, for even in Beit Yosef he wrote thus, and furthermore below at the end of siman 514 it is explained as Beit Yosef that the wording yichatech deals also by hand — see there.
