Whose practice is not, etc. It is surprising that they permitted this broadly to every learned person, for even nowadays there are people engaged in study who do not have such distinguished status. I heard from a sage who relied on the fact that the lamp was wax and not easily adjusted. This appears correct, as Maharshal is cited by Perishah. The question means that some learners, though distinguished and ordinarily accustomed to instruct another rather than adjust the lamp themselves, may while absorbed in learning lower themselves to ordinary behavior and adjust it personally. Bach implies that nowadays even a distinguished person should not rely on wax, because we are not expert in determining who has the requisite status.
