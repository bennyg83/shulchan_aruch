(10) To inspect his garments. Generally, there is no distinction here between a regular servant and one who is not regular. The Ran, Ramban, and Ritva understand the allowance as applying only when no close examination is required. Rambam and Tur connect this law to the following one and prohibit only close examination. With wax or tallow light, there is room to be lenient where only slight examination is needed by combining the views of Bach and Maharshal.
