In a lantern. If it is locked with a key, it is permitted; so did Mahara'sh, Maharam, and their students, and so wrote Hagahot Maimoniyot, Bach, and Taz. Olat Tamid forbids because they did not distinguish; see there. It is forbidden to read by candlelight even by day in a dark place. Halachot Ketanot part 2 siman 27.
Wax. Bach wrote in the name of Rashal that the practice is to be lenient to check vessels and tzitzit and to read the Haggadah by a wax candle, since there is no concern lest he tilt. Ra'avan is lenient with a tallow candle since it is repulsive; see seif 12.
