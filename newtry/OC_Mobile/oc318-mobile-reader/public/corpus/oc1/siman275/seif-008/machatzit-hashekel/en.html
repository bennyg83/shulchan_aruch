Paragraph 8: Because, etc.; but Ran, etc.; one must say that a servant, etc. According to Shulchan Aruch's reason, a regular servant can be distinguished from an irregular one: the regular servant knows which utensils the table needs and requires no examination. According to Ran, however, what distinction exists?
The text should read: 'which utensil has a crack and is therefore unclean.' Utensils without cracks are easily cleaned, whereas cracks are difficult; residue remains attached in them after cleaning. A regular servant knows which utensil is cracked and either does not place it on the table or cleans the known crack without examination. An irregular servant does not know.
