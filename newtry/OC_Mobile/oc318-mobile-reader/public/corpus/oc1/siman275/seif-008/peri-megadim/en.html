Fluent. Turei Zahav. Here too, in paragraph 2, Turei Zahav permits reading by a wax or tallow candle. If he has only an oil lamp and is not fluent in the Haggadah because he never learned it and therefore requires a siddur, even so, one can say that its law is like that of Bameh Madlikin. See the poskim.
If he has an oil lamp and beside it a candela lamp, it would seemingly follow from the opinion cited by Magen Avraham, paragraph 4, in siman 264, that it is permitted here as well, since a candela lamp is next to him. This is mistaken, however: regarding a candela lamp, the concern is a decree added to another decree, yet he may still come to tilt the oil lamp. So too writes Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 4. Nevertheless, one can say that since they permitted him only when a candela lamp is beside him, he will remember. In any event, it is prohibited. There is also a concern that he may take some of the oil, and even regarding a candela lamp and naphtha it is proper to prohibit it.
