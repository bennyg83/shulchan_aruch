8) If a window or opening exists in the wall between a person's house and his neighbor's, and a lamp in the neighbor's house shines through that window or opening and illuminates the person's house opposite it, it appears that he may read in his house opposite that window or opening. We do not decree lest he enter his neighbor's house and tilt the lamp. If, however, one person has two rooms, with a lamp in one room whose light passes through a window or opening into the second, he may not read even in the other room where no lamp is burning, opposite the window or opening through which the first room's lamplight enters. Beis Menuchah, paragraph 2.
