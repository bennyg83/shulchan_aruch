10) Similarly, one may read sacred writings on Friday until nightfall. We are not concerned that his study might draw him onward and cause him to continue reading by lamplight after nightfall. Reading at night itself is prohibited only by decree, and forbidding it before nightfall would constitute a decree upon a decree. Rabbi David Pardo in Chasdei David, chapter 1 of Shabbos, folio 51 at the end of the second column; Beis Menuchah, paragraph 4.
