Because he does not recognize them. This wording implies that he is checking which utensils he should place on the table. But Ran wrote that he checks whether they are clean. One must say that with a regular attendant, since he is familiar with them, he knows which utensil has a crack or is not sufficiently clean.
And some permit, etc. This is because it is also repulsive.
That does not require examination, etc. Even though it is forbidden even to distinguish between his own garments and his wife's garments, here they permitted it for cleanliness; Ran. Yerushalmi: but he may look at what is in the cup and what is in the bowl and need not be concerned. Some say this is because it is only momentary and there is no concern that he will tilt the lamp; others say it is because of cleanliness and danger; Raavan 338. According to the second reason, the Yerushalmi says it is permitted to clean lettuce leaves, meaning to pick vegetables, as written in siman 611. "Chazarin" is an expression for lettuce, unlike what the commentator there on the Yerushalmi wrote.
Lest he draw from it. This is because he brings his hands very close to the lamp, unlike other uses. Beit Yosef. See siman 262 seif 2.
