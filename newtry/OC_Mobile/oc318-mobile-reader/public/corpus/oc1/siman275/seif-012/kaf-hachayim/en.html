(יב) And it is simple that even if a wax candle and oil candle stand next to each other, it is forbidden to read facing them. And one should not compare this law to what is written in siman 264 in the name of Magen Avraham — if a kosher candle stands next to a disqualified candle, it is permitted to use facing them — for there it deals with other uses that do not require much examination, and it suffices with the kosher candle alone, therefore there is no concern lest he tilt the disqualified candle since the kosher suffices; but here it deals with uses requiring examination — even with kosher oil there is concern of tilting, only with a wax candle there is no such concern — therefore in such a case there is certainly concern lest he tilt the oil candle, and this is very simple. Turei Zahav, letter 4, and so Mateh Yehuda, letter 8; however, as we already wrote in the previous letter, even with a wax candle itself one should be stringent where possible. And see above, siman 264, letter 100.
