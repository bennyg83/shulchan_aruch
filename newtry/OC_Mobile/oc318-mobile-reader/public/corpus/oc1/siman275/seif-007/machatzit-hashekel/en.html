Paragraph 7: For his awe, etc.; from the unqualified wording, etc. Rambam wrote 'in his teacher's presence,' while Rashba permitted it even outside his presence. Magen Avraham says the Mechaber's omission of this condition implies he follows Rashba. Taz agrees.
See Yoreh De'ah 245:14. This is unrelated to the preceding discussion. Since children's reading was mentioned, Magen Avraham cites Taz in Yoreh De'ah: on Shabbos one should not teach children new material they have not yet studied, because of their distress; material studied once on a weekday may be taught. This is the law of the Gemara in Nedarim.
