7) Machatzis HaShekel, paragraph 1, writes that according to those who permit use of a key, an unusual knot likewise permits reading. Regarding articles sent through a non-Jew that require a seal, an unusual knot is superior to a key, as stated in Yoreh De'ah, simanim 118 and 130; see there. Petach HaDevir, paragraph 2, cites this; see there.
