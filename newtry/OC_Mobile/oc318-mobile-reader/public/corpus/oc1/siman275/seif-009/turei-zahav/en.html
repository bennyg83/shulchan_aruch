Somewhat fluent in his mouth. For this reason, one may permit a person who rises early on Shabbos to go to the synagogue and pray by the light of a lamp that was kindled there before Shabbos. The prayers and the other passages he recites are certainly more familiar to him than the Haggadah on Pesach. Studying by that light, however, is forbidden, because he must examine the text carefully. Where we permitted use of a wax candle in siman 278, there is no prohibition here. See there.
