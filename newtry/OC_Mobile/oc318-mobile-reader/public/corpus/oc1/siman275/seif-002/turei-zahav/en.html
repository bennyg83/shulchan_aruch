(א) But two may read, etc. — it implies that regarding beans there is no distinction, for even with two it is forbidden, for each one who sorts and checks for lice checks in another place, and it is like reading in two matters; unless one sorts and the other guards it — this is certainly permitted.

(ב) In the synagogue. Explanation: even there, where there are many people, and all the more so an individual in his house it is forbidden; and it implies that other prayer may be read from a siddur by candle light — the reason is that prayer is common in everyone's mouth and does not need much examination, and it is like the beginnings of sections in seif 10; and per what is written regarding wax and milk candles there is no concern at all.
