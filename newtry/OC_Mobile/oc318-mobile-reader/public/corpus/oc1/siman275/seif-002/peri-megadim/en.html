And likewise. Turei Zahav. The Mechaber’s use of the term ‘wax candle’ is not precise. There is no prohibition on account of placing a stumbling block before the blind, just as they did not prohibit a distinguished person from reading in another house; rather, the concern is that he may trim the wick. Turei Zahav writes that according to this reasoning it should be permitted, since it would be a decree added to another decree. He writes similarly in siman 278. See siman 514, Turei Zahav, s.k. 12; Magen Avraham, paragraph 23; and Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 3, who is concerned that he may trim the wick with a utensil, for which there is a remedy derived from the Torah. Beis Yosef, however, does not mention extinguishing, but only the possibility that he will trim the top of the wick; see there. This requires examination.
