Two may read, etc. But they may not inspect clothing for lice together, as Bach writes. Magen Avraham explains that Chazal permitted two people only for a mitzvah matter, not a discretionary act. Taz explains instead that each checks a different place, making it like reading two separate subjects. If one person inspects while the other watches him, it is permitted.
In the synagogue, etc. Meaning that even though fear of the congregation restrains them, the restriction still applies, all the more so to an individual at home. Other prayers are permitted. Likewise one who rises early to pray may read because everyone is familiar with the text, like section headings in seif 9. Zemirot may also be read if he is somewhat accustomed to them.
