Paragraph 2: In a glass case, etc. Bach forbids it under a uniform decree. Eliyah Rabbah explains the permissive view: when a lamp is ten stories high, no reminder exists, since even on weekdays one places a lamp high when needing to use that place; the uniform decree applies. A lock is different, since this is not the weekday manner and provides a reminder, so no uniform decree was made. It appears to me that an unusual knot likewise permits it according to the lenient view. Regarding items sent through a gentile that require a seal, an unusual knot is superior to a lock, as in Yoreh De'ah 118 and 130.
