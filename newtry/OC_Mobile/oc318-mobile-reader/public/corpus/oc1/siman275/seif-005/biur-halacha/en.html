(7) By a bonfire. Reading by a bonfire is more stringent than merely using its light, as explained in siman 255. A friend will not necessarily notice when one stirs the fire.
(8) Sitting at a distance. This apparently refers even to two books. According to the first rationale, two people studying the same subject may be lenient even with separate books, but a bonfire remains problematic. According to the second rationale, that the flame is at the end of the wood, the restriction applies in every case, even when they sit close together and read from one book [Olas Tamid].
