(Siman katan 2) And in the year, etc.—as the Rosh wrote there at the end of Kiddushin.

That is to say, etc.—these are the Rosh's words there in Kiddushin to explain that which the Gemara said there regarding lying together in physical closeness: "this one and this one" so that their breasts are developed and "your hair has sprouted." And the Rosh explained that the intent is not that they must already have breasts developed, etc., but that they are close to having breasts developed, etc.—which is the year adjacent to adulthood, end quote from the Rosh.

And the Rosh learned there from Kriat Shema, etc.—meaning that the Rosh in Kiddushin erased the girsa that was before him and had the girsa: a girl of eleven years and one day, and a boy of twelve years and one day; and he brought proof that this is the girsa in tractate Berachos regarding Kriat Shema.

If so, they must be equal for the halacha; and if so, that which we say in Berachos "until their breasts are developed," etc., means likewise "close to having breasts developed," etc.

And there is a great difficulty; and in Sefer Ateret Zekeinim he resolves the second difficulty—that it may be said that here regarding Kriat Shema it deals with a father with his son and a woman with her young daughter, for regarding sleeping in physical closeness there is no prohibition except because of Kriat Shema, see there; but that which he wrote to resolve the first difficulty—I did not understand his opinion.
