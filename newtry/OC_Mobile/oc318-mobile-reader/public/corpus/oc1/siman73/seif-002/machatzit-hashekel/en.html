(Seif katan 2) And in the year, etc., as the Rosh writes there at the end of Kiddushin.
Meaning, etc. These are the words of the Rosh there in Kiddushin, explaining the Gemara's statement there regarding sleeping together with direct bodily contact: for both of them, the measure is when 'the breasts are developed and your hair has grown.' The Rosh explains that it does not mean they must actually have developed breasts and hair. Rather, they must be close to that stage, namely, the year immediately preceding adulthood. These are the words of the Rosh.
The Rosh derived it there from Krias Shema, etc. Meaning, the Rosh in Kiddushin deleted the reading before him and read instead: a girl of eleven years and one day, and a boy of twelve years and one day. He brought proof that this is the correct reading from tractate Berachos regarding Krias Shema.
If so, the two laws must be identical. Therefore, when Berachos states, 'until the breasts are developed,' etc., it must likewise mean that they are close to developing breasts, etc.
This presents a major difficulty. In Eliyah Rabbah, the second difficulty is resolved by suggesting that here, regarding Krias Shema, the case concerns a father with his young son and a woman with her young daughter. There is no prohibition of sleeping together with bodily contact, and the issue arises only because of Krias Shema. See there. I did not understand, however, what he wrote to resolve the first difficulty.
