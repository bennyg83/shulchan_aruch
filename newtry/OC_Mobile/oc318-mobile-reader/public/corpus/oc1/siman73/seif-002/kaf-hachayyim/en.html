(ב) There — and their flesh touches one another, etc. — it is implied from the words of Shulchan Arukh and the poskim z"l that we do not require a tallit separation except specifically when their flesh touches one another, for thereby they come to improper thoughts; but if one is on one side of the bed and one on the other side so they do not touch one another at all, as is the way of most of the world among us — a tallit separation is not needed; and it is further explained per the words of Rambam in chapter 3 of Hilchot Keriat Shema — see there; and in any case turning the face suffices per Eliyah Rabbah note 1. And Pri Megadim in Eshel Avraham at the head of the siman wrote that whenever they are face to face, even if their flesh does not touch from the waist down, it is forbidden without a tallit, for we do not distinguish — until here. And see below siman 74 in Birkei Yosef note 4 and in our words note 6; and this is specifically to separate between them — not needed if they are distant from one another and turn their faces; but to separate between his heart and the nakedness, one needs as written below siman 74 seif 1.
