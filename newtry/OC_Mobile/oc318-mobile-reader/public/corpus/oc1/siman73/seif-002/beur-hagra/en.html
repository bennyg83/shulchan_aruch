Seif 2—when turning the faces away, etc. Meaning, even if he alone turns away; and as written in the Gemara, "this one turns his face and recites," etc.; and as is explained from the words of the Rambam—not like the Magen Avraham and Rashi there, s.v. "and even his wife," etc.

And "there are those who forbid," etc.—like a baraita that argues with Rav Yosef stringently; and even though an amora can uproot a baraita, that is only if he heard it, and as written in Shabbos 61a; and this is what it says there, "Are they all in one [baraita]," etc.
