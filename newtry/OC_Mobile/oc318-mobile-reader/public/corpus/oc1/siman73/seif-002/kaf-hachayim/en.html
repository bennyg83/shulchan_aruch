(2) There—and the flesh of both of them touches each other, etc.—it is implied from the words of the Shulchan Aruch and the posekim that we do not require separating with a tallit except specifically when the flesh of both touches each other, for through that they come to sinful thought; but if this one is on one side from below and this one on another side so they do not touch each other at all, as is the way of most of the world between us, separating with a tallit is not required—and more is explained to one's understanding in the language of the Rambam end of chapter 3 of Hilchos Keriat Shema, see there; and in any case when turning the faces away it suffices—from Eliyah Rabbah letter alef. And Peri Megadim in Aruch HaShulchan at the beginning of the siman wrote that whenever it is face opposite face, even if their flesh does not touch from their waists and below, it is forbidden without a tallit, for we do not distinguish—until here. And see below siman 74 in Birkei Yosef letter dalet and in our words letter vav. And this is specifically to separate between them—it is not required if they are distant from each other and turn their faces; but to separate between his heart and ervah is required, as written below siman 74 seif 1. And likewise what is written here in the Shulchan Aruch "a tallit separating between them," etc.—this is besides what must separate between his heart and the written ervah below siman 74 seif 1; afterward I saw that Aruch HaShulchan wrote thus there.
