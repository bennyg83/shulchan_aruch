If he was sleeping with his young children, he may recite while turning his face away, without placing a tallis between them. Even if their nakedness touches one another, it is permitted. This is stated in the Hagahos Ashri in the name of the Or Zarua. The Beit Yosef writes that it is astonishing to permit so much. See the words of Rabbeinu Yonah in the chapter Mi Shemeiso, folio 19a.
