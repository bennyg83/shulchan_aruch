(7) With his sons, etc.—for according to them all agree we say since he is familiar with them they are considered like his body and there is no improper thought. Nevertheless face to face is forbidden; and in Pri Chadash he is lenient on this and brought proof; and in Sefer Maamar Mordechai and Birchas Yosef they rejected his view. And it is also implied from the words of Maggid Mishneh that even if we say one need not turn away faces, nevertheless regarding touching his ervah with their flesh or vice versa—it is forbidden.

(8) Turning away faces—meaning their backs should be to one another; and if his sons' faces are against his back he must in any case be careful that his flesh not touch their ervah—so it seems to me; and proof from below siman 74 seif 5, for it is evident that if his flesh touches his companion's ervah it is likewise forbidden.

(9) Adults—and the same applies to other minors who are not his sons [so it is implied from Rambam; and see Biur HaGra siman 75 seif 4 in gloss, and it can be reconciled]; but according to what is explained below siman 75 seif 4 in gloss, that a minor as long as intercourse is not appropriate [meaning for a male until nine years and one day, and for a female until two years and one day] his nakedness is not ervah—for our matter too one may be lenient if the time has not yet arrived.
