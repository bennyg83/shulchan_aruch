(7) With his children, etc. Regarding them, all agree that because he is familiar with them, they are considered like his own body and there is no concern for improper thoughts. Nevertheless, face to face is forbidden. Peri Chadash is lenient and brings a proof, but Maamar Mordechai and Birkei Yosef reject his proof. It further appears from the Maamar Mordechai that even should we say they need not turn their faces away, it is at least forbidden for his nakedness to touch their bodies, or vice versa.
(8) By turning their faces away. Meaning, their backs must face one another. If his children's faces are toward his back, he must at least ensure that his body does not touch their nakedness. This appears to me, and proof may be brought from below, siman 74, seif 5, where it is evident that if one's body touches another person's nakedness, it is also forbidden.
(9) Adults. The same applies to other young children who are not his own. This is implied by Rambam. See Biur HaGra to siman 75, seif 4, in the gloss, though that source can be reconciled. However, according to what is explained below in siman 75, seif 4, in the gloss, that a minor's nakedness is not considered nakedness until the child is fit for intercourse, namely a male at nine years and one day and a female at three years and one day, it would appear that here as well one may be lenient before that age.
