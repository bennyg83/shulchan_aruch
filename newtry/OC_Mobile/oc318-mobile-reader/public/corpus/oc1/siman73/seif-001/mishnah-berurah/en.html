(1) Touching—he used "touching" because then it is forbidden to recite without tallit separation even if they wish to turn their faces away from each other; and if they lie face to face it is forbidden to recite without tallit separation even if they are not touching—as long as they lie in close proximity to one another.

(2) They may not recite—lest he come to improper thoughts.

(3) Between them—and then even face to face is permitted, for he will not again come to improper thoughts.

(4) And below—but from the waist upward, even if their flesh touches one another, improper thoughts do not arise. And know that in all this one must also put his head outside so his eyes cannot see the ervah, and one must also cover his heart so his heart does not see the ervah, as below at the beginning of siman 74, and see there siman katan 2 what we wrote further on this. Pri Chadash wrote it seems to him that bedi'eved if he recited without turning away his face and without tallit separation he has fulfilled his obligation—see there; and Peri Megadim is uncertain about this; and likewise in Sefer Yeshuot Yaakov, see there.
