Two people who were, etc., touching one another See Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 1, where it is written that face to face is forbidden even when they are not touching. This is from the Peri Megadim. The Maamar Mordechai is uncertain about this. It appears to me that according to the Peri Megadim, the Magen Avraham's second difficulty in seif katan 2 may perhaps be resolved. See there.
From their waists downward I am uncertain whether the barrier must separate them until the ends of their bodies, or whether even if the tallis is short and their legs touch below it, this is permitted. The Gra cites the source from siman 74, seif 6, yet there, by strict law, only the nakedness itself must be covered, as is evident from Rambam, chapter 4, halachah 7. Perhaps here the law is more stringent because of improper thoughts, though he holds that above the waist such thoughts are not relevant. Proof of this is that even if only their buttocks touch, although the buttocks are not considered nakedness, as stated in the Gemara, it is nevertheless forbidden. The reason must therefore be improper thoughts caused by contact. It is possible that even below that point such thoughts may arise, and this requires study. The sefer Avodas HaYom writes: All the laws of this siman apply when one recites Krias Shema, prays, or speaks words of Torah aloud. To think words of Torah silently is permitted even when one's own nakedness is uncovered, as ruled at the end of siman 75, and certainly in this case. See there. He further writes that for this reason one may think sacred thoughts during marital relations, since thought is not equivalent to speech for this purpose, although it is equivalent to speech with respect to thinking Torah in filthy alleyways. This is an abbreviated quotation.
