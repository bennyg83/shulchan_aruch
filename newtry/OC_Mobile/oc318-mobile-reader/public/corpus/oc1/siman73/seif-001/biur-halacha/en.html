Two who were, etc., touching each other—see in Mishna Berurah s.k. 1 what he wrote, that face to face even when they are not touching is forbidden according to Peri Megadim; and the article of Mordechai is uncertain about this, and it seems to me that according to Peri Megadim's words one can resolve the second question of Magen Avraham in s.k. 2—see there.

From their waists and below—I was in doubt whether we require a barrier until the end of their bodies, or perhaps even if the tallit is short and below at their feet they touch each other it is permitted; and from the Gra who cited the source from siman 74 seif 66—and there we only require covering of ervah alone by law, as is evident from Rambam chapter 4 halacha 7—and perhaps here it is stricter because of sinful thoughts [only that above the waist he holds that sinful thoughts do not apply], and you should know that even if only their buttocks touch each other, which have no ervah concern as stated in the Gemara, nevertheless it is forbidden—and therefore the reason is because of sinful thought through contact; perhaps even below that there is sinful thought, and it requires study. He wrote in Sefer Avodat HaYom regarding all this siman: when reciting Keriat Shema or praying or speaking words of Torah with one's mouth; but to contemplate words of Torah we do not rule that it is permitted even when one's hair is uncovered, as he wrote at the end of siman 75—all the more so here—and see there what he further wrote; and for this reason it is permitted to contemplate holy matters during marital relations, since thought is not like speech for this matter, even though it is like speech regarding contemplating impure alleyways—in short.
