(ד) [Seif 2] If he was sleeping with his wife, he recites by turning his face, etc. — and Rambam wrote: his wife is like his own body — meaning that since he is familiar with her he does not have improper thoughts; nevertheless he turns his face, for face to face or face to back — it is impossible that there be no improper thoughts — as written in chapter 3 of Keriat Shema law 18. Eliyah Rabbah note 2. And Magen Avraham note 1 explained that both should turn their backs to one another. And so Machatzit HaShekel note 1. Razah note 2.
