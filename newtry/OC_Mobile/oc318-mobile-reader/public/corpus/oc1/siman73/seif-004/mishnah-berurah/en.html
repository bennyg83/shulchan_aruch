(10) And in the thirteenth year, etc.—meaning from when the first day of this year began; and likewise what he wrote "from the thirteenth year and onward" means likewise immediately when the thirteenth year was completed; and one does not need thirteen years and one day, only completeness—and the day of birth is also included. For example, even if he was born on 1 Tishrei at the end of the day, when the beginning of the night of the fourteenth year in which he was born arrives—that is 1 Tishrei—his thirteen years are completed and he becomes an adult; and the same law for bar mitzvah purposes, but there we also require two hairs; and see above siman 55 seif 9 and Mishna Berurah there.

(11) If they brought two hairs—see Magen Avraham who wrote that according to what Tur ruled in Even HaEzer siman 21, it is forbidden once this time arrives even if they did not bring two hairs. And also even without Kriat Shema they are forbidden to sleep together naked in physical closeness once this time arrives; and see Choshen Mishpat and Bach there.
