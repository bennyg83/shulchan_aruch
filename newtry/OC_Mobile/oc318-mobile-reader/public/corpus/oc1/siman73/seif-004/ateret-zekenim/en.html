How young are they considered minors? Until he has eleven years. Explanation: that they have not yet reached eleven and twelve full years; for if each one has reached eleven and twelve full years and one day, then he is within the thirteenth year—and if they brought two hairs, it is forbidden without separation; and likewise in the thirteenth year for a child—meaning even if he is one day after the twelfth year—thus it appears to me from the implication of the Tur; and some say in the years eleven and twelve—that is, that he has already reached eleven and twelve (Maharshal).
