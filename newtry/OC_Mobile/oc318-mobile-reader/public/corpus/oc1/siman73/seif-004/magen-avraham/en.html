And in the thirteenth year, etc., it is permitted. The Tur writes this. It requires study, for in Even HaEzer, siman 21, the Tur writes that if the girl is eleven years old and the boy twelve years old, he must sleep in his garment and she in hers, and they are forbidden to lie together unclothed. He does not distinguish based on whether they have developed two pubic hairs. As the Rosh writes there, this means that they are close to the stage of developed breasts and grown hair. Here, however, he writes that if they have not developed two hairs, it is permitted. The Rosh derived the law there from this law of Krias Shema, so the two laws should be identical. Furthermore, if she is forbidden to sleep beside him with direct bodily contact, why is it necessary to state that he may not recite Krias Shema? The words of the Rosh also appear internally inconsistent. This requires considerable study.
And from the thirteenth year onward. This implies that thirteen years and one day are not required. The same is implied by Rashi's commentary to Yevamos 34a. See there, and see what I wrote in siman 55, seif 9.
