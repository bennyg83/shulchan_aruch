And in the thirteenth year, etc.—permitted. Likewise in Tur; and there is a great difficulty, for in Tur Even HaEzer siman 21 it is written that if the daughter has eleven years and the son twelve years—he sleeps in his garment and she in her garment, and they are forbidden to lie naked together; and he did not distinguish between two hairs as the Rosh said there, meaning that they are close to having breasts developed and hair sprouted—and here he wrote that if they did not bring two hairs it is permitted. And the Rosh learned from here regarding Kriat Shema; and if so, they must be equal for the halacha. And further, if she is forbidden to sleep with him in physical closeness—then why need it say forbidden to recite Kriat Shema? Also the Rosh's words contradict each other, and there is a great difficulty.

And from the thirteenth year and onward—it is implied one does not need thirteen years and one day; and likewise Yevamos page 34a in Rashi's explanation, see there; and see above siman 55 seif 9.
