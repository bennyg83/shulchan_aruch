(4) [Seif 2] If he was sleeping with his wife, he recites when turning the faces away, etc.—and the Rema wrote, "his wife is like his body," meaning that since he is familiar with her he does not have sinful thoughts; nevertheless he turns his face, for if face to face or face to back, sinful thought is impossible—as explained chapter 3 of Keriat Shema, halacha 18. Eliyah Rabbah letter bet. And Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 1: explanation, that both should turn their backs to each other. And likewise Machatzis HaShekel s.k. 1. Radbaz letter bet. And even though Birkei Yosef letter dalet wrote on the words of Magen Avraham cited that his words are not mandatory—see there; and likewise Nehar Shalom letter alef, that whoever turned his face away from her is permitted—nevertheless one may be stringent where possible, since thus understood Machatzis HaShekel and Radbaz like the words of Magen Avraham.
