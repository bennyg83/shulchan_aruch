Levush: We do not fall, etc. But we do say Lamnatzeiach. One who has a wedding on that day does not fast on it, and likewise on the Fifteenth of Av; see siman 573, paragraph 3, and the end of siman 572. For the law of a fast for a dream, see siman 288, paragraph 9, and siman 580. The glosses of Maharil, Laws of Chanukah, wrote that in Mainz and Worms they say Tachanun on the Fifteenth of Shevat and on the Fifteenth of Av.
