Should Rosh Chodesh Adar adjoining Nissan occur on a weekday—even on erev Shabbos—they advance the reading of Parashas Shekalim to the preceding Shabbos, and they interrupt on the second Shabbos so that Parashas Zachor falls on the Shabbos immediately before Purim.
