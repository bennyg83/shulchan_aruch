Levush: In the year of King, etc. Until the zeal of Hashem of Hosts will do this. Abudarham wrote in the laws of Purim: if you ask why the month of Adar is intercalated rather than the other months, one may answer that Tishrei must be the seventh, as it says, in the seventh month; and Teves also must be the tenth, as it says, in the tenth month, which is the month of Teves. Shevat also is not intercalated because it says, to the twelfth month, which is the month of Adar. Alternatively, because the month of Adar is adjacent to spring, and in it it becomes known whether it is necessary to intercalate because of the spring or not.
