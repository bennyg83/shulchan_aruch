The days on which Rosh Chodesh Adar II may properly be fixed: the mnemonic zavadlu matches weekdays to dates, and samech–lamed for the Sabbaths on which we interrupt study: zayin–tes–vav—when Rosh Chodesh is Shabbos, we interrupt on the fifteenth of that month; the sign is zayin–tes–vav. When it is Monday, we interrupt on the sixth; sign beis–vav. When Wednesday, on the fourth; sign daled–daled. When Friday, on two dates—the second and sixteenth; sign vav beis yud vav:
