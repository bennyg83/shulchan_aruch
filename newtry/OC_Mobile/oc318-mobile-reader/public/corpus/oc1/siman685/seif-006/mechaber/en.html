The days on which it is fitting to fix Rosh Chodesh Adar are the seventh, second, fourth, and sixth days of the week. The mnemonic for the Shabbosos of interruption is: 15 on the seventh day, 6 on the second day, 4 on the fourth day, and 2 and 16 on the sixth day. Meaning, when Rosh Chodesh falls on Shabbos, they interrupt on the fifteenth of it, and the mnemonic is 15 on the seventh day. When it falls on Monday, they interrupt on the sixth of it, and the mnemonic is 6 on the second day. When it falls on Wednesday, they interrupt on the fourth of it, and the mnemonic is 4 on the fourth day. When it falls on Friday, they interrupt on two Shabbosos, the second of it and the sixteenth of it, and the mnemonic is 2 and 16.
