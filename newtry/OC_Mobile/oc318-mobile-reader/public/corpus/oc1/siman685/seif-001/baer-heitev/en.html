Shekalim. If the congregation forgot on Rosh Chodesh Adar to read Parashas Shekalim, they must go back and take out a Torah scroll and read the section; Ginas Veradim, Orach Chaim, rule 1, siman 36; see there. If they erred and read the special sections and the megillah in the first Adar, they must go back and read them in the second; see Bach.
