1) [Seif 1] Rosh Chodesh Adar that is adjacent to Nissan, etc. The reason it was necessary to say, 'that is adjacent to Nissan,' is to teach us that in a leap year the reading is not performed in the first Adar, which is adjacent to Shevat, but in the second Adar, which is adjacent to Nissan, as stated in Megillah 6b, see there.
