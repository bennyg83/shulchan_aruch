In Shlah he extended to side Parashat Parah is not d'oraisa — so Bach; so in responsum Maharil simanim 182 and 267 mentioned only Parashat Zachor. Tur HaShulchan siman 108: in Ashrei chapter 6 Berachot wrote positive mitzvah d'oraisa to read Zachor with ten; Tosafot Shantz: no Torah reading d'oraisa except Zachor; Tosafot Ketuvot Berachot chapter 2 explained Zachor and Parah obligatory from Torah, etc.; Semag wrote positive mitzvah remember what Amalek did; if so be more careful they hear Zachor on 14th from Megillah in time — world not careful — end; it appears to me to resolve custom — is it written in Torah specifically this Shabbat? Chazal instituted this Shabbat since many in synagogue near Purim to connect Amalek to Haman as Levush — when hearing on Purim Vayavo Amalek remembers Amalek and fulfills positive mitzvah; Raavan siman 2; regarding Parah I hold not d'oraisa — truly no Torah reason for its reading and I do not know where hinted; it appears correct girsa as Chiddushei Rashba: they challenged we do not find Torah scroll reading unless Rashi says reading portions such as Parah and Zachor from Torah — end; nevertheless each should be careful and zealous in mitzvah to come to minyan; Megillah 18 implies plainly Zachor d'oraisa; implies there likewise Vayavo Amalek suffices. Maharil: in all four portions do not mention souls, say tzavtzav (dam). Some in leap year establish fast every Thursday from Sheuvim through Ta'anit Esther; some say also Vayakhel Pekudei, Shomer Yisrael, etc.:
