(1) (a) Rabbi Adar, etc. - Abar for short is the matter of 4 Parshiyots. And it is. The Sages decreed that 4 Parshiyots should happen a year from Rabbi Ader to Rabbi Nisan in memory of 4 Deuteronomy and they are these. The first is the shekel parshah in remembrance of the half shekel matzoh that will be explained nearby that they were obligated to give to the bureau for the perpetual sacrifice each year. The second is the parashat remember to remember the deed of Amalek and recite it on the Shabbat before Purim to approve the deed that was from the seed of Amalek and to precede the remembrance of the resurrection of Amalek to its doing and as dictated and these days are remembered and done. The third is the Parsha of a red cow, and it is on the Shabbat that precedes the Parsha of the month, since it was burned in the desert near Nissan in order to delude Israel with the ashes of the sin immediately after the construction of the Mishkan, so that they would be pure and be able to make Passover at the time.
