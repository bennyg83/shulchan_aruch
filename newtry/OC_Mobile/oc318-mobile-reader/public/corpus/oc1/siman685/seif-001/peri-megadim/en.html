Parashas Shekalim. See Turei Zahav. The Gemara in Megillah 29b says that we advance it thirty days beforehand according to the Rabbis who disagree with Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel; and even according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, the money changers sit on the fifteenth, see there. A yotzer is recited for Shacharis and also for Mussaf on Shekalim and Hachodesh. But for Zachor and Parah they did not institute one for Mussaf; see Levush and Eliyah Rabbah 14. When Rosh Chodesh Adar falls on Shabbos, we read Rosh Chodesh first, because it is more frequent, and afterward Shekalim; the haftarah is that of Shekalim, and we set aside the haftarah of Rosh Chodesh. See Eliyah Rabbah 14 in the name of Shibolei HaLeket, that the four parshiyos are fixed from our teachers; see there. See also siman 681 and siman 684. In Rambam, Laws of the Daily Offerings, chapter 6, Rosh Chodesh overrides that of Shabbos in order to know that the month was established in its proper time. Nevertheless, here they have already read the Rosh Chodesh reading in the Torah.
