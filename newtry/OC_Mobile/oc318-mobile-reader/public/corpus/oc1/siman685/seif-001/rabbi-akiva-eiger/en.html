Section 1. "Parashas Shekalim, which is Ki Sisa." If they forgot and did not read Parashas Shekalim, they take out a Torah scroll and read Parashas Shekalim with a blessing before and after it. If they did not remember until after Shabbos, it has no compensation. Even if Rosh Chodesh Adar is on Friday, so that the next day is an interruption, nevertheless, once the proper time according to the Sages' enactment has passed, they no longer read it; Responsa Ginas Veradim, Orach Chaim, siman 36.
