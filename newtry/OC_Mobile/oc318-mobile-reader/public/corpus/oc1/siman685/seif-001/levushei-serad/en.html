Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. "And it does not appear to me; the correct text, etc." His intent is that regarding what we say at the beginning of chapter 2 of Berachos, that the whole Torah was said in the Holy Tongue, Tosafos wrote this language: Rashi explained this as referring to reading from the Torah, but this is not clear, for that is only the enactment of Ezra and not Torah-level. One can say that it speaks of Parashas Zachor; end quote. Terumas HaDeshen brought in the name of the abbreviated Tosafos that it is written there "also Parashas Zachor and Parashas Parah." If so, they agreed that Parashas Parah is also Torah-level. But Magen Avraham holds that regarding Parashas Parah there is no reasoning at all that it should be Torah-level. Therefore he wrote that the correct text in the abbreviated Tosafos is like the language of the novellae of the Rashba, who also wrote regarding Rashi's words above in this language: and they challenged that we have not found Torah reading to be Torah-level, unless one says that Rashi, etc. If so, because of the pressure they wanted to say that perhaps this is Rashi's view, but they themselves do not hold at all that Parashas Parah is Torah-level, and perhaps they do not hold so even regarding Parashas Zachor. This appears to me to be the intent of Magen Avraham.
