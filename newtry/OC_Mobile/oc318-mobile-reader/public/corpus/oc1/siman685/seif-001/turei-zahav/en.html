Parashas Shekalim. The verse states, “This is the burnt offering of each month in its month.” The Torah thereby teaches: renew the fund and bring the communal offering from the new contribution. We derive through a gezeirah shavah that this month is Nissan. Since in Nissan the offerings must be brought from the new contribution, the announcement is made on the first of Adar so that the shekalim will reach the Beis HaMikdash. Afterward comes Parashas Zachor, before the reading of the Megillah on Purim, so that remembrance should precede performance, as it is written, “remembered and performed.” Afterward comes Parashas Parah, in order to remind those who are impure of the warning concerning Pesach.
