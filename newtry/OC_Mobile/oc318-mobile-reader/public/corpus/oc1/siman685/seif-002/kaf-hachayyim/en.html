Rosh Chodesh Adar, etc., that falls on Shabbos, etc. Meaning, the second day of Rosh Chodesh, which is 1 Adar adjacent to Nissan, falls on Shabbos; on that day we read Parashas Shekalim, etc. This accords with what we learned in the Mishnah, 'On 1 Adar they make announcements concerning the shekalim,' meaning that on that day they read Parashas Shekalim, to announce it publicly when it falls on Shabbos.
