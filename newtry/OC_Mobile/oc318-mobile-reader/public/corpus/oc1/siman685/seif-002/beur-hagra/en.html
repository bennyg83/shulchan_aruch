Section 2: On the second Shabbos. This is from the Mishnah and Gemara there, where we learned: on the second, etc.; and we rule like Rav.
We take out, etc., with another. Like Rabbi Yirmeyah, for Rabbi Yitzchak and Abaye hold like him, and a baraisa supports him, as Rabbi Yitzchak says: when Rosh Chodesh Teves and Adar fall, etc.; if it falls on that, etc.; if it falls, etc. This is a support for Abaye, etc. And even though Abaye said according to Rabbi Eliezer, he retracted here.
And he reads the haftarah. This is from the baraisa there.
