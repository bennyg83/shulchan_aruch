Obligated from Torah. Tur HaShulchan siman 108: must be careful reading in congregation more than Megillah reading — although Megillah preferable and every mitzvah deferred before it; per most poskim Megillah read even individually; Parashat Zachor — Ashrei three who ate: positive mitzvah d'oraisa read with ten; defers Torah prohibition always serve them — therefore R' Elazar freed slave to complete ten. Tosafot Shantz: no Torah reading except Zachor; Tosafot Ketuvot Berachot chapter 2: Zachor and Parah obligatory from Torah — end. Rashal feared from this minor does not ascend maftir Zachor — although shaliach tzibbur reads Torah — what of it? we require bar chiyuva to ascend; he also reads Torah — only shaliach reads loud not to embarrass; also Parah be careful since those Tosafot imply d'oraisa; as I found in Chiddushei Mordechai — end; Moharach z"l wrote incorrectly — for minor who knows for whom they bless certainly permitted although minor reads loud — minor counts for minyan of seven; Torah reading every Shabbat d'oraisa from Moshe spoke the appointed times, etc. — Yerushalmi, brought Rif chapter HaKorei Omed — only no fixed order per reading except Zachor; Ezra fixed order for all readings; even minor not knowing for whom they bless — why not fulfill through shaliach tzibbur's Zachor reading — what difference ascending to Torah if minor — primary obligation they hear Torah reading — end his words. In my humble opinion minor not knowing for whom they bless — do not fulfill blessing through him nor reading without blessing — therefore do not call minor maftir not knowing; but knowing — appears they fulfill; siman 282 Rama ruled minor may read additional or four Adar portions — appears when knows for whom they bless.
