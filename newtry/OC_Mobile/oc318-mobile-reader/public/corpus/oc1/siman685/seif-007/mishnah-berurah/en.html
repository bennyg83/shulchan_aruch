14) That Parashas Zachor. For it is written, "Remember what Amalek did, etc.," and we learn in the Gemara that this "remember" means specifically by speech from a scroll, and it is not enough that one fix it in his heart. Therefore the reader must intend to discharge all the listeners, and the listeners must intend to fulfill.
15) And Parashas Parah Adumah. Many later authorities wrote that this section is not Torah-level. See above in siman 60, seif katan 10 in Mishnah Berurah, that even for a rabbinic mitzvah one must also intend to fulfill the mitzvah.
16) To a place where there is a minyan, etc. This means even if he has a Torah scroll in his house but does not have a minyan in his house. Even if we say that a minyan for Parashas Zachor is only rabbinic, in any case the main reading of Zachor is Torah-level. Terumas HaDeshen wrote that hearing Parashas Zachor read with ten is more important than reading the megillah publicly, for according to most poskim, reading the megillah in its proper time suffices individually. Therefore, if he cannot fulfill both, he should seek to fulfill Parashas Zachor publicly. But Magen Avraham inclines that it is better to come to a place where they read the megillah publicly, and regarding Parashas Zachor he can fulfill, in pressing circumstances, through hearing the reading on Purim of Parashas Vayavo Amalek, for through this too he remembers the deed of Amalek and fulfills his obligation. That is to say, he fulfills Torah-level. Since we hold above in siman 60 that mitzvos require intent, at the time of hearing this section he would need to intend to fulfill the positive commandment of Zachor through it. But in my humble opinion, the main ruling of Magen Avraham requires analysis, for in the Torah it is written: "Remember what Amalek did to you, etc., how he met you on the way and cut off your rear, etc.; you shall erase, etc." The intent is not to forget what Amalek did to us, and that we tell this to our children and to our generations, saying to them, "such the wicked one did to us," and therefore we were commanded to erase his name, as the Ramban wrote in his explanation. This is not mentioned in Parashas Vayavo Amalek.
17) Nevertheless, they should be careful to read them, etc. He should seek to read this from a scroll, as above in seif katan 14.
18) With their melody and cantillation. Know that some say one must read "zecher Amalek" with a tzeirei, and some say one must read "zecher Amalek" with a segol. Therefore it is proper for the reader to read both, to fulfill the obligation according to Heaven. Maharil wrote that on all four special sections, we do not mention souls and we say Tzidkascha Tzedek, Darkei Moshe. Regarding Av HaRachamim, in Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Hagahos Minhagim it says that one should say Av HaRachamim on them, but in Darkei Moshe in the name of Maharil it implies that we do not say Av HaRachamim on them; so copied Derech HaChaim. Regarding the fast of Shovavim Tat, see Baer Hetev and Shaarei Teshuvah.
