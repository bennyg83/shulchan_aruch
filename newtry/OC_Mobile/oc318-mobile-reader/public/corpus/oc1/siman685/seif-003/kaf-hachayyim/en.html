3) Ibid. We read Parashas Shekalim, etc. For the verse states, 'This is the burnt offering of each month in its month.' The Torah said: Renew, and bring the offering from the new terumah. We derive through a gezeirah shavah that this month is Nissan. Since in Nissan the offering must be brought from the new terumah, we precede it on 1 Adar, so that the shekalim will reach the Beis HaMikdash. Afterwards comes Parashas Zachor, before the reading of the Megillah on Purim, so that remembrance should precede action, as it is written, 'remembered and performed.' Afterwards comes Parashas Parah, in order to remind those who are tamei of the warning concerning Pesach. Taz, s.k. 1. In Tikkun Yissachar, 72b, he wrote another reason in the name of Maseches Soferim: Since the Holy One, blessed is He, foresaw that Haman would weigh out shekalim against the Jewish people, He therefore caused their shekalim to precede his shekalim.
