Shekels, etc. Damer recited this as a month's offering in the new Torah, said a new Torah and brought a sacrifice from a new contribution, and Gamarinan in the Gash that this new contribution is Nisan, and since in the Nisan in the city of Akarobi, from a new contribution, they preceded it in A. And in the correction of Issachar, the Gemara says that the Holy One will pay shekels to Israel, therefore he predates their shekels.
