13) For you. Until "you shall eat matzos." When Rosh Chodesh Nisan falls on Shabbos, which is then Parashas HaChodesh, they read the haftarah of Parashas HaChodesh, not "The heavens are My throne," see Taz in the name of Levush. They take out three scrolls. In one they read the weekly portion, then they place the second and do not say Kaddish; they lift the first, roll it, open the second, and read the Rosh Chodesh portion from "and on the Shabbos day" until "and its libation." They then place the third and say Kaddish, lift the second and roll it, open the third, and read the maftir in Parashas HaChodesh, Shaarei Ephraim.
