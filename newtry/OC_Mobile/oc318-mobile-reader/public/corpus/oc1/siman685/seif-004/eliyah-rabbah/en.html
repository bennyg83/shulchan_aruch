Levush: The Shabbos of its Rosh Chodesh, etc. I found written: whenever Rosh Chodesh Shevat falls on Wednesday, there is great cold and snow that winter. The sign is Vayigash, whose initial letters indicate Friday, the third of Shevat, for then it is so, tested and proven; and one who feared the word of Hashem made his servants flee, etc. This is from Aizik Darshan.
