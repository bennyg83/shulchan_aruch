Kiddush was instituted at the place of a meal, and a verbal declaration alone does not fulfill the ordinance when no meal follows. If bread is available but wine is not, Kiddush is recited over bread. If the local beverage has the status of chamar medinah, it may be used in the circumstances explained by the authorities. One should not omit the Shabbos meal merely because wine is unavailable. The obligation of Kiddush and the obligation of the meal are related but distinct, and the blessing must be arranged according to what is actually available. The cited responsa discuss a traveler, a poor person, and one in a place where neither wine nor an accepted local beverage can be obtained. In each case, one should preserve the sanctity of Shabbos through prayer and the meal to the fullest extent possible, without reciting a blessing over an unsuitable beverage. See siman 57.
