7) There: Nor over uncovered wine, etc. It is possible that there is no objection if it remained uncovered only briefly. Even governors and officials would not object after the fact unless its aroma and taste deteriorated. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 3; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1; Chayei Adam, klal 6, paragraph 7; Chesed LaAlafim, paragraph 1. Beis Yehudah, siman 43, similarly writes that it becomes invalid only if it remained uncovered long enough for its aroma and taste to deteriorate, or if it remained uncovered overnight; see there. This implies that if it remained uncovered overnight, it is invalid for Kiddush even if its aroma and taste did not deteriorate. Ben Ish Chai, parashas Bereishis, paragraph 25, writes the same. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 1, however, writes that one should object even if it remained uncovered briefly; see there. Therefore, ab initio one should take care not to leave it uncovered even briefly. After the fact, it should be disqualified only if its aroma and taste deteriorated or it remained uncovered overnight.
