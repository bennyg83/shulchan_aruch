8) Beis Yehudah writes there that it is obvious there is no distinction between Kiddush and Havdalah in this regard. This is clear not only according to Rambam, who maintains at the beginning of chapter 29 that Havdalah is also a Torah obligation, but even according to the opinion that it is a tradition from the Sages. Whenever the Sages instituted a law, they patterned it after Torah law; see there. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 1, cites this.
