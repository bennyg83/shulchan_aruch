(7) Specks. Meaning white specks. If it has a white film, however, one may not recite Kiddush over it. Eliyah Rabbah writes that once the film has been removed, one need not be stringent.
(8) Sweet. The unqualified wording includes both grapes over-ripened by the sun, making the wine extremely sweet, and wine naturally very sweet from the fruit itself. Such wine is inferior and invalid for libations, yet valid for Kiddush. See Biur Halachah.
(9) Nevertheless, it is a mitzvah, etc. Although one may initially recite Kiddush over all these wines, the preferred mitzvah is nevertheless to select good wine. Therefore, one should not take wine indiscriminately from the cellar for Kiddush before first checking that it has not spoiled.
