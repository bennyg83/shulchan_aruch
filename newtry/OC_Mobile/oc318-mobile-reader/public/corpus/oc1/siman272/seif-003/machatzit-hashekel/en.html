(Seif katan 3) Wine, etc. Siman 600, where the Magen Avraham wrote in the name of the Darchei Moshe and the Minhagim that on the second night of Rosh Hashanah, at the time of kiddush, one should wear a new garment or have a new fruit for the blessing of Shehecheyanu. However, if he has new must, he should recite kiddush over old wine, and when he reaches Shehecheyanu he should take the must in his hand. Although one may recite kiddush over must, nevertheless old wine is the preferred mitzvah; thus far the words of the Magen Avraham.
