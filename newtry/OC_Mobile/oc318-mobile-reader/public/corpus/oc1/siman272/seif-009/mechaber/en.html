In a place where wine is not commonly available, some say that Kiddush may be recited over beer and other beverages, except water; others say that Kiddush may not be recited over them. According to the Rosh, at night one should not recite Kiddush over beer but rather over bread. In the morning it is preferable to recite Kiddush over beer so that he will recite SheHakol before HaMotzi, because if he first recites Kiddush over bread there is no recognizable change from an ordinary meal. These are reasonable words. {Rama: The widespread custom follows the Rosh. If wine is available in the city, he should not recite Kiddush over bread. One who does not drink wine because of a vow may recite Kiddush over wine and have the others reclining with him drink it. If no others are with him, he should recite Kiddush over bread rather than wine, or hear Kiddush from others. [Hagahot Maimoniyot, chapter 29; Responsa Maimoniyot, Sefer Hafla'ah, siman 4]}
