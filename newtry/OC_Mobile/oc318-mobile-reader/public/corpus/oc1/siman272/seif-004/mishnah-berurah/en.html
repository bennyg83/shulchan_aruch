(10) Over white wine, etc. According to all opinions, one should initially seek red wine. If he has no red wine, or it is not as choice, this opinion permits initially reciting Kiddush over white wine.
(11) But one may recite Havdalah over it. If it is chamar medinah there, meaning everyone drinks it. The reason is that it is no worse than beer, over which one may recite Havdalah when it is chamar medinah, as stated below in siman 296:2. For the same reason, according to all opinions one may use it for daytime Kiddush, since the later authorities state below in se'if 9 that the custom is lenient by day with any beverage that is chamar medinah.
(12) Like the first view. Eliyah Rabbah implies that when wine is excessively white, it is proper to heed the Ramban and not recite Kiddush over it except under pressure, when no red wine is available.
