4) There: Kiddush is not recited over wine with a foul odor, etc. Even if no other wine is available and only this wine with a somewhat foul odor can be obtained, Kiddush is not recited over it. Responsa Shaagas Aryeh, siman 18; Beis Menuchah, paragraph 1. If it was mixed with other wine and thereby corrected so that its foul odor disappeared, however, Kiddush may be recited over it. Petach HaDevir, paragraph 1.
