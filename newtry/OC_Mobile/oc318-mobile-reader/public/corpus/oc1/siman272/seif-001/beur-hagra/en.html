Seif 1 Although its smell. As with libations, where it is invalid; for otherwise, without this too it is invalid, like souring wine, according to the dispute of Rabbi Yochanan and Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi, and regarding libations they agree that it is invalid.
Even nowadays. As in their days, with a strainer. There.
And one does not make kiddush. Tur. Since we hold that everything follows the taste. But it is a matter that is not so, as Tosafot wrote there, 96a, s.v. ha-bodek, etc.
