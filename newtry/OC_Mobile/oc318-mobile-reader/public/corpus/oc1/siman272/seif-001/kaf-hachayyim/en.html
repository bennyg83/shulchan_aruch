1) [Seif 1] Kiddush is not recited over wine with a foul odor, etc. Kiddush is recited only over wine fit to be poured upon the mizbe'ach. This excludes wine with a foul odor, meaning that it is somewhat malodorous, and uncovered wine. Tur. Perishah, paragraph 1, writes that this principle is stated specifically to exclude the cases enumerated here. One should not derive from it that Kiddush may not be recited over everything unfit for libation upon the mizbe'ach, because Tur concludes that Kiddush may be recited over raisin wine and cooked wine; see there. This applies specifically according to Tur and the Shulchan Aruch. Rambam, chapter 29, halachah 14, however, rules that it excludes everything unfit for libation upon the mizbe'ach; see there. We maintain the view of Tur and the Shulchan Aruch, which excludes only these cases.
