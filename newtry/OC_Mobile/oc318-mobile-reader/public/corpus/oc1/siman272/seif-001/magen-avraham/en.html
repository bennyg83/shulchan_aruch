If on Friday he is in a place where wine is available, but he knows that he will have no wine on Shabbos (for example, he cannot transport it to his home) he should recite Kiddush over the wine before nightfall and drink it. He is then prohibited from performing labor. Shiltei HaGibborim in the name of a Gaon. See the beginning of siman 267 and the end of siman 293. Over uncovered wine. This is because of the verse, ‘Offer it, if you please, to your governor.’ It is possible that if it stood uncovered only briefly there is no objection, especially in these countries where wine is not so readily available and even governors and officials are not particular about it after the fact, unless its fragrance and taste have deteriorated.
And its taste is sour. This means that all people refrain from drinking it because of its sourness, as stated in siman 206. Maharshal did not write ‘many people,’ because he relied upon what was stated above. See Bach; his words do not appear compelling.
