Moisture. See Baer Heitev. Bechor Shor, page 121, column 3, writes that one must take care not to add so much water that, after the raisins swell, they constitute only one sixth of the water. In that case the blessing would certainly be recited in vain. See above, siman 202, and what is written in Yad Ephraim concerning the proper blessing in this case.
