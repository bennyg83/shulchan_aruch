(35) Exempts. Since the Kiddush wine is in the meal-place, it is a meal need and comes on account of the meal.
(36) During the meal. From the blessing of Borei Pri HaGefen. It likewise exempts wine drunk after the meal but before Birkas HaMazon.
(37) For Birkas HaMazon exempts it. The Kiddush cup is also included among things that come on account of the meal.
(38) See above, siman 174. There it is explained that even if he had no wine at all during the meal, Birkas HaMazon nevertheless exempts the Kiddush cup.
