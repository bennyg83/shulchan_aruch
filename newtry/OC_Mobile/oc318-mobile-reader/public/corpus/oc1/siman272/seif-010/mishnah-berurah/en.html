(35) Exempts - Since it is in the place of the meal, it is a need of the meal and comes because of it.
(36) Within the meal - From the blessing borei peri hagafen; the same applies that it exempts wine after the meal before Birkat Hamazon.
(37) For Birkat Hamazon exempts it - For the Kiddush cup is also included among things that come because of the meal.
(38) And see above siman 174 - Meaning, it is explained there that even if he had no wine at all during the meal, Birkat Hamazon still exempts the Kiddush cup.
