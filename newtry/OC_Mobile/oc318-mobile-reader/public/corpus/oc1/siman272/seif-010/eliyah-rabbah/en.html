[10] [Levush] Even strong, etc. — so wrote Tur in the name of Baal HaHalakhot Gedolot; this is Beit Yosef's wording — he appears to write thus from the baraita: we do not bless on wine until water is put in it — R' Elazar; and the sages say we bless, and the sages agree on a fourth blessing cup for a preferred mitzvah that water be put in. It implies all this is only when it is so strong it is unfit to drink raw; but if it stands one-to-three with water one may drink it — end of his words. It is a great wonder he did not see what Semag wrote Esin perek 9: Baal HaHalakhot Gedolot rules like the sages who bless on wine that stands one-to-three — thus explicitly R' Eliezer and the sages disagree on this wine. It seems Beit Yosef found it difficult why Baal HaHalakhot Gedolot permitted even initially when the sages did not permit initially. In my humble opinion it is not difficult: for the sages' permission is only for a preferred mitzvah — called permission l'chatchila without water for one not fulfilling the preferred mitzvah; and that he concluded 'nevertheless it is better to dilute' is for the preferred mitzvah; so I found explicit in Baal HaHalakhot Gedolot itself p. 13. Bach also wondered there on Beit Yosef and did not notice the wonder above; also his long discussion that Baal HaHalakhot Gedolot learned from chapter HaMocher — see there at length.
