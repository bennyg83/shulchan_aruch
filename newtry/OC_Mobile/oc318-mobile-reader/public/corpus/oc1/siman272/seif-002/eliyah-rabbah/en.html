From the winepress, etc., and one makes kiddush, etc. Maharshal did so even though aged wine was available for sale, as his derashot siman 70 record, cited by Malbushei Yom Tov. This challenges Magen Avraham's statement that aged wine is a mitzvah preference as in siman 600. In truth, there it appears to me that if one already has aged wine and intends to drink it, it has precedence. One who has no aged wine is not obligated to purchase it specifically for kiddush.
