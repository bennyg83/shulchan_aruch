2) There: Kiddush is not recited over wine with a foul odor, etc. Rashbam explains that although the blessing Borei Peri HaGafen is recited over it, because its aroma and taste are those of wine, it is somewhat malodorous because it was stored in a repulsive vessel. It is nevertheless invalid for Kiddush. Since it is invalid for libation upon the mizbe'ach because of the principle, 'Present it, if you please, to your governor,' it is likewise invalid for Kiddush for the same reason. These are his words, cited by Bach.
