Some say: even the length of the right strap until the navel and of the left until the chest — they have no remedy through tying or sewing, as if what encircles from the head were severed (Beit Yosef in the name of Sefer HaTerumot). And wherever it is permitted to sew the retzuot, one may sew with whatever one wishes or tie, and one need not use gidin specifically (Beit Yosef according to Rosh, and not as R' Yehuda wrote). Some say: these additions of measure as well — only tying disqualifies, because there is no adornment for tefillin to be tied; but a straight sewing on the inner side so it is not seen from outside, since there is not such disgrace for tefillin, does not disqualify even these additions of the mentioned measures; and one should be strict (Mahari Rama); but in time of pressing need one may be lenient.
