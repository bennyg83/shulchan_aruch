If the retzua was severed — some permit sewing it on the inner side. And some say: what encircles from it the head, and on the hand enough to encircle the arm to bind the tefillin with the arm and enough to extend until the middle finger and wrap from it on that finger three wrappings and tie — these have no remedy, neither by tying nor by sewing. And all the additional length that is for wrapping the retzua several times around the arm, and on the head what hangs from it — sewing and tying do not invalidate them. And in a pressing case one may rely on those who are lenient so that one not nullify the mitzvah of tefillin.
