That which encircles, etc. I have room to study this, for in the Gemara it states: Abaye was standing before R' Yehuda; the retzua of tefillin was severed for him. He said to him: What is the law regarding tying it? He said to him: "And you shall bind them" — that the binding be complete (and Rashi explained: that the knot be beautiful; and if there is another knot in the retzua besides the knot with which they tie on the head, it is not a complete and beautiful knot. Others explain: a complete knot — a whole retzua and not tied). Rav Acha said to Rav Ashi: What is the law regarding sewing it and inserting the sewing into the ma'barta? He said to him: Go out and see what the public does. Rashi explained: and since they do not practice thus, do not do it; and Rabbeinu Tam explained to permit, and this is simple like Rashi. Remnants of a retzua are valid — Rashi explained leftovers of leftovers: what hangs from the retzua after they are tied on the head — if it was severed from those remnants and giddumin remain, they are valid; but this is not so, for we say giddumei techelet are valid there specifically because they are tashmishei mitzvah, but here they are tashmishei kedushah. And he challenged from the rule that he holds a measure for hanging the retzuot apart from the knot — for remnants and giddumin appear in them. And what is the measure? One tefach — meaning the length of the retzuot should exceed the knot as much as one can stretch from the bulk to the finger; and Rosh disputes Rashi and wrote that from Rashi's words it seems he refers to the head retzua, but this is not so; rather, per Aruch's explanation it refers to shel yad — until the retzua reaches the middle finger, etc.; and for head retzuot he did not explain a measure. In Shimu'sha Rabbah he explained the measure, and the measure of Shimu'sha Rabbah is: for the right until the navel and for the left until the chest. And further Rosh wrote there regarding what R' Yehuda forbade tying to Abaye: it appears he discusses when the retzua encircling the head and arm was severed — there it requires a complete knot so there not be two knots; but the retzuot hanging below are not subject to a complete knot, end quote. And it is clear from his words that although the measure of the shel yad retzua is until the middle finger, nonetheless tying disqualifies only within the measure of the knot that encircles the hand and head; from there onward tying is permitted. And in Tur siman 47 he brings Rosh's view on this and wrote afterward: however in Shimu'usha Rabbah he wrote: therefore one should be strict and hang the shel rosh retzuot before him — right until the navel and left until the chest; and the shel yad retzua after tying on the arm should be long until the middle finger, and he wraps it around three times, etc. If the retzua was severed and insufficient measure remains as he explained, there is no remedy through tying or sewing. And it is explicit from his words that the prohibition of tying and sewing applies also outside the head and hand encirclement — for the entire length measure that the geonim explained for shel yad and shel rosh, and not like Rosh. Zero — it seems one should not forbid on the arm regarding the length of the measure except if he would stretch the retzua to the finger without wrapping; but any extra length for wrapping the retzua several times around the arm — one need not be particular about tying and sewing at all; and so I instructed several times to permit, and my heart did not fill me to permit even within the measure. However, I permitted from the measure outward to his knot, per the Ashkenazi view, since Rabbeinu Tam permits even within the knot — for the Talmud ruled to insert sewing into the ma'barta; it is difficult to be careful about the matter per Magen Avraham's explanation that one cannot insert sewing outside the knot within the ma'barta. And per Rashi too it is difficult to sew even on the inner side so the sewing not be very visible from outside; therefore one should not be lenient, end quote. And it seems clear there is a scribe's error at the end of his words; and thus it should read: however, I permitted from the knot outward to sew it, per the Ashkenazi view, since Rabbeinu Tam permits even within the knot — for the Talmud ruled to insert sewing into the ma'barta — but it is difficult to be careful about the matter, etc.; and his intention is that although the measure of shel yad reaches the middle finger, nonetheless regarding sewing one may be lenient like Rosh, since Rabbeinu Tam permits sewing even within the knot — but it is difficult to be careful about sewing, whether per Magen Avraham or per Rashi; therefore one should not be lenient even for sewing within the measure — such is my understanding of his intent. And accordingly, even for shel rosh one should not be lenient with tying, and even with sewing, whenever it is within the measure of the retzuot — meaning of the right until the navel and of the left until the chest. And it seems Sefer HaTerumah explains that which he said "complete knot" means a whole retzua — which is Rashi's second explanation above; and if so, it is difficult why Shulchan Aruch wrote here regarding shel yad that tying and sewing disqualify until it reaches the finger, etc., and regarding shel rosh he wrote only until one leaves the encirclement — and this is not like those who hold that even for shel yad it suffices with encirclement around the kiboret alone; and if per Sefer HaTerumah, also for shel rosh we require until navel and chest — and such is implied by Taz's conclusion, and so too from Rambam ch. 2: there is no distinction between shel rosh and shel yad — in both, tying and sewing within the measure do not help. And I wonder further: for in Beit Yosef he wrote on this that we listen to be strict only where it is impossible — one may rely on the lenient words so as not to nullify the mitzvah of tefillin; from which it implies that where possible we worry to be strict even for shel rosh within the measure; yet here in Shulchan Aruch he did not write any view to be strict for shel rosh, only concludes that where impossible one may rely on Rabbeinu Tam regarding sewing, which helps everywhere — safek.

So that it not be nullified, etc. It seems simple that in any case he should not bless on them, for it is possible for him to fulfill the mitzvah, and blessings are not me'akev.
