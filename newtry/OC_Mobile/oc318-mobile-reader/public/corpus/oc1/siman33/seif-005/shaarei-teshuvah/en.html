Leshmo. Be'er Heitev — he preceded the order and preceded the responsum of Dvar Shmuel and afterward Bach; but the reverse is true, for Bach came out first on this matter: if the retzuot were worked not leshmah, whether Israel blackening them leshmo helps; but the geonim — author of Dvar Shmuel, author of Gedolei Terumah, Mahari Levi, and all the greats of Venice and Italy — signed a ruling printed in Dvar Shmuel siman 19: God forbid to do so; the retzuot are invalid and one did not fulfill the eighteen [requirements] in blackening leshmah, see there. And see responsa Noda BiYehudah, Mahadura Tinyana siman 50, regarding retzuot of tefillin that grease the hides with fish fat called pishtran — there is no concern of "forbidden in your mouth," since the greasing is not the main part of the black color but only to brighten the color visually and soften the hide somewhat; and if they were not soft they would also be black and kosher for retzuot. If the black color itself came from something impure — safek. Techelet that is from chilazon depends on the dispute of Rashi and Rambam, see there.
