(Seif katan 5) It is proper, etc. This implies that he maintains they are valid after the fact. Meaning, even the straps, since this entire siman discusses straps and Beit Yosef's ruling refers to them.
We find several similar instances. Since the matter is not stated explicitly, and it is possible, though strained, to explain Beit Yosef as referring only to the batim, Rama did not formulate the dissent as “some say.”
