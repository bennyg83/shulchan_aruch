**If it was severed, etc., and for the hand [retzua] so that it encircles, etc.** — Machatzit Hashekel wrote that after sewing one must first stretch the complete retzua straight to the finger, then return with the tied retzua below and wrap the arm as customary — until the finger must be without a knot. In Sefer Shenot Chaim to the gaon Maharash Kluger he wrote people do not practice his words and the law is with them — see there; also in responsum of Maharam of Rothenburg, acharonim, siman 4, he challenged him: the main concern is the measure remaining to stretch and wrap; and even after wrapping around the arm the knot remains above the finger — we are not concerned; but this was uncertain — that the knot not be on the finger itself, since tying applies there too — see there at length. Nonetheless in my humble opinion lekhatchilah it is good to sew that place with gidin on the inner side so it not be recognizable; for even if we are concerned for Machatzit Hashekel's stringency that this is called within the measure, nonetheless it seems plain we may rely on Rabbeinu Tam and other rishonim who are lenient regarding sewing. And know: that some join the retzua with a loop without sewing is not better than a knot — and possibly inferior.

**Sewing and tying do not count, etc.** — nonetheless there is practical difference between head and hand: for the head, if it remains so and he does not attach the severed retzua — neither by knot nor sewing — he certainly may not bless on the tefillin, and we cannot validate with what remains — scraps, i.e. the second retzua; as with tzitzit above in siman 12 — for we conclude in Menachot 35b that for retzuot, scraps are invalid [nonetheless he must lay tefillin if the retzua was lost and he has no advice — perhaps the halachah follows views that hold "this and its measures" refers to the hand retzua — see Beit Yosef above in siman 27; but in truth here he has another advice: he can untie the knot and pull the retzua to both sides, and in pressing need he can fulfill thus if two handbreadths remain on each side — see there in siman 27 in Mishnah Berurah. Nonetheless practical difference from all this: if both retzuot were severed on both sides and only what encircles the head remains]. For the hand, even if he does not attach at all, he may bless on tefillin, as above in siman 27, seif 8. Accordingly sewing certainly helps — even with threads and on the outside, or a knot — but all this if he did not make the seven wrap rounds; and for us, whose custom is to make wrap rounds, we already wrote it is good to sew them with gidin on the inner side to avoid Machatzit Hashekel's concern.
