**65: And some say, etc.** In Rashi's commentary there, and so too in Yerushalmi there — R' Yirmiyah's retzua of tefillin [was severed]; he asked R' Abba bar Mamal; he told him "and you shall tie" is a complete knot, and sewing was not mentioned there. Rosh and Rashba — and it is not necessary. And that Yerushalmi case was already explained as referring to the gidin of the sewing of the batim, as above in 62; and the Rosh wrote that specifically regarding what encircles the head and arm is invalid — upon those "and you shall tie" refers, which is the tightening, as stated above in siman 25; but regarding what hangs, invalidation does not apply, since the rabbi there requires saying scraps are valid; but in Sefer HaTerumah he wrote that for the entire length it is invalid — but in Beit Yosef he was uncertain whether also for the head [retzua], and it seems his reason is that the hand [retzua] is explicit in the Gemara, not so the head [retzua], per Tosafot; and this is what Shulchan Aruch wrote, "that encircles," etc.; but in Sefer HaTerumah it implies there is no distinction between hand and head retzuot, as Terumat HaDeshen wrote in his name; see Taz.
