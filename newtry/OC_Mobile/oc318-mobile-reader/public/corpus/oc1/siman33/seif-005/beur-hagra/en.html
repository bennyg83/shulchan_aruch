Seif 5: Some say, etc. This follows Rashi's explanation there, and the Yerushalmi likewise implies it. Rabbi Yirmiyah's tefillin strap tore. He asked Rabbi Abba bar Mamal, who told him: “You shall bind them,” requiring a complete binding. Sewing is not mentioned there. Rosh and the other poskim adopt this view, though the proof is not conclusive. The earlier Yerushalmi passage has already been explained as referring to the sinews with which the batim are sewn, as above in seif 2. Rosh writes that the disqualification applies specifically to the section that encircles the head or the arm, because “you shall bind them” refers to the fastening, as explained above in siman 25. The hanging portions are not included, for Rav Pappa initially wished to say there that remnants are valid. Sefer HaTerumah, however, writes that the strap is invalid throughout its required length. Beit Yosef is uncertain whether this applies equally to the tefillin of the head. It appears to me that his uncertainty arises because the required length is explicit in the Gemara only concerning the tefillin of the arm, not the tefillin of the head, in accordance with Tosafos' explanation. This is why Shulchan Aruch writes, “the part that encircles, etc.” Sefer HaTerumah, however, implies that there is no distinction between the tefillin of the arm and those of the head, as Terumas HaDeshen writes in his name.
