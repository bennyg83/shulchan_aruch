**To sew.** Specifically with gidin and specifically to sew on the inner side; but to tie or to sew on the outer side is invalid; and if the ma'barta was severed, one may sew it — Darchei Shmuel siman 211.

**Finger.** Meaning the strap should stretch straight without wrappings; and Taz is strict l'chatchila: even if the strap was severed after being tied from it around the head, only what hangs before him until the navel on the right strap and on the left until the chest — it is forbidden to sew along its length.

**Will be nullified.** And he will not bless, for blessings are not me'akev. Taz. And see in responsa Shevut Yaakov vol. 1 siman 1 and on above siman 9 s.k. 6 from Shmuel Shmuel. {Rama: And it is written in Eliyahu Rabbah that specifically what encircles the head and arm; but what hangs from it is permitted b'dieved through sewing even not in time of pressing need; and what Beit Yosef wrote from Rambam implying it is forbidden — that is not implied so in Kesef Mishneh; and likewise ruled in Sefer Zichron to R' Yehuda HaKohen.}
