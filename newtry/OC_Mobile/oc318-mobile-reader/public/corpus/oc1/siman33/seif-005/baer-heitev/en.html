To sew. Specifically with sinews, and specifically when sewing on the inner side. If they are tied or sewn on the outer side, they are invalid. If the ma'abarta tore, it may be sewn. Devar Shmuel, siman 211.
A fingerbreadth. Meaning, the strap should be stretched out straight, without winding it around. Taz is stringent initially even where the strap tore after a sufficient portion had already been tied around the head, but the remaining lengths hanging in front, the right strap to the navel and the left strap to the chest, were torn. He rules that even these should not be sewn; see there at length.
He should forgo it. He should not recite a blessing, because blessings do not prevent fulfillment. Taz. See responsa Shevus Yaakov, part 1, siman 1, and above, siman 9, seif katan 6, and what is written there. Eliyah Rabbah writes that the prohibition applies specifically to the portion that encircles the head or the arm. The hanging portion may, after the fact, be repaired by sewing even when there is no pressing need. Beit Yosef writes that Rambam implies otherwise, but Kesef Mishneh does not indicate this. Sefer Zikaron by Rabbi Yosef HaKohen likewise rules leniently.
