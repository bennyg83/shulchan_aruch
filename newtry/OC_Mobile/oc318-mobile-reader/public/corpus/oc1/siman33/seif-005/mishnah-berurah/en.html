(25) Some permit — even within the measure that encircles the head and arm.

(26) To sew — specifically sewing; but tying within the aforementioned measure is invalid for all, m'd'Oraita, as it is written "and you shall bind them," and Chazal expounded a complete knot — meaning the retzua with which he ties the tefillin must be complete and not tied. Sewing too is specifically with gidin and not with threads. In Shmuel's responsum: if the ma'barta was severed, one may sew it.

(27) From the inner side — so that it not be recognizable from outside at all; and all the more if the place of sewing is tucked inside the ma'barta, it is permitted per this view.

(28) Finger — meaning the retzua should stretch without wrappings; this is the measure of length of the arm retzua, as above in siman 27 s.h.; and the reason he was lenient for the head retzua, see Darkei Moshe.

(29) The permitters — meaning it is permitted even to sew with gidin within the measure that encircles the head and arm; outside this measure it is permitted even to tie, and all the more to sew with threads; but in all this one may not bless, for fundamentally one must be strict like the first view — safek Torah l'chumra; but so as not to nullify the mitzvah entirely he should wear them until he finds others. See Taz, who rules that also for the head retzua one may not bless until the right strap reaches the navel and the left to the chest, whole, without tying or sewing; but Sefer Arev ruled halachah: both shel rosh and shel yad — except what is within the measure encircling the head and arm — one may sew even not b'dieved and bless, provided he be careful to sew from the inner side so the stitching is not recognizable from outside (if recognizable, tying is no better); and it must be gidin — threads or tying are forbidden even for shel rosh; likewise Yeshuot Yaakov and Derech HaChaim. Nonetheless, if he can obtain whole retzuot, it is proper to be strict l'chatchila like Taz, for many acharonim adopted his words halachically; but in pressing need he may rely on all the aforementioned geonim and bless — especially if for shel rosh two tefachim remain hanging besides what encircles the head; certainly one may rely leniently that sewing helps; see above siman 27 Mishnah Berurah s.k. 44. If the retzua was severed in width and insufficient measure remains, Pri Megadim left safek whether one may sew; see above siman 27 s.k. 42 what he wrote there.
