If he blackened them for the sake of the mitzvah, this does not remedy the fact that the leather was not processed for the sake of the mitzvah. Responsa Devar Shmuel, siman 15; see there. Responsa Be'er Eisek, siman 10, writes that blackening for the sake of the mitzvah is effective.
