The hide of the retzuot must be from the hide of a kosher domesticated animal, wild animal, or bird. And they must be processed for their sake. Retzuot, whether from hide or from parchment, are valid. It is halachah leMoshe miSinai that the retzuot be' black on the outside; but on the inside one may make them any color he wishes except red, lest people say they were dyed and reddened from the blood of his scabs.
