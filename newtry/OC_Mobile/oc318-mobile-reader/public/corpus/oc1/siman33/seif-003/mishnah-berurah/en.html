(16) The pure — for only pure [animals] were fit for work of Heaven. Gemara.

(17) And it is necessary — all this "it is necessary" is for invalidation even b'dieved.

(18) For their sake — and even the Rambam, who is lenient regarding batim as explained above in siman 32 seif 37, agrees here; and for this reason see Levush and Magen Avraham. Blackening also must be for their sake; and if one did not process them for their sake, it does not help even if he blackens them afterward for their sake [responsum of Devar Shmuel siman 7:15, see there; and in responsum Be'er Eshek he wrote that blackening for their sake does help; and see responsum of Re'em, who agreed with the ruling of Devar Shmuel above, and protested against those lenient in this — see there].

(19) Black — see in Baruch She'amar that from his words it appears it is a mitzvah to blacken them until they are black as a raven. And know that if they aged and the blackness wore off, one must blacken them anew, as above in siman 32 s.l. 39; and in the place where the knot is tightened it is very common for the blackness to wear off — one must be very careful about this.

(20) From the outside — at the side of the ma'barta, which is the smooth place. If he blackened them from the inside it does not help, and he must go back and blacken them from the outside; see Biur Halacha.

(21) "He shall make" — meaning if he wishes to dye them; but he is not obligated to dye them any color, for although per Rambam he must blacken them inside as well, like the batim are black, we do not follow Rambam here, as explained in Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe.
