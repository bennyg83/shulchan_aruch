(seif katan 3) "first," etc., and one bayit remains whole between them — meaning even if the bayit of kodesh was severed on the side adjacent to the bayit of vehayah ki yevi'acha, and the bayit of shema was severed on the side adjacent to the bayit of vehayah ki hayah, then there is no intervening partition between the tears except two partitions — nonetheless valid, since at least a whole bayit intervenes. But if the bayit of kodesh was severed on the outer side and the partition adjacent to vehayah ki yevi'acha is whole, and the bayit of vehayah ki yevi'acha was severed on the side adjacent to shema and the side facing kodesh remained whole — behold here too two whole partitions remained intervening between the tears — nonetheless invalid, since a whole bayit does not intervene.
