(Seif katan 3) The first, etc. One complete compartment remains between them. Meaning, even if the compartment containing Kadesh tore on the side adjacent to the compartment containing Vehayah Ki Yeviacha, and the compartment containing Shema tore on the side adjacent to Vehayah Im Shamoa, so that only two walls separate the tears, it is nevertheless valid because at least one complete compartment intervenes. If, however, the compartment containing Kadesh tore on its outer side, while the wall adjacent to Vehayah Ki Yeviacha remained whole, and the compartment containing Vehayah Ki Yeviacha tore on the side adjacent to Shema while the side facing Kadesh remained whole, then two complete walls likewise remain between the tears. Nevertheless, it is invalid because no complete compartment separates them.
