Magen Avraham, seif katan 3. “And one compartment remains.” His purpose is to prevent the mistaken interpretation that the law depends upon the walls, requiring two torn walls to be adjacent, unlike Taz in seif katan 3. Accordingly, one might have understood the Mechaber's phrase “the first and third” as referring also to the walls, so that even if no complete compartment separates the tears, the tefillin would be valid. Magen Avraham therefore clarifies that everything depends upon the compartments themselves, in agreement with Taz: whenever two adjacent compartments are torn, they are invalid even if the torn walls are not adjacent. A complete compartment must remain between them.
