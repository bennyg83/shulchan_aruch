**63: And it must be, etc.** Rambam and Magen Avraham — his words are not understood; and these are the words of Rambam in chapter 3: the hide of the retzua requires processing lishmah, but in the hide with which one covers [the batim] processing is not required at all — even if one made it from matzah hide it is valid. His reason: as they say in Shabbat 108b, 109a — hide fit to make an amulet, and there it covers, etc., as we learned, "hide," etc. — thus hide for an amulet is hide that is not processed, and even from matzah hide, as stated there in Beitzah; and they say in Eruvin 96b — would a person trouble himself, etc. — thus tefillin hide is amulet hide; thus we follow the first tanna in Menachot 42b, even though in tzitzit, per Keshe Muel, the sugya of the first chapter of Sukkah is like him, as explained above in 11; and the reason: since processing is not required, all the more so lishmah is not required — not so for spinning, since spinning is required, and as stated in Sdei Chemed in the name of Sefer HaTerumah regarding blackening; not so for retzuot, which are tanned black — they are processed. And this is what Magen Avraham wrote; and it seems to me, etc. — and I could have said that this is what Shulchan Aruch wrote in siman 31, seif 62, that retzuot are recognizable as not amulet [hide], as stated there; except that Rambam there adopted the view of Rif and Rosh that the knots also require recognizability, as stated. And the view of Nekudot Yosef at the end of chapter 6 of Sanhedrin is to the contrary — see there; and as stated in Mishnah Berurah seif katan 2, he holds like Tosafot in Menachot 42b — Rava like Rabban Gamliel, but processing helps since it is part of the matter itself; and as stated in Mishnah Berurah seif katan 2 in the gloss — if so, certainly designation is a matter; and it is difficult — if so, one who weaves a garment for the dead should also be like processing; and one could answer: Raza, first chapter of Sukkah — weaving is different, since it serves the matter; except that he wrote that the retzua too is a primary element of holiness. And the words of Rambam are apparently difficult — for he wrote that if a gentile covered or sewed them they are invalid because their making is like their writing because of the Divine Name; except that he follows his view that he does not require processing for every sacred writing — for a mezuzah he does not require processing, only in a Torah scroll and tefillin; and even in [other] books he does not require processing according to his explanation, as Tosafot wrote in Menachot 42b, s.v. "tefillin," and so Rambam explained, etc. And it is difficult, etc.; and perhaps here, etc.
