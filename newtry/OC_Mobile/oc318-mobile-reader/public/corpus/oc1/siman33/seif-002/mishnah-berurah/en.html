(12) According to Rambam. Other poskim disagree and are lenient in every case where two stitches were severed. Where three stitches were severed they are stringent, and repairing the severed spots does not help, because a repair visible in three places is degrading. This applies whether the tefillin are new or old. The entire section must be restitched, as explained in Tur and Beit Yosef at the end of siman 32.
(13) Not opposite, etc. Even where each severed stitch is on a different side.
(14) The base of the batim. Meaning, the titura, as above. Here too Rama's view is that the batim themselves must remain intact. He relied upon his preceding gloss.
(15) To be concerned. Meaning, initially one should be stringent in both new and old tefillin. Where no other tefillin are available and these cannot be restitched, one may rely upon the lenient authorities regarding severed stitches, whether the tefillin are new or old and whether two or three stitches were severed. If three were severed, however, he should not recite a blessing over them. See Biur Halachah.
