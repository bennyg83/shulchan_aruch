(12) Per the Rambam — but the other poskim disagree with him and are lenient in all cases with two stitchings; and with three stitchings that were severed they are stringent, for even repair at the place of the break does not help, since it is recognizable like a patch when the repair is evident in three places, whether in new or old — and one must go back and sew anew, as explained in Tur and Beit Yosef above at the end of siman 32; see there.

(13) One not opposite the other — even each on a different side.

(14) Base of the batim — meaning the titura, as above; and in this too Rama's opinion is as in the gloss above that also the batim must remain intact, except that he relied on what was stated above.

(15) To be concerned — meaning to be stringent l'chatchila in both; but where one cannot find other tefillin and cannot go back and sew these tefillin, one may rely on those who are lenient regarding severed stitching, whether in new or old, whether with two stitchings or three — but with three one should be careful not to bless on them; and see Biur Halachah.
