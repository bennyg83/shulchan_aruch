Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. “Whole.” This requires study. What did he intend by this? Perhaps he means that the entire separating wall between one compartment and another must be torn from top to bottom. If it was not entirely torn, there is no concern. This would accord with Taz in siman 32, at the end of seif katan 36, who rules like Rav Dimi that even a minimal separation suffices. Nevertheless, the matter remains difficult. The reason old tefillin are invalid is not because of a deficiency in the separation, but because it becomes evident that the leather is defective and is regarded as though entirely absent. If so, perhaps even an incomplete tear invalidates them. Likewise, the invalidity of three compartments, even in new tefillin, as Taz writes in seif katan 4, may be for the same reason: where three compartments are damaged, even in new tefillin it becomes evident that the leather is defective. This requires study.
