Magen Avraham, seif katan 1: "whole" — it requires study what he intended; perhaps he means we require that the partition between bayit and bayit be severed completely from top to bottom; but if it did not sever entirely we do not care, as the Taz wrote in siman 32 at the end of seif katan 36, that we rule like Rav Dimi that any break suffices; but nonetheless it is difficult — whence this, for behold that which we invalidate in old tefillin is not because of lack of break but because the matter is revealed that the hide is defective and it is as if it does not exist; if so one could say that even if it did not sever entirely it is invalid; and one could say likewise that which he invalidates three batim even in new ones, as the Taz wrote in seif katan 4, is also for this reason, that in three batim even in new ones the matter is revealed that the hide is defective — and it requires study.
