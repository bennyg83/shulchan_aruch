**62: if three were severed, etc.** Rambam, and he explains it refers to stitches; and so in Terumat HaDeshen and Rashba that if three stitches were severed they are invalid; and as stated in Yerushalmi ch. 3 of Megillah — R' Zeira: a gid of a retzua was severed; he asked Rav Huna and Rav Katina and they permitted him. A second was severed and they permitted him — that was not from learning, meaning under pressing need; but a third time is certainly invalid; and the text of R' Elchanan: gid of tefillin — what relevance does gid have to retzuot. Mordechai.

**In what case, etc.** Rambam 33, and "this opposite that also," etc., except "and we did not say," and he explains it refers also to the words of Rav Chisda.

**So too, etc.** As Tur explains; but Rambam wrote the language of the Gemara: "faces of the panel."

**And these are, etc.** Per the first wording in the Gemara and Rif; and it returns valid except invalid ones that are not as Rashi explained. And it is found divided in several matters with Rashi and Tur: (1) he explains stitching. (2) He has the reverse reading "but in the new," etc. (3) He explains it refers also to Rav Chisda. (4) He chose the first wording "if new." (5) The reading and explanation another way that Rif returns, etc. (6) Shulchan Aruch disputes Rashi in explaining "faces of the panel."
