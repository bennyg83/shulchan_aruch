Seif 2, "if they were severed," etc. Rambam explains the passage as referring to the stitches, and so rule the other authorities: if 3 stitches were severed, the tefillin are invalid. This accords with the Yerushalmi, chapter 3 of Megillah, where R' Zeira had a sinew of the tefillin stitching break, asked Rav Huna and Rav Katina, and they permitted it; when a second broke they again permitted it only with difficulty, but a third break is certainly invalid. R' Elchanan therefore reads "a sinew of the tefillin" rather than "a sinew of the strap," for what relevance would a sinew have to the straps? Mordechai. "When does this apply," etc. Rambam did not have the reading "opposite one another," etc.; rather, his text read "they said this only," and he explains the qualification as applying also to the statement of R' Chisda. "All this," etc. The wording of Rambam follows the Gemara's distinction between new and old tefillin and between damage occurring opposite one another or in separate places. In several details his interpretation differs from Rashi and Tur: first, he applies the discussion to the stitching; second, his reading of the qualification differs; third, he explains the terms "new" and "old" differently; and the remaining distinctions follow accordingly from his text of the Gemara.
