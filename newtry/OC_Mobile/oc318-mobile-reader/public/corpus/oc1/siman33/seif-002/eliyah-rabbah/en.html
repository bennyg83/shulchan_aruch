[B] "Also the batim," etc., "tore somewhat," etc. — meaning between bayit and bayit; but from outside they must be whole on all sides; and likewise Beit Yosef's wording, that we require they be whole on all other sides — we validate only when they tore between bayit and bayit specifically, up to here; and so ruled Lechem Chamudot, Bach, and the later authorities.
