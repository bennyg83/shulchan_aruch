If the stitching of the tefillin was severed — per the Rambam, if the two stitchings were one opposite the other, or if three stitchings were severed, even if one was not' opposite the other, they are invalid. In what case are we speaking? In old ones. But in new ones, as long as the hide of the base of the compartments remains intact, they are valid. And these are called new: any [case] where one holds part of the hide whose stitching was severed and suspends the tefillin by it and it is strong and does not sever; and if it is not fit to suspend by it but rather it severs — these are called old.': And some say that in new ones they are invalid and in old ones valid [Rashi, Tur, and Rosh]; and it is good to be concerned for both opinions as stated above.
