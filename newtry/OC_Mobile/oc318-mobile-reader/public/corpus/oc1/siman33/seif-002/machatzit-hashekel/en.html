And even though these reasons that the Taz wrote in seif katan 1, see there, are not relevant — from this I did not know whence the scribes have authority, who are accustomed that by stretching in the mold the grooves between bayit and bayit with a thread, thereby the sides of the bayit are pulled more downward than the middle of the bayit; therefore they cut the sides of the bayit at the bottom until they are level, and the batim remained connected together; and I did not know what concern I have above on the sides of the bayit that Mordechai invalidates, and what concern below at the bottom of the bayit; and one told me to excuse: specifically on the sides of the bayit there is concern, since the partition is not whole it is not called a bayit — a bayit without a partition is not a bayit; not so below, even if it were fully open there was a bayit upon it, only that it must be one hide; and since in the middle also below they remained connected, only they cut them from two sides, it is one hide.
