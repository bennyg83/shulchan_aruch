4. Rama: The batim must also remain intact, except that they tore somewhat. This means that they tore between one compartment and another, while the exterior sides remain whole. Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. The upper surfaces must likewise remain intact. Machatzis HaShekel. Beit Yosef similarly writes that the batim must remain whole on all sides, and only a tear between one compartment and another is tolerated. Bach, Knesses HaGedolah, Olas Tamid, Eliyah Rabbah, Shulchan Aruch HaRav, Malbushei Shabbos, Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, and other authorities rule accordingly. The same applies to the bayis of the tefillin of the arm: if it tore even slightly, it is invalid. Responsa Pnei Yehoshua, Orach Chayim, siman 10, cited by Shaarei Teshuvah and Kitzur Shulchan Aruch. This implies that even a minute hole in a bayis invalidates it. Eliyah Rabbah in siman 39, seif katan 6, likewise implies this. See also responsa appended to Zivchei Tzedek, part 2, siman 10.
