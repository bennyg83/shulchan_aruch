Shulchan Aruch, seif 2: the moshav of the batim exists — they are valid. It is difficult why Rama did not emend here too, as in the previous seif, that the batim must also exist; and see in Bach who wrote that according to Rambam this is the main ruling of this seif, that the Gemara in Menachot regarding stitches is more reasonable to explain what the Gemara states, that it requires that the faces of the tablets exist — referring to batim; not so the disputants who explain the Gemara refers to batim as in the previous seif, which implies "faces of the tablets exist" means the titura — see there; and if so, since Rama emended even in seif 1 that the titura alone does not suffice, all the more so he should have emended here too; and perhaps he relied on the above.
