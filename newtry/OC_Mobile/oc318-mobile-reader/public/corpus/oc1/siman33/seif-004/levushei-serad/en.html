Shulchan Aruch, seif 2. If the base of the batim remains intact, they are valid. It is difficult why Rama did not add here, as he did in the preceding seif, that the batim themselves must also remain intact. See Bach, who writes that according to Rambam, the primary authority for the law in this seif, who explains the Gemara in Menachos as referring to the stitching, it is more reasonable to explain the Gemara's requirement that “the face of the tablet remains intact” as referring to the batim. According to the poskim who explain the Gemara as referring to the batim themselves, as in the preceding seif, that phrase appears to refer to the titura. See there. Accordingly, since Rama added even in seif 1 that the titura alone does not suffice, all the more so he should have added the same here. Perhaps he relied upon what he wrote above.
