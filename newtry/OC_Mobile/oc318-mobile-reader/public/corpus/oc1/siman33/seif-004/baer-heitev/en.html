**Invalid.** Because that the batim be black is not halachah leMoshe miSinai; if so the blackening is only a repair in general and is valid through a gentile — not so with retzuot, where it is halachah leMoshe miSinai that they be black; this is like spinning of tzitzit, which is invalid through a gentile; and it seems to me that if an Israelite returned and blackened them for their sake, they are valid. Magen Avraham.
