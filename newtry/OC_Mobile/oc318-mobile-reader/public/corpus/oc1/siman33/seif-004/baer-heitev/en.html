Invalid. The requirement that the batim be black is not a halachah transmitted to Moshe at Sinai. Their blackness is therefore merely an external improvement, and may be performed by a non-Jew. This differs from the straps, whose blackness is a halachah transmitted to Moshe at Sinai and is therefore comparable to spinning tzitzis, which is invalid when performed by a non-Jew. It appears to me that if a Jew subsequently blackened them for the sake of the mitzvah, they are valid. Magen Avraham.
