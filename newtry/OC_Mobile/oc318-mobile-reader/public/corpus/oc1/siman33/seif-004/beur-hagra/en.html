Seif 4: It is proper, etc. Sefer HaTerumah is uncertain about this. Since we have a received tradition that the straps must be black, perhaps blackening too must be performed for the sake of the mitzvah, like spinning tzitzis and dyeing techeiles. Hagahos Maimoniyos rules that even after the fact they are invalid, and then writes, “Nevertheless, etc.” His meaning follows Tosafos' view that the batim need not be black at all, as explained in siman 32, seif 40.
Some permit, etc. See Tosafos there, s.v. “Go out, etc.” In the Yerushalmi cited above it says that a sinew of the strap tore, etc. Semag explains that this refers to a strap sewn with sinews. Thus it was permitted twice in addition to the original sewing, as Terumas HaDeshen writes. This applies specifically when repaired with sinews, as stated on Menachos 31b. The same applies here, because the received law requires the batim to be sewn with sinews. See Magen Avraham.
