**64: Good, etc.** In Sefer HaTerumah he was uncertain about this — since the retzuot are tanned black, for lishmah we also require it, like spinning tzitzit and dyeing tekhelet; and in his gloss he ruled that even bediavad it is invalid; and he wrote "however," etc., meaning according to Tosafot, who do not require them to be entirely black, as stated in siman 32, seif 40.

**There is, etc.** See Tosafot there, s.v. "fok," etc.; and in the aforementioned Yerushalmi — his gid of a retzua was severed, etc.; and Semag explains: a retzua sewn with gidin — thus they permitted twice apart from the first time, as Turei Zahav wrote, specifically with gidin; and as Levushei Srad wrote in (2); and the same applies here, since the halachah leMoshe miSinai is that it be sewn with gidin — Magen Avraham.
