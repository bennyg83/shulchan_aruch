However, b'dieved it is kosher. For that the batim be black is not halachah leMoshe miSinai; if so the blackening is only a general repair and is valid through a gentile, like other repairs of hide — not so with retzuot, where it is halachah leMoshe miSinai that they be black; this is like spinning of tzitzit, which is invalid through a gentile.
