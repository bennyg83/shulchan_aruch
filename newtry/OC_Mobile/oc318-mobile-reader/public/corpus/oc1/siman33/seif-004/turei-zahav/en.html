Nevertheless, after the fact they are valid. The blackness of the batim is not a halachah transmitted to Moshe at Sinai. It is therefore merely an external improvement, and may be performed by a non-Jew like other leather finishing. The straps are different: their blackness is a halachah transmitted to Moshe at Sinai and is comparable to spinning tzitzis, which is invalid when performed by a non-Jew.
