(Seif katan 4) Processed, etc. Beit Yosef gives another reason: because the leather must come from a kosher species, it must therefore be processed for the sake of the mitzvah. He later refuted this reasoning in Bedek HaBayis. If so, why does Rambam hold that the leather of the batim need not be processed for the sake of the mitzvah, when the batim too must come from a kosher species? Magen Avraham therefore writes that the straps are more stringent than the batim, because the requirement that they be black is itself a halachah transmitted to Moshe at Sinai. Regarding the batim, Rambam maintains that blackness is only for preferred fulfillment and beauty.
