(seif katan 4) "processed," etc. — and the Pri Megadim wrote a different reason, because they must be pure, therefore requires processing lishmah; and he contradicted this in Beit Ephraim — if so why does Rambam hold regarding hide of batim that we do not require processing lishmah? Behold batim also must be pure; therefore Magen Avraham wrote retzuot are preferable to batim, for even that they be black is halachah leMoshe miSinai, not so batim where Rambam holds blackening is only for mitzvah and adornment.
