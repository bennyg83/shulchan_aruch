In every — Taz. I did not understand this, for it is true Bach explained the word "all" thus — see Tur, for he holds the version of Rashi and Rosh; but the version of Rif and Rambam in the Gemara is as Derishah wrote, and that is halachah k'pshuto: two and three "we did not say" only in old ones, but in new ones we have no issue. And since the author chose the version of Rif and Rambam, surely three tears in new ones are also valid. Levush would say it refers to the first view that Rama wrote as the main one. Incidentally, in the Gemara Rava, who said "that which you said" and did not explain Rav Chisda's words — regarding new ones one could say Rav Huna: as long as they exist — implies old ones; and on this he said "that which you said." But for Rashi and Rosh, new ones are inferior — it must refer to old ones, and perforce so, and per their version understand this; and see Magen Avraham ot 3, end of this.

As the author wrote in seif 2 — Rambam's explanation; see there 20 p.g. Hilchot Tefillin 18 in the name of R' Yitzchak Alexandria z"l: Rav Huna said 27 that the faces of the panel exist — meaning if severed, even two stitches are invalid; Rav Chisda disputes that two are valid — investigate there.
