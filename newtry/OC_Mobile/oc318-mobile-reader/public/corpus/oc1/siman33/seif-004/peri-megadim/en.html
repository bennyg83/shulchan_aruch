In every case. See Taz. I did not understand his statement. It is true that Bach explains the word “all” this way because he follows Rashi and Rosh's reading. According to Rif and Rambam's reading, however, as Derishah explains, the Gemara means: the rule concerning two and three tears was stated only regarding old tefillin, but with new tefillin there is no concern. Since the Mechaber chose Rif and Rambam's reading, three tears in new tefillin should also be valid. Levush may be referring to the second view, which he regards as primary. Incidentally, Rava's statement “that which you said” may refer back to Rav Huna's phrase “so long as they remain intact,” which implies old tefillin. According to Rashi and Rosh, who regard new tefillin as worse, Rava must necessarily refer to old tefillin, as their reading requires. See what I wrote on Magen Avraham, letter 3; the matter is resolved.
