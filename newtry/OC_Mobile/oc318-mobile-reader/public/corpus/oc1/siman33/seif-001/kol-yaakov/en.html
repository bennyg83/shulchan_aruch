1) Seif 1, If the hide of the batim became spoiled, etc. If the tefillin are old, they are invalid, for they have already become spoiled due to their age. And some say that the Rama holds the reverse: that with new ones it is revealed that the hide is spoiled, since they tore in a short time. Taz, seif katan 1.
2) There, this beside this. And even if the tear of one bayis is below toward the titura and of the other is above, even so it is invalid. Olas Tamid, letter 1; Eliyah Rabbah, letter 1; Meleches Shamayim, rule 19, letter 9.
3) There, And if they are new they are valid so long as the hide of the seating of the batim remains. That is, the titura. Beit Yosef, Bach, Levush.
4. Rama: The batim must also remain intact, except that they tore somewhat. This means that they tore between one compartment and another, while the exterior sides remain whole. Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. The upper surfaces must likewise remain intact. Machatzis HaShekel. Beit Yosef similarly writes that the batim must remain whole on all sides, and only a tear between one compartment and another is tolerated. So ruled Bach; Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beit Yosef, letter 2; Olas Tamid, letters 1 and 3; Eliyah Rabbah, letter 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, letter 1; Meleches Shamayim, rule 19, letter 9; Keset HaSofer, siman 24, letter 2; and Imrei Shefer, rule 17, letter 3 (and see Levush and Magen Avraham, seif katan 1, and what Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letters 1 and 2, wrote). The same applies to the bayis of the tefillin of the arm: if it tore even slightly, it is invalid. Responsa Pnei Yehoshua, Orach Chayim, siman 10, cited by Shaarei Teshuvah, letter 1, and Keset HaSofer there. This implies that even a minute hole in a bayis invalidates it. Eliyah Rabbah in siman 39, seif katan 6, likewise implies this. See also responsa appended to Zivchei Tzedek, part 2, siman 10.
5. Rama: Some say the reverse, etc., and it appears to me that one should be stringent and invalidate both. End of his words. And so wrote Shulchan Aruch HaRav, letter 1, and Meleches Shamayim there.
