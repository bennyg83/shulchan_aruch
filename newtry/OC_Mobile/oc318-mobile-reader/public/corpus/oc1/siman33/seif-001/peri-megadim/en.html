Two — invalid, Taz: the author and Rabbeinu [their dispute] depends on versions in Menachot 35a. The author holds the version of Rif and Rambam — new ones are valid and old ones invalid, for they will spoil more from excessive softness; Rosh and Rashi hold that in new ones they are invalid, for the matter is revealed that the hide is defective and in the end it will tear more; old ones are valid, as Rashi there d"h "we did not say," see there. But Levush and Bach wrote that old ones have dried and are strong and will not spoil more, while new ones are still soft — one must be concerned they will spoil more; and I do not know why they departed from Rashi's words.
