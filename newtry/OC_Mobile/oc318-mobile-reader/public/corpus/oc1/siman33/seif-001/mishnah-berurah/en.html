(א) Became damaged — meaning it was severed and a tear was made.

(ב) Of the batim of the head — but if a tear was made in the bayit of the hand, it is invalid according to all in every case, even though the titura remains intact; and it is not comparable to the head, for there at least some whole batim remain.

(ג) One next to the other — meaning the batim are adjacent to each other, even if the tears are not adjacent — for example, if one tear was in the second bayit on its right side and the second tear in the third bayit, whether on its right or left side; and even if one tear is above and the other below; and all the more so if both are in one airspace opposite each other — it is invalid.

(ד) Old ones invalid — for they were already damaged from their age; and there are those who hold the reverse — that in new ones the matter is revealed that the hide is defective, since in a short time they tore. And even where these reasons do not apply — such as if the tear was made with a knife, etc. — one should be stringent. Nonetheless, in Sefer Pe'at Tzedek he sided that adhesion helps if we know the hide is not defective, even for those who are accustomed to make tefillin from one hide for a name.

(ה) New ones valid — even if the tear is in two batim on both of their sides.

(ו) Intact — meaning the titura; but in Beit Yosef he explained that the sides must be whole on all sides — we validate only what tore on the inside between bayit and bayit; and this is also Rama's opinion, which he brought into the halachah, writing that also the batim must remain intact except that they tore somewhat — meaning they tore between bayit and bayit, but the sides must be whole; and even a tear in one bayit if made only from outside, one should also be stringent.

(ז) To invalidate in both — therefore one should invalidate what scribes usually do when making the four batim and placing them in a mold: they cut from the hide between bayit and bayit so it will not be compressed and protrude, and they cover the place of the cut from inside so it appears whole from outside.

(ח) The bayit spreads — this applies to those who make the ma'avarta from the hide from which the batim were made; but for us, where the ma'avarta is hide by itself, this is not applicable.

(ט) First and third — meaning this is called "not opposite each other"; and even if the tear was made in the third bayit on both its sides it is valid, since a whole bayit intervenes between them. But if tears were made in batim two and three — even if one tear is in one bayit on its right and the second tear in the second bayit on its left — nonetheless it is called "opposite each other," since the two batim are adjacent to each other; and as above in s.k. 2.

(י) Three batim — even in each bayit only one side.

(11) In every case — to include even new ones, which we validated above with two even one next to the other; nonetheless with three it is invalid.
