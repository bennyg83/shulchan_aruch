**61 of the second, etc.** First and third, etc. — per the first explanation in Rashi, and so all the poskim.

**As long as the hide, etc.** Tur — for so explained Ra'avan: as long as the face of the panel, etc.

**Also the batim, etc.** So explained Rashi and Rosh and Rashba; and wrote Nekudot Yosef and Mordechai — meaning they tore between bayit and bayit but the sides from outside are whole; and so Beit Yosef.

**And some say, etc.** As our text.

**And these, etc.** Per the latter wording there; or "and if not," etc., and per the second wording in Rashi.

**In every matter, etc.** Tur that explains from Rosh; but in the old text, etc., as Rashi explained that it refers only to the words of Rava; but the words of Rav Chisda — there is no distinction, as stated above; and "this opposite that we did not say," etc.
