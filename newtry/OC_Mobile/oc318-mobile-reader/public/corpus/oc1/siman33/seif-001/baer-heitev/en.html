**Intact.** Meaning, the titura (base). But Beit Yosef explained "for us" — meaning to say that the sides must be whole from all other sides; we do not validate except when they tore between bayit and bayit specifically. And this is what Rama wrote: also the batim must be intact except that they tore somewhat — meaning they tore between bayit and bayit, but the sides must be whole.

**And opened.** But for us this does not apply.

**And third.** And one bayit remains whole between them; but the two adjacent ones — even though the tears are not adjacent but one is on this side and the other on the other side — are called "this next to this." Taz.

**Invalid.** Even new ones are valid with two tears even next to each other; nonetheless with three they are invalid.
