Remains intact. Meaning, the titura. Beit Yosef, however, explains that the language means the batim must remain intact on all their other sides. We validate them only when the tear is specifically between one compartment and another. This is the meaning of Rama's statement that the batim must also remain intact, except that they tore somewhat: the tear is between one compartment and another, but the outer sides must remain whole.
And opened. Under our present construction this case does not arise.
And the third. One intact compartment remains between the two torn ones. If, however, two adjacent compartments tore, then even if the tears themselves are not adjacent, but one tear is on one side and the other on the opposite side, they are still considered adjacent. Taz.
They are invalid. Even if they are new. Although two compartments may be valid even when adjacent, three torn compartments are invalid.
