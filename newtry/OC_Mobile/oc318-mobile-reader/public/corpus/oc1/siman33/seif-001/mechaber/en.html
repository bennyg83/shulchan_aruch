The law of repairing tefillin and the law of the straps. It contains 5 seifim: If the leather of 2 adjacent compartments of the head tefillin became damaged, if the tefillin are old they are invalid, and if they are new they are valid as long as the leather forming the base of the compartments remains intact. {Rama: The compartments themselves must also remain intact, except that they may be somewhat torn. [Beit Yosef] Some say the reverse, that old tefillin are valid and new tefillin are invalid. [Rashi and Rosh] It appears to me that one should be stringent and invalidate both.} What are considered new? As long as, if one were to pull them by the straps, the compartment would spread and open, it is called new. If it does not open, it is called old. If 2 compartments that do not face one another became damaged, such as the first and third, they are valid even if they are old. If 3 compartments became damaged, they are invalid in every case.
