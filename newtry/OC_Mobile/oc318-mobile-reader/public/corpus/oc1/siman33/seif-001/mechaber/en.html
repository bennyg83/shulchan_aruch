דין תקוני התפילין ודין הרצועות. ובו ה סעיפים:
The law of repairing tefillin and the law of the retzuot. And in it are five sections: If the hide of two compartments of the head tefillin, one next to the other, became damaged — if the tefillin are old they are invalid, and if they are new they are valid as long as the hide of the base of the compartments remains intact:also the batim must remain intact except that they tore somewhat [Beit Yosef]; and some say the reverse — old ones are valid and new ones invalid [Rashi and Rosh]; and it appears to me one should be stringent to invalidate in both cases.} And these are called new: whenever, if one were to pull them by the retzuot, the bayit spreads and opens — it is called new; and if it does not open, it is called old. And if two became damaged not opposite each other — the first and the third — they are valid even if they are old. And if three compartments became damaged, in every case they are invalid. ואלו הם חדשים כל זמן שאם היו מושכים אותו ברצועות הבית מתפשט ונפתח נקרא חדש ואם אינו נפתח נקרא ישן ואם נתקלקלו שנים זה שלא כנגד זה ראשון ושלישי כשרים אפי' הם ישנים ואם נתקלקלו ג' בתים בכל ענין פסולים:
