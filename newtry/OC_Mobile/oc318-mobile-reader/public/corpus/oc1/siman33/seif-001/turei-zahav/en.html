Old ones invalid. For they have already spoiled from their age; and some say Rama holds the reverse — that in new ones the matter is revealed that the hide is defective and in a short time they will tear.

The bayit spreads. This is for those who make the ma'barta from the hide from which they made the batim; and so too the Tur in the siman before this — that some do so; but for us, where the ma'barta is a separate hide, it is not applicable to say this.

First and third. It implies that two adjacent ones — even though the tears are not adjacent but one is on this side and the other on the other side — are called "this opposite this," and they are invalid.

In every matter — comes to include new ones, which there are valid with two tears even next to each other; nonetheless with three they are invalid.
