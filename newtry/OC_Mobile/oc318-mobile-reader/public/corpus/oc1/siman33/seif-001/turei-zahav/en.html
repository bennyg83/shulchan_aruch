Old tefillin are invalid. Because they have already deteriorated through age. Some explain that Rama holds the reverse: with new tefillin, the fact that they tore within a short time reveals that the leather was defective.
The bayis spreads. This applies to those who make the ma'abarta from the same leather as the batim, as Tur writes in the preceding siman that some do. Under our practice, where the ma'abarta is a separate piece of leather, this does not apply.
The first and third. This implies that two adjacent compartments are considered “opposite one another” and invalid even if the tears are not adjacent, but one tear is on one side and the other on another side.
In every case. This includes new tefillin. Although two tears may be valid in new tefillin even when adjacent, three tears are invalid.
