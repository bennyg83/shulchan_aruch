It is forbidden to recite Shema opposite an old beit hakisei; even if one removed the feces from it, it is nevertheless still as if the feces were inside it, and one must distance oneself before it as far as one can see and behind it four amot from the place where the bad smell ends.

But if there are partitions, etc. {Rama: on this — specifically when the partitions are ten tefachim high, then one may recite even if there is feces in it} (see above, siman 87).
