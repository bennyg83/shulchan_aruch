And it seems to me — the Taz expanded and concluded that partitions designated for a bathroom have bathroom law: four amot behind and before as far as the eye can see in an old one; and Magen Avraham, note 1, similarly. And what he wrote about a perforated bench follows his view in letter 3 and Magen Avraham, note 5. Per the Perishah's view, a perforated bench has the law of a new bathroom and one distances four amot in a new one; and a general rule: opposite it one recites — plain "opposite it" means outside four amot (thus it is proven to the questioner in Berachos 26a: in an old one opposite at a distance of four amot, as Rashi explained there; and 26b: he saw feces opposite at a distance of four amot — therefore he does not return and pray, as in siman 81, seif 2). And although in an old one with partitions one recites opposite adjacent without distancing four amot — a perforated bench does not have partition law, only perforated-bench law, and is not ten high and also not four wide; and in the Shulchan Aruch plainly it seems even without ten high — Taz in siman 79, letter 3, Pislala. And in truth four wide and not ten high is like a karmelis — see there. However, what Beit Yosef wrote that it is like a covered one requires study, for there it is like a grave, whereas here it is not another domain. The Peri Chadash's view is to permit with ten high (and four wide is possible, per Bach). And behold, in sukkos one sits with bathroom partitions opposite the entrance — one could say a doubt: perhaps like the Mechaber, perhaps like the Rosh in siman 79, seif 2; and one could say nevertheless ab initio one should not create doubt by action, as one should not create a rabbinic doubt by action, as we must say below in Magen Avraham, note 3. And squinting in such a case appears to permit — Magen Avraham 79:8; and Eliyah Rabbah, who is stringent there, one could say here he agrees. And know for the Mechaber: whatever is in another domain helps for a bathroom; and in siman 79, feces in another domain is permitted — what is "another domain"? Only four partitions, or three partitions, or even two partitions ten high and four wide — it becomes a private domain; see siman 363 and Taz there. But I saw the Bach here wrote partitions on every side ten high — implying we require a complete private domain with an opening; if a quarter wind breaks fully it is like a karmelis, and we require a private domain specifically, as Peri Chadash wrote in siman 79 for the Rosh — understand; requires study.
