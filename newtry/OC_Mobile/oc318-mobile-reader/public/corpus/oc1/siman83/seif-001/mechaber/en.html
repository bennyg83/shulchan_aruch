Laws of the bathroom regarding Kerias Shema; it contains five seifim. It
is forbidden to recite Kerias Shema opposite an old bathroom, even if' the excrement has been removed from it. And it seems to me that this is when there are no partitions; but if there are partitions, even though there is excrement, one may recite opposite it, adjacent to it, and one need not be concerned so long as no bad smell reaches him.
