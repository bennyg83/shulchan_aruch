Laws of the Bathroom with [regard to] the Recitation of the Sh'ma, containing
5 S'ifim It is forbidden to recite [the Recitation of the Sh'ma] facing' an old bathroom, even if excrement has been emptied from it. And it seems to me that this is when there are no partitions, but if there are partitions, even though there is excrement, one may recite facing it, adjacent to it, and they need not be concerned as long as no bad smell reaches them.
