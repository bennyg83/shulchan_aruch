Seif 2, "But," etc. Rambam and Rashba — since it was unresolved for them in Nedarim 7a and it was resolved in Eretz Yisrael as his way in Kesef Mishnah; and further, since R' Chisda prayed opposite, etc., and he establishes it in a new one — from which it is implied that inside it Rashba holds it is forbidden; but this is not necessary, for if so the question would be weak, and it is asked about it, etc. — resolve it only that for R' Chisda too it is doubtful.
