Seif 2, But, etc. Rambam. He maintains that since they raised the question in Nedarim 7a and the discussion concludes with an 'if you will say' formulation, the matter is resolved, following Rambam's usual method elsewhere. He further infers from the statement that Rabbi Chiya prayed opposite it, which the Gemara establishes as referring to a new privy, that in his view praying inside it is forbidden. Rosh states this, but it is not conclusive, for if so the original difficulty returns: since the Gemara raises this very question, let it resolve it from there. Rather, Rabbi Chiya too remained uncertain about it.
