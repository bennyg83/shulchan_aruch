Even if it is not ten tefachim high — Beit Yosef; Bach disagreed that specifically ten tefachim high are required, and so Lechem Chamudos explains; and the view of Taz and Magen Avraham is that even if ten tefachim high it is forbidden. However, specifically regarding partitions made only for a latrine, such as if one makes a special room in his courtyard for this; but if one leaned the latrine against the walls of the house — even though that partition is literally a partition of a latrine — one may recite even nearby, since the partition is not designated for the latrine alone. All this is in a latrine without a pit but with feces placed on the ground.
