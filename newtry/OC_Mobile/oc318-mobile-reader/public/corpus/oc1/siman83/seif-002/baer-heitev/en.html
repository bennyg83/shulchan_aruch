Within it. That is, within the partitions if it has them — see Magen Avraham, seif katan before this. But in a new bathhouse it is permitted even within it, for a beit hakisei is more repugnant; and to contemplate words of Torah there, it seems to me that it is permitted. Kesef Mishnah in chapter 3 of Laws of Shema wrote that it is forbidden; his proof is from what Kesef Mishnah says — one may contemplate except opposite a beit hakisei and a bathhouse — and since it states it without qualification, it implies that even in a new one contemplation is forbidden, similar to Shema and prayer — see there. This is not a great proof to me, for it teaches the comparison to a bathhouse, and regarding a bathhouse it must refer to an old one; examine closely. See Ateret Zekenim.
