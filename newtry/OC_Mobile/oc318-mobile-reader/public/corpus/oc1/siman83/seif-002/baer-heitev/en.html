Inside it. Meaning, inside the partitions, when it has partitions. See what I wrote in the preceding seif katan. In a new bathhouse, however, it is permitted even inside it, because a privy is more repulsive. It appears to me that it is permitted to think words of Torah in a new privy. Kesef Mishneh, in chapter 3 of Hilchos Krias Shema, writes that it is forbidden. His proof is from the statement that one may think words of Torah everywhere except in a privy and a bathhouse. Since the statement is unqualified, it implies that even in a new one it is forbidden to think, just as Krias Shema and prayer are forbidden there; see there. This is no strong proof in my view, for the privy is taught together with a bathhouse, and with respect to a bathhouse it must refer to an old one. Examine this carefully, and see Ateres Zekeinim.
