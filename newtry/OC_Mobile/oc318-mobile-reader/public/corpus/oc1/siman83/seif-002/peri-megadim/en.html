But the Taz — certainly per the Rambam one needs distancing as far as the eye can see, as explained in siman 87, seif 3, and in Rambam chapter 3, halacha 8; only regarding a bathroom with partitions the Mechaber's view is to permit, even though the Taz is stringent — that is regarding feces, not regarding urine, which is rabbinic: wherever there are partitions they were not stringent. And still requires study. And our bathrooms that have a perforated bench and waste falls there directly, not at a distance of four amot — nevertheless one could say bathroom partitions are not like a bathroom, since there is a bench besides the partition; and what they urinate inside appears lenient as rabbinic. And at any rate after the fact one does not return and recite. See what is written in siman 84 and 79 in the Taz's introductions: wherever there is a dispute among the rabbis, one returns and recites for blessings; for prayer one does not return and pray — siman 87.
