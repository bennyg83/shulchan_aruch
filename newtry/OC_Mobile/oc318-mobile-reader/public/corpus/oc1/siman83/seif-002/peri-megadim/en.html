But - See Taz. Certainly, one must distance himself from urine as far as he can see, as explained in siman 87, seif 3, and Rambam, chapter 3, law 8. Regarding a privy with partitions, however, the Mechaber permits. Although Taz is stringent, that concerns excrement, not urination, whose prohibition is D'Rabbanan. Wherever there are partitions, the Sages did not impose stringency. This still requires study. Regarding our privies, which contain a perforated bench and excrement falls directly beneath it, not four amos away, one may nevertheless argue that the partitions of the privy are not treated like the privy itself, since there is a bench in addition to the partitions. Regarding urination inside it, it appears that one may be lenient, because the prohibition is D'Rabbanan. At the very least, after the fact he need not repeat Krias Shema. See what I wrote in the introductions to simanim 74 and 79 regarding Taz's principle that whenever there is a dispute among leading authorities, one repeats Krias Shema without its blessings, whereas one does not repeat the Amidah.
