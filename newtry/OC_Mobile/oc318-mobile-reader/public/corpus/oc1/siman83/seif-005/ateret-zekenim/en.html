People, etc. — one may recite Shema and must distance four amot from it (Beit Yosef), as with a beit hakisei in which there is no feces. Some say that for us it is forbidden in our beit hakisei, for we rule like R' Yehuda; therefore a perforated bench is also forbidden, since it has the law of a beit hakisei (as one authority holds). It does not seem so at all, for what we practice as forbidden in our bathrooms even though they are sealed is because people are accustomed to urinate in them and everything is contaminated by urine; this is not so with a perforated bench, as explained.
