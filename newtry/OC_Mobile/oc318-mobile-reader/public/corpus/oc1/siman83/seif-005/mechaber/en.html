People that have a bench with a hole in it and [into which they] defecate into, it is permitted to recite the Sh'ma in front of it since there is no excrement on the hole, and also the container [which holds the waste] is not under the hole (ie. it's currently not there), and additionally the hole is always covered with a board. {Rama: [And all who recite in a place that we may not recite goes back and recites [again] (Maimoni Ch. 3)]}
