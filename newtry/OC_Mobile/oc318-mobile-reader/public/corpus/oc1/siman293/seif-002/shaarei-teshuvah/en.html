(In Shulchan Aruch seif 3) And Barkhu with extended pleasantness — and wrote in Halachot Gedolot end of the laws of tzitzit: it is required of a person to bind (meaning to extend and continue) in "Baruch" at the departure of Shabbat, for when one is saved from the harm of this Shabbat — see there; and he brought in Bereshit Rabbah; and also wrote he saw from Taharat Yad HaKodesh of Maharchu in the name of Rabbenu Hai Gaon z"l a tradition that there is success when a person draws out and extends in saying "Baruch Heaven haMevorach" at the departure of Shabbat — end quote.
