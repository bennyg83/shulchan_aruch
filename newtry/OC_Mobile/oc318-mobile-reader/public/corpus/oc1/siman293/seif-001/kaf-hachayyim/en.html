1) [Seif 1] The Maariv prayer is delayed, etc. Rabbi Yosi said, in Shabbos 118, 'May my portion be among those who conclude Shabbos in Tzippori.' Tzippori stood upon a mountain, so daylight appeared to remain there until the stars emerged. They therefore delayed the conclusion of Shabbos. Tur. Regarding the liturgical songs customarily recited after Shabbos, we already wrote above at the end of the preceding siman that the Arizal's custom was not to recite them; see there.
