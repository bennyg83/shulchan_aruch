Such as he needs to darken, etc. — from Magen Avraham's words it appears that solely so he not pray while walking it is permitted to pray early at home; and I did not copy him because in s.k. 5 he wrote one should wait to read Keriat Shema until tzeit hakochavim and not adjoin geulah to prayer — and he himself already wrote in siman 89 that the world relies now on the view that adjoining geulah to prayer is preferable to prayer standing; and likewise in Sefer Tosafot Shabbat.
