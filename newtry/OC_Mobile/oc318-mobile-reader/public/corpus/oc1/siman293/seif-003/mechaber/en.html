One who is anus, such as he needs to go beyond the techum for a mitzvah matter — he may pray the prayer of Motza'ei Shabbat from plag haMinchah and above and make Havdalah immediately; but he does not bless on the candle; and likewise melacha is forbidden until tzeit hakochavim.And the custom is to say V'hu rachum and Barkhu with extended pleasantness in order to add from the mundane to the holy (Or Zarua).
