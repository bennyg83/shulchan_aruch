(ג) Such as, etc. — and what the Tur wrote, etc., thus the Tur: such as they had some constraint that they had no wine for Havdalah on Motza'ei Shabbat, or they needed to go beyond the techum for a mitzvah need — end quote. And for the Tur one cannot explain on Motza'ei Shabbat "they needed to darken," etc., that they would have no wine in the field as Tosafot wrote — for that is the first constraint the Tur wrote, that they had no wine for Havdalah; and even though Magen Avraham in the following s.k. explains what the Tur wrote about no wine, etc., not as those Tosafot but such as one who lives in a city, etc. — in any case the first constraint the Tur wrote is that they would not have wine for Havdalah. Again one cannot explain what he wrote afterward "or they needed to darken," etc., that they would have no wine in the field — meaning the first constraint of no wine for Havdalah; and what difference whether they lack wine for Havdalah through one cause or another? Therefore perforce what the Tur wrote "or they needed to darken," etc. — and the constraint that he must pray while walking — and see above this section.
