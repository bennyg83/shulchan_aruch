8) Such as one who needs, etc. This means that he leaves his home before nightfall on Friday and goes to the end of the techum, where he sits until evening so that immediately upon nightfall he can proceed to perform an urgent mitzvah. The case assumes that he will not find a cup there upon which to recite Havdalah.
9) He may, etc. Nevertheless, the Acharonim write that one should not do so, because it appears strange to the public and they may come to be lenient regarding labor. This is especially true in our times, when the prevailing custom follows the Rabbis’ view and Mincha is prayed until evening; it is certainly prohibited by law to pray Maariv early on Motzei Shabbos. Although some are lenient on Erev Shabbos, that is because it is a mitzvah to add from the weekday to the sacred, which does not apply on Motzei Shabbos.
10) And to recite Havdalah immediately. Nevertheless, he must wait to recite Kerias Shema until tzeis hakochavim.
