Regarding Birkat Kohanim. See Taz concerning the phrase, “the Hasmonean kohanim,” etc. See Pri Chadash, who discusses at length that on every day when the Torah reading is an obligation of that day, the portions read by the kohen, Levi, and Yisrael must concern the subject of the day. One may question this from Shemini Atzeres and Pesach, when we read “You shall surely tithe” and “Every firstborn,” which at first glance have no connection to the festivals.
