He need not read more. For we do not take Chanukah into account.
To read a fifth. He should say Kaddish after him. If he already said Kaddish after the fourth, he should not say it after the fifth; Responsa Maharil, siman 212. See siman 282, section 6, and the end of siman 144. Even if they remembered during the fourth reading, he should not read Chanukah, because we do not skip, as written in siman 144. Beis Yosef, Shibolei HaLeket, Magen Avraham.
To interrupt. Turei Zahav wrote that this ruling of Rema is not authoritative, and he does not need to interrupt. Rather, the kohen reads the Chanukah portion, and afterward he reads for the others the Rosh Chodesh portion. He wrote that the great rabbi, our teacher Rabbi Naftali Katz, of blessed memory, agreed to this; see there. See also Ginas Veradim, Orach Chayim, rule 1, siman 36.
