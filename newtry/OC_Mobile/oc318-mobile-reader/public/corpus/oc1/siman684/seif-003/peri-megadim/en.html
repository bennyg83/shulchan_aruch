That of Rosh Chodesh. See Turei Zahav; Tosafos, Shabbos 23b, beginning with the word hadar; and Rosh there. Even though there are two other answers, this is the main one, as I wrote in 681:1; see there. They also read the Chanukah haftarah because of that with which he concluded, aside from the haftarah of Simchas Torah, where he reads the maftir from the offerings and the haftarah from Yehoshua, And it was after, etc.; see Levush 669 and here. Regarding beginning with On the Shabbos day, see siman 425 and Levush. See Pri Chadash: in a place where they say A song at the dedication, etc., on Chanukah after prayer, and also the song of the day according to our practice, one may say that the song of the day comes first because it is frequent, and afterward the song of Chanukah. This requires examination.
