Section 3. If the chazan erred and read the fourth on Shabbos Chanukah, and likewise on Shabbos Chanukah. The reason it mentions Chanukah with Rosh Chodesh on a weekday is because of the novelty of the latter clause: even though we do not add readers on Rosh Chodesh, nevertheless if they took out a second scroll, they add.
