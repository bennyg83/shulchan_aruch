If Rosh Chodesh Teves falls on Shabbos, three Torah scrolls are taken out. Six read from the weekly parashah; from the second scroll, one reads the portion of Rosh Chodesh, beginning with On the Shabbos day; and from the third scroll, the maftir reads the portion of Chanukah, and he reads the haftarah of Rani Vesimchi. If Rosh Chodesh falls on a weekday, two Torah scrolls are taken out. From one, three read the portion of Rosh Chodesh, and from the second, one reads the portion of Chanukah. If the chazan erred and read the fourth aliyah from the portion of Rosh Chodesh, then if they had not taken out the second scroll, he need not read more. But if they had taken out the second scroll, because of possible blemish to it, he must read a fifth aliyah from the portion of Chanukah. Gloss: If he erred and began to read the portion of Chanukah, he must interrupt and read the portion of Rosh Chodesh. Abudarham, Laws of Blessings. Whether he must bless over the reading of Rosh Chodesh, see above, siman 140.
