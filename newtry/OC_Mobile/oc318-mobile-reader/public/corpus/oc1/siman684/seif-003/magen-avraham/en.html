And in the second scroll one reads that of Rosh Chodesh. Because the frequent comes first. And he reads the haftarah of Rani Vesimchi, for the rule that the frequent comes first is for precedence, but as to overriding, publicizing the miracle is preferable. In Tosafos, Sukkah 54b, it implies that they hold that even regarding overriding, the frequent comes first; see there. This can be resolved.
He need not read more. For we do not take Chanukah into account.
To read a fifth. He should say Kaddish after him. If he said Kaddish after the fourth, he should not say it after the fifth; Responsa Maharil, siman 212. See siman 282, section 6, and the end of siman 147. Even if they remembered during the reading of the fourth, he should not read Chanukah, because we do not skip, as written in siman 144.
