(א) They read from the Nesi'im offerings. Tur wrote and explains in Pesikta because the Mishkan work was completed on the 25th of Kislev — end. Meaning Moshe erected the Mishkan only on Rosh Chodesh Nisan and then the Nesi'im brought their offering; we read the Nesi'im portion on Hanukkah after completion of the Mishkan.
