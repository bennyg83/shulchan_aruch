Section 1: And we begin. Because the miracle was performed through them. Tur.
And some say. This is explained.
On the second day. And some say. This depends on the dispute above in siman 404, section 3; see there. The reason of the first opinion is that one should not read from something that is not the subject of the day, as written at the beginning of chapter 3 of Megillah: it is different there because it is possible, etc. The second opinion holds that from the following day is not considered unrelated to the subject of the day. Tosafos also wrote this at the end of Sukkah 55a, beginning with the word instituted; see there. There is proof from the watches, where he would skip in Bereishis and read Let there be a firmament. But in Tosefta, chapter 2 of Megillah, it implies like the first view.
And we read the parashah. In order to conclude with the lamps. Tur. See Pesikta on Parashas Behaaloscha.
