Magen Avraham, paragraph 1. That on the second Shabbos, etc. Meaning: if there are two Shabbosos during Chanukah, and there is a wedding on the second Shabbos, they read the wedding haftarah, namely Sos Asis, since they already read the Chanukah haftarah on the first Shabbos. This was brought in Darkei Moshe.
