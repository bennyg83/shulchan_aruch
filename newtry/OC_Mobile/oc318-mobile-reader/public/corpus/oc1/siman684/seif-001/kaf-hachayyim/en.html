1) [Seif 1] We read the offerings of the Nesi'im, etc. The Pesikta explains that this is because the work of the Mishkan was completed on 25 Kislev. Tur. That is, Moshe did not erect the Mishkan until Rosh Chodesh Nissan, and it was then that the Nesi'im brought their offerings. We, however, read the section of the Nesi'im on Chanukah, following the completion of the Mishkan. Taz, s.k. 1. See above, siman 670, letter 12:
2) Ibid. And we begin with Bircas Kohanim. This is a proper custom, because the miracle was brought about through kohanim. Tur. Levush. Taz, s.k. 2:
