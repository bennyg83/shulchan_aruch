The order of the Torah reading on Chanukah. It contains 3 seifim: We read the offerings of the nesi'im in Parashat Nasso, three each day, and we begin with Birkat Kohanim (and some say we begin with 'On the day Moshe finished,' [Tur], and this is our custom). And that passage is read with a Kohen and a Levi, and a Yisrael reads the offering of the first day (and some say that the Kohen reads that entire passage through 'on the first day,' and the Levi and Yisrael read 'on the first day,' [Minhagim], and this is our custom). On the second day the Kohen reads from 'On the second day' through 'one young bull,' the Levi through 'On the third day,' and the Yisrael repeats and reads 'On the second day,' and in this manner each day. {Rama: And some say that the Yisrael reads the following day's portion, namely 'On the third day,' and so on each day, and this is our custom. [Hagahot Maimoniyot, chapter 13]} On the eighth day we begin with 'On the eighth day,' complete the entire section, and read the first portion of Beha'alotcha (and the custom is to conclude with 'So he made the menorah.' [Minhagim]).
