And some say that we rely, etc. And see Devar Shmuel, siman 257, that there are those who make the eruv and the shituf with a flask of oil, and this is not correct: since they wish to discharge the eruv and the shituf together, it must be made specifically with bread, for for eruvei chatzeiros we require bread; see there. And so wrote the sefer Bnei Chayya on this siman, that in the city of Ridash, where they made the eruv with oil, each courtyard must make an eruv for itself, except for those courtyards that open one to another and made an eruv by way of their entrances; see there. And there he cited from the writings of the Arizal, of blessed memory, that it states there: on erev Shabbos, [make] shitufei mevo'os and eruvei chatzeiros, and one should break bread at night on that of the shituf and in the morning on that of the eruv. End quote. This implies that he ruled like the Raavad, that one must make the shituf with bread, etc.; and presumably he would make the eruv every erev Shabbos and confer possession [on behalf of the townspeople].
