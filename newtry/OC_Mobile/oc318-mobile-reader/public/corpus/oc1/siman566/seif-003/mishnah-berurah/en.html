(14) Unless there are in the synagogue, etc. - And regarding reading Vayechal when a communal fast falls on Monday or Thursday: even though they push aside the weekly portion, there is no blessing in vain here (for it is Ezra's enactment to read on Monday and Thursday); therefore it is enough if there are six in the synagogue to read Vayechal; which is not so at Minchah, where ten in the synagogue are required; and the same law applies at Shacharis when it falls on Sunday, Tuesday, Wednesday, or Friday: specifically ten in the synagogue are required (Eliyah Rabbah and Peri Megadim). And some say that on a communal fast of the Four Fasts, since they are from the words of Tradition, even if there are only seven fasting there and the rest are not fasting because they are compelled, being ill, they may establish the blessing of Aneinu and read Vayechal (Shaarei Teshuvah; see there).
(15) Ten who are fasting - And if among them there is one who does not complete [the fast], he should not say the blessing of Aneinu.
