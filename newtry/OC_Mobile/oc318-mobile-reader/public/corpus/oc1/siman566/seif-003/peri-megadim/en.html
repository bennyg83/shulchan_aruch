Only see Taz and see Eliyah Rabbah 3 in the name of Malbushei Yom Tov; and the custom is like the Rama regarding a fast that they sometimes decree in one city and province, that they do not push aside the weekly portion; but on the 20th of Sivan they read Vayechal at Shacharis as well, even when it falls on Monday or Thursday. And if the 20th of Sivan falls on erev Shabbos, when the practice is not to complete the fast except until the hour they leave the synagogue, see siman 249, and in this they instituted and accepted upon themselves so. This requires further study regarding Aneinu, how they should act, and also regarding the communal Torah reading; and see siman 562, se'if 1 and 2, and what I wrote there; and see further below about this, Magen Avraham siman 580, and with God's help there it will be clarified further. And see Eliyah Rabbah, that one should give charity at Minchah, for the reward of a fast is charity; and so some congregations practice to announce giving "kofer nefesh." Nevertheless one should see to do so not during the chazzan's repetition of the prayer, for they distract and one must listen to the chazzan. Also on Monday and Thursday the gabba'im go about with a charity basin of coins; I have often told them not to go about during the Torah reading, for it is an enactment of Ezra and they distract, but only after the reading of the Sefer Torah, and that is proper.
