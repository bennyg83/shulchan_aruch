[3] However, they do not push aside, etc. So wrote the Rama; and Malbushei Yom Tov elaborates at length to wonder at him for ruling like Maharil against the Tur, and that so it is implied in Maseches Sofrim, chapter 14, halachah 4, and he concluded, in his words: therefore it is obvious in my eyes to rule as law not like Maharil; but in practice, since the custom has already spread like Maharil, my heart did not fill [me with courage] until today, Monday the 20th of Sivan 5412, a fast that the Council of Four Lands decreed and established for generations, I ruled in practice for that day which fell on Monday to read Vayechal even at Shacharis, and specifically for that day when it falls so again, and in this I do not contradict the custom; end of his language. And in my humble opinion, to settle the words of the Rama and the Levush: for Maharil wrote that he found so in the customs, therefore he was not concerned about the agreement of the Tur; I also found so in the Agudah, first chapter of Taanis, who wrote that he saw so from his teachers, and there is no doubt that had Malbushei Yom Tov seen him he would not have challenged the Rama. Nevertheless on the 20th of Sivan one should read Vayechal, as the Taz wrote.
