Selach. If he forgot to say [Selichos] until he finished the blessing of Selach, he should say the Selichos after the Shemoneh Esrei prayer (Magen Avraham). And they may say the Selichos even if there are not ten who are fasting; and see siman 492, what is written there. And this is specifically Selichos and confessions; but "For the sake of Your truth," Aneinu, and "Our God," one should not say except after the prayer. Later authorities.
