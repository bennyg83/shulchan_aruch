The Bach wrote: an individual who is not fasting who prays with the congregation must say Aneinu, and he should say "on this day of our fast"; end quote. On a communal fast they say "Our God and God of our fathers"; one who did not fast should also say [it] (Maharil). An individual who prays with the chazzan should not say Aneinu with him before Refaenu (responsum of Maharil); but the chazzan should say it before Refaenu even if he has not yet prayed [silently], and a chazzan who is fasting an individual fast should not say Aneinu when he prays aloud even if he did not pray silently; rather when he says silently "Elokai netzor" he should also say Aneinu (Maharshal, plain, and not like Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah); and in Eliyah Zuta I wrote the reason. And regarding Vayechal: if it is on Monday or Thursday at Shacharis, it is enough if there are in the synagogue six who are fasting; there is no added blessing, only that they push aside the weekly portion; but at Minchah or on other days of the week there must be specifically ten; and see siman 492.
