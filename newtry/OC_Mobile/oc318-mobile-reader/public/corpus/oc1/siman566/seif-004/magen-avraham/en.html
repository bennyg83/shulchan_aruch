In the blessing of Selach. Nevertheless they should say only Selichos; but "El Rachum Shemecha" and the like, which are not on the topic of forgiveness of sin, they should not say until after the prayer (Bach); and see the Tur, siman 579, on this matter. And it appears to me that if he forgot to say the Selichos until he finished the blessing of Selach lanu, he should say the Selichos after the Shemoneh Esrei prayer; see siman 294.
