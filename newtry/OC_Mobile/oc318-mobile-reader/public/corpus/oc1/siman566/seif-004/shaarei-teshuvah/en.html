Ten. See Beit HaTalmid; and see in Mishna Berurah in the name of Maharam ben Chaviv in a responsum (manuscript) that this applies only to a communal fast that the congregation accepted upon themselves for every trouble, etc.; but the four fasts that are from words of Kabbalah — even if there are not ten fasting, only six or seven and the rest are not fasting because they are anusim, sick — they may establish the berachah of Aneinu and read Vayechal even if there are not ten fasting in the synagogue, see there. It appears that at least six or seven are required; if there are only five they may not say Aneinu and may not take out a Torah scroll at all, since there is no majority fasting. Also regarding six there is doubt in my humble opinion — perhaps a majority of affliction is required, as written above siman 197 regarding zimmun with ten, see there.
