And if he forgot, see Taz, that a chazzan who forgot Aneinu, even in Shomea Tefillah, should say it after the prayer as a blessing of its own; and so wrote Magen Avraham in siman 119, letter 7. And even an individual who forgot may say it after "Yehiyu leratzon imrei fi." And see siman 565 in Magen Avraham, letter 2: a congregation say it as a blessing of its own after "Who blesses His people Israel with peace," and it is not out of order (as after Refaenu), since they have already finished their prayer.
