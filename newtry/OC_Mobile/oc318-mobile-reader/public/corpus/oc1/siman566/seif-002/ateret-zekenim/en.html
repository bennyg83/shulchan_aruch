And so is the practice during the Ten Days of Repentance, even on erev Rosh Hashanah, when the congregation is accustomed to fast, and even so the chazzan does not say Aneinu, nor Vayechal (and see the words of my father-in-law, s.k. 4). However, it is surprising regarding our custom that they read [Vayechal] on erev Rosh Chodesh and the chazzan says Aneinu, and it is not proper to do so (and see Taz, s.k. 5), unless the community decrees a fast; then the fast is for every trouble that should not come. And so ruled my teacher the Gaon Moreinu HaRav R' Yehoshua, of blessed memory, and so they practice until today in the holy congregation of Krakow. And in the Bach he expresses surprise that it would have been proper for the chazzan to say Aneinu at least in Shomea Tefillah at Shacharis and at Minchah on erev Rosh Hashanah; and perhaps regarding this [erev Rosh Hashanah], since one need not complete the fast, as below in siman 581, it is not called entirely a fast such that the chazzan should say Aneinu at Shacharis and Minchah as on the other Days of Repentance, as above. And if a bris milah falls on the days of the fast, they say Selichos and confessions, but not Vehu Rachum, and the custom of our ancestors is Torah, etc. (Mordechai, end of chapter [Kol] Me'arvin).
