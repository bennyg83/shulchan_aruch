Se'if 2: and some wondered, etc. From what is written there at the end of chapter 1, "like individuals who die"; and likewise regarding Vayechal, for in the Tosefta, chapter 2, it is taught: on a communal fast they enter the synagogue and read, which is not so for an individual fast.
And the Rosh, etc. Who wrote that not only the aforementioned BeHaB which all the Diasporas of Israel practiced, but even any fast that some congregation accepts upon themselves is called "many," and they may even establish a blessing, and all the more so read from the Torah. And that of Nineveh is because they wanted to change the fixed wording of a blessing that the prophets enacted, dew and not rain, as written in the Yerushalmi, chapter 5 of Berachos and chapter 1 of Taanis: the people of Nineveh need to make a fast after Pesach; they came and asked Rabbi; he said to them: Go and do [so], provided you do not change the fixed wording of prayer. And so wrote the Hagahos Mordechai: for he did not say to them "Do not add," but only "Do not change"; and anything established because of every trouble that should not come and every hardship of the world is no less than rain withholding.
However, etc. For according to the Rosh, who said that whenever a congregation accepted upon themselves it is called many, the same applies here; but according to the Hagahos Mordechai, who said specifically because of trouble, for then it is like rain withholding, which is not so for repentance, which is not an establishment, but each person sits on his own.
