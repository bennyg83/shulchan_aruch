The shaliach tzibbur recites Aneinu, etc. Even when the congregation prays together with the shaliach tzibbur, in accordance with our present practice when there is insufficient time to complete the full communal prayer, ten assemble and conduct another prayer. When they reach the 18 blessings, the congregation does not first pray silently and the shaliach tzibbur then repeat the prayer. Rather, the shaliach tzibbur and congregation pray together, and the shaliach tzibbur recites the first three blessings and the final three blessings aloud so that the congregation may answer Kedushah and bow at Modim. It is preferable for the shaliach tzibbur to recite Aneinu as an independent blessing between Goel Yisrael and Refa'einu rather than recite it in Shomea Tefillah like an individual. Mahari ben Lev, siman 15. Knesset HaGedolah cited his words in Hagahos HaTur and wrote that this was followed in practice. So too wrote Sheyarei Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur, os 1; see there.
