Only Monday and Thursday, etc. Because these fasts were established by all Israel in all the lands, and those who do not fast are the minority, which is not so for other decrees of a fast on a particular province; then one does not push aside the weekly portion on account of Vayechal. And it appears to me that the fast which the leaders of the Three Lands, together with the Geonim, decreed to fast every 20th of Sivan because of the harsh decrees (in our many sins) one should read Vayechal even when it falls on Monday or Thursday, for this fast is established even more than the Monday and Thursday [fasts] after Pesach and Sukkos, as it appears to my humble opinion.
And some wondered about them. Meaning, they found difficult that which we say regarding asking for rain, that even the people of Nineveh are considered like individuals; and if so, the congregation too are considered like individuals and may not read Vayechal. And the Rosh answered: granted that they are considered like individuals regarding asking, the reason is as explained in the Yerushalmi, that one may not change the fixed wording of the blessings that the Sages established according to the enactment of the prophets; but a congregation that accepts a fast upon themselves are certainly called many, and they may establish blessings. And some have the practice to treat it as an individual fast, etc. Beis Yosef wrote that he saw in this those who treat it as a communal fast, and I have seen those who disagree with them, and each river follows its own course; end quote. And already in these lands the custom of the Shomrim LaBoker society has become established, that they take out a Sefer Torah at Minchah and read Vayechal on erev Rosh Chodesh, which they call a "small Yom Kippur," and not at Shacharis. And it appears that this is in order to compromise between the opinions: they read specifically at Minchah, when they elaborate somewhat with Selichos, and one should not protest against those who practice so, since they have authorities to rely upon, especially in these times when (in our many sins) decrees are common upon our enemies, both from enemies within our midst and from outside the land, and the fasts come to annul wrath, Heaven forbid, not for repentance alone, and they have the status of a communal fast, and most times most of the congregation fasts on them, as it appears to my humble opinion.
