When the congregation decrees, etc. It is written in Sefer Agudah: sometimes my teachers decreed a fast and did not read from the Torah, but only said Selichos, as they do before Rosh Hashanah.
To treat it as an individual fast. And the Bach wrote that so is the practice in this land, for on erev Rosh Hashanah all are accustomed to fast, and even so they do not read from the Torah; end quote. And this is no proof, for there the reason is because they do not complete the fast; and so he wrote in Darkei Moshe, siman 582. And in the Levush he wrote: go out and see what the people do; end quote. And nowadays some have become accustomed to fast every erev Rosh Chodesh and read from the Torah, and this is certainly for the sake of repentance; hence they hold like the first opinion. Nevertheless where there is no custom, one should not practice so ideally; and so it is implied in Sefer Chasidim, siman 227; and see Beis Yosef and Sefer Ibbur Shanim, folio 64. And it is written in Kenesses HaGedolah, siman 568: the Tashbetz testified that his teachers decreed a fast upon the congregation and they prayed a communal prayer because of one who dreamed that the whole congregation should fast (Hagahos HaRif on Moed Katan); end quote.
