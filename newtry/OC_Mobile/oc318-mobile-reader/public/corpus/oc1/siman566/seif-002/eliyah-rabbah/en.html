[2] Vayechal: when they read Vayechal on a fast, the chazzan and the reader are silent until the congregation says "Turn from Your fierce anger" etc., and also "Hashem, Hashem" etc., for the congregation say it by way of petition, and the chazzan and the reader are not permitted to read it in the language of petition (Sefer Chasidim, siman 250). This implies that the chazzan and the reader do not say it with the congregation at all; only afterward they read; and see Rokeach, siman 211. Mateh Moshe, siman 756, wrote: the custom is to give charity at Minchah of a fast, for the reward of a fast is charity; and there is a person whose money is dearer to him than his body. "Taanis" [spelled with letters of] "tanis," and "ani" [poor]. And in a gloss I found written: "Righteousness and justice are the foundation of Your throne," the initials of which are "tzom" [fast]; on a fast day one should increase righteousness and justice; end quote. And for this reason they recite the haftarah "Dirshu" at Minchah after giving charity, for it is written therein "Seek justice and practice righteousness" (Tosafos, chapter HaKore). Some have the custom to estimate what one would have eaten on the fast day and give it to the poor in the evening. The kabbalists wrote to read on a fast day in Parashas Nitzavim from "And it shall be, when all these things come upon you" etc. until the end; end quote. And their intention as written, my mind does not agree except for those who know the hidden matters.
