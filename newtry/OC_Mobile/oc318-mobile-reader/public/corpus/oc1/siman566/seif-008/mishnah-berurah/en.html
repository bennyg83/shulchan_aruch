(23) And in these regions they do not raise [their hands], etc. - Nevertheless it appears from all this regarding saying "Our God and God of our fathers, bless us with the threefold blessing" etc., that in a place where according to the law there is nesias kapayim they say "Our God and God of our fathers, bless us" etc.; and see above in siman 129, se'if 1, and in the Mishnah Berurah there all the details of these laws and their reasons are explained.
