He should not pray [as chazzan]. It appears to me that if it happened that he passed before the ark, he should say in Shomea Tefillah Aneinu "on this fast day"; see the Tur and at the end of siman 119. And the same law applies if there is no other chazzan: it is better that one who is not fasting pray [as chazzan] than that they forgo hearing Kaddish and Barechu; see siman 53, se'if 7.
