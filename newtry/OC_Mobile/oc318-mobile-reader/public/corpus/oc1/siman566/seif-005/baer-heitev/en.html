He should not pray [as chazzan]. It appears to me that if it happened that he passed before the ark, he should say in Shomea Tefillah Aneinu "on this fast day," and the same law applies if there is no other chazzan: it is better that one who is not fasting pray [as chazzan] than that they forgo hearing Kaddish and Kedushah. Magen Avraham.
