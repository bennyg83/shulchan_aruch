(s.k. 5) Yoreh De'ah, etc. It appears to me that if among them there is one, etc. And the Tur wrote that even according to the one who says [they may arrange] even for the sake of a vav; and according to some opinions, even for the sake of one who did not hear Kaddish and Kedushah they say and arrange upon Shema, as explained in siman 68. There the reason is that one does not say a matter of sanctity with fewer than ten. And even though there are not ten who have not heard Barechu or Kedushah, in any case there are ten who attend and now say Barechu and Kedushah even if some of them have already heard. But regarding establishing a blessing, ten are required. And he wrote in Responsa Avodas HaGershoni, siman 57, that all the more so they should not read Vayechal. But to say the Selichos within the Shemoneh Esrei, in this he is lenient. But he wrote that one should skip these two words in the selichah that begins "Taanis tzibbur" (and some have "Tefillas tzibbur"); and likewise in the selichah that begins "Elokim beYisrael" etc., one should skip "pokedcha kodsho tzomos lekayem"; and likewise in the selichah that begins "Taanis tzibbur" etc., one should skip "hikdasheinu tzom olelim" etc.
And likewise wherever a fast is mentioned.
