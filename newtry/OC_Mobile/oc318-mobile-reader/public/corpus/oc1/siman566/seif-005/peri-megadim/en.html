And some have the custom; see Taz regarding erev Rosh Chodesh, when they practice to take out a Sefer Torah at Minchah to read Vayechal; and regarding Aneinu it implies that they should say it if they complete the fast, and if they do not complete [the fast] they should not say Aneinu as a blessing of its own; and see siman 562. And the reading of Vayechal requires further study in this: if they do not complete [the fast], whether they read Vayechal; and see siman 492, what is written there. And the Shovavim Tat fast that holy congregations practice, elaborating with Selichos at Shacharis: if so, one may say that at Shacharis they do not push aside the weekly portion on Thursday, and toward evening it requires further study; nevertheless one may say to read Vayechal in the evening, since he fasted, and also the chazzan says Aneinu at Shacharis and Minchah.
