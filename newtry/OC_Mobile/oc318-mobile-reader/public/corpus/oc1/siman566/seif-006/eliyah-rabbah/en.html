[6] [Levush] But a fast, etc. Like erev Rosh Chodesh, some fast for repentance; Magen Avraham wrote that in a place where there is no custom on erev Rosh Chodesh to call out, one should not practice so l'chatchila, end of his words. The same law applies that one should not say Aneinu between Goel and Rofeh. Teshuvot Tashbetz wrote that he saw from his teachers that they decreed a communal fast and prayed the communal prayer because one dreamed that the whole congregation should fast. Sefer Chasidim siman 60: a white moon is struck, a bad sign for enemies of Israel — therefore one must fast, end of his words. A congregation that decreed a fast and decreed that whoever eats pays a fine — one who ate beshogeg is exempt from the fine but may not eat until dark — responsum of Maharil 95.
