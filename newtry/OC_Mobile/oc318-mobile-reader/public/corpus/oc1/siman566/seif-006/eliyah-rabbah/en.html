[6] [Levush] But a fast, etc. Such as erev Rosh Chodesh, some fast for the sake of repentance; and Magen Avraham wrote that where there is no custom on erev Rosh Chodesh to read [Vayechal], one should not practice so ideally; end quote. And the same law applies that one should not say Aneinu between Goel and Rofei. The Tashbetz wrote that he saw from his teachers that they decreed a communal fast and prayed a communal prayer because one dreamed that the whole congregation should fast. Sefer Chasidim, siman 60, wrote: when the moon is eclipsed it is a bad omen for the Jewish people; therefore one must fast; end quote. A congregation that decreed a fast and decreed that whoever eats should pay a fine: one who ate unwittingly is exempt from the fine, but he may not eat until dark (responsum of Maharil 95).
