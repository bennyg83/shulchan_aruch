That he does not stand, etc. It appears to me that at Shacharis it is permitted to ascend even though they push aside the weekly portion and read Vayechal; nevertheless in any case they read from the Torah without the [special] fast, unless the fast is in the middle of the week. And the Bach wrote that if they called him to ascend, even at Minchah he should ascend, for there is no prohibition in this, only a mere custom; end quote. However, in Beis Yosef, siman 135, he wrote that even a kohen whom they called does not ascend; nevertheless it appears to me that there is no prohibition if he ascends.
The kohen went out. So that they not say that he is blemished; nevertheless if he is in the synagogue (another reading: in the house), there is no concern, for there is no blemish, since all know that there are people who are not accustomed to fast (Beis Yosef, siman 195). But if there is another kohen in the synagogue, it is obvious that he should enter the synagogue and hear Kaddish, and if he is concerned that they will call him to ascend, he should tell the chazzan not to call him.
