Who answers His people Israel. And the wording of the Rambam, chapter 2 of Tefillah, is "Who answers in time of trouble," and so it is in the Mishnah, chapter 2 of Taanis, and so it is in the siddurim.

Regarding what the congregation say "Turn from Your fierce anger" etc. and the chazzan repeats and says it: because the congregation say it by way of petition (Sefer Chasidim, siman 250); and in Terumat HaDeshen he explains because they said it by heart; and the same law applies to all the vows that they practice in Ashkenaz to say; see there.
