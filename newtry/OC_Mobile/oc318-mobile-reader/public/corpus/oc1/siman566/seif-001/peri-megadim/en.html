Who answers see Taz and see Magen Avraham, that one should say "Who answers in time of trouble"; and see Eliyah Rabbah, siman 565:3, that an individual in Shomea Tefillah should say "in every trouble and distress, for You hear," etc.; see there. And see siman 119 regarding a chazzan: if he says [Aneinu] in Shomea Tefillah, he concludes with two [themes], and it is one [blessing]; see there in Magen Avraham.
