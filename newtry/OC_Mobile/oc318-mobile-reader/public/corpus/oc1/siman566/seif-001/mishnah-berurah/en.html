(1) Between Goel and Rofei - for it is written "Hashem, my Rock and my Redeemer," and adjacent to it "May Hashem answer you on the day of trouble."
(2) Who answers His people Israel, etc. - And in our siddurim it is "Who answers in time of trouble," and that is the primary [wording].
(3) And they read Vayechal at Shacharis and at Minchah - that is, on every communal fast; but on Tishah B'Av at Shacharis they read "When you beget children" etc. and recite the haftarah from Yirmeyahu "I will utterly consume them," as above in siman 559, se'if 4 in the gloss; and only at Minchah is the reading like other communal fasts. Regarding what the congregation say "Turn from Your fierce anger" by way of petition, and likewise "Hashem, Hashem, God merciful and gracious" etc.: the one called to the Torah, and likewise the chazzan who reads, do not say those verses with the congregation; only afterward, when the congregation have finished, then the chazzan reads with the one called to the Torah. And it is proper to be careful that the chazzan wait until all of them finish, and afterward begin to read, so that all hear from the Torah.
(4) Whether it falls on Monday or Thursday - see below in se'if 2 in the gloss, that on fasts that are not fixed, according to our custom they do not push aside the weekly portion at Shacharis.
(5) And they read - Vayechal at Shacharis and at Minchah; and the same law applies that the chazzan says Aneinu between Goel and Rofei.
(6) At Shacharis they read the portion of Rosh Chodesh - And after the fact, if he read Vayechal, it appears that they do not make him repeat, for one may rely on the Yerushalmi which holds that even ideally they read Vayechal.
