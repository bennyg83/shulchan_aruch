Se'if 1 in the Shulchan Aruch, and they recite the haftarah at Minchah of Dirshu; and see Noda BiYehuda, Mahadura Tinyana, siman 100, whether if the third [oleh] is not expert enough to recite the haftarah, and the kohen and the Levi know [how], they may recite the haftarah, since they were called to the Torah first. And if they too do not know, another should recite the haftarah without any calling to the Torah; see there. And see what I wrote in Shaarei Ephraim, shaar 9, se'if 4.
