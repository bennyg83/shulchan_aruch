Shatz, Taz, and Magen Avraham, note 8, and Eshel Avraham here, and Kaf HaChayim note 6. Regarding reading Vayechal when there are not ten in the synagogue but there are ten in the city fasting — Aneinu is already explained that ten are required specifically in the synagogue. Regarding Vayechal in the morning when the fast falls on Monday and Thursday — even though they push off the weekly portion, there is no berachah levatallah here (for Ezra enacted reading on Monday and Thursday); if so, when there are six in the synagogue they read Vayechal, unlike at Minchah where ten are required in the synagogue, see there; the same applies in the morning when it falls on Sunday through Friday — ten are required specifically in the synagogue.
