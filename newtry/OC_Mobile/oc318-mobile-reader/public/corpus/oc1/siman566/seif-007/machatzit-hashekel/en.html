(s.k. 7) Not, etc. See the Tur. That is to say, he disagrees and holds that even ideally one who is not fasting may pray [as chazzan] and say Aneinu and establish it as a blessing of its own, only that he should say "on this fast day." And even though Beis Yosef agreed with those who disagree, that is ideally; and also after the fact, if he passed before the ark, it is enough in this that he not establish Aneinu as a blessing of its own, but say it in Shomea Tefillah and conclude only "Blessed are You, Hashem, Who hears prayer"; and since he said "on this fast day," it is fine. For he is not lying, per the Tur's reasoning, and he also did not establish a blessing of its own; and the congregation also fulfilled their obligation. For it is explained in s.k. [of Magen Avraham] on siman 119 that if the chazzan forgot to say Aneinu in its place, he should say it in Shomea Tefillah. And Magen Avraham wrote there that even if he concluded only "Blessed are You, Hashem, Who hears prayer," he also fulfills; see there. Therefore if the chazzan is not fasting, he should do so.
