One who enters where three people have eaten. It contains 1 seif: If three ate and are reciting Birkat HaMazon, and a person who did not eat enters, if he enters when the leader says, 'Let us bless Him of whose food we have eaten,' he responds after him, 'Blessed and praised be His name always, forever and ever.' If he enters when the others are responding, 'Blessed is He of whose food we have eaten,' he answers Amen after them. {Rama: Likewise, for every blessing that a person hears, he is obligated to answer Amen. [Tur; Beit Yosef in the name of Halakhot Gedolot and Semag]} If they are 10, he says, 'Blessed is our God, and praised be His name always, forever and ever.' The same applies if he was present when they finished eating but did not eat with them; this is how he responds after the leader and after those answering.
