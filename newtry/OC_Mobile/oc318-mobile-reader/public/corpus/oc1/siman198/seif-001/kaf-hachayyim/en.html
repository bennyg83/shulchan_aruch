1) [Seif 1] Blessed and blessed, etc. Levush's text reads, 'Blessed is He and blessed,' etc. Tosafos likewise writes this in the name of Halachos Gedolos. In Halachos Gedolos itself, however, the text follows the Shulchan Aruch. This is also the text in Rosh, the Tur, Rambam, Semak, and Tashbetz. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1.
