(1) [Seif 1] Blessed and Blessed is His Name, etc. — In Levush the text reads "Blessed is He and Blessed is His Name," etc.; and so Tosafot in the name of BeHaG; but in BeHaG itself it states as Shulchan Aruch's wording; and so the Rosh, Tur, Rama'im, and Sefer Mitzvot Katan and Tashbetz. Rabbi Akiva Eiger note (1).
