He cited in Shulchan Aruch seif 1: at the beginning of the night of the 14th we search, etc. It is stated in Rosh Hashanah daf 21: Rav Nachman said to those descending to the sea: you who do not know the fixing of the month — when you see the moon completing the day, on the 14th for you chametz is finished — end. And accordingly one traveling in the desert and the like who is not expert in fixing the festival must burn some days before, when the moon completes the day, so he not come to doubt; and I have not seen this mentioned among poskim.
