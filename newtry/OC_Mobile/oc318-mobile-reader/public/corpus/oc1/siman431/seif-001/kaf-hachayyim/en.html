1) [Se'if 1] At the beginning of the night of the fourteenth of Nisan, one searches for chametz, etc. Under Torah law, nullification alone suffices. One nullifies the chametz in one's heart and regards it as mere dust, and one then does not transgress because of it. The Sages nevertheless required its removal from the house because a person is accustomed to chametz throughout the year, and they were concerned that one might eat it if it remained in the house. Tur; Levush. Alternatively, since the Torah was stringent to make one transgress bal yera'eh if one does not nullify it, the Sages imposed an additional stringency. Magen Avraham, subsection 2, in the name of Tosafos at the beginning of Pesachim. They were stringent even regarding chametz nuksheh and a chametz mixture, for which bal yera'eh does not apply. Tosafos there; Beis David, siman 152. Even if one nullified the chametz and subsequently found half a kezayis, one must eliminate it. Radvaz, volume 1, siman 135. See below, siman 442, at the end of subsection 77.
