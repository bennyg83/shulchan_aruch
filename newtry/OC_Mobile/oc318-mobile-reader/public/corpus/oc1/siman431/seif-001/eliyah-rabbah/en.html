[1] [Levush] bal yera'eh, etc. — there is none, etc. — difficult from what Levush wrote siman 433 seif 6: behold all the time, etc., bal yera'eh, etc.; and there is to reconcile. And in Chok Yaakov at the beginning of siman 443 he wrote that in Rashi Bava Kamma daf 29b it is explicit that one actually transgresses bal yera'eh at night, and so it appears to one who examines Maggid chapter 3; but by day until night he does not transgress bal yera'eh as written "seven days bal yera'eh and bal yimatzei." And furthermore per his words Rashi's words contradict each other, for Maharsha explained on the Mishnah daf 10 that Rashi holds one does not transgress bal yera'eh until it darkens — and from this is the answer to what the last Kuntres of Sha'arei Ephraim explained on Tosafot chapter Kol Sha'ah: if he finds from six until it darkens, for if so he transgresses bal yera'eh — but in truth he does not transgress at all as I explained; rather Tosafot there refers within Pesach as I wrote in my responsum; and in any case it is impossible to explain Tosafot thus, for if so the answer of the Gemara on daf 46 is not answered at all — "perhaps you find after the prohibition," etc.; and what the answerer answered, that he challenges regarding before the prohibition, etc., is without scrutiny, for if so Maharshal's difficulty remains; also what Rashi wrote daf 4 s.v. keivan, etc., refers before Pesach — for if within Pesach, behold Rashi already explained above until here, there is no need to answer this as will be explained to one who examines; and furthermore per his view who explained Rashi's earlier words also before Pesach — there is also difficulty.
