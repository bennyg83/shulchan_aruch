And Eliyah Rabbah 5 and Chayyei Adam 1 ruled thus that in bein hashmashot he should be free and immediately at tzeit begin bedika — and see Magen Avraham 1 and 5 explained in this; and see Rashi Pesachim 4a s.v. nivdok bashes — at the beginning of six and at time of its prohibition he burns it, meaning there is a respite of one hour — part of the 24 for checking and no Torah prohibition; and some say a change that benefit is forbidden. And it implies from Rashi there is no distance for bal yera'eh; but on 4b s.v. vesorfim velo yamtin until seven implies they also made distance for teshbitu — at least one hour; and see 434 in Magen Avraham 7 and Taz 8 will explain whether they made distance for teshbitu. And Bach and Derishah may say therefore it said bedika requires being free and a courtyard whose light is great requires bein hashmashot specifically, and candle light that he not check on the 13th as in Yerushalmi — and see 433 more explained.
