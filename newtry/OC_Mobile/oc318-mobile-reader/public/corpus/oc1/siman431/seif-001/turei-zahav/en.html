At the beginning of the night of the 14th, etc. In the Gemara they challenge: since chametz from six hours is forbidden, checked at six? And you shall say: the diligent advance to mitzvot — let us check from morning, as it says "and Abraham rose early in the morning"; R' Nachman bar Yitzchak said: at the hour when people are in their homes, and candle light is good for checking — Rashi explained; but by day it darkens; and you shall say to check by daylight — behold we derive below searching by candles. The Tur also brought these two reasons, and it appears both are needed: in any case a clear hour is required to fix bedika therein — to say there is a change in that hour and an impression; therefore the Gemara challenged let us check at six when there is impression and change in Torah prohibition of chametz; and in the morning there is change because of advancement of the diligent accustomed at that time as Abraham; and at night there is no change except at the beginning when then a person's way is to come home from outside — but a person's coming is usual in the synagogue while still a bit of day, and he must wait until the candle shines; therefore he waits from time of his coming when there is change until the candle shines which is actual night; and that hour he waits should be completely free so he not delay nullifying bedika from the limited time — meaning there is change that it is adjacent to his coming immediately. And therefore both reasons are needed.
