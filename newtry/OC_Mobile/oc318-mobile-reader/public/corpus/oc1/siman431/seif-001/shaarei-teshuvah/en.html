(In Shulchan Aruch seif 1) We search for chametz — and he wrote in responsum Beit Yehudah that an onen is exempt from bedika and others check for him; and in Sefer Chayyei Adam he wrote that in Prague the custom is the onen transfers his share to his partner, and likewise for chametz he transfers by gift to partner or another and then the other blesses too; unlike if he does not check because of the transfer only as agency — requires study whether he recites the blessing.
