(א) (s.k. 1) At the beginning — and he explains close, etc. — for Ran in the name of Ra'avad inferred from our mishnah "light on the 14th we search," etc., and the Gemara challenges: let it teach "night of the 14th," and answers the tanna uses elegant language; and Ra'avad had difficulty — because of elegant language it is not fitting for the tanna to put darkness before light; if so, at the beginning of the night when there is still light it is fit to check, etc.; and he concluded: and from here you learn that biur must be advanced until daylight so one not be negligent — end of his words — and this is before tzeit hakochavim.

(ב) In any case bein hashmashot when there is still a bit of light. But in Sefer Chayyei Adam and Sefer Eliyah Rabbah they disagree that it requires specifically after tzeit, and they wrote one should also intend Ra'avad's view above — thus.
