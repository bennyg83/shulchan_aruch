(ב) We search. Not only per Ran who holds unknown chametz is d'oraisa and one must check every Torah doubt; but even if d'rabbanan one is also obligated, for one checks only where chametz is brought — as common doubt — Peri Chadash. Know that d'oraisa even for known chametz nullification suffices, unlike answer 1 that Kol Bo brought.
