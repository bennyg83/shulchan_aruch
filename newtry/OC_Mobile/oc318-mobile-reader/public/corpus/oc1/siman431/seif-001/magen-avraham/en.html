(ב) We search. D'oraisa mere nullification suffices — when he nullifies in his heart and declares it hefker it is no longer his and he does not transgress it; but they feared lest he come to eat it, therefore they required checking and removing it from the house. And that they did not fear so for other prohibitions — chametz is different for they do not separate from it all seven days; alternatively, since the Torah was strict on him to transgress bal yera'eh if he does not nullify, therefore the Sages were strict (Tosafot beginning of chapter) — and see what I wrote siman 442.
