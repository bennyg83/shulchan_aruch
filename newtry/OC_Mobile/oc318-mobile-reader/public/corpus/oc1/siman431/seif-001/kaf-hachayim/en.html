(1) [Seif 1] At the beginning of the night of the 14th of Nisan we search for chametz, etc. — and from what is said in mere nullification it suffices that he nullify in his heart and consider it as mere dust — then he does not transgress it; but the Sages required removing it from the house because a person is accustomed to it all year and they feared lest he come to eat it if it remains in the house — Tur, Levush. Alternatively, since the Torah was strict on him to transgress bal yera'eh if he does not nullify, therefore the Sages were strict on him. Magen Avraham s.k. 2 in the name of Tosafot beginning of Pesachim. And even in hard chametz — see mixture where there is no bal yera'eh they were strict — Tosafot there. Tur Yoreh De'ah siman 151. And even if he nullified and afterward found half an olive's volume he must burn — Radbaz part 1 siman 135. And see below siman 442 end of comment 17.
