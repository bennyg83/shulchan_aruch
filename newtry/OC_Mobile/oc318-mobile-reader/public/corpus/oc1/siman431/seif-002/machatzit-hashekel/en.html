(א) (s.k. 2) We search d'oraisa, etc. — meaning per Tosafot daf 4b and Ran wrote: even in what he nullifies and says to nullify and be as dust — this is not language of hefker; and if he declared his property hefker in this language it is not hefker; and especially per Magen Avraham siman 436 s.k. 13 as one who says even nullification in the heart helps and hefker in the heart is not hefker for all — Ran answered: since in truth chametz is not in a person's possession, for it is forbidden in benefit and it is not fitting he transgress bal yera'eh on it, but it is a decree of the verse and they made it as if in his possession to transgress bal yera'eh on it, as stated in Pesachim daf 6b — therefore in mere revealed speech removing it from his possession in thought suffices.

(2) I am chametz kaf AAG Debshaar Isorin AAG Dla Bedili Miniyahu MM A'C Laberam, that is, in Beh Vaarla and Klai Kerem T' the Tos' Rish Peschi' I am Heni Disoran Isor Olam Mash'ak chametz:

(ג) And the poskim wrote chametz is different for there is karet in it.

(ד) Alternatively — thus it should read.

(ה) Therefore the Sages were strict on him — and in Magen Avraham siman 442 at the beginning of the siman — dispute of poskim in a matter whose prohibition is only d'rabbanan whether one is obligated to remove it from the house — see there; and it appears from Magen Avraham's words he holds like poskim that bedika is not essential d'oraisa. And if he checked and did not nullify and afterward found chametz he transgresses bal yera'eh. And likewise Magen Avraham siman 434 s.k. 5; and some say there — even if he did not find and does not know whether there is chametz in his house he transgresses — see there; but Peri Chadash agreed to Ran's view that d'oraisa one of the two suffices — either nullification alone or bedika alone — but d'rabbanan requires both; and bedika alone is not enough as they say daf 6b lest he find a fine roll and his mind is on it — see Rashi and Tosafot; and nullification alone is not enough lest they come to eat it as Magen Avraham wrote here. He also agreed that both nullification and bedika help even for known chametz, and even for unknown but where it is usual to use that place for chametz — biur is required d'oraisa; not only for poskim who hold Torah doubt stringently d'oraisa, but even for Rambam who ruled Torah doubt leniently d'oraisa but d'rabbanan stringently — nevertheless in such a case where his intent is to use chametz there it is as if the prohibition was established and a doubt with presumptive evidence, which is for all d'oraisa.
