Every person must be careful, etc. Also Rabbeinu Yeruham and Ran in the name of Ra'avad proved from another verse that the essence of the mitzva is the beginning of the night when there is still light, even though Maharil in the law of the fast of the firstborn and likewise the customs of Mahari Abohav wrote that the mitzva of bedika is specifically at night but not before — nevertheless the main ruling appears according to Ra'avad, and there is no doubt they did not see Ra'avad's words on this. Know that Rambam in chapter 2 and Semak positive commandment 39 wrote one does not establish a derasha at the end of the 13th — it implies explicitly that even though night has not yet arrived, i.e. bein hashmashot, it is also forbidden, and likewise Kol Bo, and he concluded all the more so one should not engage in other matters. However Mahariv in laws of Pesach wrote and it appears to me that the same applies to Maariv — one who did not pray with the congregation is forbidden in melacha and study before he prays entirely, even though this prohibition was not mentioned in the Gemara nor in any composition; also in chapter 1 of Shabbat study prohibition was not mentioned — nevertheless one should be concerned for his words since they are words of reason as above. And in responsum of Ra'avad part 2 siman 79 he ruled regarding a sage who teaches the public morning and evening rulings in the synagogue — it appears that on the night of the 14th he may study; in any case it appears to me that if he told his friend to remind him of bedika when it darkens he is permitted to study, as the Sages permitted study on Shabbat night by the candle if he says to his friend "pay attention to me that I not err" — and likewise Beit Shmuel there siman 275.
