(2) R' Yeshaya the First proved that even for known chametz nullification helps d'oraisa, from what we learned: one going to slaughter, etc., and remembered he has chametz in his house — if he can, etc., and if not he nullifies in his heart; for if nullification did not help, how did the Sages permit him to do a mitzva that comes through transgression and to transgress bal yera'eh? And so is Ran's view at the beginning of Pesachim — see there; and so Rashbatz proved in his book on chametz — and Peri Chadash — see there.
