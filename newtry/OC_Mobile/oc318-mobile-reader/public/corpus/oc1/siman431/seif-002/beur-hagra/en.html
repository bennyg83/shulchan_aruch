(א) And be careful, etc. Tur.

(ב) That he not begin, etc. As written in chapter 1 of Berachot that one's prayer should be adjacent; and see Tosafot there that for study it is permitted as written in chapter 2 there — only melacha is forbidden; and all the more so here where even study is forbidden.

(ג) And he not eat, etc. And likewise here all the more so. And nevertheless Rama wrote as there before Maariv — even though study is permitted as written, if he is accustomed, etc.

(ד) And if he began, etc. Since it says "he should not open," etc. — and even in Shema which is d'oraisa, if he began permissibly he need not stop.

(ה) And some say. Since they said "lest," etc. — there is no distinction; and see Magen Avraham who wrote that this is when he began in prohibition and they disagree — one says since bedikat chametz is d'oraisa without nullification he must stop, and one says that nevertheless it is not d'oraisa until midday. But the words of the Tur do not imply so, and it appears his reason is since the essence of bedika is at the beginning of the night as written in Yerushalmi and as Ran wrote and derived from Yerushalmi from a verse — therefore we are concerned and it is as if there is no respite even though he does not finish completely, and as written "lest he delay," etc.
