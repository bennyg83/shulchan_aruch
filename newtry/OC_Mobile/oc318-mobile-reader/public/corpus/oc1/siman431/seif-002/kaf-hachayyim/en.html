2) Rabbi Yeshayah the First proves that under Torah law, nullification is effective even for known chametz. The Mishnah teaches that one who is traveling to slaughter an offering, etc., and remembers that chametz remains in the house, must return if able, but otherwise nullifies it in the heart. If nullification were ineffective, how could the Sages permit the person to perform a mitzvah that comes through a transgression and thereby violate bal yera'eh? Ran at the beginning of Pesachim likewise holds this opinion. Tashbetz in Ma'amar Chametz and Peri Chadash offer the same proof. See there. Erech HaShulchan, subsection 1. See below, siman 433, subsection 57.
