[2] [Levush] in any case prohibition of positive commandment of teshbitu, etc. — requires study in Pesachim daf 28, for R' Shimon who holds there is no lav prohibition only positive commandment — there is no Torah prohibition of benefit; and if so Levush who ruled there is no lav and afterward also ruled benefit is forbidden d'oraisa — he is not like any authority. Again I examined — in truth he holds like R' Yehuda that lav prohibition exists in eating from six and above, as he wrote in siman 447 seif 2 — therefore benefit is forbidden d'oraisa; only in delaying at home and not eating R' Yehuda admits there is no lav of bal yera'eh, only positive commandment of teshbitu; and so implies Rambam in simanim at the beginning of Laws of Pesach — examine; and in Hagahah Sha'arei Ephraim siman 3 he understood that whoever does not transgress bal yera'eh is permitted in benefit, and wondered on Beit Yosef siman 443 — see there where he elaborated and did not descend to all this; and in their responsum there that I wrote for another reason his proofs are nothing.
