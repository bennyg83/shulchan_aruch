(א) Slipped out. But in a new shoe it is forbidden to put the straps in on Shabbat, for it is metaken mana; and the same applies to a cloak or trousers. But a belt is permitted to insert into trousers, for he does not nullify it there, and it is made to insert and remove frequently — it is not called metaken mana (Beit Yosef end of this siman). And Magen Avraham wrote: if so, forbidden to insert a lace there, for he nullifies it there. Taz at the end of this siman wrote: according to what appears, even if his intent is to nullify it there there is no prohibition, since there is no knot at all — neither in the belt nor in the trousers by this — as is written in this siman.

(ב) And slipped out. Taz wrote: I did not know its explanation — as is written in this siman.

(ג) That he not tie. Meaning that he not make a knot at its head lest it slip out, for that is a permanent knot, as is written in siman 61.

(ד) Effort. Taz and Magen Avraham wrote there is a textual error in this gloss — as is written in this siman. And Magen Avraham wrote: if so, with our straps on shoes and trousers where they are accustomed to tie at their heads — even on old ones it is forbidden to insert them, as is written at the end of this siman.
