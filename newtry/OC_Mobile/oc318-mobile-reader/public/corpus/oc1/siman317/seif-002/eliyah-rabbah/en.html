[7] They slipped out, etc. In a new shoe it is prohibited to insert the straps on Shabbos because this repairs a utensil. The same applies to a robe or trousers. This concerns a lace that is intended to remain there. Something made for constant removal and reinsertion may be inserted even into a new item. Magen Avraham derives this from Tosafot, Rosh, and Hagahot Ashiri in chapter Bameh Tomnin. It also appears from there that even in an old shoe it is prohibited to insert a new lace that was never there previously. Taz's objection there to Beit Yosef at the end of this siman results from not having examined Tosafot and the cited authorities.
