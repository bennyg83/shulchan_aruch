(א) Slipped out. But in a new shoe it is forbidden to put the straps in on Shabbat, for it is metaken mana; and the same applies to a cloak or trousers. But a belt is permitted to insert into trousers, for he does not nullify it there, and it is made to insert and remove frequently — it is not called metaken mana (Beit Yosef end of this siman). And if so, it is forbidden to insert a lace there, for he nullifies it there.

(ב) That he not tie. Meaning that he not make a knot at its head lest it slip out, for that is a permanent knot, as is written in siman 61.

(ג) If it requires effort, etc. There is a difficulty: even if the hole is wide it is forbidden in a place where they are accustomed to tie, lest he tie; and where the hole is narrow, even in a place where they are not accustomed to tie it is forbidden, for it requires effort; and so Tosafos and Rosh explicitly. And it is possible there is a textual error in Shulchan Aruch; and if so, with our straps on shoes and trousers, where they are accustomed to tie at their heads — even on old ones it is forbidden to insert them, as is written in this siman.
