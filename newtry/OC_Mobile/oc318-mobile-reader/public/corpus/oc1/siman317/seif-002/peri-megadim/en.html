And there are those who disagree. In Taz: Rama in the gloss — Tur holds even a layman's permanent knot is liable; and there is a difference for disqualification of witnesses d'oraisa and the like; but for measuring a mitzvah there is no difference apparently, for even for Rambam layman's act with intent forever — exempt but forbidden — why permit measuring a mitzvah here? He will think to untie in seven days and the like, and it is better still. And granted a non-permanent knot and impossible without craftsman's act, as Magen Avraham wrote sign 3 — no choice to permit measuring a mitzvah; and possibly this is the intent of "see there"; and see Tosafot Shabbos; and with Hashem's help in Magen Avraham it will be explained further. And what Rama wrote: some say whatever is not made to untie that very day is called permanent — exempt and forbidden, as Magen Avraham sign 2; and some are lenient that until seven days it is not called permanent and permitted l'chatchila; and on this Taz wrote in sign 8 that he wrote it is not so, but whatever is not untied that day — exempt and forbidden.
