And specifically that he can return it, etc. — See Magen Avraham and Taz who challenged this and wrote there is a textual error; and because of the severity of the difficulty one can somewhat resolve that even though in the long commentary he copied Tosafos and Rosh's words, see there in his glosses here he retracted and ruled anonymously like Hagahot Mordechai in the chapter Bameh Tomnin: Rashbam ruled regarding straps of a cloak, etc., provided it is in that same matter that we need not be concerned lest he drive in — such as when the hole is large so he can return without effort; evidently he holds where returning is without effort we are no longer concerned he will come to tie and tighten [and what is cited from Tosafos and Rosh is not Rama's own citation, as is known]. A source for this view appears from Hagahot Mordechai from what they say there, in the Gemara 47b: if it was loose — permitted, for they do not decree lest he drive in, see there in Ran and Ritva; and according to Tosafos who hold even with a wide hole there is concern lest he drive in — therefore he holds there they are not accustomed to drive in; see Hagahot Maimoniyot there; and see in Eliyah Rabbah who wrote that also from Mordechai and Agudah it is implied he holds with a wide hole we are no longer concerned he will come to tie; nevertheless in practice it appears one should not depart from Tosafos and Rosh's words, that even with a wide hole it is forbidden where they are accustomed to tie, as I copied in Mishna Berurah in the name of the Acharonim that Tur and Rabbenu Yerucham too brought Tosafos and Rosh's method for practical law.
