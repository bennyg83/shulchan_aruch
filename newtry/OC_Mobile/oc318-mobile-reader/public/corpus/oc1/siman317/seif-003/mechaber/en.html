They untie the collar from a knot that a launderer tied that is not a permanent knot; but they do not open it anew, for it is fixing a vessel.Even if it was already opened, only that the artisan returned and tied it or sewed it together as craftsmen do (R' Yerucham chapter 14). And therefore forbidden to tear or cut a pair of shoes sewn together as craftsmen do, even though the sewing is not permanent, for there is no distinction in sewing between permanent and not permanent (Hagahot Mordechai chapter Bameh Tomnin). And some permit in sewing that is not permanent, and one should not be lenient before am ha'aretz (Beit Yosef).
