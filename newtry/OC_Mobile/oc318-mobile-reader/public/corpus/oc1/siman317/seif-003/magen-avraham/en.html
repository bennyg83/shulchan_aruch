(א) One may not open. And one who opens is liable to a chatas due to makeh b'patish; but due to tearing he is not liable unless he does so with intent to sew (Beit Yosef; and see Kesef Mishneh chapter 14 — not like Eliyah Rabbah).

(ב) That the craftsman returned and tied it. And he cannot untie it unless he cuts the threads — forbidden [Rabbenu Yerucham]. And Yeshuot Yaakov chapter 4 of Beitza siman 9 wrote: indeed, a shirt whose laces were tied and he cannot untie — permitted for everyone to undo it, for it is only made to be untied whenever he wishes; and even this he should not do before an am ha'aretz except privately, end of his words; and so Bach wrote. And Magen Avraham wrote: nevertheless it appears to me that if he is not accustomed to untie the shirt's knot except from Shabbat to Shabbat — it is forbidden, a place to sit.
