(א) One may open. And one who opens is liable to a chatas due to makeh b'patish; but due to tearing he is not liable unless he does so with intent to sew — Beit Yosef; and see Kesef Mishneh.

(ב) The craftsman. And he cannot untie it unless he cuts the threads — forbidden — Rabbenu Yerucham. And Yeshuot Yaakov chapter 4 of Beitza siman 15 wrote: indeed, a shirt whose laces were tied and he cannot untie — permitted for everyone to undo, for it is only made to be untied whenever he wishes; and even this he should not do before an am ha'aretz except privately, end of his words; and so Bach wrote. And Magen Avraham wrote: nevertheless it appears to me that if he is not accustomed to untie the shirt's knot except from Shabbat to Shabbat — forbidden, a place to sit.
