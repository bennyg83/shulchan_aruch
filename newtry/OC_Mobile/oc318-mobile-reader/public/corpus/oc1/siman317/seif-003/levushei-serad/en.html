Magen Avraham s.k. 4: That they tie it — meaning a artisan's knot, i.e., firm and strong; therefore they are stringent with two knots even if his intent is to untie that day, for according to Rif it is forbidden; and Magen Avraham's intent is that even with two knots, if we see it is not tightened at all — permitted to untie; but when tightened somewhat, we say we are not expert and perhaps this is called a firm knot.
