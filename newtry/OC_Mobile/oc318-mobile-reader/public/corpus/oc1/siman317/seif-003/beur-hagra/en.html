(א) Seif 3 "even if" already etc. As Rashi stated there s.v. "from its neck." For behold etc. but otherwise not, and see Hagahos Maimoniyos there:

(ב) "And therefore" etc. that there is not. From that which we say on Yom Tov 41b "he permits but not" etc., and Rashi wrote because it is not permanent and nevertheless it does not remove etc.:

(ג) And some permit etc. For there nevertheless the tying stands for permanence, and see Hagahos Maimoniyos that the Rav of Magdeburg challenged on what he stated "what is the difference" etc. "this is joining" etc. — it implies that if the neck-[closure] were joining it would be forbidden; but there is no building in vessels and no demolition in vessels, and he explained the opposite: "this is joining" — meaning joining of a barrel's neck is joining, and its work is already finished, and there is truly no building and demolition in vessels, and there is also no final hammer blow, for its work is already finished; but opening the neck-[closure] is not joining, for it needs opening and one is liable for the final hammer blow. And this is according to Rashi's and Ra'AM's view that there is no building in vessels at all, and one is not liable on vessels except for the final hammer blow; and see above siman 314 and Hagahos Maimoniyos chapter 11 siman 130 in the name of Ra'AM:
