One may untie the neck opening, etc. Beit Yosef brought in the name of Ram who wrote in the name of Kol Bo: a cloak and trousers tied together — one may not untie them on Shabbat, for any knot they do not untie that day — exempt but forbidden, end of his words. And it appears here it deals where they are accustomed to untie it that day. And Rashal wrote: if he cannot untie the knot with his hands — it appears to me permitted even to undo it; and this is the Gemara's law there: if the neck opening was sewn, R' Yehuda HaLevi permits undoing it since he does not make it permanent; so too Ravyah; but Rivash forbids undoing — specifically in sewing; but in tying that stands to untie the knot but he cannot untie with his hand — obviously permitted to undo, end of his words. And Morenu HaRav wrote: nevertheless one should not be lenient before an am ha'aretz; and so Beit Yosef in this siman. And it appears to me that those who hold there is no prohibition for more than one day — but according to Tur, who holds forbidden, since the strap-maker did not tie it to untie certainly that day but will do it through a non-Jew if he needs much — as stated.
