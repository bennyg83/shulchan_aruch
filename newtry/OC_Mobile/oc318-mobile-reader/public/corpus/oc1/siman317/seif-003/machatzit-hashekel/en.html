(s.k. 3) And for the need, etc. There is a difficulty why this law is needed, since we learn from the end of Shabbat in the days of R' Tzadok and in the days of Abba Shaul ben Batnis, etc., that they tied a moist reed to measure, etc., and from their words we learn that one ties for the sake of a mitzvah (I brought these Gemara words above in siman 313 in Magen Avraham s.k. 1). Therefore Magen Avraham questioned both the Gemara and Shulchan Aruch: why did they need a craftsman's act? Let him tie a layman's knot; and since it is not permanent, it is permitted even l'chatchila even for a mundane matter. Why transgress a shevut when the mitzvah can be fulfilled without shevut?
