[7] Slipped out, etc. But in a new shoe forbidden to put straps in on Shabbat, metaken mana; and the same in a cloak or trousers; and specifically a lace that nullifies it there; but a thing made to take out and insert frequently — even on new — permitted, as Magen Avraham deduced from Tosafos, Rosh, and Hagahot Asheri chapter Bameh Tomnin; and it appears to me the same law applies to an old shoe when inserting a new lace that was not there before; and what Taz challenged on Beit Yosef at the end of this siman — he did not look at the aforementioned Tosafos and poskim, see there and investigate.
