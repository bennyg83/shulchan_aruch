But one who ties a permanent knot and it is not a craftsman's act — exempt; and the same if lacking one of the two factors; and it is implied from Mechaber's words and from Rif and Rambam's words that even if it is a knot he can untie with one hand — since it is a craftsman's act and permanent knot, one is liable; but in Shulchan Shelakim he wrote in the name of Tosefta: any knot that is permanent and he can untie with one hand — one is not liable; or a knot that is not permanent and he cannot untie with one hand — also one is not liable, perforce. And it appears this follows R' Meir in the Gemara who said thus; but the Rabbis dispute him, and we rule like the Rabbis, see there section 3; and so Nachalat Shimshon section 1.
