(א) Of a weaver. And they did not decree lest an ordinary rope, as Magen Avraham wrote in seif 4, for here with a cow even an ordinary rope is not common that it would be permanent.

(ב) An ordinary rope — no. For in any case he leaves one end tied, perforce; and so Maggid Mishneh — not like Eliyah Rabbah.

(ג) But if it was tied, etc. Eliyah Rabbah did not look at siman 314 seif 9.

(ד) Permitted with any rope. For presumably that knot that was already there he leaves tied forever (Bach); and see in Mordechai on tzitzit how many types of knots there are, and one must be concerned for all of them, as is written in siman 458 seif 1. And Ran wrote in chapter 3 of Sukkah: per the one who holds a lulav requires an agud, it is forbidden to bind it on Yom Tov, since otherwise it would not be fit — it is metaken mana, perforce. And what is the reason it is forbidden to hang tzitzit on Shabbat, even with wrapping alone? Nevertheless it is possible that since with the upper knot it suffices d'oraisa and all other wrappings are only custom, perhaps permitted to make the wrappings and to make a bow — that is, tuck the end of the thread of a wrap into the middle of the seven threads and tighten them together with one knot; for this is Mordechai's view, that it is not permanent, for Rabbeinu Tam retracted there, end of his words: any knot that one sometimes considers and nullifies forever, even though at first he did not make it with intent to leave it there — forbidden; therefore forbidden to tie trouser straps that one sometimes considers and nullifies there forever until worn out (Beit Yosef, Kol Bo), as is written in seif 2. Rambam wrote in chapter 10: One who twists ropes of any kind is liable due to tying, and its measure is enough that the rope stand in its twist without a knot that is found — his melacha endures; and likewise one who separates the twist is liable due to untying, provided he does not intend only to ruin, and its measure is like the measure of twisting; and see Beit Yosef, and as is written at the end of siman 303.
