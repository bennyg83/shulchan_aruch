(א) One may move a weaver's rope, etc. For here are two prohibitions with an ordinary rope: one as Rashi explained — since he does not need it, we are concerned lest he untie one end; second according to Rambam — it would be muktzeh; but with a weaver's rope that he always needs, moving is permitted; therefore he mentioned moving here to teach that there is no muktzeh prohibition here; and afterward wrote there is not the second concern either. Beit Yosef wrote in the name of an Ashkenazic responsum: permitted to insert a belt into trousers on Shabbat, for he does not make a vessel and does not nullify it, and it is made to insert and remove — obviously permitted even on new ones, perforce. And I did not know why he needed to write "he does not nullify it"; and accordingly if his intent is to nullify it there and not remove — forbidden; and according to what appears there is no prohibition even in this, since there is no knot at all — neither in the belt nor in the trousers by this.

(ב) But if it was tied to the cow, etc. Beit Yosef wondered why Tur did not write this leniency; and in truth Tur wrote it in siman 305.
