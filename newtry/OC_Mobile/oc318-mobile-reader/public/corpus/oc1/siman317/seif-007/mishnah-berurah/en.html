(6) That very day itself — but tomorrow; however if it is made to be untied at Motzei Shabbat it is not called permanent at all and is permitted l'chatchila. Nevertheless if he ties on Friday night and intends to untie on Shabbat itself — even though not literally "that day," nevertheless some say it is permitted, for anything less than twenty-four hours in his day is called [such] — Peri Megadim.
