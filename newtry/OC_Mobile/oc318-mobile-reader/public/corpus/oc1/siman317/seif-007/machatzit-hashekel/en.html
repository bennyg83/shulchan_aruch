(s.k. 7) Slipped out; but in a new shoe forbidden, etc. For it is metaken mana; and so in the Gemara daf 48a: to insert wool into a cushion that never had wool inside — forbidden, metaken mana; but in an old one where wool was once inside and fell out — permitted to return it, for it is no longer metaken mana. If so, forbidden to insert a lace there — in a new one; but in an old one there is no metaken mana, and we should permit; nevertheless forbidden even in an old one for another reason, as Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 9, since they are accustomed to tie at their heads.
