Ends. And the same applies to two ropes one above the other; we do not say he nullifies one; but if he nullifies one end, even on one rope — forbidden — Magen Avraham. And the custom is to cut on Shabbat the binding of kids and fowl that were slaughtered and their sewing threads, for detached psik reisha is not forbidden unless he fixes a measure or makes a vessel; and even a rabbinic shevut does not apply — it is a complete permit, end of Shiltei Gibborim. And see siman 314 seif 9: any knot that one sometimes considers and nullifies forever, even though at first he did not make it with intent to leave it there — forbidden; therefore forbidden to tie trouser straps that one sometimes considers and nullifies there forever until worn out (Beit Yosef, Kol Bo), and as is written in s.k. 4 and s.k. 7. Rambam wrote: One who twists ropes of any kind is liable due to tying, and its measure is enough that the rope stand in its twist without a knot; and likewise one who separates the twist is liable due to untying, provided he does not intend only to ruin, and its measure is like twisting; and see Beit Yosef, and as is written at the end of siman 303.
