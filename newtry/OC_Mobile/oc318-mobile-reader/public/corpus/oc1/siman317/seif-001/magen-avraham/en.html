(א) It is called a permanent knot. And exempt but forbidden; and one is not liable unless it is made to endure forever or for a long time — ab initio.

(ב) That until seven days. Permitted l'chatchila.

(ג) And for the sake of a mitzvah, etc. Even a craftsman's knot is permitted; otherwise even without a mitzvah it would be permitted, and there is a difficulty: why must he make a craftsman's knot? Let him tie a knot that is not a craftsman's knot — nevertheless he can measure. And it must be said that it deals when it is impossible otherwise.

(ד) For we are not expert, etc. Nevertheless it appears from Rif's words that it is a knot that they tie tightly [Shulchan Gavoah].

(ה) And the same applies to untying it. That is according to Rambam; but for Rosh it is permitted, and so explicitly in Shulchan Gavoah there and in the Gemara the manner of the matter is stated that a craftsman makes for permanence — not like Eliyah Rabbah.

(ו) They did not decree. And in Sefer HaZichronot it is forbidden (so it is).
