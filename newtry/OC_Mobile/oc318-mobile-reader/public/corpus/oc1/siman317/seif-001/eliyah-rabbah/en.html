[1] [Dress] Whether of a artisan, etc. This is Rosh's view; and this is his language: sandal — sandal is not difficult: the one liable to a chatas is with the straps that endure forever; and the one exempt but forbidden is with the thin material of the straps — until here his language. Olat Shabbat challenged: why do we need "the straps" — let it be derived since it is not made to endure; and he answered: with a artisan's knot, even if not permanent, liable — until here. And this is not correct: if so it should be difficult why we need to establish at the beginning with straps that endure — let it be derived because it endures; rather initially nothing was difficult: what is established with artisan means presumably they make permanent ones; and what is established with straps means presumably not permanent; but when not permanent, even with artisan Rosh permits; and so appears from Rashi, Tur, Semag, R' Yehuda, and Shiltei Gibborim in the chapter VeEilu Kesharim explicitly.
