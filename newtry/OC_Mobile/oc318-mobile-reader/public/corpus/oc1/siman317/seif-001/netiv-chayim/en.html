(Taz s.k. 2.) It is difficult, for if so it would be included in pesik reisha. In answer: and likewise one can challenge regarding untying them on Shabbat; and to reconcile — for we are accustomed to say that women tie their shoes on Shabbat by bowing until Motzei Shabbat. And regarding untying them, the author of Terumat HaDeshen holds that there is no chatat liability for untying a knot except when one unties in order to tie like the work of the Mishkan, as the Rosh ruled; and since it is only a d'rabbanan prohibition, in a place of pain they did not decree, as Rama wrote in seif 1. But Rashi relocated from Terumat HaDeshen to explain it in chapter Kol Gadol, for he holds that one who unties is liable even if not in order to tie.
