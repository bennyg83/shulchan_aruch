One who ties. In Taz: Behold Rif and Rambam with Rosh and Tur disagree in two matters — both regarding liability and regarding exemption. For Rambam one is not liable until he is a artisan and forever; artisan alone or forever alone — not liable; for Rosh and Tur liable for forever alone. Regarding prohibition: for Rambam artisan alone is forbidden even if made to untie that day; for Rosh and Tur whatever is made to untie that day — even artisan permitted l'chatchila. For Rambam one may say permanent and not permanent means forever is called permanent; for a time, even half a year, is called not permanent — unlike Rosh, where there is liability, exemption, and permission, and it does not depend on artisan; necessarily one must divide between time and time — that day permitted; for seven days exempt; chatas liability for forever. And see there's help in Magen Avraham sign 3; and in Taz 3.
