5) There: And a sailor's knot. This too is like a ring made from a stick or strap in an opening at the front of a ship, and this knot remains in place. When one wishes to secure the ship, he ties a strap to that ring and secures it with it. When he wishes to release the ship, he unties and removes the strap. The first knot, namely the permanent ring at the front of the ship, is called a sailor's knot. Gemara and Rashi there.
