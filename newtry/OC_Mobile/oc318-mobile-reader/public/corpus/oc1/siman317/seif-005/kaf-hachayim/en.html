And the sailors' knot — likewise like a ring made from a stick or strap in a hole in the ship's prow; and that knot endures; and when they wish to moor they tie a strap in that ring and moor with it; and when they wish to untie they untie the strap and remove it — and that first knot, the ring in the ship's prow that endures, is called the sailors' knot — Gemara and Rashi there.
