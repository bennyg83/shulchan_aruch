[5] And nevertheless it appears that in a place of distress, etc. This is not correct: for Rosh there is concern of d'oraisa prohibition (Olat Shabbat); and it is not so — for Rosh permits l'chatchila even not in a place of distress, as I explained above; and it appears to be lenient even for any need, provided it is made to be untied that day; and this is Rabbenu Yerucham's language part 14: even if many are tied, such as many thread-ends, and he can with difficulty sometimes spread and wear with some threads permitted and others remaining tied — with all this permitted l'chatchila to tie all and untie them, since they are knots one ties with intent to untie them always every day; and likewise the hat they tie the hair in, even if sometimes they remove the hair with difficulty without untying knots, etc.
