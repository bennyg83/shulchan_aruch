And even if he made one knot, etc. Mordechai wrote: provided he not make a knot below the bow at all, but make two bows one upon the other; and it appears to me this custom of permitting applies to a knot standing to be untied that day, whose severity is only lest it be a artisan's act, as Rama wrote in seif 1; but where it is not made to be untied that day — such as a lulav and the like — forbidden unless he makes two bows one upon the other and no knot at all, as stated — obvious.
