A knot. The Acharonim wrote: this custom of permitting one knot beneath applies specifically when it is made to be untied that day, for it is not a artisan's act; but if it is for permanence, or where it is not made to be untied that day — such as a lulav and the like — forbidden to make a knot beneath; only a bow; and one may make two bows one upon the other, not a knot at all; and so one should practice.
