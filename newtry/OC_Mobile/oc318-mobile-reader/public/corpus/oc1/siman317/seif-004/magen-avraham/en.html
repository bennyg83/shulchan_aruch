(א) A bucket with a lace. And it is obvious that it deals when it is not a craftsman's knot according to Rambam, as is written in Hagahot Maimoniyot seif 1; and nevertheless they decreed regarding a weaver's rope lest an ordinary rope, for we find many similar cases in the Gemara, as above — not like Eliyah Rabbah.

(ב) With a rope. For he nullifies it there and it becomes a permanent knot; and even a weaver's rope — they decreed lest an ordinary rope.

(ג) It is not a knot, etc. For it is not made to stand there for a long time [Tosafos end of siman 61].
