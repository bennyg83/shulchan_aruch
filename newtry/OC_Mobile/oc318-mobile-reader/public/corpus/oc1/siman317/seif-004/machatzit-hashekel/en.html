(s.k. 4) For we are not expert, etc. Nevertheless the language appears inverted: Shulchan Gavoah wrote we are not expert what a craftsman's act is; nevertheless from Rif's words it appears it is a knot tied very tightly; therefore two knots one upon the other that are tied very tightly are a craftsman's act; therefore even if not permanent, according to Rif it should be forbidden; and so it is there in Shulchan Gavoah.
