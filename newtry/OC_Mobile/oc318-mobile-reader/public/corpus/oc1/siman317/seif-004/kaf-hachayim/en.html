(d) There. Such as the camel-drivers' knot — they pierce the camel's nostril and place a ring of strap and tie it, and it remains there forever; and when they wish to tie the animal they tie a long strap in that ring and tie with it; and sometimes they untie it — and that first knot, the ring in the nostril that stands there forever, is called the camel-drivers' knot — Gemara and Rashi beginning of VeEilu Kesharim.
