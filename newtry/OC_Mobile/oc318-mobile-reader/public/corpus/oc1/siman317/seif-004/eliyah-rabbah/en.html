[4] That one should be careful, etc. Meaning: since for Rif and Rambam it is forbidden l'chatchila with artisan, and we are not expert which knot they tie tightly is a artisan's knot — therefore we are careful even when untying that day, because two knots are a firm knot; and so Shiltei Gibborim wrote — the source of this law explicitly; and Olat Shabbat did not examine well.
