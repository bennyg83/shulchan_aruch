And specifically. In Taz: and so Magen Avraham sign 9 — and this is Rosh and Tosafos Shabbos 48a in the passage "if." And see Olat Shabbat, for behold for Rambam with layman's act and permanent — exempt but forbidden; one may well say where there is effort it is close that he will tie one knot at its head (Magen Avraham sign 8) — and they decreed even rabbinically when the decree is close, unlike when there is no effort they did not decree. And Rambam according to his method in what he does not return a worn garment to new, lest he sew — siman 302 in Magen Avraham sign 14, see there — not due to metaken mana; and therefore Rama emended well according to Mechaber in seif 1: there is no decree lest he tie — a decree upon a decree, unless there is effort to return it — then forbidden, for it is close he will tie so as not to trouble further. And Rama in seif 1 did not decide to say the halacha is like Tur on a permanent knot that one is liable; and with Hashem's help in Magen Avraham it will be explained further.
