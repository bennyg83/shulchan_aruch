(א) But not with a rope — He mentioned rope generally to teach that even a weaver's rope is forbidden; and like the Rabbis of R' Yehuda in the Gemara 113, as Rambam ruled, see there in Maggid Mishneh; and so appears Maggid Mishneh's own view; and so ruled Rabbenu Yonah and Ra'avad; and I saw in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah that he wrote Sefer HaTashbetz rules like R' Yehuda; and I did not find anything there; and also from Mordechai there is no necessary proof for this.

(ב) That are not fixed — See Magen Avraham who copied in the name of Tosafos that it is not made to stand there a long time; and here it is implied that according to Kol Bo brought above in seif 1 in the gloss, more than one day is called somewhat permanent and forbidden l'chatchila; nevertheless in practice one should not be stringent on this, for this language was copied also in many Rishonim — Rashba, Ran, Ritva, Rav HaMaggid — and all agreed on this; and from this is great support to be lenient where there is need, that even more than one day is not called permanent at all.
