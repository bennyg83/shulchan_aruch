5) There: One must gird himself with a belt during prayer, etc. Bach writes: "There is a prohibition against praying without a belt. Nevertheless, after the fact, Rashi and Rabbeinu Shemayah are sufficiently authoritative to rely upon, and he need not pray again." End quote. Knesses HaGedolah, in Hagahos Beis Yosef, paragraph 1, and Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2, cite his ruling. Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 4, however, writes that according to all opinions one who prayed without a belt fulfilled his obligation; see there. Nehar Shalom, paragraph 2, writes likewise and adduces proof from seif 1, which states that one who prayed without covering his heart fulfilled his obligation after the fact; see there. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1.
