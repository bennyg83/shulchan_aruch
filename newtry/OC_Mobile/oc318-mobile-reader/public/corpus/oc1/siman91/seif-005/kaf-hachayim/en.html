(5) There: one must gird with a belt at the time of prayer, etc. — Bach wrote: "and there is a prohibition if one prays without a belt; however, bedieved it is fitting to rely on Rashi and Rabbeinu Shemaya bedieved, and one need not return and pray," etc. They brought this in Sheyarei Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef, letter 1; Eliyah Rabbah, letter 2. But Maamar Mordechai, letter 4, wrote that according to all, if he prayed without a belt he fulfilled; see there. And likewise Nishmas Shabbat, letter 2, and he brought proof from seif 1, that if he prayed without covering the heart bedieved he fulfilled; see there. Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letter 1.
