In his pouch, etc. Maharam siman 38 enacted not to enter with shoes in the synagogue. See there. One may not pray in linen market-hose, for it is disgraceful to stand before great ones in linen market-hose; and how much more when wearing a sandal with exposed heel. Also one may not wear arm-garters called entshikh as travelers do; and I apply to them: "Let not the foot of pride come upon me, and let not the hand of the wicked move me." Bach. However, if the garment is long and covers the feet, or in very hot lands where they also stand barefoot before great ones — there is no concern, even if his garments are short.
