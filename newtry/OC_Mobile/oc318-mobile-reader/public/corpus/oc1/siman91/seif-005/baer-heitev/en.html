In his money belt, etc. Maharam Mintz, siman 38, instituted that one should not enter the synagogue wearing shoes; see there. One should not pray wearing linen stockings, for it is undignified to stand before dignitaries in linen stockings, and certainly when wearing sandals with the heel exposed. One should also not wear gloves, called hentshikh, in the manner of travelers. I apply to them the verse, “Let not the foot of pride come against me, nor the hand of the wicked move me.” Bach. However, where the garment is long and covers the feet, or in very hot countries where people also stand barefoot before dignitaries, there is no concern even when the garments are short.
