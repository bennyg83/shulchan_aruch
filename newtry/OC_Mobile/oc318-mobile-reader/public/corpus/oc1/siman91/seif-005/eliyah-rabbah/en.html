(5) Placing his hand, etc. — Rashal permits, and Magen Avraham wrote that one may not rely on him; up to here. And it seems specifically regarding uttering a holy matter from his mouth; but regarding walking four amot they rely on him, and thus it is implied in Taz, siman 8, seif-katan 3. And even to utter a holy matter, one may be lenient in a time of pressure; and thus it is implied in Olas Tamid, siman 2.
