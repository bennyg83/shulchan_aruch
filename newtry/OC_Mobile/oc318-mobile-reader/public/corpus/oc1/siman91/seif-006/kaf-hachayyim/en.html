6) There: One must gird himself with a belt, etc. Sefer Chasidim, siman 773, likewise writes that when a person prays, the belt must be fitted closely around his waist; see there. Levush explains the reason: when a person girds a belt around his waist, he appears to stand with greater readiness than when he is without a belt. See what the commentators write at the beginning of II Kings, chapter 9, concerning the verses "Elisha the prophet called," etc., and "He said to him, gird your waist," etc.; and in Jeremiah, chapter 1, concerning the verse "You shall gird your waist," etc. Analyze this carefully.
