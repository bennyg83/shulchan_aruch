(6) Exposed, etc. — Bach wrote: I received that one may not pray in linen market-hose alone unless he wears over them pozmeki called long zokin, for since it is disgraceful to stand before princes and great ones thus; and how much more so one must rebuke those who wear linen market-hose and wear open-backed shoes and pray with heel exposed; and some of them wear arm-garters called entshukh. And if in a long garment that covers the feet, or in very hot lands where they stand barefoot before great ones — it is permitted; thus wrote Sheyarei Knesset HaGedolah.
