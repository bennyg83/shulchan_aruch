[6] Exposed, etc. The Bach writes: I received a tradition that one should not pray wearing only linen stockings unless he wears over them long socks called zaken, since it is undignified to stand in this manner before rulers and dignitaries. Certainly one should rebuke those who wear linen stockings and slippers called pantoflin while praying with their heel exposed. Some also wear gloves called hentshukh. If, however, one wears a long garment that covers the feet, or in very hot countries where people stand barefoot before dignitaries, it is permitted. So writes Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah.
