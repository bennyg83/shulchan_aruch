(6) There: one must gird with a belt, etc. — and likewise in Shaarei Teshuvah, siman 493: when a person prays, the belt must be fastened on his waist; see there. And the reason: the Levush wrote that when one girds a belt on his waist he appears to stand more alert than without a belt. And see what the commentators wrote in II Kings, section 9, in the verse "and Elisha the prophet called," etc., "and he said to him, gird your loins," etc.; and in Jeremiah, siman 1, in the verse "and you shall gird your loins," etc.; examine this.
