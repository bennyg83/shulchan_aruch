He must, etc. — see Mishna Berurah, and it is from Bach; and Bach's words are puzzling, for they imply that for the poskim who disagree with Rashi and Rabbeinu Shemaya, even bedieved one returns and prays again. And this is because it is not as stringent as one who did not cover his heart at all and is naked from his waist upward — for certainly there is no issue of improper intent, and nevertheless he does not return, per Maggid Mishneh. And perhaps Bach's intent is that bedieved one may rely on Rashi and Rabbeinu Shemaya also regarding his heart seeing the nakedness — that he does not return and pray again; uncertain.
