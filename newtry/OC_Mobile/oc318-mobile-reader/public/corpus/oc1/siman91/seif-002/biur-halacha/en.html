He must, etc. - See Mishnah Berurah. This ruling is from the Bach, but the Bach’s words are surprising, for he implies that according to the authorities who disagree with Rashi and Rabbeinu Shemaya, one must repeat the prayer even after the fact. This is incorrect, for this cannot be more stringent than a case where one did not cover his heart and was naked from the waist upward, which certainly does not violate “Prepare to meet,” yet even there he does not repeat the prayer, as the Maggid Mishneh states. Perhaps the Bach means that after the fact one may rely upon Rashi and Rabbeinu Shemaya even where his heart sees the nakedness, and therefore he need not repeat the prayer. This requires study.
