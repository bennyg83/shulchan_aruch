(2) And if his hair is long and covers the opening of his ear — he must remove it at the time of prayer so that the opening of his ear be exposed at the time of prayer, as written in the Tikkunim, Tikkun 70: "In Gematria one must remove hair from above the ears so that the gates of hearing of prayer not be blocked, for through them one hears the prayers of the One of whom it is said 'He Who hears prayer' — to You all flesh shall come,'" etc. And he wrote further in that tikkun: "Ears contain chambers and corridors, etc.; if the ears below are not blocked, so the gates of hearing above toward Him are not blocked — and they are the gates of which it is said 'He Who hears prayer,' 'Hear, O Israel' — from there one must intend in hearing," etc. And see there further, where he wrote that hearing is in Binah; see there. And he wrote in Sefer Tzavo'os Chaim that one must also remove hair from above the lips at the time of prayer so the mouth not be blocked; see there. And they brought Petach Einayim, letter 1; and likewise Mahari Saruk, disciple of the Ari z"l, letter 55, that one must remove hair from above his ear and around his mouth at the time of prayer. And they brought Yafeh Lelev z"l in Kovetz Yosher Levav, siman 92, letter 3.
