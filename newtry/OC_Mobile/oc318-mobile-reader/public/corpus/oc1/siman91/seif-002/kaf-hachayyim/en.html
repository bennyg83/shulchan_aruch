2) If his hair is long and covers the opening of his ear, he must remove it during prayer so that the opening of his ear is exposed while he prays. Tikkunei Zohar, tikkun 70, states: "For this reason, one must remove the hair from over the ears so that the gates through which prayer is heard will not be closed. Through those gates, the One of Whom it is said, 'You hear prayer; to You all flesh shall come,' hears the prayers." End quote. It further states in that tikkun: "The ears contain chambers and porticoes, etc. If the ears below are sealed, the corresponding gates of hearing above are likewise sealed to them. These are the gates concerning which it is said, 'You hear prayer' and 'Hear, O Israel.' From there one must direct one's intention through hearing." End quote. See there further, where it writes that hearing is in Binah. Totzaos Chaim writes that one must also remove the hair from over his lips during prayer so that it will not obstruct his mouth; see there. Petach Einayim, paragraph 1, cites his ruling. Rabbi Yisrael Sarug, a disciple of the Arizal, likewise writes in paragraph 55 that during prayer one must remove the hair from over his ears and from around his mouth. Yafeh LaLev cites his ruling in the kuntres Yosher Levav, siman 92, paragraph 3.
