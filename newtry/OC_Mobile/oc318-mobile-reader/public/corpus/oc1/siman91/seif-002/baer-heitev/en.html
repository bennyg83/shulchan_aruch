To gird with a belt. Specifically one who is accustomed to gird with a belt all day; but one who goes all day without a belt — even at the time of prayer he need not gird. Beit Yosef, Rabbeinu Yeruham.

Pants. The same applies if his garments rest directly on his stomach and separate between his heart and his nakedness. See siman 74, Magen Avraham.
