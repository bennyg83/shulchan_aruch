(4) He must - After the fact, if he prayed without a belt, he has fulfilled his obligation. Some further say that this applies specifically to one who ordinarily wears a belt throughout the day, but one who goes without a belt throughout the day need not gird himself even during prayer. Nevertheless, it is a pious practice even in such a case. Magen Giborim.
(5) A waistband - Meaning the waistband of the trousers, which separates between his heart and the nakedness.
(6) Because of “Prepare to meet,” etc. - It is stated in the Zohar, Vaeschanan: One who stands in prayer must cover his head and eyes so that he not gaze at the Shechinah. This refers to a tallis used for a mitzvah, and the current practice is likewise to cover the head down to the eyes with a tallis during the Amidah. Peri Megadim. One who opens his eyes during prayer causes the Angel of Death to precede him, etc. See siman 95, Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 5.
(7) Other blessings - The same applies to Krias Shema, since “Prepare to meet your God” applies only when one stands before the King.
(8) Trousers - The same applies where his garments lie tightly against the abdomen and separate between his heart and the nakedness.
