(4) Must — in retrospect if one prayed without a belt he has fulfilled; and some say further that specifically one who is accustomed to a girdle all day [needs it], but one who goes all day without a girdle need not gird even at prayer time. Nevertheless, conduct of piety applies even in such a case [Magen Gevuros, etc.].

(5) Waistband — meaning the waistband of pants that separates between his heart and nakedness.

(6) Because of "prepare to meet, etc." — it is in the Zohar, Va'etchanan: one who stands in prayer must cover his head and eyes so he not gaze at the Divine Presence. [And this is with a tallit of mitzvah; and so is the custom today to cover the head until the eyes with a tallit katan at the prayer of the Eighteen Benedictions. Peri Megadim.] One who opens his eyes during prayer — the angel of death precedes him, etc. — and see siman 95 in Mishna Berurah, S.K. 5.

(7) Other blessings — and the same applies to Keriyas Shema, for "prepare to meet your God" does not apply except when standing before the King.

(8) Pants — and the same applies if his garments are placed pressed directly on the stomach and separate between his heart and nakedness.
