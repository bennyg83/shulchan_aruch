Since he covered his nakedness — it seems this refers to what is discussed at the beginning of the seif, that the tallis was girded on his waist so that his heart does not see the nakedness either; but if his heart sees the nakedness — such as if he wore a robe without pants — even though it is only rabbinic, as Peri Megadim wrote in siman 74, nevertheless he must return and pray again; and this is proven from Chayei Adam, law 6 of the things one must be careful about during prayer and Kerias Shema. See Chayei Adam, general rule 3, law 33; uncertain in practice.

He fulfilled — see the Rambam, who concludes "and lechat'chila one should not do so," and they strained themselves in his explanation; and it seems he informed us regarding one who is compelled and has nothing with which to cover — lechat'chila it is better not to pray at all. Later I found this in Maggid Mishneh. And it seems only for one who is compelled do they permit Kerias Shema from the waist upward while naked at the end of siman 75, but not otherwise. And thus I found in Sefer Tevuos Shor, siman 1, seif-katan 71; see there.
