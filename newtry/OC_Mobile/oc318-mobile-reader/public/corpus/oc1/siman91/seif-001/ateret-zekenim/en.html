Until he covers his heart, etc. A person must prepare his garments for prayer; thus the ancients would adorn themselves in fine garments at the time of prayer (Tur). Therefore one may not pray in linen hose alone unless he wears over them pozmeki called zokin; and how much more so one must rebuke those who wear linen hose in the marketplace and wear open-backed shoes with the heel exposed (my teacher in Bach) in the name of his teacher. Likewise it is forbidden to wear at the time of prayer arm-garters called entshikh; and one who does so — upon him is said this verse: "Let not the foot of pride come upon me, and let not the hand of the wicked move me." And thus I received in the name of the Rav Maharar Yaakov Falk z"l.
