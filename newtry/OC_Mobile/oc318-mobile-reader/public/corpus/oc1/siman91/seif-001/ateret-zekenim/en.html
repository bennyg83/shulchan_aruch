Until he covers his heart, etc. A person must prepare his clothing for prayer. The early generations adorned themselves with fine garments when praying. Tur. One should therefore not pray wearing linen leggings unless he wears stockings, called zokin, over them. All the more should one rebuke those who wear linen leggings with slippers that leave the heel exposed. My teacher Bach in the name of his teacher. One may likewise not wear gloves, called hentschich, during prayer. Of one who does so the verse says, 'Let not the foot of pride come upon me, nor the hand of the wicked move me.' I received this in the name of R' Yaakov Pollak, of blessed memory.
