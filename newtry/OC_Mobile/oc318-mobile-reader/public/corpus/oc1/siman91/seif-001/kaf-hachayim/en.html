(1) [Seif 1] If the tallis was girded, etc., until he covers his heart — Bach wrote that one must cover his entire body as if standing before the king in awe. And that which was taught: "and for prayer, until he covers his heart" — it does not mean specifically the heart; rather his entire body must be covered. And it did not mention the heart except because in the first part regarding Kerias Shema one need not cover the heart; therefore in the latter part regarding prayer it says one must cover the heart — and the same applies: his entire body must be covered, etc.; see there. And they brought Yafeh Lelev z"l, letter 1; and likewise we wrote above in siman 74, letter 24; see there.
