1) [Seif 1] If a tallis was wrapped around his waist, etc., until he covers his heart. Bach writes that one must cover his entire body, as though he were standing in awe before a king. The teaching, "For prayer, until he covers his heart," does not mean specifically his heart; rather, he must cover his entire body. It mentions the heart only because the first clause states that for Krias Shema he need not cover his heart, and the latter clause therefore states that for prayer he must cover his heart. It is indeed true that he must likewise cover his entire body, etc.; see there. Yafeh LaLev, paragraph 1, cites his ruling. We likewise wrote this above in siman 74, paragraph 24; see there.
