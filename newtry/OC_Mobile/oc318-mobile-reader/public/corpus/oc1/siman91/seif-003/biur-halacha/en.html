And some say that one should protest, etc. - Regarding what is written above in siman 2, that even without uttering Hashem’s Name and outside a synagogue it is forbidden to walk with an uncovered head, see Baer Heitev. The Peri Megadim answers that above the prohibition applies specifically to four amos, whereas here it applies even to less than four amos. See what we wrote above there in the name of the Taz, that in our times it is forbidden by law in every place, even for less than four amos.
