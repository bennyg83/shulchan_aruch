(3) There: and if he did not cover his heart, or was compelled and has nothing with which to cover — since he covered his nakedness and prayed, he fulfilled. Thus wrote the Rambam z"l, chapter 4 of Hilchos Tefillah, law 7, and concluded there: "and lechat'chila one should not do so," etc. The Tur brought this, and from his words it is implied that whether it states "or" or bedieved, or that he has nothing with which to cover — lechat'chila he may do so, for since he is compelled it is considered bedieved. And what he wrote "and lechat'chila one should not do so" applies to one who can find covering easily. And likewise the Levush: "bedieved, or that he is compelled and has nothing," etc. — it is explicitly implied that he understood the Rambam's words that whether it states "or" applies; and likewise Nishmas Shabbat, letter 1, and he wrote that this is not like Maamar Mordechai, letter 1, who wrote the opposite; see there.
