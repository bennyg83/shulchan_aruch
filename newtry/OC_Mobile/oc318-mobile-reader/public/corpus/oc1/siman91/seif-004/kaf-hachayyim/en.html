4) [Seif 2] One must gird himself with a belt during prayer, etc. One who goes without a belt throughout the day need not gird himself during prayer. Beis Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham; Olas Tamid, paragraph 1; Magen Avraham, seif katan 1; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2; Solet Belulah, paragraph 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 2. Nevertheless, it is a pious practice to wear one even in such a case. Magen Gibborim; Mishnah Berurah; Petach Einayim, paragraph 3.
