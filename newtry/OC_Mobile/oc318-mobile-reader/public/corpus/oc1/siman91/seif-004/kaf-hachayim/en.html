(4) [Seif 2] One must gird with a belt at the time of prayer, etc. — and one who goes all day without a belt need not gird at the time of prayer. Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yeruham; Orchos Tzaddikim, letter 1; Magen Avraham, seif-katan 1; Eliyah Rabbah, letter 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, letter 1; Razin, letter 2. However, it is a measure of piety even in such a case. Magen Giborim, Mishna Berurah, Petach Einayim, letter 3.
