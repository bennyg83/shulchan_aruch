Seif 4: “Hats,” etc. As stated in Tosefta, chapter 2, regarding a naked person: If one was standing naked in a field or performing his work naked, he covers himself with straw, stubble, or anything else. Although they said that it is not praiseworthy for a person to sit naked, he may cover himself with straw, stubble, or anything else, etc. See the Gemara, Berachos 25a. Likewise, Kesubos 72a states that a basket worn on the head is acceptable in an alleyway, and on a Torah level even in the marketplace, certainly in a case such as this. In chapter 16 of Shabbos, “he wraps a reed around it,” etc., because he maintains that it is forbidden to stand, etc.
“But placing,” etc. See above, siman 74, seif 5.
