Seif 4: Considered covering. And likewise hairs called peruk (wig). Nevertheless because of "fear of the place" one should be careful. Possibly specifically regarding mentioning the Name; but regarding going four cubits with uncovered head, which is only piety as Magen Avraham wrote S.K. 3 — one may say that with a peruk covering it is permitted and there is no concern for "fear of the place" here. See Beit Yosef below siman 301, brought in Taz there S.K. 27.
