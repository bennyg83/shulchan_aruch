Seif 3 Some say. In Piskei Mahari, siman 203, he writes: We have not found an explicit objection establishing a prohibition against mentioning Hashem’s Name with an uncovered head.
Seif 4 It is considered a covering. The same applies to hair called a peruk. Nevertheless, one should be careful because of maris ayin. Olas Tamid. Perhaps this applies specifically to mentioning Hashem’s Name. Regarding walking four amos with an uncovered head, which is only a pious practice as the Magen Avraham writes in seif katan 3, one may say that a peruk is a permitted covering and there is no concern for maris ayin. See Beit Yosef below, siman 301, cited by the Taz there, seif katan 27.
