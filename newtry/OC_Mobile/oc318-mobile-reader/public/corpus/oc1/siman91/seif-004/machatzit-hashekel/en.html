(S.K. 4) Placing the hand — Maharshal in responsa siman 72 permits, and he wrote that although placing the hand on the heart so that one's heart not see the nakedness does not help as stated in siman 74 — that is because it is the way of a person to place his hand on his heart and this does not appear as covering; but to place his hand on his head is not the way of a person, therefore it appears fine as covering.

And it seems to me one should not rely on him. And Taz above, siman 8, decided that regarding uncovering the head all day it is not according to Maharshal; rather it is conduct of piety. And likewise according to Taz there, because of "and in their statutes you shall not walk" — hand-covering helps even for the prohibition of "in their statutes you shall not walk" — that is, for we see that they altered Gentile customs and place the hand on the head; but at the time of prayer and mentioning the Name it does not help.
