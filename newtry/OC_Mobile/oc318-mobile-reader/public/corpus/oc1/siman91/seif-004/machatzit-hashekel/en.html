Seif katan 4: Placing a hand. The Maharshal, responsum 72, permits this. He writes that although placing one’s hand over the heart, so that the heart not see the nakedness, is ineffective, as stated in siman 74, that is because people commonly place a hand over the heart, and it therefore does not appear to be a covering. Placing a hand upon the head, however, is not a person’s ordinary manner, and therefore it properly appears to be a covering.
It appears to me that one should not rely upon him. The Taz above, siman 8, ruled that regarding keeping one’s head uncovered throughout the day, which according to the Maharshal is only a pious practice, and according to the Taz there is prohibited because of “You shall not follow their statutes,” covering with the hand is effective. Even the prohibition of following their statutes does not apply, since we see that he deviates from gentile practice by placing his hand upon his head. During prayer or when mentioning Hashem’s Name, however, it is ineffective.
