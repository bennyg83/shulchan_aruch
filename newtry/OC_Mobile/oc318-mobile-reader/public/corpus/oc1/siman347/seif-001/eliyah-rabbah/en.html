[1] And he took it outside, etc. Even if he did not place it on the ground, since it remains in his hand it is regarded as resting on the ground, as stated by the Rambam. If he placed it into his fellow's hand while the fellow stood outside, the Rashba says liability applies specifically when the poor person's hand is within ten handbreadths of the public domain. Some disagree, as stated by the Maggid Mishneh in chapter 13. This requires study because the beginning of this siman appears to follow the first view, which the Beit Yosef cites in the name of the Maggid Mishneh.
