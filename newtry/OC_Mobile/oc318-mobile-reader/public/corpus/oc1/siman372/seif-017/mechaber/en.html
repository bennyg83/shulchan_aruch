If one placed a plank over the trench like a bridge, from one edge of the trench to the other, and it is 4 tefachim wide, it is considered an opening. The 2 courtyards may make one eruv together, or, if they wish, they may make 2 separate eruvin. {Rama: The same applies if one filled a width of 4 tefachim with material that he permanently leaves there. [Beit Yosef]} If the plank was laid lengthwise along the trench for a distance of 4 tefachim, then even if its width is only the smallest amount it is considered an opening, because it reduced the trench's width from 4 tefachim.
