(17) And it is forbidden to carry from it, etc. Likewise, within it one may carry only within four amos, as explained above in siman 346, seif 3.
(18) To another karpef. This means another karpef of the same status, larger than beis sasayim and not enclosed for residential use. If the other karpef is no larger than beis sasayim, or is larger but was enclosed for residential use, it is a full reshus hayachid. One may not transfer anything between it and this karpef, which is a karmelis. See the Acharonim.
(19) Only two amos, etc. Transfer from one karmelis to another is permitted, as stated above in siman 346, seif 3. This applies only to utensils that were in those areas when Shabbos began. Utensils that were in a house when Shabbos began and were brought into the karpef by a non-Jew or in a similar manner may not be transferred to another karpef.
