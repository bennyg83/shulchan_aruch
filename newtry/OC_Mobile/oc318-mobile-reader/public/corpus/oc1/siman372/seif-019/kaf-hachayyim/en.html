One person brings it on behalf of everyone, etc. The same applies when a father and his children who receive their sustenance from him live in one courtyard, or a teacher and his students who receive their sustenance from him. We rule that whenever no other residents live in the courtyard, they need not establish an eruv, as stated in siman 370. If they wish to establish an eruv with the adjacent courtyard, one person represents all of them. Beis Yosef. He may even provide the food from his own property. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 4.
