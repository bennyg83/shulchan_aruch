A stack of straw between two courtyards — if it is ten high, each joins for itself. If it was reduced during the week from ten they need to join together. And while it was not yet reduced it is forbidden for any of them to place from the straw into his box on Shabbat to feed his animal — and even to stand it there so it eats is forbidden. But if he can stand before it so that it has no way to turn aside but only to there —' it is permitted. And per the Rosh, even during the week it is forbidden to place from it into his box and likewise to position the animal by hand.
