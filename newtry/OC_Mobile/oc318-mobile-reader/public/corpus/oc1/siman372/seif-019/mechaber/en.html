A stack of straw between two courtyards: if ten high, each makes its own eruv. If reduced below ten during the week, they must join. While still ten high, neither may remove straw on Shabbat for his animal—even to place the animal there to eat. But if he can stand before it to prevent it turning elsewhere, that is permitted.' According to the Rosh, even during the week one may not remove straw or position the animal by hand. This applies to a stack between courtyards;' between two houses, one may feed his animal from it by hand.
