Ditch between two courtyards, etc. — Beit Yosef wrote R' Yonatan: even if only ten amot of ditch closed and ditch remained on sides — like opening; one or two eruvs; not in mishnah because uncommon. I do not know why ten required or why "not in mishnah" — every such case implies four tefachim suffice, all the more with board four. In my humble opinion R' Yonatan's words apply to "placed board on it" — do not say only board four then depends on will; if more than four cannot make two eruvs as breach — teaches even closed to ten with ditches on sides not breach but opening, two eruvs — R' Yonatan's intent is clear:
