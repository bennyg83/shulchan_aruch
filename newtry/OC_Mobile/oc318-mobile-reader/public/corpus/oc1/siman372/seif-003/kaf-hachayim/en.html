(3) Utensils rested within, etc. — placed intentionally; not when in house brought to courtyard b'shogeg — forbidden to other courtyard. Minchat Ptim: if came permissibly via garment, forbidden to other courtyard but permitted in that courtyard per Rashi Shabbos 130, Tosafos Eruvin 91. Gaon Yaakov Eruvin beginning All Roofs. Rashba Avodat HaKodesh: even permissibly brought, forbidden except 4 amot like reshut ha-rabbim — see Chida Mishna 2. Beit Meir 346: utensils house to unjoined house all permit carry in house, not Ahavat Chesed 346.
