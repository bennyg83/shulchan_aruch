Two courtyards that wish to make a joint eruv to permit even items that were in houses do not need a new eruv. Rather, the eruv they already made may be taken by one resident on behalf of all and placed in one of the houses of the second courtyard. If he wishes, he may even bring his own bread there, and then all are permitted. &lt;smal&gt;Rema: This applies provided the residents of his courtyard first joined with him (Responsa of the Rosh §16), or they are not required to make an eruv as explained in Siman 370 (Beit Yosef).
