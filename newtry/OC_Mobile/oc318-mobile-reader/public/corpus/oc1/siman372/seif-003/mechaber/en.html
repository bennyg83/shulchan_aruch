Two courtyards that wish to join in eruv together to permit even utensils that rested in houses need no other eruv; rather, the eruv they already made — one of the courtyard residents takes it on behalf of all and places it in one of the houses of the second courtyard, and if he wishes he may even bring his own bread there, and all are permitted.And this is when the residents of his courtyard joined with him first (Responsa of the Rosh siman 16), or when they are not required to make an eruv as explained in siman 370 (Beit Yosef).
