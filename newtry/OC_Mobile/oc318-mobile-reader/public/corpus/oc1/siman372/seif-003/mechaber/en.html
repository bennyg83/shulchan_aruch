Two courtyards that wish to make one eruv together, so as to permit even utensils that spent Shabbos in the houses, do not require a new eruv. Rather, one resident of the first courtyard may carry the eruv they already made on behalf of all of them and place it in one of the houses of the second courtyard. If he wishes, he may even bring bread of his own there, and all are permitted. {Rama: This applies provided that the residents of his own courtyard first joined in an eruv with him [Responsa of the Rosh, siman 16], or that they do not require an eruv, as explained in siman 370. [Beit Yosef]} This also requires that the residents of the second courtyard have made an eruv among themselves. They do not require additional bread; the eruv they already made permits them.
