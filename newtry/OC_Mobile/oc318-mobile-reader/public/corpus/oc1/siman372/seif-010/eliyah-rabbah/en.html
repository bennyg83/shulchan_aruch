Opposite the reduction, etc. This is difficult, for why should it differ from the platform above, where one may use the entire wall? The Bach therefore actually deletes this phrase. The Taz explains that it means only that it is not considered an opening that permits joining in an eruv with the other courtyard, because there is no basin on the other side.
With mortar, etc. It means that one cannot remove it on Shabbos without performing labor prohibited on Shabbos, such as uprooting it with a hoe or similar implement. Otherwise it does not reduce the wall even if designated for this purpose, because we are concerned that he may need it on Shabbos and move it. Anything movable on Shabbos does not reduce the wall, as stated in Avodat HaKodesh, p. 21. See siman 394, seif katan 3.
