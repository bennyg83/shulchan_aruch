(18) [seif 3] Two courtyards wish to join — opening or window between as next seif. See 365 end, 387: if no window even partnered in mavo forbidden through hole — courtyards and houses likewise. Magen Avraham s.k. 3. Ezer MiTzion wondered: partnered — all one courtyard — why not carry like house to house? Tosafos OC 6, Chayei Adam 72:12, Beit Meir, Atzei Almogim not like Magen Avraham. Later authorities agree.
