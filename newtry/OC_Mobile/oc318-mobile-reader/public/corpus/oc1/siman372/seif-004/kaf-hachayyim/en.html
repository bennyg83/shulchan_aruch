Regarding utensils that began Shabbos within them, etc. Utensils that began Shabbos in a house may not be carried from a courtyard into a karpeif not enclosed for residential use, even if the courtyard and karpeif belong to one person. Rashi on Eruvin at the end of folio 23; Tosafos at the end of folio 76; Magen Avraham, subsection 1. This applies even if the karpeif is smaller than beis sasayim. Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 1; Eshel Avraham, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1. It is forbidden to carry from a karpeif enclosed for residential use into a karpeif larger than beis sasayim that was not enclosed for residential use. Similarly, if one karpeif is smaller than beis sasayim and the other larger than beis sasayim, and neither was enclosed for residential use, it is forbidden to carry from one to the other, just as it is forbidden by Rabbinic law to carry from a private domain into a karmelis, and an eruv is ineffective. If both are larger than beis sasayim and neither was enclosed for residential use, however, it is permitted to carry from one to the other within four amos. Similarly, it is permitted to carry from such a karpeif into an actual karmelis, as stated in siman 346, se'if 3. It is forbidden to carry from a courtyard into a karpeif larger than beis sasayim that was not enclosed for residential use, or in the reverse direction, even one amah, because this resembles carrying from a private domain into a karmelis. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 1. See below, subsection 15.
