Magen Avraham s.k. 24: "Erev Shabbat." If the opening was closed on Shabbat we say: since it was permitted, it remains permitted. I question: here they know it will certainly be ruined on Shabbat, for after nightfall the tree is not an opening — one does not go out on Shabbat to say "since it was permitted." Requires study.
