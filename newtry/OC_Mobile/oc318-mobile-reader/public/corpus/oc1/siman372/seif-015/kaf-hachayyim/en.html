Into another karpeif, etc. Beis Yosef writes in the name of Rashi that a karpeif larger than beis sasayim that was not enclosed for residential use is called a single domain relative to another such karpeif or relative to a courtyard. Accordingly, one may carry two amos in one and two amos in the other, transferring from one to the other. See there. Perishah, subsection 4, however, writes that the Tur's wording implies that nothing at all may be carried from a karpeif larger than beis sasayim into a courtyard, for this resembles carrying from a karmelis into a private domain. See there. Ma'amar Mordechai, subsection 3, writes likewise, and we wrote the same above in subsection 4 in the name of Mishbetzos Zahav. See there.
