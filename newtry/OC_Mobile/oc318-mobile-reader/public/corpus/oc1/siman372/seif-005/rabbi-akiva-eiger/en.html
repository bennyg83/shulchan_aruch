Magen Avraham s.k. 31: "Because it reduces from ten." The wording is imprecise — for in any case we require that the stack not be lower than the ceiling, for then it is not a partition as stated explicitly in the sugya; if not, it does not depend on reducing from ten at all — for if there will not be three between stack and ceiling, even if the stack is not ten high it is a partition by lavud; and if there are three, even if the stack is ten high it does not help:
