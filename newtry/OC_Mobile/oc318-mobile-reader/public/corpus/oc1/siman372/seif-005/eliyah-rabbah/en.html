Between a house and an upper story, etc. But windows between two upper stories or between two rooms must be within ten tefachim, just as between two courtyards, according to Avodat HaKodesh. This requires study, because Eruvin 76, where it says that this was interpreted as referring to courtyards, implies that upper stories and rooms have the law of houses. The poskim likewise imply that a roofed area certainly has the law of houses.
Four by four, etc. Even if it is very long, it must still be four tefachim wide. If the window is round, its circumference must be seventeen less one fifth, and four fifths plus a small amount must be within ten tefachim, so that the inscribed square is within ten, as Magen Avraham writes. However, Shiltei Giborim in the name of Ri'az, Perek Chalon, writes four tefachim and a small amount. Knesset HaGedolah discusses at length the Rashba's words, which the Maggid Mishneh on Rambam, Hilchot Eruvin 3 leaves requiring study, but he did not notice the aforementioned Shiltei Giborim. This requires study. See Adam VeChavah, p. 88.
Latticework, etc. The lattice reduces the opening with regard to Shabbos, even though it does not reduce it with regard to laws of chazakah, as stated in Avodat HaKodesh, p. 20.
