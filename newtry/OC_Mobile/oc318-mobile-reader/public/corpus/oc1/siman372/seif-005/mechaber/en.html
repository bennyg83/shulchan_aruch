A window between two houses — even if it is above ten tefachim — if they wish they may join in eruv together. The same applies to a skylight between a house and an upper story — even if it is above ten — if they wish they may join together, even if there is no ladder to ascend by it.Nevertheless we require that its width be four by four (Beit Yosef), and if they made a trellis before the window it is nullified from the law of a window (Maggid chapter 3):.
