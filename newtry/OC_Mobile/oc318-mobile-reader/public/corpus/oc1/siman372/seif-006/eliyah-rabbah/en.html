It is prohibited, etc., even with utensils, etc. For if he uses it, it is considered like his courtyard, and his fellow would then prohibit him from bringing household utensils onto it.
Provided that, etc. For example, if it was breached by any amount on each side, or there remains a strip four tefachim wide on one side. If it was breached for more than ten amot, then even if twenty amot remain, it is regarded as another courtyard.
