A house between two courtyards that is open to both and joined in eruv with both — but the courtyards did not join with each other — one may not carry utensils that rested in houses from courtyard to courtyard via this house between them. If there was between two courtyards a wall ten tefachim high, or the ground of one of the courtyards was five tefachim higher than its fellow and he made upon it a partition five to complete it to ten — they cannot join together. If the wall was ten high for both and produce of the residents of both courtyards was on its top — they may lower it to their courtyards, and likewise raise upon it produce that rested in their courtyards and lower it from it to another courtyard — whether the wall is four wide or not four wide. But produce that rested in a house — if the wall is four wide it is forbidden to raise to it or lower from it; and if it is not four wide' it is permitted (provided they do not exchange, as above siman 346 seif 1). If the wall was ten high for one of the courtyards and for the second it is not' ten high — such as when one's ground is higher than his fellow's' — whoever it is not ten high for him may use it even with utensils that rested in houses; and the second is forbidden to use it even with utensils that rested in the courtyard — provided the wall is four wide.
