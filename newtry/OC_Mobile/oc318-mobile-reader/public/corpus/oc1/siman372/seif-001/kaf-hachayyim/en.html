1) [Se'if 1] Roofs and courtyards, etc. The reason is that their use is neither exclusive nor constant, and an eruv chatzeiros is required only for utensils from the house. Beis Yosef in the name of Rashi; Taz, subsection 1; Magen Avraham, subsection 2. A roof means the upper surface of the roof. Magen Avraham, there. That is, there is no additional roofing above it, for otherwise it would be included in the category of an upper story and would be forbidden. The discussion also concerns a roof that does not project beyond the house's partitions. If it projects, see its law above in siman 345, se'if 16, and below in siman 374, se'if 4.
