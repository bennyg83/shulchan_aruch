(Magen Avraham s.k. 86) And the houses are open to him — it appears he means that if they did not join in the courtyard, then it is impossible to bring the eruv from this house to that house through openings open to the courtyard, since the courtyard is forbidden to carry in; and since it is impossible to bring the eruv from one to the other they cannot join unless there is a window of the required measure. Only above ten is permitted because a house is considered as filled; but if they joined in the courtyard and there is permission to carry from houses to courtyard, then even without a window in the measure it is fine to carry from one to the other through the window, since they can carry and bring the eruv from house to house through the courtyard; and automatically it is permitted to carry through a window without measure and through small openings, as Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 4: "If there is an opening or window of measure, even through small openings it is permitted." If so, what is the novelty that a window above ten is permitted? For even without a window measure it is also permitted. And even though above in s.k. 3 Magen Avraham wrote that partnership does not help to carry through a hole even if they partnered — there it deals with partnering only in a mavo but the two courtyards did not join, and they wish to carry through a hole — in this view it is forbidden; for even though they rely on partnership at the place of eruv, that is only to carry in each courtyard by itself even if it did not join for itself per the second view in siman 387, or if each joined for itself they may carry through openings between them (and this is specifically openings — and likewise windows of measure, as Magen Avraham hinted there to siman 387). The reason: since if they wished to join through the opening they could, partnership helps here at the place of eruv; but if there is no opening between them, eruv alone does not help unless we say partnership helps to carry the eruv through a mavo — in such a case we do not say partnership makes the courtyards as one, for the essence of partnership is that the mavo's domain is joined for them as if it were one courtyard's domain — but it does not help to make houses and courtyards one unless where eruv alone would help we say partnership rises in place of eruv; not so here with a window between two houses — if they joined in the courtyard through openings open to the courtyard, the domain of the houses becomes one and even through holes it is permitted.
