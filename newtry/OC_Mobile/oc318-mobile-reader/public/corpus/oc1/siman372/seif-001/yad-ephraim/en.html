In Magen Avraham s.k. 86: "And the houses open to him" — it appears he means that if they did not join in the courtyard it is impossible to bring eruv from house to house through openings to the courtyard, since the courtyard is forbidden; and since eruv cannot pass from one to the other they cannot join unless there is a window of measure; only above ten is permitted because a house is as filled. But if they joined in the courtyard and may carry from houses to courtyard, then even without window measure it is fine to carry through the window, since they can bring eruv house to house via the courtyard; and automatically permitted through window without measure and small openings, as in s.k. 4. If so, what novelty in window above ten? For even without measure it is permitted. And though in s.k. 3 Magen Avraham wrote partnership does not help through a hole when they partnered only in mavo — there the courtyards did not join and they wish to carry through a hole — forbidden; partnership at eruv place only permits each courtyard alone (siman 387) or each joined for itself may carry through openings (and windows of measure, as he hinted to siman 387). Reason: if they could join through opening, partnership helps at eruv place; without opening, eruv alone does not help unless partnership carries eruv through mavo — we do not say partnership unifies courtyards; essence is mavo domain joined as one courtyard, not houses and courtyards unless eruv alone would help. Here with window between houses — if joined in courtyard through openings, houses become one domain and holes permitted.
