(1) [seif 1] Roofs and courtyards, etc. — reason: no special frequent use; courtyard eruv only for house utensils. Beit Yosef Rashi. Taz s.k. 1. Magen Avraham s.k. 2. Roof means on roof — no roofing above else upper story forbidden. Also when roof does not protrude beyond house partition — if protrudes see siman 345:16 and below 374:4.
