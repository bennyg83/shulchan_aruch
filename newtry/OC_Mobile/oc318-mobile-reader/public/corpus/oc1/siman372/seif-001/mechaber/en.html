The laws of partners in dwellings for eruv. And in it are 19 seifim: Roofs, courtyards,
and karpefim are all one' domain for utensils that rested within them, and it is permitted to carry them from one to another — even if they belong to many owners who did not join in' an eruv together. It is permitted to carry from courtyard to another courtyard, or to a' roof, or to the top of the wall between them; and from roof to another roof adjacent to it, even if it is much higher than it; and from roof to a karpef that is not more than two se'ah, or that was enclosed for dwelling even if it is more than two se'ah; or to a mavo corrected with a lechi or korah, even if they did not make an eruv in it. And even though the courtyard residents made an eruv among themselves — since household utensils are commonly found in the courtyard — it is permitted to carry utensils that rested in this courtyard to' another courtyard, and we are not concerned lest one also carry utensils that rested in a house to another courtyard, or to a roof and karpef:
