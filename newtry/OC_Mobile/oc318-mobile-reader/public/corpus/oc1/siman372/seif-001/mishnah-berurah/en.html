(1) Roofs — meaning on the roof where there is no roofing above [for if on the ceiling under the roof it is part of the upper story of the house and is like a house]. Their roofs were smooth and fit for use; deals when they did not protrude beyond the house partition — if protruded see above siman 345:16:

(2) And karpefim — see above siman 346:3:

(3) One domain — all these lack special frequent use, therefore no domain division; permitted to carry one to another without courtyard eruv; all the more permitted roof to roof or courtyard to courtyard; permitted entire courtyard [or roof] even if residents did not join — Sages required eruv only so house utensils that rested in houses from before day may be brought in, and courtyard to house:

(4) That rested within — placed there intentionally; not when rested in house:

(5) And did not join — courtyards with each other or roofs and courtyards; in courtyard itself greater permission when joined as end of seif:

(6) Or to roof — of another:

(7) Between them — between two courtyards; see seif 6:

(8) Even much higher — we do not say it divides into two domains:

(9) And from roof to karpef — likewise courtyard to karpef:

(10) Not more than two se'ah — if more, karmelit if not enclosed for dwelling; forbidden to reshut ha-yachid; see 346:3:

(11) Or to corrected mavo — mavo law like unjoined courtyard not karmelit; see 388:

(12) Even if not joined — courtyards did not partner in it:

(13) To another courtyard — likewise roof and karpef:

(14) And even though courtyard joined — since house utensils common in courtyard, permitted courtyard utensils to other courtyard; not concerned lest carry house utensils:

(15) To another courtyard — likewise roof and karpef:

(16) Not concerned lest also carry house utensils — gezeirah ligezeirah:
