(s.k. 12) Forbidden because — second cannot raise even courtyard utensils; Peri Megadim wondered on Shulchan Aruch/Mishnah Berurah: we permit courtyard to courtyard without eruv — all the more wall between courtyards. Answer: not roof law for low side — courtyard law permits use even house utensils; if high side uses, becomes his courtyard and forbids fellow house utensils; give easy use to low side.

We give to easy use — not deprive high side easy wall use for fellow's house loss.
