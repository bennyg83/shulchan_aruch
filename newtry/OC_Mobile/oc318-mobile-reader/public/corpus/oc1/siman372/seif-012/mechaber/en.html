A projection from the wall four by four with any ladder placed on it permits use—provided the lowest rung is within three of the ground and no gap of three between rungs, and the ladder rests atop the projection. If leaned beside it, no. If the wall is under twenty high, one projection in the middle suffices; if twenty high, two are needed—one within the lower ten and one within the upper ten—unless directly opposite each other enabling a ladder.
