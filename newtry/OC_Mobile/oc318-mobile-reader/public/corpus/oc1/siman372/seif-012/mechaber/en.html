A projection coming out from the wall that has four' by four, and one placed upon it a ladder of any size — it helps to permit use of the wall, provided the lowest rung is not three high from the ground and there are not three between rung and rung; and this is when he places the ladder on top of the projection — but if he leans it beside it, no. And whenever the wall is not twenty tefachim high, one projection placed in the middle suffices, since there is not ten from the ground to the projection and not ten from it to the top of the wall. If it is twenty high he needs two projections — one within the lower ten and one above it within the upper ten — for if the projection were ten high a ladder of any size would not suffice for it; and they must not be opposite each other so that one may cast a ladder from this to that:
