1) [Se'if 1] A doubtful eruv. For example, it is uncertain whether the eruv existed during bein hashmashos. This means at the beginning of bein hashmashos, for otherwise the preceding siman states that even if it was certainly eaten during bein hashmashos, it is a valid eruv. Perishah, subsection 1; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1.
2) There. For example, it is uncertain whether it existed during bein hashmashos, etc. For example, it burned, and one does not know whether this occurred before bein hashmashos began or afterward.
3) There. But if it did not have a presumptive status of validity, etc. Although one may establish an eruv during bein hashmashos even though it has no prior presumptive status of validity, uncertainty whether it was placed at all is worse. Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 2.
