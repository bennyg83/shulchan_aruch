1) [Seif 1] If one had a sick person, he requests mercy for him in the berachah of Refa'einu. It is preferable to pray for the sick person in the berachah of Shomea Tefillah; the pious Rav Mahari Molcho in his responsa, siman 109. And according to the words of the Rav, it is good that one have the sick person in mind in his thoughts during the berachah of Refa'einu, and pray for him explicitly in Shema Koleinu. 'And I have heard from the mouths of holy rabbis that they would pray for a sick person in the berachah of Refa'einu, and so do I practice.' Birkei Yosef, ot 1; Shalmei Tzibbur, folio 128b; Zechor L'Avraham, ot tav. And one who has a sick person, Heaven spare us, and does not wish to interrupt his tefillah to pray for him, should have him in mind when he says 'rofei cholei amo Yisrael'; that is, when he says 'rofei,' etc., he should intend 'and the illness of so-and-so the son of so-and-so.' And see our words above in siman 116, ot 3.
