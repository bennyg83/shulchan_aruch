(s"k 1) And in Shomea Tefila, etc. — it is good to say "I have sinned," etc.; and if he has a fresh sin on that day, he mentions it in the previous Amidah with him. Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Seder HaYom.

One need not mention his name — for thus we find with Moshe, who said "please heal her now" and did not say "Miriam."
