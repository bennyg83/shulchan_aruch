Seif 1: "And when he," etc.—as written there in Berachos, etc., at the end, etc.

"And for R' Yehuda of Regensburg," etc.—"if he adds," etc., "and not," etc. This is as written above; but if he comes to say, etc.

"And if he asks." This is as written above—"if he has," etc.

"And in the blessing of," etc.—as written above. And in Berachos 31a: a person asks for his needs in Shema Koleinu; and it was stated in the two statements above—from which we learn that everything is permitted there.

"And likewise," etc.—there and there, even in order, etc.; and the addition—even before "May the words of my mouth be acceptable," although it pertains to the prayer as written in the first chapter of Berachos, since the Sages instituted it, etc.; and as written above in siman 111, seif 1—nevertheless it is not preferable to Shema Koleinu, and it is lighter than it, for even in order, etc., unlike in Shema Koleinu; and all the more so in the middle blessings. And this is what is written in seif 2: "There is," etc.; and so Tosafos in Avodah Zarah there, in the passage beginning "If," etc.
