[2] [Levushei Serad] Provided that he says it in the singular, etc. This means when he says it in the middle of the blessing, but at the end of the blessing he says it in the plural. It is explicit that this applies specifically when he has a sick person or requires sustenance, but one should not add requests in the blessings regarding future needs; rather, he should say them in Shomea Tefillah. See siman 557, seif 2, regarding Nachem, and see Beit Yosef. The Lechem Chamudos, folio 42, writes: He must actually have such a need, and may not add a request merely in general when neither he nor another person for whom he prays requires it. One who prays for another person in his absence must mention his name, as Maharil wrote. He further records that the community decreed a fast and recited Selichos because Maharil became ill.
