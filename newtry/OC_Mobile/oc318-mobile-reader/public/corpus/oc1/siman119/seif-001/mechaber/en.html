The law of one who wishes to add requests to the blessings. It contains 4 seifim: If one wishes to add to any of the middle blessings a request corresponding to that blessing, he may add it. How so? If he has a sick person, he asks for mercy for him in the blessing Refa'einu; if he needs sustenance, he asks for it in Birkat HaShanim; and in Shomea Tefillah he may ask for all his needs, because that blessing encompasses all requests. {Rama: When he adds a request, he should first begin the blessing and only afterward add the request; he should not add the request before beginning the blessing. [Tur, siman 567]} According to Rabbeinu Yonah, when one adds a request corresponding to a blessing on behalf of all Israel, he should phrase it in the plural and not in the singular, and he should add it only at the end of the blessing, not in its middle. But if he asks for his own particular need, such as a sick person in his household or his own need for sustenance, he may ask even in the middle of the blessing, provided that he phrases it in the singular and not in the plural. In Shomea Tefillah, and likewise at the end of the prayer, whether before Yihyu LeRatzon or after it, he may ask in either singular or plural, whether for his own needs or for the needs of the community.
