[1] [Seif 1:] If he had a sick person and asks mercy for him in the blessing of Refa'einu — it is preferable to pray for the sick person in the blessing of Shomea Tefila, as the pious rabbi Mahari Malko wrote in his responsa, siman 109. Per the rabbi's words, it is good that he intend in his mind on the sick person in the blessing of Refa'einu, and pray for him explicitly in Shema Koleinu. And I heard from the mouth of holy rabbis that they would pray for a sick person in the blessing of Refa'einu, and so I practice — Be'er HaChayyim, ot 1; Shulchan Hatamim, folio 128b. Zecher LeTzadik, ot tav: And one who has a sick person with him and does not wish to interrupt his prayer to pray for him should intend on him when he says Who heals the sick among His people Israel — that is, when he says Who heals, etc., he should intend on the sick person by name explicitly. And see our words above, siman 116, ot gimel.
