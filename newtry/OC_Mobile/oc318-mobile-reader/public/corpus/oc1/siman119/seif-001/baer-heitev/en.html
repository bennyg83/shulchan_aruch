Blessing. The author of Aruch HaTurei Zahav did not look at the gloss on this section; see there. And see Knesset HaGedolah.

Mercy. One who asks mercy for his fellow need not mention his name (Berachos 34). This applies in his presence; but when praying not in his presence he must mention his name. Maharil.

His needs. It is good to confess in Shema Koleinu and say: "I have sinned, I have transgressed, I have acted wickedly." And to ask for his sustenance even if he is wealthy. If he has a sin committed that day, he mentions it in the previous prayer service with him. Sefer HaKavanos.

Shema Koleinu. The Bach wrote that according to R' Yehuda of Regensburg, even in Shema Koleinu it is forbidden to state one's needs in plural language in the middle; see there. Nevertheless, the congregation may do so even in the middle of the blessing. Beit Yosef; Magen Avraham. And in the Baer Heitev before me in seif kaf-gimmel he was not precise; see there.
