And in Shomea Tefila one may [ask for all one's needs]. It is good to say "I have sinned, I have transgressed, I have acted wickedly," and to ask for one's sustenance (Shaar HaKavanot) — even if he is wealthy (the writers). So too in the Zohar, Shemot 70, 109, 110: one who asks mercy for his fellow need not mention his name (Berachos, folio 34) — and this is when [the sick person] is before him; but when he prays not before him, he must mention his name (Maharil).

And in the blessing of Shomea Tefila — Bach wrote that for R' Yehuda Riya, even in Shomea Tefila it is forbidden to speak in plural language — so far his words. Nevertheless, the congregation is permitted, even in the middle of the blessing [Beit Yosef].

"Between, before yehi ratzon" — this is for R' Yehuda Riya; but the view of Rabi Yosi is not so, as written in siman 122, s"2.
