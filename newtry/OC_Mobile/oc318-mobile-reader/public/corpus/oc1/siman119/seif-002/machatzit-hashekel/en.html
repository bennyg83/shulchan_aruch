(s"k 2) And in the blessing, etc. — for R' Yehuda Riya, even in Shomea Tefila it is forbidden to speak in plural language; and this is if he adds in the middle of a blessing — for this is the reason of R' Yehuda Riya: if one speaks in the middle of a blessing in plural language, it appears like a change from the coin that the sages coined; not so if one speaks in singular language. And since the blessing that the sages established is in plural language, it is recognizable that it is an addition and not a change of coin. So too at the end of a blessing that one said at the beginning — all the coin that the sages coined, he likewise does not care that it appears like a change from the coin that the sages coined. Therefore one should not distinguish between the blessing of Shomea Tefila and the other blessings — see Aruch HaShulchan.

The congregation is permitted; and therefore the custom is to say selichot and piyutim in the middle of blessings.
