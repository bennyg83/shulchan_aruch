In a blessing for [an individual] need — but after his prayer he may extend. And in Likutei Maharil it is found: when Maharil was sick, the congregation decreed a fast and said selichot — implying that the congregation for an individual need is permitted. One can say that Maharil is different, since many need his Torah and he is considered like many; and similarly it is found in the responsum of the Rashba, siman 148.
