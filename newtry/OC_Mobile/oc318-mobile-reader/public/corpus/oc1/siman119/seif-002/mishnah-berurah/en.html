(יב) In a blessing — even in the blessing of Shemoneh Esreh, and all the more so in the other blessings. But after prayer, even before "yehi ratzon," it is permitted to extend in all ways. And in Likutei Maharil it states: when Maharil fell ill, the congregation decreed a fast and they said selichot, and their custom then was to say selichot in the middle of the blessing "Forgive us" — which implies that a congregation for an individual's need is permitted even in the middle of a blessing; one could say that Maharil is different, for many need his Torah and he is considered like many.
