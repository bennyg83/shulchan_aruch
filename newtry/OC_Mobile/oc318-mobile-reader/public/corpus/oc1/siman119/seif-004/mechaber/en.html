A prayer leader who finished [the blessing of] "Go'el Yisrael" ["Redeemer of Israel"] and did not say "Aneinu" ["Answer us"] [on a fast day] does not go back, even if one had still not finished [the immediate next blessing of] "Refa'einu" ["Heal us"]. And if one did go back, it is an empty blessing {Rama: (Rather, one should say "Aneinu" in [the blessing of] "Shomeya Tefilla" as an individual does)}
