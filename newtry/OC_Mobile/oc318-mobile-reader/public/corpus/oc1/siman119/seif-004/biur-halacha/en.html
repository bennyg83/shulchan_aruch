"And if he returned—it is a blessing in vain"—see in Mishna Berurah in what he wrote: "this means," etc.; and it is from Taz and Magen Avraham and other later authorities. And it appears from this that on account of "Answer us" itself it is not a blessing in vain; and I have not merited to understand—for since the poskim hold that this "Answer us" is like other "Answer us" passages in general, regarding which we hold that one does not go back for it—if so, if he returns to say it now it is a blessing in vain, since its fixed place is between "Who redeems" and "Who heals," and not between "Heal us" and the blessing of the years; if we require him to say it now, it is certainly called going back in God's name. And it is possible that they hold that since Chazal instituted from the outset to say it as a separate blessing, it has priority over other "Answer us" passages that they only required to be included; and if not for there being harm thereby to the blessing of "Heal us," we would not be uncertain about what we advise to include it in Shema Koleinu—for we do not find a remedy through "Who hears prayer" except if he omitted a request in the blessing of the years, or per the Yerushalmi, "Who makes the wind blow" in the blessing of "You are mighty"—but not if he omitted an entire blessing; and as all the later authorities ruled (unlike Pri Chadash, who is lenient to complete in Shema Koleinu even if he skipped an entire blessing). And it still requires study.
