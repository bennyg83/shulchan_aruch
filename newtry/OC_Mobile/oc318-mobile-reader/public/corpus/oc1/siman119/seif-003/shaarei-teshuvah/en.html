His needs — see Tur. And see in Shulchan Aruch in the name of another sage, from the Zohar, parashat Pinchas, that proves one should not say a prayer for sustenance in the Mincha prayer — see there. And see there that he wrote one should say the prayer with humility, etc.; and in any case with a voice of weeping; and one should accept upon oneself not to do such a thing again knowingly — and if not, then prosecutors come against him, etc. And one should be careful not to multiply requests — only one inclusive [request], such as "seed of men." So too in Beit Yehuda in the name of Mahar"a Baroda. Also the disciples of the Arizal, may his memory be blessed, wrote that in Shomea Tefila one should confess briefly.
