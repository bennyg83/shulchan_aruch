(יג) If he skipped — meaning that he skipped the blessing entirely; and "erred" means that he changed it in a matter that requires returning on account of this — and see what we wrote above in siman 68 in Mishna Berurah, section 1. And both cases speak of unwittingly; for intentionally one must return to the beginning of the eighteen, as written in siman 114, section 7.

(יד) To the head of the blessing — and if he remembered his error while he is still standing in the blessing, he returns only to the place where he erred.

(טו) In order — and even if he did not remember until after several blessings, he must return and say them all after it; for if he says that blessing alone in the place where he remembered, he finds that he changed the order of the blessings and has not fulfilled the eighteen, for the order of blessings is from the Men of the Great Assembly and they based their order on the verses.
