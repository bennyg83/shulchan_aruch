(1) [Seif 1] One who carries a load on his head removes tefillin, etc. But arm tefillin that are covered may be put on ab initio even if he intends to take out manure; and needless to say, if he already bound them on his arm, he is permitted to take out manure on his head, for there is no degradation to the arm tefillin since they are covered. Bach. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur, sign 3; Eliyah Rabbah, sign 1; Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot Zahav, sign 1; Shitilei Zeitim, sign 2. But to take out manure on the arm is possible [to forbid] even when they are covered by his garments, for there is degradation to the tefillin — Peri Megadim there.
