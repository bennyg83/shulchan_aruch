Removes head tefillin — see in Mishna Berurah what we wrote in the name of Peri Megadim; and it seems his reason is due to the answer of Beit Yosef, who wrote that per the Tur and his colleagues — see there, in one explanation; but per the Rambam, who did not bring this rule of four kav at all, there is no need for this; and likewise in Rashi Bava Metzia 105b, s.v. asur, does not imply so.

And even his handkerchief — see in Taz, who wrote that per the Tur a handkerchief is not forbidden, and likewise a load less than four kav, only when it squeezes and presses the tefillin thereby; but we have only the words of Shulchan Aruch, that in every case it is forbidden, for it is degrading to the tefillin thereby [Mateh Yehudah].

And even so if he, etc. — Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Darkei Moshe: every place, etc. See Nahar Shalom and Maamar Mordechai, who wrote that Darkei Moshe did not mean this. And for the law, nevertheless Magen Avraham wrote that this requires further study.
