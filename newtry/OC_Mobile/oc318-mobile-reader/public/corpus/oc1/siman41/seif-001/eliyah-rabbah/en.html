[1] On his head, etc. But regarding arm tefillin that are covered, the Bach wrote that one may put them on ab initio even if he intends to take out manure; and needless to say, if he already bound them on his arm, he is permitted to take it out — up to here. And I found in Sefer Chassidim siman 976, thus his language: when a person has tefillin on his arm, if he has something to carry, he should not carry it between his forearms, for he would separate between the tefillin and his heart; even though garments separate, this is not considered separation, since that is his custom — up to here.
