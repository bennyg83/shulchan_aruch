(1) Upon his head, etc. Regarding the arm tefillin, which are covered, Bach writes that he may initially leave them on even when he intends to carry something out. Certainly, if they are already tied upon his arm, he may carry it out. I found in Sefer Chasidim, siman 776: “When a person has tefillin upon his arm and has an object to carry, he should not carry it between his upper arms, because he would thereby interpose between the tefillin and his heart. Although his clothing already intervenes, that is not considered an interposition because this is the normal manner of wearing them.”
