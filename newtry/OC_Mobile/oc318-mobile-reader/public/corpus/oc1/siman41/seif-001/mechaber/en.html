Laws of one who carries a load — how he should conduct himself with tefillin. And
it contains one seif: One who carries a load on his head removes his head tefillin until he removes the load. And even a handkerchief is forbidden to place on the head when there are tefillin on it. But something that is customary to place on the head, such as a hat or turban, is permitted.And even so, if it is a heavy load of four kav and the tefillin are pressed, he must remove them (Beit Yosef).
