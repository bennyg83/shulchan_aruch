Laws regarding one who is carrying a load and how he should conduct himself with his תפילין.
And there is 1 Se'if: One who is carrying a load on his head should remove his head תפילין until he removes the load. And even a cloth is forbidden to place over one's head when he has his תפילין on. But something which is usually placed on the head, like a hat or a turban is permitted.Rema: Nevertheless, if the load is [as] heavy [as] four kav's and the תפילין are pushed [because of it], you are required to remove them (Beit Yosef).
