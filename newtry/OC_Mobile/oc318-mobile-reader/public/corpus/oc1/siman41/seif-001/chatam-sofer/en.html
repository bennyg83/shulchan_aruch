In Magen Avraham, seif katan 1: they are twenty-five zekukim. Note: see there, that it is two and a half litra pound Prague weight; and this is per the Yerushalmi brought in the Rosh, Sanhedrin, siman 33, that every log has a weight of twelve hundred zuzim, which is two hundred dinar; and in a dinar are one hundred ninety-two perutot, which is half a Vienna weight; the weight of a peruta is half a barley of fine silver; and every ninety-six barleys is the weight of a quarter of a latte, called a quintal; and accordingly are his words here — delve and understand, and forget; and see what Beit Yosef wrote in Yoreh De'ah, siman 201, that a kortov weighs one dinar, and it is one of the eighths; in the eighths of a log there are found only sixty-four dinar; for twenty-four kav there are only one thousand twenty-four dinar; and each dinar is ninety-four barleys, that is, a quintal; thus one thousand twenty-four quintals amount to eight litra pound of the above-mentioned weight; and it is slightly less than a third of the above-mentioned weight; and this requires further study.
