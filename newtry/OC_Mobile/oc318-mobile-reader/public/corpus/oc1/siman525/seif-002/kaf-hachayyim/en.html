Olas Shabbos, subsection 1, writes that the omission of this law regarding Shabbos implies that a Shabbos loan cannot be collected in court. The Talmud, however, implies that no distinction is made between Yom Tov and Shabbos in this respect. Olas Shabbos, subsection 1. Responsa, siman 54, volume 1, likewise writes that Shabbos and Yom Tov have the same law. Darkei Moshe, subsection 1, cites this.
