(א) (a) The Leviatha, we were surrounded by food and drink:

(ב) It is given to claim, etc. — meaning they summon him in court after Yom Tov if he refuses to pay (and the extra reason is because some in the Gemara hold he cannot claim in court but only between themselves — if it were given to claim they would come to write; it teaches us we do not hold thus but like the other view that if he cannot claim he should not give initially and thus avoid diminishing Yom Tov joy):

(ג) Seif 11 — meaning there it is explained regarding Shabbat that one should beware of saying language of loan, but should say "give me"; and likewise for Yom Tov — see there, in the Hagahah and in Mishna Berurah that all the laws apply here too; and what Shulchan Aruch ruled, that a Yom Tov loan is given to be claimed — likewise a Shabbat loan is given afterward to be claimed.
