(1) He won't talk. And at the time of the blasting of the Peshita Dasor to Shukh Efi' for the forbidden saliva [AH]:

(ב) And the prayers are not an interruption. And even though in siman 567 seif 6 he wrote that ideally one should not speak at all — that is between a blessing and the one leading; but between tekiyah and tekiyah it is permitted ideally to speak about their matter.

(ג) One need not return. And some say one should not speak between one tefilla and another tefilla, for there are two mitzvot — unlike here where both are one mitzva [Rif]; if so, even if one spoke between the tekiyot of sitting themselves one need not return and bless [Hagahot Maimoniyot and Rosh] — and see siman 599 seif 8.

(ד) Regarding the tekiyot — requires study: in siman 567 seif 6 he ruled that ideally one should not interrupt at all; it is possible that here we discuss one who must speak, such as one who says to bring a shofar and the like; and it is obvious that if one spoke between a blessing and tekiah about prayer matters he must return and bless. And some say that if one spoke about meal needs, even though it is not the need of the one leading, he need not bless — for there the one leading also needs the meal, unlike here where what connection has prayer to tekiah; and so in Mordekhai. And what Rama wrote that prayers are not interruption — that is between tekiyah and tekiyah; but between blessing and tekiyah it is interruption and one must return and bless; and it is obvious that even the listener if he spoke between blessing and tekiyah must bless — and see siman 567 seif 6 in the gloss.
