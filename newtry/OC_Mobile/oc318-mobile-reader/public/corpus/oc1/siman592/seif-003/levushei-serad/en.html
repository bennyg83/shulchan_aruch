Magen Avraham s.k. 2 — spitting is forbidden, etc. This is explained in Darkei Moshe siman 589 in the name of Maharil: even if one coughed and cleared the throat it is forbidden to spit so as not to interrupt hearing, for we require the beginning and end of every tekiah — end of his words. See Taz siman 588 s.k. 4 and in Mishbetzot. Do not challenge from this what is written that between tekiyot one may spit, for there it is between one tekiah and another but not in the middle of a tekiah, as written in Magen Avraham there.
