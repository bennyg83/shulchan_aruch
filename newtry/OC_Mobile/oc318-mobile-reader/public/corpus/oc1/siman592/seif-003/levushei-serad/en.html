Magen Avraham s.k. 2, 'spitting is forbidden,' etc. This is explained in Darkei Moshe, siman 589, in the name of Maharil, as follows: even phlegm and mucus may not be spat out, so that he not interrupt from hearing, for we require the beginning and end of each and every tekiah, end quote. See Taz, siman 578, s.k. 4, and Mishbetzos there. Do not challenge from there, for one can distinguish somewhat; see there and analyze carefully.
