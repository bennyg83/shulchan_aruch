Speak. And during the shofar blasts, even spitting is forbidden; see siman 587, s.k. 5.
He need not. Even if he spoke between the sitting blasts themselves, he need not go back and recite the blessing. Hagahos Maimoniyos; Magen Avraham.
Between. If one spoke between the blessing and the shofar blast, even about matters of the prayer, he must go back and recite the blessing. What the Rema wrote, that the prayers are not an interruption, refers to between one blast and another; but between the blessing and the blast it is an interruption, and he must go back and recite the blessing. It is obvious that even a listener, if he spoke between the blessing and the blast, must recite the blessing. Magen Avraham.
