(1) The shaliach tzibbur repeats the prayer, etc. There are places where the custom is to blow when they pray silently, but one should not do this ab initio, so as not to confuse those praying; rather, they complete those blasts after the prayer.
(2) And they blow, etc. For all the shofar blasts of these three orders, the one blowing and those listening must stand at the time of the blasts. Therefore they are called the standing blasts. After the fact, if they sat, they fulfilled their obligation.
(3) Tashrat for Malchiyos once, etc. Since Chazal instituted that we satisfy all the uncertainties that can be raised about the teruah, as written in siman 590, it would have been fitting here as well to blow tashrat, tashat, tarat for Malchiyos, and likewise for Zichronos and likewise for Shofaros, in order to satisfy every uncertainty. However, because we do not burden the congregation, the custom is to blow only tashrat for each one, for in this way he performs all the possibilities of the teruah. The only concern is perhaps that the teruah is only a teruah alone or only shevarim alone, and then he is interrupting between the teruah and the straight blast before it or after it. We are not concerned for this, since we already fulfilled our Torah obligation with the sitting blasts, in which we satisfied all the uncertainties, as I wrote in siman 590.
(4) And so is the custom in these countries. In Shelah he wrote that the preferred way is to blow tashrat, tashat, tarat for Malchiyos, and likewise for Zichronos and likewise for Shofaros, and after Anim Zemiros another tashrat, tashat, tarat, in order to complete one hundred sounds. Nevertheless, in a place where they follow our custom, one should not change it [Pri Megadim].
(5) Today the world was conceived. For the world was created in Tishrei. Even according to what we hold, following the view that the world was created in Nissan, in any event in Tishrei it arose in thought to be created, but it was not created until Nissan.
