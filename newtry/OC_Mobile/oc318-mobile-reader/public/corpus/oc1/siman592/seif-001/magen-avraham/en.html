The one blowing and those listening must stand [Levush, siman 585], and see siman 154, end of seif 4. There are places where the custom is to blow when they pray silently, but one should not follow this practice ab initio [Knesses HaGedolah]. Today the world was conceived. For the world was created in Tishrei [Levush]. But in Tosafos, Rosh Hashanah 27, it says that we hold like Rabbi Yehoshua, who says that the world was created in Nissan. As for what we say, 'this day is the beginning of Your works,' etc., see there; and they conclude that in Tishrei it arose in thought to be created, but it was not created until Nissan. See Bach, end of siman 591.
