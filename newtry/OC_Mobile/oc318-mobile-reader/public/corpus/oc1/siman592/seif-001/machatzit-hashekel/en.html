He wrote in Magen Avraham: the blower and the listener must stand — Levush. And he wrote there that Levush [means] specifically the tekiyot on the order of the blessings, which are the essence of the mitzva — then even listeners must stand; but the tekiyot of sitting are such that the blower must stand out of respect for the congregation, but listeners need not stand. And see what Magen Avraham wrote siman 585 in the name of Radbaz — likewise.
