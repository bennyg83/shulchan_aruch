[1] [Levush] Piyyutim, etc. 'Those taught by You are taught' has no explanation according to the sense of the verse that he cites, 'and I will favor whom I favor.' Therefore, one should emend here to 'those shown mercy by You are shown mercy,' in the language of the verse, 'and I will have mercy,' etc.; and so too on Yom Kippur; see Taz, siman 582. It seems that one should not change from saying 'And You are King,' etc., because 'and You,' with a vav, means 'but You.' Nor should one reverse the text, 'who will be raised up and who will be lowered.' One should say 'and there is no explaining the concealment of Your Name,' and should not say 'and there is no explanation for the concealment of Your Name,' because there is an explanation for the concealment of His Name; Tashbetz. In 'the love of Eisan, our Master,' one should pause between 'Eisan' and 'our Master,' because 'Eisan' refers to Avraham and 'our Master' refers to the Holy One, blessed is He; Kitzur Shelah. But in Tashbetz he wrote that the Rif would say 'our father,' end quote. This implies that there is no need to pause. The Rokeach wrote: 'For the day is holy to our Master'; 'our Master,' in atbash gematria, is tav-kuf-tes, mem-tes-pei, equaling Avraham, Yitzchak, and Yaakov, in whose merit He should judge us favorably. Maharil wrote that when the congregation says 'Hashem our God,' he would say quietly: 'You have been shown to know that Hashem is God; there is none besides Him,' and 'You shall know today and take it to your heart that Hashem is God,' etc., until 'there is none else'; 'Shema Yisrael, Hashem is our God,' etc.; 'Behold, to Hashem your God belong the heavens and the heavens of the heavens, the earth and everything in it'; 'For Hashem your God is God of gods and Master of masters, the great, mighty, and awesome God, Who does not show favor and does not take a bribe'; 'For I will proclaim the Name of Hashem; ascribe greatness to our God'; 'May the Name of Hashem be blessed from now and forever,' end quote. It seems to me that Maharil was the chazzan and therefore said this, and so it is also explained in Darchei Moshe in the name of Hagahos Maimoniyos.
