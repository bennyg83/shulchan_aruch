The Musaf prayer in a loud voice and the order of tekiyot. And
in it are 4 seifim: The prayer leader repeats the prayer and they blow on the' order of the blessings: for Kings — tashrat once; for Remembrances — tashat; for Shofarot — tarat. And now the custom is to blow for Kings tashrat three times, for Remembrances tashat three times, and for Shofarot tarat three times.And some say they blow tashrat for Malchuyot once, and likewise for Zichronot and Shofarot (Tur in the name of Rabbenu Tam and Minhagim); and) such is the custom in these lands. And they practiced saying each time after blowing "today the world was born" and "betrothal" (Maharil); even on Shabbat when they do not blow they say "today was born" but not "betrothal" (Minhagim).)
