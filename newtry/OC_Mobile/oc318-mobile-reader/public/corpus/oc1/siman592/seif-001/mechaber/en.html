The repetition of Mussaf aloud and the order of the shofar blasts. It contains 4 seifim: The prayer leader repeats the Amidah, and they blow according to the order of the blessings: for Malchuyot, Tashrat once; for Zichronot, Tashat once; and for Shofarot, Tarat once. Nowadays the custom is to blow Tashrat 3 times for Malchuyot, Tashat 3 times for Zichronot, and Tarat 3 times for Shofarot. {Rama: Some say that Tashrat is blown once for Malchuyot, and likewise once for Zichronot and once for Shofarot. [Tur in the name of Rabbeinu Tam and Minhagim] This is the custom in these lands. The custom is to say after each set of blasts Hayom Harat Olam and Areshet. [Maharil] Even on Shabbos, when the shofar is not blown, Hayom Harat is recited, but Areshet is not. [Minhagim]}
