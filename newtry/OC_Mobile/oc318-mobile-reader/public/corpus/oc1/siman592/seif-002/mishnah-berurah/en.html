(6) And even if he has someone to blow for him. That is to say, he also does not interrupt in order to hear them, for they instituted blowing according to the order only for the congregation.
(7) Rather, they blow for him, etc. That is to say, if he did not hear the sitting blasts from the shaliach tzibbur, they blow for him before he prays Mussaf, in order to confuse the Satan so that he not prosecute him at the time of his prayer. If he has no one to blow for him before his prayer, he should intend to hear them after his prayer.
