In Shulchan Aruch an individual does not interrupt; and Radbaz vol. 2 siman 347 wrote that an individual may interrupt in a blessing and in Barukh She'amar — he wondered at him; and in truth it is wondrous why he did not mention the words of Tur and other poskim that one does not interrupt even if others blew for him — all the more so he himself has no [need] to blow.
