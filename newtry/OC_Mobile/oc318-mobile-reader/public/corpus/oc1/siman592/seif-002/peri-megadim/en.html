Piyyutim. It is proper to study them with commentaries. 'Today the world was conceived': one explanation is that pregnancy and birth are also called conception, and there is an opinion that in Tishrei the world arose in thought and in Nissan it was created; if so, 'conceived' is appropriate regarding thought. Another explanation is that it is the Targum of contention and strife; the abbreviation means 'Today is the Day of Judgment,' as in Abudraham. It is taught, and Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 6, and similar material appear in Ahavas Yonasan by the Gaon Rabbeinu Yonasan, of blessed memory, that the mazalos and the seven moving stars receive influence on Rosh Hashanah, and since they have not yet emerged into actuality, it is called conception. Pharaoh's dream occurred on Rosh Hashanah, and Yosef interpreted it; see there. 'Supreme King,' etc.: the word is with a beis in 'balah' and not with a kaf, Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Mateh Aharon, letter 2.
