Magen Avraham, s.v. 'the one who blows.' See siman 124, where it is explained that during the shaliach tzibbur repetition of the prayer the congregation must stand, because one who hears is like one who recites. So too here, those hearing the shofar blasts must stand like the one blowing himself. As for the fact that those hearing sit during the sitting blasts, the Knesses HaGedolah already wondered about this at the beginning of siman 565; see there in Magen Avraham s.k. 1, and what I wrote there.
