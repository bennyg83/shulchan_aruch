(b) And when they blow in a whisper they say "today the world was conceived" also in the silent prayer (meaning even per Ashkenaz custom that they do not say "today the world was conceived" in silent prayer as written above siman 591 seif 33 and below seif 7 in places where the custom is to blow in whisper they say it) — but they do not say "His anointed" there, as written siman 591 seif 7. And individuals who reached the end of the Malchuyot order wait until the prayer leader reaches there and blows; likewise in other orders. And if some individuals prolong more than the prayer leader and have not reached the order's end when he begins to blow — they must be silent and intend their ears toward the prayer leader's tekiyot, and not say "today the world was conceived" where they stand until they reach the end of that order's blessing; then they say "today the world was conceived." And if the prayer leader errs in his blowing it is forbidden for them to speak or rebuke him; even if they did not hear the sitting tekiyot — only after prayer ends they return and blow that order he erred in, as explained siman 590. Enough. Magen Avraham siman 591 seif 13. See above siman 591 seif 5.
