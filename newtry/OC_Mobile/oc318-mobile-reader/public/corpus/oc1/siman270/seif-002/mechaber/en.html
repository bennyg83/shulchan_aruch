There are those who do not say it on Yom Tov that falls to be on erev Shabbat; and there are those who do not say it on Shabbat of Chanukah:And we are not accustomed thus on Chanukah; and on Shabbat of chol ha-moed we do not say it (customs); and likewise on Yom Tov that falls on Shabbat we do not say it (Maharil, Laws of Sukkah).
