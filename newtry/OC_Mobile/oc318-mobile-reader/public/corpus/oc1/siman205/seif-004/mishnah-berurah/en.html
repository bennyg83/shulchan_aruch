(15) For small pieces — since their form and shape are still somewhat recognizable; see above siman 202 seif 7 in Magen Avraham note 18, and below in siman 208 note 138 in Mishna Berurah.
(16) Their blessing — whether something whose blessing is borei peri haAdamah or borei peri haEtz.
(17) Because of this — sesame seeds that were ground: their blessing changes from borei peri haAdamah to shehakol because their form is not on them at all [Magen Avraham]; and the view of Elya Rabba is that even ground one blesses borei peri haAdamah unless one made a beverage from them — then their blessing is shehakol; and this is brought in Shaarei Teshuvah.
