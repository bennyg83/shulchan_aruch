On the water — Taz Berachos 35, first view in stam; and nevertheless in siman 11, God-fearing person, etc.; and here stam speaks of something whose way is to cook or squeeze, which Rashba concedes per Magen Avraham in Taz note 18; and thus in note 6 it will be explained further. He rejected on this the view of Levush who distinguished between fruits and vegetables because a vegetable gives stronger taste in water than fruits; therefore when cooked with meat the meat weakens the power of the vegetable's taste; but Taz wrote as explained in Bach on Berachos 36a regarding date honey to distinguish between its ways to cook, etc.; and in fruits too: if its way is to cook, if from itself borei peri haEtz, as Taz wrote Berachos note 8 on dried plums cooked with meat — see note 5. Levush has difficulty — Berachos siman 11 wrote Bach's distinction that fruits too, if their way is to cook, all agree borei peri haEtz on water; and possibly here the Mechaber's stam implies even a vegetable that is not its way to cook — and therefore a vegetable gives strong taste. Eliyah Rabba note 5 and Nekudat Hakesef challenged Levush on this.
