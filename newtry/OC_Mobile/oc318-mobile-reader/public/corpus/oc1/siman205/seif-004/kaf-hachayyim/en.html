4) There: And legumes, etc. Over roasted legumes, such as beans, lentils, corn, and similar foods, one recites Borei Pri HaAdamah. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 1, in the name of R' Yosef Binyamin in a manuscript. The Shelah in Shaar HaOsiyos, folio 94b, rule 5, paragraph 13, writes the same. Likewise, after lupin beans are cooked, one recites Borei Pri HaAdamah. Berachos 38b. It is a legume, as Rashi, of blessed memory, writes in Beitzah 25b. It is similarly listed at the beginning of Kilayim; see Rash there. This is contrary to one who wrote that it is included among tree fruits, which is incorrect because it contradicts the Gemara. See Divrei Menachem, Hagahos HaTur, paragraph 1.
