2) There: Even if he cooked them. This refers to vegetables that are good both raw and cooked. If they are more significant when cooked, one recites Shehakol when they are raw and Borei Pri HaAdamah when they are cooked. If the reverse is true and they are more significant raw, one recites Borei Pri HaAdamah when raw and Shehakol when cooked, as stated afterward. All this applies when one eats them alone. If he eats them with bread during a meal, he recites no blessing over them, because the bread covers them, as stated above in siman 177; see there.
