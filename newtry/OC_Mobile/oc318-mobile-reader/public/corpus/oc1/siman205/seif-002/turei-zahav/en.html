On the water in which they cooked, etc. In siman 202 seif 10 it is explained that there is a dispute between Rashba and Rosh, and it appears the ruling of Shulchan Aruch there is that there is doubt and one blesses shehakol; and one must say that here it speaks of something whose way is to cook or squeeze, for in this Rashba concedes that one blesses like the type of fruit.

But if they cooked them with meat. I say: what is different from a nut fried with honey, that we do not follow the honey, for a nut also has taste of itself and is not nullified because of honey; now not so with water, for it has no taste except what it received from meat — we follow the meat; and therefore it appears to me: if one cooked vegetables in vinegar or in a beverage that exists in Russian countries called borscht, one should not bless on them as on vegetables but per the blessing of the beverage, since it still has its own taste; therefore even if one cooked them with meat it did not lose its taste, like a nut in honey, as above; and see end of this siman.
