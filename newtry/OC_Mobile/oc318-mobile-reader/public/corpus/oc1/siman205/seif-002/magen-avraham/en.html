(א) On the water. Darkei Moshe wrote: in Rambam it implies specifically when they cooked them to drink them, for it is customary to drink them and it is like the vegetables themselves; and in Rosh general rule 4 it implies one should not distinguish, and so too in Mordechai — end of Darkei Moshe's words. And Kesef Mishneh wrote that Rambam did not come to exclude except when they cooked them to remove toxins. And Rosh wrote: water in which vegetables were cooked is like the vegetables themselves, since the essence of their cooking is for the vegetables; therefore, since the vegetables gave taste in them, we follow the taste — and even if he cooked the vegetables for the sake of their water for medicine, since most of the world cooks it for eating, the blessing on the water did not change because of this — end of his words. And Mordechai wrote: specifically vegetables from which the liquid that comes out is primary for dipping does this refer, and it is considered food as before (meaning: even though when drinking it one blesses borei peri haAdamah); but water of berries and apple wine that stand for drinking — one blesses shehakol nihyeh bidvaro and borei nefashot — end of his words; and so too Agudah, and so in Yoma daf 80b, and see siman 266, and so in Tosafot daf 38, and likewise above siman 204 regarding date beer; therefore the drink they make on Pesach from apples — one blesses shehakol per his opinion. And Shelah wrote the same applies to water in which grain types were cooked and the like — one blesses mezonot. And in Shulchan Gavoah he wrote: from this one says one who drinks water in which dumplings were cooked blesses mezonot, and it seems to me one blesses shehakol, for it is not the way of the world to drink dumpling water like water of cooked vegetables, for most cooked dishes one drinks the broth and eats it as the cooked dish itself — end of his words; and this is according to Rambam. But according to Rosh likewise, since the essence of the cooking is for the dumplings, one blesses mezonot, as our teacher taught. And it is difficult: in siman 208 seif 66 it says if it was soft and fit for drinking one blesses shehakol. And one can say it is different here, since he cooked the dumplings in them the sauce is nullified by the dumplings and one also blesses on it mezonot, similar to water of legumes that one blesses borei peri haAdamah as stated; but if they cooked until mashed — shehakol, as in siman 208 seif 8. Rather, necessarily the sauce's law is like food itself; and therefore if eating the dumplings themselves one blesses mezonot and need not bless on the sauce. And see siman 168 seif 11 and siman 210. However, sometimes they make zwieback on a little flour and put a little dumplings in it — then his main intention is to eat the zwieback and it is not secondary to the dumplings; if so, one should bless on the water and on the dumplings, as in siman 208 seif 4.

(ב) With meat. The taste of the meat in the broth is primary.
