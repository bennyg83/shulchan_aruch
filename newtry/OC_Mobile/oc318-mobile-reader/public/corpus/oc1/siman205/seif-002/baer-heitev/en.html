(א) Vegetables. See above siman 202, seif kaf-tet, in the name of Maharshal.

(ב) With meat. The water has no taste except what it received from the meat — we follow the main substance. Therefore, in my view: if one cooked vegetables in vinegar or in a beverage called borscht, one does not bless on them as on vegetables but per the blessing of the beverage, since it still has its own taste; even if he cooked it with meat it did not lose its taste, like a nut and honey in siman 202, seif 13. Taz.
