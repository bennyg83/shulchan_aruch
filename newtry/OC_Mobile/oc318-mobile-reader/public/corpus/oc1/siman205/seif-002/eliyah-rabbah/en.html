(ב) And leeks, etc. Ma'adanei Melech wrote: leeks — the Aruch explained puri in a foreign language; and I heard they are a species like onion in the form of a half-round, until here. But in the Targum on Beha'alotekha — chazir is leeks; and Orach Chayim wrote that it is eshlich in Ashkenaz language.
