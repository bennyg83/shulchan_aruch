And garlic and leeks. Taz brought regarding garlic and onion when they are soft and slightly aged — raw borei peri haAdamah, cooked shehakol; when aged — raw shehakol, cooked borei peri haAdamah; meaning when aged they change for the better through cooking without meat, for raw they are not fit at all. And what Rambam wrote that they changed for the worse — he still blesses. Magen Avraham note 4 regarding aged: there is a combination of Bach's view, and possibly out of doubt one blesses on garlic and onion shehakol per Bach; meaning alone; with bread it is obvious bread exempts; and likewise roasted meat with onions — for all, meat is primary.
