The blessing on vegetables. It has 5
seifim. On vegetables one blesses borei peri haAdamah, even when cooked. And likewise all fruits and legumes that are good raw and cooked — one blesses on them after cooking the same blessing that was appropriate for them before cooking. But gourd, beet, cabbage, and the like that are better cooked than raw — when raw one blesses shehakol, and after cooking borei peri haAdamah. Carrots and parsnips — when raw borei peri haAdamah; after cooking shehakol.[For they are considered to have changed for the worse, even if cooked with meat and thereby improved — the improvement is not from themselves but because of the meat in them. (R' Yitzchak, chapter Keitzad Mevarchin)] וכרתי [פי' פורט בלע"ז] כשהם חיים בורא פרי האדמה לאחר שבישלם שהכל: הגה דמחשבי נשתנו לגריעותא אפי' בשלם עם בשר ונשתבחו אין השבח מצד עצמן אלא מחמת הבשר שבהם: (הר"י פ' כיצד מברכין.
