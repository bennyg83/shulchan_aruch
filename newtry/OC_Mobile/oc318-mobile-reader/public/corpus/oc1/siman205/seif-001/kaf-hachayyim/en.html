1) [Seif 1] Over vegetables, one recites Borei Pri HaAdamah. If one erred and recited, 'Who creates species of grasses,' over vegetables, after the fact he fulfilled his obligation. Rishon LeTziyon in his commentary, folio 14, end of side a. Accordingly, the same applies if one recited, 'Who creates species of seeds,' over legumes: after the fact he fulfilled his obligation. There is nevertheless uncertainty if one recited, 'Who creates species of seeds,' over vegetables. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 4. Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 1, however, considers it obvious that he fulfilled his obligation, based upon the verse, 'and like a garden causes what is sown in it to sprout'; see there.
