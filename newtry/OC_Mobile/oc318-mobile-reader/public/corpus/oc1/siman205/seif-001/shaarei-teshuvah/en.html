And legumes. Baer Heitev — and wrote in Rishon LeTzion that if he blessed on legumes Borei minei desha'im he fulfilled after the fact; and Beit Yosef wrote likewise if he blessed Borei minei zera'im; but he was uncertain if he blessed on vegetables Borei minei zera'im whether he fulfilled, lest vegetables also be called seeds on that view; and in my humble opinion he fulfilled, for it is written "like a garden its plants will spring forth"; see Pesachim daf 39.
