That are good cooked. Taz at Magen Avraham notes 3 and 9. He questioned somewhat regarding meyerin and rubin that are also good raw, and cited Rambam siman 12; Taz answered that nevertheless people do not plant them except to eat cooked (and it is possible that without fat and meat they change for the worse — nevertheless they plant with intent for this, as with roses in Yoreh Deah siman 11); and pickled vegetable — its law is as cooked, for they plant with intent for this; Eliyah Rabba note 1. And in note 7: vegetable kraut and salad — borei peri haAdamah; see there; and in siman 204 seif 1 regarding wild-growing herbs — shehakol. Salad in Latin with oil and vinegar — borei peri haAdamah there, meaning alone; not so with meat and other things for dipping — bless on the primary. See Ateret Zekenim 3.
