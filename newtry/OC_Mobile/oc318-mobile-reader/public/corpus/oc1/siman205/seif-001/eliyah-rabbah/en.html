(א) And cabbage, etc. Which they call sternunk, Orach Chayim; and Bach explained in the Ashkenaz language kohl kraut; and in all of these one must say: even though they are also good raw, nevertheless the practice is not to eat them raw for most people, as written in siman 202, seif 12; and with this the words of Magen Avraham in seif 3 are rejected — see there and in seif 9; see there and examine. And nevertheless on parsley and maror raw one blesses borei peri haAdamah, because if one cooks them by themselves it becomes for the worse; and see below siman 473.
