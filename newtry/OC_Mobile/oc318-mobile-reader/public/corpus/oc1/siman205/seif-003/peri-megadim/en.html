There is no — Taz does not distinguish between borei peri haEtz that changed for the worse through cooking and is improved by honey — it remains borei peri haEtz, as in siman 202 seif 13 regarding a whole nut in honey borei peri haEtz; and between fruit of the ground that changed for the worse and is improved through honey — shehakol. Roses in Orach Chayim siman 11 — one could say l'chatchila they are not fit for anything and they plant with intent for this; and wet zangvil cooked with honey in Rema siman 66 — possibly they did not change for the worse without honey per Taz. Nevertheless Magen Avraham note 5 and Eliyah Rabba note 4 — no difficulty from nut, as we need to say there regarding... and Taz himself left it requiring study; if so, one could say there is no distinction between borei peri haAdamah and borei peri haEtz in this — whatever the compote is primary and honey secondary, we follow it; even radish borei peri haAdamah compote. Kraut compote — even for Taz borei peri haAdamah, for raw they are not so fit; nevertheless raw too borei peri haAdamah per Magen Avraham note 3. Maror borei peri haAdamah includes all types of maror, even tamcha kraut; and see in Berachos Taz 11–12 on laws of preserves; requires study whether kraut is not considered wild-growing herbs siman 204, for I heard some say it is sometimes seeded.
