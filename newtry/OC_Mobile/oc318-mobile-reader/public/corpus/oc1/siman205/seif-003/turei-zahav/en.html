If one squeezed them, etc. Tur wrote, and my brother R' Yechiel wrote: all the more so if one squeezed them that one blesses on their liquids as the way one blesses on them; and it does not appear so, for certainly when one squeezes it changes for the worse. And proof to the matter: beverages that come from fruits — one blesses on them shehakol, and if one cooked them one blesses on the broth as the blessing of the fruit, etc., end of his words. And according to what I wrote in siman 202 — certainly something visible to the eyes and felt in the mind that more comes out through squeezing, and all the more crushing than what comes out by itself; and it appears to compare squeezing and crushing to actual cooking — there is no proof for our Rabbi Tur here from beverages that come from fruit, for there they come out by themselves and it is only mere moisture; and the words of my brother R' Yechiel appear correct, only that one may not be lenient against Tur, and one should bless shehakol; and further it appears that through crushing too Tur concedes that what comes out is like the fruit — then it is obvious it is like cooking, that the body of the fruit's strength comes out from it, as above.
