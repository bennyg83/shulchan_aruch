Turnip. Such as rubin and meyerin in a foreign language — and one must examine them whether if one cooks them without meat they change for the better, as written in seif 61. And also some of them are good to eat raw — see seif 61 that they are not worse than maror, as written in seif 61 in Beit Yosef siman 204: sesame — one blesses borei peri haAdamah; ground — shehakol (Avudraham). And it should be said because their form is not upon them at all — siman 208 seif 8.
