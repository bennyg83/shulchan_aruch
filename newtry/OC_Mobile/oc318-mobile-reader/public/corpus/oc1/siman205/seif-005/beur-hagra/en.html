(א) Seif 5, turnip, etc. Maharshal and water of turnip is turnip; and therefore when cooked or pickled it is discussed, and not like Rashi there s.v. parmi, etc.; and see there mishnah 2, or to a house, etc. And even though in ch. 83 of Shabbat they say "and this is when it has meat in it" — nevertheless turnip is primary, as Magen Avraham wrote seif 25; and see Tur that some hold like Rashi.

(ב) Or pickled, etc. For pickled is like cooked even for blessing; for even salted is like cooked, as written 38b; and all the more so pickled, for we say salted is likewise, pickled, etc.
