With meat. See Baer Heitev. He means that this applies specifically to water, which has no independent flavor and only receives flavor from other ingredients. A beverage that has its own flavor does not become subordinate to the flavor of the vegetables. See Peri Megadim, who rules that bran borscht requires Shehakol, while borscht made from beets requires Borei Peri HaAdamah because pickling is like cooking. Nevertheless, he remained uncertain about the matter and wrote that perhaps people rely on Magen Avraham in siman 205, according to whom in such a case one should recite Shehakol. Likewise, regarding dried plums, if the liquid is separated from the fruit, it appears that according to all opinions one recites Shehakol. See Yad Ephraim regarding this.
