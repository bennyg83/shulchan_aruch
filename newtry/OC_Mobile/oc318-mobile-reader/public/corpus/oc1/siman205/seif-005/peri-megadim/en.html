But — Taz's words are built on truth and reason Berachos 35a: everything goes after majority; and in note 9 Taz questioned: if so, vegetables in water — water is majority; explain there that water is not considered and is nullified regarding vegetable taste; and there is spice for this that has no taste of itself — therefore the vegetable taste in them is primary even though water is majority; not so wine in beer and the like, for although taste is felt, nevertheless the majority has taste on its own; and now when cooked with meat the water receives good taste from meat, then vegetable taste is nullified in the majority water since the water has good taste from meat; and he derived a law from this: if one cooked vegetables in vinegar and borscht that have taste on their own — follow the majority vinegar and borscht; even if cooked with meat one blesses per the beverage's blessing, which is primary. And now if this borscht is turnip rubin called borkis — borei peri haAdamah the borscht (not within the meal and on the meat shehakol) — beer days shehakol even from wheat and barley flour shehakol; see Orach Chayim 65. Wine vinegar — apparently if cooked with meat and sweetened with wine borei peri haEtz — it is an error, for vinegar's blessing is shehakol, for they do not plant it, etc., and it changed for the worse per Magen Avraham note 12; and one must say the beverage in borscht refers to turnip rubin; and from this, when placing in a pot meat and vegetables without water, pot roast, and much water is emitted from vegetables — one should bless borei peri haAdamah on the vegetable water, for it is not nullified in the majority, and a little fat emitted from meat. Magen Avraham note 5 and thus there will be explained.
