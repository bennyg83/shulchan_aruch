(18) Turnip when it is raw, etc. — turnip is better cooked than raw [Tur]; turnip is what we call meyerin and rubin; see Magen Avraham who is uncertain about this law; see Tur who also brings that some Geonim hold to bless on raw borei peri haAdamah; see Chayei Adam who wrote that it depends whether the custom of people in that place is to eat them raw; see Nishmat Adam who expanded on this.
