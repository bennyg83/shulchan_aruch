Its end will be neglect, and it draws sin. The sefarim write that this statement applies to the general population, because not everyone can merit rising to the exalted level of devoting himself exclusively to Torah. Exceptional individuals, however, may be found in every generation who can live in this manner. This is the meaning of the Gemara in Berachos 35b, "Many acted like Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai and were unsuccessful," specifically many. Hashem will certainly provide such individuals with their livelihood. Rambam writes similarly in chapter 13 of the laws of Shemittah and Yovel, "Not only the tribe of Levi," etc. See there. This is especially true when people are already prepared to provide for all his needs so that he may engage in Torah study. In such a case this concern certainly does not apply, as the arrangement between Yissachar and Zevulun demonstrates.
