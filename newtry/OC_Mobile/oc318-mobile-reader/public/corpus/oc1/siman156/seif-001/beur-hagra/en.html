Seif 1: For poverty, etc. Beginning of chapter 4 of Eruvin.
And he should be careful, etc. First chapter of Nedarim.
And he should be careful, etc., for a thousand, etc. Midrash Rabbah at the beginning of Matos, and there it states twenty thousand cities.
And he should be careful, etc. Sanhedrin 63b.
And some are lenient, etc. Tosafos there and at the beginning of Bechoros, and see there.
Rather, they associate, etc. Although a Jew is warned against this, as stated there on 63a because it says, “to Hashem alone,” we do not find that a ben Noach was warned against it. The verse “it shall not be heard” also does not refer to this.
And to give, etc. They prohibited dealings only before their festivals, as stated at the beginning of Avodah Zarah. See also the beginning of Bechoros.
