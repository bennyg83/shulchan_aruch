Merchants who travel long distances, however, and whose occupation is their principal activity are included in the teaching, 'Torah is good together with worldly occupation,' meaning it is good that a person know what is written in the Torah, for exertion in both causes sin to be forgotten. So writes the Bach. The Shelah writes that even householders should fulfill 'Torah is good together with worldly occupation.' He gives the mnemonic of three occurrences of the word az: 'then you will understand,' 'then you will succeed,' and 'then you will sleep.' Eight hours, the numerical value of az, should be devoted to Torah, eight to business, and eight to sleep. Regarding those who do not need to work, Magen Avraham writes that they nevertheless remain obligated to fulfill 'you shall meditate in it day and night.'
