1) [Seif 1] Afterward he should go to his business, etc. Meaning, do not ask: why do you say that he should go to his business? It would be better for him to study Torah continually. Therefore, he says that it is a proper course for him to go to his business, as we learned in Avos, chapter 2: any Torah study that is not accompanied by work will ultimately cease. This means that his money will run out, after which he will have to pursue his livelihood and will be unable even to establish fixed times for Torah study. It will also lead him to sin, for poverty will cause him to transgress the will of his Creator. Beit Yosef. We also say in Berachos 35b that many followed the practice of R' Yishmael and succeeded; see there.
