For his business. The general rule is that a person should follow the middle path. He should neither be excessively miserly nor excessively wasteful. Only pride and anger should he distance himself from to the furthest extreme. Rambam, Hilchos Deos.
Incidental. Meaning, he should work only sufficiently for his livelihood, while his primary occupation should be Torah study. It is a positive mitzvah to cleave to Torah scholars and their students in order to learn from their conduct. Rambam, there.
The non-Jew. See Responsa Shaar Ephraim, siman 24, concerning this. See also Magen Avraham, who wrote a substantial moral admonition in this siman. Eliyah Rabbah likewise cites several matters of ethical instruction in the name of Shelah and other mussar works.
