[3] [Levush] berachot of zimun for him, etc. — the wording of Shulchan Aruch: berachat zimun in explanation; and Magen Avraham wrote: and if he does not hear, he answers amen, as he wrote in siman 139 seif 7 — until here. And see what I explained there in siman 193, that they must hear the entire birkat hamazon l'chatchila. And She'erit Knesset HaGedolah wrote: and the custom is that many groups go out in zimun and each blesses birkat hamazon for himself.
