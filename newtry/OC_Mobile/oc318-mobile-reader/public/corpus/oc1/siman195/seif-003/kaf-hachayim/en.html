(3) There — if some of them see, etc. Members of the wedding canopy who cannot all eat in one house with the groom and divided into groups — even if they ate in houses that are not open to the place where the groom eats, all bless berachat chatanim; and needless to say if shames combines them — but even if shames does not combine them, since those in the other houses began when the members of the wedding canopy began, all are considered as one to bless berachat chatanim, since they eat from a meal arranged for the canopy. Tur in the name of the Rosh. However in Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 2 of Laws of Blessings they wrote in the name of Semag that Ritzba ruled to bless berachat chatanim in houses open to the house where the groom reclines there, through combination of shames — even though the two groups cannot see and hear the one blessing, end of his words. Bach and Beit Yosef wrote: for practical law it appears to rely on the Rosh z"l, end of his words. And so in Shulchan Aruch Even HaEzer siman 62 seif 11. And so Levush. And it is implied from Levush's words that only for berachat chatanim do they combine; but for zimun they do not combine except when we require seeing or shames as with other groups, see there. And so Perishah note 4, Rabbi Akiva Eiger note 4, Mishnat Zekenim note 3; and see below note 10.

(3) There — they combine for zimun. Meaning: they are able to combine and invite zimun together; but certainly they are permitted to separate — each group may invite zimun for itself. Bach. Yeshu'ot Ya'akov.
