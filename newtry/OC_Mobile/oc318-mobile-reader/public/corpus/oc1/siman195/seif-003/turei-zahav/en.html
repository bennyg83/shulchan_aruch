It appears there is a matter where reshus harabbim does not separate — namely what Tur wrote in the name of the Rosh here regarding members of the wedding canopy who divided into groups, that all are considered as one even if shames does not combine them, since they eat from a meal arranged for the canopy — for in this the matter does not depend on domains at all; and therefore when he brings this in Shulchan Aruch Even HaEzer siman 61 this law, he did not mention there to distinguish regarding the interruption of reshus harabbim.
