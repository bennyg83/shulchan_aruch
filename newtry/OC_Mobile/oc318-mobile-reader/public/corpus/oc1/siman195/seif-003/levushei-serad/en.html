There — because of hiddur of the King. This is the hagahah in siman 193 seif 2; and he does not explain like Magen Avraham there but per the simple meaning: if they want to invite zimun, the permission is in their hands. And here it deals regarding obligation — if there are not three in one place; for if there are three in each group one cannot say that he teaches here that if they entered first with intent, etc., there is a full obligation — not because of "the multitude alone," for that is when even one group of six divides.
