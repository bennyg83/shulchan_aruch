(2) There — if some of them see, etc. Two or three homeowners in summer days go out to the courtyard and each eats at his table, separate from one another — whether they combine for zimun: see Ketonet Yosef siman 7, that he concludes they do not combine, see there. And Zekhor LeAvraham brought note 7 and wrote that this is only when they did not sit from the outset with intent to combine together; but if they sat from the outset with such intent, certainly they combine even though they do not eat at one table, see there.
