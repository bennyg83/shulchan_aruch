Seif 1 — and such as, etc. Yerushalmi there: R' Yona and R' Abba bar Zimna in the name of R' Zeira: for two houses it is required — R' Yochanan said: and this is when they entered from the first hour with this intent, etc.; and the Rosh brought this there, and Magen Avraham, and so too Rashba. But Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe in the name of Or Zaru'a wrote that in one house it is not required; and so is implied in the Yerushalmi that says "these are the houses of the Nasi," etc., and the Rosh brought this there. However it appears there is a printing error in the Yerushalmi and it should read "this is R' Yitzchak," and he, etc., is before R' Yona and R' Abba bar Zimna "for two," etc.; and the meaning of "when they entered," etc., refers to the shames combining them — for otherwise he does not combine; and the above refers to "for two houses it is required." But in one house they need not enter from the outset; therefore afterward it asks "these are the houses of the Nasi, what," etc.

And there are those who say, etc. — as is clear in Bava Batra 55b–56a.
