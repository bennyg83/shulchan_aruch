And such as, Taz. He explained the Mechaber's words as Levush wrote; Magen Avraham (2) and for practical law we agree with Magen Avraham; and it is implied that "two" is like the letter of Magen Avraham in the name of Bach. However he added words according to what Rabbeinu Beit Yosef wrote in hagahah in siman 193 seif 2 — that even where they were already obligated in zimun, if they wish they are permitted and it is preferable to invite zimun (and he holds like the simple meaning, not like Magen Avraham there note 8). And now in one house from corner to corner — if there are three or ten in each group here — generally the condition is that they enter with such intent; for if they wish to invite zimun the permission is in their hands and it is preferable thus for the multitude; but it deals when there are not three or ten in every group — he teaches that there is no obligation to invite zimun specifically together except under these conditions; but requires further study — for the mishnah "two groups, berachot" (50) (1): when they see or shames, etc., and these invite for themselves — whence there is in each group enough for zimun; nevertheless when they do not see, etc., it is forbidden to invite zimun together. And it is possible for what is written in siman 193 note 3 — that when there is zimun it is not preferable except as a choice; and therefore when they do not see, etc., it is forbidden to invite zimun together — only they separate, these for themselves; and all the more so in two houses; it is possible even when there are not three or ten in every group it is forbidden to invite zimun together, for there is no such case in the multitude that they divide in two houses; and requires further study. And Magen Avraham (2) from this — and know that it is implied when there are five in every group, even though they see one another, they do not combine — as in siman 194 (2) Magen Avraham note 2, that with three one needs seeing and with ten it does not help except when there are ten in every group — then they combine for one zimun; but in Magen Avraham (55) note 12 it is implied that seeing faces combines for ten even in two domains; and requires further study now in this.
