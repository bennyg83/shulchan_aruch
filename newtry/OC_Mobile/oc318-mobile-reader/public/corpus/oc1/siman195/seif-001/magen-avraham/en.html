Two groups, etc. The translator did not examine here in Tur and also in Shulchan Aruch Even HaEzer siman 62.

And such as when they entered. From the stam of Rabbeinu Beit Yosef it implies that he holds that even in one house, if they did not enter with intent to combine, he does not combine them — for "three who ate as one" we taught, and as Rashba wrote; and even according to what is written in siman 193 seif 2 that we do not require fixedness at the meal's beginning — nevertheless we require that they be fixed at one table at the meal's end; and here each group is independently — therefore he does not combine them unless they entered for this purpose; and so Bach, unlike what Rabbeinu Beit Yosef wrote. And for the first view one must say: since there is in each group enough for zimun, he combines them even though they are not fixed together, as above.
