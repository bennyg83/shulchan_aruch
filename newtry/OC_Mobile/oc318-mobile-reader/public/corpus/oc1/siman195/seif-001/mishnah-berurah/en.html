(1) They see one another, etc. — even when each eats at his own table.

(2) They combine for zimun — meaning: if they wish they may combine and have one discharge all of them in birkat hamazon.

(3) And if not, etc. — even when they are in one house, and even if they entered from the outset with intent to combine together.

(4) And if there is a shames — meaning: even if he does not eat at all.

(5) He combines them — meaning: even if they are in two houses and do not see one another.

(6) And such as when they entered, etc. — from the stam of the Mechaber it implies that even in one house, when they see one another, the two groups also do not combine together unless they entered from the outset with this intent; but many Acharonim wrote that in one house we do not require at all that they enter from the outset with this intent — and wherever they see one another alone, or even when they do not see one another and there is shames between the two groups, it suffices for combination; and so is the view of the Gra in his Be'ur and in Aderet Eliyahu, see there.

(7) Reshus harabbim, etc. — Taz wrote: it is not specifically reshus harabbim that is sixteen amot wide; the same applies when a private path separates between them; and it is possible that specifically when it is fixed also in rainy season days [Rabbi Akiva Eiger].

(8) Under any circumstance — meaning: even when they see one another and there is shames serving both groups.
