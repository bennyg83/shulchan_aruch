Seif 1 — if some of them see one another. In Rashbash's responsum siman 107 he wrote: only when there is zimun for each group and group independently — with this they combine to invite zimun together; but that there be enough for zimun through combination — no. And see above in Beit Yosef siman 55, what he wrote on it — that the Rosh in Shulchan Aruch within the opening taught the law that here it is implied that he holds that here seeing one another helps to combine the measure for zimun. And so in Rashba's responsum siman 96, brought in Magen Avraham there note 14; and so Peri Chadash there note 13.
