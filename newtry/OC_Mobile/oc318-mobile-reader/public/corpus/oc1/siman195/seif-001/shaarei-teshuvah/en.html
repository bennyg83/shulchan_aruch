Together. Avraham ben Rav Chaim, and see Bach that he forgot it is not specifically "forgot" but likewise intentional, see there. And see in Eliyah Rabbah who was uncertain with two who ate and the third drank a revi'it — and afterward one forgot and blessed — whether the third who ate may invite zimun upon those two, or since only one is obligated in zimun, no; and so it is reasonable, end of his words; and see below siman 196, that if this one who drank a revi'it blessed the final berachah he can no longer invite zimun with them, see there. And Eliyah Rabbah wrote: with ten, if three forgot and blessed — nevertheless they bless upon them n'varech Elokeinu, as below regarding eating vegetables, see there. And see in Panim Me'irot part 2 siman 6: with ten, if another blessed alone, they do not invite with the Name and fulfill through his zimun while he does not fulfill; and see there that somewhat from his words it is implied specifically in such a case; but if one of them joined zimun — and all the more if three of them blessed in zimun — they no longer combine for ten to bless with the Name; and not like Eliyah Rabbah — for he concludes there on Beit Yosef siman 193 according to Tosafot's explanation that Tur brought, and there it states: but if one of them preceded and invited zimun with three, or blessed birkat hamazon for himself — the ten do not divide, for those with him cannot invite with the Name since he preceded and invited zimun — the zimun departed from them, end of his words Beit Yosef. And on this Peri Megadim wrote: and I did not merit to understand his words in what he wrote "if he blessed birkat hamazon for himself," etc. — that it is no worse than eating with them — from the case of a leaf of vegetable, etc., see there; it is implied specifically in that manner that Beit Yosef wrote that he blessed alone he argues; but not in the other manner that another blessed in zimun; and so at the end of his words, as stated; and requires further study, since according to Beit Yosef in every manner he does not combine.
