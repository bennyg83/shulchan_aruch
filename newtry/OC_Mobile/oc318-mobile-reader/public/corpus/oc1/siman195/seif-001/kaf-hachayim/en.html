(1) [Seif 1] If some of them see, etc. — And if a glass partition separates between the two groups and they see one another through the glass partition — whether they combine for zimun: Petaḥ HaDevir wrote in note (1) that this depends on what the poskim disputed — Rashba and Or Zaru'a whom Beit Yosef brought in siman 288 regarding berachat ner, in a case where one sees the candle inside a glass container; and the conclusion from the poskim's words is that it counts as seeing; therefore they combine, provided both hear the words of the one blessing berachat zimun in explanation. But if it is a way of seeing called "espizio" in foreign tongue — two groups do not see one another and do not combine, see there. And Ro'ach Chaim and Shomer Emunim brought in Asifas Dinim ma'arekhet 7 note 6, see there. And it appears that since there is a dispute among the Rishonim, and so too from the stam of Shulchan Aruch it appears the view of Maran z"l is to rule like Or Zaru'a, that one must take the light out of the lantern and bless on it — since he did not bring Rashba's distinction in Shulchan Aruch; and also because it is possible to distinguish between seeing light and seeing for combination — therefore one should not rely on combination through seeing a lantern except for zimun without mentioning the Name. And see further on the subject of combination above siman 55 and in our words there b'siyata diShmaya.
