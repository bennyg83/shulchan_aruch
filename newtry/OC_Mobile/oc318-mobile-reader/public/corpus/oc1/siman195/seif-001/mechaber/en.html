Groups that eat in many places — what is their law. It contains 3 seifim: Two
groups that eat in one house, or in two houses — if some of them see one another, they combine for zimun; if not, they do not combine. If there is one shames for both of them, he combines them — and such as when they entered from the outset with intent to combine together. And there are those who say that if reshus harabbim separates between the two houses, they do not combine under any circumstance.
