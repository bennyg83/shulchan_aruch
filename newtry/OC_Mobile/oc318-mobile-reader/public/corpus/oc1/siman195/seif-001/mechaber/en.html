1. Two groups that eat in one or two houses that can see one another
join to form a zimun. If not [they do not see each other], they do not join. If there is one waiter [servicing] both groups, they join [to form a zimun]. This applies when they entered their meals to begin with with the intention of joining together [to form a zimun]. And there are those who say that if there is a public domain between the two houses, they do not combine in this matter.
