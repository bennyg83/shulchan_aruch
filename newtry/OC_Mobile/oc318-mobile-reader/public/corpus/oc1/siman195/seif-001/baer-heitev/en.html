Together. — That which is in siman 193 seif 2, that they need not enter together but it suffices if they ate afterward together — means that they ate in one place; even though each ate from his own loaf, nevertheless they are adjacent to one another as is the way of eating in one group. But here it deals with each group sitting in a different place, such as from corner to corner; and regarding this we said above that there is no obligation of zimun in this except because of hiddur of the King. And here it deals with obligation to invite zimun together — such as if there are not three in one place, or to combine ten — for this we require the conditions written here. Taz, see there.

Reshus harabbim. — It is not necessary that it be reshus harabbim that is sixteen amot wide; rather even a private path [separates], Taz. And see in Halakhot Ketanot part 2 siman 148 what he challenged from the case of lashes; and there is no proof from there, Yad Aharon. And the members of the wedding canopy combine in every matter, Taz.
