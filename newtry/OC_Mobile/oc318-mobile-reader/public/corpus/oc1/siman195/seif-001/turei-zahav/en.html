And such as when they entered from the outset, etc. Beit Yosef brought this in the name of the Yerushalmi in two houses, and wrote on this: it appears that even with two groups in one house he also speaks specifically when they entered from the first hour for this purpose — for it is like reclining together; and so Ra'avad, and so it appears from our master's words. And even though Rashba wrote on this — and from here that even for berachat zimun one requires reclining — but I saw the great commentators who explain that for berachat zimun one does not require reclining; therefore according to what the Rosh wrote in this siman, that even though they did not bless ha-Motzi together they are fixed for zimun — in two groups too we do not require that they enter from the outset with intent to combine together; and if so our master need not write stam that they entered first to combine together. It is possible that what he wrote "and such as when they entered from the outset to combine" refers only to combining two houses through seeing or through combination of shames; but two groups in one house, even if they did not enter first with intent to combine together, combine; and so appears from the simple sense of the Yerushalmi, end of his words Rabbeinu Beit Yosef. (I emended some of his words, for it appears there is a printing error in his words in the edition.) And if so, his words here in Shulchan Aruch are also explained thus — and this is a far interpretation: that our master Tur cited in one expression also in one house, and on all of them wrote the condition that they enter first, etc.; and it appears to me that this is not needed at all — for according to our reasoning, for we say in siman 193 that they need not enter first, but it suffices if they ate afterward together — meaning they ate in one place, even though each ate from his loaf, nevertheless they are adjacent as the way of eating in one group, even though they divide into two groups, nevertheless in the essence of the place they are adjacent. But here it deals with each group sitting elsewhere, such as corner to corner — regarding this we said above there is no obligation of zimun except because of hiddur of the King, as Beit Yosef wrote end of siman 193 in the name of "I found written," etc.; and here it deals with obligation to invite zimun together — such as if there are not three in one place, or regarding combining ten — for this we require even in one house the conditions written here that they see, etc., or through shames, and on condition they enter first with intent to combine — then the obligation of combination applies upon them even though they are not adjacent in one place; and this itself is Rashba's intent, who wrote that from here and from this Yerushalmi it is implied that reclining is required for zimun — since it was necessary "they entered first," etc.; and this is even when sitting adjacent to one another. But the great commentators hold that for those adjacent one does not require zimun at all, even when each eats from his loaf, as above; and here that it was necessary "they entered first," etc., is when they are not adjacent but corner to corner — and then they have no combination except by entering first with intent to combine, as it appears to me clearly.
