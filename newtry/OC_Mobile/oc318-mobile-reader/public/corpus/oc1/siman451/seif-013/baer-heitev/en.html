A patch. Every patch in this se'if means what was fixed by attaching a piece upon a piece with nails; but a patch made on a tin vessel by dripping a piece of tin from heat burns whatever was absorbed and what is in kind; and also when they repair a silver spoon and the like by placing a piece of silver there, the craftsman first puts the spoon in the fire; and if so there is no concern in this. Taz; Chok Yaakov. And old vessels that are lined with tin, like copper vessels: the Raem ruled in part 1, siman 43, that they do not need hagalah, since the tin interrupts and they are like new vessels. And Magen Avraham wrote: and accordingly even if one lined them with old tin, for the tin itself is libuned. And see Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah, who lengthened to bring the dissenting views, and wrote that some of the great ones of the land are stringent upon themselves; and in any case it appears that it is good in the eyes of God and man to immerse it after the tin coating without a blessing; end quote. And see Yoreh De'ah siman 120. And Peri Chadash of Orach Chaim ruled that one needs hagalah after tin coating; and so they practice in Jerusalem; may it be rebuilt; see there.
The prohibition. And then even if the absorption of the prohibition preceded, it is permitted, since the place of the patch was libuned. And the Bach wrote: it appears to me that where the absorption of the prohibition preceded, libun of the place of the patch does not help; see there. And Magen Avraham wrote: therefore it appears to me that where absorption preceded the patch, one must place so many coals that sparks fly from it; which is not so if the patch preceded the absorption, for which light libun suffices, as is written in se'if 4 in the gloss. And Chok Yaakov disagrees with the Bach and Magen Avraham, and concluded according to the plain sense of the Mechaber. And if it was libuned thus, or one removed the patch once after hagaling, it is thereafter permitted to hagal thus with the patch each time, since thereafter it is no longer called that the absorption of a prohibition preceded the patch. And all this is where the patch is well attached such that there is no crack between the body of the vessel and the patch; for otherwise, even where the patch preceded, one needs libun, or to remove the patch, or to widen the crack so that one can scrape it. Chok Yaakov.
And scrape them. And it is better to place coals on the place of the mark. Magen Avraham.
