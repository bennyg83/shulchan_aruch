(א) Pre-talai — explanation: the talai was made when the vessel was new, or they kashered the vessel before placing the talai.

(ב) Place coals. Requires study — Rashba not so except when solder preceded absorption, concerned substance in existence; Bach: when absorption preceded solder, coals do not help — must libun whole vessel; Or U'Mussar ch. 58:19. Therefore when absorption preceded solder — place many coals until sparks fly — "until body of issur burns"; if solder preceded absorption — light libun as seif 4 gloss. Old pewter-coated vessels — some require hagalah afterward; R' Eliyahu Mizrachi CA 43: no hagalah needed — like absorption preceded solder; concludes no hagalah — pottery needs light libun unable to touch other side; melted lead is like light libun; pewter interrupts — like new (even old pewter whitens); Hagahot Maimoniyot, Shelah siman 461; YD 120 immersion — above s.k. 24.
