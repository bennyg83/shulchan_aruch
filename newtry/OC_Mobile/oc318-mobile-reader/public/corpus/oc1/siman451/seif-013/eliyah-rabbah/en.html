[13] And there are those who are lenient, etc. — because permitted was absorbed; and see siman 447 seif 6 — Rashba holds it is called prohibited absorption; if so, even b'dieved one should forbid unless one whitened it; so Magen Avraham wrote; and it appears one may rely in great loss on poskim who hold it is called permitted absorption; and furthermore in Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 17 of Laws of Forbidden Foods he wrote that light libun suffices on Rambam who deals with prohibited absorption; and even though in Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 1 of Laws of Yom Tov he wrote: specifically regarding permitted absorption — meaning there he wrote in the name of Semak and here in the name of R' Avigdor; and furthermore in Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 5 of Laws of Chametz he wrote that regarding chametz, even though its use is literally through fire, hagalah suffices; and so Kolbo siman 48 and Rabbeinu Yerucham page 40 in the name of Rashba wrote; and from this one may wonder on what Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Rashba to the contrary; and see note 38; and even though we do not hold thus — in any event here one may certainly rely.
