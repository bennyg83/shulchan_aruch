Spoons [Taz] and in Tamim De'im siman 35 — one who is not concerned can kasher; and the halacha is not so; like pottery vessels seif 1 for an oven — it does not help; and so he wrote; and b'dieved if one kashered a forbidden thing and used it intentionally — it requires study — it is like using intentionally in a pot ab initio forbidden; and we do not nullify d'rabbanan issur; see in Peri Megadim Yoreh De'ah. Peri Megadim Eshel Avraham — it is difficult and permitted to kasher — I say in the name of Mahari Weil in Rashba note 23; and Magen Avraham wrote horn spoons etc.; and in Bach seif 8 in the gloss — bone vessels require hagalah and it implies they are permitted because they are hard; see such as this — horn is concerned and bone is hard.
