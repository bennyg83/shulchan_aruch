(9) There — and it is permitted to retain them, etc. — Pesachim 30a — there is a dispute: Rav said break them and it is forbidden to retain them after Pesach and to use them even not in its kind — a decree lest in its kind; and Shmuel said give them after their time and use them whether in its kind or not in its kind; see there. And the poskim ruled like Shmuel; and even though we are lenient — the halachah is like Rav in prohibitions — this case is different because Shmuel relies on Rashi. Beit Yosef. Bach.
