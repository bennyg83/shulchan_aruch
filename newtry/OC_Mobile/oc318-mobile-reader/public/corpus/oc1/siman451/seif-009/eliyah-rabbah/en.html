[9] All rust, etc. — Taz explained: specifically what we call rost when there is substance; and when one scrapes from there there is like dust; but when there is blackness or redness and it is impossible to remove the substance entirely even after scraping — there is no substance, only appearance alone — one kashered it after scraping all that is possible; nevertheless in any event one should whiten that place — until here his words briefly. And it requires study — behold he wrote afterward in libun one need not remove rust; if so, why do I need scraping; and possibly specifically when whitening the entire vessel, which is not so when whitening one place. But it is difficult — behold even in actual rust libun helps (instead of libun) in one place, as written in seif 5; and what difference is there between no substance; and one may say: on the side of what is better Taz spoke of libun and not by law; but it requires study — behold Darkei Moshe Yoreh De'ah siman 121 wrote thus: and if one cannot remove the rust, Issur veHeter wrote that even if only a little remains it has no remedy through hagalah — until here; and so Maharil wrote; and possibly it deals with rust that has substance; and in my Yoreh De'ah there I challenged Bach there and Perishah in the name of R' Shlomo in the gloss that it is permitted — and they wondered at their reasoning that it is fit to forbid; and they did not mention the poskim mentioned above who forbade explicitly; however in Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 14 of Laws of Forbidden Foods he wrote to permit — and to this R' Shlomo leaned; and so it appears to me — for it deals with the matter Taz deals with where there is no substance in the rust; and with this everything is resolved b'seder.
