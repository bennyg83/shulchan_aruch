Rav says the utensils must be broken and may not be retained for use after Pesach, even with a different kind of food, as a decree lest they be used with the same kind. Shmuel says they may be retained until after the prohibited time and then used with either the same kind or another kind. See there. The authorities rule like Shmuel. Although the general rule is that the law follows Rav in matters of prohibition, this case differs because Shmuel follows Rabbi Shimon. Beis Yosef; Bach; Erech HaShulchan, subsection 1.
