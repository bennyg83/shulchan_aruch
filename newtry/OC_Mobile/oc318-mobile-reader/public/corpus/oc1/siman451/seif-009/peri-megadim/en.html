(א) Rather [Taz] the general rule: as its use so kasher it — if used via pouring requires pouring without interruption of the stream; if used in kli rishon on fire requires that one kasher in a large yorah and raises boiling specifically like BY siman 462 note 3; and if used in kli rishon after removed from fire — b'dieved in this that one places into such a kli rishon; Peri Chadash; and l'chatchila they practice all in a yorah on fire and raises boiling and bubbles; Levush wrote pouring requires also a second time — they wondered at Peri Megadim; and I say that in pouring from kli rishon without interruption of the stream — once; and so is the halacha; and perhaps it is a mistake there.

(ב) And know that the general rule: as their use so kasher them; and as the Mechaber decided — all the more so in kli sheini and in Bach seif 8 he brought in the name of Rabbeinu Shmuel Mitzulani that ShTat holds in kli sheini hagalah does not help — only kli rishon; and Peri Chadash in seif 5 wrote — whence does he have this; and Ramah in Peri Chadash the Great and Berach in Responsa — it appears kli sheini requires pouring; Magen Avraham note 3 and Rash STat holds pouring is a doubt whether like kli rishon and we require kli rishon — it requires study. And indeed kli sheini and kli shelishi — their absorption and emission are not equal. And also talya'ir on which they place a lump — for one who is stringent see note 94; Magen Avraham siman 462 note 15 — hagalah via pouring also does not help because water cools; and a lump in kli sheini its law is like kli rishon and requires hagalah from the law that it should be kli rishon.

(ג) Pickled from time to time — forbidden in a vessel or chametz beer for one who forbids because it absorbs and emits in whole; and even in metal vessels; and as we must say below at end of the siman — see there; nevertheless if one kashered in kli rishon removed from fire — b'dieved in this even though it does not raise boiling; see note 61; and Peri Chadash in Shach note 127 — but via pouring — forbidden even b'dieved because pouring is a doubt whether it cooks like a peel or not; and to be stringent not to be lenient — therefore even a vessel forbidden via salting from chametz — which does not absorb in a vessel except like k deposit — hagalah via pouring does not help; see in Peri Megadim Yoreh De'ah 98. And nevertheless this doubt of pouring — one does not make from it a doubt to be lenient unless if a doubt whether pouring forbids in it and similarly; and to say perhaps not pouring and perhaps it does not cook and does not absorb at all — no. And proof from Yoreh De'ah siman 98 and 92 — and pouring in blood forbids; and blood that cooked in it is d'rabbanan — apparently it is not a doubt. And also without this it is clear from many places thus.

(ד) If a vessel absorbed issur via pouring and one kashered in kli sheini and cooked in it the food — permitted without great loss — why should we be concerned for it? If per Taz Yoreh De'ah 105 note 4 Derush — it appears without the blade in kli sheini that absorbs — behold it was kashered in kli sheini; and if from the reason of pouring — pouring cooks only like a peel and there are sixty in the food against the peel of the vessel alone — in a wide bowl — see Yoreh De'ah 92 seif 2 note 4 unlike Pesach without the blade — there is to forbid Magen Avraham siman 462 note 127; and so is the halacha; and even with the blade — there is to forbid — it requires study.

(ה) See Yoreh De'ah 92 Taz note 34 — if one inserts a vessel into a yorah on the fire and raises boiling and removes the vessel from there — one does not kasher from this vessel onto a kli rishon — meaning if used in kli rishon removed from fire and absorbed chametz — b'dieved hagalah in kli rishon even though it does not raise boiling; or a vessel via pickling — kli rishon suffices even though it does not raise boiling; but to draw in a cold vessel from kli rishon — even though it waited inside kli rishon on the fire and raised boiling inside this vessel — when he removed to be stringent — it is not like kli rishon literally that was heated — its sides via fire literally.

(ו) If they poured beer strong in one's mouth via pouring to a bowl — it appears its law is like kli rishon as Shach wrote Yoreh De'ah 69 note 38 in the name of Afikei Ravivim — hot kli sheini and sharp absorbs and cooks in whole; and therefore bowls require hagalah in kli rishon literally as stated — for sometimes they pour on them in one's mouth; Magen Avraham siman 462 note 4; and see at end of the siman I will explain further.
