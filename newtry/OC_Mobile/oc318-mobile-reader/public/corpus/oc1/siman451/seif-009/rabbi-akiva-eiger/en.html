And as written siman 94. Explanation of Magen Avraham's words: the distinction between that case in siman 92 and that case in siman 94 — in siman 92 we say it absorbed in its entirety; and in siman 94 we say it does not discharge entirely; but in truth it would appear the distinction is only that in siman 92 the pot is entirely hot; but in siman 94 the spoon is not hot; and proof for this — behold it is brought there in siman 94: and one says if the metal spoon is presumed throughout — if part of it is hot, all of it is hot; and this is Rabbeinu Peretz's view — behold if hot throughout, sixty is required against all of it; but in a wooden spoon, and likewise for us in metal, sixty is not required — only against what is inserted in the pot, because the spoon is cold; if so, there is no contradiction here from that case in siman 92; and it is clear.
