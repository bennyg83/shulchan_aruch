There, in the same discussion. And as is written in siman 94. The explanation of the Magen Avraham's words is that the distinction between that of siman 92 and that of siman 94 is: in siman 92 we say that it absorbed throughout, and in siman 94 we say that it does not expel its entirety. But in truth it would seem that the distinction is only that in siman 92 the whole pot is hot, whereas in siman 94 the spoon is not hot. And a proof for this: for it is stated there in siman 94, and some say if the spoon is of metal we estimate against its entirety, for if part of it is hot, its entirety is hot; and that is the view of Rabbeinu Peretz. Thus if it is hot throughout, one needs sixty against its entirety. But with a wooden spoon, and likewise for us with metal, that we need sixty only against what was stuck into the pot, because the spoon is cold: if so, there is no contradiction from that of siman 92; and it is clear.
