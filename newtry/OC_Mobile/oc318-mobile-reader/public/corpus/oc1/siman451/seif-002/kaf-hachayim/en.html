Pottery pots, etc. — and the same applies to other vessels that are not pottery: if one does not wish to kasher or libun them, one may also use this remedy of scrubbing and preserving them until after Pesach; but he spoke in the present tense regarding other vessels — since they have a remedy, one does not do so but rather kasher or libuns them.
