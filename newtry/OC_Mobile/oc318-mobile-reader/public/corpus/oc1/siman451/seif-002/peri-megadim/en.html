(א) But [Taz] the Rosh wrote for the Mechaber in seif 2 and so in Tur, and he corrected in this because he said there specifically if one heated inside — but in a kiln whose flame is from every side it is not extinguished; how did he say in our ovens it is not extinguished, since the flame is from every side it is not extinguished? See Perishah therefore Taz explained: even in a kiln too, if one waits a long time sometimes it is extinguished; but in a short time it whitens well, and in a short time it is not extinguished — the flame rises from every side — unlike an oven that requires waiting inside and sealing and removing immediately lest it be extinguished. And if so the explanation is: since one inserts for great heating such as this, certainly he does not seal lest it be extinguished — for in a short time certainly it is not extinguished from every side, and a short time suffices; but Bach explained: since one inserts for great heating — if it is extinguished, it is extinguished immediately and he abandons it — see Magen Avraham note 4; and see there.

(ב) And if he gave heat in the oven and it became white so that sparks fly and he passed and cooked on Pesach intentionally — forbidden, since it is like cooking in a vessel ab initio forbidden; in shogeg it appears permitted. And if a noksha fell like crust in siman 462 seif 2 for a pot — hagalah does not help; it appears to me siman 113 seif 6 in Shulchan Aruch — he kashered it via fire because the essence is not in the noksha — it appears the essence is in the noksha, a fence against full chametz; or since they say noksha of dough — then the essence is in the noksha. And if there were permission even in Peri Chadash for Magen Avraham siman 462 note 5 — except we do not have it; as stated.
