(ב) And if they filled with coals etc. — the reason is that ordinary kuvia do not tend to crack, and therefore one need not be concerned lest he be cautious with it and not whiten it well. Bricks burned in a kiln have the law of pottery vessels, and if boiling chametz was poured on them they have no remedy except libun as is its law, and as will be explained in siman 461 [nevertheless libun helps for them even within our ovens, for we are not as concerned for them as for pots]. But bricks dried only in the sun have the law of earthenware vessels and hagalah helps for them; and if baking or roasting was done on them, their use is through fire and they require libun as stated.
