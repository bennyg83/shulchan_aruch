In Taz note 1: perhaps he will forget once and bring vessels and use, etc. — as it should read. And with this they did not decree due to lo pelug. It requires study — for due to this reason he should also permit placing chametz vessels on Pesach in a high place, as Magen Avraham note 2 wrote; and from Taz's words it appears here he agrees to Bach that it is forbidden; and in my humble opinion in any event it is not applicable to say lo pelug except at the time of the prohibition itself — such as placing chametz vessels on Pesach in a low place, where certainly it is forbidden lest one use them; and therefore also in a high place it is forbidden due to lo pelug; and it is literally comparable to the case of a candle on Shabbat; not so if one places Pesach vessels all year — it is simple lo pelug is not applicable — for what prohibition is in the matter, even low, since there is no chametz prohibition yet; only because he wants it to be permitted to use them on Pesach therefore he places them where the hand does not control — lo pelug of Rabbanan is not applicable here, since no prohibition applies here at all; only the main concern is on Pesach itself, as it is said: just as it is forbidden to use vessels standing in a low place lest one forget and use them — likewise it should be forbidden to use even vessels standing in a high place due to lo pelug; it is simple this is not comparable to the candle case — for here even in vessels standing in a low place themselves, if it were clear one would never use them with chametz — it is permitted to use them on Pesach; and therefore those standing in a high place there is no reason to forbid due to lo pelug; and if you come to forbid them because although standing at height nevertheless perhaps he forgot once and used chametz — for this we are not concerned, since it is nitelaf and is only Pesach stringency; even in low automatically in height one may permit; and this is Taz's intent regarding "perhaps he will forget," etc. — one need not be concerned, for it is only stringency and due to lo pelug they did not decree here, for this is not comparable to the candle case for the reason I wrote; and even if this is not Taz's intent — so it appears to me the matters are correct in their reasoning; and furthermore so it appears to me another reason to distinguish: regarding Pesach vessels standing at height one must be concerned for two forgettings — lest he forget and use vessels with chametz, and afterward forget and return them to their high place above; and it is simple we are not concerned for this; and just as we are not concerned simply lest he forget and take chametz vessels and place them at height among Pesach vessels — all the more so here where we would need to be concerned he lowers them and after using them with chametz returns them among the Pesach vessels standing there; and a reasonable matter — for all this we are not concerned; not so with chametz vessels standing on Pesach — properly there is concern lest he forget and lower them and use them all the days of Pesach.
