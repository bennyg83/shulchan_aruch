2) There. Earthenware pots, etc. The same applies to utensils made of other materials if one does not wish to kasher them with boiling water or fire. One may scrub them and retain them until after Pesach. The Shulchan Aruch speaks of the usual case, because utensils made of other materials can be kashered and people ordinarily kasher them with boiling water or fire rather than put them away. Chok Yaakov, subsection 4.
