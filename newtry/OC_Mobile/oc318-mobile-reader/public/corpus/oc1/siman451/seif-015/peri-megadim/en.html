Baskets [Taz] — hagalah does not help and one must buy new ones; see seif 18 in vessels — certainly if used chametz hagalah does not help; and there if one left a hot thing — possible even b'dieved forbidden because presumably there is chametz something in the holes; see there; and here in a doubt l'chatchila one must buy new ones; and b'dieved even without hagalah if one salted in twos — one does not forbid from a doubt to establish perhaps there is chametz there; Magen Avraham note 20; and if one salted and chametz something was found in the holes — siman 462 seif 14 and there; and Peri Chadash note 37. And in truth also Taz's intent l'chatchila in a doubt one must be stringent and buy new ones; and b'dieved permitted — unlike baskets below where even b'dieved — it requires study.
