(15) There — but heating, etc. — hagalah does not help for pottery vessels because the Torah testified regarding pottery vessels that they never leave their mouths. Gemara there. And that heating helps if we are not concerned for them or one returns them to the kiln — as explained below — Tosafot wrote there: this is not considered leaving their mouths, for since they were fired it becomes like a new vessel, for such is the way of pottery vessels when making them — one puts them in a kiln to fire them; see there.
