If their use is in a first vessel. See my work Minchas Yaakov, klal 50, s.k. 21, that a utensil that drew liquid from a first vessel has the full law of a first vessel regarding that which was used in it, for stringency.
Their kashering is in a second vessel. See the Bach, that there is one who is stringent regarding Pesach. See what I wrote above in s.k. 8. The practice has already spread to perform hagalah on everything in a first vessel, as the Rav wrote in the gloss to seif 6.
One must pour over it. The Darkei Moshe wrote that one must be careful that the stream not be interrupted. See the Levush, who wrote that the pouring must be done over it three times, and the later authorities challenged him.
And it is inside the vessel. But outside the vessel, on the other side, it causes no harm at all.
