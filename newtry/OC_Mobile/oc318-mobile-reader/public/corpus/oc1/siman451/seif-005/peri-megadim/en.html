The cited ruling. The Peri Megadim analyzes the kashering of utensils for Pesach, according to their normal mode of use, with distinctions between hagalah, libbun, pouring from a kli rishon, soaking, cleaning away visible chametz, and after-the-fact cases. He also weighs the rules of absorbed taste, visible residue, sharp foods, earthenware, and whether the absorbed taste is treated as permitted before Pesach. In particular, it addresses knives, including handles, cracks, rivets, and rust, glass or coated vessels.
