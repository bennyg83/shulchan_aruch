Their use is in a kli sheni. For example, Beis Yosef's bowls. He does not mean actual bowls, for afterward he writes, and utensils that are used through pouring, etc., and that refers to bowls. Rather, this is the meaning: bowls are considered a kli sheni, and if one inserted a spoon into it, that spoon requires hagalah. All this is initially; see what I wrote in seif 1.
