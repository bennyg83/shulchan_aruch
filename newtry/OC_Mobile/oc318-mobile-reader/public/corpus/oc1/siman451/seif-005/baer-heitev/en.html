To pour. One must be careful that the stream not be interrupted: Darkhei Moshe; and one need not pour three times: later authorities (those who place a vessel inside a frying pan on the fire and boil inside the vessel and hagal with the vessel: it is doubtful whether this is like a kli rishon; see Peri Chadash).
Cracks. Even if they were made anew: see se'if 3. Magen Avraham.
Inside. Even a kli sheni. But from the outside, or on its rim from above, it does not hold back. Maharil.
