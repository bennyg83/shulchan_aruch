In Taz note 8 — meaning Mordechai not so regarding spits, etc. — it appears he meant that Mordechai there deals with a pan in which they bake pashitida — the pashitida is not meat or milk b'eyna, but there is also meat or milk inside; and since it is not absorbed from b'eyna literally through fire — properly this reason of permitted absorption suffices with this libun alone; but if what was absorbed is from b'eyna literally through fire — such as an oven in which they baked without liquid, or a spit on which they roasted chametz b'eyna — this reason of permitted absorption alone does not help to permit through hagalah; and by law complete libun is required; and it is close that there is a printing error and Taz's words need emendation; and it should read: except regarding vessels in which they bake things absorbed from permitted, etc.; and nevertheless his words require study — for behold Mordechai's proof is from Avodah Zarah who distinguishes between permitted absorption — and there it deals with spits and skewers on which kodshim meat was roasted b'eyna and became notar — and even so he says one kashered them because permitted was absorbed.
