Se'if 5. Vessels that were used, etc. End of Avodah Zarah: things used with hot water, such as, etc.: one hagals them, etc.
If their use, etc. The Rif and Rosh in the chapter "Kol Shaah"; and learn from what is written there: a wooden stirring stick, one hagals them in boiling water; and in a kli rishon: as it absorbed, so it expels. Specifically because they use it in a kli rishon; but a thing used in a kli sheni: a kli sheni suffices.
And a vessel that one uses, etc. As is written in the Yerushalmi of Shabbos and Maaserot there; and pouring is like a kli rishon: the strength of R' Yose from this: one who cooked in it and one who poured into it, etc.
Rather one needs, etc. As it absorbed, so it expels, as above; but a kli rishon does not suffice by pouring alone, which absorbs and expels only enough for a peeling, as is written in Pesachim 76a; and therefore at the end of Avodah Zarah he required for a large cauldron a [large] vessel, and they did not kasher it by pouring; and specifically regarding wine of libation, which is cold, they said there: thrust and boil; and as they distinguished regarding vessels of glaze in chapter 2 of Avodah Zarah and in the chapter "Kol Shaah": there its use, etc.
Every vessel. As is written in se'if 3 from the Yerushalmi.
