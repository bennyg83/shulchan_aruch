(א) In kli sheini. And Bach is stringent with them; see Peri Chadash.

(ב) Libun. And there are sages who ruled: when they coated it anew with tin one need not kasher it; and R' Shlomo challenged them with three challenges; and Peri Chadash elaborated for his sake and concluded in practice one may not rely on them — for sometimes due to speed the coating does not rise on the entire vessel; and he wrote that such is the practice in Jerusalem. And so Chayei Adam — the great ones of the land are stringent on themselves; and he wrote they should immerse after coating without a blessing.
