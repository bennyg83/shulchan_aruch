Seif 4 in the gloss — light libun such as digging — requires libun; it appears at first glance that even b'dieved it is forbidden; and in my humble opinion this is specifically if one used it b'eyna; but through a pot such as fish and the like — it is no worse than two pots; see Yoreh De'ah siman 97 and Taz; and so in Mishbetzot Zahav here; and therefore if it became absorbed in a gentile's house one may be lenient:
