Permitted through hagalah. For chametz in actual substance is not baked in it, but only with fat; what difference is there between fat and water? But pans in which cakes are baked in actual substance require libbun; see the end of seif 15 and seif 6.
And there are those who are stringent to kasher the frying pan with libbun. Because sometimes the fat dries out and the dough is baked on the pan in actual substance.
The cited ruling. The Magen Avraham discusses the kashering of utensils for Pesach, according to their normal mode of use, with distinctions between hagalah, libbun, pouring from a kli rishon, soaking, cleaning away visible chametz, and after-the-fact cases. In particular, it addresses mortars used with sharp spices and possible chametz crumbs.
