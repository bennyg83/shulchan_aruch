Se'if 11. A frying pan. Even though one fries in it, as is written in the Tosefta of Avodah Zarah: the cauldrons and warmers of hot water and frying pans, etc.: rinse them in boiling water.
And some are stringent. See Magen Avraham: that sometimes, etc., and it is like use by means of fire; and the Rosh rejected their reasoning: for if so, regarding a pot too one should be concerned; rather, nevertheless there is moisture of liquid in the cooked dish, only that by the wall it is dry, and it is not called use by means of fire.
