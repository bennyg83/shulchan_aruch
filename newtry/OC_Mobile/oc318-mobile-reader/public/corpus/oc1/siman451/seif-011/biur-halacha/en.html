Permitted through hagalah — see Chayei Adam general rule 125 law 5 who wrote this first view in the name of "some say," and most poskim hold that libun is required and it is not clear at all, for Rama himself wrote that hagalah suffices and only l'chatchila the practice is to do libun; and even if we say this was not in his text (for some books have this regarding a mortar in seif 16) — nevertheless one can prove thus from what he wrote that light libun suffices, and it is known that this applies only to something one is stringent to whiten but not to something requiring libun by law; and so is proven in Darkei Moshe that by law hagalah suffices here. It appears the citation brought in Rama misled him — for Tur writes in the name of R' Yoel and responsum of Rashba and Tur in the name of Rashi and Rabbenu Tam; if so, many disagree with the first view. In truth it is a printing error, for this is not mentioned here in Tur nor in Tur Yoreh De'ah siman 121 — the entire matter in the name of Rashi and Rabbenu Tam; only Rabbenu Yoel alone requires libun, and his son Avi HaEzri and likewise the Rosh disagree with them; and in Beit Yosef he brought that Rashba too holds libun is required; nevertheless he concludes that for practical law it appears like the Rosh and Avi HaEzri. He also brought that Mordechai and Hagahot Maimoniyot are lenient; and I found in Shibolei HaLeket in the name of the Geonim that its law is hagalah. [In Beit Yosef it implies he learned from Turat HaBayit also for our matter regarding Pesach; and in Rabbeinu Yerucham I found he wrote in the name of Rashba regarding a pan that only for other prohibitions should one be stringent to require libun, but not for chametz — and this is exactly the Mechaber's words in Yoreh De'ah siman 121 seif 4, intending Rashba's distinction from his broad view.] In any event we have several poskim who hold its law is hagalah [Avi HaEzri, Rosh, Shibolei HaLeket in the name of responsum of the Geonim, Hagahot Maimoniyot, Mordechai; those who forbid are Rabbenu Yoel and Rashba according to Beit Yosef's understanding as above]; and I found that Maharam Chalavah too holds like Rashba. In any event most poskim hold to be lenient, and b'dieved certainly hagalah suffices.
