A frying pan in which they fry, etc. Specifically an oven in which chametz was baked without liquid requires libbun, but not a frying pan. We are not concerned that perhaps the oil dried and the chametz was baked without liquid. Rather, even if it baked, burned, and adhered to the wall of the frying pan or pot, there is still moisture of liquid there. So wrote the Rosh, and the same law applies to a metal pot in which they cook, as the Rosh wrote.
