Se'if 12. Every vessel. From what we say in the chapter "Kol Shaah" regarding their handles in boiling water; and as is written there 74a: if part of it is hot, its entirety is hot. Rashba.
However if. The Ran rejected his words: for if so, R' Ashi should also have said their handles in the fire; rather there are two uses for a knife: one by fire, and that use does not reach the handle; and one in boiling water, and that reaches the handle too. And specifically regarding consecrated foods we say in Zevachim 96b: if one cooked in part of a vessel, etc.; and as is written there: but terumah needs only, etc.; and so he ruled in the Tur of Yoreh De'ah and in the gloss of siman 121, se'if 6. But from the words of Tosafos in Zevachim there it appears that regarding all prohibitions the entire vessel needs kashering; see there. And the Rashba reads in Pesachim: put them and their handles into boiling water; and regarding the hagalah at the end he relied, and their iron first in the fire as a mere stringency, as the poskim wrote; and see Magen Avraham. And the Shulchan Aruch and the Rav go according to their approaches: that Beis Yosef ruled in Yoreh De'ah siman 121 like the Rashba, that even if one used only part of it, the whole needs kashering; and so he ruled in the Shulchan Aruch there se'if 6. But in the gloss he ruled there like the Tur who disagrees with him because of a doubt of prohibition; and even though he ruled there in siman 94, se'if 1 and siman 98, se'if 4 in the gloss that we do not say if part is hot the whole is hot, nevertheless one needs the reason of a doubt of prohibition, because he ruled thus only regarding this: that it does not expel from that side; but regarding absorption he ruled in siman 92, se'if 5 that it absorbs, like the view of Tosafos in Zevachim there: that if its entirety is hot, it absorbs throughout; and there is no distinction except regarding a metal vessel: that in a metal vessel its entirety is hot even if the other part is not by the fire; and not like the Shach there siman 121 s.k. 17; and so we hold above in siman 94, etc.
One should not forbid, etc. It is implied from the words of the Taz of Yoreh De'ah there and the Rav there: even if one used that side that was not hagaled. Not like Magen Avraham.
And even, etc. For its entirety is not hotter than pouring. And this is the language of Darkhei Moshe in the name of Issur VeHeter: l'chatchila it is good to hagal it, for all year people touch it with dirty hands; however, by pouring even l'chatchila; end quote. And I have seen many who are lenient even l'chatchila; and it is good to hagal it l'chatchila like the view of Issur VeHeter. And all this is not like Magen Avraham. And all this is where one did not use its entirety; but if one used it, certainly kashering of part of it does not suffice, as is written there; and as is written in Zevachim there: if one cooked in part of the vessel, etc.; but if one cooked in its entirety, certainly kashering of part of it does not suffice according to all opinions.
