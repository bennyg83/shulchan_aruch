(א) Do not forbid b'dieved. For we do not say absorption travels through whole vessel, as Yoreh De'ah 121:6 (Darkei Moshe); Or U'Mussar ch. 58: kasher pot handle; b'dieved little concern, especially wood chip inserted whole into pot requiring kashering; also we hold vessel forbidden in part requires kashering whole; if spoon tip entered issur and kashered part and stuck in food — food permitted, spoon needs kashering again and insert whole in pot — end of his words. It appears specifically if stuck the part kashered; even if absorption spread whole vessel per YD 92 — we do not say it expels from side not inserted, as siman 94; but if stuck side not kashered — forbidden, as YD 92:5 drop of milk on pot; Darkei Moshe "does not travel absorption" means does not expel — see YD 121 on Tur. What is written "do not forbid b'dieved" — if already used; l'chatchila forbidden during Pesach if impossible to kasher.
