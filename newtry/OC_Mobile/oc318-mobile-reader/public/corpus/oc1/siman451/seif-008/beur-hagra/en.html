Se'if 8. One, etc. Metal vessels are explicit at the end of Avodah Zarah and are explicit in the verse; and as is written there. And wooden and stone vessels have the law of metal vessels, against the view of Rav Hai, who wrote that their law is like earthenware and they need heating in an oven; and a proof from what is written at the end of Avodah Zarah: a stone winepress, etc., and of wood, etc., and of earthenware, etc.; thus their law is not like earthenware. And at the end of chapter 5 of Keilim: an oven, a stone basket, and of metal, are tahor and tamei because of metal vessels; a stove, etc.; and see what I wrote in se'if 14. And as Raavan wrote, that we learn prohibition from tumah, as is written at the end of Avodah Zarah 75a and Niddah 65: as they said regarding purities, etc. But in the Tosefta of Zevachim it is taught: stone vessels do not require scouring and rinsing, rather rinse them; and the Mordechai forced that "rinse them" means hagal them, and that is forced. And further, according to the view that scouring is with hot water, it is because of hagalah; and as is written in Zevachim.
And likewise a vessel, etc. As is written in Chullin at the end of the first chapter: a bone vessel is like a metal vessel.
