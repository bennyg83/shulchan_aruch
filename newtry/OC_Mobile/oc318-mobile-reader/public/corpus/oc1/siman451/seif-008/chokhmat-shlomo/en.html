(There seif 8 in the gloss) and likewise bone vessels require hagalah. Behold the source of this law is from Mordechai brought in Beit Yosef, and his language: it is written in the name of Or Zarua — it appears to me that bone vessels do not require hagalah because they do not absorb; and so explained R' Yitzchak of Corbeil that we find no absorption that forbids regarding bone; and I practice as I wrote — but my heart strikes me somewhat, for we say chapter Kelei Midian "and every goat product" etc. — Rashi explained to bring horns, hooves, and bones; all the more a bone vessel and an ox-horn vessel — apparently there is absorption in bone vessels, since it says "you shall purify yourselves"; end of his words. See what I wrote on this in my work earlier — wonder at him. Now I was asked by someone: what proof from there — behold there it deals with tumah, and for kashering of Midian it says afterward through Elazar; and this is truly wondrous. To resolve this it would appear thus: one may say a vessel that does not absorb is not a vessel even for tumah; proof — we see glass vessels do not receive tumah from the Torah, only Rabbanan decreed tumah on them — necessarily because it does not absorb it is not considered a vessel for tumah; if so here too if bone does not absorb it is not considered a vessel even for tumah. Further proof from what we learn Shabbat chapter 1 daf 16b — there it asks: metal vessels are d'oraisa, as written "only the gold and silver"; and it is difficult to reverse — behold the verse in kashering of Midian deals, and from where for tumah; and if from "that which cannot go through fire you shall pass through water" — behold there it deals with immersion from a gentile from law of immersion of 48, not from tumah; and from where that metal vessels receive tumah from the Torah — necessarily to reverse from absorption. Proven it is a vessel for tumah and the same for reverse — certainly one and the same; examine. It would appear more through pilpul according to what we learn in Zevachim daf 96 — it asks: make a Temple pottery oven that they heat from inside; and answers: we do not make service vessels of pottery. Difficulty: if bone does not absorb, make of wood — necessarily bone also absorbs; but perhaps wood is not a vessel at all and we do not make service vessels from it — therefore he brought proof from what is said in Midian "and every goat product you shall purify" — it is a vessel in any event; and since it is a vessel in any event, difficulty — make an oven of wood; necessarily wood also absorbs; and it comes out well as above temporarily. What he does not bring proof from the mishnah that bone vessels are tamei — one may say it is only d'rabbanan like glass — therefore he had to bring proof from scripture etc. I found afterward in Tosafot Chullin daf 21 they wrote thus; see there and examine well.
