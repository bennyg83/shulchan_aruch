(ב) To pour on them etc. — from kli rishon; and Mahariv siman 193 ruled that pouring does not help, because sometimes one places hot pashida on it and it is as if used in kli rishon; rather one should kasher them through a white-hot stone and pour boiling water on it and roll to another place and pour so the boiling water goes over all of it [and with this it suffices even if the boiling water is from kli sheini, for it boils on the board and is like kli rishon]; and then it is permitted to place even hot matzah on them; and b'dieved it is permitted through pouring alone; and see above in seif 66 in the gloss and in Mishna Berurah there.

(ג) Boiling water — and some wrote that one should place on them additionally a cloth or board as interruption, lest chametz b'eyna still adhered to it; and see in Ateret Zekenim who concludes that thus one should practice with boxes and magadim, for it is nearly impossible to purify them so nothing remains between the adhesions; but on simple tables one need not be stringent; and see below in siman 452 seif 1 and 2 in Mishna Berurah there.
