(20) Even — Taz; and peeling and hagalah help like a wooden mortar; Taz 27; Magen Avraham 33; Mishnah Berurah. What he wrote regarding bran if it holds water, etc. — requires study: sacks require ash and beating — Magen Avraham 36; Mishnah Berurah 19 — unlike vessels where one puts flour and bran made of boards impossible to clean entirely even if it held water — requires study to permit this; what Mishnah Berurah 24 wrote hagalah does not help peeling on a craftsman's vessel — requires study if forbidden through pouring whether it helps; l'chatchila it does not help; b'dieved one may say it helps; Pri Chadash.
