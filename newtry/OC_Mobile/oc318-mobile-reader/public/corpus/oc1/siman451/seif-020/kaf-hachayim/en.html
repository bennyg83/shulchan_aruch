(20) In the gloss there — and there are those who forbid even in kli sheini. And so is Shulchan Aruch's view as written above, or Taz. And so Peri Chadash; and Chayei Adam note 8; and what Magen Avraham note 3 wrote — Shulchan Aruch omitted the law of kli sheini — he retracted from what Beit Yosef wrote; Chok Yaakov challenged him there; and so Eshel Avraham in Hagahot Beit Yosef wrote on Magen Avraham's words mentioned — it requires study; and so HaNe'ehav note 2 wrote to challenge all Magen Avraham's words mentioned; see there. And that which is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 105 seif 2 — heat of kli sheini b'dieved is permitted — and here one holds heating does not help for it — Eshel Avraham wrote there: perhaps regarding chametz he wanted to be stringent; see there. And regarding the law Chok Yaakov wrote there: in a place of great loss and prevention of Yom Tov joy one may be lenient b'dieved if used in kli sheini if it is not ben yomo — even in pottery vessels; see there. And so Eshel Avraham there. Mekor Chayyim note 6, R' Zalman note 33. Chayei Adam general rule 125 note 3 — meaning such as a spoon made for eating in it whose majority use is in kli sheini — they stick it in a bowl after they poured boiling inside from kli rishon; if they did not kasher and stick on Pesach in kli rishon b'dieved it is permitted if it was not ben yomo and there is great loss and prevention of Yom Tov joy. R' Zalman there. And it is simple this deals if it was clean — for if not, chametz on Pesach forbids in any amount, as is known.
