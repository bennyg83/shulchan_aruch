(s.k. 20) Baskets, even though the meat itself is permitted. That is to say: for the prohibition regarding baskets is only lest there were in the salt wheat grains that became leavened; and behold the Mechaber ruled in siman 447 that if one salted meat and did not clarify the salt, the meat is permitted on Pesach. And even though the Rama disagrees there, nevertheless why was the Mechaber stringent here regarding baskets?
It is nullified in sixty; and in such a case it does not reawaken on Pesach, as is written in siman 447.
Regarding a vessel, nullification does not apply, as is found in Yoreh De'ah siman 92, se'if 5, regarding a drop of milk that fell on the pot, etc., where the Semak wrote that we are in doubt whether it permeates through the entire vessel, etc.; and see there in the Shach s.k. 18.
As is written in se'if 27: from a doubt we do not presume a prohibition; and if they certainly used the baskets with chametz, the Rama ruled in se'if 11 that even hagalah does not help. And from this Chok Yaakov challenged the Taz's wonder in s.k. 15.
For there is no salting for vessels to expel.
Siman 96 in Yoreh De'ah and in the Shach s.k. 16.
