Se'if 4. And some are lenient. That is to say: the Mordechai at the end of Avodah Zarah wrote that those vessels that absorbed permitted [food] do not need so much libun, only if the straw burns from outside, for the libun dominates from side to side, and that suffices, since it absorbed permitted [food]: hagalah suffices, as is written in the sugya; and that libun is not less than hagalah and expels more than hagalah. And Beis Yosef wrote: according to those who say that chametz, since its name remains upon it, absorbed a prohibition, even such libun does not suffice, and complete libun is required; and so he ruled in the Shulchan Aruch. And Darkhei Moshe in s.k. 5 wrote, and Issur VeHeter wrote, that chametz is called "it absorbed permitted [food]," as is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 121; and that is what [he wrote], "and some are lenient if," etc.
And we practice, etc. He wrote this according to what Beis Yosef wrote in the name of Hagahot Maimoniyot, that the world practiced like those who forbid: that chametz is equal to other prohibitions.
But a thing, etc. That is better than hagalah, as above; and it burns even what is in the grooves, as is written in Terumat HaDeshen, for the fire burns everything; and as is written in the aforementioned Yerushalmi.
