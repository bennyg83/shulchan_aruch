We do not consider this, for this purpose, as absorption of permitted matter for which hagalah would suffice, since chametz before it was absorbed is called chametz, and it is therefore like absorption of prohibition. We are stringent for this matter. See what I wrote in s.k. 14, that one should not learn from this law to other places, for sometimes they were lenient because it was absorption of permitted matter. See the Taz, who asks about this law, and see Pri Toar to Yoreh Deah siman 93, s.k. 3.
And the libun. Although later at the end of this siman the Mechaber wrote that a spit on which birds were roasted is permitted, nevertheless ab initio they require libun. Moreover, here it speaks where it is known that chametz was roasted on it, whereas later it speaks only of a mere concern. See the Taz.
