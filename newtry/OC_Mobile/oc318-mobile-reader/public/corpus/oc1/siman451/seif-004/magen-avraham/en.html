And there are those who are lenient, etc. The Mordechai and Hagahos Maimoniyos wrote this regarding light libbun from milk to meat, as written in siman 509, seif 5. But regarding chametz they require full libbun, as written regarding a frying pan; see Beis Yosef. Only the Darchei Moshe wrote that according to Issur VeHeter, who rules that chametz is called a permitted absorption, even for chametz this sort of light libbun suffices. See what I wrote in siman 447, seif 5, that the Rashba holds it is called a prohibited absorption. If so, even after the fact one should forbid it if he did not kasher it with such libbun.
