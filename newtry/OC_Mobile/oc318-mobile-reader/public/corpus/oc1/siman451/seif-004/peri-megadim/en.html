(א) And if [Taz] Magen Avraham note 4 — burned bricks — libun specifically; and brick dried in the sun has the law of an earthenware vessel (and is permitted through hagalah); but if they heated it outside — even b'dieved forbidden as its use — kasher it as they swallow chametz inside even though they always heat outside; so in the Gemara and Levush; Boukia — they heated outside and forbidden in it; and metal vessels specifically — one fills coals inside; outside even if use is always thus and it whitens until sparks fly — forbidden b'dieved; so in Tur; see below seif and the same for metals that one kasher through hagalah if one inserts into a yorah until its rim and afterward that portion and the water did not enter inside — even b'dieved forbidden.

(ב) If they heated beer hot on pottery vessels outside that absorbed like k deposit and returned to our oven — even if we say per the Gemara's meaning Pesachim 4b that such is not impure like k deposit — Magen Avraham note 3 and see there; nevertheless it requires study b'dieved intentionally whether to forbid the cooked food on Pesach; l'chatchila the Rashba already wrote note 3 in the name of Ohr Zaru'a that they are not accustomed even to return to kilns; however b'dieved or in time of pressure one may be lenient via kilns if he has no other vessels.
