However in responsum Panim Me'irot part 1 siman 24 he disagreed; see there. And so Radbaz siman 739 — they did not permit retaining twelve months except regarding wine alone, not other prohibitions; see there. Eshel Avraham note 6 — and so in responsum Chaim Sha'al part 2 siman 38 — he wrote the words of Rashba in responsum siman 575 are not like Chakham Tzvi who wrote there that aging helps only for wine vessels; and he wrote an incident came to his hand in Israel that he was sent a gift of nice porcelain vessels and it was not discernible whether the gentile sender had already used them and two years passed — and they are expensive vessels; and he wrote that at first glance one should not permit l'chatchila as Chakham Tzvi himself wrote; and also from Rashba's words it appears even b'dieved it is forbidden; but here where there is a safek-safek perhaps they were not used — and if we say they were used, safek to be lenient — and still requires answer; until here. And it appears to me that if it is possible to kasher them in boiling water three times one should do so; and if they are vessels there is no way to use boiling — yad soledes bo — one may also be lenient.
