(א) Straw burns. Since chametz is on it, it is called prohibited absorption and hagalah does not suffice. And Taz challenged from the implication of Shulchan Aruch 447 seif 7 and 452 that it is called permitted absorption; see there. And furthermore what he wrote from a pan in seif 11 — it should read that here it deals where they roasted chametz b'eyna in it, which is like an oven in which they baked. And Chayei Adam struck with an axe that one cannot decide in this matter — for sometimes they ruled it as permitted absorption and sometimes etc.; see there.

(ב) Libun is required. And he challenged Yoreh De'ah siman 18 — behold we frequently use a trivet for meat and milk vessels. And one may answer: the concern is perhaps porridge spilled from the pot onto it b'eyna; from now on all year there is no concern — behold it is only peel-thickness and the pot remains in place; see there.
