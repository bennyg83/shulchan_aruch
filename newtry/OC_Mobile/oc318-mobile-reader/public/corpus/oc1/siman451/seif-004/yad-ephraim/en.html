Note 4 — heating does not help for it to be considered like a new vessel; but, etc. — it need not be like a new vessel — immediately, etc.; however in Maharil he wrote it is not called like kilns — meaning and if so internal heating also does not help except Exceptionally when fired two or three days — for the main elevation in returning to the kiln is because the flame rises to them from every side and they do not crack; but nevertheless it does not discharge kiln heat more than other internal heating; therefore if we say a kiln requires heating two or three days regarding discharging the prohibition from within — if so, even if one wants to kasher a pottery oven where lo pelug applies lest it crack, one needs also that it be fired two or three days to discharge the prohibition from within through internal heating; however at first glance it is possible per Rosh's words and so Shulchan Aruch here — the reason in returning to kilns: since they insert it for such great heating, certainly we are not concerned; if so one may say Maharil holds specifically with great heating of two or three days lo pelug is not applicable; but regarding discharge Maharil concedes stam heating suffices; and if so in a pottery oven where lo pelug is not applicable, stam internal heating suffices even if not fired two or three days; but per the language written here in Shulchan Aruch — since they insert for such great heating, certainly we are not concerned — if so one may say Maharil wrote specifically for this that it is not called like kilns except when fired two or three days; if the heating is not so great one may say properly we are concerned; but in a pottery oven where in any event lo pelug is not applicable — one may say Maharil concedes whenever heated from inside to the measure that it whitens, this suffices.
