Coated with glass — their law is like pottery vessels. See Mishna Berurah what he wrote that it is not comparable to seif 26 because it is coated on the pottery and fused with it in the kiln — its law is like pottery; so too the Rosh. However in Tosafot Avodah Zarah daf 33 they wrote to distinguish between whole glass vessels that do not absorb where it is only a coating on pottery — they brought thus from Arukh; accordingly it is not specifically because it was fused in the kiln. Gra in seif 26 brought only Tosafot's view, and so from his words in s.k. 86 it appears he explained Rama's words there regarding silver vessels with glass lining — it applies even per the Mechaber there who permits glass, and that case is different because it is only lining in general, similar to a handle; meaning there is no distinction between pottery and glass.
