(א) Seif 23 — fused with glass — as Tosafot explained Avodah Zarah daf 33b s.v. konya, that Rabbenu Tam challenged Rashi's explanation there and in chapter Kol Sha'ah regarding coating a limb — why is it worse than if it were entirely a limb where hagalah helps; and so Mordechai in chapter Kol Sha'ah; and they further challenged there that konya does not imply a limb as written in Zevachim siman 60 s.v. vikonya; see there in Tosafot Avodah Zarah; and Rambam and Tur explained like Rashi; and so Tosafot explained Avodah Zarah daf 75b s.v. vehalakhta; and so there in Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De'ah siman 120 seif 1 and there in siman 135 seif 6 he wrote both explanations; and see Tosafot Ketubot daf 107b s.v. hani etc.
