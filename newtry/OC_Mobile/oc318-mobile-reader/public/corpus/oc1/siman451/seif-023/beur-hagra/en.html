Se'if 23. In the melting of glass. Like the explanation of Tosafos in Avodah Zarah 33b s.v. "konya," where Rabbeinu Tam raised a difficulty on Rashi's explanation there and in the chapter "Kol Shaah" regarding a lead lining: is it less than if it were entirely lead, for which hagalah helps? And so wrote the Mordechai in the chapter "Kol Shaah." And they further asked there that "konya" does not imply lead, as is written in Zevachim 60: plaster and konya; and see there in Tosafos of Avodah Zarah. And the Rambam and Tur explained like Rashi; and so Tosafos explained in Avodah Zarah 75b s.v. "and the law is"; and so is written there in the Shulchan Aruch of Yoreh De'ah siman 120, se'if 1; and there in siman 135, se'if 6 he wrote both explanations; and see Tosafos of Ketubot 107b s.v. "these," etc.
And there are places. See Magen Avraham and Taz.
