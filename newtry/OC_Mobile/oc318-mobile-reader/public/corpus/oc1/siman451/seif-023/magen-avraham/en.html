(א) In glass coating. Possibly means Mordechai: coated in glass coating — permitted hagalah in metal; so Levush; must be coated actual glass; what is called glaze even glass coating — hagalah ineffective — no substance, only color — Rambam: vessels smeared/shiny like glass forbidden; Tosafot AZ 33; YD 120.

(ב) And some places. Because hold in hoops — return only to oven merely heated — not kilns unless 2-3 consecutive days (see s.k. 1). Responsa: even those receiving little tin from other pots forbidden; reason return kilns permitted — hoop tin burns — craftsman not concerned burn more; fully coated — concerned not ruin tin; Gaon: should forbid all year lest old vessels coated — permitted because perhaps not ben yomo — stricter Pesach; responsa: not recognizable absorption — concern non-Jew used; Darkei Moshe Mahariv: ask craftsmen how made — like first reason; now some places Jew stands over work; second reason — be stringent.

(ג) Even if new. Lest coated old or dyed — Maharil: do not use dyed vessels in saffron.
