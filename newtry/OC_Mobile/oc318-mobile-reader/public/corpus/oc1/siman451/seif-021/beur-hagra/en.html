(א) Seif 21 — barrels etc. permitted through hagalah or etc. — as written regarding yayin nesech in Avodah Zarah daf 33a, halacha lema'aseh, one fills them — etc.; and there daf 75a one who wants to purify them immediately hagels them in boiling water; and they said there daf 34a; and in chapter Kol Sha'ah there is mention of yayin nesech and chametz etc. — there its use etc.; if so, in chametz for something whose use is cold it is equivalent to yayin nesech; and even though hagalah does not help for pottery vessels except when its use is hot, as Rosh wrote in the mishnah there daf 44b regarding ne'ivah artachu in pottery that pouring does not help without peeling — therefore he commanded to hagal them for then peeling is not needed; and as written daf 33a that even pouring helps for pottery; and all the more hagalah as Rosh wrote Shabbat chapter 3 siman 11 that hagalah is better than pouring, for pouring does not help for something whose use is hot even in other vessels; and in Jerusalem Talmud end chapter 2 of Pesachim Rabbi Ami taught regarding cheese baskets, one fills them with water three days from erev Pesach; and all the more hagalah as above; and so Hagahot Oshr"i chapter 2 of Avoda Zara siman 105 s.v. betashmisho etc.

(ב) And b'dieved — there in Hagahot Oshr"i and see Magen Avraham in the name of Mordechai; and what Mordechai wrote: since permitted absorbs — etc., meaning as written in chapter 3 siman 477 that we estimate against the whole; and since sometimes it absorbs and sometimes emits one cannot stand on the matter — etc., meaning that even if it is known how much exits from it, nevertheless it returns and absorbs and what was absorbed becomes nevelah and returns and emits — not so in permitted that it becomes nevelah as Tosafot wrote Avodah Zarah daf 76a s.v. bat; but in a vessel of permitted — etc., and Beit Yosef wrote; and it was already explained that for most poskim chametz is not called permitted and therefore they omitted it in Shulchan Aruch; and likewise nitelaf we hold in siman 447 seif 10 that it is forbidden; and Magen Avraham; and see Tehudah siman 201.
