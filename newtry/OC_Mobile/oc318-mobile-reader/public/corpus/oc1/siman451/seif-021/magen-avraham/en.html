The Magen Avraham discusses the kashering of utensils for Pesach, according to their normal mode of use, with distinctions between hagalah, libbun, pouring from a kli rishon, soaking, cleaning away visible chametz, and after-the-fact cases. In particular, it addresses barrels used for beer, mead, wine, or spirits, spoons, bowls, and pans, strong spirits whose absorbed taste may be significant.
