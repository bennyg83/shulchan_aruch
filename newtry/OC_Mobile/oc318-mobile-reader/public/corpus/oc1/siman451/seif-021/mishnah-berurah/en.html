(ז) Wine or honey — see Magen Avraham and Kaf HaChayyim that there is a distinction between wine and honey: in wine, even if the barrel is ben yomo from absorption of lees — permitted b'dieved, for lees in wine gives taste for spoilage; but in honey-water it is not for spoilage unless the vessel was not ben yomo from absorption of lees; but if it was ben yomo and the honey-water stood in it above — even b'dieved forbidden to drink on Pesach. But several Acharonim agreed that even in honey-water it is permitted even if ben yomo, for the reason that pickled is not called such until it stood above, and then the absorption in the vessel becomes for spoilage and is permitted b'dieved. And know that in all aspects of this b'dieved permission it applies only when they poured the wine and honey from the barrel before Pesach and put it into a Pesach vessel — for the wine and honey were already permitted before Pesach, for before Pesach taste for spoilage is permitted; but if the wine and honey remained in the barrel until during Pesach — forbidden, for in our lands we practice to forbid taste for spoilage on Pesach, as above in siman 447 seif 10 in the gloss; and poskim wrote that if they put something sharp like vinegar in it, even if not ben yomo — forbidden, for through sharpness it improves taste.
