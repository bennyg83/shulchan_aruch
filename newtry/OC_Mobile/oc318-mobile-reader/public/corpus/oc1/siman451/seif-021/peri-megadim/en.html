Peel [Taz] — its use is via fire without liquid; and from the law libun; and even though the oven is scraped — it is literally via fire; and so an oven such as this; Magen Avraham note 21. And forbidden to kasher a new winnowing fork for the coming Pesach lest they err etc.; and forbidden even b'dieved a winnowing basket via hagalah; it appears for a mortar in note 19 — there perhaps lest they err b'dieved permitted as in note 19. And a Pesach mixing tub — if they are stringent there is not so much concern because also in mixing chametz permitted from the law in hagalah; see siman 1; and nevertheless why should we do l'chatchila an act unnecessary and they will come to err. And Peri Chadash note 39 he was lenient b'dieved if they kashered a winnowing basket of chametz and removed matzah — because some say chametz is called permitted; and some say winnowing basket since it emits immediately it is not via fire; and such is Magen Avraham note 37 in the name of Darkei Moshe in the name of Mahari Weil — they kasher a Pesach winnowing basket for the next year; and this is because b'dieved even in a chametz winnowing basket if one kashered — the matzah is not forbidden; see Magen Avraham note 37 further.
