[1] And one hides them, etc. — Beit Chadash quarreled with those who place iron chametz vessels at height for decoration, and likewise Pesach vessels or Kutim vessels pawned the rest of the year; see there. And in Eliyahu Zuta I upheld the custom to be lenient, and all the Acharonim agreed; and it is not comparable to a candle two stories high, for there it is a d'oraisa prohibition — which is not so here, for stam vessels are not ben yomo; furthermore regarding eating we are more careful, as Tosafot Chullin wrote regarding the incident of R' Pinchas ben Yair; they also place markers on vessels permitted on Pesach so they will not err.
