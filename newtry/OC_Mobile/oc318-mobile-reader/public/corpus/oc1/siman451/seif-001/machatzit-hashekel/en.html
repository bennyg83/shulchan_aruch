(א) (s.k. 1) And permitted, etc. Some want proof from Tosafot Pesachim 74a: Rav — chametz pots on Pesach break; Shmuel — leave until after Pesach, cook in it not in kind and not not in kind; Gemara challenges Rav — R' Yehuda chametz after Pesach passed forbidden Torah, not in kind in amount — why break until after Pesach and cook not in kind? Answer: decree lest cook in kind. Tosafot s.v. leave them: challenge — Rav: chametz taste in pot considered something — not customary use much together — nevertheless we learn do not nullify issur l'chatchila; R' Yosef: here if Rav holds nitelaf permitted (safeik AZ whether Rav permits nitelaf) — though nitelaf l'chatchila forbidden — two leniencies nitelaf and not in kind in such case nullify l'chatchila; R' Yitzchak: pottery no remedy — considered b'dieved; pottery not ben yomo nitelaf forbidden l'chatchila though no remedy — here only amount — b'dieved. Therefore want proof for us who hold Shmuel — also not in kind nullified, no decree — still these pots only absorbed amount — because do not nullify issur l'chatchila like one Tosafot view; but absorbed much — forbidden do not nullify, etc.

(ב) Per Rav who ruled R' Yehuda — chametz after Pesach passed forbidden Torah; we hold Shmuel and R' Shimon — not forbidden except fine for violating bal yera'eh. Not so these pots — permitted to leave as head s.k. 7 — even in mixtures forbidden to leave — if left, since no bal yera'eh violation — nullified in sixty — all the more pots — therefore permitted even if absorbed — permitted cook l'chatchila — no nullifying issur — no issur at all.
