Pottery pots, etc. One may not place anything on a winter-house oven because it is a pottery vessel that never leaves its absorbed state; and likewise one may not heat with kakalin during the days of Pesach for the same reason (from R' Isserles in derashot); and b'dieved some forbid even if one returns and coats fresh clay on Pesach to the thickness of a finger (my teacher in Bach in the name of my elder relative the gaon Maharar Hirsch Shor); and some permit b'dieved (Maharam of Lublin in his responsum siman 106); but pottery vessels lined with molten lead are permitted through hagalah like other metal vessels (Beit Yosef and Mordechai and Maharam Isserles); and a metal pot — if one wishes to fill it with coals from inside — is permitted (Tur and Maharam Isserles):
