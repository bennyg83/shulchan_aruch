Seif 1 — pottery pots used for chametz all year etc. In my humble opinion see Taz s.k. 2 — his words contradict the Mechaber's reason for returning to kilns; and see what I wrote on this in my work on Yoreh De'ah laws of kashering vessels siman 121, on Yoreh De'ah edition 6 siman 23; see there and examine. See Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot s.k. 2 who wrote that if one transgressed and whitened a pottery vessel and cooked in it, b'dieved the dish is permitted in inadvertence; in intentional sin he left undecided. See my work on Orach Chayim year 515 daf 83a in responsum to the community of Batishan — I wrote the opposite that in every case it is forbidden; see his reason and examine.
