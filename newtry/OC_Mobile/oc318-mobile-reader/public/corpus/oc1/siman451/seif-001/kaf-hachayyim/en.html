1) [Se'if 1] Earthenware pots, etc. This does not apply only to pots. The same law applies to bowls used by pouring from another vessel and to utensils used as a second vessel. Chok Yaakov, subsection 1; Mekor Chaim, subsection 1. In other words, although they are not used directly over fire like pots, they must likewise be scrubbed and put away.
