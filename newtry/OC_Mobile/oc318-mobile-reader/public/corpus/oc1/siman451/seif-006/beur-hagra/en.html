Se'if 6. Every vessel, etc. As above from that of cups, etc.
And some are stringent, etc.; and likewise, etc.; and some, etc. Tosafos of Avodah Zarah there 74b s.v. "he taught," who hold specifically where it is known that they were used only cold; and so is this view; and in Shabbos 42b at the end of the discussion s.v. "but"; and in Zevachim 96a.
And bowls, etc. For the reason that pouring is not like a kli rishon is that it cools, as is written in Pesachim 76a: "as it cools for him," etc.; which is not so here.
