To perform hagalah on the bowls. The Taz explains the kashering of utensils for Pesach, according to their normal mode of use, with distinctions between hagalah, libbun, pouring from a kli rishon, soaking, cleaning away visible chametz, and after-the-fact cases. He clarifies the underlying sugyos and when the stringency is required by law or only as the preferred practice. In particular, it addresses spoons, bowls, and pans.
And he should pass the stone, etc. Even though in some place the stone does not touch, the boiling water reaches there, and it is permitted, as written in seif 16.
