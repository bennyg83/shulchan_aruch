In Magen Avraham note 13 — such as bowls, Beit Yosef, etc. — this is Rabbenu Tam, Beit Yosef — meaning Beit Yosef brought in the name of Rif and Rosh regarding their use in kli sheini, such as bowls, etc.; and what Magen Avraham wrote "and not bowls literally," etc. — meaning Shulchan Aruch here that wrote their use in kli sheini does not mean bowls literally as is the explanation in Rif and Rambam's words; rather here he means bowls are called kli sheini — and if one inserts a spoon, etc.
