And large bowls, etc. And specifically when he cannot insert them into a kli rishon; but one may not do so at all with a bowl or any item that can be inserted into a kli rishon (Maharam Isserles). And large bowls — one pours boiling water specifically from the vessel so that it becomes kli rishon, and one should not draw with another vessel from a vessel containing boiling water unless one left the vessel used for drawing inside the kli rishon (Maharil). And it appears per our custom that we pour boiling water onto the stones — then the water returns to boiling and then even hot water suffices (Maharikh likewise). And one should not kasher a drinking vessel together with other vessels lest they absorb from the hagalah water; and likewise when kashering before six (Maharam Isserles).
