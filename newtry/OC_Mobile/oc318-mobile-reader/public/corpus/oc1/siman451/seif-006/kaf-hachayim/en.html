(6) There — even those in which they make porridge, etc. — meaning then they absorbed much chametz; and if one cooks in them after Pesach there will not be sixty — nevertheless it is permitted; and the reason: we are lenient like Rashi who holds chametz after Pesach is not forbidden except due to a mere penalty, and he is penalized only when the chametz exists b'eyna; but what was absorbed in the vessel he is not penalized — therefore it is permitted even l'chatchila to cook in them. Orach Chayyim note 1, Magen Avraham note 1.
