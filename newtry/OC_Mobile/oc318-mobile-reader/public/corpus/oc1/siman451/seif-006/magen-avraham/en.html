For sometimes they use them, etc. In addition, pieces of bread are soaked in them for 24 hours, and something pickled for 24 hours is treated as cooked. [Hagahot Maimoniyot] A heated stone. Bach writes that this does not have the status of an actual kli rishon and is effective only for jugs and cups. One should be stringent with bowls that have rims and protrusions like knobs and flowers, because it is impossible to pass the heated stone over every part of them. Such bowls therefore have no remedy except to insert them into a boiling-water vessel.
