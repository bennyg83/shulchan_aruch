27) There, gloss. It is forbidden to use it even with cold food, etc. This refers to regular use, lest one come to use it with hot food. Therefore, regular use is forbidden even if one first rinsed it. Temporary use, however, is permitted after rinsing, contrary to Olas Shabbos. Regular use is forbidden even on the eve of Pesach after chametz becomes prohibited. Chok Yaakov, subsection 11; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 4; Mekor Chaim, subsection 8; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 2. Regarding old earthenware utensils, however, the custom is not to use them even with cold food and even temporarily, as stated below at the end of se'if 22. See there.
