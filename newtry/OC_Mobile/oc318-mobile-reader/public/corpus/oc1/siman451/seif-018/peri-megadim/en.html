(א) For it absorbs [Taz] — behold in Talui there are four concerns. (1) Even if made new — way to soften little by little and enters in the eye beneath it and cannot clean and hagalah does not help for adhesion in the eye. (2) Perhaps before placing the talui there was chametz in the eye; and therefore when cleaned and all the more new — no concern. (3) They do not say such as kli rishon — for when used not in this way it was used; and now talui interrupts when made old. (4) And in note 23 Peri Chadash challenged on this — it appears similar to a vessel used via fire and kashered and used in boiling water — we do not say from a half it emits bit by bit — the same here; and if one would say between them hagalah emits even thick how much — as appears from Rashba's responsum — behold Boukia heated outside and does not help etc.; and a vessel that one inserts into a yorah until its rim and water did not enter inside — simple forbidden — the same here. And see Rosh Pesachim 30b and responsum general rule 14 — it implies thus it emits bit by bit; and Peri Chadash note 8 — hagalah does not help (see there fat in hot water nat bar nat for benefit and proof from gentile's spoon and Peri Chadash note 14 note 11 and there we wrote in manuscript) and so in Tur — concerned to absorb not in the eye; and Magen Avraham wrote that there was a mitzvah to whiten the talui — meaning old talui absorbed from chametz — libun or hagalah as its use kasher it; and not enough that one kasher the talui afterward with the vessel on the eve from this concern; understand; and Bach brought Rashba's words in responsum siman 471; and there is to explain talui new; and so from words of the questioner there place of doubt is place of pressure; and similarly he equated cracks — see there well; and so he answered Rashba z"l — certainly hagalah emits even the vessel very thick all made new — only the concern lest there is in the eye that entered afterward when the talui softened; and therefore even new requires completion and a spoon suffices to burn in the eye the measure beneath the talui (unlike old requires libun of the body of the vessel more in place of talui — understand); and nevertheless note 5 appears he explains before absorption of issur to talui — it requires study; and it appears the concern is because of adhesion in the eye and if so cleaning suffices.

(ב) But Mordechai wrote explicitly siman 478 — it implies he holds like kli rishon certainly we say even before absorption of issur to talui from a half; and if cleaned in the eye it is fine when it is strong like a nail and similarly; except here in talui the way is to soften — requires hagalah before fixing the talui — otherwise perhaps at time of hagalah it was attached and hagalah water did not enter beneath the talui; and on Pesach it softened and emits from what was absorbed in the wall opposite the talui — l"sh now from a half; and so Peri Chadash. And Mordechai this that there is to permit from this reason — it is an alternate view — not as Magen Avraham wrote. And therefore Taz challenged — talui new there is concern perhaps it softened and entered in the eye and hagalah cannot help for adhesion in the eye; and missing the word "except" and it must be said this does not deal except when attached very much; and he explains Rashba's words even when made new. And on Tur he challenged — why does it help — perhaps it softened and it is like a crack that there is concern for adhesion in the eye; and it appears it deals with attached strongly; Peri Chadash.

(ג) The fourth reason in talui he brought in note 50 and so note 23 — even though they say from a half there is concern perhaps afterward it spreads outside the talui. And for the law — every new l'chatchila one must libun place of talui per Taz; and b'dieved one may be lenient. And in the prior absorption even if cleaned the adhesion — forbidden b'dieved because l"sh like kli rishon and from a half as in the third reason; see 121 Taz note 5. And until the night talui old if there are sixty against place of talui — permitted because the absorbed in the wall is not l"sh; see Peri Chadash note 94. And above in a place without gravy requires sixty times seif 1 slightly less against place of talui Peri Chadash note 24 note 23 and all this when talui inside unlike outside and they do not draw with it from a yorah and similarly — then suffices kashering the talui alone beforehand and not the vessel; and so Peri Chadash; and see Magen Avraham note 27 further.
