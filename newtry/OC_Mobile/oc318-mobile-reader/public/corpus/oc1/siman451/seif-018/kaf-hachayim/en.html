(18) There [gloss]. And some forbid even in kli sheini. Meaning: even if he did not pour from the pot into it, only poured from the pot into kli sheini and afterward placed this vessel into it. Magen Avraham note 3. Mekor Chayyim note 6 — but if he poured from bowl to pot, which is pouring of kli sheini — according to everyone it is permitted. Eliyah Rabbah note 5.
