The sieve. The Magen Avraham discusses the kashering of utensils for Pesach, according to their normal mode of use, with distinctions between hagalah, libbun, pouring from a kli rishon, soaking, cleaning away visible chametz, and after-the-fact cases. In particular, it addresses sieves, baskets, sacks, and cloths where crumbs may remain.
