Knives — if the sheath has crevices, or if it has nails in the sheath, hagalah does not help for them; and such is the practice and one should not change. Behold, there are three levels regarding a knife: (1) by law — hagalah in boiling water; above that — to whiten the iron in fire; and the sheath in boiling water if it is not of wood or deer horn; above that — entirely new; and the author mentioned only the higher level. One must remove all rust — that is, what we call rost — and some say that rust itself does not help with hagalah; but sometimes when there are black or red spots in some place and there is no substance there, only appearance alone, one may kasher it after scraping all that is possible; nevertheless it is better to whiten that place.
