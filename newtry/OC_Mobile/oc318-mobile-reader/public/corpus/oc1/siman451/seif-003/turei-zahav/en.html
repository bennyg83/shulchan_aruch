(א) Knives — kasher in hagalah, etc. Gemara: Rav Huna to Rava — Pesach knives how? New ones. If possible for you, not for everyone? Like new — handle in clay, iron in fire, then immerse in boiling — halakha both in boiling kli rishon. Why Pesach specifically? Why not all year kashering? Why "knives in Pesach"? Why "for me" more than others? Why "if possible for you" — law depends on possible? It was obvious boiling helps knives — Ravina stringent more than other vessels — frequent chametz/matza use, hidden cracks — proper extra caution beyond law — "how do we do" — counsel; "for me new" — I am stringent, not relying on minimum law; "if not possible" — counsel for impossible — like new: iron in fire; Gemara concludes both in boiling — libun in fire for iron is piety; piyyut clear: knife used for chametz for guests — if new, praiseworthy; if not, kasher in boiling — legally boiling enough, new knives better.

(ב) All rust. "Only gold and silver" — must remove rust — what we call rust has substance from sheltered place — when scraped like dust; concern chametz substance under rust — hagalah not for in existence; sometimes black/red stain on vessel especially pots — no substance, only appearance — may kasher after scraping all possible; else one city cannot kasher; therefore if crevices in knife — seif concludes; without crevices only if cannot remove black — no concern — hagalah after scrape; still whiten that place — advice.

(ג) Requires libun, etc. Libun need not remove rust — fire burns all — YD 121.
