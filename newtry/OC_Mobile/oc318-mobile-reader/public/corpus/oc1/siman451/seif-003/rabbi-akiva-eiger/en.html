Per what is written in seif 6 in the gloss "there is not" — in my humble opinion: behold the foundation of the dispute in seif 6 is whether we follow majority use; and likewise the dispute in seif 4 regarding use through fire whether libun is required — meaning in the Gemara's law regarding knives, the halachah is in boiling water; even though sometimes they use them through fire — meaning since chametz is permitted absorption, even use through fire suffices with hagalah, as is brought in the last chapter of Avodah Zarah regarding notar; if so, there is no need to say we follow majority use; and this is the Mechaber's view in seif 4 and seif 6; and the other views hold chametz is called by its name upon it and is called prohibited absorption, and use through fire requires libun; and the reason for knives is we follow majority use; if so, one of them is proven — either we follow majority use or chametz is called permitted absorption; and in the safek of the law between these two views we hold for chametz that it is called prohibited absorption and also that we do not follow majority use; and this is Rama's ruling in seif 4 and seif 6; and if so, here with knives it is permitted through hagalah.
