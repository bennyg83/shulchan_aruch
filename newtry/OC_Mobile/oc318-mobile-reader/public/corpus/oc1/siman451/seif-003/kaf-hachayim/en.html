(3) There — that they were used with chametz, etc. But new vessels bought from the market — even pottery vessels — one need not be concerned lest perhaps they were used, for there is no general way to use new vessels. But metal vessels bought from a gentile in his home, and one does not know them — that they are certainly new — require concern to kasher or whiten them per their use. Orach Chayyim general rule 58 siman 46. Chayei Adam note 2, Kaf HaChayyim note 2 — and after hagalah or libun one must immerse them, even though they are old, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 121 seif 1; see there. And furthermore see there siman 122 seif 11. Nevertheless with clear glass vessels one may permit buying from a gentile from his home even if the gentile does not speak offhand and takes the vessel from among the vessels — for since they are clear, certainly they were not yet used. R' Zalman note 5.
