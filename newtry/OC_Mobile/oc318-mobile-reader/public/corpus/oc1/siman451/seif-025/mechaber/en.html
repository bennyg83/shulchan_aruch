All things used for drinking, whether flasks or cups, are permitted after they are washed, whether they are glass, wooden, metal, or ceramic. Even though periodically hot bread is placed in them, because they are usually used for cold things, washing is sufficient, because for every object we follow its usual use. {Rama: Rem"a: However, there are those who are stringent and require scouring, and such is the practice (see above in paragraph 6).}
