In other words, although the utensils would become thoroughly white-hot even in our ovens if left there for a long time, one might not leave them long enough. In a kiln, however, they become white-hot quickly. Taz, subsection 2. Ma'amar Mordechai, subsection 5, explains that because a kiln is intensely heated, once the utensils do not crack immediately upon being inserted, there is no further concern for them.
