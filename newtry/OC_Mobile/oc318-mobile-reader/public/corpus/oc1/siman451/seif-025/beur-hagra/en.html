Se'if 25. Every drinking vessel, whether, etc. Avodah Zarah 75b.
Whether they are, etc. In the manner of not only this but also that.
Of glass. See se'if 26.
Whether they are of wood or, etc. For the aforementioned baraita deals with metal; and wood has the law of metal, as above in se'if 8.
Whether they are of earthenware. As above in se'if 22: that even of earthenware, if one used cold, it is permitted to use even hot.
And even though. As above in se'if 3 and se'if 6.
However, etc. As above in se'if 6 in the gloss; and so wrote the Mordechai, siman 844, because sometimes they boil in them, etc.
