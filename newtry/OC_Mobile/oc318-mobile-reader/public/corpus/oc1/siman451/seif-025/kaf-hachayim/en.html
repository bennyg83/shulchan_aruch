(25) There. But for our ovens — no; for there is room to be concerned lest one is worried about them and removes them from the oven before they whiten. Tur in the name of the Rosh — and he means that although in our ovens too they whiten well if left there a long time, nevertheless there is concern lest one not leave them long — unlike a kiln where they whiten even in a short time. Taz note 2. And Maamar Mordechai note 5 wrote the reason: in a kiln, since one heated greatly, as long as they did not crack immediately when placed inside, there is no further concern.
