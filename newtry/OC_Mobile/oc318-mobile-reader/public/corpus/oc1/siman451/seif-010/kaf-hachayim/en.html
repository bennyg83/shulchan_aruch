(10) There — and one hides them on Pesach, etc. And recently they began placing tin chametz vessels in the winter-house for a gentile, and they say there is no concern lest he use them since they are at height; and likewise Pesach vessels they stand at height all year — this is a complete error; and one must guard them in a place where there is no usual habit lest they err to use them on Pesach. Bach. Shach Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur note 1; see there note 1 — and possibly they practiced thus because they are not ben yomo; and even if the master uses them, the majority permit, as written siman 447 seif 10; and all the more so in those whose majority use is in kli sheini or cold, such as flasks — there is no concern; and one who is stringent — a blessing upon him. Magen Avraham note 2. However Chayei Adam note 7 and Eshel Avraham note 1 — to permit simply; see there. But Peri Chadash wrote it is good to be concerned for Bach's reasoning; and so Birkei Yosef note 2; and so Chok Yosef note 2 like Magen Avraham — one who is stringent, a blessing upon him. And so Mekor Chayyim note 5, R' Zalman note 4.
