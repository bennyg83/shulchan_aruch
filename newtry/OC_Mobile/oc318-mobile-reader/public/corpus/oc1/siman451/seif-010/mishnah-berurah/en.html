Although the gloss below in seif 18 states that hagalah is ineffective for such baskets, there the baskets are used with chametz throughout the year and crumbs commonly lodge between the reeds. Here that concern does not apply. This is only initially. After the fact, even if they were not kashered and meat was salted in them on Pesach, the meat may be eaten, even if it is known that chametz taste had entered the baskets through salting, because salt does not draw out taste absorbed within a utensil.
