The cited ruling. The Taz explains the kashering of utensils for Pesach, according to their normal mode of use, with distinctions between hagalah, libbun, pouring from a kli rishon, soaking, cleaning away visible chametz, and after-the-fact cases. He clarifies the underlying sugyos and when the stringency is required by law or only as the preferred practice. In particular, it addresses mortars used with sharp spices and possible chametz crumbs, glass or coated vessels, strong spirits whose absorbed taste may be significant.
