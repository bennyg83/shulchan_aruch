And the winter-house oven — some permit b'dieved (She'eilot uTeshuvot R' Moshe of Lublin siman 106); some are stringent even if one returns and coats fresh clay the thickness of a finger. It appears that if one does not use matzah there but only a Pesach pot, it is obvious there is no prohibition b'dieved even in a place where all year they are accustomed to place hot chametz pots there — not as my teacher wrote in Beit Chadash in the name of Maharash Hirsch in siman 447 note 1. It appears there is no prohibition at all in any manner b'dieved even for eating, unless he left matzah there as it is without a pot while it is boiling — then one must forbid b'dieved in a place where they are accustomed each year to place chametz there, as above; it is simple as written in siman 447 seif 1 that there is no extra stringency in a chametz mixture over other prohibited mixtures except regarding mashahu — there is no prohibition in two pots, one forbidden and one permitted, that touched each other, as Rama wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 92 seif 8 and see there; and in responsum of R' Moshe of Lublin. Beer barrels of wood — hagalah does not help to kasher at all; and so ruled Maharshal and Maharash of Lublin and Mohar Hirsch Shor (my teacher in Beit Chadash). Likewise a barrel of burnt wine made from grain — it is obvious hagalah does not help (Maharam Isserles); but honey-water barrels may be permitted through hagalah.
