Permitted [Taz] — he proved Yoreh De'ah 105 — a vessel of issur and permitted remained on it from above — does not forbid when did it absorb — after from above nitelaf it is; and this is the essence of the permission here in an earthenware barrel — hagalah does not help in holding beer many days — except from reason of nitelaf also; Magen Avraham siman 462 note 16. And Magen Avraham here note 40 and in Shach note 14 note 127 — he extended that all pickled issur from above absorbs and emits in whole; in other vessels hagalah helps — not pouring and we do not say like kli rishon in this; and in kli sheini hagalah does not help; and wine is different — see there; and the essence of permission is nat bar nat and nitelaf; and if they put vinegar in it — forbidden; and will be explained further below in this.
