See Baer Heitev; and see in responsum Maharshadam part 1 siman 21 regarding yufkash (see Peri Chadash — they scatter flour on the griddle and it is only a little and in a moment the dough is baked) — one who guards his soul should distance from it; and Peri Chadash permits; and in Shemesh Tzedakah siman 9 he wrote it is forbidden to throw when they roll the dough to make it thin, to bake it in the oven and cook it afterward; and it is more stringent than what Shulchan Aruch wrote regarding adding flour to the dough; see there the gloss of the Mechaber's son that b'dieved it is permitted.
