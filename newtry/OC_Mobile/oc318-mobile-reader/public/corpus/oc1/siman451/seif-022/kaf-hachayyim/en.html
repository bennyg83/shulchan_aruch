Lest one be protective of them, etc. Bach writes that earthenware ovens and stoves whose prohibited absorption came through fire cannot be kashered even by internal heating, and this is the ruling to follow. See there. This also appears to be the opinion of Peri Chadash in se'if 2. Magen Avraham, subsection 4; Chok Yaakov, subsection 9; and Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 5, however, challenge him. They write that internal heating is effective because these utensils are ordinarily used this way, so there is no concern that one will be protective of them lest they crack. See there. Beis Hillel at the beginning of siman 461 writes likewise. Chok Yosef, subsection 4, agrees, contrary to Bach. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 8, and Chayei Adam, rule 125, subsection 2, rule likewise. See below, subsection 32, and at the beginning of siman 461.
