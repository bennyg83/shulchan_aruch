The oven. The consensus of later authorities is that after the fact the cooked dish cooked inside it is permitted (for it is like two pots touching one another: Yoreh De'ah siman 93; and in any case scrape it well and plaster it; otherwise even after the fact it is forbidden; but the Levush permits, and Chok Yaakov and all later authorities forbade). And likewise if it is a time of need when another way is impossible, it is like after the fact; and nevertheless at least scrape the oven well on all sides and heat it well, and afterward plaster with clay the place where the pot sits, the thickness of a finger. (And regarding the space of the oven inside, see siman 461.) Except if one placed matzah there or other things as they are without a pot while hot: then one should forbid even after the fact; see Magen Avraham and Taz and Chok Yaakov.
