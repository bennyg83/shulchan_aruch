And the charoset house — see Yoreh De'ah siman 69 that in any event chametz was not used in it. And even if we say since its vinegar is sharp it absorbs even when cold — nevertheless one may say not to use them hot. One may rely and permit without rinsing first — go and learn from now: certainly if chametz hot was used in it, rinsing is required to use cold matzah in it. And yayin nesech vessels require rinsing because they are very sharp. If so, due to the stringency of yayin nesech — see responsum Bach Beit Moed siman 18.
