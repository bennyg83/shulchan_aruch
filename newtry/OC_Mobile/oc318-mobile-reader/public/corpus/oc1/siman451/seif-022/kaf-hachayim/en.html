(22) There — lest one be concerned for them, etc. Bach wrote: an oven and stove of pottery whose prohibition comes through heating — even internal heating does not help for them — and so we rule; see there. And so is Peri Chadash's view in seif 2. However Magen Avraham note 4 and Chayei Adam note 9 and Eshel Avraham note 5 challenged him; and they wrote heating from inside helps — for since such is their way we are not concerned lest they crack; see there. And so Beit Meir siman 461; and so Chok Yosef note 4 agreed — not like Bach. And so R' Zalman note 8, Chayei Adam general rule 125 note 2; and see below note 32 and siman 461.
