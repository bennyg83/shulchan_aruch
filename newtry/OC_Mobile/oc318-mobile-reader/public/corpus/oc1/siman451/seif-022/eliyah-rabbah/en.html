[22] And pass the stone, etc. And even if in some place the stone did not touch, the boiling water reaches there and it is permitted (Taz). But Magen Avraham wrote: bowls that have ridges and protrusions like buttons and flowers, such that one cannot pass the stone over the face of all of them, have no remedy other than to put them into a cauldron; end quote. And perhaps he deals with a case where the water too will not pass there.
