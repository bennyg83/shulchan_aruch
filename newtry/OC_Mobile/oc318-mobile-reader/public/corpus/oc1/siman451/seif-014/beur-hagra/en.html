Se'if 14. A cover, etc., that one covers. As is written in chapter 2 of Makhshirin: a bathhouse that is tamei, its vapor is tamei; and a tahor [bathhouse], with "ki yutan"; the pool that is in the house, etc.; and we learn prohibition from tumah, as above in se'if 8.
And if one placed it. There at the end of chapter 5: if hands were tamei and one was stirring the pot that is tahor: if the hands made vapor, the pot is tamei.
