[19] And large bowls, etc. — Bach's view: specifically bowls known not to have been used except via pouring; but if there is concern they were sometimes used also in kli rishon — a white-hot stone does not help, for that is only via pouring — until here. However in Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 5 it appears somewhat that it is like kli rishon; and so is explained in Mahari Weil whom I will bring in note 39; and one may be lenient with all bowls — not with something whose majority use is in kli rishon; and so is explained in Maharil.
