(19) In the gloss there — and there are those who forbid even in kli sheini. It is proven from here that one holds that even in kli sheini it absorbs something; and so is proven below seif 5; see there note 1 — meaning from what is written below seif 5: and if their use is in kli sheini — kasher them in kli sheini; and if it does not absorb at all — why does it need kashering; rather one must say that in any event it absorbs something — therefore kashering is required.
