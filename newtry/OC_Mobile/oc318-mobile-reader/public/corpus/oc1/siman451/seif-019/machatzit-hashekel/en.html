(s.k. 19) Lest, etc. Of horn, etc. And Eliyah Rabbah wrote in the name of Malbushei Yom Tov: what we call mother-of-pearl, which is very strong and hard, is not comparable to horn which melts from heat; end quote. And the Taz wrote in s.k. 16 regarding rust that holds back: specifically that which has substance; but mere blackness or redness, and likewise what is called Erd Flecken, one need not be concerned for them; and so wrote Chok Yaakov.
