Not effective, etc. Because issur absorbs via fire itself: Maharam Rotenburg responsa 177 permitted matzah on peel used all year not scraped, brought to oven — peel bottom; may be so for matzah; wheat in salted meat — all forbidden. Levush permits peeling; even real chametz on matzah — remove place as siman 461 — not like Rashal who forbids siman 447:1. If regularly bake with fat — forbids all if not clean; fatty substance forbids all; if washed well — peeling suffices — vessel absorption we are not stringent whole vessel YD 104:5; all the more not ben yomo siman 447:10; YD 94:8. Terumat HaDeshen: even peeling and hagalah not effective on peel; Darkei Moshe Mahariv: peel threshed last year needs hagalah; Hagahot Maimoniyot: sour matzah threshed — forbidden to thresh more — bring another — Shelah; Epi Ravrevei: sour removed, mixed with others — nullified — logic absorbed issur does not leave without gravy — only first bread in existence; if mixed nullified — Or U'Mussar 39; we hold YD 105:7 vessel expels without gravy — Or U'Mussar; difference: first matzah all forbidden if grease concern; without grease — remove amount; others only peel after first cleaned; if mixed — nullified not davar shebeminyan — not issur from itself, only by mouth; Pesach not nullified siman 447:9 — Acharonim. All this: matzah became full chametz; doubled puffed — stringency siman 461 — forbid peel l'chatchila not other matzot — Hagahot Maimoniyot, Shelah.
