A paddle. And in Ashkenazic vernacular, a Schwibel; and its use is by means of fire; therefore they did not permit it by hagalah. The Taz wrote that if one keeps this Pesach paddle for another year, it needs no kashering at all; and likewise a trough in which one kneads matzos; and one who is stringent to hagal it did not do well, lest one come to err and hagal a chametz paddle; rather scrape it well; end of his words. And Magen Avraham in the name of Darkhei Moshe in the name of Maharik wrote that it needs hagalah; see there. And Chok Yaakov agreed with the Taz. And if one took matzah with a paddle used all year: if it is clean, peeling the matzah suffices. And if it is not clean, since all year one uses upon it cakes with fat, its entirety is forbidden; see Magen Avraham. If one removed with it a leavened matzah, it is forbidden to remove with it further; rather one must bring another: Hagahot Maimoniyot and Shenei Luchos HaBeris. And if one did not bring another, all of them need peeling. And all this is dealing with a matzah that became fully leavened chametz. But a doubled or swollen matzah, since that itself is a stringency, as is written at the end of siman 461, is enough to forbid the paddle l'chatchila, but not to forbid the other matzos: Magen Avraham. And Chok Yaakov wrote: and greater than this I saw by my paternal grandfather, the Maharam Shick, who did not forbid the paddle when a swollen or doubled matzah remained before him, for the reason that it is considered like after the fact, for in most bakings one will not escape that one of them is forbidden because of swollen or doubled, and each time a new paddle would be required, and the oven too would be forbidden, and one would need to heat again; and the Torah is considerate of the money of Israel; see there. And see siman 461, se'if 3.
Hagalah. And it is written in Terumat HaDeshen that even peeling and hagalah do not help for a paddle.
New. The author of Imrei Noam wrote in a responsum, siman 5, a case that occurred, that they baked about a log of matzos with a paddle and were in doubt whether it was new or old used with chametz: there is no concern of a chametz prohibition in these matzos; see there.
