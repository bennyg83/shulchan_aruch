Se'if 17. The board, etc.; and likewise. From what is written there: those basins of Mechoza, etc.; and specifically where one always kneads in it, as is written in the Gemara there; and so wrote Rabbeinu Yeruham and the Mordechai; only that the Mordechai wrote that one should not be lenient, since they already practiced, because of things that are permitted.
And it does not help. As Tosafos wrote: a thing used in a kli rishon, pouring does not help, for it expels only enough for a peeling; and as above in se'if 5; and see Tosafos of Avodah Zarah 74b at the end of the discussion s.v. "he taught."
And the custom. See the Taz; and so wrote Beis Yosef in the name of the Kol Bo; and learn from a knife that has grooves, as above in se'if 3.
