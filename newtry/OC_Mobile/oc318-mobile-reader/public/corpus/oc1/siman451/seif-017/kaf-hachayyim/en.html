Beis Yosef explains that even if an earthenware utensil was used with hot food only as a second vessel, heating does not kasher it. He writes that Rambam in chapter 5 likewise equates an earthenware utensil used for chametz as a second vessel with one used as a first vessel. Rema in Darkei Moshe, subsection 1, however, writes that Beis Yosef's explanation does not appear correct. According to Tur, a second vessel can be kashered because it does not absorb. In the name of Hagahos Maimoniyos, however, he writes that even if an earthenware utensil was used only as a second vessel, heating does not kasher it. See there. Rema therefore comments on the Shulchan Aruch's words, which are the language of Tur, that some prohibit even a second vessel. He means that although Tur's language implies that only a utensil used by pouring from a first vessel cannot be kashered, while one used as a second vessel can be, some maintain that even a second vessel cannot be kashered by heating. Chok Yaakov, subsection 8, and Ma'amar Mordechai, subsection 3, write likewise. The practical ruling will be explained below in subsection 20.
