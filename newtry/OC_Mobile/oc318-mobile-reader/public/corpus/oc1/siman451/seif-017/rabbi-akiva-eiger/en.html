Magen Avraham s.k. 44. Since there is no gravy there. This is puzzling, since there is chametz in kind in the place where the pot stands: it is opposite the gravy and permeates inward, and we require sixty; and consequently with chametz it is forbidden in a mashehu. And if one set a hot pot on something cold, since the lower overpowers, a peeling suffices, and the pot is in place of the peeling; but here everything is boiling, and this requires further study.
