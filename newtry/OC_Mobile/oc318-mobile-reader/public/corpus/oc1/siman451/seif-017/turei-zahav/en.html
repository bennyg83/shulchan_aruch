Even by hagalah. The reason, in the Beis Yosef, is that it is impossible to scrape and clean them very well so that no bit of chametz remains in some crack that is not visible to the eye. For this reason people are careful not to perform hagalah for Pesach on utensils in which flour was kept, or barrels in which a beverage made in the lands of Russia from bran water, called borsht, was kept, lest actual chametz from the bran remained in it. But if before Pesach they had held water there for some time, there is no concern in performing hagalah, for it is no worse than old sacks, regarding which the Rama writes nearby that sewing and laundering help, as seems to me.
