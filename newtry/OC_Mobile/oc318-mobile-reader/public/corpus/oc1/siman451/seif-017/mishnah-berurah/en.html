(ג) Not to use on Pesach etc. — and the reason: because one cannot scrape and clean them well so nothing of chametz remains in some crack not visible to the eye; and for this reason they are careful not to kasher for Pesach barrels in which they kept a drink made in the Russian lands from subin water (called wasser in Yiddish), lest chametz b'eyna remained from the subin; unless they kept water in it some time from the beginning — then it is permitted to kasher it, for the water that stood in it rinsed and removed all chametz adhering to it; nevertheless one must return and clean well before hagalah [and likewise vessels in which they keep flour all year — they also practice not to kasher them for this reason]; and see in Chayei Adam who wrote that this applies specifically to a vessel made from one piece; but those made from many boards like our vessels — it is known filth enters between board and board — for these hagalah does not help even after soaking in water several days; and see below s.k. 131 in Mishna Berurah.
