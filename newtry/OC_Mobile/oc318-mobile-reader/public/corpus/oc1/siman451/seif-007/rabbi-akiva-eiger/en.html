Magen Avraham s.k. 17. And pickling is like cooking. At first glance the Rama's reason is preferable, that even pouring for three days does not help, for sometimes they use them hot; and if it were on account of pouring, pouring for three days would help, as below in se'if 21. But the Magen Avraham copied this regarding a case where we know clearly that they were not used with hot water: nevertheless they are forbidden by rinsing because of soaking bread crumbs; and in such a case, pouring for three days indeed helps. And nevertheless, where sometimes hot water was used in them, even if it is known that hot chametz was not used in them, still through soaking bread crumbs it absorbed because of pickling; and when afterward hot water was used in it, the chametz absorbed in them was then absorbed also into this hot water, and pouring does not help.
