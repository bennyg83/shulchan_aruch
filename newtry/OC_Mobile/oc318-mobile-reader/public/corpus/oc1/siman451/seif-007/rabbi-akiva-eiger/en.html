And pickled like cooked. At first glance Rama's reason is preferable — for even pouring for three days does not help, for sometimes they use them hot; and if the reason were pouring, pouring three days would help as below seif 21; rather Magen Avraham copied this regarding if on condition we know clearly they were not used hot — nevertheless they are forbidden through rinsing because of dissolving bread crumbs; and in such a case truly pouring three days helps; and nevertheless where sometimes they used hot water — even though it is known they did not use hot chametz — nevertheless through dissolving bread crumbs it absorbs due to pickling; and when they later used hot water in it, the chametz absorbed in them is now absorbed also in this hot water, and pouring does not help.
