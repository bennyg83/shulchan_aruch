One thoroughly scrubs them, etc. If one did not scrub them before Pesach, one scrubs them during Pesach or after Pesach, like the law of one who failed to eliminate chametz before Pesach explained above in siman 435. See there. Chok Yaakov, subsection 4; Mekor Chaim, subsection 4; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 3. Scrubbing during Pesach applies specifically on Chol HaMoed. On Shabbos or Yom Tov it is forbidden to touch chametz even to eliminate it, as stated in siman 446. See there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, there.
