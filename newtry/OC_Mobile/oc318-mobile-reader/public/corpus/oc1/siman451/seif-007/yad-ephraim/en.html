There note 37 — but if one inserts the side that was not kashered it is forbidden, etc. — his words are wondrous in my humble opinion — for he wrote: and if the spoon was inserted, etc. — this is from the words of Hagahot Orach Chayyim at the end of the siman; and there it is explained not like his words — for behold he wrote one must kasher the spoon l'chatchila another time and insert it entirely into the pot — is it that other times one kashered and it is not like deception — for the food is permitted and the spoon requires hagalah immediately, since it was cooked in a pot that is not ben yomo — until here; and if according to Magen Avraham's words — why need a reason due to deception other times; and also what concern of deception applies here — for behold one must kasher it again and insert it entirely into the pot so as not to come to insert the other side that was not kashered and come to prohibition; rather certainly this is what he said: there is no distinction in the matter; and even inserting in the side that was not kashered is kosher, since that side was not inserted in prohibition — as Hagahot Orach Chayyim wrote there: the reason is because as it absorbs so it discharges.
