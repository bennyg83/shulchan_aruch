(26) However b'dieved, etc. — and specifically when it is known they did not use the handles (Sheyarei Kenesset HaGedolah); and Taz forbids knives even b'dieved — it seems from the reason that since it is used all year, presumably one also touches them in the sheath while roasting — and it is as though known; so it appears to me.
