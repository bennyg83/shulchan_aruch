(א) There — but for our ovens, no. And see in responsum Be'erot HaMayim part 4 siman 6 who wrote our ovens that are large are permitted; see there. However in derashot of Maharil page 6a he wrote in our ovens it does not help, even the large one of a baker; see there. And so it appears from the stam words of the poskim — one should not distinguish between oven and oven; and in any event even in a kiln some are stringent, as written in the previous note — if so, do not add on it.

(ב) And if one placed a pottery vessel in the oven and whitened it until sparks fly and transgressed and cooked in it on Pesach intentionally — forbidden, as one who cooked in a vessel that is not ben yomo intentionally for other prohibitions; and in inadvertence one may say it is permitted. Mishbetzot Zahav note 2; and it appears regarding a poor person and there is Pri Megadim — also for intentional one may be lenient, but one must rebuke him that he transgressed a rabbinic enactment and should not do so again. And see in Sefer Zivchei Tzedek on Yoreh De'ah siman 103 note 23.
