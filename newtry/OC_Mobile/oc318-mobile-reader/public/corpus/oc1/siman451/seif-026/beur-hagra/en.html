Se'if 26. Glass vessels even, etc. For in such a case kashering is required even cold, as is written in Avodah Zarah 33a and 74b. And even though the poskim disagree there whether it deals specifically with earthenware or even other vessels, nevertheless regarding earthenware there is no dispute; and glass vessels are like earthenware, as is written below. Nevertheless they do not need kashering, for they do not absorb, as is written in Pesachim 74b: different is a heart, for it is smooth; and see Rashi in Shabbos 15b s.v. "with a glass vessel," etc.; and see Yoreh De'ah siman 135, se'if 8. And therefore even if one used hot, it does not need kashering, as is written regarding a heart there. And it is taught explicitly in Avot deRabbi Natan, chapter 41: an earthenware vessel absorbs and does not expel, etc.; a glass vessel does not absorb and does not expel, etc.; in a hot place, hot; in a cold place, cold. And even though they asked regarding smooth konya whether it absorbed, and resolved: we see that they bring there because it is a slight lining; but a whole glass vessel does not absorb. Tosafos in Avodah Zarah there s.v. the aforementioned "konya," etc.; and see there.
And even if one used, etc. That is to say: in the manner of not only this but also that: if one put it away for keeping, it is permitted by pouring, and even regarding earthenware; and all the more so hagalah regarding earthenware; which is not so for use with hot water, as above.
There is none, etc. From the fact that they asked regarding konya; and likewise regarding wine of libation; and see Yoreh De'ah siman 135, se'if 8; and see Ran chapter 2 there in the sugya.
And some are stringent. Because it is like an earthenware vessel, since its beginning is from sand, as is written in the first chapter of Shabbos; and so we hold there in the conclusion in Rav Ashi's answer. And even though its use is cold, nevertheless sometimes they use them hot and soak bread crumbs in them day after day, and pickling is like cooking. Mordechai in siman 845. And as is written in se'if 25 in the gloss: that they are concerned even for use that is not fixed, as above. And therefore even hagalah does not help them, like earthenware, as is written in Avodah Zarah there and in the chapter "Kol Shaah" there; and the Torah testified regarding earthenware, etc. Terumat HaDeshen. And so it is taught explicitly in Avot deRabbi Natan that it does not expel like an earthenware vessel; and what is written there that it does not absorb means like the earthenware of the first clause, which absorbs even cold immediately even if one does not put it away for keeping, as is written in Avodah Zarah 33b: when, etc. And a proof from what is written: and it does not expel; and what is written from a smooth heart does not stand in the conclusion there, as Tosafos wrote there s.v. "different"; and so it is implied in Tosafos of Avodah Zarah aforementioned that hagalah does not help, who asked on Rashi who explained regarding lead: for if so, why does hagalah not help? And they explained regarding glass only that he wrote that entirely of glass does not absorb, as above.
And likewise silver vessels, etc. Like the konya, as Tosafos wrote, as above.
But. That is to say: at the place of a handle and the like, a place whose use is not hot; and see Magen Avraham. And even though they did not say "if part of it is hot," etc., except regarding metal, as is written in Pesachim 74a, nevertheless on account of the metal the glass is heated: for in a hot place, hot, as above.
