(24) There — if one returns them to the kiln, etc. And in Orach Chayyim general rule 58 siman 49 he brought the dispute on this; and wrote therefore we practice not to fire them at all, even in a potter's kiln; see there. And they brought Eshel Avraham note 3; and so Peri Chadash brought the view of those who forbid even to return them to the kiln; and he wrote regarding the law one should be stringent; see there. But Rabbenu Tam in Sefer HaYashar siman 214 wrote they have a remedy through returning to the kiln; and so Mahari Weil Netiv 5 part 2; and in She'eltot of Rav Achai parashat Matot; and so is Tosafot's view (Pesachim 30b); and so Rokeach siman 484; and so R' Menachem chapter 5; see there. And Beit Din Chokat HaPesach note 3; see there. And so R' Zalman note 9; and therefore since there is a dispute — each follows his stream and its plain meaning.
