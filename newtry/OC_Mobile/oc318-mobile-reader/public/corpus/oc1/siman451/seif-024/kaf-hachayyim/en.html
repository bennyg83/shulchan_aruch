See there. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 3, cites this. Peri Chadash likewise cites the authorities who prohibit even returning them to a kiln and writes that in practice one should be stringent. See there. Rabbeinu Tam in Sefer HaYashar, siman 614, however, writes that returning them to a kiln repairs them. Rabbeinu Yerucham, path 5, section 2; She'iltos of Rav Achai, parashas Matos; Tosafos, Pesachim 30b; Rokeach, siman 484; and Rabbeinu Manoach, chapter 5, write likewise. Chukas HaPesach, subsection 3, cites these opinions. See there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 9, rules likewise. Therefore, since the matter is disputed, each community should follow its established custom.
