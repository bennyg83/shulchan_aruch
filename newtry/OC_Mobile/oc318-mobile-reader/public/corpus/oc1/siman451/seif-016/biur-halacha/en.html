That all such is hagalah of kli rishon — Peri Chadash expanded on this that it is not literally kli rishon and this does not help for something used on fire in literal kli rishon; the main advice of placing stones does not help except to raise the boiling upward — but specifically for a vessel whose use is by pouring and not a vessel used in kli rishon. He concludes that perhaps the poskim wrote this only here regarding a mortar, and likewise above in seif 6 regarding large bowls whose main use is by pouring from kli rishon and all such; and Peri Megadim in Eshel Avraham wrote similarly in seif 6 in the name of Bach; and see there what I wrote that one should permit only when it is not ben yomo.
