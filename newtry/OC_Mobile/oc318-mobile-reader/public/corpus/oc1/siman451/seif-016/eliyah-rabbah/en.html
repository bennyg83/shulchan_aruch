[16] [Levush] three times, etc. This requires study: from where do we know this? For from all the poskim it is implied that once suffices. Maharil wrote: those hagaled by pouring should pour with alacrity and not stand far away for fear of soiling their clothes, so that the stream not be interrupted; end quote. And also one should not take with another vessel from the lower vessel and pour upon what is being hagaled, unless it had stayed there while bringing up a boil.
