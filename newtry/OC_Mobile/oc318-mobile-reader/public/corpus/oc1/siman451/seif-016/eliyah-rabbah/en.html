[16] [Levush] three times, etc. — it requires study: whence do we know that from all the poskim it appears hagalah once suffices. Maharil wrote: those kashered via pouring should pour quickly and not stand far away from concern of soiling his garments so the stream will not stop — until here; and also he should not take in another vessel from an under-vessel and pour on the kashered item unless it was there when he raised the boiling.
