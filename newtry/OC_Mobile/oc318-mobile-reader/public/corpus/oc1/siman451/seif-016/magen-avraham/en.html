Mortar. Tur wrote they regularly grind spices and sharp things with bread crumbs — some say requires libun like sourdough house; he ruled hagalah suffices as s.k. 22. Bach: our mortars need hagalah lest chametz absorbed via spices; do not whiten with coals lest crack — copper scrap; chametz stringency — grind spices before Pesach — nitelaf in nitelaf of permitted; even hagalah unnecessary as s.k. 1 barrel; if not — grind on 14th when kashering. World practices Rama to whiten; it appears law is with him even books say practice to whiten — but our mortars never have chametz, only saffron — hagalah enough. Bach grind before 14th after hagalah — nitelaf in nitelaf — study: sharp substance — nitelaf in nitelaf not effective YD 95; possibly mere stringency; sometimes cut ginger in meat knife and grind in mortar then eat with milk — beware, cut ginger only in new knife. If ground spices in our mortar in dish — study forbid b'dieved — suspicion knife cut; this suspicion we do not forbid b'dieved as seif 10 baskets — salting also sharp — see end and YD 96, Shach — ginger not called sharp per some — see there.
