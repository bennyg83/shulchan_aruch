A mortar. The Magen Avraham discusses the kashering of utensils for Pesach, according to their normal mode of use, with distinctions between hagalah, libbun, pouring from a kli rishon, soaking, cleaning away visible chametz, and after-the-fact cases. In particular, it addresses mortars used with sharp spices and possible chametz crumbs, barrels used for beer, mead, wine, or spirits, sieves, baskets, sacks, and cloths where crumbs may remain, sharp foods and whether secondary absorbed taste is effective.
