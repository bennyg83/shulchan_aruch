Se'if 16. A mortar, etc.; and some are stringent. Their dispute depends on what is written in the chapter "Kol Shaah" there: except a yeast-house, etc.; and Rashi explained: even by means of hagalah. And the Arukh and the Ri and all the poskim explained: without hagalah; and see above se'if 22. And the same applies to a mortar. And even though the Rosh in his rulings wrote that Rashi agrees regarding a mortar, in Sefer HaPardes he did not write so; and so wrote all the poskim.
But, etc. However, etc. And if, etc. As above.
