Taz s.k. 27. And it appears that if one cooked that [vessel] in water. And that is to say: since the Taz ruled that for vessels that one cannot libun, hagalah helps for a yeast-house [vessel] and a charoses [vessel]; but vessels of hard liquor are more stringent, for the smell is sensed; therefore it helps to cook first in ashes to remove the smell. But according to what the Magen Avraham holds in s.k. 2, that we practice to forbid a yeast-house [vessel] by hagalah, this repair does not help.
