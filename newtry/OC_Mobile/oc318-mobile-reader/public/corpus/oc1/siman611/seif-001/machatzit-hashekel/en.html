The Magen Avraham wrote: Nevertheless, all of them are from the Torah. This refers to what is written in the Shulchan Aruch, that liability to kareis applies only to labor, eating, and drinking. About this the Magen Avraham wrote that regarding the other afflictions, although they do not have kareis, nevertheless they are forbidden from the Torah. This is also the view of the Rambam, Rashi, and Ran, excluding the view of the Rosh and Tur, who hold that the other afflictions are rabbinic, and the verses the Talmud brings to forbid the other afflictions are an asmakhta.
