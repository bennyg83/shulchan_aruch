And they permitted trimming vegetables. Rashi explained: to detach the leaves from the stalks in order to straighten them so that they will be ready to cut. It is permitted because it constitutes affliction, for he sees prepared food and does not eat, end quote. The Ran and the Maggid Mishneh in chapter 1 of the Laws of Resting on the Tenth explained that it is because of distress, so that it will be easier for the meal, and it is like having bread in his basket. From the words of the Tur in siman 609 it implies that he explains like the second explanation, for according to Rashi's explanation the Tur would not ask anything there about insulating hot food. According to Rashi, the reason it is forbidden before Minchah is that at that time there is no change, and it is forbidden because of a rabbinic prohibition.
