1) [Seif 1] The night of Yom Kippur is like its day, etc. For it is written, "from evening to evening," etc.
2) Ibid. The night of Yom Kippur is like its day, etc. Even during the additional time that is added from the weekday onto the sacred, both before it and after it, all matters forbidden during the essence of the day are forbidden. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 1.
