And he should drink all of it or most of it. See Mishnah Berurah. And behold, most of a revi'is is the measure of a full cheekful for an average person; and if he is a large person whose own full cheekful is more than most of a revi'is, he specifically needs his own full cheekful; so it was clarified above in siman 272 regarding Kiddush; see there in se'if 3 in Mishnah Berurah; and all the more so regarding the four cups. And see there in Biur Halacha that we further ruled that regarding the four cups we specifically require most of a cup as a stringency; and for example if he is a small person who has become bar mitzvah at thirteen, whose own full cheekful is less than most of a revi'is, he specifically needs most of a revi'is. And I have now found that the Ran, chapter Arvei Pesachim, s.v. "im ta'am," also wrote like our words; and so wrote Maharam Chalavah in his novellae there.
