Not with a major interruption. That is, that he should not delay from beginning to end more than the time of drinking a revi'is, as is written in siman 612; and it appears to me that if he delayed more than the time of eating a peras, even after the fact he has not fulfilled; and so is implied in siman 475, se'if 6; and he must return and drink even for the later cups; and if he delayed the time of drinking a revi'is, then it is a doubt: for the first two cups he should return and drink; and see there what is written in se'if 7 in the gloss. And ideally he should drink most of a revi'is at one time; see siman 475.
