The measure of the cup is a revi'it after they mix it (if he wishes to mix it), and he drinks all of it or most of it; and if there are many revi'it in it, so many people drink from it as the number of revi'it' in it. And some say one must drink most of the cup even if it holds several revi'it. And one must drink the measure without a large interruption in between (Beit Yosef in the name of Rokeach).
