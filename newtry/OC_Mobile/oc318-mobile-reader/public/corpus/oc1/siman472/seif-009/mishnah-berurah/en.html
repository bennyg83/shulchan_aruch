A revi'is. And its measure: see above in siman 271, s.k. 68, in Mishnah Berurah there.
After he dilutes it. That is, we do not require a revi'is of undiluted wine, but rather a revi'is together with the water mixed into it. And in their times, when the wines were very strong, they would dilute one to three; and in our times, when the wines are weak, see above in siman 272, s.k. 16, in Mishnah Berurah.
If he wishes to dilute it. That is to say, in our times when the wines are weak there is no obligation to dilute it even ideally. And know that even if he drinks undiluted and strong wine, nevertheless he specifically needs a full revi'is, for the Sages did not split measures.
All of it or most of it. That is, all of it ideally or most of it after the fact; and in lands where wine is expensive, even ideally most of it suffices; but the fourth cup he should drink all of it so that he can recite the after-blessing according to all opinions [later authorities]. There are those who stint and get angry at the servants if they drink a lot, and they will in the future give an accounting for causing them to stumble and preventing them from the mitzvah [Chayei Adam].
They drink from it, etc. From this wording it is implied that even ideally it is permitted whenever there is a revi'is for each one; and even though ideally we require a full cup and also that it not be deficient, here since for the first it was a full cup, all of them come by force of the first; and it is possible further that the Mechaber primarily came to teach that both fulfill with one cup.
According to the number of revi'iyos in it. And after the fact it suffices even if each one had only most of a revi'is.
Most of the cup even, etc. Even though in general most of a revi'is suffices even from a large cup, here regarding the cups it is more severe, for we specifically require most of a cup, and if not he has not fulfilled. And for the practical law we hold like the first view. And nevertheless if he does not intend to drink a lot he should not take a large cup, only a cup that holds a revi'is, in order to be concerned for this view.
Not with a major interruption. That is, that he should not delay in drinking most of the cup more than the time of eating a peras; and if he delayed more than this measure, the beginning of the drinking does not combine with its end, and even after the fact he has not fulfilled, and he must return and drink even for the later cups [for there is no issue here of adding to the cups since according to all he has not fulfilled]. And ideally one should be careful not to delay in drinking most of the cup more than the time of drinking a revi'is, to be concerned for the first view clarified in siman 612; see there. And nevertheless after the fact, if he delayed for the last two cups he should not return and drink, as implied above in se'if 7 in the gloss; see there. And for the first two cups he should return and drink. And ideally it is proper to drink most of the revi'is at one time.
