The measure of the cup is a revi'it. The measure of a revi'it is two fingerbreadths by two fingerbreadths, and its height is like the width of two thumbs and a half-thumb and a fifth of a thumb — that is a revi'it. And a small cup that does not hold a revi'it — one gives drink from it to his children and the small members of his household (Beit Yosef, Orach Chayim).
