One fulfills. That is b'dieved; but l'chatchila it is a mitzvah from the best to take the finest wine without mixture. Konditon means that honey and spices were mixed into it. Taz wrote that where wine is not available one drinks raisin water; and in responsum Rivash siman 16 it is explained that only those raisins that have moisture when they press them — but if nothing comes out from them except by soaking in water, one does not fulfill with them — see there. So too Rokeach, and such is the custom. Chok Yaakov.
