One fulfills the obligation. This applies after the fact. Initially, the preferred mitzvah is to use the finest wine without admixture. Konditon means wine mixed with honey and spices. The Taz writes that where wine is unavailable, one should drink raisin water. Responsa Rivash, siman 16, explains that this applies specifically to raisins that still contain moisture when pressed. If no juice emerges from them except by soaking them in water, one does not fulfill the obligation with it; see there. Rokeach writes likewise, and this is the custom. Chok Yaakov.
