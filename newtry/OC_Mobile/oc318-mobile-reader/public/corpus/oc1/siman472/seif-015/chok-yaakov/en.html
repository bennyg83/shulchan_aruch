Who has reached. So too the Rosh wrote in a responsum. See Tosafot at the beginning of Perek Erev Pesachim, s.v. “they should not give him less,” and the Maharil wrote: “However, the sugya of the Talmud seems at first glance to refer even to one who has not reached the age of education, and one gives a revi’is to one who has reached education.” These are his words. Reached the age of education means that he is about five or six years old; see above, siman 640.
It is a mitzvah to give each one his cup. If he did not give it, it does not prevent fulfillment; it is only initially the preferred mitzvah, as explained above in seif 9.
