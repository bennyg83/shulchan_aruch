(46) It is a mitzvah to give, etc. - But it is not indispensable.
(47) His cup before him - See siman 271 seif 13 in Biur Halachah, where we leaned toward saying that a minor does not need to drink most of a revi'it, only his own cheekful.
(48) Because, etc. - Similar to what is explained below in siman 475, seif 6, regarding matzah; after the fact it does not invalidate unless he delayed a long time, as above in seif katan 34.
(49) Otherwise one does not take it - For he must set his eyes on it, as explained there; and the same applies regarding Kiddush in such a case.
