[Levush] A remembrance of four redemptions, etc. In Mateh Moshe there are three reasons. One: the first cup is "Who chose us" in the days of Avraham; the second cup is thanksgiving for the redemption from Egypt; the third cup corresponds to the time of exile; the fourth cup corresponds to the future redemption. A second reason: the first cup, that they were saved from the enslavement of Egypt, for on Rosh Hashanah the labor ceased from our fathers in Egypt; the second cup, the Exodus from Egypt; the third cup, that He executed judgments upon them; the fourth cup, concerning the Giving of the Torah. A third reason: corresponding to the four letters of the Divine Name. R' David Abudarham wrote: some ask why we do not recite a blessing "Who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to drink four cups"; and one may say that the cup of Kiddush is already a standing mitzvah, and one should not recite a blessing to drink three cups when he is obligated in four cups; further one may say that we recite a blessing only over a mitzvah done at once without interruption, but four cups, if he drank them at once he has not fulfilled, and we do not recite a blessing; see siman 484 regarding the law of houses.
