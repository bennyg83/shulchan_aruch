(Seif katan 12) Because, etc. For one may dilute it well as long as it remains fit, etc.
Now, from the words of the Rama in siman 204, seif 5, it somewhat implies that if there are not six parts water, one recites Borei Pri HaGafen over it. But in the Eliyah Rabbah he brings in the name of the Olas Tamid that regarding our weak wines, if there is much water in it, even if the wine is the majority, nevertheless it requires study whether one should recite Borei Pri HaGafen over it.
