Without interruption. Meaning that from beginning to end he should not pause longer than the time needed to drink a revi'is, as stated in siman 612. Magen Avraham writes: It appears to me that if he paused longer than the time needed to eat a peras, he has not fulfilled his obligation even after the fact and must drink again, even regarding the latter cups. If he paused for the time needed to drink a revi'is, the matter is doubtful. Regarding the first two cups, he should drink again. See above, seif katan 7, and what is written there. Initially, he should drink most of a revi'is at once; see siman 475. The Baer Heitev before me confuses the matters here; see there.
