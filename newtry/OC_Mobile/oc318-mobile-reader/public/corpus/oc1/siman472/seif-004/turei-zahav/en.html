For in this time, etc. For Ravyah holds that in this time one need not recline, since it is not customary in our land during the rest of the year to recline, but rather one sits in his usual manner; upon this women rely in not reclining. And my father-in-law, of blessed memory, wrote: but a mourner does not recline, since sitting in the usual manner is the way of freedom, and such is the practice; and all the more so regarding those who wear the kittel at the time of the Seder, that the mourner should not wear it; however it appears that the reason for wearing the kittel is so that his spirit not become haughty because of the joy, therefore he wears a garment of the dead; and according to this the mourner too should wear it. And see what I wrote in siman 610 regarding Yom Kippur.
