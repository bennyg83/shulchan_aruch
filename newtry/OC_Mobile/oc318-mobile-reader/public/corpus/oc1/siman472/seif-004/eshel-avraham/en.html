She does not need. Because she is obligated to her husband (Ran); and according to this, a widow or divorcee needs [heseiba]. However, according to his reasoning, even a distinguished woman does not need [heseiba], for even though she sits on a chair [kathedra], she pours a cup, etc. Rather, the reason in the name of Rabbeinu Manoach is that he explains that she need not busy herself with household needs, and an attendant is different, because one who acquires [a slave] for himself is like one who acquires, etc.; see there. And the main [ruling] is as written in the She'iltot, that it is not the manner of women to pour; and from now on all women are exempt.
