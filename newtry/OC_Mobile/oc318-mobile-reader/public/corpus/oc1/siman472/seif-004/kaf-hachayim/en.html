And although on other Shabbatot and Yom Tov one may add from the weekday onto the holy, Pesach is different — for the time of eating matzah is only at night, similar to Pesach as written "on matzot and bitter herbs they shall eat it," and the Pesach offering is eaten only at night as written "and they shall eat the flesh on this night." Tur and Beit Yosef in the name of Rosh chapter Arvei Pesachim. Levush. And although before he finishes the Haggadah and reaches eating matzah and maror it will already be full night, nevertheless the cup of kiddush which is one of four cups, and also eating other vegetables and the rest of the changes we do so that children ask — and all the more so the Haggadah itself also requires being at the time fit to eat matzah and maror, for all these are mitzvot of matzah and maror that are included, completed, expounded, and "and you shall tell your son," etc., for this reason I did not say, etc. Terumat HaDeshen siman 137. Beit Yosef. Levush. Bach. See there note 1. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. Chak Yosef note 1. R' Z. note 2 — and so Taz s.k. 1 that the reason kiddush must be at night is because it comes on account of matzah — see there. However in Drashot Maharil letter of Haggadah he wrote the reason we begin kiddush at night is on account of the first dipping and the rest of the order of Haggadah that must be at night — see there; and Beit David Chok Yaakov note 3. However for the law all agree it must specifically be at night.
