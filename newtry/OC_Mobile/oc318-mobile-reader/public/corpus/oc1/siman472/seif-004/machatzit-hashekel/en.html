(Seif katan 4) And not, etc. Perhaps it will go down first, etc. This is unlike some who explain that the Rashbam wrote the reason as being that he must serve with the right hand, for if so, with a left-handed person, on the contrary, he should lean to the right. Rather, the reason is lest it go down first, etc., and therefore we hold that there is no distinction between a left-handed person and another person.
