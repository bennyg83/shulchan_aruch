4) There. But one should not recite Kiddush until nightfall. Although on other Shabbosos and Yamim Tovim one may add from the weekday onto the sacred day, Pesach differs. The time for eating matzah is only at night, like the Pesach offering, as the verse states, 'They shall eat it with matzos and bitter herbs,' and the Pesach offering is eaten only at night, as the verse states, 'They shall eat the meat on this night.' Tur and Beis Yosef in the name of Rosh at the beginning of the chapter Arvei Pesachim; Levush. Although actual nightfall will arrive before one completes the Haggadah and reaches the eating of matzah and maror, the cup of Kiddush, which is one of the four cups, the eating of the other vegetable, the other changes made so that the children will ask, and certainly the Haggadah itself must also occur when matzah and maror may be eaten. All these practices relate to and derive from the mitzvos of matzah and maror. We expound the verse, 'You shall tell your child,' and so forth, 'Because of this,' meaning only when matzah and maror are placed before you. Terumas HaDeshen, siman 137; Beis Yosef; Levush; Bach; Olas Shabbos, subsection 1; Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Chok Yosef, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 2. Taz, subsection 1, likewise writes that Kiddush must be recited at night because it comes for the sake of matzah. See there. Derashos Maharil, laws of the Haggadah, however, explains that Kiddush is begun at night because the first dipping and the rest of the Haggadah must be performed at night. Chok Yaakov, subsection 3, cites this. Regarding the practical law, all agree that Kiddush must be recited specifically at night.
