She does not need heseiba. For an ordinary woman, it is not her manner to recline at any time.
For in this time one should not recline. His reason is that it is not customary in our land during the rest of the days of the year to recline, but rather one sits in his usual manner. But within twelve months for his father and mother, or within thirty for other relatives, for example when he did not observe seven days before the festival, even though he is obligated in heseiba, nevertheless the proper [practice] is that he should not recline on an honored and adorned bed, but rather recline with some change, that is, on a bed with one cushion under his head, or on his fellow's knees. And regarding a kittel, the practice is not to wear it; and nevertheless one who wears it, we do not protest against him.
