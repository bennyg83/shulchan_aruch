Se'if 4: A woman. Like the reason of the She'iltot; and so the Rif does not have the reading "with her husband."
And all women, etc. See Kesef Mishneh, who wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Manoach in the latter wording: a distinguished woman who need not busy herself with household needs in preparing food; and see Kesubos 61a; and see Even HaEzer siman 80, and there se'if 10.
For he wrote that in this time, etc. For nowadays it is not the way of freedom, for kings nowadays are not accustomed to recline; and so according to the explanation of the She'iltot, who wrote that a woman does not need heseiba, etc.
