Even a poor person, etc. In lands where wine is not common, many stumble in this mitzvah and do not fulfill it properly; and it is good for them to make wine from raisins as mentioned in siman 272; and so wrote the Tur in siman 488, that one should buy raisins. It appears to me that wherever it is mentioned that one need not recline, there is a prohibition to recline, as is stated regarding sukkah, that whoever is exempt from a matter and does it is called a hedyot; and so I found in the name of Maharshal.
