13) Orchos Chaim, page 78c, writes in the name of Midrash Rabbah and Midrash Yelamdeinu that reclining has a basis in the Torah. The verse states, 'Elokim led the people around,' teaching that He seated them reclining in the manner of royalty, resting upon their couches. Shulchan Gavoah below in siman 486, subsection 12, writes likewise in the names of Sefer HaManhig and Abudraham. See there. Yafeh LaLev, there.
