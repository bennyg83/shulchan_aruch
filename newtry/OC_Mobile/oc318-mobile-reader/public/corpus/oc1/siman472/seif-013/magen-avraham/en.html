Even a poor person, etc. It appears to me that one who has only four cups measured out after dilution should take them on the first night, and should not take two on the first night for Kiddush and Birkas HaMazon and two for the second night: first, because we are expert in the fixed calendar, and the first day is primary; and further, for Kiddush of Yom Tov is rabbinic, and regarding Birkas HaMazon some say it does not require a cup, therefore publicizing the miracle is preferable; and further, Kiddush is possible over bread; see siman 296, se'if 4. And it appears to me that the lamp of his house is preferable to the four cups because of shalom bayis, as is written in siman 263, se'if 3.
