In the beis midrash. That is to say, when he is occupied with his learning; and the same applies regarding prayer: he must urge himself to recite Kerias Shema and pray, and go home immediately.
For it is a mitzvah to hurry and eat, etc. That is to say, to urge oneself to do the Seder so that the children not fall asleep, when they know that there will not be a long delay until the eating; and consequently they will ask "Mah Nishtanah" and he will answer them, and fulfill what is written, "And you shall tell your son on that day."
But he should not say Kiddush. That is to say, do not say that since it is a mitzvah to hurry he should begin Kiddush and the Haggadah while it is still day, as we find on Shabbos and Yom Tov that one may add from the weekday onto the holy and sanctify and eat while it is still day, as above in siman 267; it teaches us that regarding Pesach it is not so, because eating matzah is juxtaposed to the Pesach offering, as it is said, "upon matzos and bitter herbs they shall eat it," and the Pesach is eaten only at night, and Kiddush must be at the time fit for matzah; and further, the cup of Kiddush is one of the four cups, and all of them follow after the Haggadah and matzah and maror.
