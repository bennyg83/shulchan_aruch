With heseiba, etc. But within twelve months he has no heseiba (Bach), and such is the practice (Taz); and the same law applies to a mourner within thirty days for other relatives when he did not observe seven days before the festival; and the view of Chok Yaakov is that every mourner needs heseiba, only he should change somewhat.
