He should not incline, etc. For this is not called the way of freedom.
And not on his right. For that is not genuine heseiba, since he must eat with his right; and there is another reason: lest the windpipe precede the esophagus, for the esophagus is on the right side, and when he tilts his head toward the right, the cover over the windpipe opens by itself and food may enter there and he may come to danger.
And there is no distinction, etc. That is to say, even a left-handed person must recline on the left of every person; and even though he always eats with his left, and if so according to reason 1 he would need to recline on his right, nevertheless one should not distinguish because of reason 2, lest the windpipe precede the esophagus; and this reason is preferable for us because it is danger, and danger is more severe than a prohibition; and he should eat that time with the right of every person. And after the fact, if he reclined on the right side he has fulfilled, for his right is like the left of every person.
