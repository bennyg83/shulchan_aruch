Se'if 3: He should not incline, etc. See Tosafos there s.v. "parkedan," and in Niddah 17a s.v. "afarkad"; but the Ran wrote that one should be concerned for both explanations, for possibly both are called parkedan; and so wrote the Rambam and Tur. And these are the words of the Aruch in the entry "parkad," at the beginning of chapter 2 of Berachos and at the beginning of chapter 2 of Niddah: parkedan, etc., meaning one who lies on his spine with his face upward; and so explained R' Hai Gaon; and some interpret one who sleeps on his face, because it may lead to warming; and in Niddah it is explicit that the decree of warming applies, and they inferred [this] regarding sleeping on the face with bowing; and R' Meir, etc., explains regarding a bed with bowing; and in Menachos 99b, "parkuda" was lying face up; end of his words.
And there is no distinction. See Rashbam s.v. "deheseibas yamin," etc., and s.v. "shema"; and my teachers, etc. And the Rosh wrote: and there is a practical difference between the two reasons regarding a left-handed person; and Terumas HaDeshen wrote that we act according to the latter reason, "lest [the windpipe] precede," for danger is more severe, etc., as is written in the first chapter of Chullin.
