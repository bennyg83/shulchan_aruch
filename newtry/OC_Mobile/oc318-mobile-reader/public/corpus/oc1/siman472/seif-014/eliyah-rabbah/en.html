Many revi'iyos they drink, etc. And some say one must drink most of the cup even if it holds several revi'iyos (Shulchan Aruch); and that is from Orchos Chayim in the name of the Ramban in Beis Yosef; and the Levush omitted it intentionally, for most poskim disagree with him; it is also clear thus in the Kol Bo, which brought all the words of Orchos Chayim and omitted the Ramban; and so ruled Raavan, that even with a large cup one need not drink more than most of a revi'is; and the Bach was stringent not to take a large cup when one does not wish to drink the majority (Olas Shabbos); and as I wrote in Eliyah Zuta; but the practice follows the Levush.
