See Taz: the esophagus is on the right and the windpipe on the left in every person; and even a left-handed person must recline on the left, only for this reason of danger; and all the more so one who is ambidextrous; and after the fact, if he reclined on his right he has fulfilled; which is not so if on his back or face down: even after the fact it is not heseiba, and he must return and recline; and see what I write, God willing, in Magen Avraham 4.
