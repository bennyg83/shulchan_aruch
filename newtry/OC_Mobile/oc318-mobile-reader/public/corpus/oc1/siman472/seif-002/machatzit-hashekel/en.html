(Seif katan 2) With utensils, etc. One may answer that since he does not use them, etc. The Chok Yaakov wrote that it is stated in the Maharil as follows: “Even utensils held by him as collateral from non-Jews may be placed on a special table in order to rejoice at the sight of them.” These are his words. It is evident that even on Pesach he did not use them. See the Taz in Yoreh Deah, siman 120, who wrote that this does not involve theft. It is no worse than retaining a non-Jew’s debt where there is no desecration of the Name, which is permitted, since it came into his hand permissibly. There is also no deception in such a case; see there.
