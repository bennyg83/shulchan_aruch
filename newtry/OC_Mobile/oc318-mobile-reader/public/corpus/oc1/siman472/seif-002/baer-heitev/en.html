Fine vessels. All year it is good except for fine vessels, in remembrance of the destruction — except on the night of Pesach one should increase fine vessels. Maharil would not use pledges from an akum except on Pesach — see Magen Avraham. And it is not like one who borrows without knowledge, for presumably he is not particular; and the implication of Maharil's wording is that he placed them on a special table to see them and guard them without use. (And see Chok Yaakov what he wrote, and see Shach and Taz in Yoreh De'ah siman 104.)
