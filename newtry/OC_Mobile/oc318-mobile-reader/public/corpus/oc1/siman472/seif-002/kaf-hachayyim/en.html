Even if one is in the beis midrash, one should arise, etc. Pesachim 109a teaches: It was said of Rabbi Akiva that throughout his life he never announced that it was time to leave the beis midrash except on the eves of Pesach and Yom Kippur. On the eve of Pesach he did so for the children, so that they would not fall asleep; on the eve of Yom Kippur, so that people would feed their children. See Rashi and Tosafos there.
