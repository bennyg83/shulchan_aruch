With fine vessels. All year it is good to minimize fine vessels in remembrance of the Destruction, except on the night of Pesach, when one should increase fine vessels; and Maharil would not use pledged vessels of gentiles except on Pesach; and so is written in a manuscript in the name of Maharam, that it would be theft from the gentile, for one who borrows without the owner's knowledge is a thief; see Choshen Mishpat siman 359. And it is difficult: is it permitted to steal on Pesach? One may say that since he uses them only once a year, the gentile is not particular; and see Taz in Yoreh Deah siman 120, se'if 9.
He should sit on the bench. And not on the ground; and in the Mordechai it is implied that the reading is "he should recline on the bench"; and it is also permitted to lean on his fellow's thigh, as is stated in the Gemara, but not on his own thigh, for it appears like one who is anxious (Maharil).
