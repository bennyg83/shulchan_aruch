With fine vessels, etc. And regarding pledged vessels of Cutheans, Magen Avraham's view is that [use] without his permission is theft, as is written in Choshen Mishpat siman 359; and the Gemara at the end of the chapter "HaSochair Es HaPo'el" requires one to say that since his intention is to sink [the pledge into use] it is different; or alternatively, that he took permission from the Cutheans. However, for the practical law the later authorities already agreed in Yoreh Deah siman 108 that it is permitted even all year; and therefore I abbreviated.
