Even if he is not his primary teacher. And there is proof from that which is brought regarding every person reclining — even a student with his teacher — and it was necessary to establish it specifically regarding a saddle-stitcher, and since it does not establish it regarding a teacher who is not primary, learn from this that even when not primary he need not recline. Maharar Liv questioned in his book Gevurat Hashem why he did not actually distinguish between a primary teacher and one who is not primary, when we find this distinction regarding standing; he left it requiring further study. Therefore regarding a teacher who is not primary, his teacher gives him permission and then he requires reclining; but regarding a primary teacher, even if he gave him permission, nevertheless he is not obligated to recline.
