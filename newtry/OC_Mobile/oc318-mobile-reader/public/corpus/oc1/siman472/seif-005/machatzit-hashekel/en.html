(Seif katan 5) He need not, etc. But, etc. The Chok Yaakov wrote that he is obligated in reclining, and in any event he should wear the kittel when he did not observe seven days before the festival. For if he already observed them, the festival nullifies the decree of thirty and he is not a mourner. Rather, this applies when the seven days of mourning had not yet been completed when the festival entered; then the festival nullifies the decree of seven, and the decree of thirty applies to him.
