(s.k. 5) Need not, etc. — but, etc. Chok Yaakov wrote that he is obligated in reclining, and at least he should wear the kittel when he did not observe seven days before the festival. For if he already observed the festival, the festival cancels the decree of thirty and he is not a mourner — except when the seven days of mourning have not yet been completed when the festival entered, for then the festival cancels the decree of seven and the decree of thirty applies to him.
