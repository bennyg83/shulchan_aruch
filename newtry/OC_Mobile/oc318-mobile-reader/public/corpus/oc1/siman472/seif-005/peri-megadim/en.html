And it appears Taz — Rosh likewise chapter Arvei Pesachim 108a, and Magen Avraham note 7, explained that all who need to bless are like adding to the cups; therefore between the first and second it is permitted to drink as explained in the Gemara Pesachim 117a and siman 473:63 — it does not appear like adding, for he drinks without a blessing; and even for us who bless on each cup, the reason is that each is a mitzvah by itself — see siman 474 and in Tur and Magen Avraham — not so between the third and fourth, for it is forbidden to drink, and if he drinks he must bless — it is like adding. On this Rosh concludes there is no concern of adding to the cups since it was clarified, etc.; and on this Taz challenged: although Rosh initially holds it is like adding, nevertheless per what he wrote afterward, all who act lest he not fulfill — initially there is no adding at all; if so the question is greater — he should have said the reason between third and fourth is that he should not drink lest he become drunk and not say the Haggadah, as explained in mishnah 117b and Yerushalmi — not so for the first two cups, wine before the meal one does not become drunk — see siman 479. So too Agudah chapter Arvei Pesachim siman 32 — this concern; and why did he mention concern of adding, which the mishnah did not mention, and it is not so clear that all who act from uncertainty lest he not fulfill — there is no concern of adding; and see Magen Avraham note 14. And per what Eshel Avraham wrote — I have some difficulty in what Rosh wrote: fourth cup — if he drank without reclining he must bless borei peri hagafen on a fifth cup — it implies on the third cup he need not bless, but it is forbidden to drink between cups and he must bless also on the third cup, for initially Rosh also wrote third and fourth he should not drink, for it is like adding — why from what reason as Magen Avraham wrote note 7; and how did he conclude on the fourth cup he blesses and not on the third? One may resolve that Rosh holds on the third cup, if he first drank without reclining, he does not bless borei peri hagafen when he returns and drinks, since his intent is to drink the fourth cup; and per Rosh on the fourth cup he does not bless borei peri hagafen — see siman 474 — similarly when he returns and drinks the third cup. And what he wrote initially between third and fourth he should not return and drink — the reason is between third and fourth he should not drink, as Agudah wrote; and after the fourth cup the reason is it is like adding, for he would need to bless borei peri hagafen; if so Taz's challenge is well settled — why did he not give one comprehensive reason for both. Magen Avraham 7.
