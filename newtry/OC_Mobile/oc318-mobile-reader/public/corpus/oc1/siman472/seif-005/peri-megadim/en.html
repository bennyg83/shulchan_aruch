The Rosh likewise wrote in chapter Arvei Pesachim 108a; and see Magen Avraham letter 7, who explained that whenever one must recite a blessing it is like adding to the cups; therefore between the first and the second, where one is permitted to drink as clarified in the Gemara Pesachim 117a and siman 473 se'if 3, it does not appear like adding, for he drinks without a blessing; and even for us who recite a blessing over each cup, the reason is that each is a mitzvah by itself; see siman 474 and in Tur and Magen Avraham; which is not so between the third and the fourth, where it is forbidden to drink, and if he drinks he would need to recite a blessing, it is like adding. And on this the Rosh concludes that there is no concern of adding to the cups since it was clarified, etc.; and on this the Taz challenged: granted that the Rosh initially holds it is like adding, nevertheless according to what he wrote afterward, whenever he acts because he did not initially fulfill, there is no adding here at all; if so, a further difficulty: he should rather have said the reason between third and fourth is that he should not drink, lest he become drunk and not say the Haggadah, as clarified in the mishnah 117b and in the Yerushalmi; which is not so for the first two cups, wine before the meal, with which one does not become drunk; and see siman 479. And so wrote the Agudah, chapter Arvei Pesachim siman 32, this concern; and why did he mention the concern of adding, which the mishnah did not mention, and it is not so clear that whenever one acts from doubt lest he not have fulfilled there is no concern of adding; and see Chok Yaakov letter 14; and according to what I wrote it is fine. And I have some difficulty in what the Rosh wrote: the fourth cup, if he drank without heseiba, he must recite Borei Pri HaGafen over a fifth cup: it is implied that over the third cup he need not recite a blessing; but it is forbidden to drink between the cups and he must recite a blessing also over the third cup, for initially the Rosh likewise wrote that for the third and fourth he should not drink, for it is like adding for this reason, as Magen Avraham wrote letter 7; and how did he conclude regarding the fourth cup that he recites a blessing and not regarding the third? One may resolve that the Rosh of blessed memory holds that for the third cup, if he first drank without heseiba, he does not recite Borei Pri HaGafen when he returns and drinks, since his intention is to drink the fourth cup; and according to the Rosh over the fourth cup he does not recite Borei Pri HaGafen; see siman 474; the same applies when he returns and drinks the third cup. And what he wrote initially, between third and fourth one should not return and drink: the reason is that between third and fourth he should not drink, as the Agudah wrote; and after the fourth cup the reason is that it is like adding, for he would need to recite Borei Pri HaGafen; and if so the Taz's challenge is well placed, why he did not give a shared reason for both. And see Magen Avraham 7.
