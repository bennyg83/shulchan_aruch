He needs heseiba. And even before his master; for although he is always enslaved to busy himself with household needs, nevertheless he is obligated to show freedom on the night of Pesach and to eat at least a kezayit of matzah and a kezayit of afikoman and the four cups with heseiba. And the same applies to a worker who has a student to teach him a craft: the student is obligated in heseiba; and likewise Hebrew slaves are obligated in heseiba at least for the first kezayit, like an attendant; and regarding a Canaanite slave, see the later authorities.
