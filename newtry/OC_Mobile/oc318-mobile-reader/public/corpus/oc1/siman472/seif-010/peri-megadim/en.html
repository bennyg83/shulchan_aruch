Even. See Taz; see siman 272 in Magen Avraham 5: raisin-water specifically when there is some moisture; and it is proper to pursue genuine wine even with effort. And what is written, whoever is exempt and does [the act] is called a hedyot: see Magen Avraham 6; and in Chok Yaakov he answered like sukkah, where he has distress, etc. However, whenever one wishes to be stringent like one opinion even though the law follows the other, he is not called a hedyot.
