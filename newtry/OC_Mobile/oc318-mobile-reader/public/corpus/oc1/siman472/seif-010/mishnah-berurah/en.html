Because it harms him. That is to say, that he suffers in drinking it and his head hurts from this; and this does not include [only] when he would become bedridden from this.
To fulfill the mitzvah of, etc. Which is not so on Shabbosos and Yamim Tovim, where he can hear Kiddush from another and fulfill thereby; but here the obligation of drinking falls upon every person.
Four cups. And he may dilute it well; however we require that it still be fit for Kiddush [see above siman 272, s.k. 16, in Mishnah Berurah]; and he may also take raisin wine or chamar medinah.
