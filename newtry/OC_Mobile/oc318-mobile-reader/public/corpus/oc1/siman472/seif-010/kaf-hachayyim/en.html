With beautiful utensils, etc. Throughout the year it is proper to refrain from displaying beautiful utensils, reducing ostentation in remembrance of the destruction, except on the Seder nights. The Torah requires one to display freedom then. Even utensils belonging to non-Jews that one holds as collateral may be placed upon a special table so that one can rejoice upon seeing them.
