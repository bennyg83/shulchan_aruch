And likewise regarding eating matzah, etc. And ideally he should recline during the whole meal (Rama); and the Taz raised a difficulty from Pesachim folio 108: an attendant who ate a kezayit of matzah while reclining has fulfilled; evidently another person, even after the fact, has not fulfilled when he does not recline during the whole meal; and the answer is forced; see there. And to me it appears that an attendant, even when he does not recline for the four cups, has fulfilled; and also even if he did not recline for afikoman; and also even if there was no first kezayit; see se'if 6 and examine well.
