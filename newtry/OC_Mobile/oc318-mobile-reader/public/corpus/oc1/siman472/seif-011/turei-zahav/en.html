After red wine. For it is written, "Do not look at wine when it reddens"; learn from this that reddish [wine] is a higher quality; and the Tur was particular here regarding the four cups about this, and not regarding Kiddush in siman 272, for there is a further allusion to red as a remembrance of the blood when Pharaoh would slaughter the children of Israel; and nowadays they refrained from taking red wine because of false blood-libels, for our many iniquities.
