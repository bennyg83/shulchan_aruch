(Seif katan 11) That not, etc. As written in siman 612, there is a dispute regarding combining drinking: some say that if he delayed longer than the time of drinking a revi’is, it does not combine, and some say its measure is like eating, namely the time of eating a pras. Therefore initially he should not delay longer than the time of drinking a revi’is, to satisfy all opinions, even with the later cups, where it appears as though he is adding to the cups. For although if he ate without reclining it was written above that with the later cups he should not drink again because it appears as though he is adding, that is because of the reason the Rama wrote there, that in such a case we rely on the Raavyah, who holds that in our time reclining is not required. But where he delayed longer than the time of eating a pras, according to all opinions it does not combine and there is no one upon whom to rely. Therefore we are not concerned that it appears as though he is adding, according to what was written in seif 7 in the gloss. According to us, with the first cup this applies specifically when he initially had in mind to drink between the cups, as the Magen Avraham wrote in seif katan 7.
He should drink most of a revi’is, and according to the Bach mentioned above, initially he should drink the revi’is all at once.
