(א) (s.k. 11) Not in order, etc. — as written siman 472 regarding combining drinking: one view is that if he waited more than the time of drinking a revi'it it does not combine; and another view is that its measure is like eating, meaning the time of eating a peras; therefore l'chatchila he should not wait more than the time of drinking a revi'it to fulfill all views, even in the last cups — for it appears like adding to the cups, since if he ate without reclining he wrote above that in the last cups he should not return and drink, because it appears like adding — meaning from the reason Rama wrote there that in such a case we rely on Ravyah who holds that in our time reclining is not required. But when he waited more than the time of a peras, all agree it does not combine and there is no one to rely on — therefore we are not concerned with what appears like adding, per what is written in seif 7 in the gloss. And for us, specifically in the first cup — for his initial intent was to drink between the cups, as Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 7.
