Most of it. The Bach explains: initially he should drink the entire cup, or after the fact, most of it. In countries where wine is expensive, every person should be careful to take a cup containing a revi'is and drink all of it, or after the fact, most of it. See Chok Yaakov, Taz to siman 190, seif katan 3, and siman 487, seif katan 1.
