A poor person. Magen Avraham writes: It appears to me that one who has only exactly four cups after dilution should use all of them on the first night. He should not use two on the first night for Kiddush and Birkas HaMazon and reserve two for the second night, because the first day is primary. A lamp for his home takes precedence over the four cups because of domestic peace. The Taz writes: It appears to me that whenever it is stated that one need not recline, it is forbidden to recline, for anyone exempt from a matter who nevertheless performs it is called a commoner. Mahari, Maharshal, and Magen Avraham write likewise. Be'er Sheva writes that the matter requires consideration, for we often find that people act stringently in matters from which they are exempt. See Chok Yaakov, seif katan 10. One who is sent wine for the four cups and for Kiddush may not divert it to another use. Chavos Yair, siman 252.
