And it appears to me, if he did not drink, etc. That is to say, between the first cups, where it is permitted to drink as much as he wishes, as is proven below in siman (480) [473] (for regarding them we do not say that it appears like adding to the cups), he must return and drink if he did not do heseiba, for even if it is only optional drinking it is also permitted; which is not so if he forgot to recline for the later cups: he should not return and drink, for optional drinking is forbidden there because it appears like adding to the cups; and if so, since according to Ravyah one need not recline, consequently it is optional drinking, which is forbidden between the later cups.
Without a blessing. Magen Avraham wrote that all this is according to the main law, that permission is in his hand to drink several cups; but according to our practice that we do not drink any cup even between the first cups, as clarified in siman 473, se'if 3, it is like one who changed his mind, and if he drinks he must recite a blessing. And he further added that according to this, even between the first and the second it is not worthwhile that he return and drink, for since he recites a blessing over it, it appears like adding another cup to the cups [as clarified in the Tur, siman 473, in the name of Avi HaEzri]; and therefore he should not return and drink, and he should rely on the view of Avi HaEzri as with the later cups. However, if he forgot and did not recline for the second cup, he should return and drink with heseiba without a blessing, for it is not like one who changed his mind, since also during the meal if he wishes to drink he need not recite a blessing and relies on the blessing of the second cup. And it is proper that before he recites a blessing over the first cup his intention be to return and drink between the first cups, and then even if he errs and drinks the first cup without heseiba he can drink another cup with heseiba and without a blessing, and it will not appear like adding.
And likewise regarding eating matzah. That is, he should return and eat the kezayit of matzah, and without a blessing; however regarding afikoman, if he forgot to eat it with heseiba he should not return and eat it, for it is forbidden to eat afikoman twice.
The whole meal. In his eating and in his drinking; and after the fact he has fulfilled at the time of eating a kezayit of matzah and the four cups; and regarding an attendant, since he is preoccupied we judge him as after the fact relative to every person.
