(Seif katan 7) For it appears, etc. And he must recite a blessing, etc. Since our custom is to recite a blessing over each of the four cups, the blessing he recites over the additional cup causes it to appear as though he is adding to the cups. This is not so when he need not recite a blessing over the additional cup, for then it does not appear as though he is adding, since it is not comparable to the four cups over which blessings are recited. So too it is in the Tur, siman 473. Therefore, if at the beginning, etc., he should drink again without a blessing even according to us, and he fulfills the law of reclining according to all opinions.
