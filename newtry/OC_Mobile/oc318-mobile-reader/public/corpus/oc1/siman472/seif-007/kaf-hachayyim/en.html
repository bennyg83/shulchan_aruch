7) If one ate matzah during twilight, one did not fulfill the obligation and must eat again at night. Whether to recite the blessing over eating matzah at night requires consideration. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 1. One should therefore intend to fulfill the blessing through another person, and that person should intend to include this individual in the blessing. See above, siman 213, se'if 3, and our words there, with the help of Heaven.
