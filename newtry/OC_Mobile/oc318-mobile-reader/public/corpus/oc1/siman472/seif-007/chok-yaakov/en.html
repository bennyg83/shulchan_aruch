Or he drank without reclining. In the Gemara there is a doubt about which of the cups requires reclining, and the Gemara concludes: now that this and that were said, both require reclining. See the Kesef Mishneh, chapter 7 of Hilchos Chametz, who worked to answer in several ways why we do not say in such a case that a D’Rabbanan doubt is ruled leniently. According to what I wrote in my work Minchas Yaakov, klal 1, seif katan 6, see below there.
For it appears as though he is adding. Although it is not an actual addition, for one can say that it became clarified that the first was not counted among the cups, as the Rosh wrote in Erev Pesachim, nevertheless one may rely on the Raavyah in this, since it appears as though he is adding. With this the brevity and contradiction that the Taz raises against the Rama is resolved. In the glosses to the Semak and the Maharil, they rule like the view of the Rav here. However, the Bach in siman 479 ruled that with all four cups, if one drank them without reclining, he fulfilled his obligation, and this appears to me to be primary. The Agudah in Perek Erev Pesachim wrote likewise. And the Magen Avraham wrote that it appears to him that at the beginning of reclining one should have in mind to drink again between the first cups, and then if he erred and drank without reclining, he should drink again without a blessing. These are his words.
And likewise when eating matzah. See the rulings of Mahari at the end of siman 122, that from the Rambam, Semag, Rosh, Mordechai, and Hagahos Maimoniyos, from all of them we learn that the first kezayis of HaMotzi does not require reclining. See below, siman 475, seif katan 7.
And initially he should recline. Throughout the whole meal. But the statement that after the fact he fulfilled the obligation at the time of eating a kezayis of matzah and drinking the four cups implies that even initially he need recline only when eating the kezayis of matzah and drinking the four cups. This is also the implication of the Gemara, and so the Bach wrote. Although the Taz challenged him, his words are not compelling at all.
