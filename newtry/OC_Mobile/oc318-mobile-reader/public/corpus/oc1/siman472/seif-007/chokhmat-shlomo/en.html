Se'if 7. Anyone who needs heseiba, if he ate or drank without heseiba, he has not fulfilled, etc. Marginal note: regarding the law of heseiba for the four cups, see in Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 1 of Megillah, what he raised as a contradiction in the words of the Ran against one another; and see what I wrote in the omissions to the sefer Shenos Chayim on siman 153, what I wrote to resolve this correctly with Hashem's help; and it is in my handwritten work on the omissions; see there carefully and examine well.
