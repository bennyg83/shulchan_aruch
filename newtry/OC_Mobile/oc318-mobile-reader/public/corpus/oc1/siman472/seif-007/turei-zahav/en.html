(א) And it appears to me if he did not drink, etc. — meaning that for the first two cups there is permission to drink between them and it is not like adding; and this distinction exists in Ashrei chapter Arvei Pesachim, except that he concluded on this; nevertheless one may say that since he drank not per their enactment, the matter was clarified that it was not from the number of cups, and what he drinks now is a cup of obligation; and if he drank the fourth cup without reclining he must bless borei peri hagafen on a cup he will drink afterward that he will drink with reclining — for when he drank the fourth cup without reclining and was not careful to recline, he turned his mind to drink more — until here; and so in Shulchan Aruch siman 479. One may say here on Rama: in any case, if he holds this reasoning that the matter was clarified that they were not from the number — why did he write it appears like adding to the cups, for it is not adding; and if he does not hold thus, and necessarily the reason is because b'dieved he fulfilled without reclining, for one may rely on Ravyah per the second view — if so why did he give a reason of adding, which is not necessary, for one may say the matter was clarified; he should have given a better reason, namely that since he already fulfilled with those two cups he drank without reclining, it is forbidden to drink, as we learned between third and fourth he should not drink, and similarly after the fourth he should not drink — not from concern of adding to cups, but lest he become drunk, as written siman 479. It turns out there is contradiction or abbreviation in Rama's words here.
