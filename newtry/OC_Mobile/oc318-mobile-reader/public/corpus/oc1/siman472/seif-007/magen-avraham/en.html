That it appears like adding. Meaning: since it is forbidden to drink between them, therefore initially he had no intention to drink, and he must recite a blessing, and it is like adding; but for the first [cups], since it is permitted to drink between them, therefore his intention was upon it and he need not recite a blessing. And it appears to me that nowadays, when it is not the practice to drink between the first [cups], therefore it is like one who changed his mind, and if he drinks he must recite a blessing, and it is like adding; therefore he should not return and drink, and he should rely on Ravyah; and so is implied in the Tur, siman 473; and so wrote the Bach. But for the second cup he should return and drink, for even if he drinks wine during the meal, the second cup exempts him, unless he had no intention to drink during the meal. And it appears to me that at the beginning of heseiba his intention should be to return and drink between the first [cups], and then if he erred and drank without heseiba he should return and drink without a blessing.
