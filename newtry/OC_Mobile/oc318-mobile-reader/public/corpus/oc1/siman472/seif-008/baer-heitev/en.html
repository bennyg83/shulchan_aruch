Matzah. See Piskei Mahari, at the end of siman 122, who writes that from Rambam, Semag, Rosh, Mordechai, and Hagahos Maimoniyos it emerges that the first olive-sized portion of HaMotzi does not require reclining. However, the Tur implies that both olive-sized portions require reclining, and this is the custom. See Chok Yaakov, siman 475, seif katan 7.
