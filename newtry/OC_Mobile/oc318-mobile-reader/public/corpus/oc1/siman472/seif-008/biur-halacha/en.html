(ב) Not in order — he did not fulfill — see Magen Avraham who wrote that "in order" means when he recites the Haggadah in between, per Rashbam; and it appears at first glance that when he did not recite the Haggadah in between, even if he waited between cup and cup, it is called not in order and he did not fulfill. However from the wording of the Gemara it appears there is concern only when he drinks them at once — that is, either per Rashi's explanation at one time, or at least per other commentators even in this case they are consecutive; but if he waited in between, although he certainly transgressed what he did not connect recitation of the Haggadah to each cup as the Sages enacted, nevertheless b'dieved he did not lose the cups. I found a dispute on this between Beit Yosef and Peri Chadash — see Beit Yosef in siman 484 regarding Birkat HaMazon, and his view there is that b'dieved he fulfilled, as we wrote; and Peri Chadash disagrees there on him from here that when he did not say in order he did not fulfill.
