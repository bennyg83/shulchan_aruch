His teacher permission, etc. The Taz raised a difficulty: if so, what does the Gemara challenge in Pesachim folio 108 from the beraisa "even a student with his teacher": perhaps it deals when he gave permission? And he answered, and these are his words: one may say that the wording of the beraisa indicates an obligation of heseiba, and when permission is taken there is no obligation at all on the student; and from this it is proven that there is no obligation on the teacher to give permission, for if not so, why not establish it thus that there is an obligation on the teacher to give permission? Until here his words. And it is difficult: for even without this it is clear in the Gemara that it is not an obligation, for Rav Yosef said to his students, "You do not need [it]"; and one may say that I would have thought to distinguish that a teacher who is not primary is obligated to give permission, therefore it was proven that since it does not establish it thus, evidently even when he is not primary it is not an obligation. One may further say that in any case Rav Yosef gave them permission, and nevertheless said "You do not need [it]," for even when he gives permission, nevertheless it is not an obligation for the students.
