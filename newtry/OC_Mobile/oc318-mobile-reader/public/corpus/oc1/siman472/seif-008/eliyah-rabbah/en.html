[8] Teacher's permission, etc. Taz challenged: if so, what is the Gemara's question in Pesachim 108 from the braita "even a student with his teacher" — perhaps it deals when he gave permission? He answered — these are his words: one may say that the wording of the braita teaches obligation of reclining, and with taking permission there is no obligation on the student; and it is proven from this that there is no obligation on the teacher to give permission — for if not, why not establish it thus that there is obligation on the teacher to give permission? — until here his words. It is difficult: for in any case it is clear in the Gemara that it is not an obligation — Rav Yosef said to his students "you do not need to"; and one may say we would have thought to distinguish that a teacher who is not primary is obligated to give permission — therefore it was proven that since it does not establish it thus, evidently even when not primary it is not an obligation. One may further say that Rav Yosef in any case gave them permission, and nevertheless said "you do not need to," for even when he gives permission, nevertheless it is not an obligation for the students.
