Fine. The Arizal wrote that one should wear four white garments, an outer garment, an inner garment, a belt, and a shirt, corresponding to the four letters of Yud-Hei-Vav-Hei. Sefer Chassidim writes that on Shabbos a person should not take a child onto his lap until he first places a cushion there. The Maharil had a special tallis for Shabbos and folded it on Motzei Shabbos. The Agudah, siman 143, writes that one should remain in Shabbos clothing until after Havdalah on Motzei Shabbos. The writings on kabbalistic intentions state that even a mourner should change his garments.
