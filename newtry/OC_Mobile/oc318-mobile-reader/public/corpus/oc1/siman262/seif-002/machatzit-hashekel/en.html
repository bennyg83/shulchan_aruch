Se'if katan 2: He should endeavor, etc. See siman 302, se'if 3, which implies that it is permitted to remove one's Shabbos clothing. It states there that garments may be folded only for the needs of Shabbos. That is, their practice was to fold garments after removing them so that their creases would not flatten, and this was permitted only when one intended to wear them again on Shabbos. Thus it is evident that it is permitted to remove Shabbos garments on Shabbos. The Magen Avraham answers that the case there concerns one who has, etc. He could likewise have answered that it concerns nighttime, when one removes the garments before going to sleep.
For even a mourner, etc. This means that although a mourner is forbidden to wear Shabbos garments, as stated in Yoreh De'ah, siman 389 and siman 400, he should nevertheless change and wear a garment different from the one he ordinarily wears during the week.
