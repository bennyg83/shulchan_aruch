When he comes. From the synagogue. See Baer Heitev. See Machazik Berachah, who writes in the name of Tosefes Shabbos that a certain great authority deleted the stanza 'Depart in peace' from the text of Shalom Aleichem, and he agreed with him. This is incorrect. Its meaning is that whenever the angels depart, their departure should be in peace; it does not mean that he dismisses them from his presence and sends them away. Since he does not know when they will depart, he completes the statement now. Nothing practiced by the majority of Israel should be deleted. However, the prayer beginning 'Master of the worlds' should not be recited; see there. See She'eilas Yaavetz, siman 125. The custom is to recite 'Master of the worlds' only until 'the perfect King, Whose way is perfect,' since that portion consists solely of praise.
