He should endeavor. It is proper for him to have a separate tallis for Shabbos. Yosef Ometz. The writings state that it is proper not to wear on a weekday anything that he wore on Shabbos, even a shirt. The Agudah, siman 143, writes that one should remain in Shabbos clothing until after Havdalah on Motzei Shabbos. See siman 302, se'if 3, which implies that it is permitted to remove them. One can say that the case there is where he has a special undergarment for Shabbos. The writings on kabbalistic intentions state that even a mourner should change his garments.
