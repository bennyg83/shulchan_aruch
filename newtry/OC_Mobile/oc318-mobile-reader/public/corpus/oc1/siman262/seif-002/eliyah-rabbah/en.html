[b] [dressed] by two angels, etc. Commentary that Zedek is used at the last hour of Shabbat Eve and his angel Zedkiel, and Mars is used at the first hour of Shabbat night and his angel Samal, and both are found to be harming each other (Meta Moshe) and refer to the answer from the Rial Siman 63.
