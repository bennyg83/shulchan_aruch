Levush: Two angels, etc. This means that the planet Jupiter serves during the final hour of erev Shabbos, and its angel is Tzidkiel, while Mars serves during the first hour of Friday night, and its angel is Samael. Thus, the two encounter one another. Mateh Moshe. See Responsa Maharil, siman 163. The Akeidah explains that among the supernal angels there is no evil at all; rather, these are the two groups represented by the good inclination and the evil inclination, etc.
