He should endeavor, etc. For it is written, 'And you shall honor it,' and our Sages expounded that one's Shabbos clothing should not be like one's weekday clothing. It is proper not to wear on Shabbos anything that he wore during the week, even a shirt. If possible, it is also proper for him to have a separate tallis for Shabbos.
Fine. According to his means. Even if he is traveling alone or staying in a non-Jew's home, he should wear Shabbos clothing, because clothing is not worn in honor of those who see him, but in honor of Shabbos. Chayei Adam. Sefer Chassidim writes that on Shabbos a person should not take a child onto his lap until he first places a cushion there, so that the child will not soil his clothing.
Those who remain at home. This means those who do not need to lift their garments from the ground because of work.
