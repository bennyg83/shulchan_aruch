To Sanctify Shabbat With a Set Table and Clean Clothes, 3 Seifim: One should set
his table and take his cushions and arrange the appearances of the house, so that we should find it arranged and ordered when he returns from Synagogue.Rema: It is that our tables are kept set all of Shabbat and such is the custom and one shouldn't change it. (Tur; Mordechai; Hagahos Maimoni).
