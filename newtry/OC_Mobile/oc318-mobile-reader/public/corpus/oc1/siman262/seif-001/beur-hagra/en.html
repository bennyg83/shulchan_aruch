Se'if 1: And he shall prepare, etc. This follows Tosafos there, s.v. 'and found.' For 'set' refers to another place, and therefore the reason is so that he will find it, etc.
And it shall be. Therefore, in the tenth chapter of Pesachim, folio 100b, it mentions only the covering over the bread, according to Tosafos' explanation in the name of some commentators, as a remembrance of the manna.
