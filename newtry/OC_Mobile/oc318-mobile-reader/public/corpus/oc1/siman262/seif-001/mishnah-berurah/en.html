His table. From erev Shabbos in preparation for Friday night.
The beds. Those upon which people sit. It is also proper that the bed upon which he sleeps be prepared.
Set, etc. For this honors Shabbos. Our Sages said that two ministering angels accompany a person on erev Shabbos from the synagogue to his home, one good and one evil. When he arrives home and finds a lamp burning, the table set, and the bed prepared, the good angel says, 'May it be God's will that it be so next Shabbos,' and the evil angel is compelled to answer amen. If it is otherwise, God forbid, the reverse occurs. It is proper to clear cobwebs from the house before nightfall, to clean the house in honor of Shabbos. At the Friday night meal one should be careful not to throw anything in the house away from the table if it is not his practice to sweep the house in a permitted manner after that meal, as discussed in siman 337, so that the house not become dirty. The same applies at the morning meal if he will not sweep afterward.
Throughout, etc., until after Havdalah. Some have the custom to place two tablecloths on the table in addition to the upper covering over the bread, because when the cloth is shaken out the table would otherwise be left uncovered.
