See the Taz: he should loosen them so that they are not folded into his belt, etc. See the Beit Yosef; these are the words of the Kol Bo, and it appears that this is also Rashi's explanation. See there. Regarding what was written that two angels accompany a person, see Eliyah Rabbah, se'if katan 2: Jupiter at the end of erev Shabbos and Mars at the beginning, etc. He also explains them as two spiritual forces; see there. Nevertheless, we should not depart from the simple meaning, namely, two actual angels. There, in se'if katan 3, he considers covering with cloths all the tables in all the rooms, even where one does not eat, and this is proper. In the room where one eats, however, all the tables in that room must be covered.
