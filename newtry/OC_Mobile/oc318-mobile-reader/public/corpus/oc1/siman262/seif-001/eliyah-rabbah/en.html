His table, etc. The Arizal was very particular to eat at a four-legged table, corresponding to the table in the Temple, and to recite the blessing over two bundles of myrtle at each of the three Kiddushim. On Friday night he placed on the table the cup used for Kiddush, with a little wine and a few crumbs, but not a whole loaf. See Yoreh De'ah at the end of siman 178. In a place where people are not accustomed to sweep on Friday night, it is forbidden to throw anything away from the table, for the house will become dirty and the good angel who accompanies him will depart. Magen Avraham. See siman 180.
