Levush: And the custom is to leave, etc. It appears to me from the Hagahos Minhagim, which states that one should not cover the table in the winter room throughout Sukkos, that likewise on Shabbos there is no need to cover tables in the other rooms, but only in the place where one eats. Some distinction may be drawn, however, because on Sukkos the reason is that one should not establish any fixed residence in the house, and this explains why on Shavuos he did cover the table in the winter room. See below, siman 639. The Maharshal had two types of cloth on the table in addition to the covering over the bread, so that when the cloth on which they ate was shaken out, the table would not be left uncovered. Mateh Moshe. The custom is to leave the table set throughout Shabbos until after Havdalah. Agudah, chapter Kol Kisvei.
