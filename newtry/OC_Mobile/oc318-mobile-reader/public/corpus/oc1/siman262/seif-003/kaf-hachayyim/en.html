3) One who cannot obtain twelve loaves should use no fewer than four, as stated in the sacred Zohar, parashas Pinchas, folio 245a; see there. He should place them two over two and intend that they correspond to the four letters of Havayah: yud over heh and vav over heh. He should break the bread and have all the aforementioned intentions. If he has only three loaves, he should place only two upon the table. Similarly, if he has five or six, he should place only four.
