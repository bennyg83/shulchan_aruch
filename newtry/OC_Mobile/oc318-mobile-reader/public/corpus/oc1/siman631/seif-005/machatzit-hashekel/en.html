(Small paragraph 5) And he placed, etc. It becomes a majority of valid s'chach; Rashi. In truth, Rashi's language is indeed so. But he wrote this according to his approach, that the Talmud needed this explanation only according to the one who holds that breached space equal to standing material is forbidden. If so, where he placed between each spit and spit a space the size of a spit and placed valid s'chach there, it would still be breached space equal to standing material. Therefore he had to say specifically that if he placed the invalid material lengthwise, he places the valid material crosswise, for then the valid material must be the majority; otherwise the valid s'chach would fall to the ground. If so, the standing material is greater than the breached space and it is valid. But according to us, who rule like Rabbeinu Tam that even breached space equal to standing material is permitted, nevertheless the Talmud still had to establish that the invalid material was placed lengthwise and the valid material crosswise for the reason written in Shulchan Aruch: that it is impossible to be exact and fill all the open space, etc. If so, even through placing lengthwise and crosswise it is only half and half; nevertheless it is valid according to us, for we rule that breached space equal to standing material is permitted. Rather, the Magen Avraham stated the truth: perforce the valid material is the majority, for otherwise it would fall, as above.
