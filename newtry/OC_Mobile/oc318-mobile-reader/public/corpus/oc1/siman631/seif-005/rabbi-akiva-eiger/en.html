Seif 5: The language of the Rambam: in the air between the upper and lower reed. And in the name of Ri'etz Giat: also in the air between the upper and lower reed. If there is reed width above, it is sufficient in the air between the lower reeds even if not in the upper — see there. The Rambam implies if placement accomplishes the mitzvah, extinguishing is not required — see Magen Avraham.
