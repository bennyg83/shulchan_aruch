A tefach. At that measure it is considered an ohel, and we apply the principle of viewing the upper covering as though it were cast down.
The sky. This means that although there is more sunlight than shade when the sun is in other positions, it is nevertheless valid if there is more shade than sunlight when the sun is in the middle of the sky. See Turei Zahav, who asks why the Gemara said to Shmuel, ‘The Mishnah taught two cases’; why did it not say that according to Shmuel too the Mishnah teaches only one case, just as it does according to Rav? See Sukkah 22a and consider this carefully.
