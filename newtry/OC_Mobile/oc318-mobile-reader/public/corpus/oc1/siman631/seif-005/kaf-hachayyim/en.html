If in most of it the shade exceeds the sunlight by two minute amounts, etc. The explanation is as follows: If we combine all the sunlight and all the shade scattered here and there throughout most of the sukkah, the shade exceeds the sunlight by two minute amounts, meaning above at the s'chach. In the minority of the sukkah, the sunlight exceeds the shade by one minute amount. It is specifically necessary that in the majority the shade exceed by two minute amounts. One minute amount is insufficient, because if the one minute amount of extra shade in the majority were offset against the one minute amount of extra sunlight in the minority, the shade and sunlight would be exactly equal, and we learned that when they are equal the sukkah is invalid. All of this concerns the measurement of shade and sunlight above at the s'chach. Prishah, ibid.
