Se'if 8: And none are four, etc. As stated on 17a, invalid sechach disqualifies a sukkah in its middle with four tefachim, as written above at the beginning of siman 632. And there: 'Come and hear: if he roofed it with boards, etc.; Rabbi Meir concedes that if, etc.; this is well according to the one who says, etc.' We rule like the one who says that in the middle the measure is four tefachim.
And none of them, etc. There, 14b: 'Two sheets combine, etc.; this is well according to Shmuel, etc.; what does combine mean? They combine to four tefachim.'
And he placed, etc. For in the first chapter of Eruvin we rule like Rav Pappa. See Tosafos there, s.v. parutz.
