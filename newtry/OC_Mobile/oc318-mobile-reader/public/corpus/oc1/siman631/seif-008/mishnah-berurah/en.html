(16) That are invalid to use as s'chach. For example, they are made of iron, which are invalid according to everyone. See above in siman 629:2.
(17) And they do not have four. For if they have four handbreadths, we rule that invalid s'chach invalidates in the middle with four handbreadths.
(18) And none of them has four in one place. Meaning, even by combining from two or from three.
(19) For it is impossible to be exact, etc. For if it were possible, it would be permitted, since we rule that breached space equal to standing material is permitted.
(20) And placed the valid material crosswise. Between each spit and spit he placed valid s'chach there across its width, and pressed it between them so that it would not fall from there to the ground.
(21) Less than three in one place. For we rule below in siman 632:1 that in a small sukkah, invalid s'chach invalidates with three handbreadths; see there.
