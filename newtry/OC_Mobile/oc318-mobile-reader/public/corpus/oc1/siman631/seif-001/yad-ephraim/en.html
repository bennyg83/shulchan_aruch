In Magen Avraham, small paragraph 3. "This was not made for them, and the same applies to a partition by means of lavud"; this is the correct reading. These words are from Rashi in Sukkah, page 19, regarding that which he said, "the partitions were made for the portico," meaning that we do not say the edge of the roof descends and seals, etc. "But if it were a proper partition we would rely upon it, etc. However, where it has posts, even though they were made inward, lavud of less than three is like a closed partition." These are his words. These are the words of the Magen Avraham here. This is why he referred to siman 630:8, namely a portico that has posts, where the edge of the roof is effective.
