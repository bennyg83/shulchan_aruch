4) [Seif 2] If in most of it the shade is greater, etc. But if in a minority of the sukkah the shade exceeds the sunlight by a great amount, while in most of it the sunlight exceeds the shade by only a slight amount, then even though the total shade throughout the entire sukkah exceeds the total sunlight, it is invalid. Rabbeinu Yeruham, responsum 58, section 1; Darkei Moshe, os 1; Prishah, os 1; Eliyah Rabbah, os 1 and 2; Mishbetzos Zahav, os 1; Mishna Berurah, os 2.
