Small paragraph 3 "The same applies to a partition by means of lavud"; this is the correct reading. His intent is that the same applies if this partition is made of reeds through lavud: it is also a partition for behind the sukkah, even though it was not made for them. He brings proof from siman 630:8 regarding a portico, according to Rashi and Tosafos, brought there briefly in Turei Zahav, small paragraph 9: we do not say that the edge of the roof descends and seals so as to be a partition for the sukkah, since the portico roof was made inward and not outward; but with posts, which are a partition through lavud, it is indeed a partition for the sukkah according to all, even though the posts were made only to be a partition for the portico. This is because lavud is a full closure, and therefore it is effective even outward. However, the Magen Avraham is speaking where lavud is effective, according to what the Magen Avraham explained at the beginning of siman 635; see there. This is straightforward. The words of the Pri Megadim on this are not correct.
