Se'if 3: It is the proper manner, etc. This is Rambam's wording, and accords with the Yerushalmi: 'This teaches that the stars must be visible through it.' Since it says the sukkah is valid, this implies that it is only valid after the fact. Rabbi Levi said in the name of Rabbi Chama bar Chanina: 'They taught: like the stars seen by day.' Rambam explains this as the large stars visible even by day, as he writes at the end of chapter 2 of Hilchos Shabbos. The baraisa's statement, 'The Sages taught: One may not, etc.,' must refer even to the initial practice.
