(Small paragraph 3) "It was not made, etc., complete," and "the same applies to a partition"; this is the correct reading.
See siman 630:8 and Magen Avraham there, small paragraph 12, for we rule like Rava that we do not say that the edge of the roof descends and seals where the roof was not made for the sake of the sukkah, but for the sake of the portico that is inside. Nevertheless, a complete partition, and the same applies through lavud, which is called a complete partition for this matter, is considered a partition even if it was not made for the sake of the sukkah, for it is superior to the edge of the roof.
