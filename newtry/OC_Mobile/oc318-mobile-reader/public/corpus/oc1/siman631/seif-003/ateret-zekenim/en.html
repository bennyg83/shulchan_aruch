Even though the stars are not visible, etc. This is because although he cannot see the stars, nevertheless by day there are holes and cracks in several places [Mahari Weil]. However, if it was so thick that rain cannot descend through it, it is invalid; and some validate it. One should be stringent initially to make the s'chach so that large stars are visible through it [the Tur, Beis Yosef, Mordechai, and Hagahos Maimoniyos]. A sukkah that is not guarded from thieves is valid [Responsa of Maharama, siman 29].
