(15) Even though he made a wall, etc. Meaning: it is obvious that if it is only seven, his intent is to use the entire sukkah, and he made it only like the enactment of the Sages [for this is its initial law, that he may make the third wall of seven handbreadths, or by means of a broad handbreadth and a form of a doorway, as above in siman 630:2, and through this he may use until the end of the long wall]. This is unlike when he made more than seven handbreadths, where perhaps he revealed his intent to make the whole sukkah with long walls, etc.
