7) Ibid., Rama's gloss: Some are stringent if the sukkah is large, etc. So wrote the Beit Yosef in the name of the Ran: It is possible that this applies specifically to a sukkah that measures only seven by seven tefachim. But concerning a very large sukkah that contains an area of seven tefachim or more whose sunlight is greater than its shade, it requires further study whether we may validate that area through its combination with the rest of the sukkah, end quote. This means that one should not sit in the portion whose sunlight is greater, but this does not invalidate the remainder, since that remainder contains the minimum dimensions of a valid sukkah. Taz, s.k. 1; Eliyah Rabbah, os 3; Beit HaShoevah, os 2; Mishna Berurah, os 4. If the invalid section extends along the entire length of a wall, so that the area whose shade is greater would have only two walls, it is possible that the validity of the entire sukkah requires further study. Mishna Berurah, os 1, and Mishna Berurah, ibid.
