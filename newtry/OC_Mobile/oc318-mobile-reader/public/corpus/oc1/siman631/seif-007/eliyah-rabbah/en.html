[7] That have no plaster upon them, etc. One should not permit removing the plaster and afterward loosening or removing one [Rabbi Levi ibn Chaviv], and this requires analysis in practice, for it appears that it is preferable [Magen Avraham]. This requires analysis, for I examined Rabbi Levi ibn Chaviv himself, and it is implied there that specifically removing one does not suffice because the plaster is invalid s'chach by Torah law and one must repair all of it. But regarding loosening, by which all of it is repaired, he agrees that it is permitted. Also regarding removing one, it appears according to his conclusion there that it is valid. See siman 626:3.
