The sides. That is to say: it is obvious that if the second, outer wall is only seven tefachim long, the portion extending beyond the long wall may be permitted, because we say that his intention was to use the entire sukkah. If, however, he made the third wall longer than seven tefachim, he has revealed his intention to construct more than the minimum ideal measure. One might therefore have said that the wall is effective only as far as he constructed it, and no farther, and that the excessively long single wall does not form part of the sukkah. The ruling teaches that even there it is valid. We regard the walls he constructed as also serving that portion, as though the short wall extended to the end of the long wall. Turei Zahav; see there.
