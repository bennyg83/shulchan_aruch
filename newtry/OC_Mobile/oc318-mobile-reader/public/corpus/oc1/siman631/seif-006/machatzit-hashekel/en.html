(Small paragraph 6) Plaster, etc. And this requires analysis in practice. Certainly mere loosening does not help, as the Ran wrote on this Mishnah, for plaster of earth is invalid s'chach in itself, as written in siman 629:1 in the gloss. But by removing the plaster and loosening the boards, it can be validated, for he performed a significant act, and it is better than where there was no plaster and he validates it by mere loosening, which is a small act.
