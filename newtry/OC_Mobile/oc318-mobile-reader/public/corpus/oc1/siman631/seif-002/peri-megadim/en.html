The stars. See Turei Zahav, and so wrote the Bach; see there. It implies that when rain does not descend, it is invalid, from the fact that he wrote that the stars are not visible, and this is also the language of the Mishnah. See Magen Avraham, letter 2, and see siman 635 in Turei Zahav, letters 2 and 3. This requires analysis from the Gemara, Sukkah 22b: if stars are not visible, it is valid; if the stars of the sun are not visible, Beis Shammai invalidate and Beis Hillel validate. How can one say that both are one measure? But one can say that large stars and sparks of the sun are one thing.
