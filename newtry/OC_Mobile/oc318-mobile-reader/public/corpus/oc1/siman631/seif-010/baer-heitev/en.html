The ten tefachim are measured vertically, not diagonally. Magen Avraham. When the condition is satisfied, the entire sukkah is valid. Turei Zahav. See Rabbeinu Asher, who writes that Rambam and Rabbi Yeshayah of Trani rule like Shmuel, while Raavad rules like Rav and Rabbi Meir, and that all these formulations ultimately amount to the same rule. This concludes his words. The matter requires precision, for there is a practical difference regarding whether the Gemara’s resolution about nullifying a ceiling is required; see there.
