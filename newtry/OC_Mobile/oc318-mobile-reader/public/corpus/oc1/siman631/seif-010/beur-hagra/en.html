Se'if 10: If it had, etc.; or he raised, etc. As Rashi explains, and as stated in the Yerushalmi cited by Rosh there. The beginning of its wording, 'it had a roof,' requires examination.
And some say, etc. According to Tosafos's explanation there. His words imply that Rambam cited above disagrees, unlike Beis Yosef.
And it must contain, etc. The minimum dimensions of a sukkah explained in siman 633:8 and 634:1. This rejects those who explain that only its floor must be seven by seven tefachim and that, once it reaches ten tefachim in height, it suffices even if the tenth tefach narrows to a point. See Tosafos there, s.v. ha'oseh, unlike Rashba, Ran, and Maggid Mishneh, who require the entire slope apart from the tefach to total only sixteen tefachim. Rather, everything depends upon the wall's height. If the wall is eighteen tefachim high, the slope must be twenty-two and one-half and the distance from the wall fourteen and one-half, where it is set one tefach away from the wall. If it is raised one tefach from the ground and the wall is seventeen high, the slope is twenty-two and one-half or more and the distance sixteen. The same proportional rule applies to every wall and distance.
It also requires, etc. In both arrangements explained above, this refers to the upper ten tefachim of the slope.
And if the entire, etc. Just as with invalid sechach extending from a sukkah, there. We also learned in Ohalos 7:2: 'All sloping tents, etc.; impurity in the tent, etc.' Rosh.
