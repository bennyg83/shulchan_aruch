Se'if 9 That the boards not be four wide. The Ritva in his novellae wrote in the name of the Ramban that even when there is not a width of four, since it was the ceiling of the house there is a decree of ceiling, and loosening does not help; only removing one from between them specifically helps, for we rule that the halachah follows Rabbi Meir in his decrees; see there. And where they are four wide, it is implied in the Ramban in Milchamos and in Ritva's novellae that even removing one does not help. Examine carefully.
