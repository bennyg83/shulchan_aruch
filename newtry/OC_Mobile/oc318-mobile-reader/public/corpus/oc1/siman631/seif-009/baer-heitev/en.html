Rashi wrote there that if he were now coming to cover with them, it would be invalid because of the decree of ceiling; but now, since this was a ceiling, and he came to perform an act to make it for the sake of sukkah, this demonstrates that he knows that the Torah said, "You shall make," and not from what was already made, etc. There is therefore no further reason to decree, etc.; see there.
