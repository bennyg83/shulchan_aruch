So that the stars can be seen through it, etc. In Derashos Maharil, Hilchos Sukkah, it is written that his teacher, Maharam Shalom, would place the s'chach very thickly, because it is impossible to place so much s'chach that no opening through the roof will be visible from within at any point. Even if one cannot see through it at night, during the day several holes and cracks can nevertheless be seen in many places, end quote. His words were cited by Darkei Moshe, os 2. See Bikkurei Yaakov, who writes that one who is lenient in accordance with this view has not lost. His words were cited by Mishna Berurah, os 5. See below, os 12.
