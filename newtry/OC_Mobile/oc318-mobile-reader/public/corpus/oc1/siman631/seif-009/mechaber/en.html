[The following applies to] a house roofed with boards that are not covered by a maazivah (i.e., a clay floor that [sometimes] overlays them), and [one desires] to make [the house] valid so that he can dwell under [the boards] for the sake of [the mitzvah of]' a sukkah, or one grabs hold of one board of a set of two and places valid s'chach in its place - all of it is valid, even the' boards which are wider than four tefachim.
