(א) Or remove one plank between two planks etc. — per what we hold later siman 632 that invalid sekhakh invalidates in the middle within four handbreadths — established e.g. two invalid in middle without four amot from wall to invalid [e.g. eight amot walls and alternating plank invalid twice each side leaving two invalid in middle] — therefore validated via bent wall [Rashi, Ran]; so Beur HaGra first view; but Bach wrote for Raavad and Tur view [and Shulchan Aruch copied them]: once one acts with planks, the remaining planks there are not invalid sekhakh and all validate when placing plank-invalid-plank-invalid throughout; but second view is Rambam who disagrees — therefore holds planks must not be four handbreadths wide whether shaking or removing one between — see there. [See Bach who brought Ramban that removing one plank suffices even if rest remain without shake etc. — Gra did not cite this view.] Here one need not prefer valid sekhakh over invalid as previous seif, since invalid sekhakh is also validated by the act of removing one between.
