And there is one who says. See Turei Zahav; I explained it in the previous letter. The Levush concluded: one should be stringent. See Eliyah Rabbah and Bach. Now the Levush wrote three reasons for the invalidity of a ceiling: first, that rain does not descend into it [see Magen Avraham, letter 2]; second, an ordinary ceiling has earth plaster and is not fit for s'chach [as in siman 629:1]; third, "You shall make," and not from what was already made; see there, and see siman 626 in Magen Avraham, letter 1, and siman 636. According to this, where one loosens or removes one from between them, according to the view that if rain does not descend it is invalid, here too it is invalid, and nullifying the ceiling does not help for this matter.
