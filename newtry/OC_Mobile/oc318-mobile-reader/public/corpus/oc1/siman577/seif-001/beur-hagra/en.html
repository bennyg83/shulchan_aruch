(1) Seif 1 — in every place, etc. As written — and in exile they sound trumpets for it, as Tur wrote, because rain is difficult for it; and as written, that it not be, etc. — this is an additional reason; and so too if the rains are excessive, etc., as written, the Sages taught: and I will give, etc. — all this when rains are abundant, etc.

(ב) And if the matter [is urgent] etc., that there is no [time] etc. As stated in the Tosefta: a city etc., and upon all of them an individual etc., R' Yehuda Ish Amra etc.; and therefore it is logical only regarding an individual, but the congregation — all agree that they do not fast. And the Ran wrote in the mishnah there that since every warning is in Usha as stated there 19b, and what is their hand in Usha, and all this is because the danger is not within one day; but a city etc. where the danger is within one day and they needed to fast consecutively because the matter is urgent — one does not decree upon the congregation every day but only warns, as stated in the Tosefta and in Shulchan Aruch siman 571 seif 3: a city etc. And the reason is because they need to fast consecutively, and this is what the Gemara says: and upon all of them an individual is permitted etc., but not the congregation, and all the more so according to R' Yehuda.
