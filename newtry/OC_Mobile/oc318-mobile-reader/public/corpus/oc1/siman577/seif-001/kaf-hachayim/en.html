(1) [Seif 1] In every place, if rains are abundant, etc. — thus wrote Tur: they pray for every trouble that should not come upon them, except for abundant rain, for one does not pray over abundance of good; and this is in a mountainous land that needs much rain; but a lowland country — abundant rain is difficult for it, and moreover houses collapse from it — you have no greater trouble than this, and they pray over it — end of his words; and so Levush. And this is what Shulchan Aruch wrote: in every place, if rains are abundant, etc. — meaning even in a mountainous land that needs much rain, if rains are abundant and blur the ground and prevent plowing, they sound trumpets, etc.
