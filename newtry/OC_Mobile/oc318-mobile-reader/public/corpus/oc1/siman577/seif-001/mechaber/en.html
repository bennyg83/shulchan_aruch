If rains increased until they blur the ground — how do we conduct ourselves. And it has one
seif: In every place, if abundant rains blur the ground and prevent plowing, they sound trumpets and seek mercy. And if the matter is urgent and there is no time to sound as the order of other fasts, they do not decree a communal fast upon the congregation, for the congregation cannot fast every day; rather they sound every day and in every prayer, and with verbal sounding — that is, in our distress, Our Father answer us; one says: May it be Your will before You, Adonai our Elokeinu and Elokeinu of our fathers, that Your mercies subdue Your anger, and Your mercies prevail over Your attributes, and You deal with Your children with the attribute of mercy, and enter for them within the line of the law; and in Your great goodness have mercy on them, for punishment comes to the world only on account of Israel, and they are Your people and Your inheritance whom You brought out of Egypt, and they cannot receive neither much evil nor much good — You bestowed upon them much good and they cannot receive; You were angry with them and they cannot receive; may it be Your will that there be relief in the world, and remember Your mercies.
