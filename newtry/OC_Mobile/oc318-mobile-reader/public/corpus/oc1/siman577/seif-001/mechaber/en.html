If rains become so excessive that they muddy the ground and prevent plowing, how should one act? This siman contains 1 seif. In every place, if excessive rains muddy the ground and prevent plowing, they sound an alarm and plead for mercy. If the matter is urgent and there is no time to follow the ordinary sequence of communal fasts, they do not impose a communal fast upon the congregation, because the congregation cannot fast every day. Rather, they sound the alarm every day and at every prayer, and they cry out verbally in their distress: "Our Father, answer us." They say: "May it be Your will before You, Hashem our God and God of our fathers, that Your mercy subdue Your anger, that Your mercy prevail over Your attributes, that You deal with Your children through the attribute of mercy and enter for them beyond the strict line of judgment. In Your great goodness have mercy upon them, for punishment comes upon the earth only because of Israel, and they are Your people and Your inheritance whom You brought out of Egypt. They are unable to bear either an excess of evil or an excess of good. You bestowed much good upon them and they could not bear it; You became angry with them and they could not bear it. May it be Your will that there be relief upon the earth, and remember Your mercies."
