Seif 1, what they are accustomed to do, etc. Because of the ways of the Emorites; and see Rashba, siman 395.
And there are, etc. See Rashi at the end of chapter 8 of Shabbos, 81b.
And they are accustomed, etc. For its name is gever; and see the end of the first chapter of Yoma.
A remembrance, etc. See in chapter HaNizakin.
And for a pregnant woman they take, etc. For we rule that a fetus is like its mother's thigh. Rabbi Yochanan, who holds in Temurah 25a that a fetus is not like its mother's thigh, was refuted there. So too is the sugya at the end of the second chapter of Gittin, and so too at the beginning of the fifth chapter of Bava Kama: with regard to a cow that damaged, he collects from its offspring; what is the reason, etc. So too in the Mishnah in Arachin 7a; and see Rashi there, s.v. Ein Mamtinin, etc., and in the Gemara there: it is obvious, it is her body, etc. See also Tosafos, Bava Kama 47a, s.v. What is the reason, etc. So the Rosh rules there, and in Toras HaBayis 53a and in Mishmeres HaBayis there, unlike the Raah and Rabbeinu Tam. And see Tosafos, Sanhedrin 80b, s.v. A fetus, etc. The Magen Avraham wrote that the reason is because two bodies achieve atonement through one atonement, as stated in Menachos regarding all offerings except for a flour-offering, concerning which it says nefesh. This does not appear correct, for a voluntary offering is not comparable.
And they choose, etc. See Turei Zahav and Magen Avraham, and as written in the first chapter of Avodah Zarah.
And they practiced. As it is written, Redeem your sins through tzedakah.
Or to redeem them, etc. See Magen Avraham and Turei Zahav, and see in the first chapter of Chagigah.
And there are places, etc. As written at the beginning of the second chapter of Taanis.
And to increase, etc. As it is written, Is this the fast that I choose, etc.; is it not to break your bread for the hungry, etc.; the entire passage. Shabbos 156b; Rosh Hashanah 16b.
And one should place it adjacent, etc. As written at the beginning of the third chapter of Zevachim and in the sixth chapter of Berachos.
And they throw, etc. See the seventh chapter of Chullin.
