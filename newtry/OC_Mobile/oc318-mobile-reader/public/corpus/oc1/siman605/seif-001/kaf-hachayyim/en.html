1) [Seif 1] Regarding the custom of performing kapparos on erev Yom Kippur by slaughtering a rooster, etc. So wrote the Tur in the name of the Geonim. Regarding why roosters are taken rather than a domesticated or wild animal, he wrote there that the reason is because a rooster is more commonly found in the home than any domesticated animal, wild animal, or bird. Furthermore, a rooster is preferable because its name is gever, as we say in Yoma 20a: What is meant by the proclamation of the gever? Rav Sheila said: The call of the rooster. Since its name is gever, the substitution of one gever for another is effective and beneficial, see there.
