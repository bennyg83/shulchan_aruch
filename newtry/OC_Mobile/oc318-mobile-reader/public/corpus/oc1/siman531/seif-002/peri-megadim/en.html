And one who shaves the whole head on Chol HaMoed: it appears at first glance that there is no Torah prohibition, only rabbinic, so that he not be unkempt; and see Beis Yosef in what he wrote on the unresolved inquiry regarding a lost object: according to the view that melacha of Chol HaMoed is according to the Torah, one can say a full melacha is forbidden, namely shaving the whole head; and see Eliyah Rabbah 1 and [siman] 530 in Magen Avraham, that according to the Ramban whatever is not a need of the Moed, a full melacha is forbidden; and one can say shaving the whole head is not so much beautification; or a melacha that one could do before Yom Tov, even for food preparation some say a full melacha is forbidden; and "unkempt" is only [after] thirty days, similar to growing locks; and what Shulchan Aruch wrote, that it is a mitzvah to shave on erev ha-regel, is not explicit in the Gemara; and see Magen Avraham 10, and God willing there it will be explained further, for one can say "so that he not enter the festival unkempt" means more than thirty days.
