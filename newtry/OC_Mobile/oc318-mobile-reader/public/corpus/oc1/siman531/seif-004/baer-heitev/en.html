On the festival. And Bach ruled specifically where they released him before the festival and the days of his nidui were not completed until the festival, for our nidui is not less than seven days; but if he did not ask that they release him until the festival, it is not considered ones [unavoidable circumstance].
On the festival. And in this it appears that even though he could have had it released before the festival, he is not called negligent, for it was his intention to fulfill his vow and his neziros. Magen Avraham.
The sea. [Meaning] that he did not arrive in a settled place until Chol HaMoed (Beis Yosef; Rabbeinu Yerucham). This implies that if he was on erev Yom Tov in a settled place, it is forbidden to shave on Chol HaMoed. And it appears to me that if he was two or three days before Yom Tov in a settled place, and on erev Yom Tov he was not in a settled place, it is permitted to shave on Chol HaMoed; however, specifically when he comes from overseas, for then the matter becomes public knowledge, but not by another route. Magen Avraham.
