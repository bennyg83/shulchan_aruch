[4] [Levush:] And he changed his mind, etc. He wrote this because the Beit Yosef wrote that it is implied from the Rif and Rambam, since they did not bring the Yerushalmi, and so ruled the Magen Avraham. But I say that they did not see the Rambam's Commentary to the Mishnah, where he explicitly explained like the Yerushalmi, that it is prohibited where he changed his mind, and there is no permission except where he did not find a sage before the festival or did not find an opening. So ruled Bartenura there, and so ruled the Bach. Darkei Moshe also wrote that the Rosh, Tur, and Ri ruled like the Yerushalmi. We also do not leave the Yerushalmi and the poskim mentioned above because of an implication in the Rif and Rambam. So too ruled Sefer Zikaron by the Gaon R. Yisrael HaKohen.
