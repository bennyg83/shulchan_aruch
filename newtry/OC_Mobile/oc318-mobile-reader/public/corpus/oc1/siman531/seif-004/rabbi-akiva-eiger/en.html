Se'if 4: And these may shave. And a mourner for his father or his mother whose time for ge'arah [friends rebuking him to cut his hair] falls on Chol HaMoed: see Kenesses HaGedolah; and Responsa Noda BiYehuda, Orach Chaim section, siman 14; and Responsa Shevisas Yom Tov, Yoreh De'ah section, siman 12.
