A minor. See Taz: it implies whoever is not thirteen years old; and Beis Shmuel, one can say it is permitted to shave on the Moed, for he is not obligated in mitzvos; and even though he is [subject to] chinuch by rabbinic [law], even for rabbinic mitzvos, as implied in several places, nevertheless the reason is only because of unkempt appearance; and furthermore it is a penalty, and we do not penalize his son because of his father who did not cut his hair before the festival; and see Magen Avraham 9, that if he appears like an adult, one can say he should shave privately, not publicly; whereas a minor born with much hair before the festival: in this even publicly it is permitted; and see Rambam of blessed memory, chapter 7 of Hilchos Yom Tov.
