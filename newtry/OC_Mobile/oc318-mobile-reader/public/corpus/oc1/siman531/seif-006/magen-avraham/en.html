A minor, etc. For the reason of not entering the Moed looking unkempt does not apply to him, since he is not obligated in mitzvos. And Nimukei Yosef wrote, and specifically when he has much hair and is distressed, end quote. And specifically for a minor it is permitted to cut [hair], for it is a need of the festival; but for a non-Jew it is forbidden to cut [hair], for it is no better than other melachos, which it is forbidden to do on the Moed unless he has nothing to eat; see above, siman 542.
Publicly it is permitted. For all see that he is a minor; and it is possible that regarding a minor who appears like an adult, it is forbidden to cut [hair] publicly.
