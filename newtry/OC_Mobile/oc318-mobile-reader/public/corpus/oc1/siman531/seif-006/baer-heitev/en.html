A minor. For the reason of not entering the Moed looking unkempt does not apply to him, since he is not obligated in mitzvos. And Nimukei Yosef wrote specifically when there is much hair and he is distressed, see there. And specifically for a minor it is permitted to cut [hair], for it is a need of the festival; but for a non-Jew it is forbidden to cut [hair], for it is no better than other melachos, which it is forbidden to do on the Moed unless he has nothing to eat; see above, siman 542, se'if 1. It is permitted to defer the celebration of the haircut that they make for a minor until the Moed, and they cut via a Yisrael barber (Gan HaMelech, siman 62).
Publicly. And it is possible that regarding a minor who appears like an adult, it is forbidden to cut his hair publicly. Magen Avraham.
