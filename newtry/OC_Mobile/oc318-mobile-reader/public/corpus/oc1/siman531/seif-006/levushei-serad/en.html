s.k. siman 548 seif 8. His intent is to explain the Mechaber's words in siman 548 seif 1 — it is clear that when the seventh day falls on Yom Tov, since part of the day is like its entirety, it is permitted to cut hair on Yom Tov; and if so, if he did not shave on Yom Tov it is forbidden to shave on Chol HaMoed — it teaches here that if it was Shabbat so he could not shave on Yom Tov, it is permitted to shave on Chol HaMoed.
