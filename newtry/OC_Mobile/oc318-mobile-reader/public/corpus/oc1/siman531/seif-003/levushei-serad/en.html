Magen Avraham s.k. 2: See se'if 4; that is to say, from se'if 4 it is also proven that one who comes from overseas is permitted even if he did not arrive in a settled place until Chol HaMoed, as Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 6; if so, he had no barber on erev Yom Tov, and even so they permitted only because he came from overseas, for the matter becomes public knowledge.
