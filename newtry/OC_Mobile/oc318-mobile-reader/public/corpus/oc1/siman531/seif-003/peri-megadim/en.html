And he recovered. See Taz; and I already wrote in letter 2 that we do not hold like Rabbeinu Tam; even as a double doubt maybe the halacha is like Rabbeinu Tam we do not make of it. And if he shaved on the Moed, it requires further study whether he receives makkas mardus; see Rambam of blessed memory, chapter 7 of Hilchos Yom Tov, halacha 1, for one can say that since it is only a rabbinic penalty so that he not enter unkempt, it is not a rabbinic melacha prohibition and he is not flogged; and likewise for an unresolved inquiry such as a lost object and the like, one can say he is not flogged; nevertheless for a doubtful unresolved inquiry, where one cannot flog on a doubt, see Beis Yosef, Yoreh De'ah 294, who instituted regarding uzza and shibuta, see there.
