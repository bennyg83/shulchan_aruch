Every person, etc. In Yalkut Pardes it is written that hair at the place of bloodletting, like "rivda de-kusilta" [cupping], it is permitted to remove with a razor on Chol HaMoed, because bloodletting is a matter of pikuach nefesh, and even on Shabbos it is permitted, all the more so on Chol HaMoed, end quote. And this implies that he holds that one who has a wound that is not pikuach nefesh is forbidden to cut the hair in order to place a poultice; and one must say that he holds that shaving the mustache is forbidden if it does not impede eating; but according to us, who hold that everything is permitted, if so all the more so for the need of a wound; and so it appears at the end of siman 546 and siman 536, se'if 3; and see Beis Yosef, siman 532.
