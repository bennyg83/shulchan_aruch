Mordechai, first chapter of Moed Katan, brings a proof from the end of the first chapter of Avodah Zarah and Rashi there, who writes that for us it is permitted to heat bathhouses on Chol HaMoed. See there. This is also stated in Rashi's commentary to Moed Katan 19b. See there. Ritva likewise writes in his novellae at the beginning of the chapter VeEilu Megalchin that bathing and all work associated with a bathhouse are permitted. See there. Beis Yosef, siman 544, writes in the name of Responsa Rashba that repairing bathhouses is permitted even when the work was deliberately scheduled for the festival. Bathhouses also involve necessities of life because bathing the body prevents boils and sores. See there. Responsa Beis David, siman 305; Shulchan Gavoah, siman 534, subsection 7; Maharikash in this siman; Erech HaShulchan, subsection 2; Ruach Chaim, subsection 3, write likewise.
