And his custom — Taz; see Taz in Yoreh Deah 390 letter 5 and siman 268 there and in Turei Even chapter 6 — it appears for one answer: even if he had leisure before the moed, if he repented and did not have time to shave and immerse before the moed, nevertheless they shave on the moed like a nazir and metzora even when there is leisure, so as not to delay the offering — all the more so here all the mitzvot; and for answer 2 it appears similar to a minor who is not obligated in mitzvot — it appears regarding one who repented beforehand one does not shave him on the moed and he should suffer thus; and Magen Avraham letter 11 agreed to answer 1 even when there is leisure and he did not immerse and they did not shave him before the moed, since they practiced shaving according to what Rashi wrote in the portion Beha'alotcha in the name of R' Moshe the Darshan z"l that it is fitting to do so; and see Rashi in the mishnah at the beginning of "and these shave" when they had no leisure — requires study in the gemara 17b it is clear even when there was leisure; and in any case chapter 7 from letter 19 and in Turei Even and in Magen Avraham chapter 7 from letter 19 he elaborated there; and see in Raavad's explanation it appears his text is "and the metzora ascending from impurity to purity" and it refers only to metzora, but nazir whether pure or impure — so it is; and shaving is a minor matter — see there; and this will be explained in Magen Avraham letter 12. And know that a metzora shaves twice: once on the day of his purification when the affliction heals, the second after seven days as explained in chapter 1 of laws of tzara'at seif 1 letter 2; and the first shaving is permitted on the moed when there was no leisure beforehand, and the second even when there was leisure — Magen Avraham chapter 7 letter 19. And behold if melacha on Chol HaMoed is from the Torah — anything that is not davar ha'aved and not for the need of the moed — siman 530 — nevertheless shaving the sideburns of the head and beard and all the hair of the body — on the contrary, it is disgraceful when one shaves these; and without saying for the need of the moed — nevertheless the second is permitted for use and permitted for kodashim when he will later bring his eight offerings, and the first is permitted to enter inside the wall — that is also for the need of the moed; and requires study whether he shaves for the need of an offering on the eighth — perhaps we require need of the moed that day, only use — one could say it is permitted. And Rashi in the mishnah and gemara 17b — one could say in the mishnah it deals only with the first shaving when there is no leisure; if there is leisure — no; and a positive commandment pushes aside, etc. — one could say negligence; Tosafot Eruvin 100; and in the gemara after seven because of offerings — as Maggid Mishneh wrote chapter 7 letter 19; and one should not elaborate for this is not its place.
