(ב) Every person is permitted — the wording of the Rambam, and so the Rif, and Maggid Mishneh wrote that although they said specifically a mustache that interferes or one who is fastidious — the Rambam holds it refers to mourning and not to Chol HaMoed; and his words require study, for behold in mourning the Rambam wrote in chapter 6 that it is forbidden entirely; and therefore it must be that he holds it refers to Chol HaMoed, and so the Tur here — see there — and so Nissim and Shiltei Giborim, and like the view of Raavad from the Tosefta, and so in Yerushalmi that he said a mustache is like trimming nails for the twenty-four, etc.; and see Turei Even and in Beit Yosef Yoreh Deah siman 390 and in Knesset Hagedolah; and Beit Yosef wrote that the Rambam's text is like our text in the gemara and explains not like R' Ami as the Rosh wrote — and his words require study, for according to our text R' Ami explains his words; and furthermore Ran, who is the latter authority, holds like him that specifically before Abbaye and Rava the halachah is not like him; granted, for the text in Beit Yosef and Raavad Ran is like all who say; and see Yoreh Deah siman 390 that a mustache is like R' Ami, and the Rif's words may be explained as Maggid Mishneh's explanation that what they said regarding a mustache and an interfering growth for mourning — they said, but on Chol HaMoed it is permitted in every matter, as written regarding trimming nails — and this is only on days, etc.; and in Yerushalmi mustache and trimming nails — there is a baraita: it teaches on the festival permitted and in mourning forbidden, etc.; Rav said a mustache is like trimming nails for the twenty-four; and with this the words of Shulchan Aruch do not contradict either; but the Rambam's words require study. And after consideration it appears to me the essence is that the Rambam explains like the Aruch entry zavit: a mustache from corner to corner — an interfering growth all the more interferes; he explains: a mustache made from corner to corner — meaning if all of it needs repair it is permitted, but part of it not; an interfering growth all the more interferes — he explains: if he made his mustache and left part unmade it is permitted to remove it because it interferes with his eating and the like, and therefore it is permitted — end of his words. And accordingly what R' Ami said "a mustache all the more interferes" means even if he did not shave at all ever it interferes, and ever permitted; and he ruled like R' Ami, and as Raavad wrote that he is the latter authority; and even the Rosh agrees to this that in matters of the Scribes one follows the more lenient — and this is the view of Rav Aleksandri, not like the Rosh there — see there; and in any case the Rambam's view is like what was said last.
