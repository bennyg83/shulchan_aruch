A mitzvah. See Taz; Gemara Moed Katan, folio 14a; and see siman 468: on erev Pesach after midday it is forbidden to shave; thus on other erev Yom Tov days, if one forgot, it is permitted; but l'chatchila one should shave before midday; see siman 251, letter 5, in Magen Avraham in the name of the Arizal; and see Bach, siman 251, and in other places it implies that Shabbos and Yom Tov are equal; and see in the Gemara; and God willing, in Magen Avraham it will be explained further.
