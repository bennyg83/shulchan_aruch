1) [Se'if 1] It is a mitzvah to remove one's hair on erev Yom Tov. This is in honor of the festival, so that one not enter the festival unkempt. Levush; Taz, subsection 1. It is possible that this does not apply if one removed his hair within thirty days, because an unkempt appearance develops only after thirty days. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 2. It appears that in a place where people do not shave with a razor but only cut with scissors or a machine, even less than thirty days is considered unkempt. Since the hair is not removed completely and some remains, it grows noticeably even within thirty days and is considered unkempt for the festival.
