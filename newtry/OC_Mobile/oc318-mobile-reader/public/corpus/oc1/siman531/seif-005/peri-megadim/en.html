And likewise. See Taz in Yoreh De'ah 334, letter 6, who elaborated; and see Magen Avraham 4 and in Taz letter 1. And it appears specifically regarding nidui for money: to appease his litigant he is considered ones, because he thinks his litigant will not be appeased; see Beis Yosef and Levush; which is not so for nidui for a transgression and the like: one can say all agree that wherever the days of nidui were completed before the festival and he did not ask and did not return, one does not shave on the Moed; and God willing in Magen Avraham it will be explained.
