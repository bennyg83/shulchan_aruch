He should not leave. If he erred and concluded two verses before the next section, he should read the two verses from this section and three from the following section, and should not go back three verses. Magen Avraham. See Taz, siman 423. Where a section begins in the middle of a verse, as at the beginning of Pinchas, one may conclude even with the verse adjacent to it, for there is no concern that people will say that the next reader will read only until the section break. It is known that one does not conclude in the middle of a verse, even where there is a section break. For this reason, Vayechal is begun although it is two verses before a section break, and Vehikravtem is begun although it is two verses after a section break, because these readings are universally known and there is no concern for error. See Magen Avraham.
He should not begin. This means that he should not conclude for one reader two verses after a section break, for then the next reader would begin two verses after the section break, and those entering would say that the first reader read only two verses. Otherwise, why did he not conclude when he reached the section break? The same applies if the end of the sidrah contains a section of five verses: the maftir should not begin two verses after the section break, but should read all five verses. If an error is found in a Sefer Torah and another is brought, they continue from the place where they stopped. If only two verses or one verse remain for the reader, this is permissible, for he reads only what remains and there is no concern for those entering. Knesses HaGedolah. See Magen Avraham.
Good. The good of non-Jews is not called good, and the same applies to their misfortunes. Likewise, one should not conclude with a person who committed an evil deed. Rambam, end of Yadayim. A blind or lame person should not be called for the section dealing with blindness or a broken limb, and similarly in all comparable cases, so that he not be embarrassed. Likewise, when the chazzan reads blessings, he should not have in mind to bless his friend. Sefer Chassidim; Magen Avraham. If it is certain that a person committed sexual transgressions and did not repent, it is proper to call him for the section dealing with forbidden relations so that he will be embarrassed. Knesses HaGedolah.
