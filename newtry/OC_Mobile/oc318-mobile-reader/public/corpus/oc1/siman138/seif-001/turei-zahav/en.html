And a section containing only two verses. It appears that the reason we are not concerned that people will think the reader reads only two verses is that where they see no unusual arrangement, we are not concerned. Only if he leaves two verses in the section where he concludes is there an unusual feature that creates suspicion. When he reaches the end of a section, however, there is no unusual feature.
