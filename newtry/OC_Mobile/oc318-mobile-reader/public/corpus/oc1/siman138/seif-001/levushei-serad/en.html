Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. “The next reader should begin with an earlier verse.” Agudah. This is because of those who may err, or because of those entering, who may think that it is permissible to conclude two verses before a section break and will always act that way; or because of those present at the beginning of the reading who leave in the middle and will think that the next reader will read only until the section break.
