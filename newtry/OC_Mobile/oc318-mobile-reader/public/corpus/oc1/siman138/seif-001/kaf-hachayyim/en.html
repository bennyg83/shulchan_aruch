1) [Seif 1] One who reads from the Torah should not leave [fewer than three verses], etc. That is, he should not stop such that two verses remain before a [section-break] parashah in the Torah, because they will say that the reason he did not read up to the parashah is that he intends to read one more [aliyah] up to the parashah. Magen Avraham, s.k. 1.
