Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. “If so, the same applies if he erred.” In my humble opinion, this is not compelling. Even if Agudah’s ruling is disproved, that may apply only where the reader concluded two verses before a section break, because we are not concerned for those leaving during the reading. But if he erred and concluded two verses after a section break, there is concern for those entering at the beginning of the reading, who may think that the first reader read only two verses, just as we are concerned initially. It may therefore be that even after the fact the next reader must begin again from the beginning of the section. Although Ran proves that where an error occurred we are not concerned, that proof addresses only the concern that people may think it is generally permitted to begin within fewer than three verses. Regarding the concern that the first reader read only two verses, however, perhaps we remain concerned even after the fact.
