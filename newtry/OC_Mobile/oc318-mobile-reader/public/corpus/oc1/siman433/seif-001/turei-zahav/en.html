(א) And if he passed. In Tur it is written: even if one checks by day — meaning not only that l'chatchila one should not rely to check by sunlight, i.e. wait for daylight, but even if he already passed and must check by day, nevertheless he needs a candle; and so Beit Yosef, and this is correct. And since he did not find to write a novelty except for the 14th and not the 13th, it implies checking on the 13th does not help since the Sages fixed the time for the night of the 14th — it is as one who checks a long time before Pesach; and so Beit Yosef concludes for halacha.

(ב) And a portico whose light is great. Beit Yosef wrote: when checking on that day — it appears his intent is that this law of a portico also applies when one does the entire checking on the 14th, e.g. he passed and did not check at night; but l'chatchila he should also check the portico at night by candlelight. Rashi implies even l'chatchila he need not use candlelight but by day; and so Rif, and the conclusion of Mahari Weil and most poskim is that it is specifically b'dieved.
