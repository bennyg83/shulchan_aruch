Taz s.k. 1 — and since he did not find to write leniency, etc. — at first glance requires study: what leniency of the 13th, for even if checking on the 13th helps, l'chatchila he should not check even by candle since the night of the 14th remains when the candle shines more at night unlike the 14th by day one would think sunlight is better — he teaches; perhaps he means he could have taught this leniency, e.g. he knows he will be constrained at night and cannot check properly and wants to check on the 13th by candle — why specify only "he passed and did not check," etc. — if so checking on the 13th does not help at all; accordingly even if he knows he cannot check on the night of the 14th, better leave checking for the 14th than check on the 13th — not the Sages' enactment at all; Taz's language "since the Sages fixed the time on the 14th," etc. does not imply the case I wrote; perhaps his intent: since by sunlight even b'dieved does not help as s.k. 3, he could have given the case he checked on the 13th by sunlight — even b'dieved no, must check again by candle at night; if he wrote so it would imply only on the 13th by day, but on the night of the 13rd he fulfilled — in truth otherwise it does not help, as one checking long before Pesach; therefore he specified the 14th.
