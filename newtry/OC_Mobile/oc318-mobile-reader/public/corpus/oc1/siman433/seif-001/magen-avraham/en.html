(א) And if he passed. But l'chatchila it is forbidden to check by day, even in a dark house, by candlelight.

(ב) And a portico. Meaning built on pillars and has no wall.

(ג) If he checked. The Gemara states that l'chatchila they check a portico by sunlight — it must refer when checking on the 14th; but l'chatchila he must check on the night of the 14th by candlelight, as this siman states; therefore he wrote b'dieved language — so there is no dispute, as Ri'az and most hold, unlike Bach.

(ד) Opposite a skylight. Meaning a window in the roof; so in mishnah chapter 1 of Ohalot and chapter 5 of Beitzah; therefore Ri'az wrote it is forbidden to check opposite the window — window means in the wall; and Beit Yosef's view is that even opposite a window in the wall is permitted, and so most hold in Rambam: holes and cracks in the house — those opposite the skylight are checked by daylight, etc. — implying even if the sun does not shine; and it appears for a window with a glass partition all forbid checking opposite it.
