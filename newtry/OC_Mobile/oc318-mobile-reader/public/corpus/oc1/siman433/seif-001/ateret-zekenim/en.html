(א) Checking must be, etc. — but on the 13th one should not advance destroying by candle; it appears even if the house is dark so that by day he can check well by candle as at night, nevertheless if he already checked on the 13th even not by candle he must return and check on the night of the 14th.

(ב) Whose light is great, etc. — some wrote even porticos and synagogues whose light is great must check on the night of the 14th even if they already checked on the night of the 13th by candle or on the 13th by sunlight; the reason: not to divide destroying from destroying, for most Geonim hold one does not check by day unless he did not check at night; even b'dieved checking on the 13rd by day in a house with light does not help even b'dieved; only in a portico if he checked by sunlight it suffices; but Levush implies even l'chatchila one may check by daylight in a portico; so Rashash; so appears in the Gemara.
