(Magen Avraham s.k. 5) And oil candle — fearing to put into holes lest oil spill. Nevertheless its law is not like a torch; you might ask wax too — should read as printed. NB: otherwise difficult to include oil with torch in one clause; therefore three laws: torch — even b'dieved must return and check; oil — l'chatchila forbidden by law but b'dieved need not return; fat candle — stringency; practical difference if a place did not accept them — examine.
