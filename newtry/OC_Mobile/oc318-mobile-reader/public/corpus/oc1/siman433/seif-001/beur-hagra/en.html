(א) s.k. 1 And if he passed, etc. Yerushalmi: "is there sun at night" — resolve, etc. — the inference: even by day one must check by candlelight.

(ב) If he checked. Rambam's language, and so Rosh — although in the Gemara they said "is checked by its light," it refers to what he wrote "and not by sunlight" — if he did not check at night, as explained, unlike Rashi's explanation; and it is possible they wrote l'chatchila to check, for there is still another reason "when people are in their homes," etc. — see Magen Avraham.
