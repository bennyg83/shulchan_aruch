(א) By candlelight. On the night of the 14th; and even if he checked on the 13th by candlelight he must return and check on the night of the 14th. However, if he checked on the night of the 13th and was very careful not to bring more chametz, he need not check again — Kaf HaChayyim, see there, Levush, and Bach wrote that even if he checked on the night of the 13th he must return on the night of the 14th; Kaf HaChayyim concluded: if he wishes to be stringent on himself, at least he should not bless then lest he enter a doubtful blessing situation, but he may return and use chametz there and then bless and check.

(ב) Skylight. Meaning a window in the roof (see chapter 5 of Beitzah mishnah 1 and chapter 10 of Ohalot). If there is a glass partition there, it is forbidden to check opposite it — Magen Avraham.
