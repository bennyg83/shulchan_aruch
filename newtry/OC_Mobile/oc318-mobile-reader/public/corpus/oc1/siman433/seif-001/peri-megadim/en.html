And if — Taz: Tur wrote not by sunlight even when checking by day, e.g. he did not check on the night of the 14th and checks on the 14th — nevertheless he needs a candle; Beit Yosef: what if there is sun at night — he explains even prolonged, etc.; meaning not to rely initially on day; forbidden even if he forgot, etc.; some explained: the case is he usually sweeps rooms before checking and sometimes checking extends by sweeping — nevertheless he must check afterward; Magen Avraham: if he swept at night and forgot and checks on the 14th by day, he needs a candle lest he rely on night sweeping; if he did not sweep at all, he checks by sunlight and checks well — teaches; Beit Yosef rejected this explanation — see there. To check l'chatchila on the 13th by sunlight is obviously forbidden; even by candle on the 13th obviously forbidden, for at night it shines more than by day in a dark house; in a house with light one may darken per Rashi Pesachim 4a s.v. and candlelight is good for checking; nevertheless the night of the 13th appears permitted; however do not forget Torah of checking and not to divide destroying from destroying, as we will say in Magen Avraham note 1 and note 20 — also forbidden, as Yerushalmi courtyards in Jerusalem, etc.; unless he checks some rooms on the 14th then perhaps permitted. Taz's words appear difficult: more leniency on the 14th than the 13th, as said; if Beit Yosef intended Tur's meaning — if checking on the 13th it should say so; since he did not explain leniency, and anyway not relevant; perhaps Tur's long case: he did not check on the night of the 14th and checks on the 14th — not abbreviated "even checking on the 14th" — meaning even Magen Avraham on the 13th by day not by sunlight, and likewise not by candle, for at night it shines more; but if he passed or forgot and checks on the 14th he needs a candle — meaning even on the night of the 13th forbidden not to divide times; so Beit Yosef on Tur: he needs on the night of the 13th — from end of siman one who sweeps a room; so Shulchan Aruch seif 11; even on the night of the 13th by candle intending to check well he must check on the night of the 14th, as Mordechai and Terumat HaDeshen 133 — from Yerushalmi courtyards in Jerusalem not to divide destroying from destroying; as in Magen Avraham it will be explained further; and in siman 431 it implies on the 13th by day one may not; in Machatzit HaShekel chapter 2 law 4 one may say on the 13th by sunlight, and likewise certainly not by what I would say candle forbidden; Darkei Moshe 432 explains even checking by day as on the night of the 14th near darkening — Magen Avraham 431 beginning — see there.
