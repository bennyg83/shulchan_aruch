Laws of Checking for Chametz. Containing 11 paragraphs: The checking should
be done' by candlelight, not moonlight.' If one did not check on the night' of the 14th, when checking during the day of the 14th, one should not check by sunlight but by candlelight. However, for a room with bright light,' if one checked by sunlight it suffices, and the same applies to checking opposite a chimney in a room.
