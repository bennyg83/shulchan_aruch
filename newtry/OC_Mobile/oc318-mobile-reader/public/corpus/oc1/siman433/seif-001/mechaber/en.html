Laws of bedikat chametz. Containing 11 seifim. Bedikat chametz must be
by candlelight,' not moonlight. If one' did not check on the night of the' 14th, when checking on the day of the 14th one should not check by sunlight but by candlelight. However, for a portico whose light is great, if' one checked by sunlight it suffices; and the same applies to checking opposite the skylight in a room.
