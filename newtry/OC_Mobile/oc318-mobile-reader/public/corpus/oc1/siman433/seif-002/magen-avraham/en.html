(א) And not by candle. That he separated torch and tallow candle and did not write them in one clause — because a torch appears in the Gemara to forbid, and there are many reasons permitting putting it into holes and cracks lest he burn the house, and also because its light is behind him and flickers; if so, even b'dieved he must return and check. But a tallow candle — they were stringent that it is permitted lest it drip on vessels and garments; b'dieved he has fulfilled. Oil candle has the law of a torch, fearing to put it into holes lest oil spill. You might ask: wax too — one fears dripping on garments; one may say it can be fixed with a full spoon of coals.

(ב) Even braided. This language is not in Maharil — what does "even" mean, etc.; certainly if not braided it is permitted since each can be brought to holes; it appears the intent is: it is obvious if glued together and not braided it is like a torch; but even if braided, one might think it is like one candle — he teaches otherwise.
