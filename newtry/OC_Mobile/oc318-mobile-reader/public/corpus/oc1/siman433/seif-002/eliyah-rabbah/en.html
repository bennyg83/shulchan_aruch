[2] [Levush] is checked by its light, etc. — meaning b'dieved when he did not check on the night of the 14th he checks by day by its light and checks in the morning because the diligent precede, and he should not eat until he checks; Bach explained he may even check l'chatchila on the 14th by the portico candlelight, and Acharonim disagree. Bach further was stringent in all matters requiring checking that one must return to check on the night of the 14th by candlelight even if he already checked on the night of the 13th (Yerushalmi in Shas) — so Levush seif 11 implies, unlike Olat Shabbat that permits checking on the night of the 13th and their words were omitted. Chok Yaakov expanded to uphold our Shas — see there; one may reject it speaks when going on a journey; proof: Mordechai did not write on Yerushalmi only this language in our Shas: a portico is checked by candlelight — therefore no question except from our Shas; also Bach is correct per our explanation: if he did not check on the night of the 14th, our Shas speaks of checking by day by candlelight but does not disagree with Yerushalmi; one may align Mordechai's view so; also in Emek Berakhah he ruled like Yerushalmi in general and did not conclude our Shas disagrees.
