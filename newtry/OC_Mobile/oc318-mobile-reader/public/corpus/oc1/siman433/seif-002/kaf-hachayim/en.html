Must be by candlelight, etc. — and on the night of the 14th; and even if he checked on the 13th by candlelight he must return and check on the night of the 14th per Chazal's enactment that candlelight is good for checking and people are in their homes, as above (siman 431 note 4); so Acharonim agreed. However, if he checked on the night of the 13th and was very careful not to bring more chametz, he need not check again; but Levush seif 11 and Bach wrote even if he checked on the night of the 13th he must return and check — not compelled; if he wishes to be stringent, at least he should not bless to avoid doubtful blessing, but he may return and use chametz there, then bless and check — Chayei Adam note 1; Machatzit HaShekel note 1; Mekor Chayim annotation note 1; further explained below seif 11 — see there.
