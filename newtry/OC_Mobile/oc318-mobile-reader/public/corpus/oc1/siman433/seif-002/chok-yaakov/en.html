By the light of a torch. Even after the fact, such a checking is not effective. This is unlike the other lights, where after the fact the checking is effective, as the later authorities wrote. In pressing circumstances, when he has no wax candle, he may initially check with another lamp, for pressing circumstances are treated like after the fact. Nevertheless, if it is possible for him to use an oil lamp, he should check with that, for it too is proper even initially according to several poskim, as the Rosh and the Kol Bo wrote. See there.
And it is single. It appears to me that for this reason no posek mentions whether one may check with a wooden torch, because it does not burn singly, as stated in the Gemara: just as wood does not burn singly. Even with resinous wood, called kien in the vernacular, although it sometimes burns singly, nevertheless, since it has a bad odor, and for this reason it is not used for Havdalah as explained above in siman 298 in the words of the later authorities, and also sparks fly from it onto the utensils, we are concerned that because of this he will not check properly.
