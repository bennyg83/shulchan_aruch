(א) By torchlight — one cannot put it into holes and cracks, and one fears lest he burn the house and not check well; one does not check with wooden torches (called kiyin) whose light is also great like a torch.

(ב) And not with a tallow candle — fearing it may drip on vessels and ruin them.

(ג) And not of oil — fearing to put it into holes and cracks lest oil spill. B'dieved if he checked by torchlight the checking does not count and he must return with a single candle; tallow and fat candles count b'dieved; regarding oil there are opinions among Acharonim whether it counts b'dieved.

(ד) But with a wax candle — if he has no wax he may check with those candles that b'dieved suffice (Acharonim).

(ה) Even braided — meaning all the more if glued together it is considered a torch; but even braided one might think it is like one candle — he teaches otherwise (Magen Avraham); from Peri Megadim it implies whatever he holds together in his hand without separating is a torch; a candle with two wicks as sometimes made in tallow candles — if there is space between wicks it is a torch (there).
