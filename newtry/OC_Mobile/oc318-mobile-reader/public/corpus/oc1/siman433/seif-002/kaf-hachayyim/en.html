11) [Se'if 2] One may not search by the light of a torch, etc. This is because a torch cannot be inserted into holes and cracks. One is also afraid that it might burn the house, causing distraction and preventing a careful search. Tur; Levush. See Pesachim 8a for additional reasons. Bach writes that according to those reasons, a search performed by torchlight is effective after the fact. Taz, subsection 3, writes likewise. Magen Avraham, subsection 5, however, writes that it is ineffective even after the fact and the search must be repeated. Peri Chadash; Chok Yaakov, subsection 5; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 4; Chok Yosef, subsection 5; Mekor Chaim, novellae, subsection 4; Birkei Yosef, subsection 6, who writes that this is contrary to Taz; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 9; Chayei Adam, rule 119, subsection 8. Regarding the blessing, Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes there that it should not be recited again, because one must take into account the authorities who maintain that the original search is effective after the fact.
