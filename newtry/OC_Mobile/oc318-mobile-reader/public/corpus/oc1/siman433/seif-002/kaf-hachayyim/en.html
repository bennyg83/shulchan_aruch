Even if one searched by candlelight during the day of the thirteenth, one must search again on the night of the fourteenth in accordance with the enactment of our Sages, because candlelight is effective for searching then and people are present in their homes, as explained above in siman 431, subsection 4. This is the consensus of the later authorities. If, however, one searched on the night of the thirteenth and was then meticulously careful not to bring in any more chametz, no additional search is necessary. Levush, se'if 11, and Bach nevertheless write that even if one searched on the night of the thirteenth, one must search again. Their conclusion is not compelling. If one wishes to be stringent, one should at least not recite another blessing and thereby enter a possible doubt concerning a blessing. One may, however, resume using chametz in the area and then recite a blessing and search again. Chok Yaakov, subsection 1; Chemed Moshe, subsection 1; Mekor Chaim, novellae, subsection 1. This will be explained further below in se'if 11. See there.
