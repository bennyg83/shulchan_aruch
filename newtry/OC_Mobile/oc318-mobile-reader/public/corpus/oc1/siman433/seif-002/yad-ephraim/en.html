(there Magen Avraham s.k. 5) fears putting into holes and cracks lest he burn the house — note: in Shas daf 8 these are two answers — Rabbenu Nissim Beit Yosef said this one can put into holes, etc.; Rabeinu Peretz said this one fears lest he set the house on fire — see there; what Magen Avraham wrote regarding oil having the law of torch, etc. — many were puzzled, for it should be with torch in one clause; they answer practical difference, e.g. if he checked holes with wax candle he may check the rest with oil candle, unlike torch — see Peri Megadim; in my humble opinion: regarding torch it depends on Gemara reasons — fear lest he burn the house (as Magen Avraham appears here) — if he checked and knows he was not afraid and inserted into all places needing checking, b'dieved checking is valid; but in general we fear he did not check properly and must return; only holes and cracks separately, unlike reasons of light behind him and flickering — even b'dieved even if he says certainly I checked even holes, it does not help and he must recheck entirely; therefore Magen Avraham wrote for tallow those reasons do not apply, only custom stringency lest it drip, etc.; b'dieved fulfilled — we assume he checked properly; regarding oil he wrote like torch — fear putting into holes lest oil spill — oil also like torch for that reason, nevertheless unlike actual torch: there he must recheck everything even if he knows he checked well; with oil it suffices to return and check holes and cracks alone; also if he says certainly I checked holes too, it helps; nevertheless unlike tallow which is only custom lest drip on vessels — that concern is not in the Gemara, therefore b'dieved fulfilled; more correct to emend oil "like torch" — common error; I came to settle: at first glance why be stringent on oil — for tallow they were stringent only lest drip on garments; for garments alone no stringency since fixable as Magen Avraham wrote for wax; repair exists also for oil and tallow; since one also fears dripping on vessels, they were stringent; fat candle also has that concern lest drip on dairy vessels — unlike oil; therefore oil seems like wax; Magen Avraham wrote oil like torch for fear putting into holes, etc.; with this it is well also why he separated tallow, fat, and oil each separately, not "or fat or oil" — each has its own reason; at the beginning of Magen Avraham emend: fears putting into holes and cracks lest he burn the house.
