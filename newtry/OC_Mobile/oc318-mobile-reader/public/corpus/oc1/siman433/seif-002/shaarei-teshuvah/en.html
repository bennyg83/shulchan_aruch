Seif 11 in Shulchan Aruch there (in the gloss) and everyone — must sweep, etc.; therefore the masses are lenient to check superficially without searching holes and cracks well, because they first sweep, wash, and scrape everything well, and even if gentiles wash and scrape it is reasonable they are believed — anpikta they are careful and do not harm themselves — so responsum at the end of Mahari Weil's novellae on Pesachim.
