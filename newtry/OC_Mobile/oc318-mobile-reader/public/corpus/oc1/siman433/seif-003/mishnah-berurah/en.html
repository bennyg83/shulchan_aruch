(א) Where there is concern chametz was brought — not only places regularly used with chametz, which obviously require checking, but even where chametz is not regularly used all year, only where there is concern chametz was brought there incidentally — also requires checking, as explained below.
(ב) All rooms of the house — even rooms used for pledges where sometimes one enters mid-meal to return a pledge to its owner, lest he forgot bread there; likewise similar cases such as cellars where fruits, preserves, and cheeses are stored, entered mid-meal to take them; likewise wood storage and coal, for the servant enters mid-meal for candles to light and wood for cooking.
(ג) That bread is not suspected — in places where bread is not suspected, sometimes one enters mid-meal with bread in hand to bring more wine for his meal; likewise storehouses of other drinks where they regularly drink — require checking.
(ד) From them — storehouses where he takes from them and has a fixed practice to take at once what he needs for a meal — no checking, since they are not accustomed to enter mid-meal (Acharonim).
(ה) Do not require checking — presumably chametz is not brought unless he knows chametz was brought.
(ו) Require checking — because they are regularly used and there is concern a little chametz remained.
