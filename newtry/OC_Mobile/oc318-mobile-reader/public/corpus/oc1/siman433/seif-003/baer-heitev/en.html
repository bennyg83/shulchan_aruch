That bread is not suspected. If bread is suspected and he has a fixed practice to take at once what he needs for a meal, no checking is required — Magen Avraham. Maharil (Mahari Weil) wrote in a responsum: where they eat there all year, one should check under the benches, for it is impossible chametz did not fall there — it is clear chametz (if there are no chickens there). One should not throw grain to chickens in a damp place thirty days before Pesach — Maharil.
