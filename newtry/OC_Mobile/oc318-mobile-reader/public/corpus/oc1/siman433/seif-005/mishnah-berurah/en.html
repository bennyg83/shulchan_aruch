(א) Lean-to — a low structure.

(ב) And the tower — called alma'or or similar; they used slanted roofs; but like ours with level roofs where people use them — checking required; all the more inside the tower if chametz is sometimes used, or things taken mid-meal — certainly requires checking.

(ג) Their roofs are unfit — their house roofs were level and regularly used on them — checking required. Our ceilings under a slanted roof — if regularly used, checking required, for sometimes one goes mid-meal with bread in hand, as seif 3. Where effort is great, it appears one may be lenient by selling that place to a non-Jew together with chametz there.
