(א) (s.k. 5) And not by candle — for he separated them, etc. — for holes and cracks and lest he burn, etc. — so it should read: these are two reasons in the Gemara. Rabbenu Nissim Beit Yosef said because one cannot put it into holes and cracks; Rabeinu Peretz said because one fears lest he burn the house; there is a practical difference between these two reasons: for Rabbenu Nissim Beit Yosef, if he checks holes and cracks with a single candle, he may check the rest of the house with a torch; but for Rabeinu Peretz who gave the reason that one fears lest he burn the house, even in such a case it is forbidden. If so, even b'dieved, etc. But a tallow candle, etc. — I have seen people err and think all this follows to the end: why he separated into two clauses regarding torch mentioned in the Gemara which has many reasons, therefore even b'dieved he has not fulfilled. Unlike a tallow candle which is only a stringency, therefore b'dieved he has fulfilled; therefore he did not include them in one clause since their laws differ regarding b'dieved. Accordingly they ask: why did he include oil with tallow — he should have included oil with torch since he wrote oil has the law of a torch; but in truth this is not Magen Avraham's intent in his answer: what he wrote is because torch appears in the Gemara to forbid — and with this he concluded his answer. Meaning: since the others are only from custom stringency, and torch is forbidden by Gemara law, therefore he did not include them as one; but where their law is not the same regarding b'dieved, that does not cause separating into two clauses; and even if their law were the same, that would not cause including them in one clause. Afterward he wrote from his own reasoning:

(ב) And there are many reasons, etc. — therefore since there are many reasons in it, one may say even b'dieved he has not fulfilled; unlike tallow, since there is no other reason except stringency lest it drip, etc., therefore it appears to Magen Avraham that in any case b'dieved he has fulfilled. Oil — since in any case there is another reason stated in the Gemara for torch that permits putting it into holes — therefore it appears to Magen Avraham that even b'dieved he has not fulfilled, like a torch. He wrote in Chayei Adam: if he has no wax candle he may take those candles with which in any case he fulfills, for a time of pressure is like b'dieved; he also wrote wood is forbidden because wood alone does not burn, and a torch is forbidden; even greased wood called kiyin that burns alone one should not take, because its smell is bad and sparks fly from it onto vessels, and we fear thereby he will not check well.
