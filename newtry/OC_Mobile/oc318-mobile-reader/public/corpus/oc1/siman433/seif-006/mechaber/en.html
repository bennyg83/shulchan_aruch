A cattle shed does not require checking, for if there was chametz there the animals would eat it. Likewise a chicken coop does not require checking, for if there was chametz there the chickens would eat it. Likewise the middle of a courtyard does not require checking, for if there was chametz there the ravens and other birds found there would eat it. This is regarding possible chametz; but for definite chametz, no.But below siman 445 seif 3 it' is explained that it is permitted to discard chametz where birds are found — certainly one need not remove even definite chametz from there before its prohibition time.:
