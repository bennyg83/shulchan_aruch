A cattle shed does not require checking, for if there was chametz there the animals would eat it. Similarly, a chicken coop does not require checking as the chickens would eat any chametz there. And the middle of a courtyard does not require checking, for if there was chametz there the ravens and other birds found there would eat it. This applies to possible chametz, but for definite chametz, it does.Rema: But in Siman 445:3 it is' clarified that it is permitted to discard chametz where birds frequent, certainly not needing to remove even definite chametz from there before its prohibition time.:
