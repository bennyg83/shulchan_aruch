(6) There, and a portico, etc. — meaning built on pillars and has no wall — Magen Avraham s.k. 2; that is the Roman portico in Shas; but unspecified portico has three sides, as Shas Bava Batra daf 11b, Tosafot s.v. here, chapter HaKometz, Yoreh Deah siman 286; if so here unspecified portico also means so and has great light since the fourth side is open — so explicitly Riaz in Shulchan Gavoah — portico explanation; Chayei Adam note 2; Ateret Rosh note 1; Berakh Kehay note 3 agreed and answered Chok Yosef's attack on Chayei Adam — it is explicit in Riaz, Tashbetz, and Orchot Chaim that a portico with three sides is checked by its light — see there; so Rabbi Zalman note 2.
