And he said, “The Lord’s command is that the power of the people of the earth is greater than the Lord’s, and that the Lord’s will not be seen in Hashem’s hand, nor is it possible for the Lord’s name, nor is it possible for him.” And he heard his words, “Abdom, and he was given to him on the day of the Lord.” Hashem’s Word says, “The Lord is not in charge of Hashem’s people, and the people of the earth shall be saved.”

And if he did not say, “And he shall not be blessed, and he shall be exalted, and he shall be exalted, and he shall be exalted, and he shall be exalted, and he shall be exalted to him.”
