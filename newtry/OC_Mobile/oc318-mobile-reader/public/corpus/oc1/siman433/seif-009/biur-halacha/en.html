Need not check, etc. — know that Mechaber left unspecified like Rambam and Rosh, and so Rabbenu Yonah and Semag who all ruled like Rav in the Gemara; but in truth it is not a clear matter, for in Orchot Chaim it is brought that Rav Sharira Gaon, Rav Hai his son, R' Gershom, Rabbenu Efraim hold like Shmuel [that one must check the upper row seeing entrance and beam like Rav, and the second before it seeing only the ceiling]; so too Rabeinu Chananel before us [but if his words at the end are explained plainly — both check from beam to ground — what does "and it suffices" mean, for then Shmuel is stricter than Rav]. So too Ittur holds like Shmuel; Rambam himself in mishnah commentary ruled like Shmuel; and in Gra's explanation it also appears he sides with this view, and he brought that this is also Rabeinu Chananel's view [but what he brought that this is also Rif's view — Bach surrounds those lines in Rif that are not from Rif — see there]. It is difficult to dismiss all these great ones, especially since Rambam himself was not so clear on this, as explained.
