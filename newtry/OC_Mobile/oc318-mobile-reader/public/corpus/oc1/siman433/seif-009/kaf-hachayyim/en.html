This means a window in the roof, as stated in the Mishnah at the beginning of chapter 10 of Ohalos and at the beginning of chapter 5 of Beitzah. Rabbeinu Yerucham therefore writes that one may not search directly opposite an ordinary window, since a window is in the wall. The Tur, however, maintains that one may search even opposite a window in the wall, and this also appears from Rambam. Magen Avraham, subsection 4. Levush likewise writes that no candle is required directly opposite a window. Chok Yaakov, subsection 4; Mekor Chaim, novellae, subsection 3; Chemed Moshe, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 4, agree. If the window contains a glass pane, all opinions agree that one may not search opposite it without a candle. Magen Avraham, there; Chok Yaakov, there; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 3; Mekor Chaim, there; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, there. Therefore, one must open the window when searching opposite it without a candle.
