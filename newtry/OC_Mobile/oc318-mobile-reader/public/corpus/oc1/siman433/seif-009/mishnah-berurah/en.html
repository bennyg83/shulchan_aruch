(א) Cellar where rows are arranged — specifically cellar used for meal needs where sometimes the servant goes mid-meal with bread to bring wine to the table and forgets bread there (Acharonim).
(ב) Need not check — see Biur Halacha.
(ג) And not the entire cellar area — meaning need not check all upper rows seeing the ceiling along length and width — only the last row opposite the entrance seeing ceiling and entrance together, and another barrel row below seeing only the entrance.
