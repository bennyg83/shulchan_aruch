(א) (s.k. 9) Chicken coop — he wrote "and all in it," and it appears his intent, etc. — I am dealing with this: meaning two difficulties: first he brought Magen Avraham in the name of several poskim that chickens cause exemption from checking — how can he say "all in it" the opposite view that chickens cause obligation to check; rather it should say "they ate it."

(ב) And further: if checking is required, what does sweeping help, as Magen Avraham wrote in this siman seif 11 that sweeping alone does not help, and nevertheless checking is required. Therefore sweeping suffices — meaning: although in this siman he wrote sweeping does not help, that is because of concern for leavening in holes that is not removed by sweeping; unlike a large piece that cannot be in a hole without coming out entirely outside the hole, and since in any case it comes outside it is well removed by sweeping — see s.k. 7 afterward where he agreed to the law in this.

(ג) s.k. 7 here — and it is like clear chametz. Mahari Weil — so it should read.
