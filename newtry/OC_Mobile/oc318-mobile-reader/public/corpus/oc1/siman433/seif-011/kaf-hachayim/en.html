(11) [seif 2] One does not check by torchlight, etc. — because one cannot put it into holes and cracks and fears lest he burn the house and his heart is troubled and he will not check well — Tur; Levush; see Pesachim 8a for more reasons; Bach wrote for those reasons b'dieved if he checked by torch it helps; so Taz s.k. 3 b'dieved helps; but Magen Avraham s.k. 5 wrote even b'dieved it does not help and he must return and check; so Peri Chadash; Chayei Adam note 5; Ateret Rosh note 4; Chok Yosef note 5; Mekor Chayim annotation note 4; Berakh Kehay note 6 — not like Taz; Rabbi Zalman note 9; Chayei Adam general 119 note 8; regarding blessing Rabbi Zalman wrote one should not bless again lest one rely on those who hold b'dieved helps.
