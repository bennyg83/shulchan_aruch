(א) And intending — not only without presumption of sweeping, but even if intending for bedika sweeping does not help.
(ב) Nevertheless must check — sweeping is not complete bedika lest chametz remain in a crevice; even if he checked holes and cracks by candle he must return at night like all Israel, not to divide bedika from bedika (all explained from Turei Zahav in Mordechai); per seif 1 in Biur Halacha one may not rely on daytime checking even by candle, for candle does not shine by day as at night.
(ג) Must clean his rooms — one does not check well without cleaning; custom to clean whole house on the 13th so bedika can be done properly at start of night of 14th, as siman 431 seif 1; at bedika time custom to take feathers and clean well in holes and cracks to drag chametz with the feather.
(ד) That they sometimes place chametz — even if he says "I am certain I did not place chametz" — a matter not dependent on him and not in his awareness — therefore checking required; world practice only shakes pockets at biur time — correct to check at bedika time; nevertheless shake again at biur lest he returned and put chametz from what he ate after bedika (Acharonim).
(ה) Require checking — both his own and children's in the house.
