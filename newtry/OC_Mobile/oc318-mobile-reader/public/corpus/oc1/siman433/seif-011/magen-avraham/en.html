(א) One who sweeps his room. Some practice checking afterward only some rooms — not proper; although Mordechai concludes the reason checking by day does not help is not to divide checking from checking — if so, wherever he did any checking by daylight on the 14th, sweeping suffices; nevertheless he wrote another reason lest something remain in a crevice — then rooms would not be exempt from full checking; therefore full checking is required in all rooms — end Turei Zahav; even if he checked holes and cracks by day he must return at night, as this siman; however if he checked a portico by sunlight he has fulfilled, as Beit Yosef siman 437 in the name of Tosafot; nevertheless at least one room at night so as not to divide checking from checking.

(ב) Must sweep. One does not check well without sweeping (Mordechai); therefore they practice taking feathers and sweeping.

(ג) That they place in them. One whose practice is not so need not check (Darkei Moshe).
