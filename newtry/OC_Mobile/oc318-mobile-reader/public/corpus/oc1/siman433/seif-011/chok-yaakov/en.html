On the day of the thirteenth. Specifically by day; but if he checked on the night of the thirteenth, he need not check again, as I wrote above in seif katan 1. So wrote the Olas Shabbos, unlike the other later authorities. See there.
Nevertheless, he must check on the night of the fourteenth. But an achsadra, if he checked it on the night of the thirteenth by sunlight, he has fulfilled the obligation. So wrote the Magen Avraham. Nevertheless, in any case one should check one room at night. End quote. See seif katan 1.
To sweep his rooms. Therefore they have the custom to take feathers and sweep, as the Magen Avraham wrote. In truth, this was not said about such sweeping, but about actually sweeping the entire room. The feathers are taken only at the time of checking, when one finds chametz, to scrape with the feather from the cracks and holes. Or one can say that his intent is large feathers joined together, namely a goose wing, called a feather brush in the vernacular, with which they sweep all the places before Pesach.
In which they sometimes place chametz. Even if he says, I am certain that I did not place chametz in them, it is possible that nevertheless they require checking, since it is the way to place chametz in them, and something that is not imposed upon a person is not in his mind. Furthermore, sometimes a crumb of chametz is stuck to his hands and he puts it into his hood or sleeve without knowing it, for hands are busy. See below, siman 447, seif 5 in the gloss, and what I wrote there.
They require checking. At first glance this implies that it refers to the time of checking, namely the night of the fourteenth. But in truth, the world is not careful about this, and they shake out the pockets at the time of burning. Perhaps the reason is that they still eat chametz, and perhaps something sticks to their hands and they again put it into the hood. The Levush also implies that it does not need checking at the time of checking. One who wishes to be stringent must at least go back and shake out his pocket at the time of burning.
