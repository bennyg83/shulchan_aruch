(א) s.k. 11 One who sweeps, etc., and intends. Meaning: not only without presumption of sweeping, as in Niddah 66 — they asked until how much, etc.; and from where that he said, etc. — learn from this; and Tosafot in Pesachim 7a s.v. made for, etc.; but one may say because of holes, as stated in the Gemara chapter 1, such as, etc.

(ב) Nevertheless. As stated in Yerushalmi there: R' Yosa asked — are courtyards in Jerusalem where they eat there thanksgiving loaves and nazirite wafers required to be checked? Without that they are not checked because of leftover. Let this come like that Niddah case: she combs and braids like this one; she does not comb and braid like that one — Niddah; so too here, not to divide destroying from destroying — Raavan. But Ran wrote one does not check on the 13th by day by candlelight, as Yerushalmi there: dark porticos — how to check them initially by candlelight? Their words are rabbinic: no — not like one who lights at night he lights by day, etc. — see there; and so in the Gemara they said: when candlelight is good for checking — no; but if he comes to check on the night of the 13th, etc.; and so Yerushalmi there: why at night? R' Yosa — because checking by candle is good only at night; R' Mana did not say so — "and you shall observe this day" — make day and night guarded; begin on the 13th and day and night shall be guarded; he also has R' Yosa's reason — practical difference: what he wrote "shall be," etc. — that one must check at the beginning of night; this is proof for Raavad and Shulchan Aruch siman 431 s.k. 1: light begins on the 13th — this challenges s.v. not so even from Rosh Chodesh. Raavan brought proof from that Yerushalmi — it is different there because he did not check because of Pesach; and it says there even if he checked them on the 13th by day it helps b'dieved, as stated there — R' Yirmiyah: the mishnah said checking by day is checking, etc. — see there; and so Gemara 9b: there is no difficulty — this on the 14th, etc.; and Tosafot 4b s.v. one who rents, etc.

(ג) And everyone. As stated in Niddah there — presumption of Jewish girls, etc.; conclusion there: presumption is checked, and nevertheless at sweeping time they check specifically.

(ד) And pockets, etc., that they place. Mishnah and "with what," etc., and Gemara there 8a, as explained; in Mahari Weil he wrote as in the first chapter of Shabbat — great law, etc.
