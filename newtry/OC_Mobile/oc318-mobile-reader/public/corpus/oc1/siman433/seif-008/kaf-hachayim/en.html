(א) There, if he checked by sunlight, it suffices — and specifically a portico; but another house that is not a portico — although it has great light — requires checking by candlelight — Bigdei Yesha on Mordechai beginning Pesachim note 28 in the name of Ri'az — see there; Beit Yosef; but Bach wrote the same law wherever light is great as a portico — see there; so Rabbi Zalman note 37: other houses and rooms with many windows and great light have the law of a portico — end.

(ב) And one who has only a portico to destroy — nevertheless he blesses on destroying chametz; so too if he is where there is no candle and must destroy his house by day he blesses — Halakhot Ketanot part 2 siman 194; meaning if the house has great light like a portico so he can see in holes and cracks as explained — otherwise he did not fulfill the mitzvah properly and how can he bless; see below note 82.
