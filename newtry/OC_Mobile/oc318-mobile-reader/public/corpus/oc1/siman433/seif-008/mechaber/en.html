A wall that was used with chametz in its holes, and it collapsed and became a mound — even if not three handbreadths high, enough for a dog to dig — one need not check underneath, since there is danger of scorpions found in mounds; we are concerned that after he finishes his checking, no longer engaged in a mitzvah, he may search there for a lost needle and come to danger. This is in general; but if it is known there is chametz under a mound less than three handbreadths high, he must remove it with a hoe and shovel in a manner without danger. If it is three handbreadths high or more, he nullifies it in his heart, which suffices.
