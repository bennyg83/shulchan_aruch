Another house that is not a portico must be searched by candlelight even if it has abundant light. Bigdei Yesha on Mordechai, chapter 1 of Pesachim, subsection 28, in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham. See there, and see Beis Yosef. Bach, however, writes that the same law applies to any place having abundant light. See there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 37, likewise writes that other houses and rooms with many windows and abundant light have the law of a portico.
8) One who has only a portico to search, which does not require candlelight, nevertheless recites the blessing over eliminating chametz. Similarly, if one is in a place where no candle is available and must search the house during the day, one recites the blessing. Halachos Ketanos, volume 2, siman 194. This applies when the house has abundant light like a portico, enabling one to see into the holes and cracks, as explained above. Otherwise, one has not fulfilled the mitzvah in its prescribed manner and cannot recite a blessing. See below, subsection 82.
