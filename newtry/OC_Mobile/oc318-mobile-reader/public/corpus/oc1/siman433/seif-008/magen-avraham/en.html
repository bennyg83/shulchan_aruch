(א) Scorpion danger. It appears specifically where scorpions are found; therefore Rif and Rosh omitted it; perhaps where snakes are found one must check, for their harm is not as certain — see siman 107 seif 3.

(ב) After he finishes his checking. For during checking, even searching for a needle, he is an agent of a mitzvah, like one who says "this rock for charity so my sons live" — he is completely righteous even if he intends his benefit (Gemara).

(ג) That was lost to him. Beforehand (Rashi) — for during checking he is an agent of a mitzvah and is not harmed even in property.

(ד) And this is in general. Even though chametz was used in it, perhaps they will eat it — there are many doubts, as seif 6.

(ה) He nullifies in his heart. For Rashi and Ran, nullification is d'rabbanan, since with height of three handbreadths it is as concealed; for Semak, nullification is d'oraisa — so in Magen Avraham, Beit Yosef, siman 438; if he did not nullify until after prohibition time he must open the mound and destroy it since it is not in his power to nullify; but if a large mound fell that he cannot open and it is lost from him and everyone, it is permitted, for we do not call it "yours." Shach wrote: since he did not transgress "it shall not be seen," permitted even in eating after Pesach with no trickery concern since it fell from a collapse — end; requires study (Bach); Rashba responsum 70: Yerushalmi — chametz that fell from collapse is permitted after Pesach, but Rav Hai z"l forbade; so Moreh Nevukhim chapter 3 — see siman 436 seif 1 and siman 448 seif 5.
