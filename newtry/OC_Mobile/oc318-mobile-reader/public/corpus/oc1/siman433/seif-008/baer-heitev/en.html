(א) Scorpion. Specifically where scorpions are found; and see siman 104 seif 2 that implies a snake's harm is not certain and checking is required — Magen Avraham.

(ב) He nullifies it. Acharonim wrote he may eat it after Pesach since he did not transgress "it shall not be seen," and there is no concern for trickery since it fell from a collapse. Bach left this in doubt; Taz also divides this law; Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Rashba's responsum that Rav Hai forbids, and if he did not nullify it and it is after prohibition time he must clear the mound to destroy the chametz since it is no longer in his power to nullify — Acharonim; Magen Avraham wrote: if a large mound fell on him that he cannot open and it is lost from him and everyone, it is permitted even without nullifying, for we do not call it "yours."
