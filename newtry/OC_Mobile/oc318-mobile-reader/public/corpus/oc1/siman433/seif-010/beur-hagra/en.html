s.k. 10 Houses. Yerushalmi chapter 1 halacha 1: R' Yirmiyah asked — are synagogues and study halls required to be checked? What does he need — do they not bring chametz there on intercalated years and on Rosh Chodesh? So Ran's and Tur's text — it should be obvious to him that so it is; he needs it because their light is great — may one check them initially by day by candlelight, etc.; Ran and Tur learned the same for ours because of children — as stated regarding the attic — the inference: they were not concerned about falling; one fears a child will go there.
