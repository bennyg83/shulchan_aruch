Synagogues and batei midrash require checking. This means on the night of the fourteenth, as the later authorities wrote. Nevertheless, it appears that if he transgressed and did not check on the night of the fourteenth, he may check even initially on the day of the fourteenth by sunlight, like an achsadra, whose light is also abundant. So I found explicitly in the Mordechai, first chapter of Pesachim, in the name of the Yerushalmi. See above, siman 150, seif 4. I have not seen people careful to check the synagogue; they suffice with mere sweeping by day. It is fitting to warn the synagogue attendants about this. So too Mahari Weil wrote, and he further wrote that in some places they announce after checking the synagogue that the children should no longer bring chametz there.
