Checking. Meaning on the night of the 14th — Magen Avraham; and it appears if he passed and did not check on the night of the 14th, he checks even l'chatchila on the 14th by sunlight like a portico whose light is also great. I have not seen people careful about this to check the synagogue — sweeping suffices; one should warn the shamashim of the synagogue about this, and so Maharil. He also wrote some places announce after checking the synagogue that children should not come there again with chametz.
