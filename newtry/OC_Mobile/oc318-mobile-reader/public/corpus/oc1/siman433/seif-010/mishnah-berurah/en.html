(43) Require checking — on night of 14th by candle; shamashim are not careful to check at night but sweep well by day — not proper; they must be warned to fulfill the Sages' mitzvah properly and may bless on this bedika; but need not nullify afterward since they cannot nullify chametz not theirs. If he passed and did not check synagogues and study halls on night of 14th, he may check them l'chatchila on the 14th by daylight without candle, since they have many windows and great light — law like portico in seif 1 (Acharonim); per s.k. 7 if windows have glass, even opposite window is not like portico — when checking by sunlight he must open windows.
