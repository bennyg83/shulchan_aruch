(א) Holes of the house, cornices, and a bed dividing the whole house wall to wall — if there are wood and stones underneath, one need not check except the outer side facing outside; if no wood and stones underneath — if high enough to pass under without bending, check even the inner side; if low, no inner side checking, for certainly no person passed and will not use for anything; some disagree and hold one always checks only the outer side — first chapter Pesachim daf 8 and Tur.

(ב) Less than three handbreadths — and if chickens are found there, checking is also required even less than three handbreadths, as above (see Magen Avraham s.k. 9).
