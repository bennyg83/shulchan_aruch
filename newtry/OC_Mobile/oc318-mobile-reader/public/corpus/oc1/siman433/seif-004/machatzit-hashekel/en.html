(s.k. 4) Opposite, etc. — therefore Ri'az wrote it is forbidden to check opposite the window, etc. Beit Yosef brought Ri'az's words: he wrote specifically a portico, but another house — even if it has great light — requires checking by candlelight. It appears he wrote so because of what they said regarding a skylight in a room, and he did not explain fully: it should mean specifically from the sides, but in the middle of the room one checks by the skylight's light — end Beit Yosef; and so Magen Avraham: Ri'az speaks where he wrote "but another house" like our houses that have no skylight but windows, and Ri'az holds even opposite the window is forbidden, for it is not like a skylight — with this what Ri'az left unspecified is resolved. It is likewise in Rambam — I did not know a clear meaning: Rambam did not copy the skylight law at all; if Bach meant to settle why Rambam omitted skylight because it is included in his words that a portico whose light is great, etc., and Rava said only a portico is checked by its light to explain so, to learn from it for every place whose light is great — end; therefore Magen Avraham holds a window is also included in "great light." Although a skylight's light is greater, nevertheless Rambam did not mention skylight at all — Magen Avraham should have inferred so. Also from other poskim: author of Tzitz Eliezer and Bach also for Rambam — he did not copy skylight intending as Bach said for Rambam; also other poskim did not copy skylight law except Tur — see Bach. Perhaps because Rambam wrote in chapter 5 law 4: one does not check, etc., nor by sunlight, etc. — in what case? in holes and hiding places; but a portico whose light, etc. — he only excluded holes and hiding places, implying opposite a window is permitted. Shulchan Gavoah wrote: they check by daylight, etc. — implying even, etc.; in Shas and poskim sunlight was mentioned and one would think specifically when the sun shines; but from Shulchan Gavoah who wrote "by daylight" it implies "by sunlight" is not precise.
