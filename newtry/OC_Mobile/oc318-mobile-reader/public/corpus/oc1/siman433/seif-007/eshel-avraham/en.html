(א) And it suffices — but definite chametz must be destroyed, for it is like chametz in a pit — Bach; in truth in a pit they also dispute: for Raavad it is like hiding, other poskim hold only hiding with hands; in a pit no ladder needed to remove — see there; Taz; for Mechaber even by day checking suffices; but Bach in the name of Maharam requires at night and candle — Magen Avraham s.k. 12.

(ב) Danger — Bach asked: why not write the reason as seif 8; he inferred from this even definite chametz need not be removed; Chayei Adam answered: in a collapse it is the way of falling, in a hole it is not common; Magen Avraham rejected Bach's reasoning — see there.
