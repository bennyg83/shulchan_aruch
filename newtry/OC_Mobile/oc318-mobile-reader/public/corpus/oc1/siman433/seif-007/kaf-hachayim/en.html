(7) There, if he checked by sunlight, it suffices — this is Rambam's language chapter 2 law 4, implying specifically b'dieved; l'chatchila he must check at night by candlelight, as everywhere there; see Bach: three views — Rashi and Rif even on the 13th may check a portico by sunlight even l'chatchila; Rambam even on the 14th l'chatchila only by candlelight — so Semag and Semak; Ri'az and as Rosh's view: only on the 13th not, but on the 14th may check l'chatchila by sunlight; it implies on the 13th even if he checked by sunlight he must return at night per Rosh and Ri'az; conclusion: we rule stringently — only on the 14th if he checked b'dieved by sunlight it suffices — see there; however Magen Avraham s.k. 3 explained Shulchan Aruch: l'chatchila check on night of 14 by candle; if he did not check on night of 14, l'chatchila he may check portico by sunlight — not like Bach — see there; so from Levush on the 14th l'chatchila by sunlight; so Shulchan Arukh HaRav in Hagah Beit Yosef note 2; Peri Chadash; Chayei Adam note 3; from Maamar Mordechai note 3; Mekor Chayim annotation note 2; however Berakh Kehay note 2 and Chakat HaPesach note 2 and Kissei Shlomo note 1 explained Shulchan Aruch: even on the 14th l'chatchila by candle — see there; but Rabbi Zalman note 2 ruled like the first view that even l'chatchila without candle; so Ben Aryeh chapter 96 note 3; so most Acharonim; Rabbi Zalman wrote even on a cloudy day one may check a portico without candle.
