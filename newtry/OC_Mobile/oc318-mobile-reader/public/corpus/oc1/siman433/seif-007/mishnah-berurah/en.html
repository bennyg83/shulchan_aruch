(א) And the rest he nullifies in his heart — lest it rolled into the hole. Even if definite chametz was there, nullification also helps — not like hiding in a pit since he does not act with hands — see siman 436 whether benefit is permitted from such chametz after Pesach.
(ב) Lest he say sorcery — in what he searches at night by candle in holes; nevertheless tomorrow on the 14th he checks by daylight so a non-Jew will not suspect him.
(ג) Comes to danger — although agents of a mitzvah are not harmed where harm is common it is different; even though they exempted from night checking for this reason, nullification is still required; Acharonim wrote even definite chametz need not be checked at night but by day in daylight, and we certainly require nullification here, as above.
