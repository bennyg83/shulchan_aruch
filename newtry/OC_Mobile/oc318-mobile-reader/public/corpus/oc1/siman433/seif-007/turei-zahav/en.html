And between a Jew and a non-Jew. It would appear to say that even for definite chametz one need not check because of danger; and although regarding a mound definite chametz must be removed with a hoe and shovel — there is different, for there he can do so without danger, unlike here where he cannot act without danger; but in truth here too definite chametz requires checking — by day, as proven in the Gemara which asks "how did he use it" and answers "when he used it by day in light" — he does not consider it, as I wrote.
