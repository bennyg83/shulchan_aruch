And the rest he nullifies in his heart. The Bach wrote: but definite chametz must be removed, for it is like one who hides it in a pit. End quote. In truth, this matter is an old dispute among the poskim regarding chametz in a pit, whether one needs a ladder to raise it or whether mere nullification suffices, as explained below in siman 438. The view of the Raavad is that nullification does not suffice, for it is like hiding it in pits, whereas the Rambam and the other poskim do not hold so, because only one who hides it by active deed is called hiding it. So we rule, as explained below. It is strained to distinguish and say that here there is not so much exertion involved. Also, the plain language of the Talmud and the poskim implies that in every case nullification suffices. So too the Olas Shabbos ruled, though in the end he left it requiring study because he understood that the Bach cited this in the name of Rashi. This is not so, for it is only the Bach's own words. On the contrary, Rashi's view is the opposite, as the Taz wrote in seif katan 3. He also did not notice that this is a dispute among the early poskim. See below, siman 436, seif 1 in the gloss, whether it is permitted to benefit from such chametz after Pesach even though he nullified it, and see what I wrote there.
He is performing sorcery against me. The Kesef Mishneh wrote in the name of the Ramah that nevertheless, on the day of the fourteenth, he should check by daylight in the manner in which he used that place. So it is also implied in the Gemara; see there.
He comes into danger. Although agents of a mitzvah are not harmed, where harm is common it is different. So the Gemara answers there. It did not want to answer also that perhaps after completing the checking he will lose a needle, at which point he is no longer engaged in the mitzvah and will come into danger, as explained in seif 8. It appears to me simple that there, with a collapsed heap, it is relevant to suspect that he will lose a needle inside the heap, since that is the manner of falling. This is unlike here, in a hole in the wall, where such loss and falling is not the normal way and is uncommon. Therefore here it needed the reason that harm is common. So it appears to me. See what the later authorities wrote on this. It is clear from the words of the Bach that even with definite chametz one need not check here because of danger. According to what I wrote in seif katan 14, he has a remedy, that he should at least check by day.
