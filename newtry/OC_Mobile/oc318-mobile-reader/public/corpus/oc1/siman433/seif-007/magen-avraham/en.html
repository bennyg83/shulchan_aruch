(ב) Comes to danger. Although agents of a mitzvah are not harmed where harm is common it is different; Bach asks why not answer perhaps a needle was lost, as in seif 8; one may say here even for definite chametz he need not remove it; and it appears to me there is no proof from this difficulty — one may say it is different: a hole used daily — whence that he would now search for a needle; but in seif 8 it refers to a wall already used and fallen, not now in use — we fear he may now search for a needle already lost; Peri Chadash did not examine the Gemara and wrote on Bach that he did not understand it refers where he did not use it — if he did not use it, obviously no checking, as the Gemara explicitly states — see there; and it appears one must check by daylight all he can, as Rashi's language daf 8 end of 1a.
