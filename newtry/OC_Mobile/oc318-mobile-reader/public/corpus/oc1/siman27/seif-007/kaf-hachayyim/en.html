(7) One born without a left arm may be obligated in arm-tefillin according to everyone, although they dispute one whose arm was amputated after birth. To avoid a doubtful blessing, he should put on arm-tefillin as well, bless twice over the head-tefillin, and intend to cover both [Shevus Yaakov I 3; Yad Aharon]. Under our custom of only one blessing for both, he should say only Al Mitzvas Tefillin over the head-tefillin and intend it also for the arm-tefillin [Beis Oved].
