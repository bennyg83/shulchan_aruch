Se'if 7: Although, etc. Up to 'two pairs of tefillin.' So I found written. This implies that with a large wound he is exempt, as in se'if 1 [Magen Avraham].
For the bone, etc. This is the Mechaber's wording, following the gloss to Semak, whose meaning he misunderstood; in Bedek HaBayis he retracted. Eruvin likewise implies that the kibores itself has room for two pairs of tefillin. See above, se'if 1.
