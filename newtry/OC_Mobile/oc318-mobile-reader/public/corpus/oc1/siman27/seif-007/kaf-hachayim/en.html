(7) Born without left arm — possibly all agree obligated in arm tefillin despite dispute on later amputation. To resolve berachah safeik — don both and bless two on head intending both (Shevut Ya'akov 1:3; Yad Aharon on Mishnah Berurah Hagahot Taz 1). For us who bless once on both — bless only "al mitzvat tefillin" on head intending arm too (Beit Yosef letter 8).
