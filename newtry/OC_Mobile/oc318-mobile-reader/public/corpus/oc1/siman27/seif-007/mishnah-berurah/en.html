Until the elbow. Meaning on the raised flesh toward the elbow, not literally until the elbow. If the wound spread over all the raised flesh toward the elbow, he may rely on the lenient opinions to place it on the raised flesh of the upper half of the bone. If the wound is large and he suffers from placing the tefillin, he is exempt from the shel yad even if some space remains, and puts on only the shel rosh, as above in siman 26.
