There. On the right as by chalitzah. The questioner thought he should put them on the right, like a left-handed person. Rama answered that it is not comparable: by chalitzah, a left-handed man removes with the left, yet if his right was cut off he does not perform chalitzah at all. Furthermore, by tefillin the Merciful One made it depend on the weaker hand, and the right is not weak; this is unlike a left-handed person, whose right is weak.
