(s"k 7) Hat, etc. Regarding crown — Avodah Zarah 44a — placed David's crown (from Ammon) on head instead of tefillin; Gemara: must don tefillin; R. Shimon b. Yochai: place on head for two tefillin; Rashba per his view — could place on crown; Rashba answered: perhaps brain-droops place; Beit Yosef — same for thick hat.
