[9] And one should not wrap, etc. I do not know the reason; perhaps the strap lacks the sanctity of the titura, so one should not leave it on during the mitzvah; or it would be like two knots and violate bal tosif (Levush). In Eliyahu Zuta I omitted Maharil's reason: like the head "patty" (titura bulge) — one does not tighten on the head titura; it hangs on the strap inside the ma'avarta; so too the hand patty should hang on the strap without tightening (Lechem Chamudos 93). Perhaps Levush states the main reason even for head. I saw in Shiyurei Kenesset HaGedolah challenging Maharil: for head Scripture says "they shall be totafot between your eyes," for hand "you shall bind" — so far. I wonder: "you shall bind" surely applies to head too, as I clarified in se'if 7. I also found in Migdal David Maharil's reason: Chazal expounded "they shall be totafot" — one being for both — so far. Needs study per siman 28 se'ifim 2 and 5. Shelah 112: those who do not wrap the arm before head should place the hand strap on the titura until after head, lest it shift and require a new berachah — so far. Per Levush's reason that too seems forbidden; therefore wrap the arm immediately, as I wrote siman 25 se'if 11.
