The roots of the hair. 'Between your eyes' is not literal; we derive by verbal analogy from the prohibition of making a bald spot between the eyes for the dead. Just as there the place is where baldness is made, namely the head, so here the place of tefillin begins where baldness is relevant, from the beginning of the hair roots on the head. Many err and place the main tefillin on the forehead, thereby transgressing according to the Torah and reciting a blessing in vain. Even the lower edge of the titura must rest at the beginning of the hair roots. Long hairs lying on the forehead do not determine the place; the beginning is the main hairline of the forehead and upward. It is better to place them somewhat higher so they not slip down. One who places them on the forehead follows the Karaite practice and has not performed the mitzvah, and every careful person should warn his fellows.
