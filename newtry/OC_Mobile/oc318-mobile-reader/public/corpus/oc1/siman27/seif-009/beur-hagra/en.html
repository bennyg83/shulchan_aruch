Se'if 9: The place, etc. Menachos 37b calls it a place where the hair can be removed and which can contract impurity. 'Where a child's brain is soft' means up to that point; certainly below it is even more literally 'between your eyes.' Eruvin's final chapter says there is room there for two pairs. The Yerushalmi similarly says that the upper part of the head has room for two, and Rav Zeira explains: up to where a child's brain is soft. 'Up to the upper part' and 'up to where the brain is soft' imply from the beginning of the head until that point; 'has room' likewise means beginning at the hairline. See Sefer HaTerumah.
