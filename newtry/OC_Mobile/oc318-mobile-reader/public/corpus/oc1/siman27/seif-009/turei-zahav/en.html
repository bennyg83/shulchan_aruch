From the beginning of the roots of the hair. The phrase 'between your eyes' is not literal. Rather, we derive it through a verbal analogy from 'between your eyes' written regarding the dead: Just as there it refers to a place where one can make a bald patch, here too the proper place for the tefillin begins where making a bald patch is possible. Many people violate this prohibition. They mistakenly think that the upper edge should begin at the hairline, so that most of the tefillin rest on the forehead, and thereby violate a Torah prohibition. The entire tefillin must be positioned in the place where one can make a bald patch, meaning that their lower edge must be at the roots of the hair.
