From the beginning of the main points of the gate. P. Davin your eyes, is it not really, but a gemirinan in the Gash, from your eyes, the following is written in a place that makes a bald spot. Even here, the mikva tefillin begins from a place where a bald spot belongs: and many fail in this prohibition and make the mistake of saying that the upper edge starts from the place of the gate and the main part of the tefillin is placed on the forehead and violates the prohibition of Dauriyta.
