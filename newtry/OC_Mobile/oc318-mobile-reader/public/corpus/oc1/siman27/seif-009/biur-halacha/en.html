Until end, etc. Tur, Rabbeinu Yerucham — whole head height included; Sefer HaTerumah, Yerushalim (Gra copied); Rambam seems not — Beit Yosef; Semak, Kol Bo — until half slope of head — lechatchilah not oversized tefillin extending full height; generally better extend upward than lower toward forehead — satisfies many rishonim and Yerushalmi; not toward forehead (Ma'aseh Rav, glosses Rav Elazar Harlap — extend up not down; Ma'aseh Rav may agree not to raise to full head end — Semak, Kol Bo). Even Sefer HaTerumah permitting full height — one reading: not larger than four fingers with titura and ma'avarta (Beit Ya'akov) — two tefillin size in Gemara times; siman 32 s"א no measure — lechatchilah careful.
