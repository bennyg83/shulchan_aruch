Until the end, etc. Tur and Rabbeinu Yerucham imply the entire height of the head is included; Sefer HaTerumah and the cited Yerushalmi imply likewise. Rambam's plain wording appears otherwise, and Beis Yosef discusses it. Semak and Kol Bo explicitly limit the total tefillin area to halfway up the head's slope. Initially one should therefore avoid excessively large tefillin that extend over the entire head height. In general, it is better to extend them upward than lower them toward the forehead: upward placement is valid according to several Rishonim and supported by the Yerushalmi, unlike forehead placement. Maaseh Rav and Rabbi Elazar Charelap's manuscript gloss likewise imply it is better to raise them to prevent descent toward the forehead. Maaseh Rav may still agree not to raise them to the very top initially, satisfying Semak and Kol Bo. Even Sefer HaTerumah, who validates the entire height, appears in one explanation to limit their size to four fingerbreadths including titora and ma'abarta [Beis Yaakov], the space for two pairs in Talmudic times. Although siman 32:41 says there is no size limit, it is certainly proper initially to observe this.
