The hair. The Taz ruled that all of the tefillin must be placed in the place where baldness could be made, meaning that the lower edge must be at the roots of the hair. This is not like those who err and say that the upper edge begins at the hairline while the main tefillin lie on the forehead; they transgress a prohibition according to the Torah and recite a blessing in vain. Responsa Beis Yaakov, siman 131, disagrees with the Taz and rules that the titura, not the box, should begin at the roots of the hair. See Knesses HaGedolah.
