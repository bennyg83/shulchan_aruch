And one should not. See Taz, for there is a prohibition in the matter. It is not only the box; even on the titura it is explained that it is forbidden, and these are the words of the Levush. Lechem Chamudos and Eliyah Rabbah write that the reason is that shel yad is compared to shel rosh: just as there they hang, so too by shel yad. Those who put on shel yad as the Rosh in siman 25, seif 18, should likewise not place the strap on the titura for this reason.
