(9) Born with defective right hand — has right arm and forearm but does all work with left, claiming right is stronger — for matters not needing the hand-piece he holds between right forearm bones; dons tefillin on left. Right did not become left because cut; unlike lefty where right became left — he has two hands proving nature changed. With only one hand — no proof; who says left was not switched to right? Left that exists remains left; yet "yad" follows strength (Bach) — here right is stronger (She'erit Yehudah Oh 2; Pitchei Olam 4; see end of letter 4; below letter 31).
