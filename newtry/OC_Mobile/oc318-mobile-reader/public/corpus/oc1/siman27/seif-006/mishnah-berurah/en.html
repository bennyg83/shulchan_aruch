(21) Lefty — see above s"k 12.

(22) Right hand — habitual lefty (not born) — don right; heaven-caused (illness, right weakness, right palm amputated working left) — full lefty on weaker hand; if healed and equal — like everyone.

(23) All his work — majority of work suffices.

(24) Don left — if placed on his right (everyone's left) — bedi'eved not yotzei [Chut HaShani 28]; see Biur Halachah.

(25) Controls both hands — equal in all crafts; if easier on left though can use right — not "controls both" [siman 651].

(26) Weak hand — writing has no special status over other work; follow majority of deeds — that is called left.

(27) Writing hand is "right" — bind/write: tie on opposite hand. If later accustomed left write, right work — left like everyone. One who cannot write — follow which hand does most work as "right."

(28) Thus practiced — see Biur Halachah.
