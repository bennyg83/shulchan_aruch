And cover them. See Taz. The Beit Yosef implies that even if his wound is evident, others may learn from him to put them on without sufficient cause. Therefore Rama concludes that one who puts them on this way should bless only 'lehaniach.' But where he puts shel yad on a bandage, he recites two blessings on the shel rosh, as written in letter 5. One cannot say that he should place the shel yad somewhere not actually fit for its placement and recite two blessings on the shel rosh; this is erroneous, for when the shel yad is not in its proper place it is like lying in a pouch.
