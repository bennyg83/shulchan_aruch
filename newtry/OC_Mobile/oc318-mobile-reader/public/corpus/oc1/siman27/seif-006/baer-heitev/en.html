Anyone. If placed on his right (which is everyone's left) — even bedi'eved not yotzei. Second thread siman 28.

Right. Ambidextrous — left for everyone. Habitual left writer doing work with right — left for everyone (Magen Avraham; Eliyahu Rabbah on Magen Avraham, rules like Shulchan Aruch; one who cannot write — right if all actions with left).
