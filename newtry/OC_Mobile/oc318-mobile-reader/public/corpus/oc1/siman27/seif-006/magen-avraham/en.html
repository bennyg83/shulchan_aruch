Lefty. Beit Yosef Mordechai: if accustomed himself to lefty — ruled lefty; Mordechai — disputants there.

Writing hand. Semak: even writes left and works left — still don left since also writes right; Bach disputes Shulchan Aruch — if all work right, writes left — all agree don left; dispute only writes right, works left; we follow Shulchan Aruch; if later accustomed left write, right work — don left, rely on Bach.
