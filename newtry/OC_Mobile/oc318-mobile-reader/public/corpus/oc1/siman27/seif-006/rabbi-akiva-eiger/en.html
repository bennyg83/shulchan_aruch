Se'if 11: “To the navel or above.” The Zohar and the writings of the Arizal state that the right strap should extend to the navel and the left strap to the chest. This is also written in Shimusha Rabba, the Rif, the Rosh, and the Tur. The Tur further writes that some say the right strap should extend to the circumcision and the left strap to the navel.
