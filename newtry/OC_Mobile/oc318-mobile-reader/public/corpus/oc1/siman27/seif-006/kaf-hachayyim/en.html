(6) One who has no arm and is exempt from arm-tefillin must still put on head-tefillin [Shaagas Aryeh 37], as is also proven by the Riaz cited by Shiltei HaGibborim. Machazik Berachah, Beis Oved, and Shaarei Teshuvah cite this, unlike Responsa Tzemach Tzedek 5, whose view opposes the implication of all the poskim.
