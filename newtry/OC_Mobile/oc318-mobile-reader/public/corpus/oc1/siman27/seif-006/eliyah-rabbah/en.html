And some say that the hand with which he writes, etc. Since it is written 'you shall bind' and 'you shall write,' binding is compared to writing, which is with the right. Thus even if he does all his work with the right but writes with the left, he places on the right. The Bach and Olas Tamid objected, but Magen Avraham made a compromise. To me the primary view is like the Shulchan Aruch: this opinion does not derive from the weak hand at all, but from binding and writing. The words of Rabbenu Yechiel also imply that the matter depends principally on writing. If a person cannot write, it appears he should place on the right when he does all actions with the left.
