[6] And some say — hand that writes, etc. "Bind and write" — tying like writing on right — even if all work right but writes left — don on right. Bach and Olat Tamid challenged — all don left; writing not enough for "left," for also yadekha weak hand — see there at length. Magen Avraham compromise — if not born lefty but later accustomed writing left — rely on Bach. Main view like Shulchan Aruch — "some say" do not hold yadekha weak-hand drasha at all; learned left placement from bind/write alone; Menachot 36a, Rashi there; perhaps yadekha includes gidam; Mordechai seems even drasha holders hold yadekha weak hand, gidam also weak hand — Gemara meaning not so; Rashi; Mordechai end — see there. Shulchan Aruch learned from R. Yechiel in Semak gloss Beit Yosef: lefty should practice writing right too, then don left — main depends on writing — so far; even all work and write left — don left since also writes right — can fulfill right donning and bind/write since also writes left, and yadekha weak hand since all work left — but other work irrelevant; Mordechai Hilchot Tefillin question R. Elchanan to Rabbeinu Peretz on writing alone; Mordechai Ketan on parchment; Agur 40 — only when writes left; if writes right, etc. — all depends on writing. One who cannot write — don right if all work left.
