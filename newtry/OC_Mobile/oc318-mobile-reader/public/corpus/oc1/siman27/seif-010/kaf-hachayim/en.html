(10) Born without right hand-piece — does everything with left — dons on left like anyone (Teshuvat Divrei Nehemiah Oh 11). Tzitz Eliezer 5: don for prayer on complete left, may bless per our custom (one berachah for both), then also on right and may bless and read Shema — Pitchei Olam cite. Per siman 25 letter 78 — if donned intending to remove, no berachah when re-donning; for arm doubt — no berachah when wearing for safeik.
