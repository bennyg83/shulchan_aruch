From the beginning. See Taz: from the beginning of the main roots of the hair begins the place of tefillin, and it ends where an infant's skull is soft. Eliyah Rabbah implies that a bit of the box begins where the hair roots begin at the forehead and the other side is above, but not the box alone, for the entire titura must be in the place of hair. Main hair roots do not mean long hairs that lie halfway down the forehead. The measure of the rear knot is explained in Shulchan Aruch; the knot must be high behind, at the end of the skull.
What the Rav wrote in the gloss of seif 1, to place at the head of the bone but not on the half, I do not know what he teaches us, for the Mechaber wrote this explicitly in seif 7, and these are the words of the Semak. See Magen Avraham, letter 2. This currently requires study.
