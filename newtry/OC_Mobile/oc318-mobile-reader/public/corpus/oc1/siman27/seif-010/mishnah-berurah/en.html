At the nape. It is the end of the skull opposite the face, not so low opposite the throat. It is good for the main knot to be above the hollow, and one must at least be careful that no part of the knot rest in a hairless place, for there is the back of the neck and not the nape. The strap around the head must be fitted and tight, not larger than the head, both because actual tightening is needed for binding and because loose straps cause the box or knot to fall out of place.
That it be in the middle. In the width of the head, not leaning to one side, so that 'they shall be frontlets between your eyes' is fulfilled. Many later authorities wrote that if he changed this he did not fulfill the mitzvah. Initially one should also avoid overly large tefillin, for then it is almost impossible for them to be tightened on the head and also rest in their place.
That it appears like a daled. Two straps emerge below the knot: the one from the wearer's left extends across like the roof of the daled, and the one from the right extends downward like the leg of the daled. He should see that the knot not become reversed in making it or wearing it; see below, siman 32, seif 52.
