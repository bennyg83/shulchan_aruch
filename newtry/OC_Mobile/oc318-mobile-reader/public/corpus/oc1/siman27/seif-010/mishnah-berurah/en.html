(35) On nape — end of skull, opposite face, not throat level; bulk of knot above hairline; no knot on bare neck (cervical joint not oref); head straps tight — loop not larger than head — else two problems: need real tightening ("bind them"); if loose — patty falls to forehead or knot to neck.

(36) Centered — head width; not tilted — totafot between eyes; many acharonim: if changed — did not fulfill; lechatchilah not oversized — cannot tighten; at proper height not tight, knot wrong — siman 32 se'if 41.

(37) Dalet shape visible — two straps from knot: wearer's left strap horizontal (dalet roof), right vertical (leg) [Ateret Zahav]; knot not reversed when made or worn — siman 32 se'if 52.
