(s"k 5) On straps, etc. Arm and head not equal — asked Rashba: wrap arm straps on garment not on flesh — like head straps on garment? Answered: head no proof — even head bayit no disqualifying chatzitzah; arm specifically — "sign for you" not others; head — totafot between eyes only fixes placement, not chatzitzah prohibition; cannot learn arm from head straps; anyway arm strap on garment known — otherwise "for you" includes strap on hand wrapping finger exposed; verse "for you" only on bayit. Rashba lenient two points: arm straps on garment; head even bayit no chatzitzah disqualifies. Concludes lehalachah but lema'aseh arm straps and head bayit — see Rabbi Yosef — lema'aseh Rashba did not permit straps on garment; how Rama decided lema'aseh? — on straps not stringent.

As said at start. Answered questioner about wrapping on garment — did not want to permit lema'aseh.

Were it not, etc. Place straps on thin cloth and bless — though se'if 5 head on thin garment — no berachah; if head wound — bayit on flesh, straps on wound — thin cloth, wrap straps on cloth — two advantages: head where Rashba lenient on bayit; chatzitzah only encircling straps not between bayit; arm straps Rashba lenient; two stringencies lenient — may bless; all permit even Rosh.
