The head tefillin over a cap. See Beit Yosef. All the authorities discussed specifically the head tefillin. If one has a wound on his arm, he may not place the arm tefillin over a garment, because the verse says, “It shall be a sign for you,” and not a sign for others, as explained at the end of this siman, contrary to Bach. For this reason, where there is no alternative, it appears permissible to place the tefillin over the bandages covering the wound, provided another garment is worn over them. The kohanim could not place tefillin over their tunics because they were forbidden to wear an additional garment over the priestly vestments.
The thin cap close to the head. It is forbidden to place the head tefillin over a thick cap, because that is not considered placement upon the head at the location where an infant’s skull is soft. The same principle is stated concerning the crown of the Davidic kings, over which tefillin could not be placed.
He should not recite a blessing. If he places the arm tefillin in this manner, he should not recite the blessing, because some authorities hold that an intervening substance invalidates the placement. See siman 26. See citations 12.
