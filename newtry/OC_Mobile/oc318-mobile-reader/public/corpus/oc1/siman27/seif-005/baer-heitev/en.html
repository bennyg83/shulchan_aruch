Thin. On a thick cap it is forbidden. If he has a wound on his arm, it is forbidden to place them on the garment because of 'for a sign for you' and not a sign for others, but it is permitted to place it on the shirt when there is no other way, since another garment is worn over it. Magen Avraham.
Of the hand. The same applies if he places the tefillin shel yad in this manner: he should not bless on the shel yad, but only on the shel rosh. Magen Avraham. The Bach wrote that nevertheless, at the time of Krias Shema and prayer he should see that it be without any interposition; see there.
