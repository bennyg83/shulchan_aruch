"Yud." See Baer Heitev. Shalmei Tzibbur writes that in Zer Zahav, Mahari Tzemach proved from the words of the Arizal that the yud of the knot must never move away from the bayit, even while the tefillin are resting in their bag. Therefore many God-fearing people customarily tie the yud to the tefillin with a thread of sinew, and do not tie it to the bayit by means of the strap at the time they put on the tefillin. In my view this requires study, because through such a knot the sinew thread will surround the titorah and may constitute a chatzitzah between the arm and the tefillin. Perhaps, however, since the knotting thread is at the end of the titorah, in the area of the ma'abarta, a chatzitzah there is of no concern, and the concern applies only beneath the portion of the titorah where the parshiyot are situated. This requires study. Regarding the statement that it should not be tied with the strap, see above, seif katan 15.
