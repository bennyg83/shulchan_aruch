The thin cap, etc. The Bach forbids this during Krias Shema and prayer, requiring no interposition at all. Mekor Chaim wrote that it is good to instruct Jews and assure them not to worry about this, for one who puts on tefillin with proper intent will certainly not be harmed. Regarding the shel yad, it appears from the Bach and Taz that all agree it is forbidden, though Mateh Moshe permits placing it over bandages.
