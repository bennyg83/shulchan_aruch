Prone to discharge. Taz Rashba/Rivash (Beer HaGolah) — not from "on hand" but "for you"; head — "they shall see"; thin hat only — thick cannot aim infant-brain place (Avodah Zarah crown). Arm: forbidden on rags — "for you/not others" even under clothes — on flesh where "for you"; Rav Ashi torn clothes — Beit Yosef permits "for you" place; Magen Avraham 21,6 — rags for head; siman 26 Magen Avraham 1 explained; Arukh HaTaz letter 5 permits arm on rags; therefore arm without berachah, head two berachot; thin hat — one berachah like arm siman 26; letter 6.
