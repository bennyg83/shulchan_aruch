Prone to discharge. This means that his head hurts and exposing it would cause the cold to harm him. Since some authorities maintain that there is no concern of an intervening object with head tefillin; for the verse states, 'All the peoples of the earth shall see that the name of Hashem is called upon you,' while 'between your eyes' was said only to designate the location; this person, who is acting under compulsion, may rely upon that view. Nevertheless, he should place them over a thin cap, for then he can position them opposite the place where an infant's skull is soft, unlike when wearing a large cap.
He should cover them because of those who see him. For they do not know that he is acting under compulsion.
