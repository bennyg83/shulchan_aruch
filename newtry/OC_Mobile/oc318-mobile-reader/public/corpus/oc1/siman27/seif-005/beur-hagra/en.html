Se'if 5 — person, etc. Rashba permits head on hat (Megillah 24b "venatnah al rosho"); Rashi ad loc. — not same as head tefillin "ve'aru…"; Rashba lehalachah only, poskim argue — rely bedi'eved when needed.

Thin hat. Thick forbidden — Yerushalmi Berakhot 2 — R' Yochanan summer: wore both; winter: only outer when head covered; Rosh Hilchot Tefillin; fits Beit Yosef Magen Avraham 1.

And he covers them, etc. See above siman 30 s"ד — place his hand, etc.; and Magen Avraham there s"k 6.

And one who places them, etc. For all poskim disagree, and even Rashba was in doubt as above.
