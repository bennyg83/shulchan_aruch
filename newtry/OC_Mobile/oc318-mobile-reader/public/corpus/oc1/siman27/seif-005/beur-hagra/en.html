Se'if 5: A person, etc. Rashba permits head-tefillin over a cap based on Megillah 24b. Rashi there implies that placement over clothing is objectionable specifically because of the exposition 'for you as a sign,' which does not apply to head-tefillin, where it says, 'All the peoples shall see.' Although Rashba wrote this only as a theoretical ruling and all other poskim disagree, one may rely upon it when no alternative is possible.
A thin cap. A thick one is certainly forbidden. The Yerushalmi, Berachos 2, says that in summer, when Rabbi Yochanan's head did not hurt, he wore both tefillin; in winter, when his head hurt, he wore only the arm-tefillin. See Rosh, Hilchos Tefillin. This also resolves the question in Avodah Zarah 44a, 'But he must put on tefillin.'
And he should cover them, etc. See below, siman 30:4, 'he should place his hand,' and Magen Avraham there, paragraph 6.
And one who puts them on, etc. For all poskim disagree, and even Rashba himself remained uncertain, as above.
