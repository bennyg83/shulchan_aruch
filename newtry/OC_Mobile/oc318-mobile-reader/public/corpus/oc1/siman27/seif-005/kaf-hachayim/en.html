(5) There — gloss. No hand, only arm, etc. If no arm either — fully exempt, need not don on right (Magen Avraham; Bi'urei HaGra 6; Panim Me'irot 3:11; Siddur Beit Yosef 6). Some say don on right (Tiferet Shmuel on Rosh Hilchot Tefillin 18:2 citing Rashal; Pitchei Olam 4) — don right without berachah, then head with berachah intending to cover arm.
