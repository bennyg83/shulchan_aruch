The straps should not be wound around the titora to secure the tefillin; rather, they should hang like those of the head tefillin. The Torah's command “You shall bind them” is fulfilled by the knot made with the strap that passes through the ma'abarta. Darkei Moshe. The reason is that the Sages expounded the verse “They shall be as frontlets between your eyes” to mean that the two tefillin must have one manner of attachment. This applies to fastening them firmly and tightly against a person's flesh.
