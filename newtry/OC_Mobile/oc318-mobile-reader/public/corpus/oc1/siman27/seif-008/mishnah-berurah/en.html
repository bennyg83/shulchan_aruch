Three wraps. Two on the lower joint and one on the middle joint. Some say first one on the middle joint and then two on the lower joint. These wraps are made after putting on the shel rosh.
Or seven. We practice seven. Shaarei Teshuvah concludes that these seven wraps should be made only on the forearm, unlike some who make three wraps on the kibores and four on the forearm.
And one should not wrap. The mitzvah of binding has already been fulfilled by the strap on the maavarta, as with the tefillin shel rosh that hang by the strap in the maavarta. Since there is no mitzvah at all in this wrapping, one should not place it on the titura, which has greater sanctity than the strap. Those who put on the shel rosh before wrapping on the arm may wrap the strap over the box so that the shel yad not move until they put on the shel rosh, then remove it and make the seven wraps. One whose sleeves are narrow and the tefillin move because of this may wrap around the titura to strengthen them.
