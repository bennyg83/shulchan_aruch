(l) Three covers - two in the lower chapter and one in the middle chapter and 11 Dathila A in the middle chapter and then B in the lower chapter. And he will do the same bindings according to the assumption of the head:

(no) or seven - and we used seven [last] and refer to the Sha'at, which concludes that it is necessary to do these 7 covers only on the arm, rather than the few that are customary to make 3 covers on the burial and 4 on the arm:

(Lev) And it is not to be wrapped - we have already done and tied them in the strip on the transom, and like the tefillin of a head that hangs from the strip inside the transom, and since there is no mitzvah at all in the wrap, therefore it should not be placed on the Torah, which is holier than the strip. And those who customarily place the Sha'ar before they wrap eleven folds on the arm to wrap the strip on the patty so that it is not removed from its place until the Sha'ar is placed and after that he puts it on and wraps the seven folds.
