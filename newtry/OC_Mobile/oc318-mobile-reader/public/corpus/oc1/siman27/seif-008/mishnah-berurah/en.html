(30) Three wrappings — two lower knuckle, one middle; some: one middle then two lower; after head tefillin donned.

(31) Or seven — we practice seven [acharonim]; Sha'arei Teshuvah: seven only on arm — not three on kubitza and four on arm as some do.

(32) Do not wrap on titura — "bind them" fulfilled on ma'avarta strap; like head hanging on ma'avarta strap; no mitzvah in wrappings — don't place on holy titura. Some don head first then strap on patty to prevent shift — then remove and wrap seven; narrow arms — may wrap titura to stabilize [Aruch HaChayyim].
