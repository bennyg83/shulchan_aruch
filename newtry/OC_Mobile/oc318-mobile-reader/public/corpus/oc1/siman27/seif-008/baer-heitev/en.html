Wraps. One on the middle joint and two on the lower joint. The Arizal.
Wraps. So too wrote the Arizal; see Yad Aharon.
The titura. Rather, it should hang like the tefillin shel rosh, according to Maharil. Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah disagrees with Maharil and wrote: go out and see what the people do, for everyone customarily ties the maavarta with the straps. Ateres Zekenim also wrote from the implication of the Shulchan Aruch and Rabbi Yitzchak Luria that one should place the strap of the shel yad on the titura immediately after tying so that it not move from its place, for if it moves he may come to a doubtful blessing. Then he ties three wraps on the kibores and four on the arm, and afterward puts on the shel rosh; see above, siman 25, seif katan 12.
