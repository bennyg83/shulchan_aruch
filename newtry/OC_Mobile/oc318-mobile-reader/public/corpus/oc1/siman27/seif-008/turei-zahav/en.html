On his finger three bindings. According to the Ari's intentions, two of them will be on the lower knuckles of the finger and the third on the middle joint and its flavor is on the Kabbalah:
One should not wind, etc. Maharil writes there that some customarily do so in order to fulfill through this the words 'you shall bind them.' Maharil does not consider this correct, since that command has already been fulfilled with the strap that passes through the ma'abarta. Rather, the straps should hang down as they do with the head tefillin. Since this is not part of the mitzvah, the strap should not be placed over the ketzitzah, which has greater sanctity than the strap.
