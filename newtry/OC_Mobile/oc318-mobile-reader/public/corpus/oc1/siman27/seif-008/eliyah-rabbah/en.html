[8] Three wrappings, etc. Taz cites the Ari's kavanah: two on the lower knuckle of the finger and the third atop them on the middle knuckle — so far. I saw in Makor Chaim 73 that the Ari z"l would tie upward and place a wrapping in the middle — three — then wrap seven around the arm, don head tefillin, return and wrap two on the arm — nine wrappings, three of three; then wrap three on the middle finger, first on the middle knuckle and two others on the upper knuckle — so far. So too Devarim Chachamim in the Ari's writings: wrap the strap on the bayit three times like a shin — so far. One who practices chassidut should follow all above. In any case Reishit Chochmah explains it is forbidden for everyone to wrap finger-wrappings before donning head tefillin, and finger-wrappings must be done standing. This needs study: from Makor Chaim the first seems specifically on the middle knuckle, while the Taz above implies the third wrapping is on the middle knuckle.
