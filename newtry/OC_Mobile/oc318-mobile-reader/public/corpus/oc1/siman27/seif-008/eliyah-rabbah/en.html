Three wraps, etc. The Taz wrote in the name of the Arizal's kavanos that two wraps should be on the lower joint of the finger and the third above them on the middle joint. Mekor Chaim records the Arizal's practice differently, that he would tie above and put one wrap in the middle, making three, and then two below. See there.
And afterward, etc. He should first put on the shel rosh and afterward make the wraps on the finger, for the wraps on the finger are not the essential binding of the shel yad; see the authorities cited there.
