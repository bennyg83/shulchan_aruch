[H] Three covers, etc. The text of the 16th century, in the name of the Ari's intention, states that two of them will be on the lower knuckle of a finger and the third on top of them in the middle knuckle, so far. And I saw in the book Makor Chaim page 13 that he wrote this to his tongue, the late Ari would tie it up and put a cover in the middle, that is three, and then wrap seven covers around the arm and place a head prayer and go back and wrap two covers on the arm, nine covers, three of three, and then wrap three covers on the middle finger, first in the middle chapter and two covers Others in the last chapter so far have been changed. And also in the book of the Rabbis of the Sages, he wrote in the name of the Ari's writings that the band of the burying ground should be folded three times, as in S., up to this point. And one who practices chassidism should follow all of the things mentioned above. And from every place it is explained in Berishit Chochma, it is permissible for all scholars to wrap the bandages around the finger before placing it on the head, and likewise one should do the bandages around the finger while standing.
