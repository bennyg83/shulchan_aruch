Se'if 8 — length, etc. Tosafot there d"h vekama; but Aruch, etc.; and Rif and Rambam as Shulchan Aruch; explained from there Rabba tied for them — on the finger, needs greater measure; R. Acha — wraps three times and ties — should be reversed, but because Rabba mentioned tie first he also mentioned Mar brei d'Ravina, etc., like us — did not tie on finger at all (Mordechai). More plausible: "tie" means on the arm — knot and tightening; "Rabba tied" and stretched — after knot stretched and threw — did not wrap middle finger; R. Acha; Rambam "and tie" — otherwise wrappings not fulfilled; we do not tie there since not me'akev, only noted for fulfillment; Mordechai: they took main view of R. Acha; see Mishnah Berurah 1 — took main view of Mar brei d'Ravina; must be lechumra unlike there (bal tosif).

And stretch. As explained — Rabba tied and stretched, etc.

Middle finger. As Tosafot there — tzareda is middle, as Tosefta and Yoma first chapter.

And we practice, etc. Not from Gemara law; possibly included in Mar brei d'Ravina's words.

And one should not, etc. Taz and Magen Avraham.
