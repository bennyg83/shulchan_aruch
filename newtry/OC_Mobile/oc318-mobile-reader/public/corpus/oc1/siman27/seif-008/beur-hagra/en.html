Se'if 8: The length, etc. See Tosafos there, s.v. Vekamah. Aruch and the other explanation understand the measure as stated here; all the Geonim and Rif explain so, and Rambam's wording matches the Shulchan Aruch. The Gemara's statement that Rabbah tied them means upon the finger, requiring extra length. Rav Acha wrapped three times and tied. Mordechai explains the order as incidental. More likely 'tied' means fastening on the arm: Rabbah first fastened them, then stretched and let the straps fall, without wrapping the middle finger; Rav Acha wrapped. Rambam mentions tying because otherwise the wraps would not remain, but we do not tie there because the wraps are not indispensable. Mordechai says the halachah follows Rav Acha. Elsewhere the halachah follows Mar son of Ravina; here we act stringently, unlike there where stringency would create bal tosif.
And it should be stretched. As the Gemara says: Rabbah tied and stretched them.
The middle finger. As Tosafos explains there, 'tzarda' is the middle finger, in accordance with the Tosefta and the end of Yoma's first chapter.
And the custom is, etc. This is not found as law in the Gemara; perhaps it is included in the words of Mar son of Ravina.
And one should not, etc. See Taz and Magen Avraham.
