Upon. See Taz. He should wrap six or seven wraps, put on the shel rosh, and afterward wrap three wraps on the middle finger: first two on the lower joint and one on the middle joint. Eliyah Rabbah, in the name of Mekor Chaim, has one wrap on the middle joint and afterward two on the lower joint. Magen Avraham's 'upper joint' may mean the middle.
