(3) There, gloss: It must be placed on the upper portion of the bone near the elbow, etc. The entire half of the upper-arm bone adjacent to the elbow is valid, as Beis Yosef writes from the gloss to Semak and Shimusha Rabba; so rule se'if 7, Levush, and later authorities. Although Bedek HaBayis says not to rely on this for the entire half but only the bulging flesh, Maran apparently retracted when later composing Shulchan Aruch. Rambam and Tur can likewise be read this way. 'From its midpoint to the elbow' in se'if 7 does not mean literally up to the elbow, where there is no bulging flesh, but excludes the half toward the armpit [Pri Megadim; Shulchan Aruch HaRav; Chayei Adam; Ben Ish Chai]. The Gra, however, holds that all bulging flesh along the entire upper arm between elbow and armpit is valid; Chayei Adam and Ben Ish Chai cite him. Ben Ish Chai says one wearing Rashi and Rabbeinu Tam tefillin together should initially make them small enough to fit on the bulging flesh of the lower half. When impossible, he may rely on the Gra that the whole kibores is valid. Although Chayei Adam says not to bless when placing on the upper half, for us there is no concern of a doubtful blessing, since one blessing covers both arm- and head-tefillin and he also wears Rabbeinu Tam arm-tefillin. See Kol Yaakov, siman 34, paragraph 10.
