An interposing item. See Taz. These are the words of the Rosh: 'upon your arm' is like the priestly garments 'upon his flesh,' that there be no interposition, and likewise by shel rosh, 'between your eyes.' Initially, without compulsion, interposition is certainly forbidden. But one who is prone to flow may rely on the Rashba's reasoning, that by shel yad it is forbidden because of 'for you a sign' and not for others, while by shel rosh it is permitted, as written in letter 5. In Olas Tamid, letter 9, 'for you a sign and not for others' belongs in seif 5, not seif 4.
