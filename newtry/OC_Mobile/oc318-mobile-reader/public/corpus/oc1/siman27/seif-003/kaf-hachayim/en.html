(3) There — in the gloss. Place on the head of the bone near the cubit — meaning the half of the bone called "arm" adjacent to the cubit is kosher (Beit Yosef citing Semak and Shibolei Leket; Shulchan Aruch below se'if 7; Levush and acharonim). Though Maran in Bedek HaBayit wrote not to rely on permitting the whole half except the swollen flesh — perhaps when compiling Shulchan Aruch he retracted and ruled like Semak (Rambam/Tur support; Shulchan Aruch compiled after Bedek HaBayit — Kenesset HaGedolah end general rules 52; Ma'amar Mordechai 1). Se'if 7 "from half to cubit" — not literally to the cubit where there is no swelling, only the half of the bone near the armpit (Peri Megadim A"A 2; Raz 2; Chayei Adam 14:10; Ben Ish Chai Vayera 6). Gra (s"k 5): all swollen flesh on the joint between cubit and armpit is kosher — "cubit" in Gemara language (Piskei HaGra 1; Chayei Adam; Ben Ish Chai). Ben Ish Chai: lechatchilah follow poskim who forbid the upper half; one wearing Rashi and R"T together should make them small to fit swollen flesh in the first half; where impossible — rely on Gra that all cubit-area flesh is valid. Chayei Adam: if placed on upper half of bone — no berachah concern lehakel, since we bless once for both; also wears Rashi/R"T — may bless (Kol Ya'akov 34 letter 10).
