Toward the heart, etc. Responsa Nachalas Shivah, siman 41, discusses a left-handed person who wished to put on tefillin of a non-left-handed person and had to move the strap to the other side, whether he may bless although the yud is reversed opposite the heart or must first fix the yud. He answered that there is no need to be particular, but I wonder, for in siman 32, seifim 46 and 47, we rule that the beginning of the parsha must be toward the reader's right, not toward the heart. There is therefore no distinction between placing on the right or left. Also, perhaps there is greater concern regarding the yud than the beginning of the parsha. It appears from Terumas HaDeshen that changing the strap alone does not help; the knot must be fixed. Therefore one should be careful.
