(s"k 3) Amputee, etc. Question: unlike those who hold like Tosafot — if so all obligate when he has an arm; if they disagree — Tanna Kamma would exempt only with no arm, but with arm would admit obligation.

Others obligate even without an arm. Or Zaru'a exempts even with arm — unlike them; his girsa was "he has only an arm" — implying Tanna Kamma exempts even with arm; Or Zaru'a rules like Tanna Kamma.

I wondered, etc. Like chalitzah — questioner thought don right like lefty; answer: amputation worse than lefty. Chalitzah proves: we require removing with right; lefty uses left foot; if right foot cut — no chalitzah on left. So too tefillin require weak hand — if lefty, his right is weak; if left amputated, why call right weak? Rather all his power is in the right.

Is this not Talmud, etc. He does not rule from his own reasoning and did not cite Gemara at all. Sifrei Va'etchanan; Yalkut Bo remez 222: al yadekha is left; R. Natan; R. Yechiel HaChorom (place name per Tosafot); we find right called yad — what teach al yadekha? (so too Menachot 37); Sifrei adds in R. Yehudah's words and concludes what teach — he would give on right.

Implies he has no arm at all yet must don on right — why did Tosafot write "others" only when he has arm but no arm exempts?

And some say he holds, etc. Tosafot hold R. Yechiel HaChorom too — only when he has arm — still dons on right, not on arm, for forearm is not called hand here.

Still question on R. Eliezer's two answers, etc. Gemara challenges R. Yechiel HaChorom who holds right also yad — whence don left? R. Eliezer answers from bind/write — writing right, tying right — therefore don left. R. Eliezer: yad means right; here yadekha — weak left. How say R. Yechiel drashes yadekha to don left? Sifrei: R. Yehudah needed yadekha to include gidam.

Still question — R. Eliezer states his own reason. No proof for Tosafot to reject Sifrei — R. Eliezer not answering R. Yechiel (who needs bind/write); R. Eliezer himself holds right is yad, yet knows don left from yadekha weak hand — R. Eliezer does not hold like R. Yehudah including gidam.

And as written se'if 6 — we do not rule like, etc. Per Rama custom — all depends which hand writes — called right though all work other hand; therefore reason like R. Natan bind/write, not weak hand — since all work left, right is weak hand; yet right that writes is called right — therefore drasha bind/write.

And in Mordechai, etc. Two anomalies — settles above difficulty for R. Eliezer drashing yadekha weak hand — R. Yehudah expands yadekha gidam; Mordechai explains — two anomalies: weak hand and gidam.

One who drashes this, etc. Challenged: writes left, work right — don right? Why need yadekha for weak hand — teach bind/write; or needed for writes left/work right — thought don right, must tie right — teaches weak hand — don left since all work right. Magen Avraham: not a difficulty — bind/write drasher does not drash yadekha; yadekha drasher does not drash bind/write — exempt from donning on right; even if forearm per Or Zaru'a.

But per Tosafot interpretation, etc. Without berachah — not that Tosafot never bless — surely bless since all obligate when has arm; for us who are stringent like Tosafot — safeik berachah since Or Zaru'a exempts — do not bless.

Know what Shulchan Aruch wrote se'if 2 and 3 customs, etc. Lefty with only non-lefty tefillin or reverse — yud must face heart; then yud on left side of bayit; non-lefty opposite — how act?

Teshuvat Nachalat Shiva 41 — pull strap from ma'avarta, reverse to other side so yud near heart; though yud still reversed. Shevut Ya'akov disputes — no need — leave strap in ma'avarta, flip tefillin — patty to armpit, ma'avarta to cubit — though not usual custom in Shulchan Aruch; not so stringent — see there.
