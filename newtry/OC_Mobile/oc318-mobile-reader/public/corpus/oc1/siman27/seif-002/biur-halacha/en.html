Correct custom — yud of knot, etc. Beit Yosef Mahari ben Chaviv — knot and yud below bayit toward heart; strap insertion above bayit (correct per Darkhei Moshe); Darkhei Moshe: better our custom — strap and fold to his right (heart side); Bahag — tying itself toward heart; Rosh end letter 12 — distance knot from ma'avarta (Ma'ayan Ganim 20 — perhaps other reading — not strained per Bahag) — do not widen fold much — tightening place near bayit, not below; yud knot not far below — se'if 2; all in Gemara knot above not below. Shulchan Aruch ostensibly like Mahari ben Chaviv — yud of knot implies insertion not toward heart but outside — reject: only to avoid Baal HaMa'or (bayit to heart, knot outside) — permission how he wishes. We prefer Darkhei Moshe; one practicing Mahari ben Chaviv — do not object — support; his custom — yud never leaves bayit; our custom needs constant care — other advice in Mishnah Berurah.

One must be careful — Gra cited as above — this rule also from Gemara — cannot distance knot from tefillin, as he wrote earlier.
