The elbow. See Taz. In Menachos 34b one opinion says: just as the head placement is on the height, so too this is on the height; another derives from 'for a sign for you'; another from 'upon your heart.' It seems their dispute is as follows: according to the view of height, the raised flesh of the forearm may suffice; according to 'for a sign for you,' only the palm is excluded; according to 'upon your heart,' the kibores opposite the heart means the actual upper arm. The Levush brought the derivations of height and 'for a sign,' and both are necessary. See Magen Avraham, letter 2. What the Taz wrote, that until the elbow is the way to be exposed, is also written by the Levush. The Gemara at Menachos 37b permits where the arm was torn and the tefillin were visible in the place of 'for you a sign'; see Magen Avraham, letter 21. From here there is proof that until the elbow is the way to be exposed. The Rambam wrote like the opinion of 'upon your heart.'
