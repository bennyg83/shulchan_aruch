(11) [Se'if 2] The proper custom is that the yud of the arm-tefillin knot face the heart, etc. Maran calls this 'the proper custom' because the matter is disputed. Menachos 35b says the tefillin knot must face inward. Nimukei Yosef applies this to arm-tefillin: the knot should be inside the end of the arm, toward the heart. Other commentators apply it to head-tefillin. Baal HaItur makes it dependent on custom, with the knot inclining slightly right. Mahari ben Chaviv saw both customs and concludes that the second is correct: the yud-knot is made at the strap's end and the doubled strap inserted through the right side of the ma'abarta, leaving the knot and yud below the bayis near the heart. Therefore Shulchan Aruch calls it proper, taking account of Nimukei Yosef and Mahari ben Chaviv. Likewise, the next se'if calls it proper to arrange the ma'abarta toward the shoulder. Terumas HaDeshen reports opposite customs, and Beis Yosef says the world now follows the first: ma'abarta toward the shoulder and ketzitzah toward the hand. Since Maran describes a proper custom rather than an absolute prohibition, one may deviate under pressure. A left-handed person who lacks tefillin made with the yud inward for the right arm may borrow ordinary tefillin, put them on the right with the ma'abarta toward the hand and ketzitzah toward the shoulder, and keep the yud-knot inward toward the heart. The same applies to one who must, because of doubt, place on both arms but cannot afford two arm-tefillin. Conversely, an ordinary traveler who can borrow only a left-handed person's tefillin may bless and put them on this way. There is no doubtful-blessing concern because his blessing covers both arm- and head-tefillin [Shevus Yaakov I 3, disagreeing with Nachalas Shivah 41; Yad Aharon; Shaliach Tzibbur; Beis Oved; Yeshuos Yaakov; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch; Chayei Adam and Nishmas Adam]. It is no concern that the first passage was formerly on the reader's right and is now on his left; see Kol Yaakov, siman 32, paragraph 214. See also paragraph 31 below.
