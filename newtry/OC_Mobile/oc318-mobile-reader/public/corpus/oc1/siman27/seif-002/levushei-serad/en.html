Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. On half the bone. This is the statement of the Semak in the Beit Yosef, and in Bedek HaBayis he wrote that one should not rely on this to permit placing tefillin on the entire half of the arm, but only on the raised flesh, not at its bottom right near the elbow where it is not raised. Magen Avraham asked why the Mechaber then wrote in seif 7 from half until the elbow. He therefore answered that even the Semak did not intend to permit the whole half of the bone, but only the raised flesh of that half, excluding the raised flesh of the other half. According to this, the raised flesh itself must have space to place two tefillin, and when the Mechaber wrote from its half until the elbow, he too meant the raised flesh of that half.
