Magen Avraham s"k 2. Half the bone — Semak in Beit Yosef; Bedek HaBayit: do not rely to place on whole half of bone, only swollen flesh, not lowest part adjacent to cubit where there is no swelling. Magen Avraham challenged: why did Mechaber cite in se'if 7 "from half to cubit"? Answer: Semak too means only swollen flesh in that half, excluding swollen flesh in the other half; thus two tefillin fit on the swollen area; se'if 7 "from half to cubit" also means swollen flesh in that half.
