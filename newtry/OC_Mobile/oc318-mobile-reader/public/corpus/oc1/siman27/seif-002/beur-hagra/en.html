Se'if 2: The custom, etc. Menachos 35b says it must face inward. Nimukei Yosef explains this as referring to the knot of the arm-tefillin, which should be inward toward the tefillin and the heart; Rav Hai Gaon and the Rosh in the name of Rav Amram explain likewise. They also apply 'it must be above' to the arm-tefillin: the knot should not be distanced from the bayis, for that would put it below, while the tefillin must incline toward the heart. This is unlike Baal HaMaor, who explains 'above' as outward.
One must be careful, etc. As above, and Zohar III 236b.
