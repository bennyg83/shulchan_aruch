(s"k 2) On the head of the bone — in half the bone. I.e. from the end "but not on the half of the bone near the armpit" — implies the half near the cubit is entirely kosher; Gemara "this is cubit" — drasha: yadekha is cubit; Tosafot: language of kibbutz like kevurat de'ahini — cluster of dates, here cluster of flesh — swollen flesh, not whole half of arm.

Question why se'if 7 says so — answer: Rabbi Yosef in his book rejected Semak permitting half the bone (Rama's gloss derives from Semak); se'if 7 says bone near armpit from half to cubit is place of tefillin — like Semak; Magen Avraham left this in parentheses — his style. Magen Avraham's intent: reconcile Semak from Bach's challenge against Gemara "this is cubit." Therefore even Semak comes to limit: Rama/Semak "but not on half of bone" — not to exclude half near armpit (even near cubit only at swollen spot, a fortiori near armpit); rather limits "but not on half of bone" — half the swollen flesh near armpit, only swollen flesh on cubit side; swollen flesh may be called "half bone" as it spans nearly the length of half the bone near cubit.
