(8) Yud of knot — see Biur Halachah: do not widen knot fold too much — tightening place near bayit toward heart.

(9) On it (bayit) — not that bayit sits on yud; yud must be beside bayit, not below; because bayit tilted toward body (se'if 1) — yud is lower side, bayit upper outer side.

(10) Be careful — Zohar Pinchas very strict; some strict even in pouch — yud must not move; some tie yud with sinew — should abolish (sinew chatzitzah on titura); Levushei Serad: abolish wrapping strap under titura on kubitza — also chatzitzah.

(11) From bayit — cut titura from above; tighten yud with bayit.
