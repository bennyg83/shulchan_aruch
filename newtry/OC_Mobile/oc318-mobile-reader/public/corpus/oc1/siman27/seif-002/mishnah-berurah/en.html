That the yud, etc. In Biur Halacha we wrote that it is proper to be careful not to widen greatly the loop-knot through which the strap passes, so that the place where the strap tightens should also be near the tefillin toward the heart.
Upon it, etc. This does not mean the tefillah should rest upon the yud, for certainly the yud must be beside the tefillah evenly and not beneath it. Rather, since he tilts the tefillin toward the body, as written in seif 1, the yud on the side of the body is called below and the tefillah above it.
One must be careful. The Zohar in parshas Pinchas is very stringent about this. Some are stringent that even when they are in their bag the yud should not move at all. For this reason some tie the yud to the tefillah with a sinew thread, but it is proper to abolish this because the sinew around the titura interposes between the arm and the tefillin. Levushei Serad wrote that one should also abolish the custom of wrapping the strap at the kibores under the titura, for this too is an interposition.
