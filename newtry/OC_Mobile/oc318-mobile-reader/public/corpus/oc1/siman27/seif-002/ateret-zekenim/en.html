One must take care that the yod not move away from the tefillin. One who distances it distances himself from the unity, and concerning this it is written: “Those who honor Me I will honor, and those who despise Me will be disgraced.” Zohar, parashat Pinchas. There must be no interposition beneath either the arm tefillin or the head tefillin. This applies specifically to the tefillin themselves; regarding the straps, one need not be particular. Nevertheless, the parts of the straps used for fastening both the arm tefillin and the head tefillin must not have an interposition. Lechem Rav.
