Magen Avraham s.k. 2 — why se'if 7 wording? Mechaber wrote "from half to kovedo" per Semak — seems until kovedo literally is placement; near kovedo no swollen flesh — Shulchan Aruch contradicts Bedek HaBayit. Therefore Semak too means swollen flesh only; se'if 7 likewise. Do not rely on plain Semak implying whole half arm — only swollen flesh; se'if 1 already requires swollen flesh on bone — no need repeat; Hagahat Semak "half arm" follows Semak's "height of arm place"; Hagahat Maimoniyot: no flesh on half arm — default as his explanation, height of that half.
