On the swollen flesh — Be careful the tefillin end is not below the swollen flesh; as with head — end not on forehead (below); we learn Menachot 37b gezeirah shavah height of arm from height of head — see there.

On the head of the bone — See Magen Avraham and Machatzit HaShekel; apparently swollen flesh spread equally on whole bone; Rama forbade placing only on half of swollen flesh toward cubit — surprising: how permit only half cubit when Arakhin 19b explicitly — whole cubit called "hand," and for tefillin Gemara discusses; Semak lacked girsa "whole cubit" — all rishonim copied this (Tosafot Shabbat 92a, Yere'im, Sefer HaTerumah); Menachot 37a — whole cubit kosher: yadekha is cubit, between eyes is crown — where Beit R. Yehudah? place infant brain droops — not explaining special spot on cubit — whole cubit kosher; Rif, Rambam, Rosh, Tur, Rivash never divided cubit flesh; how Rama follows Semak? Not only to be stringent — se'if 7 implies if wound spread on whole area from half bone down — exempt (Mordechai, R"ch; Rama to exclude his view — still difficult). Helped: Sefer HaTerumah after study agrees with R"T — place is upper bone between cubit and armpit: place tefillin on upper bone called "arm" and Torah "hand," near forearm (forearm = cubit); cubit is gathered flesh — against heart — R. Chiya placed opposite heart. Rama and Shulchan Aruch se'if 7 — cubit spread only until half bone down; Bach: upper arm from cubit up; Magen Avraham difficult — answer: even if some swollen flesh above, main cubit only from half down; Gra disagrees — from half up kosher if cubit flesh remains; likely agrees Sefer HaTerumah — main cubit toward cubit, but extends past half bone.

That he has no hand — See Mishnah Berurah; not as Peri Megadim who thought Or Zaru'a exempt in all cases — did not see Or Zaru'a (not printed in his day).

He shall place, etc. — On left hand (clear from Or Zaru'a — others' exposition on obligation of right only on left; still rules like Tanna Kamma — exempt even if cubit remains — he explains "no arm" means forearm; Rama: Tosafot Hakometz obligate even if only cubit; Tosafot d"h kiboret — arm from cubit down; Magen Avraham lacked Or Zaru'a — his words very difficult).

Without berachah — Bach, Netziv, Ateret Zahav, Gra imply must bless when cubit remains; Chayei Adam agrees on obligation but lenient on berachah; even only half bone between cubit and armpit — rely on Gra if any cubit flesh — no berachah; better bless two on head (our custom Rama siman 25, one per Beit Yosef) intending arm too; even if cubit remains (Sha'arei Teshuvah).
