Where and How To Put Them On. Containing 11 Se'ifim: The [proper] place to put
the hand tefillin is on the left arm, on the bicep that is on the bone between the elbow and the armpit. The tefilla should be tilted a bit towards the side of the body such that when he straightens his arm downward, it will be opposite his heart, thereby fulfilling [the obligation of] "And these words, [which I command you today], shall be in your heart." (Deuteronomy 6:6).Rema: It is necessary to place [the tefillin] at the top of the bone closer to the elbow, but not on the [upper] half of the bone close to the armpit] [(SM"K). [An amputee who does not have a hand, but only an arm, should put on [tefillin] without a blessing. (Tosfot, in chapter Hakometz, writes that an amputee is obligated, Or Zaruah writes that he's exempt).]:
