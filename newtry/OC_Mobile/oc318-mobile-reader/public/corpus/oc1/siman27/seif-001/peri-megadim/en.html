Left arm. Taz: Gemara Menachot 37a — Tanna Kamma: yadekha is left from "My foundation is earth, right hand heavens"; R' Natan: "bind/write" — writing right; Rav Ashi: yadekha the Omnipresent's name — yad keihah (Shemot, Va'etchanan, Eikev). Beit Yosef brought only Tanna Kamma and R' Natan; we rule Rav Ashi — yad keihah; se'if 6 follows writing — still non-writing hand keihah (Taz letter 7). Need both "bind/write" and yad keihah; else follow work alone, or both hands writer — left is everyone's left, opposite heart (Levush yadekha keihah — right of world when works left, writes left). Magen Avraham letter 10: controls both hands. Still need yadekha — "bind/write" alone insufficient. Now "keihah" teaches non-writing hand; gidam doubt in se'if 1 if yadekha includes gidam — Magen Avraham letter 3.
