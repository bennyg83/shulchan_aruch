On the left arm. For it is written yadekha — meaning yad keihah, i.e. the left hand that is weak.

Kovedo (cubit). In Old French: alinebogin. We derive: "and it shall be for you a sign" — not for others — outsiders should not see; one's way is to expose until the cubit, and from there onward it is "for you," not for others.
