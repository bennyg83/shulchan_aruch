Kovedo (cubit). Old French: alinebogin.

On head of bone. Meaning half the bone; Bedek HaBayit: do not rely to permit whole half of arm, only swollen flesh — Rambam, Tur, Gemara "this is cubit" — question why se'if 7 — seems even Semak means only on swollen flesh, excluding other half toward armpit which is also swollen.

Gidam. Darkhei Moshe in Or Zaru'a: gidam exempt even if forearm remains; Tosafot end Hakometz — halachah obligates if has forearm; there: no arm exempt; others yadekha includes gidam; Tosafot dispute; others obligate when has forearm — Agudah; question Or Zaru'a rules unlike them — girsa "only forearm" exempt; Yere'im, old Mordechai on parchment; Bach: even if dispute on amputation, if cubit remains all obligate and bless; Yere'im/Mordechai seem exempt; wonder on Rama teshuvah 122 left amputated exempt from right like chalitzah, no weak hand — Talmud: no arm exempt; also wonder Tosafot others when forearm remains — Sifrei: right called yad — what teach yadekha? include gidam who gives on right — literally others' words; if no arm at all, if has arm don left; Tosafot: others not like R. Yehudah — from whence? perhaps forearm not called hand; Arakhin 45 — still called hand for this — don right; no arm fully exempt; strained; Rav Ashi answers R. Yehudah yadekha weak hand; Sifrei includes gidam — Tosafot rely not like Sifrei; Rav Ashi own reason; se'if 6 we rule like bind/write drasha; Mordechai: two anomalies from yadekha; Eshel Avraham did not see Gemara one drasha excludes other; we rule Tanna Kamma exempt from right; per Tosafot no dispute — with forearm don right without berachah.
