Se'if 1 — on the arm. In several places in Shas.

Left. Menachot 36–37.

On the flesh, etc. There; Tosafot ad loc. s.v. kibburat.

Between, etc. Tosafot there from Avot — arm is between kovedo; see there.

On the head. Meaning height of the bone. Semak: place at height of arm; hagahah — not entire arm kosher, only half toward kovedo (Shimusha Rabbah: half the arm). Rashi: kibburat specifically; all poskim — high place only. Hagahat Semak disputed — Gemara "yadekha" is kibburat; without his words, Shulchan Aruch "half arm" means part of kibburat; placement measure as Eruvin end (all kibburat kosher).

Gid, etc. Derech Mitzvosecha / Or Zaru'a: gid exempt though forearm remains; Tosafot obligates. Tosafot "ein lo" — not one-handed; Magen Avraham: Or Zaru'a text like Ra'avan — only forearm; Tanna Kamma. Gid on another's right — Mekhilta Bo R' Yosei: right called yad — "al yadekha" for gid on right; not left from yadekha like Rav Ashi; yadekha = kadosh = his right for gid; others like Tanna Kamma — left, yadekha for gid on right (R' Abba). Dispute like tannaim; our girsa better — no exemption for gid from tefillin (Erchin 19b); gid = cut through kibburat (Bach). Halachah like Tanna Kamma, Rav Ashi; R' Yosei HaChorem / R' Natan other sources; Tosafot main; Or Zaru'a like Tanna Kamma per Ra'avan.
