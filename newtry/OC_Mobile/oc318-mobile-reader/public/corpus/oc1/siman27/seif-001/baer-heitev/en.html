For kovedo. Place tefillin on biceps flesh. See Magen Avraham.

Only arm. Left hand amputated — exempt from right (Rama 152). Shevut Ya'akov 1:3 disputes — halachah: place on gid (no hand, only forearm). Born with one right hand — place on right, bless two on head — yotzei all (see Kenesset HaGedolah, Chavat Ya'ir 167, Magen Avraham 3, Beit Ya'akov 149).
