Left. If he placed it on his right arm, even after the fact he did not fulfill the obligation. Chut HaShani, siman 28.
The elbow. In the vernacular: elbow.
Toward the elbow. The tefillin should be placed on the raised flesh. See Magen Avraham.
Only an arm. One whose left hand was severed is exempt from placing tefillin on the right, Responsa Rama, siman 152. Responsa Shevus Yaakov, part 1, siman 3, disagrees and concludes in practice that an amputee who has no hand but only an arm, or one born with only one hand and it is the right one, should place tefillin on his right and recite two blessings on the tefillin shel rosh, thereby fulfilling according to all. See there, and see Knesses HaGedolah, Responsa Chavos Yair, siman 167, Magen Avraham seif katan 3, and Beis Yaakov, siman 149.
