(1) [Se'if 1] Place of arm tefillin on the left arm. If placed on the right, even bedi'eved he did not fulfill. Chut HaShani siman 28; Yad Aharon on Hagahot Taz; Siddur Beit Yosef laws of donning tefillin letter 5. So per the Ari z"l in Sha'ar HaKavanot — arm tefillin specifically on the left arm, with sod (see there). Not as Beit HaRo'eh 42a and Zekan Aharon 1:400 — if placed on the right bedi'eved yotzei. One who placed on the right and blessed, then moves to the left as required — must repeat the berachah (Beit Yosef there). If he also donned head tefillin — repeat; if not yet head and he remembers (or others remind him) before completing donning and his intent covered both — no repeat (see siman 25 letter 80). If he finished donning including head, or had only arm — repeat; we do not say safeik berachot lehakel here, for per the Ari this is not a valid donning. If only arm — berachah was le'vatalah; if both — berachah applied only to head; when fulfilling arm he must bless again. Against Ari we do not apply safeik berachot lehakel (siman 25 letter 75). See below letter 7.
