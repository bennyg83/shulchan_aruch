(11) [Se'if 2] Correct custom — yud of hand knot toward heart, etc. Maran wrote "correct custom" because dispute in Menachot 35b — knot faces inward; Nekudot Yosef: hand — knot not outside end of arm but inside toward heart; others: head tefillin. Ittur: custom varies; Mahari ben Chaviv saw both customs, ruled second correct — yud-knot at strap end, fold inserted right side of ma'avarta, knot and yud below bayit near heart (Beit Yosef; Maran in Shulchan Aruch fears Nekudot Yosef and Mahari ben Chaviv; Beer HaGolah). Later se'if: ma'avarta toward shoulder — Beit Yosef cites Tur: some ma'avarta to shoulder, patty to hand; some reverse; now all follow first (ma'avarta shoulder, patty hand). Maran wrote "correct custom" without forbidding — in pressing need (lefty without inward-yud tefillin) may borrow and wear on right with ma'avarta to hand, patty to shoulder, yud inward toward heart; same one in doubt needing both hands but cannot afford two sets (Sha'agat Aryeh 1:3; disputes Nachalat Shiva 41; Yad Aharon on Mishnah Berurah 1; Shatz 38b; Siddur Beit Yosef 12; Yeshu'ot Ya'akov 3; Kitzur 10:7; Chayei Adam 14:13; Nishmat Adam 2 rejects Ateret Zahav 3; Mesoret Zahav on Kitzur 9:5 — no concern if parashah head was reader's right, now left (Kol Ya'akov 32 letter 214). See below letter 31.
