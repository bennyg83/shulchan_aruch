Se'if 11: It must, etc. Following the second explanation. Menachos there also implies this, as do Tosafos and Rashi there. Rosh and Mordechai write that it refers to both tefillin, as above.
Between, etc. The plural 'their beauty' is used, and no distinction between head- and arm-tefillin is found [Mordechai].
And he should let them hang, etc. See Tosafos, Menachos 35b, s.v. Avid.
And they should reach, etc.; the width, etc. See Tosafos there, end of s.v. Vekamah. The Geonim, however, write like Rambam and Shulchan Aruch. Hagahos Mordechai at the end of the first chapter cites Rabbi Yehudah HaChasid's allusion: 'It shall be healing to your navel' refers to the length, 'and marrow to your bones' to the width, since the significant size of bone regarding impurity is a barleycorn.
If he made them shorter, etc. Tosafos implies they are invalid, and Rambam rules so; Rosh and Tur validate them. This appears limited to Shimusha Rabba's measure. Insufficient length in the arm-tefillin strap is explicitly invalid in the Gemara, from Rav Pappa's statement about remnants; the same applies to head-tefillin according to Rashi and Mordechai.
Tefillin, etc. Berachos 7 and 57; Sotah 17; Chullin 89; Menachos 35, following Tosafos there and as above in se'if 4.
But a student, etc. See Magen Avraham: they are worn in a manner of honor, as it says, 'All the peoples shall see.'
And regarding the arm-tefillin, etc. Menachos 37b: 'for you,' etc.; Rav Ashi, etc. See Magen Avraham.
