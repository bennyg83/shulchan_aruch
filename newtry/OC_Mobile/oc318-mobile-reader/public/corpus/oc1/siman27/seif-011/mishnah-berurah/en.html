(38) Do not flip — if flipped — chassidut to fast or redeem charity; strict only encircling head and kubitza; later wrap and dangling front straps of head — not strict (not core mitzvah); still black outward for beauty even on excess strap.

(39) Hand and head — hand: can see knot/strap not flipped; head: feel from patty all sides if proper [Bach].

(40) Until navel — Rama added; we don't rule on this.

(41) Or above — Chiddushei Ra'avad; Aruch HaChayyim: right to navel, left to chest; Tur: some right to milah, left to navel.

(42) Barleycorn length — in husk without long thorns; some: width like wheat, less than barley length; pressing need — rely. Acharonim: careful at double-tightening fold — crease or break below minimum.

(43) Wear as is — poskim dispute bedi'eved; Beit Yosef ruled wear until valid pair.

(44) As they are — if arm strap too short (se'if 8) — no berachah; if reaches tzad finger — short wrappings don't block. Head straps: Aruch HaChayyim — besides head loop need two tefachim extra else no berachah; width: if less than wheat length — no berachah. Repairing straps — siman 33 end, Mishnah Berurah there.

(45) Good to be visible — "all peoples see"; knot visible too — not practiced [Ateret Zahav]; bedi'eved covered — yotzei.

(46) Not derech eretz before rebbi — tefillin honor; do not compare to rebbi — cover with tallit or hat.

(47) Arm not strict visible — lechatchilah better covered.

(48) If visible — torn clothes — not our issue; forbidden on garment (s"k 5).
