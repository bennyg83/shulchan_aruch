Interposes. In Zevachim 19 there is a question whether a living louse or his hair is an interposition regarding the priestly garments. Magen Avraham wrote that with a dead louse and dirt one must in any event be careful, for in that case there was no question at all. The Shelah wrote that one should wash the place where the tefillin are put on.
To be particular. Nevertheless, one should be stringent even regarding the straps wherever they relate to the tying, whether the straps of the head or of the arm. Taz, Magen Avraham, and Olas Tamid. In Devar Shmuel, siman 292, he wrote open rebuke against those who place tefillin over a wig called a peruke; see Responsa Yad Eliyahu, siman 3, where he discusses this at length.
