(4) There — gloss. Amputee with no hand, only arm — don without berachah. Many opinions: Or Zaru'a exempt; Tosafot Menachot 37a obligates. Or Zaru'a exempt — Magen Avraham 3 cites alternate girsa. Terumat HaDeshen — Tanna Kamma disagrees and exempts (as Kenesset HaGedolah on Hagahot Taz). Bach: cut above cubit — exempt entirely; below cubit — obligated with berachah, unlike Rama's gloss. Per Gra above — even above cubit, place on remaining bone. Shevut Ya'akov 1:3 — place on right hand. To avoid doubts: whether cut above or below cubit — first don arm tefillin on remaining left arm without berachah; when donning head, bless (two berachot Ashkenaz / one Sephard) intending to cover arm; pray; after prayer remove head and hand tefillin, then don arm on right and head again without berachah (intent to re-don). Same if left hand withered from illness (Beit Yosef letter 9). Do not don right first then left — Ari requires left; in doubt, left first, intend in head berachah, pray, then right.
