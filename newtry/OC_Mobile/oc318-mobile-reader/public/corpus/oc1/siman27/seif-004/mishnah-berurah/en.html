(14) Any chatzitzah — lechatchilah careful even live hair; dead skin/dust too; wash placement site (Rav Po'alim); strap between bayit and flesh — min b'mino (Ravid HaZahav); Levushei Serad s"k 11 — lechatchilah strict on tying straps.
