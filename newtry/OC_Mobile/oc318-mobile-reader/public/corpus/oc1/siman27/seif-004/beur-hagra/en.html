Se'if 4: There should not, etc. Zevachim 19a and Arachin 3b, following the Rosh's explanation that the concern is an interposition. This is unlike Rashi and Tosafos in Arachin; on their view, what does Zevachim mean by asking that the same should apply to the head, when head-tefillin bear the verse, 'All the peoples of the earth shall see,' as Menachos 35b and Tosafos explain? The reason is that 'on your hand' and 'between your eyes' require no interposition, as with priestly garments. This is the meaning of 'between,' as in Zevachim.
Specifically, etc. 'It shall be a sign for you upon your hand' refers only to the bayis itself, as Menachos 37b derives: 'for you as a sign,' in a place that is a sign to you. The strap, however, passes over the hand and wraps around the finger. This appears correct as law, though not for practical ruling, both for arm- and head-tefillin. See Taz and Magen Avraham.
