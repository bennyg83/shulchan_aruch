Chatzitzah. Zevachim 19 — live hair chatzitzah for priestly garments? Rokeach: shave neck hair; Agur 55 — not practiced; dead skin/dust — careful, no doubt; Shelah: wash tefillin site.

Straps — not strict. Rashba concludes lehalachah but lema'aseh see common practice; Yerushalmi: loose halachah — follow custom (teshuvah 327 on straps). Rashba: mainly not wrapping on garment; slight chatzitzah on straps not strict. This gloss may apply to one prone to head discharge — thin cloth on straps and bless (s"k 6).
