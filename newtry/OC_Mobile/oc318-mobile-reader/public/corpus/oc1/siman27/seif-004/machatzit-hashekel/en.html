(s"k 4) Something, etc. Priestly garments — "he shall wear on his flesh" — nothing intervening; Gemara asked: live hair chatzitzah? Hair exited into garment — Rashi: hair from head down between tunic and flesh (see Rambam Klei Mikdash).

It seems therefore, etc. On neck — since unresolved, be stringent; Rambam Klei Mikdash 10 lechatchilah chatzitzah; perhaps facial short hairs are "majority"; armpit hair proves — Gemara did not doubt chatzitzah in priestly garments; such case — haircut every Erev Shabbat, not razor only scissors.

Did not shave like razor — left some hair as Yechezkel "cover heads"; Sanhedrin 22; Rambam ch. 5 Klei Mikdash and Ra'avad — still donned head tefillin with hair exposed between tzitz and mitznefet; little hair not chatzitzah (majority); nape hairs large and protrude — Rashi in Gemara question above is chatzitzah.

We do not practice so. Perhaps since unresolved — rely on Rashba brought se'if 5 (Magen Avraham s"k 5) — no chatzitzah on head — though not lema'aseh.

However, etc. Here no doubt; they said there — chatzitzah; since doubt — main view Rosh — head also has chatzitzah concern.

Therefore do not be lenient.
