An intervening object. For it is written, 'as a sign upon your hand,' teaching that there should be no intervening object between the tefillin and his hand. The same applies to the head tefillin.
But regarding the straps, etc. Responsa Rashba, siman 827, concludes that as a matter of halakhah it appears proper to distinguish between the ketzitzah and the strap, but in practice he remained uncertain and left the matter to custom: Whatever the established practice is, they should continue to do. I am surprised that Rema rules leniently without qualification regarding the straps. Rabbi Mordechai Yaffe writes that the custom is to be stringent concerning the portion involved in tying, both for the straps of the head tefillin and those of the arm tefillin. This is correct.
