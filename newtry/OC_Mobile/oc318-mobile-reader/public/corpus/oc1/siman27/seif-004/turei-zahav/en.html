Chatzitzah. For it is written "for a sign on your hand" — nothing interposing between it and the hand; likewise for head tefillin.

But regarding straps, etc. Rashba responsum 327 concludes lehalachah to distinguish ketzitzah from strap, but lema'aseh was uncertain and left custom; I wonder that Rama ruled leniently on straps while Mahari ibn Chaviv wrote custom is strict on tying — head or arm straps — and he is correct.
