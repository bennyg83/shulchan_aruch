His pouch to a non-Jew; and if it is a heavy load, it seems to me that he should give the donkey to the non-Jew or declare it ownerless, as written at the end of siman 246, provided that he does not drive it, as written in seif 2 (Magen Avraham). According to what I wrote above, since he gives it away or declares it ownerless, there is no prohibition of driving an animal.
Even if he did not give payment, etc. However, in Rokeach, siman 136, he wrote that if it did not grow dark while he was on the road, he gives payment, and so too it implies in the Rosh. The words of the Rambam, from whom the Beit Yosef derived this law, seem to me to discuss when it did grow dark while he was on the road. But the words of the Shulchan Aruch and Levush are difficult to explain that way. See siman 247, seif katan 8.
