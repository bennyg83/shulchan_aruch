10) There: He may not give it to a non-Jew, etc. Nevertheless, it appears that he may say to the non-Jew, 'My wallet fell and I do not know what to do.' Tiferes Yisrael; Pischei Olam, paragraph 6. It appears that he may likewise say, 'Would it please you to bring it to my home?' since he does not use the language of a command, but presents it only as a possibility.
