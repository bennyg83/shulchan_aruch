Levush. He moved his feet. In a manuscript of the Shulchan Aruch it is written, hand and foot, and afterward, when it returns, etc., it wrote foot, and so Rashi wrote there. But here Rashi did not specify, only stated generally, and wrote, after it moved. This author wrote feet, which implies two, and I do not know where he got this from. Melbushei Yom Tov. I found this in Bartenura in the chapter Mi Shehechshich, and so it seems to me to be primary. When Rashi wrote foot, it seems to me that his intent is also this, as it is written, those who send forth the foot of the ox.
