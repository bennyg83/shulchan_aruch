While it is still day, etc. So wrote the Rema. The Magen Avraham wondered: the Ramban and Rashba prohibit in chapter 10, and regarding the words of the Rambam, the Maggid wrote that they are ambiguous. In my humble opinion, this is not so, for we find this explicitly in the Ramban and Rashba. Rather, one can say that they speak specifically when he gives it after nightfall, and this seems to be the view of the Rema. But the Rambam seemed to the Maggid to be speaking even from nightfall, for he previously stated the law that one should give it to a non-Jew, and there it speaks even from nightfall, as the Beit Yosef wrote at the beginning of this siman. Therefore the Maggid returned and wrote that the words of the Rambam are ambiguous. So it seems to me. Examine this carefully.
