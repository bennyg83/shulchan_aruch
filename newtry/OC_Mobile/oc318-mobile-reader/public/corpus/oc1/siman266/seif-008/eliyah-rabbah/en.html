Levush. Because of fear, etc. As below, because of fear of bandits, etc., or even within, etc., for there is no prohibition at all when he goes on foot. It implies that outside the techum it is better to ride, and so it implies in Rivash, siman 100, Tashbetz, and Piskei Maharai, and so the Beit Yosef wrote. However, he wrote the reason as being that the laws of techum do not apply above ten handbreadths, and when riding he is above ten. The Magen Avraham challenged this, that the laws of techum apply to riding, because its use is comfortable; see there. This is difficult, for it seems to me that there is another reason, namely that a person does not violate anything in such a case, as Tosafot and the Rosh wrote in the chapter Mi Shehotziuhu, and as the Magen Avraham himself wrote in siman 246, seif katan 12. At first glance, there is a difficulty against all the great geonim mentioned above from the Gemara in Beitzah 37a, that one may not ride, as a decree because of techum, with respect to the person himself. However, there is the proof brought by Hagahos Mordechai from the incident of Acher, and the Beit Yosef elaborated in resolving it, and so did the Magen Avraham; see there. I am surprised that he did not see in Midrash Koheles on the verse, better is the end of a matter, etc., the following language: He said to him, with all this wisdom, will you not repent? Thus it refers to his sins, for since he was so wise that he could measure the techum by the hoofbeats of his horse, he should have repented.
