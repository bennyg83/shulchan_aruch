The same applies to his pocket. This refers to a case where he cannot carry it in stages of less than four amos, such as where he fears thieves. The same applies if he has a donkey with him and fears that when he sets the item down and takes it up again, thieves will steal it, as stated in seif 2. In such a case he may run. Sefer HaTerumah. According to our practice, where we have no true public domain, perhaps all authorities permit running under all circumstances. Shiltei HaGibborim.
