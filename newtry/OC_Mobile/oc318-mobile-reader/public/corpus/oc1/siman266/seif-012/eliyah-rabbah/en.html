Levush. By himself, etc. Giving it to his fellow within less than four amos at a time is more preferable than walking by himself. Tosafot Yom Tov, chapter 10 of Eiruvin. See below, siman 301, seif 42, and siman 349. It is obvious that whether he does it himself or gives it to his fellow, this does not help from a private domain to a karmelis, and certainly not to a public domain, for the prohibition of transferring from one domain to another is forbidden even by half a cubit.
