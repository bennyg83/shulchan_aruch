11) [Seif 2] If no non-Jew accompanies him, etc. The same applies if he does not trust the non-Jew: he places it upon his donkey. Sefer HaTerumah, siman 226; Magen Avraham, paragraph 4; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 3.
12) There: So that he will not be liable for driving, etc. This means leading a loaded donkey. He is liable if he causes both an uprooting and a setting down. Taz, paragraph 2.
13) There: So that he will not be liable for driving, etc. The reason the Shulchan Aruch does not say that the concern is the requirement that one's animal rest is that it teaches that one must take this precaution even if the animal is not his. All the more so, if the animal is his, two concerns apply: driving and the requirement that his animal rest. Ran. Rivash, siman 24, and Magen Avraham, paragraph 5, write the same. Tosafos Yom Tov in the chapter Mi Shehechshich writes the same; see there. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 5; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 4; Chayei Adam, klal 58, paragraph 1. See above, paragraph 1.
