2) Nevertheless, if he rented the donkey from a non-Jew, some say he is commanded to ensure that it rests, as stated in siman 246, seif 3. See our words there in paragraph 24. This applies specifically when he rented the donkey. If he hired the non-Jew to transport him to a particular place, however, he did not rent the donkey, since the non-Jew remains responsible for feeding the donkey and liable for it, and he did not specifically rent this particular donkey from the non-Jew. It is therefore obvious that he is not commanded to ensure that it rests. Magen Avraham there; Tosefes Shabbos there.
