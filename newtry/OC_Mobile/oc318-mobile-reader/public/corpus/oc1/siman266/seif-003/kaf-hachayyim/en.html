3) There: He gives his wallet to a non-Jew, etc. This is because a person cannot restrain himself when his money is at stake. If this were not permitted, he might carry it four amos in the public domain. This applies specifically to his own money, because he cares for property he acquired through effort. Taz, paragraph 1. This excludes a found object that has not yet come into his possession. If it came into his possession while it was still daytime, however, it is like his own money. All money ordinarily comes through effort, and even though he did not labor for this particular money, the leniency applies. Levush Serad.
