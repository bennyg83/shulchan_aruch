5) There: He gives his wallet to a non-Jew, etc. Some forbid sewing gold coins into one's belt and carrying them on Shabbos because one is afraid to entrust them to a non-Jew while traveling. I found, however, that Rabbi Yehudah of Troyes permitted it, since the coins are covered on every side and the person does not appear to carry a muktzeh article. Bach. See below, siman 301, seif 33.
