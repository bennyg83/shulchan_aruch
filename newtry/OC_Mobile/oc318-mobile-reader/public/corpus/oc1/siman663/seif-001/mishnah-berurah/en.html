(1) Four for the festival offerings - And they say half-Kaddish after the four.
(2) And the Yisrael returns and reads, 'And on the third day' - He does not hold like the second opinion, that he should read 'and on the fourth day,' because that is not relevant to the matter of the day: if you count from the first day of Yom Tov, it is the third day, and if you count from the second day of Yom Tov, it is the second day.
(3) The doubt of the day - But on the second day of Yom Tov, although we also make it Yom Tov because of the doubt of the day, we do not read 'and on the second day,' for it is degrading to call Yom Tov a possible weekday.
(4) The first ones - If the Levi read 'on the third day' and 'on the fourth day,' the third aliyah should read 'and on the fifth day,' and the fourth should read 'on the second day' and 'on the third day.'
(5) The entire doubt of the day - See above, siman 137, Mishnah Berurah seif katan 11.
(6) And the fourth reads 'on the sixth day' and 'on the seventh day' - Likewise in prayer, in the Mussaf offerings on Chol HaMoed, we also mention the doubt of the day. That is, on the first day of Chol HaMoed we say 'and on the second day, twelve bulls,' etc., and 'and on the third day, eleven bulls,' etc. The sign so that you never err is that the bulls and the days together equal fourteen (Tur). One must say 'and their meal-offering' between 'the second day' and 'the third day,' and likewise every day one must say 'and their meal-offering' for each day independently, unlike those who err and say 'and their meal-offering' only once. In Mussaf on a weekday one says 'the Mussaf,' and on Shabbos, 'the Mussaf offerings' [Peri Megadim]. The order of the hoshanos: on the two days of Yom Tov we say Lema'an Amitecha and Even Shetiyah; on Chol HaMoed, A'erokh Shu'i, E-l Lemosha'os, Adon HaMoshi'a; and on Shabbos, Om Netzurah. If the first day of Sukkos occurs on Shabbos, he says Lema'an Amitecha on the second day, A'erokh Shu'i on the first day of Chol HaMoed, Even Shetiyah on the second day of Chol HaMoed, E-l Lemosha'os on the third day of Chol HaMoed, and Adon HaMoshi'a on the fourth day of Chol HaMoed, while Om Ani Chomah is pushed off entirely. If the sixth day occurs on Shabbos, then they say them in this order: Lema'an Amitecha, Even Shetiyah, A'erokh Shu'i, Om Ani Chomah, E-l Lemosha'os, Om Netzurah, and the piyyut Adon HaMoshi'a is pushed off. For the reason for this order, see Machatzis HaShekel.
