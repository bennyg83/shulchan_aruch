1) [Seif 1] On Chol HaMoed, one takes out a Sefer Torah, etc. The reason that two Sifrei Torah are not taken out, as they are on Chol HaMoed of Pesach, is that on Pesach there are many passages concerning the festival, whereas on Sukkos there is no passage concerning the festival other than the offerings alone. Abudarham; Eliyah Rabbah, os 2. All the laws of the Chol HaMoed prayers were explained above in siman 490 and siman 491; take them from there.
