The order of prayer on Chol Hamoed; it contains 3
seifim. On Chol Hamoed we take out a Torah scroll and read in it four (1) sections from the festival sacrifices in the parashah of Pinchas (2). On the first day of Chol Hamoed the kohen reads; on the second day the Levi; on the third day Yisrael reads again (2); on the third and fourth days they read safek yom (3), and on the second and third days — and likewise on the remaining days.Some say the first two read safek yom, the third reads the section for the following day, and the fourth goes back and reads all the safek yom that the first two read — and this is our practice. On the seventh day the kohen reads the fifth day, the Levi the sixth day, Yisrael the seventh day, and the fourth reads the sixth and seventh days — and this is our practice (Rashi in the name of his teachers, Mahariv, Minhagim).} In Eretz Yisrael, where there is no safek yom, they read each day only that day's sacrifice, for on the second day — which is the first day of Chol Hamoed — the kohen reads, and on the second and third days those who follow him read that same parashah again; and likewise on the other days.] ובארץ ישראל שאין שם ספיקא דיומא אין קורים בכל יום אלא קרבן היום בלבד כי ביום ב' הוא א' לחולו של מועד קורא כהן וביום השני והג' העולים אחריו חוזרים וקורים אותה פרשה עצמה ועל דרך זה בכל יום משאר הימים:
