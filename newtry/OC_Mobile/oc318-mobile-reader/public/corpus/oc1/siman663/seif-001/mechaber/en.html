The order of the Torah reading on Chol HaMoed. It contains 3 seifim: On Chol HaMoed of Sukkot, 4 people read the passages of the festival offerings in Parashat Pinchas. In the Diaspora, because of the uncertainty regarding the calendar day, the readings include the offerings of the 2 possible days. {Rama: Some say that the first 2 readers read the 2 doubtful-day portions, the third reads the offering of the following day, and the fourth returns and reads the 2 doubtful-day portions read by the first 2 readers, and this is our custom.} On the seventh day, the first reader reads 'On the fifth day,' the second 'On the sixth day,' the third 'On the seventh day,' and the fourth reads 'On the sixth day' and 'On the seventh day.' This is our custom. [Rashi in the name of his teachers; Mahariv; Minhagim] In Eretz Yisrael, where there is no uncertainty as to the day, the offering of that day is read.
