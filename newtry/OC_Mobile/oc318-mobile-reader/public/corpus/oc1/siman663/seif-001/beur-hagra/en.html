1. And on the first day, etc. For one should not read a passage that is not relevant to the matter of the day. See Tosafos, Megillah 22a, s.v. 'shani,' and 23a, s.v. 'keivan.' A proof is from the fact that they required skipping, and as it says there: if this is so, according to the one who says he skips, etc., 'shani,' etc.; it is evident that skipping is preferable.
And some say, etc. As with the maamados.
And in Eretz Yisrael, etc. So it is in Tosefta, chapter 3 of Megillah: on the second day, 'on the second day'; on the third, 'and on the third day'; on the fourth, 'and on the fourth day'; on the fifth, 'and on the fifth day'; on the sixth, 'and on the sixth day'; on the seventh, 'and on the seventh day.' From here there is proof for the Shulchan Aruch's view regarding outside Eretz Yisrael, that we do not read the following day.
