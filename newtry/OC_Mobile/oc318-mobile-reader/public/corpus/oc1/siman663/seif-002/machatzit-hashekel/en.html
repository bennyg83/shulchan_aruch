(Seif katan 2) And the maftir, etc. And he concludes, etc.; see the end of siman 490. This means that on Chol HaMoed Pesach that falls on Shabbos, one does not conclude 'and the seasons.' The Magen Avraham wrote the reason there: since on Sukkos the days are divided by the number of offerings, it is more stringent than Pesach, where all the days are equal in the Mussaf offering. As for why we do not take out two sifrei Torah on Chol HaMoed Sukkos, as on Chol HaMoed Pesach, the Eliyah Rabbah wrote in the name of R. David Abudraham that the reason is that on Pesach the Torah has many passages that speak about the matter of Pesach; therefore each day we read from one sefer Torah in a passage that speaks about Pesach, and from the second in the passage of the Mussaf offerings. But on Sukkos they did not find a passage about the matter of Sukkos, apart from what is read on Yom Tov, except in the passage of the offerings. Therefore we take out only one sefer Torah, and four men read from it.
