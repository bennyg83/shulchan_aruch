On Shabbat of Chol Hamoed, Arvit, Shacharit, and Mincha are prayed as on Shabbat, and one says Yaaleh Veyavo in Avodah. In Musaf one says Attah Bechartanu, and because of our sins [we mention] this day of rest and this festival of Sukkot, and concludes "who sanctifies Shabbat, Israel, and the seasons."The practice is to read Kohelet on the Shabbat of Chol Hamoed, or on Shemini Atzeret if it falls on Shabbat (Maharil).] [See siman 490.]:
