On Shabbat of Hol Hamoed, Arvit, Shacharit, and Mincha are like on Shabbat with the addition of "Yaale v'yavo" in the Avodah section. In Mussaf, we say "You have chosen us," "Because of our sins...", "this holiday of Sukkot and this day of pleasure," finishing with "who sanctifies Shabbat and Israel and Holidays." {Rama: Rem"a: The practice is to read Ecclesiastes on the Shabbat of Hol Hamoed or on Shmini Atzert if it falls on Shabbat (Mahari"l).
