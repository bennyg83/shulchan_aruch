(8) 'You say to me' - For it contains the matter of sukkah.
(9) And the maftir reads, etc. - In the haftarah he concludes: Who sanctifies Shabbos, Israel, and the seasons. He also mentions Sukkos in the middle of the berachah, as on the first day of Sukkos [Peri Megadim in siman 490].
(10) 'On the day Gog comes' - Because in the future the war of Gog and Magog will be in Tishrei.
