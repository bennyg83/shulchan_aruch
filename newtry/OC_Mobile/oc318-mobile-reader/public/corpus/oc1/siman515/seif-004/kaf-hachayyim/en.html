If some of that species remains attached to the ground, etc. Regarding why we do not presume that it was detached the preceding day, Rabbeinu Yonah writes: 'Whenever some of that species remains attached, people ordinarily gather it on the day it is needed and do not gather it the preceding day. Since the majority act this way, we do not presume leniently that it was gathered earlier.' These are his words. Ran in the chapter Shoel of Shabbos and Rashba in his novellae to Beitzah cite him. Only when it is recognizable that the produce was not gathered or trapped that day do we presume leniently, as written below, se'if 3. See there.
