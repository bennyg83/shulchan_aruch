If a non-Jew brought a gift to a Jew consisting of items whose species is still attached to the ground — on the first day of Rosh Hashanah that falls on Thursday — some permit eating them on Shabbat after waiting the required time, and some forbid it.Rema: According to our custom to be stringent regarding the two Yom Tov days of the Diaspora, the same rule applies there. Regarding the halachic ruling: it seems that for the two days of Rosh Hashanah one should be stringent regarding Shabbat, but for the two Yom Tov days of the Diaspora one may be lenient regarding a Shabbat that follows them — and specifically for fruits that do not require preparation on the Yom Tov preceding Shabbat; but something that needs to be prepared on the second Yom Tov for Shabbat is forbidden, since the custom is to forbid it — and even to handle it on the second Yom Tov, unless it is for guests or similar needs.
