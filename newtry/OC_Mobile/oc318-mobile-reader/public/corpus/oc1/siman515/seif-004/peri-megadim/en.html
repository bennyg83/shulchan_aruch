Even. See the Taz in seif 7; so it should read in letter 13; and see Beer HaGolah, Yoreh Deah, in the name of the Ran, that even on the second Yom Tov day; and it is not like a double doubt, for the way is to detach and to trap on the very day; and accordingly there are two doubts: perhaps they were picked yesterday, and perhaps for a non-Jew's need; one should forbid; and see siman 325, two views, and the halacha follows the first view regarding waiting the time it takes for them to be done; and here this is included, all the more so to forbid; and see Eliyah Rabbah.
