There are those who permit eating them on Shabbos. For we do not say preparation from two days, only from one day.
One should be stringent on Shabbos. Maharshal wrote that one need not wait the time it takes for them to be done; but if it was brought on Friday, on Motzei Shabbos one does need that waiting time.
But a matter that needs preparation. Meaning such as fish that need cooking, and on Shabbos it is forbidden to cook them, and on the second Yom Tov likewise one cannot cook them, for they are muktzeh and forbidden even for another Jew according to the Semak per Rabbeinu Tam; so Terumat HaDeshen implies, not so for practical law but only according to the Semak's view; and the Rama's words are puzzling in this transfer of wording, for we did not adopt the Semak's stringency, and as he himself concludes here that for the need of guests and the like it is permitted; if so there is no muktzeh at all on the second Diaspora Yom Tov day, and there is no handling prohibition; therefore it appears there is no prohibition against cooking them on the second Yom Tov in order to eat them on Shabbos; and all the more so according to Maharshal who permits in every manner for another person on the second Yom Tov, and there is no muktzeh prohibition at all; and as we say explicitly afterward in this seif regarding [things brought from] outside the techum; and even according to the Rama's decision that one would want to explain that there is no permission for another except for guests, nevertheless we hold that we say "since guests may happen upon them," as Beis Yosef wrote at the end of siman 512.
