(ב) To prepare. For example fish that need cooking — on Shabbat it is forbidden to cook them, and on the second Yom Tov likewise one cannot cook them, for behold they are muktzeh and forbidden even to another Israelite per Rama's view — end quote. And Taz wrote it appears there is no prohibition that he cook them on the second Yom Tov in order to eat them on Shabbat; and all the more so per Maharshal's view who permits in every manner for another person on the second Yom Tov, and there is no muktzeh prohibition at all; and even per Rama's ruling that there is no permission for another except for guests — nevertheless we rule that we say: since guests may happen to come on erev Shabbat — see seif katan 7.
