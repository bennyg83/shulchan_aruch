On Shabbos. Maharshal wrote that one need not wait the time it takes for them to be done; but if it was brought on Friday, on Motzei Shabbos one does need that waiting time.
To prepare. For example fish that need cooking, and on Shabbos it is forbidden to cook them, and on the second Yom Tov likewise one cannot cook them, for they are muktzeh and forbidden even for another Jew according to the Rama at the end of seif 1. And Taz wrote that it appears there is no prohibition against cooking them on the second Yom Tov in order to eat them on Shabbos; and all the more so according to Maharshal who permits in every manner for another person on the second Yom Tov, and there is no muktzeh prohibition at all; and even according to the Rama's decision that there is no permission for another except for guests, nevertheless we hold that we say "since guests may happen to come"; see there; and see s.k. 6. And Magen Avraham permits to cook on the second Yom Tov through a non-Jew; see there.
