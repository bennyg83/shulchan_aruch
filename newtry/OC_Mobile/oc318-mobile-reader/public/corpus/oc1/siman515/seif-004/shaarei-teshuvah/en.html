That they be done. See in Har HaCarmel, siman 16, who wrote that it appears one may be lenient if he brought fish to a Jew to sell on the first Yom Tov, where enough time passed as on the night of Yom Tov, even without a fixed price, because there is no seller who traps them with intent for this particular Jew; and there is here a double doubt: a doubt whether it was for the sake that this Jew have them done, and a doubt whether they were trapped today, and a doubt of the law that there are those who permit even regarding a gift where enough time passed for them to be done overnight of the first Yom Tov, because of the joy of Yom Tov; see there.
