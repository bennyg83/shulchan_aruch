It is permitted on the second day. Yam Shel Shlomo writes that this follows Rashi's view. According to Rabbeinu Tam, whose view Rama cites in seif 1, it is forbidden to the person for whom it was brought because of a decree lest he tell the non-Jew to bring it. This accords with Yam Shel Shlomo's general approach. According to Rama's ruling in seif 5, however, that one may be lenient after the first day of Yom Tov because we do not suspect that he will tell the non-Jew to bring it, the same applies to Rosh Hashanah or to Shabbos followed by Yom Tov.
