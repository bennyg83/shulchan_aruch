[8] [Levush] Their prohibition etc. And in siman 325, seif 7, Shulchan Aruch and Levush wrote two views whether we require waiting the time it takes for them to be done; and it appears to me that therefore they wrote here anonymously "their prohibition" and did not conclude with waiting the time it takes for them to be done, and relied on above; also one can further say that since the first reasoning there is to forbid, evidently he holds that such is the law, as is known that Shulchan Aruch's way is not to write the second [view] except to give honor; therefore here he wrote anonymously like the first reasoning. But Magen Avraham disagrees, that here regarding a non-Jew who brings a gift we say he intends to bring from the choicest and brings from those he picked or trapped today; and the same law applies to one who brings to sell; and above deals with where a non-Jew told him on erev Yom Tov to give him fruit and he went to the non-Jew's house and [the non-Jew] gave him. And Bach distinguishes that here on Yom Tov, where we hold to be more stringent so as not to treat it lightly; and Olas Shabbos in the name of Maharshal answers that there it is like a double doubt: a doubt whether he said there is of that species among what is attached, and if you say there is, perhaps they detached them yesterday; and all the answers are forced, and what I wrote is the main view.
