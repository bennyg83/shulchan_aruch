(ח) There. Forbidden even to anyone etc. — in this muktzeh even R' Shimon agrees — Rashi Beitza 24b. And Beit David, Beit Yosef. And so Levush. See there law 1 — Magen Avraham seif katan 1. And accordingly, even per the view of R' Yehuda who wrote the Rav above (siman 497 seif 4 in the gloss) that he has no muktzeh even on Yom Tov — here all agree it is forbidden because of muktzeh like dried figs and raisins that are similar. And that which is said above siman 497 seif 15 that what is with the non-Jew does not need preparation — that deals with muktzeh of a vessel and the like; but what is because of attached and trapping, even of a non-Jew it is forbidden.
