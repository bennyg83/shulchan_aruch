Something whose species is not attached to the ground and does not require trapping — if it came from within the boundary it is permitted to everyone. If it came from outside the boundary, it is forbidden to eat for the one for whom it was brought and all members of his household, but it is permitted to handle it within four cubits, or within a walled city, or a fortified settlement known to have been enclosed for dwelling purposes. Others are permitted even to eat it. After the holiday [the first Yom Tov], the one for whom it was brought must wait the required time.
