(Seif katan 1) Forbidden etc. because of muktzeh; even though even at Motzei Yom Tov it is forbidden with "so that they be made" — whether because he should not benefit from melacha of Yom Tov per Rashi, or per Rabbeinu Tam lest he tell a non-Jew to bring them to him — all the more so on Yom Tov itself it is forbidden without the muktzeh reason. And certainly per Yesh Seder in seif katan 8 afterward there is relevance in what is forbidden because of muktzeh — for from this even handling is forbidden. But per Rif and Rosh who hold that whatever is forbidden in eating is also forbidden in handling — why was the muktzeh reason needed? And so for most poskim according to Rabbeinu Tam who hold that for one for whom it was not brought it is permitted, for there is no "lest he tell a non-Jew" etc., for a person does not sin for another — if so the muktzeh reason was needed. But these matters forbidden because of muktzeh are Rashi's words there on what R' Pappa taught — for according to him why was the muktzeh reason needed? And it must be said there is relevance if the non-Jew detached or trapped for his own need — they are permitted to Israelites as written seif 2; in any case on day one they are forbidden because of muktzeh as explained there; and so Ran there in chapter Ein Tzadin:

For such muktzeh even R' Shimon agrees; if so, per those who permit muktzeh on Yom Tov in siman 497 who rule on Yom Tov like R' Shimon — nevertheless such muktzeh is forbidden even for R' Shimon; and the reason Rashi wrote there daf 24b, and these are his words: and even for Rashi there is muktzeh in dried figs and raisins (which they reject with liquid — and as explained above siman 510 seif 2); and attached is like dried figs and raisins, since they did not gather from yesterday he rejected them from his intent and does not wish to benefit from them; therefore even what a non-Jew gathered is forbidden etc.; and because of this the Sages decreed on all attached even where he had no opportunity to gather, for the Rabbis did not distinguish etc.; and it appears that from this reason there is muktzeh in something lacking trapping even for R' Shimon — end quote Rabbeinu Yehuda in siman 510:
