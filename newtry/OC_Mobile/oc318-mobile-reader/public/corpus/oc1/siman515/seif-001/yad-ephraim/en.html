In Magen Avraham seif katan 5: but fish for all etc. — in Dagul MeRevavah he emended: even eggs etc., because fish are like fruit not eaten raw; and in my humble opinion the essence of the reason that they are not raw is considered preparation because one cannot say that on Shabbat he prepared for himself, because they lack cooking; unlike fish, where otherwise it is not considered preparation, and it is like birth that per Maharshal is not considered preparation — see there end of siman 513; and likewise end of siman 501 regarding torches, since the body is in the world, he wrote it is not considered preparation; and only detaching from a tree that does an act in the fruit itself to detach them is considered preparation; and nevertheless there is slight study per what is written above siman 497 in the name of Maharshal himself that regarding fish he is liable because of uprooting a thing from its growth; if so apparently there is no distinction between this and detaching fruit:
