[1] Even to handle it etc. complete muktzeh etc. And according to this, from the night of the second Yom Tov onward it is permitted in handling even according to those who are stringent; and so ruled Yam Shel Shlomo, chapter Ein Tzadin, siman 9, and Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah; but Magen Avraham ruled to forbid, since since they are forbidden in eating they are forbidden in handling; and see seif 5; and in Taz at the end of siman 102 he permits, and at first glance this contradicts his words at the end of siman 405, and this requires study. Myrtle that was detached for a Jew is forbidden to smell, like eating (Maharshal and the poskim).
