(א) [Seif 1] A non-Jew who brought a gift to an Israelite on Yom Tov etc., and likewise if he brought to sell in a city where the majority of residents are Israelites — from the presumption he brings for the need of the majority. As proven in Eruvin 40a regarding that turnip etc.; and so explicitly in tractate Machshirin chapter 2 Mishnah 6: he found vegetables sold — if majority non-Jew etc. — see there. And so is proven from what is written below seif 6 and siman 518 seif 1 and in later authorities there; and what Chayei Adam wrote general 100 law 9 to be lenient regarding a non-Jew who sells as if he trapped for himself — meaning he need not wait at night with "so that they be made" — they already challenged him in Mishna Berurah Biur Halacha this siman; and in responsa of Maharam of Rothenburg, later section siman 14 — see there.
