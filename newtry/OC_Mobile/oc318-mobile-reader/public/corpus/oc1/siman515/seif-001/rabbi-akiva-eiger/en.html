Seif 1 And in the evening they are permitted after waiting the time it takes for them to be done. See Sukkah 10b, Tosafos s.v. "until"; and likewise on the two Diaspora Yom Tov days, that what was born on this is permitted on that, etc.: it is clarified that it would have been fit to forbid on the second Yom Tov because of muktzeh, namely lest today is Yom Tov; and even though it was born on a weekday, nevertheless since it is not known to us that it is a weekday, it would have been forbidden at twilight, and once it was set aside at twilight [it remains forbidden]; except that the reason of muktzeh for forty days that have passed, we do not say; see there carefully. Accordingly here, where at twilight the prohibition is only because perhaps it is still daytime, for even if it is night it is forbidden until after waiting the time it takes for them to be done: if so, according to the Mechaber who ruled above in siman 295, seif 4, that on Yom Tov we hold like R' Yehudah that muktzeh is forbidden, then here the entire second day should be forbidden by reason of "once it was set aside" (and see Tosafos Yom Tov, Eruvin chapter 5, mishnah 48, and his words require further study). And this requires further study.
