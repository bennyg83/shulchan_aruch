A non-Jew who brought a gift: see the Taz, Beitza 24b; and even though there is no muktzeh regarding a non-Jew's [property], see siman 310, seif 2, and simanim 325 and 497, seif 3; nevertheless regarding what is attached and regarding trapping they forbade even regarding a non-Jew's; see the Levush, and for the practical ruling, etc.; and see what I write, God willing, in letter 2, and in Magen Avraham on this, and siman 495 in Magen Avraham 4: trapping and harvesting are rabbinic; see letter 60, and God willing in Magen Avraham it will be explained.
