(ב) Brought for him. Explanation: at first he trapped for himself and afterward reconsidered and brought them for an Israelite — permitted for him even for those who are stringent above; and it deals within the techum see there; Bach wrote siman 325: even though a non-Jew brings a gift to an Israelite we do not say he certainly picked them for him but it is a doubt until he knows he picked for him — end quote; and the simple meaning of the Gemara does not imply thus, for we say a non-Jew who brought a gift to an Israelite — if there is of its type attached it implies even though they did not know they were detached today and also did not know they were detached for an Israelite — bichdei sheya'asu is required; for we say since he brought them for him, from the default he picked for him; and Tosafot in Eruvin daf 39 wrote that the incident of bar Tavya who came to the house of the Exilarch that is permitted on Yom Tov deals with that it was trapped on its own because traps were set from erev Yom Tov, and not a non-Jew who brought a gift to an Israelite — end quote; it implies that if it were a non-Jew who brought a gift it would be forbidden on the second Yom Tov day even if they did not know they were trapped for an Israelite; and it is possible that Bach too was not lenient except requiring bichdei sheya'asu on motza'ei second Yom Tov day:
