[2] [Levush] Even from the melacha of non-Jews etc. It implies even if the non-Jew did it for himself, and this is not so, as proven from siman 276; and if a non-Jew did it for him, it is permitted for a Jew to benefit from it; and it is possible that his intent is also thus, only he abbreviated in his wording. However, from what he wrote, "therefore if a non-Jew did it for himself etc. according to the view of some commentators," it implies that according to Rashi it is not so; and this is not correct, for regarding this everyone agrees; see there. And Beis Yosef and Bach, who wrote in the name of the Rosh and Tur that also according to Rashi it is forbidden when a non-Jew did it for himself, are not precise; and Beis Yosef did not write that according to Rashi it is permitted, only according to the view of Tosafos and R"i; and what differs from siman 276: Beis Yosef already distinguished there that regarding a matter of eating it is different; also there one need not handle.
