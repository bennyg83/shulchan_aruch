And in my humble opinion: if there is a measure of his walking, his detaching and return — if walking takes two days, there is distinction in this: if the non-Jew went from his place on Shabbat and detached and returned on day one at night — day one that is weekday does not count toward the measure, as we say opposite Shabbat day, and they are forbidden until day two at night; for even though the non-Jew's return on day one was on a weekday day, nevertheless if he had not gone on Shabbat and had been walking from his place after Shabbat, he would not have returned until day two at night; if so, if he benefited before this, he benefited from melacha of Shabbat; and for Tosafos it applies on day two: a decree lest he say in order to benefit from this on day two; but in the reverse, if the non-Jew preceded his walking on erev Shabbat and returned on Shabbat — this walking of the non-Jew on erev Shabbat is not counted, and we judge his actions from the beginning of Shabbat, for we judge that the non-Jew brought fruit from there to here on Shabbat day, and as if it was already in that place at the beginning of Shabbat; and there Eliyah Rabbah siman 528 seif 174 and in Knesset HaGedolah there per his reasoning it applies to case two stated above, but there is no proof for case one stated above:
