{23} Forbidden to eat it etc.: because of muktzeh, since it was attached on erev Yom Tov at twilight; and muktzeh of what is attached, or because it lacked trapping, is forbidden according to everyone; and regarding fruits there is a further prohibition lest he ascend and detach; therefore they forbade in eating and in handling.
{24} Even for one for whom it was not brought: meaning, at first he detached or trapped them for himself and afterward reconsidered to bring to a Jew: it is forbidden on that day both for this Jew and for another, since the prohibition here is because of muktzeh and there is no difference from this to that.
{25} In the evening they are permitted immediately: and even if in the evening it was Yom Tov, such as the two Diaspora Yom Tov days, for what was forbidden on the first because of muktzeh is permitted on the second according to everyone; and only on the two Yom Tov days of Rosh Hashana it is forbidden also on the second, for it is like one long day; and also on Shabbos adjacent to Yom Tov it is forbidden because of preparation as above.
{26} For whom it was brought: and specifically if he brought them from within the techum; but if he brought them from outside the techum, even though the detaching was for himself, it is at least forbidden because of techumim for the one for whom it was brought until waiting the time it takes for them to be done, as clarified in seif 5.
