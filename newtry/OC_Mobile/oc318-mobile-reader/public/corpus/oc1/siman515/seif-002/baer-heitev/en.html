For him. Meaning: at first he trapped them for himself, and afterward he reconsidered and brought them to a Jew: they are permitted to him immediately at the departure of the first Diaspora Yom Tov, and waiting the time it takes for them to be done does not apply here, since the non-Jew took them for his own need; and we deal with within the techum; see the end of the seif and see Magen Avraham.
