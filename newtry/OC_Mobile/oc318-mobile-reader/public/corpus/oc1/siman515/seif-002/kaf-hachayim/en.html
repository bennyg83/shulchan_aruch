(ב) There. A non-Jew who brought a gift to an Israelite on Yom Tov etc. — it implies that if he does not bring for free but the Israelite stipulated payment that he bring fish from erev Yom Tov and the non-Jew waited and brought them on Yom Tov — they are permitted at night immediately; and if it is a matter that has no species attached and lacks trapping, it is permitted even on Yom Tov even if he brought from outside the techum, for the non-Jew acts with his own intent — Magen Avraham seif katan 50. Except he wrote: perhaps it is similar to a non-Jew who kindled a lamp on Shabbat, that stipulation does not help, because he acts with the Israelite's intent as written above siman 276 — see there what he tends to argue. And Magen Avraham there ruled leniently that "so that they be made" is not needed. However in Beit Me'ir he challenged Magen Avraham's words stated there — see there. And likewise the Rav in Biur Halacha challenged Magen Avraham's words stated there, except he concluded: nevertheless it appears that if there is further doubt one may permit with stipulation even regarding full melacha for the night of Motzei the first Yom Tov immediately; and regarding techum one may permit with stipulation that he need not wait "so that they be made" for Motzei the first Yom Tov even without further doubt combination — see there:
