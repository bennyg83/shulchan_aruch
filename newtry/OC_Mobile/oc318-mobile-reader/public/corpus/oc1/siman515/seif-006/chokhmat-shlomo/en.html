(Seif 6) In a city whose majority are non-Jews, as a matter of course anyone who brings does so for the need of the majority, etc. Note: see in my composition on Yoreh Deah, clause 47, siman 103, what I wrote in passing in the laws of terefos concerning the holy community of Trebiovla, that even if one Jew alone bought, it is forbidden even for others, for all the people of the city; see there for his reason, and also for the reason of the law that it is forbidden even if he intends for a Jew; see there, and examine carefully.
