And if there is concern they will increase bringing for Israelites it is forbidden, as we say in that turnip incident (Yesh Seder, Beit Yosef, Geonim); and this is specifically when we see they increase bringing; but from the presumption we are not concerned, as written siman 518; however regarding a non-Jew who sells fish, if we permit buying from him there is concern he will increase bringing for Israelites, and one must settle the matter; and a non-Jew who lives in a village and brought fish to a city whose majority are Israelites — there is doubt whether we say from the presumption he trapped for Israelites' need, or we follow the majority of the world and he trapped for non-Jews' need and afterward changed his mind and brought here; if so they do not have the law of lacking trapping; and it appears to be lenient — see there end of seif 2:
