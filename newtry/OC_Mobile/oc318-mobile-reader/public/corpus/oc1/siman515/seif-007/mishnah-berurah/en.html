{64} In the city: that is, within the techum; for since he dwells in the techum and also the fruits are found within the techum, we are not concerned that these fruits he brought from outside the techum; on the contrary we say here they were found and here they were. But if he dwells outside the techum, even though fruits are found within the techum, we assume he brought them from his place outside the techum; and likewise the reverse: even if he is within the techum, since fruits are not found there we are concerned that he brought them from outside the techum.
{65} And if not, it is forbidden: and even though a rabbinic doubt is toward leniency, it is forbidden in this because it is a matter that has a permitted time after Yom Tov; and we already wrote above in siman 325 in Mishnah Berurah s.k. 48 in the name of the later authorities to be lenient in this in the evening immediately; and there are those who are lenient in the essence of this matter, and according to them in a doubt of outside the techum we go toward leniency and even on Yom Tov itself it is permitted. And for the practical law the main view is like the first reasoning; and there are those who leaned to be lenient in this on the second Yom Tov because it is a double doubt; and see above in siman 497, seif 3, what we wrote on this; and see above in siman 325 where the Mechaber explained the details of the laws in this seif, and here he abbreviated.
