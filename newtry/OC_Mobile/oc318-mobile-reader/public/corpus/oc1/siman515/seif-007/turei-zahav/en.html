(ז) If it is a doubt etc. — in the Gemara: Shmuel says we are concerned perhaps outside the wall for us — Rashi explained: for leniency, that we suspend they were at twilight within the techum; and per this explanation, wherever we have doubt we suspend to leniency; but the Geonim explained Shmuel for stringency: even though they entered in the morning, forbidden, for we are concerned perhaps at night he brought them from a distant place, and for us outside the wall; and per this, in every such doubt we go to stringency; and it is difficult from what is written regarding attached in seif 1, forbidden at night with "so that they be made"; and for Rashi we go to leniency — Rabbeinu Yonah answered: whenever there is of that species attached, people's way is to gather them on their day and they do not gather from erev; and since the majority act thus, we do not suspend to be lenient; and Beit Yosef ruled like the Geonim, and in every such doubt to stringency; and it appears the reason: it becomes a matter that has permission after Yom Tov, and we are stringent in its doubt; nevertheless if common in the city, we go to leniency, for it is like certainty:
