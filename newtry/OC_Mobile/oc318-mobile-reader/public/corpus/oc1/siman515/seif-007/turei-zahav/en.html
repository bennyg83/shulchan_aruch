If it is a doubt etc. In the Gemara, Shmuel says we are concerned perhaps they lodged outside the wall. Rashi explained for leniency: we assume that at twilight they were within the techum. And according to this explanation, wherever we have a doubt we assume toward leniency. But the Geonim explained that Shmuel is for stringency: even though they entered in the morning it is forbidden, for we are concerned that at night they brought them from a distant place and lodged outside the wall; and according to this, in every such doubt we go to stringency. And it is difficult from what is written regarding what is attached in seif 1: they are forbidden in the evening until waiting the time it takes for them to be done; and yet according to Rashi we go to leniency. Rabbeinu Yonah answered that wherever there is of its species among what is attached, people's way is to pick it on its day, and they do not pick it from the evening beforehand; and since most do thus, we do not assume toward leniency. And Beis Yosef ruled like the Geonim, and in every such doubt to stringency. And it appears to me that the reason is that it becomes a matter that has a permitted time later, and we are stringent in its doubt. Nevertheless, if it is common in the city, we go to leniency, for it is like a certainty.
