Taz, s.k. 7 Maharshal wrote that one need not wait the time it takes for them to be done. The reasoning appears: just as they permit on the two Diaspora Yom Tov days that it is permitted on the Shabbos after them, for we do not decree so far that he would tell a non-Jew two days earlier to bring them, as the Rosh wrote on this: there is no difference between Diaspora Yom Tov and the Yom Tov of Rosh Hashana, for that reason too applies, that he would not tell him a long time earlier to bring them; only that on Shabbos it is forbidden because of the prohibition of preparation, for the two Yom Tov days of Rosh Hashana are one sanctity and it is preparation from Yom Tov to Shabbos; but for Motzei Shabbos one need not wait the time it takes for them to be done. However, for the practical law one should examine this, for as a stringency we take the view of Rashi that the reason is that one not benefit, and automatically here on Motzei Shabbos it is forbidden, for behold if he brought a gift on Friday it is forbidden for Motzei Shabbos until waiting the time it takes for them to be done so that he not benefit, as stated at the beginning of the siman; the same law applies here on the Yom Tov of Rosh Hashana if he brought on Thursday, for they are one sanctity and are like one long day. See Taz, seif 1, that we do not rule leniently like Reish Lakish. Afterward I saw in Yam Shel Shlomo, siman 6, that Rosh Hashana Yom Tov is not mentioned at all, only regarding the two Diaspora Yom Tov days that fell on Thursday and Friday: if they brought on Thursday, it is forbidden on Shabbos, for we are concerned even for two days lest he tell him; regarding this Maharshal decided from his reasoning that nevertheless on Motzei Shabbos one need not wait the time it takes for them to be done, since it is a double advantage: it is weekday, and also a distant time, so he would not tell him on Thursday so that it be ready for Motzei Shabbos; but regarding the two Yom Tov days of Rosh Hashana nothing of this is mentioned there. And it appears to me in my humble opinion that according to Rabbeinu Tam it is permitted, but according to Rashi it is forbidden as above; and if so one should be stringent in this.
