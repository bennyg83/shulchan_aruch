Taz seif katan 7: Maharshal wrote "so that they be made" is not needed — the reasoning appears: just as we permit on the two days of Diaspora Yom Tov, permitted on the Shabbat after them, for to this extent we are not concerned he will tell a non-Jew two days prior to bring them, as Rosh wrote on this — there is no distinction between Diaspora Yom Tov and Rosh Hashanah Yom Tov regarding this concern lest he tell a long time prior to bring them; only on Shabbat forbidden from preparation prohibition — two days of Rosh Hashanah are one sanctity and it is preparation from Yom Tov to Shabbat; but per what is written "so that they be made" is not needed — nevertheless for practical law one should examine this, for behold for stringency we cited Rashi's approach that the reason is not benefiting, and automatically here at Motzei Shabbat forbidden as stated at the beginning of the siman — likewise on Rosh Hashanah Yom Tov if he brought on the fifth day, for they are one sanctity and are like one long day — see Taz seif 1 that to be lenient he did not rule like R' Yehuda Levi afterward; I saw in Yesh Seder siman 6 that Rosh Hashanah Yom Tov was not mentioned, only the two days of Diaspora Yom Tov where he wrote initially to be stringent like the view of HaItur that also on the two Diaspora days that fall on Thursday and Friday, if he brought on Thursday it is forbidden on Shabbat, for also for two days we are concerned lest he tell him on this day — Maharshal wrote to decide from his reasoning that nevertheless at Motzei Shabbat "so that they be made" is not needed, since it is doubly weekday and also a distant time he will not tell him on the fifth day for Motzei Shabbat; but regarding the two days of Rosh Hashanah Yom Tov he was not mentioned there; and in my humble opinion: per Rabbeinu Tam's approach it is permitted, but per Rashi's approach forbidden, as explained; if so one should be stringent here:
