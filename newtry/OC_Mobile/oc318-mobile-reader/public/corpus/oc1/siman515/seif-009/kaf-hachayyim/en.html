It is forbidden even to one, etc. This follows the rule for every forbidden melachah performed by a non-Jew for a Jew: it is forbidden to every Jew until evening and until the time required to perform the act has passed. This is explained in Shabbos 122a regarding a lamp and a ramp, and in Shulchan Aruch above, siman 266, se'if 1, and siman 325. See there. There is no distinction between produce belonging to the non-Jew and produce belonging to the Jew, because the prohibition results from the melachah performed for a Jew. Even if the non-Jew harvested it for the Jew's animal, the Jew may not feed it to the animal. If the non-Jew feeds it to the animal, however, the Jew need not object, as explained in the Talmud there and in siman 325, se'ifim 11, 12, and 13, and our words there, with Heaven's help.
