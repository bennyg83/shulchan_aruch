Forbidden even to anyone etc. — like the law of any forbidden melacha that a non-Jew did for an Israelite, forbidden to every Israelite until night and "so that they be made," as explained in Shabbat 122a regarding a lamp and sheep, and in Shulchan Aruch above siman 266 seif 1 and in siman 528 — see there.
