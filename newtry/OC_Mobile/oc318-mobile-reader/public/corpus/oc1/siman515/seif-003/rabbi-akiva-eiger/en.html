Magen Avraham seif katan 8: forbidden on the second because of preparation — at first glance it seemed wondrous to me: if here the prohibition is because of preparation, why does one need on Motzei Shabbat and Yom Tov also "so that they be made"? And after examination, it appears Magen Avraham's intent in his difficulty was: since there are two sanctities, it should suffice with "so that they be made" on the first night, for even though not fit for doing, it counts as the measure of "so that they be made" (see Machatzit HaShekel who proved Magen Avraham from two Yom Tov days of the Diaspora permitted at night on the second Yom Tov with "so that they be made" as he wrote — behold even if before us it is clear the second Yom Tov day is weekday, it is not fit for doing, for we suspend it is forbidden to do — see there; and in my humble opinion a stronger proof from seif 4: there are those who permit to eat them on Shabbat as he wrote — behold it counts for "so that they be made" even though not fit for doing); nevertheless, all this is if the waiting as he wrote is only from the melacha done for him — here there is recognition and it counts for this; therefore Magen Avraham answered: even if trapped on their own, forbidden all Shabbat because of preparation; if so all Shabbat does not count for "so that they be made," since without the melacha for an Israelite it would have been forbidden to eat because of preparation; therefore one must wait at Motzei Shabbat with "so that they be made" — examine:
