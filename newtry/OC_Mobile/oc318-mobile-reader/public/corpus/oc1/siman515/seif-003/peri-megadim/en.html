Forbidden. See the Taz; see letter 1 and in Magen Avraham 1; and in Yoreh Deah regarding muktzeh of trapping and of what is attached, even regarding a non-Jew's it is forbidden; and in the evening of the first day onto the second day of the Diaspora Yom Tov days it is permitted immediately, and one need not wait the time it takes for them to be done, since the non-Jew detached them for his own need there is nothing to be concerned about; and what he wrote "and this is not for everyone's reasoning" means according to Rashi: there are those who say that even for a non-Jew's need, all the more so we require that one not benefit from melacha of Yom Tov; see letter 1.
