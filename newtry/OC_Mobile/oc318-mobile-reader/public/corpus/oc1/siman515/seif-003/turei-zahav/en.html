Even if it is a doubt etc. Included in this also is if there is a doubt whether he brought them for his own need or for a Jew's need, regarding waiting the time it takes for them to be done; and likewise to forbid even on the second Yom Tov according to Rabbeinu Tam's view: we go to stringency in all the doubts, and the reason will be explained in seif 7. And what the Tur wrote here, "and even if it is a doubt whether they were picked today, they are forbidden," means on that very day; and it refers to the beginning of the siman that forbids when it is certain, and he said here that also in a doubt there is a prohibition, unless it appears and is recognizable that they were picked yesterday; and in the evening it is permitted even in a doubt, and one need not wait the time it takes for them to be done; and he relied on what is written in siman 325 on this matter; and my master, my father-in-law, of blessed memory, challenged the Tur's words against each other for nothing.
A non-Jew speaks casually. The reason for this is that he is not improving [the situation]; on the contrary he worsens it, for those trapped today are better; and this casual speech means that the non-Jew does not know that what was trapped today is forbidden to a Jew; for if not so, it is forbidden, like flax threads in Yoreh Deah siman 302, that a non-Jew is not believed through casual speech.
