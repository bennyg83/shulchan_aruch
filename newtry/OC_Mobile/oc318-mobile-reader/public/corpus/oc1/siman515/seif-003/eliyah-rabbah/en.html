[3] [Levush] That the Sages forbade etc. a gift etc. It appears to me that if he does not bring for free, only that the Jew fixed a price with him beforehand that he bring him fish from erev Yom Tov, and the non-Jew delayed and brought on Yom Tov, one may rely on the first reasoning that it is permitted on the second night after waiting the time it takes for them to be done; and see in Magen Avraham regarding muktzeh whose permission depends on designation on erev Yom Tov for that purpose, and by hands or by speech, as explained in siman 308: it is forbidden to do so on the first Yom Tov for the second, for it is like a weekday act; and if he transgressed and did so, it is permitted on the second (Taz) at the end of this siman.
