Even if there is doubt whether they were gathered or trapped that day, they are forbidden. But if it is evident that they were not gathered or trapped that day, it is permitted for one for whom they were not brought. And if they did not come from outside the boundary, it is permitted even for the one for whom they were brought.Rema: A non-Jew who speaks casually that it was not gathered or trapped today is believed.
