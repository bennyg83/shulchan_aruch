3) There. If some of that species remains attached to the ground, etc. We are concerned that it may have been detached that day. Anything detached from the ground on Yom Tov is forbidden, because the Sages decreed lest one detach it. If it is known that it was not detached that day, it is permitted even if it appears as though it was detached that day. Riaz. Shiltei HaGibborim on Rif, chapter 3 of Beitzah, cites him.
