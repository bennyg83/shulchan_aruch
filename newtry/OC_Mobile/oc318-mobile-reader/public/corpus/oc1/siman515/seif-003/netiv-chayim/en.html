(Taz seif katan 3) But he challenged etc. Gloss: and it appears he holds like the view of Halakhot Gedolot that Magen Avraham brought in siman 518 seif katan 37: specifically a thorn not visible, but a large thorn we do not permit to handle in a karmelit, for it is evident to every eye it will not injure; if so it is difficult (and to me the young Shimon Hirsch it appears in Halakhot Gedolot he establishes this spit dealing with after roasting and it is still hot, and therefore he handles it, lest he touch its sides; and there he is not careful not to touch because when cold it does not injure, and the comparison of Ravina to a thorn fits well) from that spit that presumably it is large — how does Ravina compare a spit to a thorn on Rosh Hashanah? Therefore Ravina permits only handling from the side; and this is what he means: like a thorn on Rosh Hashanah, full handling is permitted; at least on a large spit handling from the side is permitted, for sometimes a person walks his path and is injured by the spit; but Rashi and Rambam do not hold this law in such a case, and therefore they permitted full handling like a thorn even large that is evident; and with this Magen Avraham's difficulties in siman 518 seif katan 37 are resolved:
