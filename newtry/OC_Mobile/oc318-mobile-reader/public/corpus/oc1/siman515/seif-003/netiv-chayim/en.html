(Taz, s.k. 3) But it is difficult, etc. Note: and it appears that he holds like the view of Halachos Gedolos that Magen Avraham brought in siman 308, s.k. 37, that specifically a thorn that is not visible [may be handled], but a large thorn we do not permit to handle in a karmelis, for it is recognizable to every eye that it will not cause damage. If so, it is difficult (and to me, the insignificant Shimon Hirsch, it appears that Halachos Gedolos establishes this spit as dealing after roasting, while it is still hot, and therefore one handles it lest one touch its sides, and there one is not careful not to touch because it does not damage when cold; and Ravina properly compares it to a thorn) from that case of the spit, which is presumably large, and how does Ravina compare a spit to a thorn in the public domain? One must say that Ravina permits only handling from the side; and this is what he means: just as regarding a thorn in the public domain it is permitted to handle with complete handling, in any case with a large spit it is permitted to handle by handling from the side, for sometimes a person walking in his path is injured by the spit. But Rashi and Rambam do not hold that ruling of Halachos Gedolos, and therefore they permitted complete handling like a thorn, even a large one that is recognizable. And with this the difficulties of Magen Avraham in siman 308, s.k. 37, are resolved.
