(א) [Seif 1] Bearers of the bier and their replacements and replacements of replacements, etc. — Rabbenu Yonah wrote on replacements of replacements: and although the last ones have time to read while the second group is carrying, nevertheless they are exempt because sometimes they decide that that third group in two [hours] will travel, and because of this they should not begin.
