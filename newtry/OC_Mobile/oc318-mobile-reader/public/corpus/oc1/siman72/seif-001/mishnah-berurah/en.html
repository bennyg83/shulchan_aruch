(1) And the substitutes of their substitutes — even though the last [group] have time to read while the first are carrying, nevertheless they are exempt because sometimes they change their minds and give [the duty] to them to carry; and therefore they may not begin.

(2) In them — such as in a city where there is a society designated for this, and all members of the society take turns carrying because they all wish to gain merit through it — all are exempt from Keri'as Shema because they are occupied with the occupation of a mitzvah, even those far from the bier. And in a place where there is no society designated for this, those adjacent below are exempt because it is customary to give them the merit of the mitzvah when they relieve the carriers; but those far from the bier are obligated.

(3) Are obligated — in Keri'as Shema [and even though escorting the deceased is included among gemilus chasadim and gemilus chasadim is from the Torah, nevertheless they are not occupied at all and can recite the first verse with intent and standing, and the rest while walking]; but they are exempt from tefillah, as written in siman 106.
