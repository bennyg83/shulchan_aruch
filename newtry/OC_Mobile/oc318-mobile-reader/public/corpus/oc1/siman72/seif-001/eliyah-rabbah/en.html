"Because they are close to the bier," etc. Levush explains that this refers specifically to those who are physically close to the bier. Lechem Chamudot, page 19, however, explains Rambam's words differently: in those times, and perhaps still in some places, there were designated groups appointed for carrying the bier. Accordingly, the law applies whether they were presently close to the bier or not. This is also implied by Rambam's commentary on the Mishnah in Perek Mi SheMet. Nevertheless, in a place where there are no designated groups of bier-bearers, but rather whoever wishes may carry it, Levush's interpretation is correct in practical halacha. Beit Yosef writes that even though the later groups would have enough time to recite Kriat Shema while the second group is carrying the bier, they are nevertheless exempt, because it can sometimes happen that the third group is called upon to leave in place of the second.
