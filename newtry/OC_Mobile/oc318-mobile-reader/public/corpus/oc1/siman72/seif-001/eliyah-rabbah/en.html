(1) "Since below...," etc. The Levush explained specifically those adjacent to the bier; but in Lechem Chamudos page 19 he explained the Rambam's words—that the matter was in those times, and it is possible that in some places there are still groups designated for this; and if so, whether adjacent or not adjacent—until here; and so it is implied in the Mishnah commentary on the Rambam, chapter Mi sheMetu. Nevertheless, in a place where there are no designated groups of bier-carriers but whoever wishes carries—the Levush's words are correct for practical halacha. Beit Yosef wrote that even though the last carriers have time to recite while the second shift is still carrying, even so they are exempt because sometimes they reconsider and the third shift travels like the second.
