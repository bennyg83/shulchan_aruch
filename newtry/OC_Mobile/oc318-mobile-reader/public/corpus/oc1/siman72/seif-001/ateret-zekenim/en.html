Since the bier needs them, they are exempt. Even though there is time for them to recite while the second group is carrying, they are nevertheless exempt, because at times the bearers change their minds and call upon them. Some explain that those near the bier, whether immediately before it or immediately behind it, are exempt, since the bier certainly needs them. They are close to it, and it is customary for the bearers to exchange places with those nearest them. The other escorts, who are far from the bier and will not be called upon to carry, are obligated. This applies where there are no designated bearers. Where designated burial societies are appointed for carrying the bier, as in the holy communities of Krakow and Lviv and certain other communities, it is obvious that members of the designated group are considered needed by the bier even when they are far from it. The rest of the people, who are merely escorting and are not bearers, are considered unnecessary to the bier even when nearby. See Yoreh Deah, siman 358.
