Since the bier needs them below, they are exempt; and even though there is time for the last carriers to recite while the second shift is still carrying, nevertheless they are exempt because sometimes they reconsider and there are those who wrote that those near the bier—whether near before the bier or near after it—since the bier needs them, they are certainly adjacent to it, and the way of the carriers is to exchange with those adjacent to them; therefore they are exempt. And the rest of the escorts who are far from the bier and will not reach it to carry are obligated (Maharam). And all this is when the bier-carriers are not designated; but in a place where the bier-carriers are designated and there are groups ready for this, as in the holy community of Krakow and the holy community of Lviv and in some other communities, it is straightforward that those who are in the group of bier-carriers and ready for this—even if they are far from the bier—it is called that the bier needs them; and the rest of the people who are not bier-carriers but only escorts—even if they are near—it is called that the bier does not need them, as above (see Yoreh De'ah siman 358).
