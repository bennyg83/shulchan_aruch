(Siman katan note 1) Below, etc. — there are societies, etc.; meaning even if they are far from the bier. And see Beit Yosef in the name of the Tur, who wrote: even if there are three groups that replace one another, and it is common thought that while the first ones are carrying, group three is obligated — for they can read while group two carries. And the Tur wrote that nevertheless they are exempt, for sometimes they change their minds and the third group carries in the second [round]. End quote.
