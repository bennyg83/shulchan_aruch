Seif katan 1: The bier, etc. Where there are designated groups, etc. Meaning, even those far from the bier are exempt. See Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah, who writes that even if there are three groups replacing one another, and one might have thought that the third group is obligated while the first group carries, since it can recite while the second group carries, nevertheless all are exempt. At times the arrangement changes and the third group may carry in place of the second.
