(1) [seif 1] Those who carry the bier and their substitutes and the substitutes of their substitutes, etc. R' Yonah wrote regarding the substitutes of their substitutes: even though the last carriers have time to recite while the second shift is still carrying, nevertheless they are exempt because sometimes they reconsider and that third shift in the second [shift's place] will travel; and for this reason they may not begin. Beit Yosef. Prisha letter 1. Ohr Torah letter 1. Eliyah Rabbah letter 1. Machatzit HaShekel letter 1.
