(4) There: "near Kriat Shema...," etc. Beit Yosef wrote in the name of R' Yonah: "At first glance it appears that the same applies to evening Kriat Shema—one does not bring out the deceased near it; but it is possible that one need not be strict regarding evening Kriat Shema, since its time is all night"—end of his words. However, from the implication of his words here in Shulchan Aruch it appears that one does not distinguish, and even near evening Kriat Shema it is forbidden. And so explained Ohr Torah letter 3 and Magen Avraham seif kaf-bet—the words of Maran. And so too Eliyah Rabbah letter 2. But Magen Avraham wrote there: "and nevertheless, it appears to me that one should be lenient regarding evening, as Terumat Yarden wrote—that if they do not bury him, they will violate 'his corpse shall not remain overnight.' And it appears to me that if it is from plag haMinchah and onward, he recites Kriat Shema and buries him afterward—see siman 235"—end of his words. And so too Eliyah Rabbah there, that regarding evening Kriat Shema one should be lenient per Magen Avraham—see there. And so too Raz letter 2. And he wrote there that even if they could bury at night, they would violate a positive commandment, for "you shall surely bury him" on that day—if they do not bury him before sunset—end of his words. And it appears that everything depends on the time and the matter; and if possible, one should not deviate from Maran's words. See Yoreh De'ah siman 358 in the gloss, seif 2.
