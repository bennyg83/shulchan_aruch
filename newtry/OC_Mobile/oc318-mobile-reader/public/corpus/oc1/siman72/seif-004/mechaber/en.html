They buried the deceased and the mourners returned to receive condolences, and all the people go after them from the place of the grave to the place that the mourners are standing to do a line to receive condolences - if the people are able to begin and to finish even one verse before they arrive at the line, they begin. And if not, they don't begin.Rem"a: if there is time [left] in the day to recite afterwards (Beit Yosef in the name of the Ramban).
