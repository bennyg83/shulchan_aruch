They buried the deceased and the mourners returned to receive condolences, and all the people go after them from the place of the grave to the place that the mourners are standing to form a row to receive condolences — if the people are able to begin and to finish even one verse before they arrive at the row, they begin. And if not, they do not begin.if there is time left in the day to recite afterwards (Beit Yosef in the name of the Ramban).
