(ב) There — since those below need them they are exempt, etc. — Levush wrote: the explanation is those who are before the bier, etc. — meaning those who are adjacent to the bier, whether adjacent before the bier or adjacent after it; since the bier needs them, certainly they are adjacent to it, and the way of bearers is to exchange with those adjacent to them — therefore they are exempt; and the rest of the escorts who are far from the bier and will not come to carry are obligated — until here. And Lechem HaPanim brought it in chapter Mi sheMeito note 22 and wrote on it that his explanation is not compelling, and his explanation is also unnecessary; for the matter was in those times and possibly still in some places and countries where there are groups designated for this — if so, whether adjacent or not adjacent they are exempt — until here; and he brought Magen Avraham note 1, Eliyah Rabbah note 8; and wrote there Arukh HaShulchan: and in any case, in a place where there are no designated groups of bier-bearers but whoever wishes carries — the words of Levush are correct for practical law — until here.
