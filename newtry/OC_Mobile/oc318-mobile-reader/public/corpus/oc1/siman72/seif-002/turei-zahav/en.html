We do not bring out, etc. — In the Gemara they challenged: but Rabbi Yehuda took him out close to Keri'as Shema — an important person is different; and therefore the Rambam distinguished between an important person and another. And the Tur wrote in the name of the Rosh that he did not distinguish in this, and so too the Rif. And as the Beit Yosef — his reason: perhaps such an important person is not common in our time who would be fit to annul Keri'as Shema on his account. And I say: even as a general rule in this, that all we find in the Gemara to distinguish between an important person to be stringent where there is stringency for an important person — surely every person will make himself important to be stringent; and as the Tur wrote below siman 554: "Arishevet, a person should always make himself a Torah scholar and annul" — and we are not concerned about presumptuousness. But if importance causes leniency, whatever will be — in our generations there is no one important enough to be lenient. An example: they do not fine now a litra of gold for one who disgraces a Torah scholar, as written in the Agudah; and likewise to permit vows in private, and the like. Therefore the Rif and Rosh did well not to distinguish regarding an important person in our generations.
