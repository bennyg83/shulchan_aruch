Seif katan 2: Close, etc. As Rabbeinu Yonah writes, but not for Rabbeinu Yonah's reason. Rabbeinu Yonah explains that the evening Krias Shema may be recited throughout the night. Magen Avraham explains instead that reciting Krias Shema first might lead to negligence in the burial.
And if he will not be buried that day, they will violate the prohibition of leaving a corpse overnight, etc. In Yoreh Deah, siman 358, seif 2, Rama cites two opinions on this. See there. Eliyah Rabbah writes likewise.
