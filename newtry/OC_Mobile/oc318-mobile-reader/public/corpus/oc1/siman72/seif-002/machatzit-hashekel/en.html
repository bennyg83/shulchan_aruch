(Siman katan note 2) Close, etc. — as the Tur wrote; but not for his reason. For the Tur wrote the reason: since for evening [Shema] the time for Keri'as Shema is all night. And the Magen Avraham wrote the reason: perhaps through reading Keri'as Shema he will become negligent.

And if we do not bury him today he transgresses "you shall not leave [him overnight]," etc. And in Yoreh De'ah siman 358 seif 2 he brought the Rama's two opinions on this; see there, likewise in the book Eliyah Rabbah.
