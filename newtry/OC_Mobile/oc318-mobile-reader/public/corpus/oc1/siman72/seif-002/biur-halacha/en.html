"We do not bring out...," etc., "that there is no sufficient time...," etc.—that is, the "near" we say here does not mean like "near Minchah" in siman 232, which is half an hour; rather it means enough time to bring him out and bury him before the time to begin Kriat Shema arrives, which is when one can recognize an acquaintance at a distance of four amos [Terumat Yarden and Beit Yosef].
