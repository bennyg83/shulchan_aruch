They do not take out, etc., when there is not enough time, etc. Meaning, the term 'close' here does not mean, as it does regarding Minchah in siman 232, half an hour. Rather, it means that there is not enough time to take the deceased out and bury him before the beginning time for Krias Shema arrives, namely when one can recognize an acquaintance at a distance of four amos. Rabbeinu Yonah and Beit Yosef.
