For Krias Shema. This implies that even shortly before the evening Krias Shema, whose time extends throughout the night, the deceased may not be taken out. Rabbeinu Yonah writes that one should not be stringent regarding the evening Krias Shema. The Magen Avraham likewise writes that, when failure to bury him that day would violate the prohibition of leaving a corpse overnight, then from plag haminchah onward one should recite Krias Shema and bury him afterward. See siman 235. Magen Avraham.
