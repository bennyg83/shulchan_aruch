Close to Krias Shema. Since the ruling is stated without qualification, it implies that even close to the evening Krias Shema it is forbidden. Nevertheless, it appears to me that one may be lenient regarding the evening Krias Shema, as Rabbeinu Yonah writes, for if they do not bury him that day they will violate the prohibition of leaving a corpse overnight. It appears to me that, from plag haminchah onward, one should recite Krias Shema and bury him afterward. See siman 235.
