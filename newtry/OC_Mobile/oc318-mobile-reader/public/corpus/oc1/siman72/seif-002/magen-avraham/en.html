Close to Keri'as Shema — from his being silent here it is implied that he holds that even close to evening Keri'as Shema is forbidden; and nevertheless it seems to me that one should be lenient regarding evening, as the Tur wrote: if we do not bury him today he transgresses "you shall not leave." And it seems to me that if it is from plag haMinchah and onward he reads Keri'as Shema and buries him afterwards; see siman 235.
