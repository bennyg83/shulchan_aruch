There, in the continuation of his words: “For if he does not bury him today, he will violate Bal Tosif.” It is possible that he wrote this wording precisely, because even if he buries him at night he violates Bal Talin by leaving him unburied for part of the night. See Responsa Ginat Veradim, Yoreh De'ah, klal 5, simanim 2 and 3.
