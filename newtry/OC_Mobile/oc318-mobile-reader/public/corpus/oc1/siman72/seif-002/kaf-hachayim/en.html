(2) There: "Since below they are needed, they are exempt...," etc. The Levush wrote that the intent is those who are before the bier, etc.—that is, those who are near the bier, whether near before the bier or near after it; since the bier needs them, they are certainly adjacent to it, and the way of the carriers is to exchange with those adjacent to them; therefore they are exempt. And the rest of the escorts who are far from the bier and will not reach it to carry are obligated—end of his words. And they brought Lechem Chamudos in the chapter Mi sheMetu, letter 22, and wrote on it that his explanation is not necessary, and one does not need his explanation at all—for the matter was in those times, and it is possible that in some places and countries there are groups designated for this; and if so, whether adjacent or not adjacent, they are exempt—end of his words. And they brought Magen Avraham seif kaf-alef; Eliyah Rabbah letter 8, and quoted Eliyah Rabbah: "and nevertheless, in a place where there are no designated groups of bier-carriers but whoever wishes carries—the Levush's words are correct for practical halacha"—end of his words. And so too Peri Megadim in Eshel Avraham letter 1.
