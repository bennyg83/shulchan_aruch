seif 3, "and they pray...," etc. Even though in the conclusion it teaches "they recite and pray," and in the opening it only teaches "they recite"—nevertheless one does not say this; rather, in the conclusion it comes to inform us how they act regarding Kriat Shema and how regarding prayer. And proof from what is written in the Tosefta chapter 2 and in the Yerushalmi there: the eulogizer and all who are occupied with the eulogy interrupt for Kriat Shema and do not interrupt for prayer. And an incident occurred in which the Sages interrupted for Kriat Shema and for prayer; and an incident of Rav—Terumat Yarden—and this requires study, for in the Yerushalmi it concludes there, "and we learned: if they can begin and finish"—the Mishnah on the first day; and that which the Tanna teaches "on the second day"—evidently it refers to the conclusion, when the deceased is not lying before them. See Tur, Beit Yosef, and Taz.
