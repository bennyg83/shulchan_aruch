The inner ones — Taz: for in the mishnah they said the inner ones are obligated and the outer ones exempt; and in the Gemara 19b: a row that sees inward is exempt and that does not see inward is obligated; and Bach explained they stand in rows in a circle like an onion, and two circles are exempt because the mourner is in the middle and a row surrounds him, and then a second row — two rows are exempt because it is possible through the shoulders of row one to see row two and see the mourner is called; and Taz challenged this: if so, row after row — why teach two rows are exempt? Perforce he explained the mourner stands in an equal row without a circle, and afterward they surround in rows; and the first surrounding row is exempt because those before it see the mourner's face and console him too, even though that row is also behind the mourner in the circle — the whole circle is exempt; and this is what "row" means — one circle seeing inward — an equal row inside and not seeing the mourner's face behind is exempt; and "circle" is missing a letter — they stand in a square; if so there is difficulty of four rows — why say row? Therefore he said circle in a round form and one row.
