(3) [seif 2] One does not bring out the deceased near Kriat Shema, etc.—and one does not distinguish between an important person and another. So is the view of the Rif and the Rosh, as written in Beit Yosef; and see there the reason. And so it is implied from Maran's words here in Shulchan Aruch. And so too Taz seif kaf-alef. Be'er Hetev letter 2.

(3) There: "that there is no sufficient time to bring out...," etc.—that is, the "near" we say here does not mean like "near Minchah" that we say below, which is the measure of half an hour; rather it means enough time for an hour to bring him out and bury him before the time for Kriat Shema arrives. Ohr Torah letter 2.
