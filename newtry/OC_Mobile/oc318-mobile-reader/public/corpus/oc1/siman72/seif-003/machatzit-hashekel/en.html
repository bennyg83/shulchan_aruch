(Siman katan note 3) They may begin, etc. — but "in Yoreh De'ah" means in the Tur [section] Yoreh De'ah; but in the Shulchan Aruch he did not write anything. And from what the Tur in Yoreh De'ah wrote "what they can," it is implied that when they reach the row they stop, even [if they are] standing in the middle of Keri'as Shema.
