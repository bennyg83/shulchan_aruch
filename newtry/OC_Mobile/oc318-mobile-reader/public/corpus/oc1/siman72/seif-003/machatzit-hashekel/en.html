Seif katan 3: They should begin, etc. But in Yoreh Deah. Meaning, in the Tur, Yoreh Deah, whereas the Shulchan Aruch writes nothing about it. Since the Tur writes that they should recite as much as they can, this implies that when they reach the condolence row they interrupt even if they are in the middle of Krias Shema.
