(9) Slip away — for it is impossible that they recite here next to the deceased because of "the scoffer."

(10) And pray — and many decisors disagree and hold that they were lenient regarding tefillah with the deceased lying before them, since tefillah is rabbinic; and it seems regarding the eulogy-chazzan one may certainly rely on leniency here that he need not interrupt for tefillah, for even regarding Keri'as Shema some say he is exempt in this; see Tur and Beit Yosef.

(11) And is silent — and he does not answer anything after them for the reason explained in siman 71.
