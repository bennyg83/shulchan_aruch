(9) They slip away. Because they cannot recite near the deceased, due to the prohibition of mocking the helpless.
(10) And they pray. Many authorities disagree and maintain that, when the deceased is lying before them, the Sages were lenient regarding prayer because prayer is Rabbinic. Regarding the chazzan delivering the eulogy, one may certainly rely on the lenient view and not require him to interrupt for prayer, since some maintain that he is exempt even from Krias Shema in this situation. See Tur and Beit Yosef.
(11) And remains silent. He does not answer anything after them, for the reason explained in siman 71.
