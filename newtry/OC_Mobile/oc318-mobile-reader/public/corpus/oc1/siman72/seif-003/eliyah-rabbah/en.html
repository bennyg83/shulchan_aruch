(3) "lying before them...," etc. But when it is not lying before them, they recite and pray (Tur)—meaning the deceased is in another house; and there are those who explain that he is placed on one side—Rashi—and Beit Yosef brought them. Nachalat Tzvi ruled straightforwardly like those who explain, and so it is implied in Olah Tamid, except that there is a scribal error in his words, and I do not know from where they derive it. And in this manner Bach and Shach ruled in Yoreh De'ah siman 340 regarding words of Torah—that the entire room in which the deceased lies is like four amos.
