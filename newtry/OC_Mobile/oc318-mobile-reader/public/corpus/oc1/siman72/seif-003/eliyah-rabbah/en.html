[3] Lying before them, etc. If the deceased is not lying before them, they recite Krias Shema and pray. Tur. This means that the deceased is in another room. Others explain that he is placed to one side. Rashi, cited by Beit Yosef. Nachalas Tzvi rules simply according to the latter explanation, and Olas Tamid appears to do so as well, although there is a scribal error in his words. I do not know the basis for this. In a similar case, Bach and Shach rule in Yoreh Deah, siman 344, regarding Torah study, that the entire room containing the deceased is treated like the four amos surrounding him.
[4] And they recite and pray, etc. See Lechem Chamudos, folio 19, who discusses this at length and rules that they do not pray, since prayer is Rabbinic. The Tur writes likewise in Yoreh Deah, siman 344, and Derishah also rules this way and offers lengthy proofs.
