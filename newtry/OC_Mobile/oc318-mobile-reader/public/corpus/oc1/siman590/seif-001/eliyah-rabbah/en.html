[1] [Levush] When he cries, etc. For we must blow a teruah that resembles the sound of Sarah's crying when she cried over the news of the Akeidah. Another reason is that a person must wail before Hashem, blessed is He, over his sins, for presumably, etc. For this reason one must make the order tashat before the order tarat [Abudraham]. However, after the fact this is not indispensable, as he wrote at the end of siman 593.
