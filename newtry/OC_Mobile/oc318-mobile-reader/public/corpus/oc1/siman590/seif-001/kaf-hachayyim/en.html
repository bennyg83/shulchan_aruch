1) [Seif 1] Because teruah is stated three times concerning Yovel and Rosh Hashanah. Twice concerning Rosh Hashanah and once concerning Yom Kippur of Yovel. Concerning Yom Kippur of Yovel it is written, "You shall cause the shofar blast of teruah to pass," etc. Concerning Rosh Hashanah it is written, "A day of rest, a remembrance of teruah," etc., and, "It shall be a day of teruah for you." Rosh Hashanah 34a.
