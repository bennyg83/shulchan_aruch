Magen Avraham s.k. 1. And the second is an interruption; see seif 8. According to this, one could say that for the listener tashrat suffices, according to what we wrote in siman 585 in the margin to uphold the words of the Levush. However, one who examines the Levush there will see that what he is lenient about for the listener is only something that belongs to that order, such as two teruos or two shevarim. But something not from that order is an interruption. If so, in tashrat, if shevarim alone or teruah alone is the teruah, automatically the other one is not from that order and is an interruption even for the listener.
