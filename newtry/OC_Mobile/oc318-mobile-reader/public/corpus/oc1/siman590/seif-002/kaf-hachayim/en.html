(b) Stated there. And every plain tekiah before it, etc. — gemara daf 33b. Whence plain before? It is written vehaverata shofar teruah — Rashi 34a: vehaverata means plain, implying passing one sound. Whence plain after? It is written ta'aviru shofar — Rashi: behold passing beginning and end and teruah written between them. They also bring another baraita: we need plain before and plain after — from ve-takeatem teruah, plain before it; from teruah yitke'u, plain after it — see there.
