2) Ibid. Every teruah has a straight blast before it, etc. See the Gemara there, 33b. From where do we know that a straight blast precedes it? The verse teaches, "You shall cause the shofar blast of teruah to pass." Rashi explained, 34a, that "you shall cause to pass" implies a single continuous sound, end quote. From where do we know that a straight blast follows it? The verse teaches, "You shall cause the shofar to pass." Rashi explained that the term "passing" appears at the beginning and at the end, with teruah written between them, end quote. The Gemara there also cites another Baraisa, which derives that a straight blast is required before and after from the verses, "You shall sound a teruah," indicating a straight blast before it, and, "A teruah they shall sound," indicating a straight blast after it, see there. Meaning, from the phrase "You shall sound a teruah" it is implied that a tekiah is required before the teruah, and from the phrase "A teruah they shall sound" it is implied that a tekiah is also required after the teruah.
