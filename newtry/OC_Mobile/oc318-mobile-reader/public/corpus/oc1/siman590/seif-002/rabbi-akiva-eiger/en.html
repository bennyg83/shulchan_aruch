Magen Avraham s.k. 5. He must go back to the beginning and blow. This is astonishing, for the Mishnah explicitly teaches that he has only one. If by reasoning it would be considered merely occupied and therefore an interruption, the Mishnah actually proves that it counts for him as one; see the Ran. In any event, it is impossible to say that he must go back to the beginning. This was already noted in Korban Nesanel on the Rosh, chapter 4, siman 28.
