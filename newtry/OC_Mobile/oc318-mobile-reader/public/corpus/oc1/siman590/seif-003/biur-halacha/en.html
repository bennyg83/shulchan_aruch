And some say that one need not be concerned, etc. That is, even according to this view, that the measure of a teruah is only three mere forces, nevertheless the shevarim are longer. If so, the tekiah of the tashat set is not less than three shevarim. Therefore there is no concern as long as he does not extend one shever for the measure of three shevarim, for it does not become a tekiah in tashat with less than this measure. What the Mechaber wrote at the end of the seif, 'and according to this there is no concern,' refers to the second view there, and the Rema added in our matter to be lenient even according to the first view [Taz, and it is obvious].
And one who did not extend the tekiah for this measure and extended the shevarim, etc. The Mechaber's intent, according to his view above like the first opinion, is that if one made a shever of three forces it becomes a tekiah. So too here, if he made the tekiah only seventeen forces, so that he does not fulfill according to the latter view, and in the shevarim he made one shever of three forces, he does not fulfill according to the first view. However, according to our practice like the Rema above in the gloss, even if he made the shever in tashat three forces, he certainly fulfills; and likewise in tashrat, if he drew out the shever less than the measure of shevarim and teruah, he also fulfills. The measure of one shever in tashat, the Mateh Ephraim wrote, is that he may extend it up to six forces, but not including six; consequently in tashrat, where the measure is shevarim and teruah, it is up to nine forces, but not including nine. The practical result is that we invalidate only if he made the shever in tashrat the measure of nine forces; then, if he made the tekiah in tashrat less than eighteen, we say that he fulfilled according to neither master. Likewise in tashat, if he made the tekiah less than nine forces and in the shevarim made one shever of six forces, he also fulfilled according to neither master. However, Magen Avraham has already instructed us above that for us, according to all views, since our custom is to make the shevarim from three up to nine forces, but not including nine, one must make the tekiah at least nine forces, for otherwise even after the fact he does not fulfill.
