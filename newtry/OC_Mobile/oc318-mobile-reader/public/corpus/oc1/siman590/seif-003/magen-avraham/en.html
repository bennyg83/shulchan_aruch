(א) Too much. That is like the measure of three shevarim and teruah — for in tashrat we act from doubt lest perhaps the teruah of the verse is both together — if so the tekiah too must be in both together; if so even if he lengthened somewhat in shevarim we have no concern; but in tashat he should not lengthen in one shever like three shevarim — from this reason Bach wrote; nevertheless per Raavad that tekiah does not depend on teruah but always the measure of tekiah is nine terumitin — if so even in tashrat one must beware not to lengthen in one shever like nine terumitin; and if he did not lengthen in the tekiah of tashrat and tashat like nine beats he must return and blow, for our custom is to lengthen in shevarim more than three beats up to nine beats — if so there are two leniencies contradicting; but in the tekiah of terat one may rely b'dieved on Rashi that teruah is three beats whatever they are; and for this reason if he did not make in every teruah only three beats he fulfills b'dieved; but three shevarim — all agree are like nine beats and with less he does not fulfill and must return to the beginning as above — end. And now the practice is l'chatchila not to make nine beats in teruah per Rashi; the stringent will be blessed. And what he wrote but three shevarim, etc. — requires study, for on the contrary Tur and Rabbeinu Yehuda and poskim wrote l'chatchila one should not lengthen in one shever like three beats — how did he write for all it is nine beats? Requires study; perhaps it is a typo; and so the custom is not to lengthen them so much like nine beats; and the custom not to add beyond three shevarim (Maharil); and three shevarim are needed besides the long sound made at the end of shevarim — also in teruah they make such a long sound; if he did not do so he does not fulfill (Bach Minhagim); and they did not practice thus — also it appears to me in Minhagim "and make," etc. — explanation there in Bach otherwise and emendation "besides the pause" — meaning one must pause slightly between them only not wait for a breath; and so Maharai z"l.
