And therefore the Taz, his words may he rest in peace, siman 592. And what he wrote "up to 9 tremolins"—behold, more than 9 is the measure of a tekiyah, in my humble opinion "up to" and not "up to" included in 9 tremolins is the measure of tekiyah for this view, and that is in teruah-shevarim-tekiyah. And what he wrote in tesha tekios the tekiyah is 18 tremolins per what he emended below in letter 3, 18 tremolins—and it is difficult from what he wrote that in tesha tekios it is forbidden to extend, let it be derived that in teruah-shevarim-tekiyah the tekiyah measure is 9 tremolins and the Mechaber does not hold as the Gra, but rather he says it is preferable not to distinguish between fragments of tesha tekios and fragments of tesha tekios; and there is practical difference for the ruling as he concludes below. And what he wrote that it is difficult for him—what did we gain—that is, it is difficult for him both relative to the Mechaber and relative to the Rosh, for behold the dispute in the Mechaber between view (1) and "some say" is in babbling: "some say" teruah is three small kolos of three tremolins and its tekiyah likewise is the measure of three tremolins, and "some say" teruah is nine tremolins and its tekiyah is nine tremolins—behold in groans, in my humble opinion all agree they are equal between view (1) and view (2) (printing error: the word "there is" and it should read "there is not") equal groans to wails, and likewise the tanna of the Mishnah teaches wails and the baraita groans—see there; if so, although the Mechaber does not hold as the Gra, nevertheless how did he write "and therefore one may extend in fragments"—after all one cannot extend in one fragment more than the measure of teruah. And the Rosh—why did he not bring "some say" after the Mechaber's words, which are more lenient than the Mechaber, for the Mechaber may not extend in one fragment more than the measure of teruah, and for the Gra it is permitted to extend in one fragment one groan of almost the measure of three groans, for in tesha tekios the tekiyah measure is as the measure of three groans. And he concludes that thus it is reasonable as written in Tehilla l'David siman 141 and as the Rosh wrote and as written in letter 2. Also the Mechaber should have mentioned: therefore one must extend somewhat in one fragment more than three tremolins for this view, and the Taz concludes for practice that one should not shorten the fragment so much, and in any case one should not extend the fragment except by the measure of three groans, otherwise it will be as its tekiyah—for after the fact we follow—and likewise in tesha tekios one should not extend in one fragment except by the measure of three groans, although there tekiyah is 18 tremolins, so as not to distinguish among fragments; and although if we say as Rabbeinu Hai that it exited in whatever amount, nevertheless if so he would blow teruah-shevarim-tekiyah and 18 would exit in this, and in tesha tekios it is fit to extend somewhat, etc. Nevertheless the Taz's language may he rest in peace still requires study; see Rabbi Akiva Eiger and Eshel Avraham see there.
