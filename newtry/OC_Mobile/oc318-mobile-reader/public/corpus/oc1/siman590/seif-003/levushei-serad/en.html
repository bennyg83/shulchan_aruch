Taz s.k. 1. In the Gemara, Rabbi Avahu was uncertain about this. This is difficult: from where would one derive to say so? In the Mishnah we learned that the measure of a teruah is like three yevavos, while the baraisa says it is like three shevarim, meaning groans, which are longer than yevavos; and we set them against one another. This is how the text should read.
