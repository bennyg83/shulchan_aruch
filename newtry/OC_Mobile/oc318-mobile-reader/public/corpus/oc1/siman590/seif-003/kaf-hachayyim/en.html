9) [Seif 3] Some say that the duration of a tekiah is equal to that of a teruah. This follows the explanation of Rashi and the other poskim, unlike the Rambam, chapter 3, who holds that the duration of a tekiah is half the duration of a teruah. Ateres Zekeinim. Regarding the Rambam's reasoning, see the Beit Yosef in the name of the Ran and Maggid Mishneh, see there.
