(There seif 8) If he blew after teruah a tekiah like one unawares, etc. It appears to me: see in Magen Avraham who brought Ran's view that unawares is not interruption — see there. Proof for his words from explicit mishnah in Tractate Parah chapter 4 mishnah 2: if one sprinkled from sixth to seventh and returned and sprinkled seventh to eighth — invalid from sixth to seventh, for we require seven times in order; but from seventh to eighth valid, for there is no eighth in sprinkling of the cow — this is mere speech error and returned and sprinkled eighth not precise — in any case valid whether he returned and sprinkled or not, etc. All who see how strained it is to say the mishnah when it says "returned and sprinkled eighth" is all superfluous and not precise — also what it says "or if not" is strained to explain there. But it appears it says precisely: just as with the cow we require intent for its sake — if he did not intend for its sake it is invalid per Tanna Kamma there and we hold thus — likewise we require intent in sprinklings for which sprinkling he sprinkles; if he did not intend for its sake but for another sprinkling — in any case per the explicit mishnah "if he sprinkled from sixth to seventh" — in any case if it counts for sixth, behold it is without intent since he intended for seventh; if it counts for seventh and afterward he sprinkles sixth — it is out of order and invalid; but it is explicit in Pesachim regarding a sin-offering slaughtered for a burnt-offering — invalid for burnt-offering, for its kind destroys it, not its kind does not destroy it — and it appears thus: nevertheless it does not count for obligation and is not worse than all other kodashim valid not for their sake — nevertheless they do not count for owners for obligation — for obligation requires specifically for its sake; if so here too — chullin is not its kind and does not disqualify, not its kind does not destroy it — nevertheless for its sake it is not valid and does not count for obligation; therefore here too when intending seventh for eighth — since there is no eighth in the cow it is like sin-offering for chullin and is not its kind and does not destroy — nevertheless that sprinkling did not count since he did not intend it for seventh — therefore another sprinkling eighth for seventh is needed; if there are seven sprinklings and interruption is not in the middle — since this is like unawares and not for the mitzva since there is no eighth in the cow — if so it is only like unawares and unawares does not make interruption in the middle — therefore specifically he must sprinkle eighth intending for seventh — literally as the tanna taught at first "returned and sprinkled seventh" — meaning for sixth — then he did not fulfill for he lacks sixth; perforce it discusses he returned seventh for sixth — therefore it teaches even though there is sixth-seventh, nevertheless he did not fulfill for not in order — cannot say it discusses sprinkling seventh for seventh — obvious he does not fulfill for he lacks sixth; perforce he returned seventh for sixth — therefore it teaches. So too the intent as I wrote "returned and sprinkled eighth" — meaning for seventh — therefore with this he fulfills from necessity as above — understand; mishnah language is correct. From this it is proven even in what requires order — out of order applies only to its kind; but all that is not its kind does not make interruption and is considered in order literally — and the law of tekiyot is exactly like these sprinklings — and understand; correct in my humble opinion in the mishnah's intent — clear proof for this law — examine well: meaning on the day — even on Shabbat — meaning even if Shabbat preceded the first day and it rests — even on Shabbat refers to the preceding — for even if Shabbat was before, nevertheless it is called the fifteenth day first for counting seasons and is counted only from day fifteen — so it appears to explain per aggadah; and it comes out well — examine.
