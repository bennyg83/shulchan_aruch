(S.k. 3) Twelve, etc. The text should read eighteen, etc., for we make tashrat because perhaps the teruah of the verse is both of them together. If so, the tekiah also must be the measure of both together, and they are eighteen tromitin, for according to the 'some say,' meaning Riva and Rivam, three yevavos are nine tromitin, and three shevarim are likewise nine tromitin, as above.
