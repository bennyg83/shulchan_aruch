5) It is stated in the Midrash that Yitzchak was bound upon the altar on Rosh Hashanah. On that day Sarah our mother heard, and she cried out, sobbed, and wailed. Therefore, Scripture says, "It shall be a day of teruah for you," which is translated as "a day of wailing," so that the Holy One, blessed is He, will remember for us the wailing of Sarah our mother and forgive us. The author of Sefer HaMinhagos wrote that a person must wail before his Creator over his sins; therefore, the Torah commanded us to sound the shofar in a manner resembling wailing and groaning. Abudraham, 100a; Eliyah Rabbah, os 1. See above, siman 584, os 16.
