(8) And you ask why all these — is not tashrat three times enough, then shetar tashat tashat? Initials: terat terat — if it were only moaning and wailing one would fulfill with the acronym of tashrat; and if only moaning alone, the last tekiah of tashrat would count for the plain one before tashat — and you would say the same for terat. Ran already answered in Tosafot's name: lest people err — if you say in the first tashat one need not return and blow, they might err to think one need only do shetar. Rosh answered: perhaps since he made the tekiah as a plain one after teruah they did not want it to count for the plain before teruah. Ra'am answered: he holds like R' Zeira who said mitzvot require intent — then one cannot consider the last tekiah of tashrat for the first tekiah of tashat nor the last of tashat for the first of terat — and stipulation does not apply here, for then there is intent neither for the last nor the first tekiah and one does not fulfill either — end of his words. His words are not pleasing to me, for with stipulation it is properly called intent — although because of doubt one stipulates, nevertheless he intends to fulfill eighteen tekiot. Beit Yosef.
