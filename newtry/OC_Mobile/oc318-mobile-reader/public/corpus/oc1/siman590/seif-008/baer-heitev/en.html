And he interrupted. But if everything was done in one tekiah, even if he began to sound a teruah, it is valid, as written in seif 7. The same applies if he began to blow and his sound did not come out well, and he began to blow a second time: it is all considered one tekiah. Know that all this, even in tashrat, is specifically when he sounded the teruah within the shevarim, for it is similar to the teruah he must blow. This is unlike where he makes the teruah before the shevarim, or two teruos one after the other, where he has not fulfilled his obligation. The same applies if he heard in between something he did not need, for that is an interruption; see siman 588, seif 2. If he erred in the blasts after the prayer, it is nothing; only with the sitting and standing blasts is the law so. See Taz and Magen Avraham.
