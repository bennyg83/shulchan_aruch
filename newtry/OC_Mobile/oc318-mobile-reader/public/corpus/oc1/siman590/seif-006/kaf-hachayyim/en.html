We are uncertain regarding the teruah stated in the Torah, etc. In the Zohar, Parashas Pinchas, 231b, it is written as follows: "For this reason, those Babylonians do not know the secret of sobbing and wailing, and they do not know that both are required. The wailing is severe judgment. The three broken sounds are softened judgment. Groaning is a softened groan. They do not know, and they perform both; we know, and we perform both, and all emerge upon the path of truth," see there. Likewise, Shaar HaKavanos gives a mystical reason for all 100 sounds that are sounded on Rosh Hashanah, and we cited his words above, siman 585, os 28, see there. What it says, that wailing is severe judgment and groaning is softened judgment, means that they serve to sweeten both the severe and softened judgments, for judgment can be sweetened only at its root, as is known to those who know the hidden wisdom.
