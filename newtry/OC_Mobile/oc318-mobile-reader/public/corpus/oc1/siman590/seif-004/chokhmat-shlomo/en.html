Seif 4: But three shevarim and teruah — the sequence tesharat — need not be done in one breath, etc. This is the view of Rabbeinu Tam; and the reason poskim wrote in his name: groaning and wailing cannot be done in one breath. At first glance we do not know from where Rabbeinu Tam derives this from his own reasoning before R' Eliezer's doubt in the Gemara — if so, even for R' Eliezer who permits by reasoning alone, groaning and wailing are different and the Gemara only challenged R' Yehuda.
