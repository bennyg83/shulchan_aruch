Three shevarim must be made, etc. If he did not do so, they make him repeat it, and so is the view of all the later authorities, and so is the custom [Rivash]. There is one who wrote that he does not fulfill his obligation if he made them in one breath [Terumas HaDeshen, siman 142]. Even according to those who say to make shevarim-teruah in one breath, he should not blow the shevarim-teruah in one force without any interruption, for in such a case it is not called one breath; rather, he should pause slightly, only not take a breath in between [Terumas HaDeshen and Beis Yosef].
