(S.k. 7) A shever, etc. It appears from his words that it need not be in one breath, meaning the three shevarim. For if he now makes one more shever, he has interrupted between the two shevarim and the third with the fact that he sounded a bit of teruah between them. This follows what was written above in seif 4.
Even in the sitting blasts, etc. That is to say, the Beis Yosef, who copied this ruling that he should blow another shever, even though he ruled in seif 4 that one must make three shevarim in one breath, must necessarily hold that after the fact we rely on this opinion that he should blow another shever. Therefore he must hold that one need not make the three shevarim in one breath. This is as Magen Avraham wrote, and on this Magen Avraham wrote: even in the sitting blasts, etc.; even so, after the fact we rely on the opinion that one need not make them in one breath.
