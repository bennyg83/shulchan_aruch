The 'some say' hold that since the beginning of the tekiah is for the order tashrat and he blows for the order tashat, one of them is true, either tashrat or tashat, etc.; therefore he loses the tekiah entirely. This is unlike where he stipulated that this tekiah should be for whichever order is true, in which case it is certainly considered one, as the Rav wrote in the gloss: eighteen forces for tashrat, and he should blow a new tekiah for the order tashat. With Hashem's help, in Magen Avraham, letters 5 and 7, this will be explained. See Levush and Eliyah Rabbah, letter 12.
