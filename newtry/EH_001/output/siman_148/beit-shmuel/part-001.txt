(ד) <b>A decree lest they say, etc.</b> Meaning that people will say he gave the get on the day it was written, and afterward she conceived from him, creating a blemish for the child. We do not say that there is no blemish for the child because if he had relations with her he certainly had relations for kiddushin, as written in the next siman, because sometimes she marries another; if so, it is necessarily clear that he did not betroth her. Perishah. According to the Yerushalmi brought by Tosafot and Ran in chapter HaZorek, it is explained that if he divorced her because of a matter of ervah, we are not concerned lest he had relations with her, because she is repulsive in his eyes. With this one can answer Tosafot's difficulty on folio 81: Beit Shammai hold that one who divorces his wife and lodges with her at an inn, we are not concerned for kiddushin, because Beit Shammai hold that a person makes his intercourse promiscuous; yet in Yevamot Beit Shammai hold that a person does not make his intercourse promiscuous.
