**** ENGLISH ****
(ב) <b>He collects.</b> Baal Panim Chadashot wrote in the name of She'erit Yosef, siman 37: one who wrote his daughter a half-male-share document, and before his death made a large ketubah for his wife, and his sons-in-law claim that their document comes first for collection, they must first pay the ketubah, and afterward they take by the half-male-share document. In Responsa Rama, siman 109, he concluded: although one whose date is earlier collects first, if the creditor collected through gentile courts and the woman could not object and seize from the creditor, then if the creditor sold the land to another Jew, she may not seize from them. See there.

**** ENGLISH ****
(א) <b>Agav karka.</b> See Choshen Mishpat siman 113. Regarding an earlier oral loan and a later ketubah in which movables were written as collected agav karka, there is a dispute between Rashdam and Rashach. Rashdam, Choshen Mishpat siman 210, wrote that if the creditor of the oral loan seized on his own, he has acquired it. Rashach, part 1, siman 35, wrote that we remove it from his hand and give it to the widow. See Knesset HaGedolah, Choshen Mishpat siman 104, and the Shach there.
