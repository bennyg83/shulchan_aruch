**** ENGLISH ****
<b>One who forbids his wife from several matters. It contains 12 seifim.</b>

If he forbade her not to adorn herself and hung it on marital relations — saying konam the enjoyment of your marital relations is upon me if you adorn yourself — she adorns herself immediately and is forbidden in marital relations; and he waits seven days and then divorces and gives ketubah. Some say if he forbade her not to adorn herself — in poverty one year he upholds; more than that he annuls his vow or divorces and gives ketubah; and in wealth thirty days he upholds, more than that he annuls his vow or divorces and gives ketubah.

If the husband forbade his neighbor's utensils upon him or his utensils upon his neighbors so she not lend or borrow from them; or she said konam your marital relations upon me if I borrow utensils from my neighbors or lend mine — and he heard and upheld for her — he divorces immediately and gives ketubah.
