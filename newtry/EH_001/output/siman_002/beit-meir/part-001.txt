In Tur "but a kohen," etc. — Beit Meir explains: need not distance — meaning one who knows from mixture need not reveal except for modesty; but one who knows from mixture is forbidden to marry from them, per his words siman 7. I question the language "but a kohen shall not marry" — implies one who wants to marry; why specify chalal became mixed when safek applies? If on one who marries — fine; see Beit Shmuel s.k. 16. If on the revealer — Ran and all commentators hold even definite mixture unknown who — also need not reveal, as Eliyahu will not reveal. Perhaps for all this Beit Shmuel expanded explaining Tur.

**** ENGLISH ****
Presumption of fitness — Beit Shmuel wrote in Rama's name, etc., we are concerned lest mamzer or slave; Tur mentions only slave concern. Perhaps he holds no concern for mamzer since Israelites recognize mamzerim among them — unlike slave. He proved from Gemara difficulty Tosafot 28b "perhaps slave kohen" — see Choshen Mishpat siman 3. Therefore must bring proof his mother is Israelite — thereby exits slave and gentile concern. On his father truly no proof needed — no concern for mamzer. Yevamot 45 — go reveal — they check only mother per Tosafot there 47 "in presumption." So our custom not checking at all per Rashi Yevamot 47a — need proof lest Gibeonite circumcised; implies otherwise word would be conversion; Gibeonites not found today; Ishmaelites not uncircumcised; circumcised slaves also not found per Choshen Mishpat s.k. cited — therefore not concerned.

**** ENGLISH ****
Further Beit Yosef and Bach dispute their words. He explained: Tosafot in name of R' Tam — like Tosafot 24 "to serve on altar needs proof" — meaning he himself is not trusted to qualify daughter to kohen even if he marries a checked woman — implies daughter forbidden to every kohen from chalal concern. But Rosh, Tur, Beit Yosef copied R' Tam's language: his daughter is not kosher to kohen serving on altar — implies plain kohen need not beware chalal concern and his daughter chalal is forbidden to him. Beit Yosef and Bach disagree explaining Tur. Therefore cannot explain what Maggid wrote letter 2 — Rambam holds even to marry kohen we are concerned lest chalal; not only not implied but opposite is explicit — some commentators wrote he is not trusted for lineage: every kohen who checks (per view — to serve on altar) does not marry his daughter — clear as he concludes; but if he does not want to check (plain kohen not wanting to check in order to serve) — he marries his daughter. So too checked attributed family — they do not check him for that reason: daughter not checked for attributed matters — implies; but plain kehunah prohibition concern — daughter kosher. Nevertheless his difficulty is correct regarding Bach's understanding of Beit Yosef per Tur per Rambam; main thing per my letter 2.
