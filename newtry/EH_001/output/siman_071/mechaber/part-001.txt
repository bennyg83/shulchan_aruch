**** ENGLISH ****
<b>He is obligated to feed his sons and daughters when they are small. It contains 4 seifim.</b>

A man must feed his sons and daughters until they are six years old, even if they have property that fell to them from their mother's father's house; from then on he feeds them per the Sages' enactment until they grow up. If he does not wish, they rebuke him, shame him, and pressure him. If he still does not wish they proclaim about him in public and say so-and-so is cruel and does not wish to feed his children — behold he is worse than an impure bird which feeds its young — but they do not compel him to feed them. When does this apply? When he is not assessed as wealthy; but if he was assessed as having money suitable for charity that suffices for them — they take from him against his will by reason of charity and feed them until they grow up.

{Rama: And this is specifically regarding daughters' sustenance; but they do not compel him to marry off his daughters; and even though there is a mitzvah to give one's daughter a fitting dowry — nevertheless we do not compel him; only what he wishes he gives, provided he marries them off (Hagahot Mordechai on Kiddushin and so too TaZ Netiv 22).}

**** ENGLISH ****
One who went overseas and left his children here — they sell from his property for sustenance until they are six years old; but from six and above they do not feed them from his property.

{Rama: Some say this is specifically when he had not begun feeding them after six; but if he had begun feeding them they are sustained from his property, for certainly he prefers this (Mordechai chapter HaNa'arah in name of R' Yitzchak). Some say if he is assessed as wealthy they feed them in every matter by reason of charity (so implies in Tur).}
