{Rama: If she said first "betroth me" and he threw kiddushin into her lap and said "behold you are betrothed to me" and she shook her garments within the time of an utterance to cast the kiddushin from her and says she did not intend from the start except to jest — nevertheless she is betrothed (responsum Maharam end Laws of Women). **** ENGLISH ****
One who betroths without witnesses, even with one witness — they are not kiddushin; even if both admit; even if he betrothed before one witness and afterward betrothed before another witness not in each other's presence — she is not betrothed.

{Rama: Some are stringent if he betrothed before one witness (Tur in name of Semag). If both admit — but if one contradicts the witness there is no concern (so Rashbetz explains Semag).
