**** ENGLISH ****
And husband unspecified. I already wrote previous siman these laws; there are two reasons: first reason — he betroths at time of bringing in — therefore requires witnesses of seclusion; second reason — he forgives the condition and first kiddushin take effect and other kiddushin not needed and witnesses of seclusion not needed. Rambam holds second reason; Tur holds first reason and requires witnesses of seclusion. Below explained: if he did not stipulate and betrothed and vows or blemishes found; likewise if betrothed and married without specification — she needs get from doubt and has no ketubah; if he stayed with her she has ketubah. If he said to her: since first ketubah is void how can she collect this ketubah? Say his intent: if he will not be particular afterward her ketubah stands as previous siman. Likewise if stayed with her afterward — certainly betrothed and certainly needs get — from reason when he stipulated nothing it is doubt whether he is particular; if stayed afterward it is clarified he is not particular — certainly betrothed.
