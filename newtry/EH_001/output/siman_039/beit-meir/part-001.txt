**** ENGLISH ****
Beit Shmuel wrote — see Shitah Mekubetzet, etc. Per Rashi I wanted to say Shitah Mekubetzet's view: why we are doubtful like Rava — automatically for us who hold "he gave a finger between them" even non-important woman there is doubt; for Rava is challenged: in important woman who says "I do not want a vowed woman" — known this language only in vows of affliction; see Maharshal who explained main reason we are concerned she will vow under him — he proved well and Gemara challenges — not like Perishah. If so what if she vows further — he can annul; must be concern she will vow and from anger he will make her deaf and be forced to divorce and he does not want prohibition of relatives. Difficult: Scripture teaches per this even non-important can claim he does not want to give ketubah — therefore like forced to establish in one tanna neither R' Meir nor R' Eliezer; also forced to establish in one opinion that holds "she gave" — we establish in two reasons: one in important woman, one in non-important. Found per Rava's reasoning for us who hold "he gave a finger" — even non-important are doubtful. However this is not so — if so for all challenged even non-important; even if he gives — nevertheless can claim he does not want betrothal because of feast he toils and loses — nissuin feast. Or Rava's reasoning to void kiddushin is only in important woman because of loss of relatives, not because of monetary loss — therefore simpler as Beit Shmuel explained.
