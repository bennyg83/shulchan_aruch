**** ENGLISH ****
<b>One who betroths a woman on condition she has no vows or blemishes, and who must bring proof if they are found. It contains 7 seifim.</b>

One who betroths a woman on condition she has no vows, and one of these three vows is found on her — which people are accustomed to be particular about: that she not eat meat, or not drink wine, or not adorn herself with colored cosmetics — she is not betrothed (and does not need a get) (Tur). The same applies to vows between them and her, which are included in vows people are particular about; if a vow besides these is found on her, even if he says "I am particular even about this" — she is betrothed. If he said to her on condition you have no vow at all, even if it is found she vowed not to eat carobs — she is not betrothed. Some say we are concerned lest she go to a sage and he permit it for her (and therefore she is forbidden to marry without a get).

**** ENGLISH ****
What are the disqualifying blemishes in women? All blemishes disqualifying kohanim disqualify women, and in addition for women: bad odor, sweat, and mouth odor (and some say also nose odor) (Mordechai in name of RaMaH); thick voice; breasts coarse compared to her peers by a handbreadth; and a handbreadth between breast and breast; dog bite where the place became a scar (meaning the wound healed and skin formed over it and only the wound's mark remained); and a mole (meaning a wart in Old French — Rashi) on the forehead, even if very small and even if there is no hair — this is the mole that permits a woman to kohanim, which stands under the cap on her head: sometimes visible, sometimes not. If always visible — "he saw and was appeased." If always not visible — it is not a blemish.

{Rama: If there is a bathhouse in the city, even hidden blemishes do not void. When do these words apply? When daughters' way is to go to the bathhouse openly; but where daughters go to bathe only at night and hidden — even exposed blemishes void. Epilepsy: if for a fixed time it is a hidden blemish; if not for a fixed time it is an exposed blemish (and see below siman 116 more laws of blemishes). Some say epilepsy and mouth odor — no distinction whether there is a bathhouse in the city or not (R' Yehudah Netiv 23).}
