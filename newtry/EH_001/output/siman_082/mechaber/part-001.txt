A divorced woman — they do not compel her to nurse her son unless she wishes; he gives her wages and she nurses. If she does not wish she gives him the son and he cares for him. When does this apply? When she did not nurse him until he recognized her; but if he recognized her (and does not wish to nurse from another) (Tur) — even if he is blind they do not separate her because of danger to the child but compel her and she nurses until twenty-four months.

{Rama: And he gives her nursing wages (Tur). Some say even another woman who nursed a child until he recognizes her — they compel her and she nurses him for wages because of danger to the child (Hagahot Alfasi ch. Even Af). Some say that which a divorced woman is not obligated to nurse when he does not recognize her — is when he finds another wet nurse and he can hire; but if he cannot — they compel her and she nurses (R"Y). But if she already hired herself to others and that child recognizes her — they do not reject that child because of her son but beit din hires another wet nurse for her son (Rosh klal 17).}
