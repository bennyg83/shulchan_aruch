**** ENGLISH ****
(ג) <b>All this does not speak, etc.</b> Rashba, Ran, and Maggid Mishneh brought the above dispute of Rabbenu Tam and Ri, and wrote that according to Ri it speaks where she closed her hand with her husband's consent; then she is divorced. This implies that according to Rabbenu Tam, even if she closed her hands with her husband's consent, it is not considered giving. But in Darkei Moshe he wrote this to resolve what the Tur wrote, that if she closed her hands it is not giving, according to Rabbenu Tam's approach, while at the beginning of the siman he wrote that if he holds the get at one end and extends it to her and she takes the other end, it is giving. He distinguishes between where she took it or closed her hand with her husband's consent, when it is giving. This is not compelling, for one can say that what he wrote at the beginning of the siman speaks where, once she holds the other end, he lets go immediately and removes his hand. So too Bach wrote. It follows that this law divides into two cases, namely where both are holding the get, and where she takes it entirely.
