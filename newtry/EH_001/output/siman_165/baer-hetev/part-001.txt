**** ENGLISH ****
(ב) <b>For free.</b> If afterward he says that he wishes to perform yibbum with her, they do not compel him to perform chalitzah, because perhaps the halachah is that the mitzvah of yibbum takes precedence, and it is like a stipulation against what is written in the Torah. Bach; Beit Shmuel. In Responsa Maharshdam, Even HaEzer siman 71, and Responsa R. Aharon Sasson, siman 28, it is stated that nevertheless they may apply the distancing measures of Rabbenu Tam: not to speak with him and not to conduct business with him. But they should not say not to speak with him until she performs chalitzah, for then it would be coercion; rather, they should make a general distancing, not to speak with him. If he gave a debt document and did not write that he will perform chalitzah for free, then one may say that the document itself indicates that he cannot say he wishes to perform yibbum. But regarding chalitzah that he will give, his intent is like the communal enactments; therefore one must write that he will perform chalitzah for free.
