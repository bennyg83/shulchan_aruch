One who has no wife shall not teach children because mothers of boys come to schoolhouse for their sons and he thereby incites with women; and likewise woman shall not teach minors because their fathers come on account of sons and become secluded with her; and teacher need not have wife dwelling with him in schoolhouse — rather she in her house and he teaches in his place.
