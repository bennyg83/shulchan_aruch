**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Which woman is called a katlanit ("fatal woman"). It contains 2 seifim.</b>

A woman who married (or became betrothed — Hagah Maimoniyot and Nekudat HaKesef, chapter HaBa al Yevimto) two men and they died — she may not marry a third, for she has already been presumed that her husbands die. If she married — she does not leave; even if only betrothed, he may consummate the marriage. If he recognized her — she has a ketubah; if he did not recognize her — she has no ketubah from the third. But from the second she has a ketubah even if he did not recognize her.

{Rama: Some say specifically if they died natural deaths; but if one of them was killed, or died from plague, or fell and died and the like — it is nothing. Therefore many are lenient in these matters and we do not protest (Beit Yosef in the name of responsa of the Ramban in She'eilot uTeshuvot). Some say the same applies to a woman divorced twice — she may not be married (Rashi chapter 10 of Yevamot; Ran beginning of Ne'arah). Some say death specifically (Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of Tosafot) — and so is the primary view.}
