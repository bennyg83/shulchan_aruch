**** ENGLISH ****
And if she married, etc. As stated beginning of Sotah "and he didn't add," etc. — since he knew, etc. But Rosh's view chapter 4 Ketubot — Tanna holds danger stricter than prohibition; and just as we compel regarding prohibition as stated end of HaMedir, etc.; proof from what stated there regarding mochin shechin — we don't leave him because of his pain; and Yevamot ch. 4 regarding pregnant and nursing — even per Chachamim he divorces with get even for kohen; we say there because she will become thin or her milk will spoil — we compel. "And if she married," etc., even, etc. Rishonim in name of Ritva: we compel to remove — beit din are warned regarding this, danger stricter than prohibition. But Rambam learned from what Tosafot stated: they told daughter of R' Chisda "you are fatal to him three" and you came, etc.; if so how can she — we don't leave, etc. See responsa Rivash at length in Likut.
