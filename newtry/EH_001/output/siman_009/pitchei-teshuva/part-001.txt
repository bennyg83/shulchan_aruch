<b>אשה שנשאת. </b> כ' בר"י אות ג' יש לחקור אם שתי אחיות מת בעליהן הראשונים אי נחשבו קטלניות ושוב כל חדא מנייהו לא תנשא אף לשני והאחות השלישי לא תנשא גם לראשון כי היכא דקי"ל ביו"ד סימן רס"ג לענין מילה דאחיות מחזיקות. ועלה במחשבתי דלמ"ד מעין גורם יש צד לומר כן כמו לעיל סי' ב' במשפחת נכפין וגבי מילה ביו"ד שם אבל למ"ד מזל גורם פשיטא דלא אמרינן בזה אחיות מחזיקות והא דלא אמר בפ' הבע"י דאיכא בינייהו הא כבר כתבו התוס' ב"ב דף ה' דבכמה דוכתי לא חייש לומר אלא חדא או תרי מילי ע"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
A woman who married. Brit R' Yehuda letter 3 wrote: investigate if two sisters whose first husbands died — were they considered katlanit; again each alone may not marry even a second; and third sister may not marry even the first — since we hold Yoreh De'ah 263 regarding circumcision that sisters establish. It occurred to me: per ayin causes there is room to say so, as above siman 2 regarding family of epileptics and regarding circumcision there; but per mazal causes obviously we don't say sisters establish. That Gemara HaBa al Yevimto didn't say between them — Tosafot Bava Batra 5 already wrote in many places we don't concern to say except one or two matters — see there.

Or became betrothed. Avnei Heitev what he wrote; Rosh explains mazalah garam — livelihood, etc. See responsa Noda BiYehuda siman 9 who found one leniency based on this: woman who is capable and succeeds in commerce even after husband's death — permitted per we hold mazal causes, for her mazal doesn't cause poverty; nevertheless he didn't resolve to permit explicitly — but one who wants to act (and rely on Rambam Beit Shmuel s.k. 2) has some peg to hang on and remove fear from heart — see there. See responsa Teshuvah MeAhavah vol. 1 siman 92 who quoted Rambam's words at length from responsum Pe'er HaDor and wrote implication: specifically if married first and second; but if married three men and died — your question — even Rambam agrees, etc. However after you wrote one husband died old and one she married against doctors' advice who ruled he'd die if he married — not so clear this grieving woman is presumed katlanit; similar to what they said regarding collapse — healthy not weak; likewise young men not old; healthy men not sick. Also combine what my teacher wrote in Noda BiYehuda to find leniency, etc. What he wrote if one was old not so clear — so Beit Shmuel s.k. 6 brought in Avnei Heitev s.k. 7; see Brit R' Yehuda letter 10 who wrote in name of Mahari Weil siman 183: if one old — not counted; wrote on this there is support from Yerushalmi ch. 3 Ta'anit "houses they said healthy," etc. Later I saw responsa Mahari Malko siman 47 brought cited proof; but for law he and Rav Avraham Tzalach agreed opposite. Brit R' Yehuda further wrote: possibly even per Mahari Weil if old very advanced — killed alone or fell and died — per we hold mazal causes this old man from the count, since killed and didn't die in bed even until old age she is katlanit — so he concludes. But Rama already wrote in Hagah below many are lenient per ayin causes and killed is nothing. However where her sister also died — first husband — room to say necessarily she is katlanit — see above s.k. 1. See Ikarei Dinim Yoreh De'ah siman 14 letter 8 on this. See responsa Kehillat Yaakov from Gaon of Karlin siman 4 who also expanded; in his case she had only two men. He expanded proofs primary halacha is Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel everywhere — only general stringency they were stringent to rule marriage like Rabbi; wrote therefore we can rely wherever there are reasons to be lenient since poskim testified many and great were lenient nowadays; poskim already wrote regarding marrying minor forbidden by Gemara — now practice to be lenient because hard to find proper match (above siman 37 seif 8 Hagah) — say here especially woman who stayed with first husbands long time and bore sons and daughters who grew and succeeded — attribute husbands' deaths not to her mazal or ayin but other reason. Further in this case proper basis to be lenient from Noda BiYehuda permitting wealthy successful woman, etc.; combined with report first husband wasn't healthy since arriving here and first likewise — known what they sided to leniency from Gemara regarding collapse, etc. Therefore per my view don't be stringent; good soul may rely; his words imply for three men don't rely on any reason to be lenient. See responsa Galia Mesechet siman 11 at length. See responsa Chatam Sofer siman 23 — clear there even per Rambam if married doesn't leave — one who married without asking sages and here didn't insist — no danger per Rambam; but one who comes to ask — then insisted and they ruled she may not marry and afterward he married her — even if married she leaves since insisted — danger stricter. He further wrote what Hagahot Maimoniyot and Beit Shmuel agreed: if one was old — attribute to old age; this old I say bar seventy not bar sixty — Abaye also lived sixty years as stated Rosh Hashanah 18a; how say Rava's wife to Abaye's widow "fatal three" as stated Ketubot 65 — nevertheless old age for this matter only already seventy — see there. There siman 24 regarding woman who stayed with first husband twenty years and he died in old age; married second known sick from pain since then and died; married third very old and died; now sister died and her husband wants to marry her saying not afraid at all per Rambam; but Mahari Weil wrote ten men — even Rambam agrees; for Rosh compel to remove — all the more not to consummate; but asker wrote she's suspected and mitzvah to marry her and she supports herself from handiwork; Noda BiYehuda wrote to permit, etc.; he answered Noda BiYehuda's words don't sound to me — don't hold against Gemara against reasoning, etc.; but seems some leniency: in Beit Yosef brings Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote we overlook because sometimes they're young and concern for mishap — intent seems he holds like Ranach in yibbum don't concern katlanit because guardian of mitzvah, etc.; if so also young where concern for mishap — this one marrying is guardian of mitzvah; if so for general concern because she's young — all the more here truly suspected — good to save from sin. Also combine she was with first husband twenty years supported by him died normally old seventy; afterward only established twice; Rambam and Rivash permit; even for disputants say both weren't such that one wasn't fit to die from himself, etc. Concludes: here are sides to permit so if fourth wants to rely we don't protest; but won't decide to permit publicly — only one who wants to act for himself we don't block — see there.
