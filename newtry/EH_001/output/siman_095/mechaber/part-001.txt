**** ENGLISH ****
A widow sustained by orphans who left land and she enhanced it — some say all enhancement is theirs and even labor wages she does not take. And some say enhancement is for herself. And some say she takes labor wages but her hand is on the lower portion. And if she is not sustained from them and her husband left scant property insufficient for her ketubah — some say if she said see what my husband left me and I will enhance for myself and they were lax in collecting her ketubah — even if the property was enhanced a thousand zuz the enhancement is for herself. But if she enhanced the property without specification — she takes her ketubah and the remainder goes to heirs. And some say whether she said see or did not say — all enhancement goes to heirs and she does not even collect her ketubah from it except she takes labor wages and her hand is on the lower portion.

{Rama: And this is primary — all enhancement belongs to the orphans and she does not collect ketubah or sustenance from that enhancement (in Mordechai chapter Mi SheMet). Therefore a widow who sat a long time in her husband's house and was sustained from them until certainly the principal her husband left was consumed — even though there is much enhancement she enhanced — she does not collect her ketubah from there (Tur and Mahariu siman 176). And some dispute — rather whatever she took they deduct from enhancement and she collects ketubah from principal and not from enhancement (Maharil siman 46 and see below siman 100).}
