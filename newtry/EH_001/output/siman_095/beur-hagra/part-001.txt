(ב) <b>And she can.</b> As written in Arakhin 22a, "we do not attend to," etc., because of sustenance. Although it adds there "because of favor," that is because of a divorcee, but it is obvious that the reason of sustenance also remains, and all the more according to Rashi, s.v. "that case," etc. Terumat HaDeshen, siman 217; see there. See Rambam, chapter 12 of Hilchot Malveh, halachah 3: therefore if the woman hastened and married and afterward came to claim her ketubah from the orphans' property, we do not attend to her until the orphans grow up, for she has no sustenance and she has married. See Kesef Mishneh there, who wrote in the name of Rashba, etc.; and in my humble view, etc.
