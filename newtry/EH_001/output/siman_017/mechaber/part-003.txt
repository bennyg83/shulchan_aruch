**** ENGLISH ****
A woman whose husband went to another country and they came and told her "your husband died" and she married, and afterward her husband came — whether she married based on one witness or based on two witnesses (even if she was not consummated) (the Rosh and Rivash and Ritva and Nimukei Yosef siman 508) — she must leave from this one and that one and requires a get from both of them and has no ketubah from either of them (even if the first took her back) (Hagahot Alfasi). And not the produce that they ate from her melog property — even though they are not obligated for its depreciation; and specifically when the second ate before the first came. But what the second ate after the first came — he must return. And he is not obligated to give the worn-out value from her iron-flock property, provided they wore out completely. But what remains of them in existence she takes. And if she took ketubah or produce from this one or that one — she must return; and specifically what she took from the second after the first came; but if she took from him before the first came — she need not return. And both of them do not become impure for her if they are kohanim, and they have no rights in her finds and her handiwork and her vow annulment; and she is disqualified from kehunah and from terumah and from maaser if she is a Levite's daughter (but her first husband inherits her if she dies) (Beit Yosef and Maggid Mishneh). If the brothers of this one and the brothers of that one died — they perform chalitzah and do not perform yibum; and the child from the second is a mamzer from the Torah if he fathered her before the first divorced her; but if he did not father except after the first died or divorced — the child from the second is only a mamzer rabbinically. And if the first had relations with her before the second divorced — the child he will father from her is a mamzer rabbinically.

**** ENGLISH ****
If she did not marry the second but was only betrothed and her husband came — she does not need a get from the second and is permitted to return to the first; and likewise she is permitted to the second if the first died or divorced her.

{Rama: A woman who was betrothed and erred and thought she was not betrothed and married another — she must leave from this one and that one and all the above ways apply to her. But if they forced her to marry, or the court instructed her in error and she married on their word — it is as one compelled and she is permitted to her first husband (responsum of Rashba 1088 brought in Beit Yosef).}
