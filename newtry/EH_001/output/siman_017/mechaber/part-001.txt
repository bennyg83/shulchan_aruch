Permitting a married woman whose husband died, and she says "he divorced me," and the laws of forbidden relations and the law of one who married in prohibited circumstances and in error. It contains 58 seifim.

**** ENGLISH ****
They found him killed or dead — if his forehead and nose and the form of his face remain and they recognized him through them that he is so-and-so — they testify about him. And if one of these was removed — even though they have the most prominent signs on his garments they are nothing; for we are concerned for the question — even if they had signs on his body, and even a mole — they do not testify about him. But if they had on his body the most prominent signs — they testify about him.

{Rama: Such as if he had an extra or missing limb or a change in one of his limbs; but short or tall, or pale and ruddy — is not a prominent sign (responsum of the Rosh kelal 51). And even a hundred signs that are not prominent are nothing, and even to combine with other proving estimations is nothing (Piskei Mahari siman 161 and 224). A large growth on his nose, or his nose very crooked, or the like — is a prominent sign; but a slight crookedness — no (there siman 234). And likewise regarding a scar on his body or on one of his limbs; but large teeth — even if very large — is not a prominent sign (siman 161 in Piskei Maharai). And every sign that helps with an Israelite witness — likewise with a Samaritan speaking offhandedly (in the responsum of Maharam in laws of women and Maharam Padua siman 2 and 36).}
