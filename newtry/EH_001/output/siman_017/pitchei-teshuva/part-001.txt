**** ENGLISH ****
In our time when brazenness is common. See Beit Meir who wrote that where there is a convincing excuse proving she speaks truth — even in our time she is believed, as stated siman 154 seif 7 gloss 2 — one law and one reason; see there he wrote to resolve Rabbeinu Yeruham's words in Beit Yosef — he did not contradict; Beit Yosef wondered it seems if he contradicted she is not believed — but this is certainly not so; per Beit Meir R' Yonah also adopts stringency of our time — therefore believed only when excuse joined because he did not contradict; see there.

See responsum Galia Massechet siman 5: a woman lived with her husband in her city several years; they traveled to Russia; after about three years she returned with a gentile wagon-driver to an inn; her husband came — she screamed "why pursue me — you divorced me with invalid get three months ago"; he denied, snatched her box and fled; she says the box contained written proof from Rabbi R' Meir who arranged get at an inn near city P.; her husband wanted to leave Judaism; through intermediaries she obtained invalid get; afterward he regretted and sought to seize her and her daughter — she hid weeks. Inquiry to that city: rumor a wagon was sent after Rabbi R' Meir to village P. to arrange invalid get — unknown if divorce occurred; rabbi deceased; his wife knows he traveled for this but not village, woman, or man. Husband left Judaism among soldiers — whereabouts unknown; woman sits agunah seeking permission to marry — says he certainly divorced her and had written proof; presumed modest — end question.

He answered at length: permitted to marry without doubt. Even one witness. The law whether one witness for woman's testimony is deoraita or only derabbanan (with practical differences throughout this siman) is disputed among early authorities: from Nekudot Yosef ch. Ha'ishah Rabah it appears in name of Ra'ah and Ritva — deoraita — because matter done to be revealed and precision — certainly truth testified — considered full Torah testimony; what Talmud says "due to agunah they were lenient" — Ritva explains rabbis lenient not stringent on Torah. But Tosafot there — believed only derabbanan (see Beit Shmuel below s.k. 109); likewise Rashi Yevamos 92b s.v. teda (and Shabbat 145b — whoever betroths per rabbis, etc. — however per Noda Biyhuda below Rashi there no proof — speaks of witness from witness); likewise Rashba in his rulings 87.
