**** ENGLISH ****
Witnesses who contradict each other regarding kiddushin, or she says "I was betrothed" and retracted. It contains 4 seifim.

Two say we saw she was betrothed and two say we did not see she was betrothed — behold she is betrothed; even if all of them dwell with her in one courtyard — "we did not see" is not evidence, for it is possible she was betrothed in private.

**** ENGLISH ****
Two say she was betrothed and two say she was not betrothed — it is no different than if he threw kiddushin to her: two say closer to him and two say closer to her. It is no different than if two say there was never doubtful kiddushin in her — she may not marry; and if she married she must leave. And if she married one of her witnesses and says it is clear to me I was not betrothed — she need not leave.

**** ENGLISH ****
If she herself said "I was betrothed" and retracted and said "I am unmarried" — within the time of an utterance she is believed; after the time of an utterance she is not believed. And if she gives an excuse for her words that she said so initially so that unworthy men would not pounce on her and the like, and we see substance in her words — behold she is believed. And if she does not give an excuse, or gives one without substance — she is not believed. And if another betrothed her — it is doubtful kiddushin; therefore he gives her a get and she is forbidden to him and to everyone until the betrothed man comes.

{Rama: Some say all this applies when she said "I was betrothed" plainly; but if she said "I was betrothed to so-and-so" — she is no longer believed to say "I am unmarried," for she is not entirely in his liability to release herself from him.}
