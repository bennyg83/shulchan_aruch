(ב) <b>A yavam, etc.</b> Yerushalmi, chapter HaZorek, halachah 11: one who divorces his wife may not live with her, not in the same courtyard and not in the same place. If the courtyard belonged to the woman, the man leaves; if it belonged to the man, the woman leaves; if it belonged to both, who leaves because of whom? The woman leaves because of the man. If they can arrange that this one opens here and this one opens there, in what case was this said? When she remarried; but if she did not remarry, no. And with a kohen, even if she did not remarry. A betrothed woman in Judea is like a married woman. One who performed chalitzah with his yevamah, even though he performed maamar with her, does not leave, because maamar does not acquire a full acquisition. This implies that she is like a betrothed woman, as it says there that she is not like a married woman.
