**** ENGLISH ****
Which divorced woman is permitted to return to her husband. It contains 7 seifim.

One who divorces his wife and afterward she committed adultery — she is permitted to return to her husband. {Rama: So too if she committed adultery with the first and was betrothed to a second and he divorced her — she is permitted to return to the first, even if she was designated to him initially (responsum of the Rosh klal 53 and Maharam of Padua siman 19).} But if she was betrothed to another and he divorced her or died — she is forbidden to return to the first; {and even if she was not actually divorced but only due to a general rumor (the Rosh klal 5) (and see above siman 6 and above siman 46 seif 5 and seif 6).}

**** ENGLISH ****
A deaf man who divorced by hint and she went and was betrothed to another deaf man — and needless to say to a competent person — she is forbidden to return to her deaf husband. But the wife of a competent man who was divorced and went and married a deaf man and was divorced — she is permitted to return to her competent husband. (And see above siman 119 whether it is permitted to dwell with her in a courtyard after he divorced her.)

**** ENGLISH ****
One who divorces his wife because of a bad reputation that spread about her, or because she is a niddah, or because she is an ailonit — he may not return her. And some say specifically when he said to her: "Because of this I am divorcing you," and doubled his words to say: "Were it not for this I would not be divorcing you." But if he did not double his words he may return her. And some say that if he said to her "Because of this I am divorcing you" — even if he did not double his words he may not return her. And there is one who says that even if he did not say to her "Because of this I am divorcing you" — he may not return her.
