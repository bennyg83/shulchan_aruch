**** ENGLISH ****
<b>He must be of sound mind from writing until giving. It contains 8 seifim.</b>

He must be of sound mind when commanded to write it; therefore if a bad spirit seized him when commanded to write it, they do not write it even for when he recovers; and likewise a drunk who reached Lot's drunkenness and said write — they do not write; and if he did not reach that — behold this is a doubt.
