<b>Prohibition of wasting seed and matters that lead to it. It contains 7 seifim.</b>

**** ENGLISH ****

{Rama: In the Gemara chapter Kol HaYad it implies it is forbidden to wear trousers if not made like leggings because it leads to wasting seed; and though one can reject that in the Gemara it speaks only of their days when they had terumah and there is concern for bodily impurity — nevertheless since Rosh brought in his rulings it implies forbidden even nowadays (all this is individual opinion); and what is practiced to permit in the bathhouse — one can say for a short time they did not forbid as above.
