**** ENGLISH ****
Forbidden to emit, etc. — it is stated Yevamot 12: Rav Bibi taught three women may use a mokh: minor, pregnant, nursing, etc. per R' Meir; and sages say one and one, etc.; and Rashi explained: may be intimate — permitted to give mokh, etc.; and Rashba and Nimukei Yosef wrote it is not clear — for if so it implies before this time forbidden; and why — she does not conceive and there is no wasting seed here; rather he says liable because of danger. And the practice there in Tosafot appears this is difficult for Mahari Weil — for regarding explanation of intimacy with mokh at time of intimacy it is not like being intimate with a minor where he is intimate normally; unlike on the mokh where there is wasting seed because there is no manner of intimacy thus and it is like casting seed on wood; and so in Tosafot Ketubot 39a; and it appears for this reason he does not explain like Rabbenu Tam — meaning to wipe after intimacy — for "may use mokh" implies to him like his explanation; and further possible regarding wiping there is concern she will not wipe well; and per his explanation possible he holds for a woman there is no prohibition of wasting seed — the prohibition is only for his pleasure; but in their wording it implies from his explanation that she too is warned. And per his explanation why we hold like Rabbans all forbidden; however Tosafot initially appear to infer from Rashi that she too is warned; and it appears to them thus from chapter Kol HaYad; and afterward wrote: however we find explanation that they are not subject to arousal prohibition — meaning since they are not commanded regarding procreation; and they further wrote Rabbenu Tam says before intimacy certainly forbidden — meaning even the three women; because it can be fixed after intimacy; and this for all women permitted — he is intimate normally and she wipes — she was not warned, etc.; and "may use mokh" that the mishnah teaches means they need; and we derive for law from his explanation that at time of intimacy all forbidden even the three women; and after intimacy all permitted; and why we hold like Rabbans all equal and not liable; and in my view difficulty: if after intimacy permitted for all women — no prohibition neither for his pleasure nor hers; if so what is taught Ketubot 39b "or she fills and pours to the ash heap" and challenged and acts — R' Yosi, R' Shimon: she fills and scatters — mishnah taught, etc.; granted for Shmuel therefore he emits and gives Tosafot; and so we rule Yoreh De'ah siman 232 and Even HaEzer siman 76; difficulty: why — she is not commanded regarding procreation and whatever does not come with a claim what does she care and she acts; and strained to say specifically if she wipes willingly — if so there is no prohibition even for his matter; but in a mitzvah her matter in any case is considered prohibition — therefore he emits because she is doing a prohibition before the blind because of his pleasure; for it implies here where he is intimate normally there is no prohibition even for his matter even willingly; otherwise no need to strain that "may use mokh" means they need; rather permitted willingly; and per Perishah proof from Rashi that she too is warned — we hear from this intimacy with mokh at time of intimacy all forbidden for all women; and if not it depends on dispute of Rashi and Rabbenu Tam; and fitting to be stringent like Rashi; and in my poverty I wonder why poskim omitted this baraita — for all explanations there is practical difference for law; see Chiddushei Rashal Niddah chapter Kol HaYad.
