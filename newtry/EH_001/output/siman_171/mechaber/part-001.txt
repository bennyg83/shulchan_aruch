<b>דין ב' יבמות הרשות קטנות. ובו י סעיפים:</b><br><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="א" data-order="1"></i>מי שהיה נשוי יתומה קטנה <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="1"></i><small> (שהשיאוה אחיה ואמה שאין קדושיה רק <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ב" data-order="2"></i>מדרבנן) </small> וחרשה ומת ונפלו לפני אחיו לייבם <i data-commentator="Beit Shmuel" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="1"></i>אין ביאת אחת מהן פוטרת צרתה <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="2"></i>וכיצד תקנתם <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ג" data-order="3"></i><i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="3"></i>מלמדין הקטנה שתמאן בו וכונס את החרשת <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="4"></i>ואם רצה לגרשה כותב לה גט אחר שיבא עליה ומותרת לזר:
**** ENGLISH ****
A man who was married to an orphan of minor age (note: who was married off by her brother or mother, and whose marriage is valid by Rabbinical decree) and a deaf woman (whose marriage is also only valid by Rabbinical ruling) and he died, and the women have fallen to his brother for possible yibbum, consummation of yibbum with one of these (Rabbinically married) women does not free her co-wife from levirate bonds. What is their resolution? We instruct the minor to reject him (annul the marriage), and the brother marries the deaf woman. If he wants to divorce her, he writes a divorce after consummating the marriage, and she is then permitted to a stranger.
**** END BLOCK ****

<i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="6"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ה" data-order="5"></i>היתה אחת גדולה ואחת קטנה ביאת גדולה או חליצתה פוטרת הקטנה ואין ביאת הקטנה פוטרת את הגדולה:
**** ENGLISH ****
If one wife was of majority age, and the other was a minor, consummation of yibbum or chalitzah (ceremony severing the levirate bond) with the adult wife frees the minor [from levirate bond], but consummation of yibbum with the minor does not free the adult wife.

**** ENGLISH ****
If both [wives] were minors, and the living brother consummated yibbum with one of them, and then he or another brother had sexual relations with the second [minor wife], this did not invalidate the yibbum of the first, but he is forbidden to stay with the second. Therefore we instruct the second to reject (annul) her marriage, and he upholds marriage with the first.

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="מ" data-order="13"></i>קטנה וחרשת שבא היבם תחלה על הקטנה וחזר ובא הוא או אחיו על החרשת <i data-commentator="Beit Shmuel" data-order="4"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="נ" data-order="14"></i><i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="11"></i>לא פסל את הקטנה והחרשת צריכה גט <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="12"></i>שביאת הקטנה מעולה מביאת החרשת שהקטנה ראויה לאחר זמן לפיכך יקיים הקטנה שנבעלה תחלה:
**** ENGLISH ****
A minor girl and a deaf girl, when the levir first has relations with the minor and then he or his brother has relations with the deaf girl, he has not disqualified the minor, and the deaf girl requires a get. For relations with a minor is has greater legal consequences than relations with a deaf girl, for the minor will be fit for marriage after some time. Therefore, he should uphold the minor who had relations first.

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ס" data-order="15"></i>בא על החרשת וחזר ובא הוא או אחיו על הקטנה פסל את החרשת ומלמדין את הקטנה שתמאן בו <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ע" data-order="16"></i>ויוציא החרשת בגט: <small> (זהו דעת הרמב"ם <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="13"></i>אבל אחרים חולקים <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="פ" data-order="17"></i>עליו כמפורש בטור ובנימוקי יוסף פרק בית שמאי)</small>
**** ENGLISH ****
If he had relations with the deaf girl, and then he or his brother had relations with the minor, he has disqualified the deaf girl. And we should instruct the minor to refuse marriage, and then he divorces the deaf girl through a get. This is the opinion of the Rambam, but others disagree with him, as is explicit in the Tur and the Nimukei Yosef, ch. Bet Shammai.
**** END BLOCK ****

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ת" data-order="22"></i>אחת גדולה ואחת קטנה בא על הגדולה וחזר ובא הוא או אחיו על הקטנה לא פסל הגדולה ומלמדין הקטנה שתמאן בו בא על הקטנה וחזר הוא או אחיו ובא על הגדולה מלמדין הקטנה שתמאן בו ויקיים הגדולה:
**** ENGLISH ****
If one was of majority age and one was a minor, and he had relations with the adult, and afterwards he or his brother had relations with the minor, he has not disqualified the adult, and they instruct the minor that she should reject her marriage.
