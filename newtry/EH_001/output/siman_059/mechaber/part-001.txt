**** ENGLISH ****
<b>One who marries off his adult son in a house he bought. It contains 4 seifim.</b>

One who marries off his adult son in a house he bought — he acquires the house — provided the marriage is the first marriage for this son, and the father did not marry off another male child before him, and the father did not leave anything in this house that he set aside for himself.

But if he left anything there, even one jug — he did not acquire the house.

(And some say we also require that he marry him to a virgin, and that the father have another house to dwell in) (Tur).
