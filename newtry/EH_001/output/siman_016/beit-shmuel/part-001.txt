<b>ויש חולקים</b>. פלוגתא זו תליא בפלוגתא שכתבתי ס"ד לרמב"ם קי"ל כדאמר רבא מעיקרא בגיותן איתא חתנות ולא תתחתן קאי בגיותן גם פסק כר"ש דכל אומות בכלל לאו זה וי"א אלו ס"ל כדאמר רבא להמסקנא לא תתחתן קאי בז' אומות בגירותן אבל בגיותן לא שייך חיתון, גם לרמב"ם משמע דלא לקי אלא בבעל דרך אישות ולא בחיתון לבד ולא בביאה לבד, ולשאר פוסקים חייב בז' אומות בביאה בלא חיתון משום לא תקח ובגירותן חייב משום לא תתחתן מיד בעת הקידושין אפילו לא בעל, וע' בדרישה וב"ח:
**** ENGLISH ****
And some dispute. This dispute depends on the dispute I wrote siman 4 regarding Rambam — we hold like Rava initially in their gentile state there is marriage and "you shall not intermarry" refers in their gentile state; also he ruled like R' Shimon that all nations are included in this negative command. And some say: those who hold like Rava to the conclusion — "you shall not intermarry" refers to the seven nations in their conversion, but in their gentile state marriage is inapplicable; also for Rambam it appears he is liable only through marriage in the manner of marriage and not mere betrothal alone nor intercourse alone; and for other poskim he is liable for the seven nations through intercourse without betrothal because of "you shall not take," and in their conversion liable because of "you shall not intermarry" immediately at betrothal even if he did not consummate. And see in Derishah and Beit Chadash.

<b>מן התורה משום זונה</b>. כ"כ הרמב"ם והסמ"ג אבל תו' והרא"ש חולקים ע"ז וס"ל דוקא כשנבעלה אז חייב הכהן משום זונה וב"ד של חשמונאי גזרו אפילו אם לא נבעלה חייב משום זונה ולכאורה משמע פוסקים אלו פליגי על דעת הפוסקים שהבאתי בסי' ו' דס"ל בגיורת אפילו אם לא נבעלה כשהיתה עכו"ם חייב משום זונה שוב ראיתי בתו' פ' הע"י כתבו גיורת שאני דשייך בה קיחה וחתנות לכן אסורה משום זונה אפילו לא נבעלה משא"כ בכותית:
**** ENGLISH ****
From the Torah because of zona. So too Rambam and Semag; but Tosafot and Rosh dispute this and hold specifically when she was lain with then the kohen is liable because of zona; and the court of the Hasmoneans decreed even if she was not lain with he is liable because of zona. And apparently these poskim dispute the view of the poskim I brought siman 6 who hold regarding a convert even if she was not lain with when she was a gentile she is liable because of zona. I further saw in Tosafot Perek HaIsh that they wrote a convert is different because betrothal and marriage apply to her — therefore forbidden because of zona even if she was not lain with; unlike a non-Jewish woman.

**** ENGLISH ****
There is in it a loss that is not in all forbidden relations. Not that its punishment is more severe than other forbidden relations which are death and karet; rather meaning there is loss thereby that he will ultimately cling to idolatry and profane the sanctity of Heaven. And what is stated in Eruvin that idolatry does not remove one — an Aramean woman removes him from Gehinnom — means when he has other sins for which he descends to Gehinnom; then because of this sin it does not remove them from there — so Maharshal and Beit Chadash wrote.

**** ENGLISH ****
They strike him nevertheless. But he is not liable Torah lashes — Maggid wrote Rambam holds he ruled thus: the warning "she shall not be a kedeshah" refers to one who comes on an available Israelite daughter in promiscuity; therefore Mahari Weil holds one who comes on kedeshah Israelite daughter receives lashes; but it does not refer to intercourse with slave and maidservant — not like Targum Onkelos who holds it refers to slave and maidservant intercourse — their kiddushin do not take and all their intercourse is promiscuous intercourse. Tur siman 26 wrote one who comes on available receives lashes for "she shall not be a kedeshah"; this siman wrote in name of Rambam one who comes on maidservant receives lashes only rabbinically. Perishah there challenged siman 26 — Tur's words contradict; he divided and wrote this siman discusses maidservant not prepared for promiscuity; but if prepared for promiscuity or Israelite daughter prepared for promiscuity — lashes for "she shall not be a kedeshah." Not so — for Rambam disputes Targum as stated explicitly in Rambam — then "she shall not be a kedeshah" does not refer to maidservant at all, only Israelite daughter. And one cannot challenge why Beit Yosef unrestrained receives lashes on "she shall not be a kedeshah" and maidservant even unrestrained prepared for promiscuity receives no lashes — Maggid already answered and I brought siman 26; nevertheless bring proof for Targum's view from sugya chapter HaChalitz — gentile and slave who comes on Israelite daughter offspring mamzer — even per view no mamzer in those liable to lashes — gentile and slave are different; learn per view mamzer in those liable to lashes properly offspring mamzer because negative command in slave and gentile — we find no negative command except "do not be kadesh" and "she shall not be a kedeshah"; see Rashi chapter widow to High Priest and Tosafot Bava Batra 13 and chapter HaOmer implies also negative command in gentile and slave — R' Tarfon can purify mamzerim marry maidservant — asked if R' Tarfon lekhatchilah says; Rashi and Ran even though negative command "do not be kadesh"; Rambam can say difficulty for R' Tarfon permits lekhatchilah mamzer marry maidservant — mamzer obligated all commandments how marry maidservant; Rabbenu Tam and Ran answered because mamzer stands kedesh and remains; Rambam proved from this drush of Targum does not apply to slave — no negative command in slave and maidservant only rabbinic prohibition — they said permitted marry maidservant to mamzer because enactment for offspring — hear views Tosafot Rashi and Ran negative command in maidservant and slave per Targum; Rambam no negative command; but per Rambam also negative command in maidservant and slave — holds no lashes because negative command in generality — we derive from this negative command also one who comes on available in prostitution; if so difficulty why lashes one who comes on available; Perishah rejects this reasoning siman 26; great lion of composition Mahar Wolf R' Yehudah Dayan community Fintshov noted to me.

<b>ספק שפחות</b>. אבל אין כופין לאדון לשחרר' דאשה אינה מצווה על פ"ו, ומיהו א"י להשתעבד בהם מספק כ"כ המגיד פ"ה, ואם שתיהן בנותיו כגון שבא על שפחתו וילדה ונתערב עם בתו יש לו בהן זכות מה שיש לאב בבתו ממ"נ כ"כ המגיד, זרע שפחה וזרע עכו"ם אינן נקראין זרעו ומותרת בקרוביו משא"כ ישראלית שילדה מן העכו"ם נקרא זרעה:
**** ENGLISH ****
Safek maidservants. But we do not compel the master to free them — for a woman is not commanded regarding procreation; however he may not enslave them from safek — so Maggid chapter 5; and if both are his daughters — such as he came upon his maidservant and she bore and became mixed with his daughter — he has rights in them that a father has over his daughter in any case — so Maggid. Seed of maidservant and seed of gentile are not called his seed and she is permitted to his relatives; unlike Israelite woman who bore from gentile — it is called her seed.
