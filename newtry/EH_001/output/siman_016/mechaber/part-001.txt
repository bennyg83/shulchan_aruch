**** ENGLISH ****
Prohibition of a non-Jewish woman and a maidservant. It contains 6 seifim.

An Israelite who had relations with a non-Jewish woman in the manner of marriage, or an Israelite woman who was lain with by a non-Jew in the manner of marriage — behold these receive lashes from the Torah, as it is said: you shall not intermarry with them (and some dispute this) (Tur). But one who comes upon a non-Jewish woman in the manner of promiscuity incidentally is liable for her rabbinically because of non-Jewish woman and because of zona; and they strike him lashes of rebellion. And if he designated her for himself in promiscuity he is liable for her rabbinically because of niddah, maidservant, non-Jewish woman, zona. And if he was a kohen, even if he came upon her incidentally he receives lashes from the Torah because of zona.

**** ENGLISH ****
One who comes upon a non-Jewish woman — if zealots did not strike him and court did not lash him — behold his punishment is explicit in the words of tradition that it is karet, as it is said: for Judah has profaned the holiness of Hashem which he loved, and has married the daughter of a strange god; Hashem will cut off the man who does this, etc. If he is an Israelite he will have no share among the sages and no portion among disciples.

{Rama: And this sin has a loss that is not in all the forbidden relations — the son from the maidservant and from the gentile woman is not his son, unlike other forbidden relations (Tur in name of Rambam). One who comes upon a non-Jewish woman in public whose law is that zealots strike him as will be explained in Choshen Mishpat siman 425 — he is among the forbidden relations and his law is to be killed and not transgress as in other forbidden relations (Beit Yosef in name of Orach Chaim who so wrote in name of Ramban) (and as will be explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 157).}
