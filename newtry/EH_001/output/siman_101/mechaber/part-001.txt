**** ENGLISH ****
How much she collects of her ketubah. It contains 4 seifim:

A widow — as long as she holds the ketubah document in her hand, she collects forever, whether they give her sustenance in her father's house or in her husband's house, and she has sworn — even after she remarried. Even if a sabbatical year passed over her, she is not released unless she impaired it — that she collected part or stood it as a loan. And if her ketubah is not in her hand and she comes to collect under beit din conditions in a place where they do not write — if they give her sustenance in her father's house (or she remarried afterward) (Mordechai end of ch. HaNosei) — she does not collect except until the end of twenty-five years. But if she was silent twenty-five years and did not claim, she waived. And if she claimed within twenty-five years, they count twenty-five years for her from the day she claimed. And likewise her heirs must claim within twenty-five years of her death, and if they were silent more than twenty-five years they waived.

{Rama: And all who remembered and said that what she is silent about is not due to waiver is considered as having claimed (Ran ch. HaNosei).}
