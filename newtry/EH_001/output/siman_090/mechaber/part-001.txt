**** ENGLISH ****
Law of the husband inheriting his wife and what he does not inherit, and law of a man's sale of property collateralized to his wife, and the claim "I did it for my husband's pleasure." It contains 20 seifim.

The husband inherits his wife — whether melog property or tzon barzel property. When does this apply? In what is possessed; but not in what is due, such as an inheritance she was fit to inherit and died in her bequeather's lifetime — he does not stand in her place to inherit.

{Rama: Document of half male share customary nowadays where the father writes for his daughter to take in his inheritance like a half-male share, and if the daughter goes without surviving offspring the obligation is void — if the daughter died in her father's lifetime and left surviving offspring and the offspring died and afterward her father died — the husband inherits the power of that document through his offspring who would inherit his mother, and he inherits his offspring (Mahariu siman 16). If her bequeathers wrote for her land from lifetime and produce after death — the husband inherits that land from her (Mordechai start of Yesh Nochalin). But enhancement he takes in what he inherits from her, for he enhanced it with his authority. And he does not inherit his wife's loans, such as if her bequeathers died and they had a loan in others' hands when she married and she died before collecting them.

{Rama: But if her bequeathers left property even though the daughter did not collect in her lifetime, and even if they were collateralized for a widow's ketubah — they are called possessed for the daughter and the husband inherits them (Mordechai chapter Kol HaNishba'in). And likewise if she had a loan in others' hands when she married and the woman died before collecting it — he does not inherit it; but if she lent from melog property and died before collecting — in this the husband inherits it.

{Rama: All property that belonged to the woman over which the husband has no authority and she lent them to others — the husband does not inherit them (Beit Yosef in name of responsum of Rashba).}

**** ENGLISH ****
A husband who sold his property and afterward his wife wrote to the buyer "I have no claim against you" and agreed to his act — even though they acquired from her — behold she repossesses; for she wrote to him only so there not be quarrel between her and her husband, and she can say "I did it for my husband's pleasure." And even if she wrote to him that she cannot say "I did it for my husband's pleasure" — it is nothing. But if they first acquired from the woman's hand that she has no lien on this place and afterward the husband sold it — she does not repossess it. And likewise if the husband sold and told his wife to write to the buyer "I have no claim against you" and she did not agree to his act, and the husband returned and sold to another person whether that field or another field, and after the husband sold she agreed to his act and they acquired from her hand that she has no lien on this field — she cannot protest, for she cannot say "I did it for my husband's pleasure." And likewise if she accepted liability upon herself explicitly that if a creditor of her husband repossesses from him she will pay him — she cannot claim "I did it for my husband's pleasure."

{Rama: And some say if the woman received the money she can no longer say "I did it for my husband's pleasure" (Nimmukei Yosef chapter Chazakah and Meisharim siman 123 ch. 4). A woman who admitted she is liable with her husband must pay and cannot claim in this "I did it for my husband's pleasure," for we do not say so except regarding property he sells (Beit Yosef in name of responsum of Ramban). A woman who removed her power from her husband's property before the husband acquired them — he can sell them afterward and she does not repossess from them, and it is not relevant to say in this "I did it for my husband's pleasure" (Mordechai start of HaKotev and Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 23).}
