**** ENGLISH ****
Law: there is no kiddushin for a minor; and the laws of a minor boy and girl if they matured. It contains 2 seifim.

A minor who betrothed or married — it is nothing, for the Sages did not institute marriage for a minor; and it is forbidden to marry him to a woman while he is still a minor.

{Rama: However if he already married — need not divorce her and she may remain until he matures (Mordechai start He Comes Upon His Yevamah) (and so Ashiri chapter One Who Betroths).}

And even if he sent gifts after maturing — it is nothing, for we are not concerned perhaps he sent for kiddushin. But if there are witnesses that he was secluded with her after maturing — she needs a get, for presumably he married for kiddushin. And if she fulfilled ketubah obligations after maturing — her ketubah stands and need not write another; specifically maneh and two hundred; but no additions.

{Rama: If he did not write her a ketubah when he was a minor — when he matures he writes her only maneh, for at that time she is not a virgin (Hagahot Alfasi start One Who Was Married).}

<i data-commentator="Beit Shmuel" data-order="5"></i>המקדש את הקטנה היתומה או קטנה שיצתה מרשות אביה אימתי ממאנת וכל דיניה יתבאר בסימן קנ"ה: <small>הגה וע"ל סי' קס"ט מי נאמן על קטן וקטנה אם נתגדלו <i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="8"></i>ספק אם הם גדולים או קטנים הוי ספק קידושין יכולין ב"ד לפסוק להשיא את הקטנה כמו שיש רשות לאמה ולאחיה ויכולין להתנות שאם תמאן תאבד כל אשר לה אבל אין להם רשות להשיא את הקטן (נ"י פרק חרש) וע"ל ריש סימן קנ"ה:</small>
**** ENGLISH ****
One who betroths an orphan minor girl or a minor girl who left her father's authority — when does she refuse (mi'un) and all her laws will be explained in siman 155.

{Rama: And see above siman 169 who is believed regarding a minor boy and girl if they matured; if there is doubt whether they are adults or minors — it is doubtful kiddushin; beit din may marry off the minor girl as mother and brother have authority; and they may stipulate that if she refuses she loses all she has; but they have no authority to marry off a minor boy (Nekudot Yosef chapter Deaf-Mute). And see start of siman 155.}
