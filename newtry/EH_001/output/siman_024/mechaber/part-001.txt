**** ENGLISH ****
Israel is not suspected regarding bestiality and regarding a male. It contains one seif.

Israel was not suspected regarding relations with a male and regarding an animal; therefore there is no prohibition of yichud with them. And if one distances himself even from yichud with a male and an animal — behold this is praiseworthy. And the great Sages would distance an animal so they would not be alone with them. And in these generations, when the licentious have multiplied, one should distance oneself from being alone with a male.
