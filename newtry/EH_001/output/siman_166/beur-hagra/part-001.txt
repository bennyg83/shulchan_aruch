<b>And there are those, etc.</b> Tosafot there explain that this applies to the stated matters, but regarding initial penetration, etc.; and in the Yerushalmi, etc. Although the Gemara says, "when the Merciful One included unwitting, etc.," it was taking the first clause of the Mishnah, and the same applies to initial penetration. Therefore the Gemara's citation is directed to initial penetration, and there is no need to force the explanation that it refers only because of the word "acquired"; rather, it applies to the whole Mishnah. This is the language of the Yerushalmi there: Shmuel said, he acquired only for the matters stated in the passage, to inherit the dead brother's property, etc. Rabbi Yochanan said, he acquired for all matters. Rabbi Yitzchak bar Elazar said: where do they disagree? Where he performed initial penetration with her. But if he completed intercourse, all agree that he acquired for all matters.
