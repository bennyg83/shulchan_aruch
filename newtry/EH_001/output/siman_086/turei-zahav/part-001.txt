**** ENGLISH ****
(ב) <b>Conducts business within the house.</b> Meaning that it is known that the husband entrusted his property to her. He wrote further that all our women are considered to conduct business within the house. This is the same uncertainty that Rama wrote in siman 1, regarding a woman who went overseas and the husband came. My teacher and father-in-law, z"l, wrote in the name of Mordechai that he was uncertain about this and asked his teachers, and the matter was uncertain in their hands; therefore Rama wrote that they should not give it to him, etc. We say in chapter HaMafkid: if one received something from a minor and made it into a treasure for him. Hagahot Ashri wrote: but he should not return it to the minor, lest he lose it. By mere lifting he became obligated in safeguarding because of doubt, perhaps it is a lost item and he became obligated in returning it. But initially there is no obligation to take it from his hand, for perhaps the owners gave it knowingly and will return and take it from him. End quote.
