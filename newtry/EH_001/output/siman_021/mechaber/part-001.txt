<b>To distance oneself from forbidden relations. It contains 7 seifim.</b>

A man must distance himself from women very greatly; and it is forbidden to signal with his hands or feet and to hint with his eyes to one of the forbidden relations; and forbidden to laugh with her, to ease his head toward her, or to look at her beauty; and even to smell perfumes on her is forbidden; and forbidden to gaze at women standing at the laundry; and forbidden to look at colored garments of a woman he knows even if not on her lest he come to lewd thought. If he encountered a woman in the market it is forbidden to walk after her — rather run and move aside or go behind her; and he shall not pass through doorway of a prostitute even four cubits distant; and one who gazes even at a woman's little finger intending to enjoy from it — as if he gazed at her nakedness; and forbidden to hear voice of forbidden relation or see her hair; and one who intends one of these matters — they strike him lashes of rebellion; and these matters are forbidden also regarding chiyvei lavin.
