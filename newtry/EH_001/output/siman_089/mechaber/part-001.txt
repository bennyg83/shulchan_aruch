**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Obligation to bury one's wife. It contains 4 seifim.</b>

If the woman died during her husband's lifetime he is obligated to bury her and attend to all needs of her burial; and included in this is the stone placed on the grave. And he is obligated to make for her eulogy and lamentations as is the way of the entire country; and if their custom is to eulogize with flutes he does not use fewer than two flutes and female mourners — even a poor person in Israel; and if he was rich everything is according to his honor; and if her honor exceeds his honor they bury her according to her honor that ascends with him and she does not descend.
