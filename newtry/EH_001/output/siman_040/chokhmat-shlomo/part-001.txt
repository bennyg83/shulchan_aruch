**** ENGLISH ****
In same seif — and per Rambam, etc. if she was, etc. and fetus recognized, etc. NB it appears in my humble opinion Rambam emerges thus from baraita in Temurah and Gittin 23a "behold you are maidservant and your offspring" — if she was pregnant she acquired for him; why behold it is freeing davar shelo ba le'olam — how freeing helps? Behold freeing similar to hekdesh and kiddushin and all matters of transfer; why helps in davar shelo ba le'olam — must be proven when fetus recognized not davar shelo ba le'olam — examine; see in my composition on Megillat Esther Kohelet Yaakov part 2 on four parshiyot and Esther part signed 97 what he wrote there proof for Rambam from sugya Megillah with God's help examine; see what he further wrote proof for Rambam in my composition Milei Nezikin on Bava Kamma chapter Merubah 47b what he wrote there great proof for Rambam when fetus recognized considered come to world with God's help examine.
