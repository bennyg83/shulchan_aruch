**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Law of one who betroths after time, such as after she gives birth and similar. It contains 8 seifim.</b>

One who says to a woman "behold you are betrothed to me with this perutah after thirty days" — even if the money was consumed within thirty days — she is betrothed after thirty days. If he retracted within the thirty days, or she retracted — she is not betrothed.

{Rama: Some say if he betrothed with a document and it is not in existence after thirty days — rather torn or lost within thirty — she is not betrothed (Ran chapter HaOmer). One who betroths a woman for thirty days — betrothed forever (there).}

**** ENGLISH ****
A second man came and betrothed her within thirty days — she is betrothed to the second forever; for at the time the second betrothed her she was not betrothed, and he acquired her with second's kiddushin and she became a married woman. After thirty days when first's kiddushin come — he finds her a married woman; the first is as one who betrothed a married woman — kiddushin do not take effect in her.

{Rama: But if the second died or divorced her within thirty — first's kiddushin return (Tur in name of some). Therefore she needs a get from the first (Taz, Beur HaGra). And some dispute (Tur in name of Rosh).}
