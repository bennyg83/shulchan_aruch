<b>דיני צרת היבמה וקרובותיה. ובו ה סעיפים:</b> <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="א" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="1"></i>הכונס את יבמתו נאסרו צרותיה עליו ועל שאר האחים <i data-commentator="Beit Shmuel" data-order="1"></i><i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="1"></i>בעשה:

**** HEBREW ****
<i data-commentator="Beur HaGra" data-order="2"></i>החולץ ליבמתו נאסרה החלוצה וצרותיה עליו ועל שאר האחים <i data-commentator="Beit Shmuel" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ב" data-order="2"></i><i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="2"></i>בעשה:
**** ENGLISH ****
A man granting chalitzah (separation ceremony for a woman bound for yibbum) to his [late] brother's wife, is Rabbinically forbidden to marry her relatives, even second degree relatives. Similarly, she is forbidden to his relatives. But his brothers are permitted to her relatives. Note: Even if he performed chalitzah out of doubt, he is still forbidden to all her relatives (Mordechai, beginning of chapter haCholetz).
**** END BLOCK ****
