Seif 1: Man may make agent to betroth etc. NB I present one note Divinely inspired: Gemara ch. 2 Kiddushin "man betrothes through himself and through his agent" and challenges "now through his agent he betrothes — a fortiori through himself?" I wondered what is the challenge — explicit in Makkot that in testimony if one found disqualified their testimony voids; Gemara challenges "sodomized saves rapist saves" and answers in fulfillers of written matter not doers. If so what is challenge — we hold agent becomes witness; if agent disqualified perhaps only through agent then kiddushin with witnesses but not through himself since he voids testimony being one disqualified — therefore teaches even through himself helps because doers of act do not void testimony. To say automatically we hear without agent cannot make agent — this rule "cannot do" is when inherently cannot do e.g. non-priest sacrificing; but here he can betroth himself only blocked because voids testimony — not in cannot-do category; especially since Gemara there in Makkot discusses e.g. sodomized from behind etc. and could answer blinded eyes etc.; if so since could betroth from behind or blind her eyes considered can do; and if betrothes before her seeing cannot betroth — therefore teaches man betrothes through himself and agent — through himself like agent even seeing betrothal as doer of act. Uncertain. Likewise difficulty on Makkot Gemara for why not challenge from Gittin "and he gives in her hand" — get voids being invalid; difficult per one who says on condition cut off and even per one who says on pain of cut off; uncertain; examine well. Mishnat Melekh was uncertain if one made agent to betroth and agent went and made doubtful kiddushin — is this change of agency; I found proof Nedarim ch. One Who Vows From Vegetables — taught there agent who completed his agency etc.; mishnah not per R' Akiva for R' Akiva said all matters agent appoints is for owner's benefit not agent's detriment; why challenge — Rosh and Ran wrote above R' Akiva does not pay; and since doubt — in vows stringently forbidden from doubt but for misappropriation perhaps doubt — therefore agent misappropriates; and should have asked why misappropriate agent from doubt rather than owner — for either way if liable for doubt misappropriation applies to agent perhaps change; if misappropriation does not apply to doubt why challenge misappropriate owner — also doubt perhaps change and no misappropriation in doubt; could only say not misappropriate agent but why challenge owner — must prove doubt not considered change of agency and certainly completed agency; therefore challenges owner; examine — clear proof per my humble opinion with God's help; examine.
