**** ENGLISH ****
(א) <b>And we do not write the name, etc.</b> Although the Mishnah says "his city and her city," Tosafot explain that this means the place of residence, and that is the core enactment concerning the city name, as the Rosh wrote in chapter HaSholeach. Still, omission does not invalidate; only a change invalidates, as the Rosh wrote there. A proof is that in the third chapter, regarding one who writes forms of gittin, it mentions that one must leave blank the essential names, but does not mention the city name. Likewise the Gemara says "we require his name and her name," and does not mention his city and her city. This is the reading of Rashi and Tosafot, unlike our printed reading in some places. The Rosh wrote that "his city and her city" means place of residence, which should be written, but again the primary invalidity is only where it was changed. Beur HaGra surveys the readings of Rashi, Tosafot, Mordechai, Hagahot Ashri, the Rosh, and the Tur, and explains that the Mechaber's rule follows the distinction between omission and alteration: one should write the place correctly, but a missing place name is not like a changed personal name unless local practice and the wording create a real misidentification.
