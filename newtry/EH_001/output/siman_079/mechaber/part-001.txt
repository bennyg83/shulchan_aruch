<b>Obligation of her medical treatment. It contains 3 seifim.</b>

Her medical treatment — how so? If she was struck — he is obligated to heal her — whether medicine that has a fixed allowance or medicine that has no fixed allowance; but his widow is not healed from his property except with medicine that has no fixed allowance — for it is similar to sustenance.
