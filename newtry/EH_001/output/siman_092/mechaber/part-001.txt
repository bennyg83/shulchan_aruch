**** ENGLISH ****
The man who removed himself from produce of his wife's property and from her inheritance. It contains 8 seifim.

One who writes or says to his wife while she is betrothed "I have no claim or right in your property" — if she sells or gives, it stands; and he has nothing at all in the sale money (Hagahot Alfasi). And as long as she did not sell and did not give — the husband eats the produce, and if she dies he inherits her. And if he wrote for her thus after marriage — they must acquire from his hand, and then her sale and gift stand.

{Rama: And his removal is effective only after betrothal; but before betrothal removal does not help (Ran beginning of HaKotev and Rashba siman 465).}
