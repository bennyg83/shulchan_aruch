**** ENGLISH ****
Kiddushin take effect in chiyvei lavin. Dispute of R' Akiva and the Rabbis in several places; the main one is in the mishnah chapter 4 of Yevamot 44a — one reason, one reason; and halakhah is like the Sages. And likewise stam mishnah like them there 41a, and as stated 9a; and behold prohibition of mitzvah, etc.; and there 24a and chapter 4a and Kiddushin 66b. And even though Yevamot 32a stam is like R' Akiva — nevertheless that of Kiddushin is taught in a fixed halakhah, as stated in the first chapter of Avodah Zarah 6 and Bava Kamma 102a. And likewise the sugya Yevamot 10a-b, and some say 2, 13b, 14a-b, 20b in the dispute of R' Yosi and R' Elazar 22a, R' Papa, etc.; and the sugya there 45 where they divided regarding a gentile and a slave, and halakhah was decided fit; and in several places there and Ketubot and Kiddushin — all like the Rabbis.
