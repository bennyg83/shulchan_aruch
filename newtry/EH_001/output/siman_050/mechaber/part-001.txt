<b>When kiddushin money and savlonot are returned; laws of penalties. It contains 7 seifim.</b>

One who betroths a woman — whether she retracted or he retracted, or he died or she died, or he divorced her — the kiddushin money is never returned; even if he betrothed her with a thousand dinar, behold they are an absolute gift without retraction. And if they were mistaken kiddushin — the money returns. And likewise if she refused (mi'un). And likewise if the kiddushin were conditional and the condition was not fulfilled. And likewise if they were doubtful kiddushin (and all this applies only from erusin; but from nisuin the kiddushin money returns (Maharil siman 61)).

**** ENGLISH ****
If one gives collateral to his fellow to assure he will not retract from the match — he must say: If I retract, you acquire such-and-such money from the body of this item. {Rama: Two who swore to each other to marry this one to that one, and one transgressed, or only one swore — all is explained in Yoreh Deah siman 236. Laws of matchmaking — see Choshen Mishpat end of siman 87 and end of siman 185 and siman 264.}
