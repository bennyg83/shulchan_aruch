**** ENGLISH ****
They appraise for a widow her garments. It contains 2 seifim.

A widow who comes to collect her ketubah — they appraise all her garments, whether weekday or Shabbat, and deduct them from her ketubah.

(And a chalutzah has the law of a widow) (Beit Yosef in name of Rivash siman 302).

But a divorcee whose husband divorced her willingly without a claim — they do not appraise weekday garments for her. But festival and Shabbat garments — they appraise for her.

And they cannot remove her from her garments with money (Beit Yosef in name of Ritva). Rather she takes them at their value (responsum Rosh kelal 80).

**** ENGLISH ****
One who gives a gift to his wife — even though he divorces her willingly — she acquired her gift.

{Rama: It seems here there is an error; rather it should be: even though he divorces her unwillingly, such as if she was rebellious against him — nevertheless her gift is hers. And likewise a widow who takes a gift — all that are not items that are clothing or jewelry made to adorn oneself with; and specifically that the husband made them for her. But others who gave her jewelry at the time of marriage — they intended a complete gift; behold they are like her melog property (Beit Yosef in name of Rivash siman 301).}
