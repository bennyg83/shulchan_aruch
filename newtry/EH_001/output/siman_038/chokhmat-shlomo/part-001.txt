Seif 2: Every condition etc. and these are that it be doubled etc. nb: Rambam's view that with "from now" doubling not required and laws of conditions — Acharonim challenged from Yevamot that all agree chalitzah has conditions, they dispute only doubling; chalitzah certainly takes effect from now since delayed chalitzah is impossible — why require doubling? Likewise challenged from "if when I die there will be a get" which is also from now since no get after death — see there. Per my humble opinion: reason with "from now" doubling not required — Tosafot Kesubos chapter 5 wrote reason requiring similarity to sons of Gad and Reuben — without it mere embellishment; if so presumption he intends thus — how with presumption not require condition laws as Rosh chapter Yesh Nochalin? One can say: since act is at future time, though now he wants thus perhaps later he changes mind and wants without condition; if he doubled condition shows he specifically stipulated; if not doubled only presumption — for future no presumption perhaps later he changes and waives; if so works when he wants act later; but "from now" wants act now — at least now presumption he wants specifically thus — no four elements needed, presumption suffices. Works for discretionary matter; but chalitzah is mitzvah — if unwilling to yibbum and stipulates condition in chalitzah it is like voiding mitzvah — therefore holds no stipulation and doubling — no presumption he intends only embellishment; rather presumption he does not intend to void mitzvah — like other future acts no presumption — requires condition laws. Question and examine Rambam's view. What compared "if when I die" to chalitzah condition — in chalitzah if no condition without doubling complete chalitzah fulfilling mitzvah no presumption; but "if when I die" if condition without doubling helps complete get — ultimately not his benefit. Answer: intent is clear he hates her divorces; since he hates her what is clear if he does not die no get since he did not double — mere embellishment and complete get in any case; if intent only if he dies then intent only for her benefit — how abandon his benefit for hers — if dies without get more benefit perhaps yibbum and certainly soul prefers — must be due to hatred; therefore embellishment no presumption — requires condition laws. Question and examine well. In condition for past requiring condition laws see Mishlei Lemelekh and Avnei Miluim siman 10; see my edition 6 siman 63 teshuvah Lemberg Trampal bought place Laws of Gittin siman 143 — see and examine.

Seif 34: One who betrothes etc. even though they retracted within utterance etc. nb: Beit Yosef's view — even in those not like speech nevertheless where one speech preferable; I elaborated in several places; now appears compelled proof from Shevuot 32b — except per R' Yehuda first section within utterance end within utterance why two — perhaps first section one speech immediately after first speech second denied — no pause at all; end teaches even with pause only within utterance helps — perforce not like speech even one speech not like speech; if one speech like speech also this like speech — examine; still sugya not resolved — small utterance and large utterance — perhaps first small utterance end large — requires study. While on this arose to resolve Tosafot there in name of R' Yehuda of Orleans — also from first proves admission and denial are one — see there. Per my opinion answer: we find in several places what is one matter for another purpose counts as it; what is admission and denial not one speech because separate matters no similarity; works for second's admission no connection to denial; but first's admission connects to second's denial — if his admission helps second obligated by his denial — considered denial; if admission does not help denial not considered denial — first admission joins second denial to first denial — one denial; therefore first admission within utterance as denial needs denial; but second admission not needed for denial — first view not like speech; therefore teaches. This is correct — examine well; see Choshen Mishpat on this law; see my edition 8 siman 85 teshuvah bought place Laws of Kiddushin siman 27 lengthy challenge — examine.
