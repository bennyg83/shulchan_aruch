**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Law of kiddushin of deaf-mute, idiot, minor, forbidden relations, and those liable to negative commandments. It contains 12 seifim.</b>

A deaf-mute man and deaf-mute woman are not subject to kiddushin from the Torah — whether they married their like, or a deaf-mute man married a hearing woman, or a deaf-mute woman married a hearing man. But the Sages instituted marriage for them; therefore if a hearing man came and betrothed the wife of a deaf-mute hearing woman — behold she is betrothed with two complete kiddushin and he gives a get, and she is permitted to her deaf-mute husband.
