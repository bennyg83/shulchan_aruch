<b>Chuppah Regarding What Is Acquired — 2 Seifim</b> <b>Seif 1.</b> When a man brings his betrothed to chuppah even though he has not had relations with her, behold she is as his wife for all matters and collects principal ketubah and additions if she is widowed or divorced when she is fit for relations. But if she was a niddah and afterward entered chuppah and was secluded with her — she is not as his wife; rather for all matters she is still as betrothed since she was a niddah. And some say specifically regarding not collecting additions she is still as betrothed, but for all other matters behold she is as married.

{Rama: And see above siman 54 what is called chuppah. And they must write the ketubah before chuppah so that it be chuppah fit for relations (Rambam ch. 10 Laws of Marriage). And per the one who says a niddah's chuppah is chuppah even though she is not fit for relations — likewise if he did not write a ketubah; nevertheless ab initio the custom is to write it beforehand. A woman mortally ill — even though she was brought with the bed under the chuppah so her husband would inherit her — this is not chuppah at all since she is not at all fit for relations and her husband does not inherit her (responsum of Rosh kelal 14). She entered chuppah and he did not bless the blessing of bridegrooms — it is complete chuppah for blessings are not me'akev and he blesses after several days (Tur siman 62). And ab initio he should bless her before he is secluded with her (so it appears from Rosh's responsum). And see siman 55. And the custom now is to bless under the chuppah before he is secluded with her. And the custom is that the groom and bride fast on the day of their chuppah (and see Orach Chayyim siman 573). If he betrothed in error and had marriage with her and afterward it became known the kiddushin were in error — even though he betroths her again he need not bless again the seven blessings and the first blessings suffice. Some say to make chuppah under the heavens for a good omen that their seed should be like the stars of heaven.}
