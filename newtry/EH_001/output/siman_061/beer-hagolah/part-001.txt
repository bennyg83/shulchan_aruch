בעיא שם ולא נפשט' וכ' ה"ה וכתוב בהלכות הלכך אינו קונה ודעת רבינו שה"ה לכל דבר שאין חופת נדה קונה לא ליורש' ולא ליטמא לה וכן דעת מקצת הגאוני'
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>(ג) The question remained unresolved; Maggid Mishneh's ruling.</b>
The question there was left unresolved. The Maggid Mishneh wrote — and so it is written in the Halachot — therefore it does not acquire. And Rabbenu's view is that the same applies to all matters: a niddah's chuppah does not acquire — not for inheritance, not for becoming impure for her. So too holds a portion of the Geonim.

<b>(ד) The Rosh's view: question was only about the additional ketubah.</b>
The Rosh in his rulings, as cited by the Tur, holds that the question was only regarding the additional ketubah amount. So too wrote the Ran. The Maggid Mishneh there also recorded the dissenting view.
