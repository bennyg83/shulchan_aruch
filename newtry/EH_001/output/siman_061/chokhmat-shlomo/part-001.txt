**** HEBREW ****
<small>סעיף ב'</small> <b>כשר הדבר שלא תנשא עד שתטהר וכו'</b> נ"ב הנה בחופת נדה אם ראוי לקדשה אח"כ כשטבלה עיין בחבורי לחו"מ מהדורא ה' סימן כ"ז קנה מקומה הל' ערב ייע"ש מ"ש דרך אגב ל"ק ניזנאב דח"ו לעשות כן ייע"ש בעזה"י ודוק:
**** ENGLISH ****
<b>"It is proper that she not marry until she purifies etc." — Whether to betroth after a niddah's chuppah.</b>
Seif 2 — "It is proper that she not marry until she purifies etc." Note: Regarding a niddah's chuppah, whether it is proper to betroth her afterward when she has immersed — see my work on Choshen Mishpat, fifth edition, siman 27, "Kanah Mekomo," Laws of Surety — see there for what I wrote in passing. There is no difficulty regarding the matter of Niznav [a place name], for Heaven forbid that one do so — see there, with Hashem's help, and examine carefully.
