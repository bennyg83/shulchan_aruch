**** ENGLISH ****
(ג) <b>Even if he made, etc., and set them up, etc.</b> Although setting up a debt is like collection, here actual collection is required, because as long as they did not come into his hand we say that he did not write, etc. Therefore Tosafot there had to establish that case of the added ketubah as where he had possession, even though regarding the added ketubah it teaches this one who stipulated, etc., and they did not establish it in such a case. Terumat HaDeshen; see there.
