**** ENGLISH ****
(Collection) And Netinim, etc. See in Bahag's hagahah — it is Rambam's view that all nations are equal and "you shall not intermarry" refers to their gentility; but once they converted, even the seven nations are permitted — only Yehoshua and David decreed on them.
