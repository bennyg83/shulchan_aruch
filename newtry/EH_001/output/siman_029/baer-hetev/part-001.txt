To me also difficult from what we learned Kiddushin 52b — woman marrying etc. man snatched money from fellows — none concerned for R. Per Rashba, Rav Betzalel's difficulty and my difficulty stand — not applicable to Beit Shmuel's answer that at giving said be betrothed; per Rashba only yes works. Per Ran my difficulty also resolved — there he said initially betrothed — then need not Beit Shmuel's answer. See Keneset Hagedolah 60, 61.
