(ה) <b>He divorced her after, etc.</b> The Mishnah there says "after," etc., and as written in the Gemara, "when her get," etc. Ramban, Rashba, and Maggid Mishneh explained that he gives the get now; for if it had been before he took her in, even after thirty days she would be believed, since where there is a prohibition he restrains himself. Further, how did he take her in? What Rav Ashi wrote there, "a get," etc., in the Mishnah, is where he gave a get for his zikah and he admits that he did not have relations. Here it is where he wrote the get plainly, and she says that he did not have relations and gave it for his zikah, while he says that he had relations and gave it for his intercourse. Although according to both of them she needs only chalitzah, nevertheless we impose the stringencies of both upon her: we believe him that he had relations, while she has rendered the get a shard by saying it was for the zikah, as written in the Yerushalmi.
