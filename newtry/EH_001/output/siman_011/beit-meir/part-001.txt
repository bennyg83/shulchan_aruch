{Rama: But with one of them, etc.} See Beit Shmuel who wrote and so Rambam, etc. — impossible what proof he brings from Rambam requiring also ugly matter as explained in part 4; and without doubt per Rif and Rambam there is no difference at all in husband's sight and non-decisive rumor alone they spoke; and presumptively per his explanation implies to them any rumor without basis through witnesses is not a rumor at all to remove woman from husband even defamer; therefore also what he further wrote this rumor implies even without establishment in beit din — requires examination, for truly plain Gemara language implies it only depends whether stopped or not; and per early commentators like Rif Rambam and Rosh for both explanations in She'iltot they do not need at all to explain whether must be established in beit din because it always has basis either through ugly matter or through husband who saw ugly matter; but per hagahah's ruling from defamer with non-decisive rumor alone we remove — I wonder, for general principle Rav Ashi Gittin ch. 8: any rumor not established is not a rumor; and both laws were stated together — law of rumor after marriage no distinction; on this agreed Maharshal — take also here; and what distinguishes this from that; and I already saw in Mechaber Beit Yosef this siman who wrote: with this woman there was neither this nor rumor forbidding defamer nor establishment in beit din; and do not distinguish rumors where she becomes forbidden to entire world requires beit din and where she is forbidden only to defamer — end quote; but in responsum Rashba siman 496 also implies he did not require establishment in beit din — not like what he wrote above; the reason: initially he may not marry, for in this marriage he reveals on the rumor that it is true; and so per Rama's view if he married plainly to rumor without great ugly matter — these are Ramban's words above; and thereby Beit Shmuel spoke correctly that here establishment in beit din; but to remove from husband they properly challenged per Maharshal from R' Eliezer; and per this there is also room to hesitate on Tosafot Sotah 27 s.v. domeh who proved it discusses non-decisive rumor since no distinction except initially; and bring proof from law here — meaning Rabbenu Tam's view that with non-decisive rumor alone halakhah is like Rabbi if he married he removes; and per above one may say here plainly to rumor and it is like ugly matter — not so there discussing kohen who marries; one may say since not established in beit din even though non-decisive rumor suffices to be concerned initially.
