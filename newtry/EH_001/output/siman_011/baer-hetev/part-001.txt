**** ENGLISH ****

Meaning: if her husband is a kohen and a Yisrael had relations with her by force — rape of a Yisrael is permitted; and if her husband was a Yisrael she was permitted to husband and paramour as stated in Ketubot 9a. Nevertheless if her husband is a kohen and she is forbidden to her husband, and we hold where forbidden to husband forbidden to paramour — therefore if forbidden to her kohen husband because of this forced relations, she is also forbidden to the paramour even though the paramour is a Yisrael — Beit Shmuel. What part 4 wrote on Bach: the Mishnah teaches defamer on a married woman and removed him from under his hand — even if he married he divorces, and it does not teach even if he preceded and married — see there. He was not precise, for Bach means: the first clause teaches "and if he married we do not remove" — it should have taught "and if he preceded and married" like regarding two sisters, etc., which also teaches "preceded and married" to teach before they consulted beit din he married — therefore we do not remove; but they consulted beit din and forbade and nevertheless he married — we remove; for now what it teaches "and if he married" implies even if they consulted beit din and forbade and nevertheless he married we do not remove — impossible, for truth of law here is indeed even if they consulted we do not remove. Not so in the end regarding defamer on a married woman, etc. — no distinction; it teaches preceded and married or married stam — always we remove automatically; why should the tanna lengthen the Mishnah in vain. One may say: now it comes to err that if they preceded and married before consulting we do not remove regarding defamer on a married woman — this is not so, for biblically forbidden to paramour as Rashi on the Mishnah Yevamot 24b s.v. yotzi — see there; and see Tosafot 25b s.v. amar lehu matnita, etc.; and understand.
