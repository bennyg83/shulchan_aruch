**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Witnesses to a get and likewise witnesses who testified to a woman that her husband died — whether they may marry that woman. It contains 4 seifim.</b>

One who brings a get that requires saying "before it was written and before it was signed," and likewise the single witness who testified to a woman that her husband died — he may not marry her because of suspicion. And if he married her he need not divorce. But one who brings a get that does not require "before it was written and before it was signed" is permitted to marry her, since she is not permitted on his account. And two witnesses who testified to a woman that her husband died — one of them is permitted to marry her, since two are not commonly found to sin for one. And see in the Tur itself siman 141.

{Rama: In the teshuvah of the Rosh that the Tur brought in Even HaEzer — and some say that even with two witnesses, although permitted, nevertheless a scrupulous person should distance himself from this (Hagahot Maymoniyot chapter 10 of Gittin and Levush and Tosafot Yevamot).}

**** ENGLISH ****
A woman who vowed enjoyment from her husband and he did not annul it for her, and she came before a sage to permit it and he forbade her because he did not find an opening to permit it — he may not marry her, lest they say he forbade her in order to marry her. And if he married her he need not divorce. And this is only regarding a single expert; but in court they are not suspected. And for this reason, if she refused or performed chalitzah before a court of three, one of them may marry her.

**** ENGLISH ****
And all of them who had wives at that time and afterward died or were divorced — and the women were those who accustomed their husbands to divorce them — behold these are permitted to marry them lekhatchilah. (If their wives were sick and died, they may not marry them) (Nimukei Yosef chapter 2 of Yevamot). And each one of them is permitted to marry the son of the witness who testified for her, or the son of the sage who forbade her upon her husband, or other relatives, for there is no concern that they will divorce them because of their relatives.
