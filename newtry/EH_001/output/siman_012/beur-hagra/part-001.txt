**** ENGLISH ****
And if he married, etc. Per Be'er HaGolah; but the Rashba's view is that it does not refer to the testifying witness since it is possible to touch Torah prohibition as stated regarding mere suspicion we do not remove — specifically where there is nothing to bring except suspicion; but regarding one accused of a married woman we remove by force and scandal of a married woman; and so in Tosafta: one who redeems the captive is permitted to marry her; the witness on the captive — he may not consummate; and if he consummated he need not divorce; "I killed him" / "we killed her" — he may not marry his wife; R' Yishmael: one accused, etc. of a married woman and she was divorced through another, etc. — he need not divorce; gentile and slave who came upon a daughter of Israel, etc. — he need not divorce; Israelite who came upon the maidservant, etc. — he need not divorce; sage who forbade the woman, etc. — he need not divorce; behold it taught in all of them he need not divorce; and it omitted only "I killed him," etc.; and so they established in the Gemara regarding gratuitously specifically.

{Rama: And some say even with two, etc. So too Tosafot there from what is stated "he judged," etc. and likewise witnesses, etc. "but," etc. And the poskim who omitted relied on Yerushalmi there that distinguishes between our case and that case as stated there: "there we taught one who deposits produce with his friend — even if lost," etc. Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, etc. "what about the money" — R' Yochanan says dispute of R' Akiva and R' Tarfon who taught court may not take, etc. R' Chiya bar R' Shabbetai challenged: here you say if she refused or performed chalitzah before him he may marry her because he is court; and here you say thus — R' Yosi said: there purchase tends to involve three parties; but here two are not commonly found to sin for one."
