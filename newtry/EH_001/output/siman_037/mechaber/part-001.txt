**** ENGLISH ****
<b>All laws of betrothing a minor. It contains 27 seifim.</b>

The father betroths his daughter without her knowledge all the time she is a minor; and likewise when she is a na'arah she is in his authority, and her kiddushin belong to her father. Likewise he is entitled to her finds, to the production of her hands, and to her ketubah. If she is widowed or divorced from erusin he is entitled to everything until she comes of age. Therefore the father accepts kiddushin for his daughter from the day she is born until she comes of age. Even if she was a deaf-mute or insane and the father betrothed her, behold she is a fully married woman. If she was three years and one day old she may be betrothed by bi'ah with her father's consent; less than this, if her father handed her over for bi'ah kiddushin she is not betrothed. {Rama: Some say kiddushin do not take effect with a nephel; and if his father accepted kiddushin for him and the betrother afterward betrothed his sister, she needs a get (Or Zarua).}
