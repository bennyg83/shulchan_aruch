**** ENGLISH ****
<b>The oaths of a widow and divorcee on the ketubah. It contains 21 seifim.</b>

A widow does not collect her ketubah — principal and tosefet — nor nedunya and tzon barzel, nor property he designated for her in her ketubah, except with an oath. If those movables she brought to him are themselves still intact, or things known to have come from them — she takes them without an oath. But if the tzon barzel property is not intact and not things that came from them, and she comes to collect from his property — she is not paid except with an oath. The same applies if he designated movables for her in her ketubah that are subject to the same division as we divided regarding tzon barzel. And if he designated land for her in her ketubah, even if he specified only one side — she takes it without an oath. And if she died before swearing — her heirs do not inherit her ketubah, for a person does not bequeath to his sons money that can only be collected with an oath.

{Rama: And see the legal distinctions in this matter in Choshen Mishpat siman 108. Some say that which her heirs do not inherit her ketubah — is when she did not seize; but if she seized — her heirs inherit her ketubah, since they are in possession — thus implied in Tur Choshen Mishpat siman 108 and in Mordechai beginning Almanah Nizonet and in Mahariu siman 95 and in Choshen Mishpat siman 300 (and there are disputants) (Mordechai ch. Kol HaNishba'in in name of Maharam, and see Choshen Mishpat siman 108). And if the heirs say she swore on them and can bring proof — (Rivash siman 169). And if they paid her part of her ketubah and made a debt document on the remainder — her heirs inherit that debt even if she did not swear (Beit Yosef in name of responsum Rashba siman 486). A mute widow who indicated before her death that she took nothing — that suffices and it is as if she swore (Beit Yosef in name of responsum Rashba). A woman who became insane — her heirs do not take her ketubah, since she cannot swear (Beit Yosef in name of responsum Rashba and so writes Ramban in responsum siman 66). And see below siman 105 seif 3.}

But if she was divorced and died — her heirs swear "you did not command us" and collect, even from his heirs if he died after her.
