**** ENGLISH ****
<b>Not to betroth through intercourse and prohibition of intercourse with an unattached woman even if he designates her. It contains 4 seifim.</b>

A woman is not considered a married woman except through kiddushin whereby she was betrothed properly; but if he came upon her through prostitution not for kiddushin — it is nothing. And even if he came upon her for marriage between him and her — she is not considered his wife even if he designated her to himself; rather on the contrary they compel him to remove her from his house.

{Rama: For certainly she is ashamed to immerse and he came upon her in her niddah (Tur); but if he designates a woman to himself and she immerses for him — some say permitted and she is the concubine mentioned in Torah (Ra'avad and some commentaries); and some say forbidden and they strike lashes for this because of "do not be kedeshah" (Rambam and Rosh and Tur); a Kutite who married a Kutit and likewise an apostate who married an apostate woman in their customs and afterward converted — there is no concern of kiddushin at all and she is permitted to leave him without a get even though he stayed with her many years — it is only ordinary prostitution (Ribash siman 6 and Teshuvot Maharil siman 200) (and see above siman 149 seif 6).}

**** ENGLISH ****
A woman is betrothed in three ways — with money or with a document or through intercourse — from Torah; but the Sages forbade betrothal through intercourse because of licentiousness; and if he transgressed and betrothed through intercourse they strike him lashes of rebellion and she is betrothed. And even if he betrothed with money or with a document — if he did not arrange a match first, or if he betrothed in the marketplace, they strike him lashes of rebellion and she is betrothed.

{Rama: And some say they do not strike if he betrothed with money or with a document even in the marketplace and without matchmaking (Tur in name of Rosh); and I have never seen in my days that they struck one who betrothed without matchmaking.}
