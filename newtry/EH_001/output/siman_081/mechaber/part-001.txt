**** ENGLISH ****
One who consecrates his wife's earnings. It contains 2 seifim.

One who consecrates his wife's earnings — behold she works and eats, and the surplus is non-sacred. If he said to her "your hands be consecrated for their work" — behold all her earnings are sacred. And some say it is not sacred, for behold she can say "I am not sustained and I will not work" (Ran chapter Even Af and so too Tur's language implies).

**** ENGLISH ****
A woman who said "my hands be consecrated for their work" or vowed not to enjoy her earnings — he is not forbidden in her earnings because her hands are subjugated to him; but he must annul for her lest he divorce her and she be forbidden to return to him.
