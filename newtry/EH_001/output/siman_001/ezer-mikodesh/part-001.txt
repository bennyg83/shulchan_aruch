<b>סעיף א' (לישא אשה) </b>על האב מצוה להשיא בנו משמע שגם גדול מופלג ואינו על שלחן אביו מ"מ מוטל כי מתביש. ובזיווג ב' יש לומר דקיל כי אין מתבייש כנהוג. ולישא לפני כ' שנה יש לומר שאין להבן לסמוך את עצמו על אביו ולא יתבייש לשלוח בצינעה:<br>לישא יש לומר דלא שייך כיבוד אב ואם שאומרים לא זו כי חן מקח על חלקו. וכתיב יעזב איש את אביו כו' ודבק באשתו היינו רצויה לחלקו היטב. ומה שאינו מצד חן רק בשביל ממון וכוותיה מוטל כיבוד אב ואם אשר הם רוצים ישא. ואחר תקיעות כף תנאים כנהוג לא שייך עוד כיבוד אב ואם. רק כשממון מפקיע מתיר לכתחילה אם נהוג כן. גם כי בממון כיבוד אב ואם משל אב. מ"מ בכך אם האב ישיב אליו הקנס שיתן יצייתנו היטב:
**** ENGLISH ****
**Seif 1 (to marry a woman).** Upon the father there is a mitzvah to marry off his son — it implies even an exceedingly adult son who is not at his father's table; nevertheless it is incumbent upon him because he is ashamed. And in a second match one can say it is lenient because he is not ashamed as is customary. And to marry before twenty years — one can say the son should not rely on his father and will not be ashamed to send discreetly. "To marry" — one can say kibbud av does not apply; and if they say no — for grace of acquisition is on his portion. And it is written "a man shall leave his father," etc., "and cleave to his wife" — meaning desired for his portion well. And what is not from grace but only for money and the like — kibbud av is incumbent; and if they wish to marry. And after palm-slapping of conditions as is customary — kibbud av no longer applies. Only when money nullifies — permitted initially if practiced thus. Also because with money kibbud av is from the father; nevertheless in this, if the father responds to him with the fine he will comply well:

<b>סעיף ג' (לא נהגו לכוף) </b>מה שמעלימים עיני העדה מלכוף למי שדר עם אשתו עשר שנים ולא ילדה שיגרשה. וכמ"ש בשו"ת הריב"ש ז"ל ובש"ע. ועל פי הרוב אין אומר גם כן בניחותא בלי כפיה בזה. יש על זה צד זכות מצד דהוה גם כן ספק ספיקא. ספק אין המניעה מצדה רק מצדו. ויש גם כן ספק אם יעשו הדברים תנובה ויהיו נשמעים. ויש בזה עוד ספיקות ספק מצדה יהיה מניעה מלקבל גט. ספק מצדו יהיה מניעה מלגרשה הן לאהבתו אותה הן מצד בצע מוהר הכתובה וכדומה ספק ששניהם אינם ראוים להוליד. וגם שאין זה ספק שקול. כי רובא דעלמא מולידין וכאן דאתחזק ריעותא די לתלות הריעותא באחד מהם ולא בשניהם. על כל זה אולי שייך בזה קבוע. גם כתבתי במקום אחר פלפול בשם מו"ח נ"י אודות ספק אחד שקול והשני אינו שקול. אודות מ"ש בזה הפ"י ז"ל פ"ק דכתובות. ומ"א ז"ל סי' תס"ג. ועל כל פנים חזי לאצטרופי לכמה ספיקות הנ"ל ויש גם כן צד שאסור לגרש אשתו ראשונה. ויש גם כן סברא אודות ארץ ישראל תובב"א. לזה יש זכות גמור על ההעלמת עיין בזה לגמרי. אך אם כבר התחילו הזוג בעסקי פשרות אודות גירושין נכון להכניס את עצמו לגמור לטובה הגירושין. שמסתמא ידע בנפשיה שראוי להוליד ואין כאן עוד ספיקות כלל. ועל כל פנים נכון להודיעו. שמצד ספק הוא פטור משאר וכסות אולי על ידי זה יתפשרו. וכן עשיתי והודעתי לאחד שכבר עסק לפני עם אשתו. אודות גט מחמת שלא הולידה לו יותר מעשר שנים. וכבר היו קרובים להתפשר רק מצד אהבת בצע לא נתפשרו והוא בעל מלאכה ועשיר. וגם היא נקל לה להתפרנס בנחת. או להיות בשירות אצל בעל בית ויהיה לה עוד יותר מכדי פרנסתה. וכן אפשר לה להנשא לזקן. ולא הודעתי אז זכותו מה שהוא פטור משארה וכסותה. והודעתיו עכשיו. כדי שיזדרזו להתפשר בטוב:

**Seif 3 (they are not accustomed to compel).** What they turn the community's eyes from compelling — one who lived with his wife ten years and she did not bear a child that he divorce her; as written in responsum Rivash and in Shulchan Arukh. And according to the majority they do not even say gently without compulsion in this. There is merit on this from the side that there is also a double doubt. Doubt — the impediment is from her side only. And there is also doubt whether they will accomplish the matter and it will be heard. And there are more doubts: doubt from her side there will be prevention from receiving a get; doubt from his side there will be prevention from divorcing her — whether from love of her or from desire for the ketubah money and the like; doubt that both of them are not fit to bear. And this too is not a balanced doubt — for the majority of the world bear children; and here, since presumption of companionship was established, it suffices to hang the companionship on one of them and not on both. On all this perhaps "fixed" applies here. I also wrote elsewhere pilpul in the name of Maharach regarding one doubt balanced and the second not balanced; regarding what Pnei Yehoshua wrote on this in first chapter of Ketubot; and Magen Avraham siman 463. And in any case it is fit to combine several of the above doubts; and there is also a side that it is forbidden to divorce his first wife. And there is also svara regarding Eretz Yisrael, may it be rebuilt speedily in our days. For this there is complete merit for turning a blind eye — see this fully. But if the couple has already begun compromise dealings about divorce — it is right to enter himself to complete favorably the divorce; for presumably he knows in his soul he is fit to bear and there are no more doubts at all. And in any case it is right to inform him that from doubt he is exempt from she'er and kesus — perhaps by this they will compromise. And so I did and informed one who was already engaged before with his wife regarding a get because she did not bear him more than ten years; and they were already close to compromise — only from love of gain they did not compromise; and he is a craftsman and wealthy. And she too can easily earn a livelihood in comfort, or be in service with a household owner and have more than enough for her livelihood. And she can also marry an old man. And I did not inform him then of his merit that he is exempt from she'er and kesus; and I informed him now — in order that they hurry to compromise well:

**(I found further)** regarding turning a blind eye in matters of matches — regarding marrying an old woman; and one who stays ten years without a child and does not divorce her; as written in Shulchan Arukh in the name of Rivash — besides what Rivash wrote there reasons for turning a blind eye in this. Besides this, according to the majority there are doubts — doubt that he can no longer bear; and all the more so if it has not yet been established that he can bear. And even if he already bore — nevertheless there is doubt that perhaps from now he cannot bear anymore. And this is not against such a strong majority and presumption regarding one who stayed ten years and did not bear — for from her side too presumption applies because of the majority of women who conceive and bear. And all the more so if she already bore and miscarried or her children died — eighty from the time of the children's death as written in Beit Yosef in Beit HaEfodi in the name of Maggid Mishnah in the name of Rashba in Mordechai haRiv — see there; in this both are equal and why do you see to hang that the delay is from her — perhaps from him? And she is not commanded regarding peru u-revu, and we have no need to involve ourselves in this at all. And doubt that even if he divorces her — perhaps a proper match will not present itself to him, and some barren woman or old woman will present herself before him. And also that from an old woman he can guard himself, and the majority of women are not barren. On all this — from the side that the owner of a lost object must seek his loss — behold this is in a presumption of fixed, and majority does not apply; as Chazal said in Ketubot first chapter regarding "he goes to her." And also that now we judge stam what he will marry — whether from the majority or the minority — and there would be room to say that fixed does not apply here, like we establish that they go after the majority regarding refusal and chalitzah since the majority are not aylonit. Nevertheless there it is different — for we judge on what is before us, one soul, and decide by the world's majority; for even among those who move there is also majority in this. And in any case among those who move in the entire world there is decision through one alone; unlike here, where we do not judge on the soul before us — only we judge when he returns to marry a woman what will fall in his fate; and in this fixed applies as above — and see and consider. And perhaps this was the reason they do not compel when she did not bear, or bore and the children died and she aged — and according to what is known in women's custom she is no longer fit to bear; for also by law of the Gemara they do not compel in this; as written in Beit Yosef siman 154 in the name of responsum Rosh — see there. And only when she stayed ten years is there Chazal's enactment to divorce. Also when according to law it would be right to turn a blind eye from doubt or the above doubts — and there are further doubts: perhaps he himself will awaken tomorrow to divorce, and we need not involve ourselves in this; and all the more so for those who hold they do not compel fools, only in matters where there is further doubt that perhaps compulsion will not help at all in the matter (and all the more if they already involved themselves and urged once — as happened here, where I urged one who has no children with many harsh words; and there is also doubt perhaps he is not fit to bear; and there is here one who was established with several women that the impediment is from him; and they did not prevent a child from her since she is not commanded regarding peru u-revu); and all the more so in the length of the exile — that according to the majority it is impossible to establish religious judgments on their basis through compulsion; and all the more so regarding one whom they know knows obligation of peru u-revu — that from presumption of his fitness there is to remove him from presumption of majority who bear, and perhaps he cannot bear; and in any case perhaps a woman will not wish to marry him because she thinks perhaps he cannot bear and will say "I want a staff," etc.; and regarding what is only an enactment through custom — there is merit. Similar to the above in doubts regarding one who stayed ten years and did not bear — so too regarding bachelors who stay more than twenty years and do not marry; in this one can say perhaps he feels in himself he is not a son of sons, and perhaps he avoids this himself and a proper match does not present itself to him; and perhaps he will not heed even after warning; and perhaps in his haste an unsuitable match will present itself — not fit to bear or not his match — and he will not return to it; and all the more so in this, that it was not explained in Chazal's words to involve ourselves and compel — the above excuses help to merit the custom of turning a blind eye; and according to human nature sometimes there is cause for opposition to honor of the holy Torah if rabbis involve themselves in this regularly; and perhaps according to this one can explain what Chavot Yair and Beit Shmuel wrote to be precise whether staying ten years is more severe than marrying an old woman — see there in siman 1, that there are aspects for every side;
