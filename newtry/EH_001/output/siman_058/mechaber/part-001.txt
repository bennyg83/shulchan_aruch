**** ENGLISH ****
<b>How much clothing must one give his daughter. It contains 2 seifim.</b>

The Sages ordained that a person give from his property a modest amount to his daughter — and this is called sustenance (parnasah).

One who marries off his daughter without specification should not give her less than the clothing they assess for a poor Jewish woman — when the father was poor.

But if he was wealthy, it is fitting to give her according to his wealth.
