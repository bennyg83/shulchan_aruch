<b>רק בשעת הדחק. </b> עבה"ט וע' בס' ישועות יעקב סק"ב שהאריך בזה ומסיק לחלק דאם בשעה שקיבל בקנין הי' פנאי לכתוב והיה בכח האשה לבקש מהעדים שיכתבו לה דהא קיי"ל קנו מיניה לא צריך אימלוכי ביה מהני הקנין ואף שלא הלכה האשה לבקש מהעדים היא גופא ראיה דסמכא דעתה ולכן מהני הקנין כמו כתיבה אף בלא שעת הדחק וכדעת הב"י משא"כ אם באותו עת שקיבל בקנין לא היה אפשר לכתוב כגון בשבת או שאין פנאי לכתוב בזה אמרינן דלא סמכה דעתה בקנין ולא מהני רק כמו התפסת מטלטלין דלא מהני רק בשעת הדחק ע"ש:
**** ENGLISH ****
Only in a time of duress. See Ba'er Hetev and Yeshuot Yaakov s.k. 2, who elaborated and concluded to distinguish: if at the time he accepted kinyan he had leisure to write and the woman could have asked the witnesses to write for her — for we hold "they acquired from him" needs no further consultation — the kinyan helps even not in duress, per Beit Yosef's view; and even if she did not go ask the witnesses, she herself is proof she relied, so kinyan helps like writing even without duress. But if at that time he could not write — such as Shabbat or no leisure — we say she did not rely on kinyan and it helps only like seizing movable property, which helps only in duress. See there.

<b>גדולה כראשונה. </b> עיין בתשו' מוצל מאש סי' כ"ה שנשאל מי שאבדה שטר כתובתה וכתב לה בעלה כתובה אחרת והכתובה הראשונה היתה עשרת אלפים לבנים ועכשיו כתב לה חמשת אלפים ולא מיחת' האשה ולא קפדה כלל. אם אח"כ תמצא הכתובה הראשונה ותתגרש או תתאלמן אם נאמר שכתיבה הראשונה כבר נמחל שעבוד' או נאמר דכיון שהיתה בטעות שחשבה שהיא אבודה אבל עכשיו שנמצאת עדיין שעבוד החוב הראשון קיים. והשיב דאם בכתובה השני' אינו כתוב כלל בשביל נדוניא או תוספת אלא סתם כתב לה ה' אלפים לבנים מפני שאמרו חז"ל אסור לשהות עם אשתו שעה א' בלא כתובה לא מבעיא אם נמצאת אח"כ כתובה הא' דמעולם לא נמחל רק שעבוד המנה או מאתים הראשונים ועתה הרי פירש שבעבור האיסור עשה לה כתובה וזה אינו רק למנה ומאתים שזהו האיסור דוקא אבל נדוניא ושאר דברים שהם מלוה וחוב גמור עליו לא נמחל השעבוד בשביל שכתב כתובה אחרת בעבור המנה ומאתים ומה שכתב חמשת אלפים אע"ג שהי' מספיק המנה ומאתים יש לנו לומר שהוסיף לה כמ"ש ז"ל אם רצה להוסיף כו' ואפילו שהכתוב' הראשונ' לא נמצאת כלל אם אין הכחש' ביניהם רק שהבעל טוען שכיון שנכתב כתוב' אחרת נמחל שעבוד' לאו כל כמיני' אבל אם הזכיר בתוך הכתוב' החדש' שבשביל נדוניא ותוספת כתב לה זה והיא ידעה זה וקבילת הא ודאי דבטל' לה כתוב' הראשונ' ואפילו נמצאת חספא בעלמא הוא עכ"ד:
**** ENGLISH ****
As large as the first. See responsum Motzal Me'esh siman 25: a woman whose ketubah document was lost and her husband wrote another — the first was ten thousand, the second five thousand, and she did not protest. If the first is later found and she is divorced or widowed — is the first obligation waived, or since she thought it lost but now it is found the first debt still stands? He answered: if the second ketubah does not mention nedunya or addition but plainly writes five thousand because the Sages forbade remaining without ketubah — if the first is later found, the obligation was never waived; only the maneh or two hundred principal was waived, and he wrote the second only for the prohibition, which is only maneh and two hundred. But nedunya and other matters that are a full loan were not waived by writing another ketubah for maneh and two hundred; and the five thousand written — even if maneh and two hundred sufficed — we say he added as Rama wrote. Even if the first is never found, if there is no contradiction and the husband claims the second ketubah waived the first — that is not in his power. But if the new ketubah mentions nedunya and addition and she knew and accepted — certainly the first ketubah is void, even if only a shard is found. So he ruled.

**** ENGLISH ****
And for a widow a maneh. Dagul MeRevavah wrote: the questioner asks how much is a seduced woman's ketubah — see Tur siman 177, Rambam ch. 1 Laws of Na'arah halakhah 3, and Maggid Mishneh there; and it seems all this when she was a minor, but an adult who was seduced — certainly even per Rambam and Tur she has only a maneh even if the seducer himself marries her. These matters are stated at length in Noda B'Yehuda second edition siman 33; see there and below siman 177 s.k. 3. See also Chasam Sofer end of siman 133: regarding one who marries his own seduced woman I would write two hundred per Tur siman 177 until I found in Dagul MeRevavah that this applies only to a minor, not an adult who has only a maneh — the elder so ruled and so is proper. And there siman 125 regarding one who conceived soon after the wedding and they admit the pregnancy is from him without doubt of another man's nursing — a sage challenged that two hundred was written as for a virgin, and Dagul MeRevavah wrote that if she is an adult she has only a maneh (and thus the ketubah is invalid); he answered it is correct, even though we learn "if he wishes to add he may add" and Ran holds additions may be included in one sum with the ketubah plainly, not like Mordechai — nevertheless Ran agrees one cannot write "that which is fitting for you from the Torah," and Dagul MeRevavah's words are very correct for an adult who has no fine and whose first intercourse was with consent — Rambam also agrees she has only a maneh. Nevertheless in our case there is no concern, for all this is a generally seduced adult who later marries; but for his betrothed with conditions and penalties as customary, her heart was confident she would marry him and knowingly gave herself to him that he would marry her with two hundred like virgin bride-price — certainly she waived only with this understanding. And even if one stubbornly says if this seducer will not write two hundred we cannot compel him — at minimum if he wrote two hundred "that which is fitting for you," how can we say it is not fitting from him, for certainly it is fitting and he is obligated to write so. See there. And what Ba'er Hetev cited from R' Meir Mintz regarding one who raped a virgin and married her — see below siman 143 s.k. 6 for explanation of R' Meir Mintz's words.
