**** ENGLISH ****
How the claim of virginity works, and when she becomes forbidden and loses her ketubah. It contains 10 seifim.

Every virgin whose ketubah is two hundred has a claim of virginity. And everyone whose ketubah is a maneh, or for whom they did not enact ketubah — has no claim of virginity. And everyone who is alone with his betrothed before marriage — she has no claim of virginity.
