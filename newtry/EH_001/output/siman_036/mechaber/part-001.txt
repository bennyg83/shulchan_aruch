**** ENGLISH ****
All laws of agency for a woman. It contains 12 seifim.

A woman may appoint an agent to receive her kiddushin. It is a mitzvah that she be betrothed by herself if she can; but there is no prohibition, as with a man. And the man says to the agent: "Behold so-and-so whom you sent is betrothed to me," and the agent says "I betrothed her to you." If he said "yes" — sufficient; and even if he was silent, if they were engaged in that matter and he gave to the agent and did not explain and said nothing — sufficient. If he betroths her with a document he does not write it except with the agent's knowledge. Some say he does not write it except with her knowledge.

**** ENGLISH ****
One who betrothed via agent should not betroth again at time of chuppah, so as not to cast aspersion on the first kiddushin that they will say kiddushin of an agent are nothing (and see end of siman 34). If she retracted and cancelled the agency before he accepted the kiddushin — it is void and she is not betrothed. If it is unknown whether she cancelled the agency before acceptance of kiddushin or afterward — she is doubtfully betrothed.

**** ENGLISH ****
If his agent received kiddushin from one man and she herself received kiddushin from another, and it is unknown whether the kiddushin his agent received preceded hers or hers preceded — she is forbidden to both and requires a get from both. If they wish, one gives a get and one marries her (and betroths her with other kiddushin) (Rabbenu Yerucham 22). When do these words apply? Among distant relatives; but if the agent betrothed her to a father and she herself to a son, or to a brother — both give a get and she is forbidden to both.
