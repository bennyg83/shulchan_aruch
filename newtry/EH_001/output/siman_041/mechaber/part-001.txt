Law of one who betroths two sisters or many women among whom are gentile women. It contains 4 seifim.

A man may betroth several women at once, provided he gives a perutah to each and every one; and one of them may receive the kiddushin on behalf of all of them with their consent.

**** ENGLISH ****
One who betroths two women at once whom it is forbidden for him to marry due to forbidden relations — they are not betrothed. How so? Such as if he betrothed a woman and her daughter, or two sisters at once when he said "behold both of you are betrothed to me" — they are not betrothed and none of them needs a get. But if he betrothed only one and did not specify which — such as if he said to the father "one of your daughters is betrothed to me" and the father accepted the kiddushin; or if two or three sisters appointed one of them as agent for her fellow to receive her kiddushin and he placed it in her hand and said to her "one of you is betrothed to me" — all of them need a get from him and it is forbidden to have relations with any of them, lest she be his wife's sister.

**** ENGLISH ****
One who betrothed many women at once among whom were two sisters or a woman and her daughter — if he said "the one among you fit for intercourse is betrothed to me" — behold all of them are betrothed to him except two sisters or a woman and her daughter and the like. And if he said "all of you are betrothed to me" — gentile women are betrothed and sisters are not betrothed. And some say even gentile women are not betrothed; therefore gentile women are doubtfully betrothed. And if he said "one of all of you is betrothed to me" — all of them, whether gentile women or sisters, are doubtfully betrothed.
