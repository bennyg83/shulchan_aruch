They fine him, etc. — Perishah challenged: why different from bestiality forbidden to tell gentile breed unlike kind and if transgressed don't fine to sell and offspring permitted to keep — answered castration different because animal before us and will enjoy prohibition therefore fined unlike bestiality prohibition not recognizable; if because offspring — offspring born has life independently — didn't fine; Bechorot cow that damaged pays from offspring — why body itself; rather main as Yoreh De'ah 298 s.b. bestiality with unlike kind uncommon even regarding gentile — didn't decree fine; also seems castration at time of sin clear will be praised for eating and plowing unlike bestiality at time of sin not clear praise perhaps won't conceive.
