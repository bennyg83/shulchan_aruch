**** ENGLISH ****
Who is petzua dakah? Anyone whose testicles were wounded; and kruat shafcha — anyone whose gid was cut. A male can be disqualified in three organs: the gid, the testicles, and the ducts in which the seminal layer is cooked — called testicular cords (chutei beitzim). Once one of these three organs is wounded, cut, or crushed — he is disqualified. How is the gid wounded or crushed or cut? If the atarah or above the atarah was cut — disqualified; but if cut from the head of the atarah and a remainder of even a hair-thread's width surrounds the entire gid — kosher.

Forbidden to tell a Kutit to castrate our animal; and if he took it himself and castrated — permitted; and if an Israelite deceived in this matter — they fine him (even if he did not deceive and the Kutit recognizes him and intends for his benefit) and he sells to another Israelite; and even to his adult son it is permitted to sell, but to his minor son he does not sell or give it. {Rama: Permitted to give an animal to a Kutit for half-wages even though the Kutit will certainly castrate it (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 16 of Avodah Zarah) — for the Kutit acts at his own risk (Beit Yosef); and permitted to sell animals and roosters to Kutim even though the Kutit certainly buys them to castrate (Beit Shmuel, Chelkat Mechokek, Turei Zahav); and some forbid — explained in Teshuvat HaRosh siman 294; but if the buying Kutit does not castrate them himself but only gives to another Kutit to castrate — all permit (there as well); anything needed for healing or other matters has no prohibition of tzaar baalei chayim (Or Zarua siman 59); therefore permitted to pluck feathers from live geese and no concern for tzaar baalei chayim (Maharai siman 105); nevertheless people refrain, for it is cruelty.}
