**** ENGLISH ****
(ב) <b>Until he has all the signs.</b> Do not ask how it is possible for him to have signs, since he has a beard. Tosafot answered that it is not called a beard unless his beard is full, and all the poskim agreed to this, unlike Raavad, who holds that when he has two hairs in the beard he is no longer called a saris. Tosafot's intent in what they wrote, "he has a beard," is that his beard is full; that is, if his beard is full, it is no longer possible to say that he has all the signs of a saris, and if so he is not a saris. But one can still say that nevertheless he does not have the law of an adult, but remains a minor until most of his years. Therefore I do not understand what Beit Yosef wrote at the beginning of siman 169, on the Tur's statement that if his beard is full one need not examine further; he wrote that he learned this from the above Tosafot. In truth, nothing of this is implied from that Tosafot.
