**** ENGLISH ****
The [primacy of] commandment is on the eldest brother to marry [{Rama: or release]. If he doesn't want [Rama: or he can't free her or marry her, e.g he's married to her sister (Rivash 159)] [the commandment] returns to each brother in the order of their age. If none of them want, we return to the eldest and say to him: the commandment is yours, either free her or marry her. If he doesn't want to free or marry or free her, we force him to free her, but we don't force him to marry her. {Rama: even if the eldest had taken an oath not to marry and not to free her, we force him and not the younger brother. In any case we free the eldest of the oath, even though it never took hold, because of the way it appears (Rivash 159).}

לא רצה הגדול לייבם אלא לחלוץ והקטן ממנו רוצה לייבם <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="ח" data-order="8"></i>ביאת קטן עדיפא אבל אם אין שניהם רוצים לייבם אלא לחלוץ <i data-commentator="Beit Shmuel" data-order="7"></i><i data-commentator="Pithei Teshuva" data-order="3"></i>חליצת הגדול <i data-commentator="Ba'er Hetev" data-order="5"></i>עדיפא: <small>הגה וע"ל סי' קס"ה דבזמן הזה מצות חליצה קודמת ואפי' למ"ד יבום קודם אם היא אינה חפיצה ביבום רק בחליצה חליצת הגדול קודם (תה"ד סי' ר"כ) .</small>
**** ENGLISH ****
If the older [brother] did not want to do yibbum preferring halitza, and the younger [brother] wants to do yibbum: sexual relations with the younger is preferable. But, if neither of them wishes to do yibbum, rather, halitza, the halitza of the elder is preferred. {Rama: see below paragraph 165 - that in our times halitza is given priority. If she is not desirous of yibbum, only halitza, then the halitza of the elder is preferred (Trumat HaDeshen paragraph 220).}

<i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="י" data-order="10"></i>היו לו ארבעה אחים ומתו ונפלו נשותיהם לפניו <i data-commentator="Be'er HaGolah" data-label="כ" data-order="11"></i>אם הוא עשיר ויכול לזונן אם ירצה לייבם את כולם מייבם ואם לאו חולץ למי שירצה מהם ומייבם למי שירצה מהם אחת מכל בית:
