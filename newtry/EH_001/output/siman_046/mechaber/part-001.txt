**** ENGLISH ****
Law of rumor (kola) in kiddushin and whether the rumor is nullified. It contains 8 seifim.

An unmarried woman about whom a rumor spread that she was betrothed today to so-and-so in this city — we are concerned for her and behold she is doubtfully betrothed, even though there is no clear evidence. And any rumor that was not upheld in beit din — we are not concerned for it. And what is the rumor that upholds her as betrothed? Such as if two witnesses came and testified that they saw candles burning and beds spread and people entering and leaving and women rejoicing for her and saying so-and-so was betrothed. But if they heard them saying so-and-so shall be betrothed today — we are not concerned for her, lest they happened to arrange betrothal and she was not betrothed.

{Rama: And likewise if they said she was betrothed plainly without saying to so-and-so, or they said she was betrothed in another city — it is not called a rumor (Tur).}

And likewise if two came and said, "We saw something like an engagement celebration," or "We heard a rumor," or "We heard from so-and-so who heard from so-and-so that so-and-so was betrothed before so-and-so and so-and-so," and the witnesses went to another country or died — behold this is a rumor that upholds her as betrothed.

{Rama: Some say it is not a rumor unless two heard from two, but one from two is not (Tur in name of Rosh, Ran, and Maggid Mishneh in name of Rashba). We are not concerned for a rumor unless it was upheld in beit din — that beit din investigated the matter and heard there is substance to the rumor, as explained; but as long as it was not upheld in beit din we are not concerned, unless it is a place where there is a strong presumption she was betrothed — then we do not require upholding in beit din (Maharik root 87). And beit din may investigate the rumor to clarify whether there is substance (Beit Yosef in name of Tashbetz and Rashba).}

**** ENGLISH ****
A rumor spread that she is betrothed to so-and-so, and another came and betrothed her before us — we examine the first kiddushin that are by rumor. If clear witnesses came with clear sight that she is betrothed to the first — the second kiddushin are nothing. And if the second divorced her — the first may not marry her, lest people say he remarried his divorcee from betrothal after she was betrothed to another. And if the second married her before the first divorced her — behold she is forbidden to him forever, even after the first divorces her (and see above siman 30 seif 4).
