**** ENGLISH ****
That there is no chazakah in a married woman's property. It contains 2 seifim.

A man has no chazakah in his wife's melog property through two who ate — even if he stipulated with her that he has no produce from her property. And likewise a woman has no chazakah in her husband's property through two who ate.

{Rama: And specifically regarding such chazakah; but regarding chazakah of damages such as opening windows and the like — a man has chazakah in his wife's property, and likewise a woman against her husband (R' Yitzchak in Meisharim siman 123, TaZ) (and see Choshen Mishpat siman 149).}

**** ENGLISH ****
One who took chazakah in a married woman's property — his chazakah does not take effect unless he held them three years after her husband's death. (And see Choshen Mishpat siman 149.)
