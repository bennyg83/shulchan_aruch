**** ENGLISH ****
(ה) <b>Like other, etc.</b> Rashi there. The Ramban, Rashba, and Rosh wrote that against his will, from the law of condition, we do not require him to give, because whenever one says “on condition,” we do not obligate him to fulfill it. Rather, if he fulfills his condition, the act stands, etc. A proof is from what is stated in Gittin 74 and 75 regarding Hillel’s enactment, etc. If it were otherwise, since he became obligated in the money, it would be obvious that payment against his will is considered payment, as stated in Bava Metzia 73b, “he paid him the money,” etc. See Tosafot in Gittin there, s.v. miklal, etc. Rather, here, since the chalitzah cannot become void, he is obligated by the law of wages.
