<b>When to bless the blessing of bridegrooms and if they eat in many houses. It contains 13 seifim.</b>

One must bless the blessing of bridegrooms in the groom's house before marriage; and they are six blessings. And if wine is there he brings a cup of wine and blesses on the wine first and arranges all of them over the cup — and behold he blesses seven blessings. And if wine is not available he blesses on beer.

**** ENGLISH ****
One who dines in the groom's house from when they begin to occupy themselves with needs of the wedding meal and prepare it until thirty days after the marriage blesses Blessed is He whose joy is in His abode that we ate of his if there were ten he blesses Blessed is our God whose joy is in His abode etc. and they answer Blessed is our God whose joy etc. And likewise a meal they make after marriage because of the marriage until twelve months he blesses whose joy is in His abode; and nowadays joy has become sweet and they do not say whose joy is in His abode except during the seven days of the feast; nevertheless it appears that even for a widower who married a widow they say whose joy is in His abode during the three days he rejoices with her.
