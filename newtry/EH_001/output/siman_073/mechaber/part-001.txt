**** ENGLISH ****
He is obligated in clothing, dwelling, and household utensils for his wife. It contains 9 seifim.

Her clothing — how so? He must give her garments fit for her in rainy season and in sunny season, no less than any housewife wears in that country. And if in that place it is not customary for a woman to go out to market until a head-covering covers her entire body — he gives her the least head-covering of all head-coverings. (And a woman should not accustom herself to go out much, for a woman's beauty is only in sitting in corners of her house.) (Tur) And included in clothing that he must give her are household utensils and the dwelling where she sits; and what are household utensils? A spread bed and a mat (meaning like a mat) or mat to sit on, and eating and drinking vessels such as pot, bowl, barrel, cruse, lamp, cup, bottle, and the like.
