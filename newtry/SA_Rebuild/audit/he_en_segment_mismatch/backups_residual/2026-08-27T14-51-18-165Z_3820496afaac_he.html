and for this reason also on Yom Tov one does not redeem; and likewise in Rivash's responsum siman 257. It appears one redeems at night too, and likewise chapter 1 of Kiddushin page 29 end 1a and Rashi and Tosafot there; however practice is to redeem by day; however where the 31st day falls on a public fast day one must see if the son reached 29 days 12 hours 993 parts before the fast day which is the 30th day — he must redeem on the 30th day after 29 days 12 hours 993 parts as below end s.k. 19 (and one can say because of the meal alone we do not precede redeeming on the 30th day); and if not, then redeem on the night of the 31st of the fast day, but do not redeem on the fast day and make a meal the night after the fast, for there is no publicity thus; and as Maharai wrote in Teshuvot HaGeonim siman 269 regarding when the 31st falls on Shabbat: if they redeem on Shabbat eve the redemption does not take effect until after thirty days — how make the meal to publicize the mitzvah while the son is not redeemed? And if they make the feast on Shabbat when already redeemed — who says there is publicity thus when redemption is not visible at the meal? So who? I heard practice is to redeem on the fast day and make the meal the night after the fast, and one can divide in Maharai's case) — requires study. In responsum of R. Eliyahu ben Chaim siman 79 on a firstborn child (circumcised) [sick] who did not recover by the 31st day — it appears that though the mitzvah of pidyon haben is performed primarily at its time, circumcision mitzvah should be preceded because circumcision is the sign of entering covenant, and without accepting covenant there is no obligation for pidyon at all; and he gives the kohen five sela within thirty so he be redeemed after thirty days; and regarding blessing he does not bless before thirty as Maharai wrote in his writings siman 231 in the ruling when the 31st arrives on Shabbat: and furthermore I challenge the blessing that the father bless on erev Shabbat for the time of obligation has not yet arrived, etc. — end quote; and not clear, for if so how bless after thirty since immediately upon entry of the 31st day the son is redeemed and his mitzvah already performed; and Maharai himself wrote in Teshuvot HaGeonim siman 269 on the ruling when the 31st arrives on Shabbat: on erev Shabbat he does not bless since time of obligation not yet arrived, and on Shabbat not appropriate to bless for he does nothing now, and automatically the son is redeemed at entry of Shabbat which is the 31st day — end quote; and though one can say here since the mitzvah's main time it is appropriate to do so, nevertheless it seems we should not divide, for since his mitzvah was already performed he should not bless afterward, as I brought above siman 19 s.k. 3: all mitzvot one blesses before performing, and if he did not bless beforehand he does not bless afterward; and also not reasonable to give money before thirty for several reasons; therefore circumcise first on the 31st day and afterward redeem and bless.
דאין לעשות כו'. משום דאין מערבין שמחה בשמחה: **** ENGLISH **** For one should not do, etc. — because one does not mix one joy with another joy.
ויש מתירין כו'. וכן כתב המחבר בא"ח סי' תקמ"ו סעיף ד': **** ENGLISH **** And some permit, etc. — and the Mechaber wrote in Orach Chaim siman 546 seif 4.
וכן עיקר. וכן נוהגין דהא אין מברכין שהשמחה במעונו כן כתב העט"ז ואין זה ראיה דהא סעודת אירוסין אסור לעשות בחולו של מועד כמו שכתב הט"ו בא"ח ר"ס תקמ"ו אע"ג דאין מברכין שהשמחה במעונו אלא הטעם מבואר בדברי התוס' שם דלא אסור לערב שמחה בשמחה אלא בשמחת נשואין לבד ול"ד שמחת נשואין קאמרי אלא ה"ה סעודת אירוסין נמי דשייך בה נשואין: **** ENGLISH **** And such is essential — and so practiced, for one does not bless shehasimchah beme'ono; so wrote Taz; and this is not proof, for an engagement feast is forbidden on chol hamoed as Taz wrote in Orach Chaim siman 546, even though one does not bless shehasimchah beme'ono; rather the reason explained in Tosafot there: forbidden to mix joy with joy only for wedding joy alone; and some say they speak only of wedding joy — rather likewise engagement feast in which marriage is included.
