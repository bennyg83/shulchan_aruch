"afilu b'sahad," in what they wrote "and nevertheless we do not say that salt that was forbidden due to blood," etc.; and likewise Ran in the sugya of a drop of milk also labored to resolve this difficulty of Tosafot — behold they all adopted that it is fit to say the salt became neveilah and forbids the meat; and likewise it is explained in Hagah (above siman 105) and in Pri Megadim there — and it requires study.
