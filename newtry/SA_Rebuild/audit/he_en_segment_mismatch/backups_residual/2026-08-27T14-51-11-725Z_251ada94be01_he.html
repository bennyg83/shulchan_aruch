כגון ד' פנים בהדי הדדי. פרש"י פני שור ואדם ואריה ונשר לחיה א' דוגמת חיות הקודש כדכתיב לא תעשון אתי השרויות אצלי ע"כ ונראה דמ"ש שור הוא ל"ד דהא אמרי' בחגיגה (דף י"ב ע"ב) בא יחזקאל והפכו לכרוב אלא נקט שור משום דבשעה שנאמר המקרא דלא תעשון אתי היה עדיין שור ואה"נ דהשתא דאיכא כרוב אסור לעשות ד' פניהם בהדי הדדי דהא מ"מ בכלל לא תעשון אתי השרויות אצלי הוא א"נ קמ"ל דאף עתה דאין שור במרכבה אפ"ה אסור לעשות ד' פנים בהדי הדדי ושור ביניהם שהיה בשעה שנאמר המקרא:
וכן צורת אדם לבדו. נפקא מדכתיב לא תעשון אתי אותי כלומר דמות שאני מתראה בו לנביאים והיינו אדם וא"ת הא דאמרינן לעיל דד' פנים להדדי אסור תיפוק ליה משום אדם כבר תירצו התוס' בעבודת כוכבים (דף מ"ג ע"א) ובר"ה (דף כ"ד) בד"ה לא תעשון אתי דאתי לחיובא אף כשמוצא צורת אדם והשלים לד' פנים וכן תירץ בהגהת סמ"ק סי' קס"ב א"נ לעבור בשני לאוין ובר"ה תירצו עוד דסד"א דאם עשה ד' פנים גרע מאדם לבדו וכן תירץ הר"ן והרמ"ך הביאו הכ"מ ספ"ג מהל' עבודת כוכבים וע' בס"ק ל': **** ENGLISH **** And likewise human form alone. Derived from "do not make with Me" — "with Me" — likeness I show prophets — meaning human. And if you challenge: above we said four faces together forbidden — derive from human? Tosafot already answered in Avodah Zarah (43a) and Rosh Hashanah (24) on "do not make with Me" — it comes to obligate even finding human form who completed to four faces; and so answered in hagahah Semak siman 162 — "or to transgress two prohibitions"; and in Rosh Hashanah they further answered: he thought if he made four faces it is worse than human alone; and so answered the Ran and Ramach — Kesef Mishneh brought them laws of idolatry; and see s.k. 30.
אסור לעשותם אפילו לנוי. וכ"כ ברמב"ם פ"ג ובספרי המחברים הישנים כתוב אפי' לעובד כוכבים וכן הוא בטור ואלו ואלו דברי אלהים חיים דכיון דקפיד קרא אעשייה אין חילוק בין עושה לנוי או לעבודת כוכבי' אסור: **** ENGLISH **** Forbidden to make them even for ornament. And so Rambam chapter 3 and in old authors' books written even for a gentile; and so in the Tur; and both are words of the Living God, for since the verse was particular about making, there is no distinction between making for ornament or idolatry — forbidden.
אסור להשהותן. משום חשדא וכבר נתבאר בס"ק י"ז דה"ה דאסור לו' לעובד כוכבים לעשותן אפילו אינו רוצה להשהותם משום דאמירה לעובד כוכבים שבות אפילו בדבר שאינו של שבת: **** ENGLISH **** Forbidden to leave them. Because of suspicion; and it was already explained in s.k. 17 that likewise forbidden to tell a gentile to make them even if he does not want to leave them, because telling a gentile is rabbinically forbidden even regarding a matter that is not of Shabbat.

with human form — if its seal protrudes permitted to sign, if sunken permitted to leave; reason: forbidden only similar to them that are protruding; and from Torah what is depicted on a wall and board — although flat, called sunken since not protruding like themselves; therefore sun, moon, and similar forms that are also sunken in the firmament forbidden even sunken. The Magen Avraham wrote: specifically those forbidden only in making because of "do not make with Me" — in finding permitted because there is no way to make them for idolatry — not forbidden in making except protruding; but sun and moon customarily worshipped — forbidden in making, finding, protruding, and sunken; for in finding forbidden lest worshipped; and since we suspect perhaps idolatry was done, even sunken there is concern; and in making although no Torah prohibition except protruding since they appear protruding in firmament — nevertheless forbidden to make even sunken because of suspicion of idolatry — end; and his words are wondrous, hanging a baraita on what is not taught; and what relevance finding permitted to sunken permitted, and vice versa. Investigate yourself: if there is proof in four faces together or human made for worship forbidden in finding — shall we say therefore forbidden in making and leaving even sunken? But we forbid only protruding; and vice versa sun and moon nowadays permitted in finding — shall we say permitted sunken? Certainly not; and further already explained in s.k. 8 sun and moon literally — their circle permitted for finder and nevertheless forbidden to make even sunken — see there; and further wonder how he changed the known that sun and moon appear sunken in firmament — as Tosafot, Rosh, Ran, Semag, Tzeda Laderech, Shiltei HaGibborim, and other decisors wrote — he wrote they are protruding; and it seems the Tur misled him — the Tur wrote: it appears all those forbidden in finding forbidden even sunken, since we suspect they were made for an idol, even sunken there is concern — end; he thinks meaning all those forbidden in finding because customary to worship them — sun and moon although protruding (if sunken anyway forbidden) forbidden sunken even in making; and the matter is simple this interpretation does not exist; rather meaning wherever we forbid in finding — meaning because there is proof for an idol they were made — no distinction in finding between protruding and sunken; since we forbid for finder because made for idol, if so forbidden for finder both sunken and protruding; but making and leaving do not depend at all on what is forbidden in finding or not; and as he wrote regarding making and leaving everything depends on the thing itself — if human and similar that is protruding, forbidden only protruding; if sunken forbidden even sunken; and it is simple — I did not need to extend except to remove from the reader's heart in his book: And it appears all forms we forbid are forbidden only in a complete form — such as human form with two eyes and complete nose and whole body and similar; but not half the depiction as some painters paint one side — it appears to me.
