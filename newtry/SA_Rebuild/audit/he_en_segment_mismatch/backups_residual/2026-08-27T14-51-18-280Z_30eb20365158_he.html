דראוי להרצאה וה"ה הכא אבל כשאינו ראוי לא להרצאה ולא לאכילה ודאי דאפי' כלו לו חדשיו מונין לו מיום ח' וכדאמרן: **** ENGLISH **** One may keep it all twelve months — from its birth if its months ended, or until its year is completed from the eighth day if its months did not end; so wrote Taz from Beit Yosef's words (and likewise in Perishah); but in truth from Rambam and Semag asin 211 (which is the Mechaber's wording) it does not appear so, for they wrote at the beginning: if born blemished one counts from the day it was born, etc., for it is fit for eating on the day of its birth — implying specifically born blemished; and since they were silent here and did not divide, it implies since not born blemished one always counts from the eighth day of its birth, for though known its months ended, nevertheless it was not fit for eating on the day of its birth for it was unblemished; and likewise Rashi and Tosafot: why does the Gemara in Rosh Hashanah chapter 1 page 7 say "if unblemished (count from eighth day), if blemished (count from birth)" — blemished, who can eat it since its months are known? Rashi: blemished born with its blemish and stands to be slaughtered outside from the day born it is fit for this and we count from birth — end quote; and Tosafot: if originally unblemished and became blemished, since not fit for eating we count from time of fitness (meaning eighth day when fit for offering) — end quote; and if not so the Gemara's question returns: blemished, who can eat it? And in truth the wording "may keep until completion of its year from birth" that Tur wrote here is strained for Beit Yosef and supporters, and therefore they wrote so; and it would imply to them that the Gemara's question "blemished who can eat" is because perhaps it fell prematurely; but because it was unblemished we are not concerned, for afterward it became blemished — for otherwise we count from eighth day when blemish developed within its year though after eighth day it was unblemished; but in truth it appears Tur has a textual error and should read "may keep until completion of year from eighth day of birth," for Tur also wrote at the beginning: born with its blemish if we know within it its months ended, etc. — evidently specifically born with blemish; moreover otherwise he should combine and give all in one passage; moreover for what purpose write "from birth" — it should be closed; moreover according to this it must deal where its months ended and this is not in the wording; and if he relies on what he wrote at the beginning — then he need not write "from birth" at all; rather plainly Tur has textual error and should read "from eighth day of birth"; and on the contrary he comes to inform us that here there is no distinction and always count a year from eighth day of birth since born unblemished and afterward a blemish developed; however from eighth day we count even though then not fit for eating for not worse than unblemished counted from eighth day because fit for offering, and likewise here; but when not fit for offering or eating, certainly even if months ended we count from eighth day as we said.
