Beur HaTur; and in She'elat Yaavetz part 1 siman 101 regarding silver that one person printed in Amsterdam at the reception of the Gaon the pious R. Eliezer z"l — it was a head form to the chest with a complete protruding face — certainly not made with intent that this is absolute prohibition, even if others made for him; all the more here where Israel is the maker etc.; and see Tosafot Bava Kamma on Abraham our father's coin etc. — see there. And see there how his father the Gaon, called from the congregation of Sephardim, had in his hand to merit them to receive the light of His countenance, and his peaceful men could not seduce him and turn him from the Supreme opinion to allow drawing his portrait and anointing with oil; even though in color depiction most decisors as all hold there is no prohibition concern — all the more half a face alone there is no concern in such a case — nevertheless he did not want, although they pleaded much because of his piety and holiness; and they did not rest; and through a wondrous craftsman all depiction of his awesome face was made with tremendous alacrity not in his presence; and the light of his countenance did not fall and nothing of his likeness was lacking except the breath of life; and forms were made from it etc. — see there. And see in part 2 siman 104 regarding a Chanukah lamp with a protruding recognizable half human body form — it must be broken and removed, although it was in a great man's hand one should not heed honoring him — separating from prohibition is permitted even in his lifetime, and all the more after his death it is like an error etc.; and we also say in Chullin "and he smashed the copper serpent" etc. "rather he left the place" etc. — see there.
