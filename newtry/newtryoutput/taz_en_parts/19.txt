Kli sheini that contains water — the Gemara has it in chapter Keirah: Rabbi said to Rabbi Shimon bar Avdimi, take from kli sheini and give — we wrote in Yoreh De’ah siman 92; there it was explained that if one draws with an empty vessel from kli sheini, that drawing vessel has the status of kli rishon for stringency, not leniency; and above siman 258 we wrote one should not bury the whole vessel in kli sheini — that would be hatmanah; it must be exposed above; and the Mordechai in chapter Keirah wrote one may not put fruit inside the kli sheini of the oven in the winter house to warm — no less than water.
