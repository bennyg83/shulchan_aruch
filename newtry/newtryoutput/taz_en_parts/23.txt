Even in a pot, etc. — Mahariv wrote there one should not stir with a spoon — it is like cooking — as said in the first chapter of Shabbat “the cauldron overturned and coated” — and I saw so in Mahari’s house — end quote. What we say from the first chapter of Shabbat is not proof — there we deal with wool to a cauldron which is stricter as written nearby — but here already fully cooked — permitted — so explicitly siman 21 regarding wood chips in the pot — permitted because already fully cooked — only because of excessive distance he erred — therefore it appears to me simple removal without stirring in the pot only to take a piece — no concern here even if not fully cooked — forbidden even removal where one might stir — but when fully finished one need not be stringent on removal only if he meant to warm to turn in the pot — one who wants to be stringent — but siman 21 permits even lechatchilah — see there.
