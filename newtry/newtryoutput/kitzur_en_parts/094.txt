If it has not cooled completely: That is, the Rema rules in principle like the lenient authorities, that one may heat a dish even if it contains sauce. However, because of the importance of the stringent authorities, he forbids it in practice if the dish cooled completely. Therefore, according to the Rema, a dish with sauce that is lukewarm and no longer yad soledet bo has no prohibition of cooking; according to the Mechaber it is forbidden to cook it, and the prohibition is biblical.
