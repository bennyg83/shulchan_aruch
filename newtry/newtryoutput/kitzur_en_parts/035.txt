The completion of their preparation: Their rinsing from a kli rishon completes their preparation, and one is liable for cooking them even by pouring from a kli rishon. The rule is that a kli sheini does not cook, except for certain items that cook easily. Our own observation shows that the vast majority of foods do not cook even in a kli rishon that has been removed from the fire, and we have not seen anyone cook in a kli sheini.
