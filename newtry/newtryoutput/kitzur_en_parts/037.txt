Completion of preparation: It is forbidden because it completes the cooking process. It appears in practice that a food which we see cooks in a kli sheini and becomes edible through the heat of the kli sheini would be forbidden even according to the Mechaber. This situation is very rare, and exists for example in preparing instant foods such as couscous, which becomes edible even in a kli sheini. As for preparing foods called instant meals, such as pasta or mashed potato cups that become ready to eat by pouring boiling water over them, one should know that the main ingredients in these products are already cooked, aside from spices that are sometimes uncooked. Therefore, they may be prepared on Shabbat in a kli sheini, because a kli sheini does not cook.
