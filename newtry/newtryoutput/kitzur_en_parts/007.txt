Immediately: That is, when the act was done unwittingly, the Sages forbade benefit from it during Shabbat itself so that people would not come to treat Shabbat prohibitions lightly. However, they did not distinguish between the person who erred and others.
All this applies to a Torah-level melachah. For a rabbinic prohibition, if it was done intentionally it is forbidden like a Torah-level melachah; but if one violated a rabbinic prohibition unwittingly, one may benefit from it even on Shabbat itself.
