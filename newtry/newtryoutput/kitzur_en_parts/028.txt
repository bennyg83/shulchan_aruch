Mitztamek veyafeh lo: According to this view, the prohibition of cooking a moist dish that has cooled applies only when further cooking improves it. But if it is mitztamek vera lo, there is no cooking after cooking even if it cooled. Accordingly, a dish with a little sauce is presumably one in which he does not want the sauce to evaporate, so it is considered mitztamek vera lo and may be warmed on the plata. The same applies to similar cases. In practice, one may rely on this view.
