And it has the status of muktzeh: The later authorities questioned these words of the Rema: why should the small amount that grew on Shabbat not be nullified to the fruit itself? It appears that this refers to vegetables that grow quickly, such as gourds, where the addition that grew on Shabbat is significant, and therefore the fruit is muktzeh. But if a fruit such as an orange was detached on Shabbat for a dangerously ill person and the patient ate one segment from it, a healthy person may eat the rest of the fruit.
