Introduction to the section: Part of the cooking process is stirring. Therefore, stirring an uncooked dish that is on the fire is forbidden as cooking by Torah law. The Sages also decreed not to remove food with a ladle, lest one stir it. With a dish that is fully cooked, there is no prohibition of cooking, and according to the Mechaber one may even stir it; however, according to the Rema the Sages forbade this. The details of the laws of stirring are presented in this section.
