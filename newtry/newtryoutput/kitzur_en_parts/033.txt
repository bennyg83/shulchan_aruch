One may rinse it with hot water on Shabbat: That is, one may pour water over it from a kli rishon, because pouring from a kli rishon does not cook. The Mechaber's words in section 10, that one may not pour from a kli rishon onto spices, refer specifically to very fine spices, where there is concern for cooking through pouring. This is the Mechaber's view and the explanation of his words here, but in practice the consensus of the poskim is to forbid all pouring from a kli rishon.
Some poskim, including the Magen Avraham and Mishnah Berurah 35, forbid any soaking in a kli sheini because it looks like cooking, and they explain the Mechaber as referring to rinsing from a kli sheini. Therefore, many Ashkenazic poskim forbid soaking in a kli sheini, except for spices that are added to give flavor and not to be cooked. One who is lenient like the first view has an authority to rely on.
