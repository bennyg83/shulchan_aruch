Tosafot wrote: Rashbam holds forbidden only to put spices into the first vessel itself; permitted to put spices into the bowl and pour first vessel onto them — pouring from first vessel is not like first vessel; Rabbi Yehudah forbids — pouring is like first vessel. Rosh and Ran wrote Rabbenu Tam forbids; Ran’s words lean toward Rashbam; Rosh’s toward Rabbenu Tam; Rabbeinu Yerucham chapter 22 wrote Rashi explained pouring like second vessel — and so seems from Rambam’s wording; some commentators permitted — this seems Ramban and Rashba for halacha; practically one should be stringent like Rashi — end quote.
