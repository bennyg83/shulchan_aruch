A first vessel cooks even after removal from the fire; therefore forbidden to put even ox meat… permitted to put salt… Keirah (42a): We learned: pan and pot removed boiling — one may not put spices inside; but into bowl or plate one may. Rashi: removed from fire — boiling — between twilight — one may not put spices after dark because first vessel while boiling cooks; “into bowl” — second vessel does not cook. Gemara: Rav Yosef thought salt is like spices — first vessel cooks, second not — Abaye: Rabbi Chiya taught salt is not like spices — even third vessel cooks — disputes Rabbi Nachman who said salt needs cooking like ox meat. Another version Abaye: Rabbi Chiya salt not like spices — even first vessel does not cook — that is Rabbi Nachman. Rif and Rosh ruled like latter version; Rambam chapter 22; Tosafot — except they concluded: stringent like first version not to put salt in bowl where “yad soledet” — good.

Rosh: from here beef does not cook in first vessel — permitted to put raw meat into first vessel removed from fire — however if heavily salted old possibly forbidden — cooks quickly as said above; even newly salted — although meat does not cook, moisture blood and salt inside cook and absorb into meat — forbidden.

Ran wrote: salt even first vessel does not cook — after removal from fire; if on fire even beef cooks — obvious.

Shvilei HaLeket in Rabbeinu Simchah’s name: salt put in boiling cauldron intentionally Shabbat — I see no prohibition except “davar she’yesh lo matirin” — not leaven/spice reasoning — for taste purposes where taste not nullified like other prohibitions — Chullin 99a yeast… “zeh v’zeh gorem” — surely salted erev but not fully salted… from matirin should not forbid — nullifies before prohibition by cooking — needs salt cooking like beef — once dissolved nullified — end quote; Hagahot Mordekhai: likely not called first vessel unless removed from fire immediately; if left standing to cool boiling becomes second — from Keirah 38a placing pitcher opposite bonfire anywhere “yad soledet” counts first vessel — even second vessel hand burns — not first vessel law because different — first vessel heated by fire itself — Tosafot Keirah “second vessel does not cook” — even “yad soledet” walls not hot continuously cooling.
