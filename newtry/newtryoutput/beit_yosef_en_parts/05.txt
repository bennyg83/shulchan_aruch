But Ba’al HaTerumah wrote in siman 248 that Rabbi Meir permits only when one cooked unintentionally on Shabbat, because originally [the food] was fit for drinking; but slaughter or other melachot where originally it was not fit at all are forbidden to everyone that same day — so too Semag and Semak. Even though in our teacher’s works it is written “forbidden to him that Shabbat,” which might imply that for others it is permitted, that is not so: since the reason is that originally it was not fit at all, even for others it is forbidden — and Sefer HaTerumah wrote this explicitly.
