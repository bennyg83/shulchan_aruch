So too permitted to put pitcher in second vessel… Keirah (38a): Rabbi Yitzchak bar Avdimi — once I entered after Rabbi to bathhouse — asked to place oil flask in basin — told me take from water [into] second vessel and put — Rashi: take from water — cooled somewhat — second vessel does not cook — then put flask into that second vessel. Tosafot — second vessel even “yad soledet” permitted — walls not continuously hot and cooling.

Place of this law is reversed — our teacher should have written it before “permitted pitcher of water” — those laws connect above.
