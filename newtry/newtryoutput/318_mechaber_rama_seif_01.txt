OC 318 · Mechaber & Rama · Seif 1 (א)

הַמְבַשֵּׁל בְּשַׁבָּת, {אוֹ שֶׁעָשָׂה אַחַת מִשְּׁאָר מְלָאכוֹת (טוּר)},
בְּמֵזִיד, אָסוּר לוֹ לְעוֹלָם, וְלַאֲחֵרִים מֻתָּר לְמוֹצָאֵי שַׁבָּת
מִיָּד; וּבְשׁוֹגֵג, אָסוּר בּוֹ בַּיּוֹם גַּם לַאֲחֵרִים, וְלָעֶרֶב מֻתָּר
גַּם, לוֹ מִיָּד; {וְאִם אָמַר לְעַכּוּ״ם לַעֲשׂוֹת מְלָאכָה בְּשַׁבָּת,
עַיֵּן לְעֵיל סִימָן ש״ז סָעִיף כ׳.}

One who cooks on Shabbat, {or who performed one of the other categories of
labor (Tur)}, intentionally—for himself it is forbidden forever; for others
it is permitted immediately after Shabbat ends. Unintentionally—it is
forbidden that same day even for others; toward evening it is permitted for
others as well, and for him immediately. {And if he instructed a non-Jew to
perform melacha on Shabbat, see above, siman 307, seif 20.}
